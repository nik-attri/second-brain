😮 AI can turn a meeting into a perfectly organized task list, but what if the team is working on the wrong things in the first place? 

Claude's latest features from Anthropic show how much everyday work in companies could change. AI can now retrieve information from connected apps during meetings, record decisions and prepare task lists. There are also new tools for working with spreadsheets, data and collaborative planning.

What particularly caught my attention was the experience of the finance team at Advantage Solutions, which reportedly reduced the time needed to prepare forecasting materials from approximately 10 hours to less than 30 minutes.

That's a significant amount of time saved.

From a product management perspective, I see huge potential in how quickly information from meetings, workshops, and other discussions can be turned into actionable tasks. It could eliminate a lot of administrative work that someone currently has to handle manually, review and pass on to others.

However, I wouldn't want AI to start deciding priorities for the team. If we can't agree on what's truly important during a meeting, generating a task list faster won't solve that problem.
I'd also be cautious about access to internal information. Connecting AI to company systems is a great opportunity, but we need to be clear about which data it can access and what it's allowed to do with it. 

Personally, I'm looking forward to using tools like these because they could save us a lot of time currently spent on administrative tasks. At the same time, I believe people's ability to make good decisions and take responsibility for them will become even more important.
