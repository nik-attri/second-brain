I came across an interesting finding from Microsoft that got me thinking. Apparently, employees are interrupted almost every two minutes during working hours by emails, messages, or meetings.

Every TWO minutes!

And then we wonder why we struggle to finish our work on time.I personally think we're approaching this problem the wrong way.For instance, why not automate routine updates, task assignments, and follow ups so people don't have to constantly chase each other for information? 

Instead of using AI to respond to more emails and messages, shouldn't we be using it to reduce the need for them in the first place?

Maybe the problem isn't how we manage our time, but how we've designed our workflows.
