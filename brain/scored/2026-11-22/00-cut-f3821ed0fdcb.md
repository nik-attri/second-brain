At 24 I decided to buy my first house.
I spent weeks trying to convince my Mom this was a brilliant idea.

Her approval meant EVERYTHING to me, so hearing her say I was making a mistake hurt like hell.

I did it anyway. 

That property went on to help me buy another one, which has since doubled in value. 
And guess who lives there now? 

My mum 😂.

At 41, I decided to quit my corporate job and buy a franchise.

Naturally, I tried to get my Mom on board  🫢.

She pulled out a calculator, tore the spreadsheet apart, listed every single way I could fail, and told me I was taking a reckless risk.

I signed the papers anyway (despite feeling guilty as HELL).

Have all my big leaps worked out perfectly? No.

That franchise tested me in ways a corporate job never could.

But at least I made those decisions. And I learned a hell of a lot along the way.

As for the need for my Mom’s approval?

I got tired of having to sell my decisions to her like I was pitching on Dragon's Den.

I still ask for her feedback sometimes, I just don’t feel guilty when we disagree.

For all the over-explainers out there….

Where are you still trying to convince someone that you've made the right decision?
