⏳ "Never build what you can buy" is solid advice until your off-the-shelf CRM turns your operations team into full-time copy-pasters.

While supporting a multi-track international summit with 200+ speakers, our operations coordinators were drowning in manual data entry inside a commercial CRM. Standard CRM tools are built for linear sales pipelines, not complex event logistics like multi-track speaker packages, local vs. international payment routing, and hotel rooming batches.

⚖️ At the same time, ripping out the CRM right before a live summit carried real risk. If a brand-new custom system hit an unexpected edge case during event week, operations could not afford to stall.

🛠️ To fix it, we built a custom operations platform around the exact workflow and ran a silent live backup into the old CRM during Year One. That eliminated over 200 hours of manual work, kept every team safe through a full summit cycle, and now lets us retire the CRM for good, saving around $3,500 a year with zero database hosting costs.

🧭 If you are weighing custom software against off-the-shelf tools, three things stand out:

• Manual workarounds are a build signal: When staff spend more time formatting data for a tool than doing their real job, generic software has become the bottleneck.

• Hedge Year One instead of betting the launch: You do not need an all-or-nothing cutover. Syncing back to the legacy tool during the first cycle buys operational trust without risking live delivery.

• Count coordinator hours in software ROI: Custom internal tools often pay for themselves twice, first by cutting recurring seat licenses, and again by giving a small team its time back.
