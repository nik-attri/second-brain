One chapter ends. Another one begins.

Almost a year ago, I took up the role of HOD Marketing at DJSCE ECell.

I thought the role was going to be marketing.

Turns out, it was 30% marketing, 20% convincing people, 50% fixing things that broke 1 day before the event, and 100% figuring out what the hell we were doing.

And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.

From the absolute chaos of Rush Hour, to the madness that was Unhinged, and finally the scale of ESummit, this year had everything.

Rush Hour taught me that getting people to stop walking and actually visit a stall is harder than any marketing textbook makes it sound.

Unhinged was… well, unhinged.

And ESummit was probably the point where all the months of planning, calls, spreadsheets, designs, follow-ups and “guys, we have a problem” finally came together.

But if there's one thing I'll take away from this year, it's not the events.

It's the people.

The team that had the same passion as I did for ECell. The team that stayed back after meetings. The people who somehow managed to pull things off at the last minute. The countless random conversations that had absolutely nothing to do with work but somehow became the best part of the day.

So, after a year of being HOD Marketing, it's time for me to step down from the role.

And step up to something new.

I'm extremely excited to share that I'll be taking on the role of Chairperson of DJSCE E-Cell.

Which basically means I now have to pretend I know what I'm doing at a much higher level. :)

Jokes apart, I'm genuinely excited for what's ahead.

I want to build on everything we've done, push ECell beyond limits, and most importantly, make this a year that the entire team remembers.

For the ideas, the chaos, the arguments, the last-minute work, the laughs, and everything in between.

Here's to another year of making things happen.

Vidit Jain
Chairperson, DJSCE ECell
