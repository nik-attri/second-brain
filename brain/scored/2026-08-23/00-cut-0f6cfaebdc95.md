“How many Excel files exist in the world?”

That was one of the weirdest guesstimate questions I've been asked in an interview.

Not Excel users.
 Not Excel installations.
 Excel files.

And honestly, this got me in the first 5 minutes. 😂

I remember thinking:
“Okay… where the hell do I even start?”
Do I estimate Excel users? Companies? Students? Files per person? Do we count the 47 versions of the same spreadsheet sitting in someone's Downloads folder?

What made it even more interesting was the person asking it.
He had worked across Product and Analytics, founded his own startup, and was now back in Product. So I was expecting an interesting conversation.

I just didn't expect to spend the first few minutes of it mentally counting Excel files across the planet.

Later, when I interacted with him again, he told me he had asked the question just for fun.
And honestly, I loved that.

My interview journey so far has taught me that you can prepare for SQL, analytics, product cases and the usual guesstimates.

But there will always be that one question that makes you go:
“Yeah… I definitely didn't prepare for this.”
And maybe that's the point.

You don't always need to know the answer immediately. Sometimes, the real test is figuring out where to start thinking.

I still don't know how many Excel files exist in the world.

But now I really want to know. 😅
