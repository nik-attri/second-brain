🎯 DESIGN LEARNING BACKWARD: A WALMART STORY

What does it look like in practice?
It looks less like a course catalogue—and more like how Walmart prepared associates for the rollout of Pickup Towers.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🚨 THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Not:
“How do we increase training completion?”

But:
“How do we help frontline associates operate, troubleshoot and support customers with new technology—across thousands of stores—without disrupting operations?”

Pickup Towers were being rolled out across 4,700+ locations. The existing training approach took 8 hours and required instructors, e-learning, hands-on practice and launch support.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Walmart worked backward from the capability it needed:

🧩 ORGANIZATIONAL CAPABILITY
Associates had to operate the technology and support customers confidently.

🛠️ CRITICAL SKILLS & BEHAVIOURS
Set up, stock, troubleshoot, reboot or manually override the tower—and handle customer issues.

🥽 LEARNING EXPERIENCE
Walmart and STRIVR created live-action VR practice based on the real work, not a generic course.

🔄 WORKPLACE APPLICATION
Associates could practise safely before the equipment was installed, without disrupting store operations.

📈 PERFORMANCE CHANGE → BUSINESS IMPACT

Reported results:
⏱️ 8 hours → 15 minutes of training

📉 96% reduction in training time

📊 10–15% higher post-training scores

✅ Higher performance in 70% of reported assessment comparisons

😊 30% higher trainee satisfaction

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The real lesson is not “use VR.”

It is:

🎯 Start with the business outcome.

🧩 Define the capability required.

🛠️ Identify the behaviours that matter.

🥽 Choose the fastest credible way to build them.

🔄 Reinforce them in the flow of work.

📈 Measure whether performance moved.
