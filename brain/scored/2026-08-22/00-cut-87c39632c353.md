Recently, during a discussion on digital transformation and the endless "AI vs. HI" debate, someone said to me: 

“You need to be smart to use AI.”

I replied: “No. You need to come to the realization that AI is created by HI (Human Intelligence).”

True intelligence in leadership is understanding that AI is a support tool, not a replacement for human mastery. It is a powerful complement to executive efficiency and productivity. And this reality extends far beyond generating images or writing generic media copy—it applies across every domain of expertise. 

Let’s look at the reality of a fast-paced, high-demand corporate environment:

■ The Graphic Designer: AI cannot totally replace the creative soul, cultural nuance, or brand strategy of a talented designer. But when equipped with AI, that designer can accelerate their workflow, scale their productivity, and easily keep up with the aggressive, fast-paced demands of our clients. 

■ The Junior Project Manager: AI won't resolve a complex team conflict, read the room, or negotiate with a difficult stakeholder. But it will automate the logistical heavy lifting and data tracking, allowing a junior PM to operate with the efficiency of a seasoned veteran.

■ The Executive: AI can process millions of data points and generate predictive models in seconds. But it cannot weigh the ethical, relational, or neurobiological impact of a high-stakes decision. The data belongs to the machine; the final call—the true leadership—belongs to the human.

AI doesn’t replace your team. It handles the processing so your people can handle the leading. 

Are you using AI to attempt to replace your talent, or to empower their productivity?
