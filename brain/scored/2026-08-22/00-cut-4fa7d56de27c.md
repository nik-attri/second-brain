Here's what happened when we automated client onboarding for a coach drowning in manual work.

The problem was familiar: every new client triggered the same 5+ tasks. Schedule the intake call. Send the contract. Generate the invoice. Fire off the welcome email. Remind them to book their first session.

All manual. All on the owner's plate.

The result? 10+ hours a week lost to admin. Leads going cold if they didn't hear back within 24 hours. And growth that stalled because the owner was the bottleneck — if they were in a session, the follow-up didn't happen.

We replaced that with a single automated flow. The trigger is a simple "book a call" link. From there, the system handles everything:

- Calendar invites with reminder sequences
- Contracts via e-signature
- Invoices with automatic payment reminders
- Welcome packets and pre-session questionnaires
- CRM updates with the correct status

No spreadsheets. No copy-paste. No "I'll do it later."

The outcome: onboarding time dropped from 10+ hours per week to under 30 minutes of oversight. Lead response time went from 24+ hours to immediate. The coach stopped losing prospects to slow follow-ups, and the system works even when they don't.

Now they scale by adding clients, not hours. That's the difference between fragile growth and a system that actually supports growth.

You don't need a tech team. You need one person who can see the whole picture and make it work.

Cut the busywork.
