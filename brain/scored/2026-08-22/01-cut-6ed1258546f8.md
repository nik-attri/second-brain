From ServiceNow ITSM to ServiceNow Agentic AI: How AI Agents Are Transforming in ServiceNow Operations.
For years, IT Service Management (ITSM) has been helping organisations manage their Incidents, Service Requests, Problems, Changes and more. 
So what happens if we can go one step further 
🔹 What is ITSM?
ITSM stands for Information Technology Service Management .It is about managing IT services and how they support your employees & customers.
For example:
A user can’t access their application — A ticket gets created — An IT agent investigates the problem and resolves it — ✅ A ticket gets closed 
Although this is working fine for you, there are numerous cases of repetitive, manual efforts when handling IT Service Management (ITSM) tickets. 
So what’s Agentic AI? Let’s find out
Traditional AI usually works like this:
User asks → AI responds
Agentic AI works differently:
Understand → Reason → Decide → Act → Verify
This means an AI agent will be able to understand the problem, figure out what comes next, interact with systems, carry out some actions and verify that the problem has been solved.  So how is this going to affect ServiceNow’s operations?  
Imagine a user reports: What I can’t do is create a ticket.
If an AI agent had been around at that point, it might have done all of this – understood my issue, checked my access and other info, searched for related incidents, identified what was wrong, took some approved action to fix it (like changing passwords), verified that I now did have access again, updated our ServiceNow record with details about this incident, informed me that things were fixed, and escalated to someone else where necessary.
Importantly, unlike current implementations, this AI isn’t just giving recommendations about what your typical IT engineer should be doing. It’s able to actively participate in those processes, within its own set of parameters – called “permissions,” “policies” and “guardrails.”
