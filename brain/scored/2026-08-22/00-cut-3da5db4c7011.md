Recently, a security and auditing company approached us with a very specific requirement.

They had invoices stored locally on their computers.

They were already maintaining client-wise folders and directories, but they did not want to upload those invoices into another cloud application just to extract the data and push it into Tally.

𝗔𝘁 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁, 𝗶𝘁 𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗱 𝗹𝗶𝗸𝗲 𝗮 𝘀𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝗶𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗮𝘂𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺.

But the real problem was data movement.

And this is something I believe many CAs, audit firms and compliance teams face.

They already have a workflow:
𝗖𝗹𝗶𝗲𝗻𝘁 , 𝗟𝗼𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗙𝗼𝗹𝗱𝗲𝗿, 𝗜𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗶𝗰𝗲𝘀 , 𝗧𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆

The problem starts when automation tools say:

“Upload your documents to our platform.”

For sensitive client documents, that isn't always acceptable.

So instead of asking the client to change their workflow, we changed the approach.

𝗝𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗽𝗼𝗶𝗻𝘁 𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗴𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝘁𝗼 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗲𝘅𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗹𝗼𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗶𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗱𝗶𝗿𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗼𝗿𝘆.

That's it.

The integration can:

→ Watch the designated local folder
→ Read PDF/image invoices
→ Extract the required information
→ Construct the Tally data
→ Send it directly into the client's local Tally Prime environment

  • 𝗡𝗼 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱 𝘁𝗼 𝗺𝗮𝗻𝘂𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗽𝗶𝗰𝗸 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝗶𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗶𝗰𝗲.
  • 𝗡𝗼 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱 𝘁𝗼 𝗴𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗳𝗶𝗹𝗲𝘀 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗺 𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝗯𝘆 𝗼𝗻𝗲.

And most importantly, the accountant can continue using the client-wise folder structure they already have.

This experience made us realise something important:

Automation doesn't always mean moving data to the cloud.

Sometimes the best automation is the one that works around the existing workflow.

For a CA firm handling multiple clients, this can be especially useful because every client already has their own folders, documents and accounting process.
