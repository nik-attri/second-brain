Was talking to a friend who’s a practising CA last week, and he casually mentioned the stuff that eats his firm’s time every week — matching GST inputs from the portal against client purchase bills, manually entering bank statements into Tally, the usual repetitive grind that nobody enjoys but everyone has to do.

We ended up sitting down and mapping out where automation actually fits into that workflow. Not replacing the judgment calls, just the matching and data-entry parts that don’t need a CA’s brain to do — they need consistency.

He’s genuinely excited to build this out with me, which honestly says a lot. These aren’t people resistant to change, they’re people drowning in work that shouldn’t need a human doing it manually in 2026.

This is the part of “AI in finance” that doesn’t get talked about enough — not flashy, not a chatbot demo, just hours getting freed up every week. But it only works if a human is still validating the output, not blindly trusting it.
