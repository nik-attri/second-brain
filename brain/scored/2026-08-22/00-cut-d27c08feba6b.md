A bit gutted not to make the cut on the C:\> Global Agentic Hackathon, but so grateful to have had my submission considered by the judges!

My proposal focused on solving a major regulatory bottleneck: how supervisors audit autonomous AI advice systems without drowning in manual evidence. I designed a hierarchical agentic architecture (featuring an Obligation Mapper, Evidence Verifier, and strict Critic gate) that ingests structured JSON evidence packs to generate replayable, audit-ready case files for case officers in under 90 seconds.

Wishing the absolute best of luck to everyone who made the finalist list! We’re all cheering you on and looking forward to seeing what gets built. 

Thanks to Cambridge Digital Innovation and Regulation Initiative for organising such a top-tier event! 🙌
