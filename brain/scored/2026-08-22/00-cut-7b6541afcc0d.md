I got tired of manually hunting for those financial leaks on my AWS architecture. So, I built an AI agent to do it for me.

Meet CloudPulse: a serverless AI co-pilot I built to keep cloud costs in check, automatically.

Powered by Python and Amazon Bedrock, I designed it to act like a senior DevOps engineer looking over your shoulder. Here is how CloudPulse works:

1.  It Sniffs out waste: It actively monitors your environment to flag idle resources and over-provisioned instances.
2.  Speaks Human Language: Instead of throwing raw logs or AWS jargon at you, it explains the required fixes in plain English.
3. It Takes Action: It can auto-apply safe optimizations instantly (but always waits for your one-click approval first).

The Pilot Result:
We recently deployed this and slashed non-prod spend by **68%**—saving about $1,200 a month that was otherwise just vaporizing.
