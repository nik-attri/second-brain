The best GenAI products don’t just give better answers. They make better decisions possible.

One of the biggest shifts I’ve seen in GenAI product thinking is moving from:

“How intelligent is the model?”

to

“What decision or action does this intelligence enable?”

A GenAI feature can produce a brilliant summary, an impressive response, or a highly accurate analysis.

But if the user still has to:
→ interpret the output
→ decide what matters
→ figure out what to do next
→ execute the action manually

then we’ve built an impressive AI feature — not necessarily a great product.

The real product opportunity is to build the intelligence-to-action loop:

Data → Understanding → Insight → Recommendation → Decision → Action → Outcome
