My AI agency makes $31k/mo.
With a team of 3.

Earlier this year, we were 6 people making way less.

It was a mess. More people, more meetings and somehow more work still landing on me.

So I spent every day building internal AI systems to organize and automate the business. 

Now revenue is growing month over month.
The team stays at 3.

We even have enough capacity to build our own products alongside client work.

A few of the systems behind it:

1. Agency Brain
One place for our knowledge, processes, decisions and project memory.

Any AI we use can read it and understand how Tasken works. No more explaining the same business context in every session.

2. PM agent
It reads client messages and tickets, creates tasks, assigns them to the right person and asks for any missing details.
We don’t have a project manager.

3. QA agent
It tests every build like a client would, catches the small issues and fixes the ones it can before a human reviews anything.

You get it. 

Boring systems, running every day to makes us make more money. That’s the goal.
