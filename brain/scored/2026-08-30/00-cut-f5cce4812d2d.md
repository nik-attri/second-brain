Auditing shouldn’t mean drowning in manual data reconciliations or spending hours drafting initial analytical review memos.
When applied strategically, AI can act as a high-speed analytical assistant for audit teams and finance leaders. Here are 5 high-impact prompts you can use right now to streamline your internal audit, compliance checks, and analytical procedures:
1. Variance & Analytical Review Memo
"Act as an experienced financial auditor. Analyze the following quarterly P&L variances [insert numbers/percentages]. Draft a preliminary analytical review memo highlighting potential operational drivers, key risk areas, and 3 specific follow-up questions for management regarding the material increases."
2. Internal Controls Testing Design
"Draft a Risk and Control Matrix (RCM) for the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) cycle for a mid-sized enterprise. Include key risks, preventive vs. detective control descriptions, assertion mappings (Completeness, Accuracy, Cut-off), and suggested audit test procedures."
3. Journal Entry Anomaly Detection Framework
"Provide a structured checklist and Excel/SQL query logic to test General Ledger journals for fraud and anomaly indicators, including: round-sum postings, off-hours/weekend entries, unusual user access, and entries posted just below approval thresholds."
4. Related Party Transaction (RPT) Scrutiny
"List critical audit verification steps for identifying unrecorded Related Party Transactions (RPTs) under applicable statutory frameworks. Focus on vendor master reviews, unusual credit terms, and loans/advances scrutiny."
5. Audit Query & PBC List Structuring
"Refine the following rough audit observations into clear, professional Provided by Client (PBC) documentation requests. Ensure each item specifies the required document, audit rationale, sample period, and format to minimize back-and-forth delays: [insert list]."
