𝐖𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐚 𝐆𝐨𝐨𝐝 𝐄𝐑𝐏 𝐏𝐚𝐫𝐭𝐧𝐞𝐫 𝐀𝐬𝐤𝐬 𝐓𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐚 𝐁𝐚𝐝 𝐎𝐧𝐞 𝐍𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐫 𝐃𝐨𝐞𝐬

A bad ERP partner starts by asking what features you want.
A good ERP partner starts by asking why you need them.
There's a big difference between the two approaches.

Before recommending modules or discussing customization, a good consultant wants to understand how your business actually operates.

Here are a few questions every business should expect from a serious ERP partner:

How does your current workflow operate?
Where do delays and bottlenecks happen?
Which processes are still managed manually?
Who approves important business decisions?
What problems are you expecting Odoo to solve?
What should improve after implementation?

These questions aren't just part of a discovery call. They help prevent unnecessary customization, unrealistic expectations, and expensive mistakes later.

𝐑𝐞𝐚𝐥-𝐥𝐢𝐟𝐞 𝐞𝐱𝐚𝐦𝐩𝐥𝐞

In our Odoo implementation work, we've learned that asking the right questions often reveals problems that clients initially don't mention.

A client may request an automated approval process, but deeper discussions can reveal that approval responsibilities aren't even clearly defined.

Instead of immediately building a customization, we first understand the process and identify what actually needs to change.

A good ERP partner doesn't just listen to what you want to build. They understand what your business needs to improve.

If you're evaluating Odoo partners, look beyond their technical skills. Pay attention to the questions they ask before proposing a solution.
