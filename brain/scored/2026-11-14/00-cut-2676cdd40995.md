Email has a time and a place, but the scope of its application and use is much broader than it should be, why is that? Too difficult to replace with something else, that may have been the case, but not anymore!

- How badly were you affected over the last couple of days, no email, teams down, no access to business information?

I’ve had a flurry of calls from people wanting alternatives, and particularly if cyberattacks are going to have more and more of an impact, targeting centralised services like Microsoft 365 & Teams.

When outages occur like this it sharpens the mind, and helps you imagine what the future might look like in the event of even more focused cyberattacks.

People are talking about Sovereign AI, Sovereign Data, but this extends to Sovereign Business Operations Platforms, risk mitigating disruption and outages.

In 2011, Atos set out to eliminate internal email. In 2026 maybe that’s something to consider?

Under CEO Thierry Breton, Atos launched the Zero Email initiative, challenging the inefficiency of employees spending hours every week managing internal communications.

By 2013, Atos reported a 60% reduction in internal email.

It was an ambitious move, but largely focused on replacing email with alternative collaboration tools.

The real opportunity isn’t changing how we communicate. It’s changing how we operate.

Email has become the default mechanism for managing business processes that, frankly, should have been engineered into effective operational systems years ago.

Requests, approvals, updates, escalations, instructions, reporting and endless follow-ups. All flowing through unstructured conversations, creating inefficiency, duplication, limited visibility and lost organisational knowledge.

Agentic AI changes the equation.

With TAAP our AI engineering & agents can now help define, build and operate structured business applications, turning fragmented communications into intelligent, connected and auditable workflows.
