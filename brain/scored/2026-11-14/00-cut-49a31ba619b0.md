☀️ A sunny Saturday morning in Paris, and a little payment experience to reflect on before heading to my next destination.

At Visa, we talk a lot about bringing the card-not-present experience up to the card-present level.

Well, what a funny coincidence that on my last night in Paris, my own Visa card got declined! 😅

The reason? A manually keyed card transaction, without the additional authentication required by my issuer.

Luckily, I was just around the corner from the hotel. I called and asked them, "Please don't cancel my reservation! I'm coming!"

A few minutes later, I arrived in person, did a classic dip (aka Chip & PIN) and... voilà! 💳✅ Approved!

Problem solved. Old-school technology to the rescue!

But here's the question that stayed with me:

What if I had been far away?

What if I had been on a train, on a plane, or simply unable to get to the hotel in time?

The same card, the same customer, the same merchant, the same amount. Yet two completely different payment experiences.

Of course, authentication is there for a reason. But this little adventure reminded me that improving digital payments isn't just about security or approval rates. It's also about making sure legitimate customers can complete their transactions without unnecessary friction.

And sometimes, the best reminder of why we do what we do comes from being on the other side of the payment terminal.

Have we really brought card-not-present up to the card-present experience yet? 😉

Bonjour from Paris! 🇫🇷
