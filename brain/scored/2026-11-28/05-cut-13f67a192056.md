WE ARE HIRING | RETAIL STORE MANAGER
Company: Nirvana Corporation
Brands: SOMNIA & The Super Kid
Location: Kokapet, Hyderabad
Experience: 3–5 Years
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role
Nirvana Corporation is looking for an experienced and responsible Store Manager to oversee the operations of our two retail stores, SOMNIA and The Super Kid, located in Kokapet, Hyderabad.
The candidate will be responsible for managing both stores, supervising sales staff, achieving sales targets, maintaining inventory, and ensuring excellent customer service and store standards.

Key Responsibilities
Manage the daily operations of both SOMNIA and The Super Kid retail stores.
Supervise, coordinate, and manage the sales staff across both stores.
Ensure proper staff allocation, attendance, and work discipline.
Achieve monthly sales targets and improve overall store performance.
Monitor daily sales, billing, cash handling, and POS operations.
Manage inventory, stock transfers, replenishment, and stock audits.
Ensure proper product display and visual merchandising in both stores.
Maintain store cleanliness, presentation, and brand standards.
Handle customer queries, complaints, and escalations.
Coordinate with the warehouse, accounts, and operations teams.
Prepare and submit daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports.
Monitor store expenses and ensure operational efficiency.
Ensure smooth coordination between both stores.

Required Skills & Qualifications
3–5 years of experience in retail store management.
Experience managing multiple retail outlets is preferred.
Experience in bedding, home furnishings, baby products, or lifestyle retail is an advantage.
Strong leadership and team management skills.
Good communication and customer handling abilities.
Knowledge of POS billing, inventory management, and retail operations.
Proficiency in MS Excel and sales reporting.
Ability to manage sales targets and handle multiple store operations.
