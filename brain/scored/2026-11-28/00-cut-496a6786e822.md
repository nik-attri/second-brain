DAY 19 OF THE TS ACADEMY 30 DAYS CHALLENGE! 🚀

This was a whole lot!

Despite being 30 minutes late for the live class, it was still value-packed, and I learnt a lot.

Because I missed the first few minutes, I joined the class just as they were connecting the weather notification to Telegram in Zapier. That part is still a bit fuzzy, though. 😂

We also worked with an example company that receives customer enquiries through its website and wants to automate how those enquiries are handled.

Here's the request we worked with:

THE TASK:

We are a company that receives customer enquiries through our website and wants to automate how these enquiries are handled.

We need a form where customers can submit their details, including:

Full Name
Email Address
Phone Number
Company Name
Type of Inquiry (Sales, Support, Partnership)
Budget (Below $500, $500–$1,000, Above $1,000)
Message

Whenever a customer submits the form, their information should be cleaned and properly formatted before being processed.

We want customers with budgets above $500 to be added to our Google Sheets database and receive a confirmation email.

Customers with budgets below $500 should also have their details recorded, but they should receive a different email explaining that our services may not be suitable for their current budget.

We also want a short delay before confirmation emails are sent, as well as an email notification to our sales team whenever a new enquiry comes in.

THE TOOLS AND THE LEARNING:

We also explored some more Zapier tools, including Delay, Formatter, Webhook, and others.
I created and published my form, although it is yet to receive any submissions.

One thing I learnt is that Webhooks are a premium feature on Zapier. So, although you can use them in your workflow, you won't be able to publish that workflow without a paid subscription.

An insightful lesson all in all!

Nothing was particularly difficult. I'm getting a better understanding of how these tools work together,
