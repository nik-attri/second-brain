I know the people who have a problem with AI don't bake nor cook... 😂

Because, if I mix all the ingredients, whip them up, prepare my cake and put it in the oven, it's not the oven that gets the credit for the cake!

Heck, people rarely even ask which brand of oven you used to achieve your end product. But suddenly, when it comes to AI, we're having a whole existential crisis.

So what now? Because ovens exist, we must stop using them? We must go back to baking cakes in pots? DO NOT ADVANCE! How dare you embrace technology when you could be doing things the old-fashioned way? 😭

And let's talk about cooking!

When we're cooking, we don't just throw everything into a pot and walk away. We stir, we taste, we adjust the seasoning, we add a little something, perhaps take something out, stir again and keep tasting until we get the desired outcome.

We are still the cooks! We are still exercising judgement. We are still making decisions about what stays, what goes and what needs changing.

In fact, we're constantly changing the output until it reflects what we had in mind.

But nobody stands over your pot screaming, "AI GENERATED!" because you used a food processor, an electric stove or a blender. 😂

I don't understand the hostility towards AI.

It's giving: Why are you using a computer? You have a pen and paper! Please continue using pen and paper. Search and research, manually, the old-fashioned way. Never mind the time you could save.

And while we're at it, why are you on your phone, kid? Go outside! Get dirty! Enjoy nature! Why are you playing Xbox when you could be outside playing?

Now, don't get me wrong. There's value in traditional methods. There's value in human interaction, craftsmanship, independent thinking and knowing how to do things without technological assistance.

But why must embracing one mean abandoning the other?

To me, some of these arguments are essentially saying we're against progress and evolution.

It's like saying, let's stick to letters.
