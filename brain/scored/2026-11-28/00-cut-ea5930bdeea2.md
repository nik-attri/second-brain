My brother logs his work hours in an Excel spreadsheet. Every shift, every hour, typed in by hand.
So today I built him a simple website where he can log each hour he works.
Is there anything groundbreaking about it? No. There are plenty of time-tracking apps that do more. But this one is his. No ads, no subscriptions, no features he'll never use, and no relying on a tool built by people who don't know him.
Under the hood it's plain HTML and JavaScript, hosted on Netlify, with Netlify Blobs handling the data storage. Sometimes simple is exactly what's needed.
Next comes the real test: seeing if he actually ditches the spreadsheet.
