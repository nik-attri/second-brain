Three years. That's how long the average South African small business lasts before the numbers catch up.

Not a guess — the data says 70-80% of SMMEs in South Africa don't survive their first five years. Three quarters of every business started this year will be gone by 2031.

Here's what doesn't get said enough: those same small businesses make up over 90% of all formal businesses in this country. They contribute roughly 34% to GDP. They employ between 50% and 60% of the workforce.

South Africa runs on small business. And most of them are drowning quietly.

The reasons aren't exotic. Cash flow gaps that nobody warns you about. Admin that eats the hours you should be selling. VAT201 deadlines that arrive like clockwork whether you're ready or not. One bad month erasing three good ones.

The businesses that make it past year five aren't the ones with the best product. They're the ones who built systems early — even basic, ugly, manual ones — before they needed them.

If you're running a small business in SA right now, the system you haven't built yet is the one that will save you.
