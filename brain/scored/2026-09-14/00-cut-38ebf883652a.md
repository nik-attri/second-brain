Imagine a shipping company's process that has 47 manual data entries per day.

47.

Someone is copying order numbers from WhatsApp → Excel → their CRM every single day. 

By hand.

That is revenue being left on the table. 

Because while someone is copying numbers, your operations leader isn't doing pricing reviews or customer retention calls.

The math is simple. 47 entries × 5 min per entry = 235 minutes. That's almost 4 hours per day in spreadsheet hell.

Most people don't see it as a problem until someone shows them what's possible.
