VIRTUAL ASSISTANTS: AI is coming for some of your tasks. Not your value....
If you're a Virtual Assistant, stop trying to become better at doing everything manually.

AI is already getting very good at some of the work you're being paid to do.

And no, I'm not here to tell you to panic.

I'm here to show you where your value needs to move.

Last week, we started our journey into different professional territories.

Today, we're going inside:

THE VIRTUAL ASSISTANT.

After researching how work is changing across high-AI markets, including the US and China, this is the breakdown I recommend.

 STOP

Let AI take over work it is already incredibly good at:

• Calendar scheduling and rescheduling
• Routine email sorting and drafting
• Meeting transcription and summaries
• Routine data entry
• Repetitive research and information gathering

If AI can reliably handle it, your value should not depend on how quickly you can do it manually.

Your time shouldn't be trapped inside routine tasks.

 AUGMENT

Use AI to become better at work that requires judgment, context and understanding:

• Prepare executive briefings
• Research people, companies and meetings
• Identify patterns across information
• Prepare drafts and options for decisions
• Monitor multiple information sources
• Connect workflows
• Surface information your executive may need before they ask

The goal is not:

“How can AI do my job for me?”

It is:

“How can AI help me operate at a higher level?”

STRENGTHEN

Your fundamental VA skills are not becoming useless. In many cases, they're becoming more important.

Strengthen:

• Communication
• Judgment
• Problem-solving
• Executive understanding
• Prioritisation
• Stakeholder management

When AI handles more of the routine, understanding the person, business and situation becomes more valuable.

 ADD

Build capabilities around:

• AI-assisted research
• Workflow design
• No-code automation
• AI agents and agentic workflows
• Knowledge management
• AI output evaluati
