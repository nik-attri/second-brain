जय श्री राम 🙏

Some Tuesdays call for more than routine. They call for Ram.

Goswami Tulsidas did not write the Ramcharitmanas as mythology. He wrote it as a manual for the restless mind. Centuries later, the lines still work like medicine.

◇

THE ETERNAL DESIGN

होइहि सोइ जो राम रचि राखा।
को करि तर्क बढ़ावै साखा॥

Whatever Shri Ram has already ordained will come to pass. Why stretch the branches of argument and worry over it.

We spend so much energy debating outcomes we cannot change. This line is not resignation. It is relief.

◇

BEYOND OUR CONTROL

हानि लाभु जीवनु मरनु जसु अपजसु बिधि हाथ।
सोचिअ नहिं सोइ जो विधि लिखा, सोइ होइहि साथ॥

Loss and gain, life and death, fame and disgrace, all rest in the hands of destiny. Do not grieve over what is written. It will unfold as it must.

Leadership, parenting, ambition, all of it asks us to try fully and hold the result loosely.

◇

FOUR THIEVES OF PEACE

काम, क्रोध, मद, लोभ सब, नाथ नरक के पंथ।
सब परिहरि रघुबीरहि, भजहिं जेहि संत॥

Desire, anger, pride and greed are the paths that lead a person downward. The wise let go of all four and turn toward the Divine.

Nothing has changed in a thousand years. These four still run every boardroom battle and every family quarrel.

◇

THE BARREN FIELD

क्रोधिहि सम कामिहि हरिकथा।
ऊसर बीज बएँ फल जथा॥

Speaking of the Divine to a mind full of anger and craving is like sowing seed in barren land. Nothing will grow until the soil is prepared.

Wisdom cannot enter a mind that is not first made ready.

◇

TWO KINDS OF DEAF EARS

ममता रत सन ग्यान कहानी।
अति लोभी सन बिरति बखानी॥

Teaching knowledge to one drowning in attachment, or preaching detachment to one consumed by greed, both fall on deaf ears.

Timing matters as much as truth. Even the right words need the right moment.

◇
