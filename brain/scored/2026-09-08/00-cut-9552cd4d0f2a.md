Security and compliance used to feel like a tax on building speed. Here's how we stopped treating it that way.

At Lamatic, we've been quietly wiring together a stack that lets us move fast without cutting corners on security. No dedicated security team — just a few well-chosen tools and some AI in the right places.

What we're running:

→ Aikido Security: continuous vulnerability scanning across our codebase and dependencies. Catches things before they become incidents.

→ Vanta: handles our compliance monitoring and evidence collection automatically. SOC 2 prep without the manual spreadsheet hell.

→ GitHub: the backbone — everything runs through PRs, and the audit trail is built in.

→ Code Rabbit: AI-powered code review that flags issues during the PR itself, not after.

→ Claude plugin: we use it for reviewing configs, drafting security policies, and sense-checking architecture decisions quickly.

The honest insight: most early-stage teams treat security as a one-time checkbox before a big customer or audit. We've learned it's cheaper to build it into the daily workflow than to retrofit it later.

The tools exist. The AI layer makes them actually usable for a small team without a full-time security hire.

Curious — what's in your security stack at the early stage? Always looking for what we might be missing.
