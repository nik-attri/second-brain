Remember when dashboarding meant hours of data integration hell?

Back then, you'd wrestle with fragile connectors, battle schema evolution and null-poisoned aggregations, pray the distributed query wouldn't time out and end up with a "production" table that still needed a data whisperer to translate.

Two weeks ago, I sat down with some semi-large datasets on my local machine.

No ETL pipelines. 

Just an AI powered IDE, natural language, and a bit of contextual know-how.

Extract. Transform. Load. Present.

What used to require a small team, a budget, and a prayer now happens in conversation. 

Dashboards that normally would've been rudimentary spreadsheet pivots are now interactive, polished, and shareable, built in minutes, not days.

This is what genuine democratisation looks like.

Not the vendor-hyped "anyone can be a data scientist" nonsense that most professionals are rightfully sceptical of. 

But real, tangible access: the ability for someone with relevant domain knowledge to ask questions of their data and get answers.

The execution barrier is gone. 

The bottleneck shifts from execution to cognition. 

What's left is what always mattered: knowing what question to ask.

AI doesn’t make data analysis trivial. 

It makes it accessible.
