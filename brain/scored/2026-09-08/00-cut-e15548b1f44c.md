Growth doesn't create complexity. Disconnected systems do.

As an eCommerce business scales, new platforms become part of the workflow.

Orders come from Shopify and Amazon.
Shipping is managed through Shiprocket, Blue Dart, and DTDC.
Payments, COD remittances, and delivery updates all live in different systems.

Each platform works well on its own.

The problem begins when someone needs a complete picture.

Operations teams end up downloading multiple Excel files, manually matching order IDs, verifying delivery statuses, and reconciling payments across different platforms.

Hours are spent answering a simple question:

"Has this order been completed from end to end?"

The data already exists.

It's just scattered across different systems.

At Handysolver, we believe software shouldn't force people to become data analysts. It should bring information together automatically.

By reconciling orders, deliveries, and payments into a single view, businesses can:
✔️ Reduce manual reconciliation.
✔️ Eliminate spreadsheet matching.
✔️ Improve payment and COD tracking.
✔️ Get real-time visibility into every order.
✔️ Spend less time compiling reports and more time serving customers.

Good software doesn't replace people.

It gives them a clearer view of their business, so they can focus on growth instead of spreadsheets.
