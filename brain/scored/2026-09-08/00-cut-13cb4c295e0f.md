Here's the question I keep coming back to — not 'are you using AI?' but 'what actually changed when you did?'

There's a specific version of this I want to hear.

Not the transformation deck. Not the productivity stat. The concrete before-and-after from someone on your team who used to own a particular task every single week — and then didn't anymore.

The ops manager who stopped rebuilding that one spreadsheet every Monday.
The HR lead who handed off the first-pass candidate screening and spent the afternoon doing something that actually needed her judgment.
The marketing coordinator who stopped manually pulling channel reports and started using that hour to brief the creative team properly.

Not hypothetically. Not 'we're planning to.' The task your team actually stopped doing manually — and what filled the gap.

Because that second part is the part we talk about least.

The time reclaimed is obvious in hindsight. What's less obvious — and more interesting — is what the team chose to do with it. Did strategy finally get the space it deserved? Did someone pick up a piece of work they'd been putting off for months? Did the team just collectively exhale?

This isn't a survey. It's an invitation for the specific story.

Ops, HR, marketing, finance — wherever you sit: what's the one workflow your team finally handed off to AI, and what did you actually do with the time?
