So, I did a thing. TL;DR - www.psychlog.com.au

Every single provisional psychologist understands the Sunday night logbook dread. We have to record every single session, what we did, how long it took, what we thought about it, yadadada. (Timesheets?  Pffft, child's play by comparison). 

I tried ALL the apps and ALL the spreadsheets and they were ALL clunky as hell. Well, some were okay but man, they are not designed to be quick, efficient, and easy. 

So I built something that is! https://psychlog.com.au

I'm super proud of it. My logbook used to take whole Sunday nights. Now it takes minutes. Literally. 

Here's what Psychlog fixes: 

* Speed: Built in a grid layout (like a spreadsheet) so you can "type, Tab, next" - and you can drag cells just like in a spreadsheet too; Ctl +D to duplicate (coz honestly, some days are just like yesterday, right?); Ctl +Z to undo - oh and predictive type-forward text for common phrases. (No other app does this - they *all* have really slow, form-based data entry. My time is more precious than that.)

* Import: A super fast import facility so you can bring your existing logbook entries across in one upload - not just your totals. I'm amazed none of the other systems I tried had this function. 

* AHPRA Compliance: Automatically tracks every single AHPRA requirement in real time so you know exactly where you stand, how far you've come, and how much more you've got to go. And it does all the maths for you! 

* Frictionless Sign-Offs: Supervisors can review and sign off via a secure link without needing to create an account or log into a portal.

* Privacy First: Row-level encryption, hosted locally in Sydney, and strictly de-identified (privacy and security - obvs, based on my background - was a fundamental design principle)

* Instant Reports: It just works. Just click "Weekly Report" then "Email to Supervisor". Everything's done for you. I love it.
