🚛For a logistics company, What do you think it costs more? The vehicle ir a vehicle accident?
Or the time the team loses figuring out what happened? 👀

In logistics, reporting friction matters.

If drivers need to explain the incident manually, teams need to identify the vehicle, reconstruct the context, classify the problem, and then create the ticket, the resolution is already starting late.

💻Based on that problem, we created a Forge and AI solution that approaches the problem from the opposite direction.

We have built FleetOps to give the system for logistics companies the context first by allowing drives just sharing an image and leave to JSM, AI, and Jira Assets do the rest

So when the support team has the ticket, it's already classified and prioritized, shortening the distance between “something happened” to “someone act on it.”
