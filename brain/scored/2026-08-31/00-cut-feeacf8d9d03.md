I thought building a fictional brand would be… easy.
Pick a name.
Make it look pretty.
Build a website.
Put some numbers in Excel.
Make a Canva deck.
Done. ✨
Yeah… no. 

I recently built Nest & Form, a fictional interior design brand, as a practice project.
And while the final project looked pretty neat, the process behind it was basically a long series of:
“Wait… I have to figure THIS out too?”

🌐 The website
I thought building a website was mostly about making it look good.
Then I actually tried building one.
Structure. Content. Layout. Responsiveness. Tiny things randomly breaking. Fixing one thing and somehow creating another problem.
I've tried building websites multiple times now, and I'm still very much a beginner.
But every attempt makes a little more sense than the last one.

📊 The Excel financial model
I thought:
“I know Excel. How hard can this be?”
Turns out… knowing Excel ≠ knowing how to build a financial model. 😭
I had to understand what each number actually meant, how assumptions affected the business, how different parts connected, and why one small mistake could throw everything off.
The biggest lesson?
Excel wasn't the hard part. Understanding the business behind the spreadsheet was.

And then there was AI
I used ChatGPT and Claude throughout the project, and honestly, one thing became very clear:
Getting AI to make something ≠ understanding what it made.
Sometimes AI helped me move ridiculously fast.
Sometimes I had to stop and figure out what the hell was actually happening. 

I'm still a beginner at all of this.
But that's probably the biggest thing this project taught me:
You don't really understand something until you've tried to make it work.
So I'm trying to consume less and build more.
Even if the first version is messy.
