Yesterday, something interesting happened.
For just 2 days of setting this up and sharing that I'm hosting this Free Masterclass: 
63 people registered for The AI-Powered Analyst.
28 joined us live.
But honestly, the number I'm most interested in isn't either of those.
It's what happened during and after the conversation.
We talked about LLMs, AI architecture, workflows, data, Business Analysis, Product, Project, Transformation and career opportunities.

But underneath all of that was one much bigger question:
“What happens to the way I work now that AI can do more of the work?”
And that question is becoming impossible to ignore.
AI can already help us:
→ research faster
→ structure messy information
→ analyse data
→ generate first drafts
→ challenge ideas
→ redesign workflows
→ identify opportunities for automation
And we did live demos of this.

But here's the part I think we often get wrong:
The goal isn't to make humans less involved.
It's to make humans more valuable.
Because when AI can produce the first draft, the analyst's job becomes less about producing and more about questioning, validating, interpreting, deciding and creating value.
That is a very different skillset.
And it creates some interesting career opportunities.

Business Analysis.
Data.
Product.
Project.
Transformation.
AI-enabled roles.

The people who can connect business + data + technology + AI + people are going to be increasingly useful.
That was the heart of yesterday's session.
And I'm grateful to everyone who showed up, participated and challenged the ideas.
