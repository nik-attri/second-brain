I keep seeing people use “AI” and “automation” like they mean the same thing. They don’t.

Automation is simple: something happens automatically because you told it to.

Employee submits a leave request, it lands on their manager’s desk without anyone lifting a finger. That’s it. No thinking involved, just a process running on rails.

AI is different. It actually works with information, spotting patterns, understanding language, generating stuff, making a call based on context.

So:

Automation does what it’s told.

AI figures out what to do with what it’s given.

Both are useful. But before you reach for either, ask yourself what you’re actually trying to fix.

Doing the same manual task over and over? You probably just need automation.

Drowning in information and need help making sense of it? Now you’re talking about AI.
