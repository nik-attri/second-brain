🔐 Security has a cost. But insecurity costs more. 💸

Every organization pays a "cost of security" — the time, tools, and people spent protecting the business. But the real budget-killer isn't the money you spend on security... it's the risk you don't see. 👀

And some of the biggest blind spots are risky accounts:
• 👻 Orphaned accounts from employees who left
• 🔓 Over-privileged users with access they don't need
• 😴 Dormant accounts nobody is watching
• 🕵️ Shadow and unmanaged identities

Each one is a quiet, open door — and attackers love quiet doors. One forgotten account can turn into a breach that costs millions in downtime, fines, and lost trust. ⚠️

This is exactly why a-count.io delivers such high value. 🚀
Instead of drowning security teams in noise, a-count continuously surfaces and prioritizes the risky accounts that actually matter — so you can act before they become incidents. ✅

The result? 👇
📉 Less exposure
⏱️ Less manual effort
💰 A lower true cost of security
Security shouldn't be about spending more. It should be about spending smart — closing the doors that matter most. 🔒
