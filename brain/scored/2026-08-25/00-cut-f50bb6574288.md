Just designed and built the landing page for Duevero — an invoicing & profit-tracking tool made for freelancers, contractors, and service businesses.
 
The core problem it solves: most freelancers know what they're earning, but not what they're actually keeping. Duevero brings estimates, deposits, invoices, expenses, and real profit tracking into one simple dashboard — no spreadsheets, no complicated accounting software.
 
For this landing page, the focus was on:
 ✅ A clear, benefit-driven headline — "From First Estimate to Final Profit, Now You'll Always Know"
 ✅ A frictionless CTA — create your first invoice in under 60 seconds, no credit card needed
 ✅ A live product preview that builds instant trust before signup
 
Research + structuring (via Google Docs) helped shape messaging that speaks directly to the target user — solo freelancers and small service businesses drowning in manual tracking.
 
Landing pages aren't just about design — they're about translating a product's value into 5 seconds of clarity for a stranger.
