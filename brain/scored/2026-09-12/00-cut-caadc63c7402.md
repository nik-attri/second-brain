We make a decent amount of money.
We pay our bills. We work hard. We’re not constantly buying expensive things.
But somehow, at the end of the month, we look at our bank account and think:
“Where the hell did all our money go?”
Maybe you can relate.
A few dinners out.
A couple Amazon orders.
Subscriptions.
Gas. Groceries.
Random $10, $20, $50 purchases.
None of them feel like a big deal individually… but somehow they add up to **hundreds or even thousands of dollars** every month.
And the frustrating part?
You don't realize it until the money is already gone.
That’s exactly why I built this financial tracker app.
I wanted a simple way to track **where my money, time, habits, and everyday life were actually going**—before the month was over.
Not another complicated spreadsheet.
Not another app that makes you feel guilty about spending.
Just a simple way to **see your life clearly, understand your patterns, and make better decisions.**
Because sometimes the problem isn't that we don't make enough money.
**We just don't see where it's going.**
And that's what I'm trying to change with this app
