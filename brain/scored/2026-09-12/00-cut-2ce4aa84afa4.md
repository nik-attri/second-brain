No AI was used in this post production 😅

Founder by accident? 🤣 

Landing our first major client at OmniSource Solutions recently brought back a very specific feeling. It was the exact same rush I felt in the summer of 2020. Building Kriol Animation Studio ignited a founder instinct in me. I was intuitively executing advanced business frameworks before I even knew their formal names.

Cape Verdean Creole cartoons did not exist. Total white space. A textbook Blue Ocean strategy. I had no budget. I built the first Escola Kriolu pilot manually to test the waters with Synfig (thank you Synfig) that was free to use.

The execution relied strictly on Lean Startup principles. To bypass a non-existent marketing budget, I utilized Growth Hacking. I negotiated a strategic B2B barter agreement with a massive cultural page (@Do you papia...kriolu?), trading my digital marketing services for their audience reach.

To secure instant brand authority, I integrated a recognized subject matter expert in education. My mother. A teacher called Cecy. The show name became "Escola Kriolu com a Professora Cecy" (Creole School with Teacher Cecy).

It exploded. We hit 5000 followers instantly. That early traction gave me the exact leverage needed to secure a national broadcast deal with RTC Radiotelevisão Cabo-verdiana. Get the contract first. Figure out the rest later.
To guarantee absolute product-market fit across the country, I hired voice actors in every regional dialect. Total cultural saturation.

Thank you RTC Radiotelevisão Cabo-verdiana for my first freelance contract and for making this happen! I grew up watching cartoons on RTC and I got to see my own on there 😍
