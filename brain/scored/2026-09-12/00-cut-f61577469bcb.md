Just wrapped up an intense build day at the Agents, Everywhere hackathon hosted by @aitinkerers! The challenge was to build AI agents that integrate seamlessly into existing workflows, and our team, 'Army of Interns,' delivered exactly that with our project, 'AI Intern Team Automates Grunt Work.'
We focused on creating a suite of AI agents designed to tackle repetitive administrative tasks, freeing up human colleagues for more strategic work. Our agents can now handle tasks like scheduling, data entry, and preliminary research directly within common workplace tools. It was a tight deadline, but we managed to get a working prototype demonstrating end-to-end functionality.
Huge thanks to my teammates for their incredible collaboration and hard work. This project wouldn't have been possible without their dedication.
We're excited about the potential of agents showing up where the work actually happens. This hackathon has been a fantastic experience, pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AI integration.
Shout out to our global sponsors: OpenAI, CopilotKit, and OpenRouter, as well as our developer infrastructure partners like Exa and Trigger.dev. And a big thank you to the judges for their time and expertise!
