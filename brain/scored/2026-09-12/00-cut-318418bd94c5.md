A few weeks ago I got annoyed by something very small. Which, as it turns out, is how some projects begin.

Parents get information everywhere: a message from school, WhatsApp from a coach, an email, a screenshot, a photo of a note.

And then we do something slightly ridiculous. We manually copy all of that into a calendar.

Date.
Time.
Place.
Details.

The information is already digital. We just move it from one digital place to another.

So I thought: this is exactly the kind of boring work AI should do for us. I opened Figma and designed a simple app. Then I got slightly carried away.

A couple of days later it was already running on my Android phone. And now Tuttofatto is live on Google Play!

You give it a screenshot or some text. AI reads it, finds the important information and prepares the event for you.

You check it.
Edit something if needed.
Done.

There is more to the app, of course, but that was the original idea:

less typing,
less copying,
less stupid admin work.

I am still not a mobile developer. I am a designer who apparently now has an app on Google Play.

That part still feels a little strange.

Android is live.
iOS will come next.

For now, if you want to try it, search for:

Tuttofatto AI

Link: https://lnkd.in/esxZCv6W

And now comes the interesting part.

Building it was one problem.

Getting people to know it exists is another.
