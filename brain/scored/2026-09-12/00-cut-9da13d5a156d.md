Product Owners don't need more hours.

They need less busywork.

Most POs are drowning in Jira busywork. Rovo fixes that.

You didn't become a Product Owner to write tickets all day. But that's where the hours go — formatting stories, chasing updates, digging through comment threads.

Here's how Rovo changes that:

→ Notes to tickets, instantly. Paste a rough PRD or meeting notes. Rovo turns them into epics and stories in Jira. No manual typing.
→ Ask, don't search. "Show me high-priority bugs in Marketing" — Rovo writes the JQL for you.
→ Skip the scroll. Ask for a ticket summary instead of reading 50 comments before a stakeholder call.
→ See risks early. Rovo pulls status across Jira and Confluence, so you're not compiling updates by hand.

One catch: Rovo writes acceptance criteria as plain text — nothing enforces it at execution. Pair it with a checklist habit so criteria don't quietly get skipped.

The real win isn't the AI. It's the time back.

Less formatting. More product thinking.
If you're a PO still doing this all manually
 — this is worth 30 minutes this week.
