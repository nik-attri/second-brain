You know what a real CTR evaluation looks like. Multiple vendors, hundreds of line items, different currencies and units, someone manually normalizing it all into one master file while everyone argues about who's actually cheaper.

We just watched our new CTR Evaluation module do that in real time on a live project. Comparisons that would have taken a team two to three weeks to reconcile, normalized and scored in minutes.

That's the shift. CTR evaluation stops being a spreadsheet exercise and becomes a decision-support tool that catches what fatigue and time pressure usually let slip through.
