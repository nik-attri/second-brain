🛎️When a new global cyberattack, campaign, or critical vulnerability appears, there are 3 types of security teams:

🔴 The Manual Investigator

“Are we impacted?”

Check EDR → Firewall → Identity → Cloud → SaaS → collect IOCs → correlate evidence manually.

👉🏻Answer: 24+ hours or days later.

🟡 The SIEM Operator

Everything is centralized, but threat hunting still requires queries, scripts, manual analysis, and thousands of alerts.

GenAI? Additional token cost.
Historical hunt? Only 3 months searchable—older data must be retrieved from archive.

👉🏻Answer: hours later.

🟢 The Threat-Informed SecOps Operator

Threat Intelligence + SIEM + 12-month searchable telemetry + Retroactive Threat Hunting + Embedded GenAI.

🌝Simply ask:

📕”Are we impacted?”
📗”Has this attacker touched us in the last 6–12 months?”
📘”Do we have detections covering this threat?”

Then correlate the evidence, identify gaps, and act.

👉🏻Answer: potentially minutes, not hours or days.

The future of SecOps isn't just collecting more logs.

It's being able to 🔑turn emerging threats into answers and action fast 🔑


Which type is your security operation today: 1, 2, or 3?😀
