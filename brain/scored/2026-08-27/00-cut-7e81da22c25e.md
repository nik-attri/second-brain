Private markets are not all that different from a cinema hall in India. 

Everyone obsesses over the Entry, but completely ignores the Exit.

The Entry: Pristine and welcoming. Pitch decks, press releases, high fives, and shiny valuation headlines.

The Exit: A crappy, unkept passage behind the screens. Spreadsheet hell, legal back and forth, manual approvals, and secondary market friction.

Joining the cap table gets VIP treatment, while the infrastructure required to actually return capital feels like an afterthought.

When it is time to process real liquidity, whether through secondary transactions, ESOP buybacks, or share transfers, investors and employees should not have to stumble through a dark back alley to get out.

Bringing capital in is only half the job. 

Building a transparent, compliant, and frictionless way to realize that capital completes the cycle.

At incentiv, we build the private market infrastructure that turns the messy passage into an effortless exit. 

True value is not just created when you issue shares, it is realized when you clear the path to liquidity.
