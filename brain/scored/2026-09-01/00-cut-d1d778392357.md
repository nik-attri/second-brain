HR Bash 2026: An evening well spent. 💛 
Thank you to Talent Crunch Berlin (TC) and The Secret HR Society for organising such a well-run event. The evening brought together thoughtful discussions, diverse perspectives, and plenty of ideas to reflect on.💡 

Here are my learnings from the sessions: 📒 
🔸 Screening a Saturated Funnel Without Breaking the EU AI Act: Build a better process, not just a faster one.
With AI increasing application volumes and making CVs look increasingly similar, the focus should be on designing a process that helps the right candidates stand out.
One idea that particularly resonated with me was translating requirements into the problem → expected outcome → evidence, rather than relying on generic job descriptions. A meaningful recruitment process starts with a relevant funnel.
🔸 Comp Confessions: A valuable reminder was that hiring managers may not always have complete visibility into compensation budgets. This highlights the importance of transparency and alignment between HR, hiring managers, and candidates throughout the hiring process.
🔸 AI in HR Operations: An important reminder was to start with the work, not the technology. AI can take care of repetitive tasks, while humans remain involved where judgment and context matter. There was an example with payroll that showed how AI could support checking changes, explanations, and approvals, while trust still depends on good data and reliable processes.
🔸 Human Relevance in the Age of AI: This session challenged a different question: if technology gives us back time, what are we going to do with it?
For me, that means moving HR closer to business decisions, measuring our impact in numbers, and creating more space for the human side of our work.
What I loved about the evening was that it was not just about “AI will transform HR.” It was more about where we should use it, where we should not, and what HR should become because of it.
