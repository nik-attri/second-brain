If you are already registered for the 38th GMP Symposium in Prague (Oct 5th–7th, 2026), don't forget to take advantage of our free satellite workshops!

How to build a PBPK model for a large molecule in PK-Sim.
Implementing Target-Mediated Drug Disposition (TMDD) in MoBi.
Creating flexible, scalable, and reusable model structures that support efficient model extension and PBPK-QSP integration.

Topic: An Introduction to Fast and Reliable Modeling with RsNLME and Agentic AI

Core concepts and workflows of population PK/PD modeling with RsNLME.
How RsNLME integrates with the R ecosystem for flexible, reproducible analyses.
How Agentic AI can automate and accelerate your modeling workflows using RsNLME’s MCP server.
