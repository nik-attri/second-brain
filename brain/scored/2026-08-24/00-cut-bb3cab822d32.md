A property management company was drowning in maintenance call chaos.

Tenants called in emergency repairs, leaving detailed voicemails. An administrator listened to audio files, typed work orders into a central spreadsheet, and texted local plumbers and electricians to assign jobs.

Calls were missed, assignment rules were violated, and tenants were left waiting.

We did not replace their property database.

We built a simple online tenant intake form that routes requests automatically.

Tenants submit issues and photos directly from their phone. The lightweight routing tool evaluates the location and repair category, then alerts the correct local contractor instantly.

Work order processing time fell by 80% with zero manual dispatching needed.
