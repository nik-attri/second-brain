A lot of women run their businesses in their heads (hell most people do!) but we cannot do it if we want a long and prosperous business. 

We especially can’t run our financials in our heads, but there’s often shame and frustration when looking at the numbers (or even the thought of looking). 

Here’s my tips: 

Look at it from a CEO perspective rather than a personal one. 

Knowing our finances, even if disappointing, frustrating and full of shame, is like opening the light on a Vampire. It gets quieter, you know your business situation better and you can make informed decisions.

Flip it on its head. Ignoring or avoiding means we make personal fictional decisions rather than a CEO perspective. The numbers don't mean anything about you as a person.

A simple in and out spreadsheet doesn't have to take time. And make it a regular date with yourself once a week
