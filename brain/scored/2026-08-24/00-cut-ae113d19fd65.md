Delete one task. Which one?

Forget another productivity hack for a minute.

Look at your workweek and find the task you repeat because “that is how we have always done it.”

The report you rebuild every Friday.
The follow up you send by hand after every inquiry.
The client information you copy from one system into another.
The status update your team keeps asking for because they cannot see it anywhere else.

Now ask a better question.

Why does a person still need to do this?

Some repetitive work needs human judgment. A lot of it needs a better process, clearer data flow, or the right automation.

Removing one recurring 20 minute task may not sound significant. If five people do it every week, you are spending more than 80 hours a year on that single activity.

Before asking your team to work faster, find something they should stop doing manually.

Your turn. What task would you delete from your workweek?
