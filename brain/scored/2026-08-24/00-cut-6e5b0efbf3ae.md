That manual task your team does every day has a price tag. Most businesses never calculate it.

Every business tracks salaries, rent, and software costs. Few ever calculate the cost of repetitive manual work happening inside the day-to-day.

Manually entering customer data. Updating CRM records. Following up with leads one by one. Sending appointment reminders. Preparing the same reports every week. Copying information between systems.
None of this shows up as a line item. All of it has a cost.

Here's a hypothetical example:
5 employees spend 1 hour a day on a repetitive task.
5 × 1 hour × 22 working days = 110 hours per month.
At an effective cost of $15/hour:
110 × $15 = $1,650 per month.
That's nearly $20,000 a year — spent on one repetitive activity most businesses never actually measured.

The right question isn't "Can AI do this?"
It's "How much is this process currently costing us?"
Once that number is clear, deciding whether automation makes financial sense becomes simple.
