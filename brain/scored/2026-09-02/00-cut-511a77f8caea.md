🖤 The Black Sheep Recruiter

Everyone sees the sheep.

The recruiters sending the same copy-and-paste messages.
The recruiters who care about filling a job more than finding the right job for you.
The recruiters who disappear once the contract is signed.

And then there’s me.

I’m the black sheep. 🖤

I don’t want to be another recruiter in your inbox.

I want to be the recruiter you remember.

Think about the lawyer representing someone when the entire world has already decided what they think. A good advocate doesn’t just say, “Here’s the case. Good luck.”

They learn the details.
They know the person.
They fight for their client.
They communicate.
They negotiate.
And when things get complicated, they stay.

That’s how I approach recruiting.

When you work with me, I want to know more than your discipline and the states you’re licensed in.

I want to know what you actually want.

Where you want to go.
What you’re willing to compromise on.
What you absolutely won’t compromise on.
What makes a contract worth leaving home for.
What makes you say hell yes—and what makes you say absolutely not.

Because you’re not a number on a spreadsheet.

You’re trusting me with 13+ weeks of your life.

And I don’t take that lightly.

Will I always get you the job you want? No.

Will I always win the negotiation? No.

Will every facility say yes? Definitely not.

But I can promise you this:

I will show up. I will communicate. I will tell you the truth. I will fight for you when I can. And I will never stop looking for the right opportunity just because the first one didn’t work out.

There are thousands of recruiters.

Most are just copies.

Be the black sheep. Find the recruiter who is willing to be one too. 🖤🐑

I’m Caycee with TGL Medstaff — and if you’re going to have someone representing you, make sure they actually represent YOU.
