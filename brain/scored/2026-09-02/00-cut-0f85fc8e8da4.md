I sold to 50+ businesses across the world before I could legally drink.

No sales team. No co-founder. No inherited leads.

Just me, a laptop, and a lot of cold messages that got ignored 
before they got answered.

Here's how it actually started:

I noticed small businesses were drowning in manual, repetitive work — 
the kind that doesn't need a person, just a system. So I built BRUNCHFY, 
an AI automation service for SMBs, and went and found out if anyone 
would actually pay for it.

The first 2 weeks were brutal, a founder ghosted me after 2 messages. I felt demotivated. I almost stopped.But I didn’t give up.

By the end, I’d worked with 50+ clients across India, the US, and the UK helping them cut manual work and recover time 
they didn't know they were losing.

No one taught me how to cold outreach. No one taught me how to 
handle a founder saying "we don't have budget for this."

I just kept showing up until the no's turned into yes's.

Still early in figuring out what's next — but building this taught me 
more about sales, resilience, and ownership than anything else has so far.
