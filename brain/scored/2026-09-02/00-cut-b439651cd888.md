“We scan our documents. Isn’t that enough?”

It certainly helps.
But scanning a document and automating the process around it are two very different things.

You can scan an invoice.
But someone may still have to:

→ read it
→ capture the information
→ find the purchase order
→ check the details
→ send it for approval
→ chase the approver
→ update the system
→ file the supporting documents
→ remember what still needs to be done

The document is now electronic.
But the process can still be completely manual.
And this is where DocuWare can make a significant difference.

DocuWare can capture information as it enters the business, organise it, route it to the right person, manage approvals, connect related information and trigger the next step in the process.

So instead of simply asking:
“Where do we store this document?”

you can start asking:
“What should happen to this information?”

And that applies far beyond Finance.

Invoices, purchase orders and supplier documents.
Customer and sales documents.
Employee records and HR processes.
Quality documentation and approvals.
Operational forms and workflows.
The document is only the beginning.

The real opportunity is to automate what happens after the document arrives.
