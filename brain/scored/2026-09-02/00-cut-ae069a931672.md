There is only one question that matters after an AI launch:

What got better?

Ask it before you build anything.

If the answer is “we are now using AI”, you do not yet have a measure of success.

For the intake workflow, the target was clear: reduce the time spent manually preparing submissions, while maintaining a useful and consistent starting point for review.

That made the outcome measurable. Manual handling in that part of the process fell by more than 30%.

Not every project needs a perfect dashboard or a 20-page business case.

But every project needs a definition of better.

Otherwise, you are left with a live tool and an awkward conversation about whether it was worth it.
