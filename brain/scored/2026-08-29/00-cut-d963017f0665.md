If your team is drowning in repetitive tasks, it’s not a capacity problem. It’s a workflow problem.

Here are 5 signs your business needs workflow automation:

1. You’re living in spreadsheets
If half your day is updating sheets, chasing versions, and fixing formula errors, that’s hidden payroll cost.

2. You’re copy pasting the same info everywhere
Customer details, order data, reports. If people re type the same data into 3 different tools, you’re paying for the same job 3 times.

3. Approvals slow everything down
Quotes, purchases, refunds. If work stalls because someone’s “out of office” or missed an email, your process isn’t built for speed.

4. Customers spot mistakes before you do
Manual steps invite errors. If you fix issues only after a client points them out, you’re risking trust and repeat business.

5. Your team works late just to stay “caught up”
When smart people spend evenings on admin instead of improving the business, you’re burning money and morale.
