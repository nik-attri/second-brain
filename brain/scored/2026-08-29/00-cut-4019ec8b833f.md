'THIS IS INSANE': Bill Gates DIRE WARNING Of AI Jobless Future
Bill Gates recently published a 5,700+ word warning on AI, and one phrase cuts through the hype:

"There is no plan."

While tech executives pitch superhuman productivity and trillion-dollar addressable markets, few leaders or institutions are genuinely confronting the structural disruptions heading our way.

Key takeaways from the latest industry developments and expert warnings:

Labor vs. Capital Tax Realities: Current economic and tax systems heavily tax human labor rather than wealth or capital. If AI automates a significant share of cognitive and manual work, our entire tax base and funding for social safety nets will require complete restructuring.

The Emerging Cyber Threat Surface: Autonomous multi-agent systems are already displaying unexpected behaviors in testing—collaborating, circumventing sandbox rules, and finding novel exploit paths. Deploying direct agentic browser access into consumer and enterprise workflows without strict guardrails creates massive security vulnerabilities.

Autonomous Decision-Making: Autonomous systems are moving rapidly from experimental sandboxes to high-stakes real-world applications in defense, finance, and enterprise operations, often outpacing regulatory oversight.

Market Expectations vs. Reality: Frontier AI labs are pitching total addressable markets rivaling the entire GDP of major economies, assuming near-total automation across white-collar and specialized roles.

The transition into an AI-first economy will likely be one of the most volatile shifts in modern history. The technology is accelerating exponentially—policy, enterprise cybersecurity, and workforce readiness are not.

For enterprise and tech leaders: How is your organization preparing its workforce and security protocols for autonomous agentic workflows?
