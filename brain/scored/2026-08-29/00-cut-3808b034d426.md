EN
Electricians in the field are asking:
“Do we enter the CT value manually, or does the device set it automatically?”

The answer is clear: On Aha Teknoloji devices, the CT value is entered MANUALLY. ✍️

Why is this critical?
Incorrect CT ratio = incorrect measurement = incorrect compensation.
Proper system operation starts with the correct CT input.

How do you find the CT value?
Check the label on the current transformer. It will list ratios such as 100/5A, 200/5A, or 400/5A. Simply enter this ratio into the corresponding field in the device menu. 🔧

If you have any questions about installation or setup, please contact us.
