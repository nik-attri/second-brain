Cashfree recently launched an AI Agent called Relay, and I think the bigger story here is not really about payments. It’s about what AI is starting to do inside business operations.

Relay is designed to help SMBs handle payment-related back-office work, including failed payment follow-ups, COD confirmations, subscription renewals, payment recovery and certain payment disputes.

According to Cashfree, 7 out of 10 SMBs still manage payment operations manually, spending up to 60 hours per week on these processes.

Payment platforms like this may not be as common in our market. In Malaysia, many SMEs, especially trading companies, still handle much of this internally. Customer orders, invoicing, payment checking, bank reconciliation, outstanding balances and payment follow-ups are often managed manually by Admin or Accounts teams.

That’s why what caught my attention wasn’t the payment solution itself.

It’s the fact that AI is starting to take over parts of these repetitive back-office workflows.

The same idea can be applied to many of the processes we see in SMEs today.

A customer sends a PO by email. Someone opens the PDF, checks the product, quantity and pricing, then keys everything into the system. A payment comes in, someone checks the bank statement, identifies the customer and matches it against the correct invoice. At the end of the month, the team pulls an outstanding list and starts following up with customers one by one.

On the supplier side, there are quotations, POs, DOs and invoices moving back and forth as well. None of these tasks are particularly complicated. But they are repetitive, time-consuming and prone to human error.

AI changes part of that equation because it can understand documents, extract the relevant information and pass structured data into existing systems or workflows.
And for SMEs, I don’t think the goal needs to be 100% automation.
