🤖 AI is changing finance forever. And honestly? It's about time.
I see so many young professionals spending all their time drowning in spreadsheets and manual data.

✨ But I believe the game has completely changed.
With AI now doing the heavy lifting and crunching numbers in seconds, being a human calculator is no longer enough. The real value is shifting to crucial human skills like:
Clear communication to explain complex numbers simply 🗣️
Strategic thinking and problem-solving 🧠
Building trust and strong working relationships 🤝
Here is what I really think about how we get there:

Senior leaders and managers already have these skills mastered. But how can a junior professional learn them if they start their career entirely remote, hidden behind a screen? You can't learn empathy, executive presence, or leadership through a Zoom chat.
I am definitely not saying we should force everyone back to the office full-time. Hybrid work is great. But we need a focused, smart hybrid approach—one where young talent gets the physical face-to-face time, mentorship, and guidance they desperately need to develop these next-gen human skills. 🚀
The future of finance doesn't belong to the machine. It belongs to the human who can lead. 💡

What do you think? How is your team balancing remote work with real human connection?
