Is your finance team busy, or just busy with admin?
5 signs a team is drowning in manual work:
Copy-paste between spreadsheets
The same report, rebuilt every month
Month-end depends on one person
Errors found late, sometimes at audit
No time left for analysis
The good news: every one of these is fixable. Start with the task that repeats most often, document it, then automate the steps that don't need judgement.
