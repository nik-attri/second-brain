A SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) platform is the backbone of most Security Operations Centres.
 
Done well, it lets analysts focus on critical alerts instead of drowning in noise. Done poorly, it becomes an expensive log bucket. Knowing the platforms and the logs that feed them is a core skill for anyone on the blue team.
