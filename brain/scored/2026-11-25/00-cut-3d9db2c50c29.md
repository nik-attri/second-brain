Planning your own wedding when you're an event planner is a very particular kind of pressure.

Because it's not enough to organise a wedding. I also need to organise the most epic wedding anyone has ever been to.
Otherwise won’t everyone assume I'm not very good at my job?!

I'm the event planner, the project manager, the client, the bride and, depending on the day, probably my own worst stakeholder.

We recently had a walkthrough at our venue with our incredible team of suppliers. And whilst I was completely caught up in the excitement of envisioning our special day, I was also leading the meeting, running through logistics, guest experience, timings and 300 things that still need doing.

Guy, meanwhile, keeps reminding me of one very important detail:

"It's our wedding day too, you know."

And whilst he is correct, I've tried explaining that this is a rather unique experience that no one other than an event planner will fully understand, as there is no equivalent. 

He'll never have to showcase his medical skills in front of all his family and friends, with his industry network also watching to see whether he's actually any good at his job.

I, on the other hand, am essentially inviting 200+ people to a live demonstration of mine. 

There is something quite fascinating about being both the person responsible for creating the experience and the person who really wants to enjoy and live it, when you are far too aware of how many things can go wrong, and how much effort goes into making it look effortless. 

I am incredibly grateful to have a team of suppliers who I trust so much to the point of allowing myself to put the clipboard and spreadsheets down. 

If you’re also an event planner bride - don’t forget to enjoy it all and don’t lose sight of why this is all happening in the first place 🩷
