Are you still doing your SOA manually?
If you watched our last video, thank you. We talked about manual work, scattered records, and chasing payments.
So here is the follow-up question:
Are you still struggling with the same thing?
Are you still using spreadsheets to send your billing?
Do you still have no clear pipeline for what is owed, what is overdue, and what has already been paid?
If yes, this is for you.
Let us take one part: your Statement of Account.
For many teams, an SOA still takes days. Copy from one file to another, check again, fix a mistake, send it late. Then the customer questions the total, and your team starts over.
An automated SOA does not promise magic.
What it can do is pull together the records you already have - sales, payments, and rebates - into one clear statement, generate it in a consistent format, and send it on schedule by email, with a history you can review.
Your accountant still reviews it. Your team still controls it.
It will not fix every collection problem, and it will not work if your underlying records are incomplete. But if your process is ready, it can save time and reduce confusion.
