Two critical security flaws in PHP went public this week. What do they mean for business owners and their websites?

If your business website runs on WordPress, it runs on PHP. So do many other website builders and sites built by agencies.

What's the issue about it? Layman - it lets a stranger take over the server behind your website; no password needed.

This flaw have been there for years and nobody found them. AI exposed it fast and furious. And now, everyone is scrambling to patch their systems. Unless your host does it for you, the fix doesn't install itself. Someone has to do it manually.

I checked my servers and the AI services we run for clients with one question. 5 seconds later, I knew we were not affected. Being AI-native does help here :)
