The most common worry I hear about offshore teams: "I won't know who is actually doing the work."

With Zouma, you will.

Every person in a dedicated team is named. You see their CV, you interview them, and you decide who joins. They work in your tools, follow your processes, and take direction from your managers.

We handle everything around them: hiring, workspace, IT, security, HR, supervision and cover when someone is on leave.

Your team. Our operation behind it.
