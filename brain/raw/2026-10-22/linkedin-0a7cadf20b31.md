This is some of the stuff I love, love, love to do: hob nob at conferences. It was great to be at the AISWMD annual retreat!

Was a bit shocked at the level of interest in Oliver this year.

Oliver was a dream that started here in the county we live in, Porter County, and our own recycling district. Being able to track pickups of bulbs, batteries, smoke detectors around the county; help run events; let contractors register for access to compose sites; let your master recyclers enter and track their hours.

It's very specific to recycling and solid waste districts, but at its core it's a big cloud database to put data in one place and a simple app to enter and use that data in the field.

Their website allows purchases for events (like a sponsorship) and lets attendees/volunteers register online and have their data whisked right into Dataverse. No spreadsheet or email hell, just a simple to use system using Dataverse, Canvas apps and their Microsoft 365 platform.

That's our job in this field: provide simple solutions that work.
