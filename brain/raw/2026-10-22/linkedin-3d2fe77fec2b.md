# 🚀 𝗙𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗠𝗮𝘁𝗵𝘀 𝗣𝗗𝗙 𝘁𝗼 𝗗𝗶𝗴𝗶𝘁𝗮𝗹 𝗤𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗕𝗮𝗻𝗸: 𝗔𝗜 + 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗶𝗻 𝗘𝗱𝗧𝗲𝗰𝗵
Preparing educational content for digital learning platforms can involve hours of repetitive data entry. I worked on a workflow to simplify this process using **ChatGPT
The goal was simple: **Reduce manual typing and automate the journey from a Maths PDF to our Panda Question Bank.**
## 🔄 𝗢𝘂𝗿 𝗘𝗻𝗱-𝘁𝗼-𝗘𝗻𝗱 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗳𝗹𝗼𝘄
📄 **𝟭. 𝗦𝗼𝘂𝗿𝗰𝗲 𝗣𝗗𝗙**
Started with a Maths Olympiad question paper on Circles for Grades 9 and 10, containing questions, options, answers, and solutions.
🤖 **𝟮. 𝗖𝗵𝗮𝘁𝗚𝗣𝗧-𝗔𝘀𝘀𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴**
Used ChatGPT to help extract and structure the educational content into a standardized question-bank format.
📊 **𝟯. 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗲𝗱 𝗘𝘅𝗰𝗲𝗹 𝗤𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗕𝗮𝗻𝗸**
Organized the questions into a 14-column Excel template covering question details, options, answers, and analysis.
🔢 **𝟰. 𝗠𝗮𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗰𝗮𝗹 𝗘𝗾𝘂𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗛𝗮𝗻𝗱𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴**
One of the key challenges was preserving mathematical expressions such as fractions, square roots, powers, and angles.
To address this, I used a custom markup format:
• Fraction: `[[MATH:\frac{3}{4}]]`
• Square root: `[[MATH:\sqrt{13^2-12^2}]]`
Our JavaScript automation identifies these markers and processes mathematical expressions through Panda's formula editor.
⚙️ **𝟱. 𝗕𝗿𝗼𝘄𝘀𝗲𝗿 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻**
Used **JavaScript + Node.js + Playwright** to automate the question-entry workflow.
The script reads Excel data, fills question details, enters options and explanations, handles mathematical formulas, selects answers, and saves questions.
🎯 **𝟲. 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗥𝗲𝘀𝘂𝗹𝘁**
**PDF → ChatGPT → Excel → Playwright Automation → Panda Question Bank**
Instead of manually typing every question and mathematical expression into the platform, we can use a structured Excel file as the input for automated entry.
## 💡 Why This Matters
✅ Less repetitive manual typing
✅ Standardized question-bank structure
✅ Automated question-entry workflow
✅ Dedicated handling of mathematical expressions
✅ A reusable approach for educational content operations
## 🚀 Key Takeaway
Automation is not just about automating individual tasks. It is about connecting multiple steps into one workflow.
