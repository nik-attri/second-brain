In this session, we cut through the online panic and doom-mongering around layoffs to analyze the ground reality of AI employment in India using Quess Corp's 2026 AI Workforce Report. Rather than viewing AI as a job destroyer, we examine where hiring demand is actually expanding across Global Capability Centers (GCCs), IT services, and enterprises. We break down the real numbers across experience brackets—explaining why freshers face a tough 2% entry barrier while mid-level professionals (3–5 years) command nearly 50% of active postings. You will discover why tabular machine learning and traditional models remain industry staples alongside GenAI, how to target massive talent shortages in deployment and AI security, and how both technical and non-technical professionals can position themselves for high-growth roles like Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs).
