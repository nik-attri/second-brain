The old way of being a lazy marketer was way cooler.

You stole your best lines from customers.

You’d read reviews until an irritated buyer hammered out a sentence about what was happening in their life that nobody in your product meeting would have written.

And you’d think... “Shit, that’s the headline”

In 2026, a lot of product marketers ask AI to write everything before they’ve fed it a single sentence like that.

Of course, finding that line takes work.

You have to tell the difference between a funny quote and a buying situation your product can actually address.

A lot of tech marketing skips that part and gets very scientific about everything after it.

We map every feature to a benefit, then generate 12 headline variations, then test them, then pay to put the “winner” in front of buyers...

...but all 12 just rearrange the product details we fed AI.

Congratulations! You found the most efficient way to spend money on copy nobody recognizes themselves in.

The LLM didn’t screw up. It’s a LANGUAGE model that LOVES language.

Give it the language customers use when they’re frustrated.

Then give it the situation around those words and your judgment about why they matter.

I use AI to organize huge piles of VOC. Then I read the raw language myself and look for the weird line that makes me stop.

Sometimes the customer practically hands me the copy. And sometimes they give me a clue I have to follow.

After that? Hell yes, use AI to move faster.

But you don’t get to be lazy at the beginning.

You gotta pay your dues in the raw VOC first.

And judging by a lot of the messaging I see…damn, a lot of y’all are trying to skip the dues.
