As the industry embraces agentic AI in operations, the big question isn’t can we automate, but can we trust the outcome?

In a recent APB+ feature on automating agentic AI without industry-wide guardrails, Bibhas Samanta, Sales Head for India and MENA at Interra Systems, shared key insights on how we should approach autonomy in high-stakes workflows like QC and compliance:
🔹 Autonomy is a ladder, not a light switch—start with human review on automated results and scale up progressively. 
🔹 The operator's role shifts from manual fault-checking to tuning, trusting, and validating the QC engine. 
🔹 The final go/no-go must remain human—trust comes from explainable, production-proven systems with a clear decision trail, not a black box.
