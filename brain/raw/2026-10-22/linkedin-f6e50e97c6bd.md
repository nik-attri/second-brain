I quit my job to start an AI automation agency.

Three weeks in, reality hit.

No clients.
No case studies.
No portfolio.

Four courses I barely touched.

My first prospect asked for examples of past work.

I had nothing.

Just a LinkedIn post saying, “Just launched my AI automation agency!” and a whole lot of confidence I couldn't back up.

This was the part I wasn't seeing in all the success stories.
They showed the $10k months.

Not the 47 cold DMs that got ignored.
Not the panic when rent was due and the pipeline was empty.

𝗛𝗲𝗿𝗲'𝘀 𝘄𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗮𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗲𝗱...

I stopped trying to look like an agency.
I started building for free.

Found a local business owner drowning in manual work.
Built them one automation.

No charge.
Documented everything.
That became my first case study.

That case study got me my first paid client.
That client referred me to two more.

100+ production agents later, and a couple months after I first shared this story, I still believe the same thing:

The courses don't build your business.
The portfolio does.

And you build a portfolio by doing the work before anyone pays you to.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 part nobody tells you about 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗻 𝗔𝗜 𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗻𝗰𝘆...
→ My prospects weren't asking about my courses
→ They weren't asking about certifications
→ They wanted to know one thing: can you solve my problem?

Prove it once.
Document it.
Show it.
That's still the playbook.

If you're in the “this is fine” phase right now, you're not behind.

You're just at the part that doesn't make the highlight reel.

If I had to start over tomorrow, I'd do the same thing:
Find one real problem.

Solve it.
Document the proof.
Then earn the next opportunity.

Keep building.
