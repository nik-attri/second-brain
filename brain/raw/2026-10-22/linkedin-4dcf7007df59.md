BPO and ITES was among India's top growth sectors repeatedly through the year, at double digit rates in several months, including months when IT hiring was flat.

Our own crawl found the same category holding 133 of 230 classified entry level listings. That is 46 percent, against 8 in software engineering.

It is also the lowest paying function in our salary data, at a 3.5 LPA midpoint against 5.5 nationally. The gap against data and analytics is three lakh.

So the most accessible way into the Indian job market and the least well paid one are currently the same thing.

It also discloses pay least: 10.5 percent of listings publish a band, the lowest of seven functions. The people entering through the most open door get the least information about what they will earn.

One underrated advantage: reporting describes this expansion anchored in non-metro markets. It is one of the few functions where opportunity is not concentrated in five cities.
