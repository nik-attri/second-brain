Found a role 💪😱

It was with a company I’ve followed for years, whose product I use almost daily for investing and I’d have been proud to join the team. Reading the JD, my reaction was pretty much: yaaas, LFG.

I clicked apply, found they wanted a cover letter and smashed one out myself, without AI, before uploading my CV. Everything parsed correctly except my education, because obvs we need that degree from over 20 years ago captured properly 😇

After correcting that, I completed the usual RTW and DEI questions, then reached another 7 app questions. These covered my motivation, products I’ve built, handling difficult stakeholders with a specific example, plus my “relationship with AI” 🤝.

I had no issue answering because I use the product and had plenty to say. Hell, I even shared what I love about the platform and how the UX could improve from an active trader’s perspective with a detailed example on how we could apply this feature.

I submitted everything just after 10pm last night and at 2:36am the rejection arrived, complete with “after careful consideration” and an “overwhelming amount of applications”.

The email addressed me as “[Candidate Name]”,  after I’d taken the time to write a cover letter and 7 detailed answers specifically for them.

The Head of Product role had only been published that day, not reposted and an overwhelming response is absolutely possible. I don’t know how the decision was made, but “careful consideration” is pretty hard to swallow when they haven’t even replaced the name in their template.

It stings more because I’m also a customer and it’s left a super sour taste about the company and how my app was handled.

The more this continues, the more I think maybe it’s time to put that energy into starting my own business.

I don’t want AI making my CV mirror a job description where my experience doesn’t fit or sending apps for roles I haven’t properly considered. I read each role, decide whether I can do it and track every app in a spreadsheet, so if a recruiter calls, I know exactly what we’re discussing and why I applied, ya know, logical and all that.

I don’t want to add to a system I already think is broken just to follow the crowd. I’ll either keep going with that approach or put the same energy into working for myself and delivering a service people need.

Happy Wednesday job seekers!
