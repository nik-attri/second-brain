One evening: an idea, a working product and a public website.

I built Legal AI Tracker⁠ because I needed a better way to follow AI guidance, judgments and legislation relevant to legal practice. Once I started building this as a personal project, I decided that it could be a valuable public resource, so I decided to deploy it for free. Given how little time these projects take these days, commercialisation often seems unnecessarily greedy!

Behind it is a searchable database of sources, filters by role and task, detailed topic articles, email alerts and much more. AI supports the research and drafting; an editorial approval process keeps me responsible for what gets published. The update process is automated every morning, proposed updates are manually checked before they go live.

Getting a sophisticated app built and deployed online quickly is one thing. Building something useful (and designing a process for checking its accuracy) is what matters.

If you want to learn how to do this accurately and at speed using our methods, there’s one place we teach them: the LegalQuants AI residency.
