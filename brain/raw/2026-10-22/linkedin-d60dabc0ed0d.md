DO YOU KNOW WHAT MAKES A FIRE ALARM SYSTEM WORK?
A fire alarm system is more than just a control panel and a few smoke detectors. Each component has a specific role in detecting fire, alerting occupants and supporting a timely response.

1. Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP): Receives signals from detectors and manual call points, processes alarm conditions and activates programmed outputs.

2. Smoke Detectors: Detect smoke particles and send an alarm signal to the control panel.

3. Heat Detectors: Detect a specified temperature or rapid temperature rise, depending on the detector type.

4. Manual Call Points (MCPs): Allow occupants to manually trigger an alarm when they discover a fire.

5. Sounders / Hoothers: Provide an audible warning to alert occupants during an emergency.

6. Strobe Lights: Provide visual alarm notification, particularly useful where audible alarms may not be sufficient.

7. Fire Alarm Cables & Power Supply: Provide the necessary power and communication pathways between system components.

8. Input/Output Modules: Allow the fire alarm system to monitor or control connected equipment and other building systems.

9. Remote Annunciators / Repeater Panels: Provide additional locations where alarm and system information can be viewed.
