Last week, at a networking event, someone described preparing and cleaning Excel files before giving them to AI as "going back to the Stone Age."

I could see their point. When tools like ChatGPT and Claude can connect directly to business applications, manually exporting and preparing spreadsheets feels like a step backwards.

This week at Tallinn Data Week, I've been listening to discussions about AI agents, data platforms, semantic layers and data governance. And I keep thinking about what all this means for smaller businesses.

Let's say you run a small online store. You have WooCommerce, GA4, advertising platforms and perhaps an email marketing tool. You want to use AI to understand how your business is performing.

Getting an answer is becoming easier. But what data does AI actually need access to? Do you need to share customer details to analyse product sales? And are the numbers reliable in the first place?

Even a simple question like "How much did we sell last month?" isn't necessarily straightforward. Does the answer include VAT, shipping, refunds or cancelled orders? Unless those definitions are clear, you might get a perfectly calculated number that doesn't answer the question you intended to ask.

This connects closely with what I see in digital analytics. A technically working tracking setup doesn't necessarily mean the data is complete or suitable for every business question. And a convincing AI-generated dashboard doesn't mean the underlying calculations or assumptions are correct.

So what does a practical approach look like for a business without a dedicated data team?

I don't think every small business needs complex data infrastructure. But manually cleaning spreadsheets every time they want to analyse something doesn't seem sustainable either.

There should be a sensible middle ground that accounts for data quality, business definitions, privacy and security, without making the whole process unnecessarily complicated.

I'm curious how others are approaching this in practice. What are realistic, maintainable ways for smaller businesses to use AI with their own data while addressing these challenges?
