Heres the single most productive thing Ive ever done in my business?

I started putting myself under pressure.
Deliberately.

And now I do it every single day.

Whether Im updating spreadsheets, working on my website, creating content, finishing a project or spending time on LinkedIn, theres one thing I absolutely cannot work without.

A timer. ⏱️

Not just time blocking, where you allocate a couple of hours to a task and hope for the best.

Oh no, I want to see those seconds disappearing before my very eyes. 😂

Because heres what I discovered about myself.

Give me three hours to complete something and theres a good chance Ill take three hours.

Ill tweak things, fiddle about with something that probably doesnt need changing, get distracted by another brilliant idea and suddenly half the morning has disappeared.

And the frustrating thing is, Ive been busy the entire time!

Just not necessarily productive.So now I do things differently.I decide what needs doing, work out roughly how long it should take and then give myself an uncomfortable deadline.

And I mean uncomfortable.

The sort of deadline that makes you look at the clock and think, bloody hell, Id better get a move on!

Theres something about watching that timer count down that completely changes how I work.

Suddenly the unnecessary distractions disappear and I become ridiculously focused on getting the job finished.And the results have been amazing.

Tasks that used to drag on for hours are getting finished in a fraction of the time.

And here's why I think this matters when youre running a business.

Its incredibly easy to fill your day with work without actually moving your business forward.

You can spend hours tweaking your website, organising your CRM or scrolling through LinkedIn and convince yourself youve had a productive day.

But have you actually generated any new opportunities, followed up with potential clients or done something thats going to bring more business through the door?

Being busy and being productive are two very different things.For me, that little countdown clock has made a massive difference.

And who do I have to thank for this ridiculously simple but effective idea?

Alex Hormozi 

So thank you, Alex. Youve probably saved me hundreds of hours and upped my productivity no end. 

Anyway, Id love to tell you more, but my timer is ticking away and I have the next task waiting Tick tock.Id better get cracking! ⏱️😂
