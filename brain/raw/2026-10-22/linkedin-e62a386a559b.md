What an incredible and spectacular moment to sit amongst the top global and local thought leaders at #SingularitySummitSouthAfrica2026. Yesterday felt like stepping into a time machine. Attending the Singularity South Africa Summit 2026 reminded me of why it is so crucial to step out of the daily grind and look at the macro picture of human ingenuity. From deep dives into AI, advanced robotics to the mind-bending potential of longevity and metaverse innovation. Day 1 was a masterclass in exponential thinking, but one session completely reframed how I view our immediate future. Global expert David Roberts took to the stage with a piercing reality check: 

"Change How Leaders See: Why Everything You're Doing to Prepare for AI Will Not Be Enough."

His message was very clear. Most organisations are treating AI as a faster, sharper tool to optimise existing, linear workflows. But we aren’t facing an efficiency upgrade, we are facing a total systemic disruption. If you are merely trying to automate tasks, you are already falling behind. 

Day 1 reflection and biggest takeaways from David's session.

• We must shift from Task Management to True Ownership. AI demands that we cultivate leaders who can navigate deep uncertainty, not just manage checklists.
• The Intelligence Age requires a Mindset Shift: To leverage exponential technology effectively across Africa, leaders must completely change how they see the world, abandoning outdated benchmarks.
• Technology for Human Elevation: True exponential leadership means utilising these technological leaps to solve Global Grand Challenges and elevate human potential, rather than simply cutting costs. We are no longer preparing for a distant future, we are actively living it. 

Thank you Celiwe Ross and the teams at Old Mutual, Google Cloud, and Singularity South Africa for an incredibly provocative first day.
