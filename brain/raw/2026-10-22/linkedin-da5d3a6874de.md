We recently put a team of AI bots to work inside Like Media.

And something interesting happened.

The biggest change wasn't what the bots started doing. It was what our people no longer had to do.

No more hours copying and pasting data into spreadsheets. No more hunting down documents someone filed somewhere perfectly logical and promptly forgot about. No more juggling endless emails, Slack messages, calls, and texts just to keep things moving. No more burning evenings on first editorial passes.

None of that was ever the real job. It was the tax we paid to do the job.

Here's what surprised me. We're still the same company. Same talented, high-functioning, Type A personalities. Same standards. Same arguments about commas.
What changed was the ceiling.

What changed was the ceiling. The chains of administrative hell are finally coming off. The busywork that once consumed our days is being handed off to AI, freeing our people to do 
what they do best.

Create. Innovate. Build relationships. Sell. Think bigger.

And that's where I think a lot of businesses are missing the bigger picture. Everyone is talking about AI. Millions use ChatGPT, experiment with prompts, write emails, and discover clever shortcuts. That's great. But that's barely scratching the surface.

There's a massive difference between using AI and actually putting AI to work.

For many businesses, AI will become another subscription. They'll sign up, play with a few prompts, get excited about the possibilities, and eventually go right back to business as usual.
Not because AI failed. Because they never built a plan to make it succeed.

Here's what I've learned.
AI doesn't have to replace your business, your people, or the products you've spent years building. Quite the opposite. It can make everything you've built that much better.

At Like Media, we already have the talent, the products, the relationships, and the vision. We're not asking AI to reinvent who we are. We're using it to remove the obstacles that keep our people from being extraordinary at what they do.

Let technology handle the busywork. Let your people bring the brilliance.
That's the opportunity.

So if you're running a business and waiting for all of this to settle down, I understand the instinct. But I think it's a mistake.

Embrace it. Learn it. Challenge yourself to understand it. And most importantly, BUILD A PLAN AND ADOPT IT. Because learning without adopting is just reading. And AI without a plan is just another subscription.

A big shout-out to the team at BlackAtlas.ai. We couldn't have moved this quickly or effectively without their expertise, guidance, and ability to turn our vision into reality.

The future isn't something we're waiting for anymore. We're building it.
