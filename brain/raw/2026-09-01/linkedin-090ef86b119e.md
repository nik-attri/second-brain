4 questions to ask before switching practice management software:

 1️⃣ Where do your documents actually live? If the answer is "in our proprietary storage," you don't own your data — you’re just renting it. Your documents, emails, and client history belong in your firm's environment, not a vendor's walled garden. Ask them what happens to your files if you leave. If they hesitate, pack your bags.

2️⃣ How long does onboarding take, including migration? If a software vendor tells you to "manually re- your matters" or hands you an extra invoice for data migration, run. Migration isn’t a DIY weekend project, and it definitely isn't a premium add-on. Your history, contacts, and deadlines belong with you—included in the price, not held for ransom.

3️⃣ What happens when you need help? If the helpline needs a script to survive a customer conversation, it’s not a helpline, it’s a rehearsal. We all know the difference between a tool you can tolerate and an expert you can trust.

4️⃣ Is your software bill clear, or are you paying for features you'll never touch? Per-seat pricing with everything included is easy to budget. Tiered plans with "bolt-on" modules are just a trap designed to upsell you later. Know exactly what you're getting — and what it actually costs — before you sign.
