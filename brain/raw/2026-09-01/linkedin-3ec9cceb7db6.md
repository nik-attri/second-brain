🧠 ADHD UNFILTERED #6 — PAYDAY IS A LIE

It’s the 1st of the month.

PAYDAY.

For a brief, beautiful moment, your bank account looks healthy.

You feel rich.

You start mentally spending money you absolutely do not have. 😂

Then…

💸 Mortgage
💸 Council tax
💸 Electricity
💸 Insurance
💸 Phone
💸 Broadband
💸 Car
💸 Subscriptions
💸 Food
💸 Fuel

…and that’s just the bills that have gone out TODAY.

Then you look at the calendar.

There are STILL bills going out later this month.

And suddenly you’re sitting there thinking:

“How the actual hell am I supposed to make this last until payday?”

The particularly horrible part?

You’ve literally just been paid.

This is something I don’t think we talk about enough.

Financial stress isn’t always about being irresponsible.

Sometimes you’re working.

You’re earning.

You’re paying your bills.

You’re trying to do everything right.

And you’re STILL sitting there staring at your bank balance wondering how you’re going to get through the month.

Then there’s the added pressure of making sure your wife has enough money for the essentials.

Because suddenly every purchase gets analysed.

Do we need it?

Can it wait?

How much is left?

What’s coming out next?

What if something goes wrong?

And then throw ADHD into the mix.

🔥 Overthinking.

🔥 Impulsive spending.

🔥 Difficulty tracking multiple payments.

🔥 Time blindness.

🔥 Constant mental calculations.

🔥 Anxiety about unexpected expenses.

My brain doesn’t need 37 browser tabs open.

Apparently it needs 37 tabs AND an Excel spreadsheet trying to work out whether I can afford a loaf of bread.

😂

But this is where the judoka in me kicks in.

🥋 You don’t panic when you lose your balance.

You recover it.

You adapt.

You work with what you’ve got.

You don’t waste energy fighting something you can’t control.

You focus on the next movement.

The next decision.

The next problem.

And sometimes that’s what surviving financially feels like.

Not winning.

Not thriving.

Just staying upright.

Getting your family through the month.

Making the money stretch.

Taking the hits.

Adapting.

And getting back up again.

Because maybe resilience isn’t always some inspirational story about overcoming enormous obstacles.

Maybe sometimes resilience is simply looking at your bank account on the 1st of the month and saying:

“Right. Let’s figure this out.”

Then doing it again tomorrow.

And the day after.

And the day after that.

🥋 The fight isn’t over just because you’re tired.

💷 And payday doesn’t mean you’re financially comfortable.

Sometimes it just means you’ve got another 30 days to survive.

Be honest — does anyone else feel like payday is immediately followed by financial panic?

👇 I genuinely want to know how many people are quietly doing the exact same mental maths every month.
