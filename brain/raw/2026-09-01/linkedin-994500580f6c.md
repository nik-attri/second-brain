I spent 3 weeks building a tool that saves supply chain managers 20+ hours a week.
Here's the problem I saw:
❌ Warehouses overstocked → $500K+ tied up in excess inventory
❌ Empty shelves during peak season → Lost revenue
❌ Ops managers drowning in Excel → 20+ hours/week on manual planning
Sound familiar?
I've worked with supply chain teams who had ALL the data but couldn't use it effectively. So I built something to fix that.
Introducing: Demand Forecasting + Inventory Network Optimizer 
A complete system that predicts demand and optimizes inventory across 5 warehouses, 50 SKUs.
🔧 What I Built:
📊 Data Engineering Layer
PostgreSQL database with dbt star schema
3 years of synthetic retail data (334,750 records)
50 SKUs × 5 warehouses
🤖 Machine Learning
XGBoost demand forecasting (72% accuracy)
100+ models trained per SKU-Warehouse
30-day rolling forecasts
📈 Optimization Engine
Linear Programming (PuLP) for cost minimization
$593,452 cost optimization achieved
95% service level maintained
🎨 Forward Deployment
Streamlit dashboard with what-if analysis
Ops managers can adjust service levels in real-time
See cost impact instantly
💡 The Real Impact:
In just 5 seconds, an operations manager can now:
✅ Test demand spike scenarios
✅ Simulate supply disruptions
✅ Optimize safety stock levels
✅ Save millions in working capital
No more Excel crashes. No more guessing. No more fires.
🛠️ Skills I Used:
✅ Data Engineering: ETL pipelines, Star Schema, dbt
✅ Machine Learning: XGBoost, Feature Engineering
✅ Optimization: Linear Programming (PuLP)
✅ Forward Deployment: Streamlit, Interactive Dashboards
✅ Business Impact: Cost Reduction, Working Capital Optimization
🤔 Why I Built This:
I wanted to bridge the gap between complex ML models and the operations managers who need simple answers.
The result? A dashboard where anyone can make data-driven decisions in seconds.
