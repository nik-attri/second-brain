#InternshipOpportunity - PSR Administration Intern

PT SMART Tbk (Sinarmas Agribusiness and Food) is looking for a PSR Administration Intern, placement in Sinar Mas Land Plaza Thamrin, Central Jakarta.

Job Summary
You will support the PSR BPDP program through data administration, documentation, data verification, data entry, and monitoring of farmer and land information through Excel and the SARPRAS Online application.

Job Description
- Support the documentation of farmers and land participating in the SARPRAS program.
- Recap and maintain farmer and land data using Microsoft Excel.
- Verify the completeness and accuracy of farmer and land data.
- Input and update farmer and land data into the SARPRAS Online application.
- Assist in organizing and maintaining data and supporting documents.
- Support administrative activities to accelerate the implementation of the PSR BPDP program.

Job Requirements
- S1 student (with NO ACTIVE CLASSES) or fresh graduate from any related major.
- Previous experience using government applications or platforms is a plus.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel at an intermediate level.
- Comfortable handling large volumes of data entry.
- Detail-oriented, thorough, and accurate when working with data.
- Fast learner and adaptable to new systems and processes.
- Able to work independently and responsibly with administrative tasks.
- Available to work full-time from office (Monday–Friday, 08.30–17.30).
- Available to COMMIT to a minimum 6-MONTH INTERNSHIP.

If you are interested and meet the qualifications above, please send your CV (PDF FORMAT) to recruitment.smart@sinarmas-agri.com with subject:
PSR_Name_Major_University

Please also include this information in the body email:
- Earliest Join Date: If unable to join immediately, please state the reason.
- For current students, please describe any remaining academic commitments (e.g., classes, thesis, or supervision schedule, including frequency and online/offline format).
- Please mention if you have experience using PSR, SARPRAS, or other government applications/platforms.

Thank you!
