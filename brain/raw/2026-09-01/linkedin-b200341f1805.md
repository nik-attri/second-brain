Where is your Golden America now?

Is it in a stock market that people cannot eat?

Is it inside a house working families cannot afford?

Is it in a paycheck taxed before the worker ever touches it?

Is it inside a corporation that calls employees “family” before cutting them from a spreadsheet?

Is it buried beneath debt sold as opportunity, surveillance sold as convenience, and extraction sold as economic growth?

Is it sitting inside a rocket while children on Earth remain hungry, families remain broken, and powerful institutions still refuse complete accountability for the exploitation of the vulnerable?

Golden for whom?

The executive?

The institutional investor?

The government contractor?

The landlord collecting half a worker’s income?

Because it sure as hell does not feel golden to the people holding the entire system together.

A nation is not golden because its markets rise, its billionaires multiply, or its machines become more intelligent.

A nation becomes golden when a worker can afford a home.

When a full-time job provides an actual life.

When healthcare does not depend on obedience to an employer.

When taxes produce visible public value.

When children are protected more fiercely than reputations.

When justice does not check someone’s bank account before deciding how hard the law applies.

When technology returns time, dignity, ownership, and power to human beings.

You can wrap extraction in a flag.

You can paint exploitation gold.

You can call manufactured scarcity “freedom.”

It is still fucking extraction.

I am not attacking America.

I am refusing to accept a cheap imitation of it.

The Golden America is not some perfect past we can retreat into. It is not a slogan handed down by powerful people. It is not an escape hatch to another planet.

It is something we have not built yet.

That is why I am building Horizon Intelligence as a public benefit corporation.

Intelligence applied should strengthen workers, communities, and existing systems. It should regenerate what extraction has depleted. It should make power more visible and human beings more capable—not make exploitation faster.

That is the peaceful revolution available to every generation:

Not burning the country down.

Refusing to let its purpose be burned out of it.

So where is your Golden America now?

It is buried beneath layers of bullshit.

But I can see what is standing in its way.

And now, so can you.
