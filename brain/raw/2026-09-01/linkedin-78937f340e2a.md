Introducing DataAnalystAI — AI-powered financial intelligence for smarter decisions.
Finance and accounting teams still spend significant time preparing spreadsheets, reconciling transactions, investigating exceptions, and converting financial data into useful reports.
We’re building DataAnalystAI to make those workflows faster, more structured and auditable — while keeping human oversight at the center of important financial decisions.
DataAnalystAI brings together:
• Financial data preparation and analysis • Transaction reconciliation • Exception detection and human review • Financial reporting • Audit trails and transparent workflows • AI-assisted financial intelligence
Our objective is not simply to automate financial work. It is to help accountants and businesses spend less time on repetitive spreadsheet tasks and more time understanding what their financial data is telling them.
DataAnalystAI is developed by CircuitSphere.
We’re just getting started.
