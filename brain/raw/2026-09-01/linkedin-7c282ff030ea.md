I’ve spoken to many finance teams over the past few months, and the same issues keep coming up. Every team is being asked the same question by the business leaders.

When are we bringing in AI?

And almost every one of them is thinking the same thing back.

With what data?

The pressure from the top is real. Boards want automation, AI, faster numbers, and they want it now.

But one floor down, the finance team is still producing reports manually, or with many workarounds at least.

Reconciliations that never quite reconcile. A report that only one person knows how to run, and a quiet panic when they book time off. Spreadsheets held together with copy-and-paste and some good intentions.

These teams aren't resisting change. They simply can't get to it. It's hard to step back and rebuild the engine when you're flat out keeping the reporting on the road every single month.

You can't automate your way out of this.

AI sitting on top of messy data and broken processes doesn't clean the mess.

It just scales it.

The organisations that will actually get value from automation are the ones that fix the foundations first.

They need reliable data, processes that don't live in one person's head, producing reporting you can trust without checking it twice.

This is the work I do and why I’m launching Libiakova Advisory. This is the work I love to do, helping finance teams get their reporting foundations right so they're genuinely ready for what comes next.

If your team is stuck in that gap between what leadership wants and what the data can actually support, I'd like to hear about it.  Are you being encouraged to implement AI? Maybe you’ve already done it. Was it a success? Or do the same issues still exist?
