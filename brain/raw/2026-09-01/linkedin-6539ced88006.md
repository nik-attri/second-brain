A great day of technical enablement and partner engagement in Kolkata! 🚀
As the Product Technical Specialist for Red Hat, I had the opportunity to conduct a comprehensive technical session for our Kolkata partners, covering key Red Hat solutions and how they come together to build a modern IT environment.
The session covered:
🔹 Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) – exploring the enterprise Linux platform and the latest capabilities introduced with RHEL 10, designed to enhance security, performance, manageability, and support modern workloads.
🔹 OpenShift Virtualization – understanding how organizations can modernize their virtualization environment by running virtual machines alongside containers on the Red Hat OpenShift platform, helping create a more unified and flexible application platform.
🔹 Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform – exploring how automation can simplify IT operations and configuration management, and how Ansible can work together with OpenShift to automate and streamline application and infrastructure workflows.
It was a great opportunity to interact with our partners, exchange technical insights, understand their requirements, and demonstrate how different Red Hat technologies can work together as part of a modern IT strategy.
A special thanks to Anish for the excellent coordination and to Abhishek(Cluster Sales Manager), for arranging a strong set of partners and making this session possible.
Looking forward to many more such technical enablement sessions and collaborations! 🤝
