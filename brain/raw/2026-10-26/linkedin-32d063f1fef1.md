Building a startup is basically paying money to find out whether your own idea is stupid.


Except sometimes you are not paying money. You are paying with your time, sleep, savings, sanity and the last few functioning brain cells you had.

First comes the “what if this works?”
Then the “what if nobody buys it?”
Then the “what if I just get a job?”

And finally, the most dangerous question of all:
“What if I am actually onto something?”

No Shark Tank India fantasies. No investor flex. No LinkedIn post about disrupting an industry before acquiring a single customer.

Just an idea, a spreadsheet, a few uncomfortable questions and an ego slowly being taken out behind the shed.

Turning “what if?” into “watch this” sounds great.

Right now, I am still negotiating with “what the hell?” 😂
