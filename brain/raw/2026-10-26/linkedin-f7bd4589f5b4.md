We Are Hiring | IT Manager
Company: Shivani Locks Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Chakan, Pune
Experience: 10–15 Years
Department: Information Technology (IT)

Role Overview
We are looking for an experienced and result-oriented IT Manager to develop small-scale software applications, automate routine business processes, and provide ongoing support and enhancements for our existing Sofgen ERP system. The ideal candidate should have strong programming and problem-solving skills, along with a good understanding of business operations across multiple departments.
🔹 Key Responsibilities
- Design, develop, test, and deploy small-scale software applications to automate routine tasks and improve operational efficiency.
- Collaborate with Accounts, Quality, Production, Administration, and HR teams to understand business requirements and develop effective technical solutions.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and enhance the existing Sofgen ERP system to ensure smooth business operations.
- Identify opportunities for process automation, system integration, and workflow improvement.
- Ensure the reliability, accuracy, security, and user-friendliness of software applications.
- Provide technical support and resolve ERP-related issues within defined timelines.
- Prepare technical documentation, maintain program records, and provide training to end-users.
- Work independently and coordinate with cross-functional teams to implement IT solutions aligned with business needs.
🔹 Skills & Qualifications
- 10–15 years of relevant experience in IT, software development, ERP support, and system implementation.
- Proficiency in relevant programming languages, database management, and software development tools.
- Hands-on experience in ERP system support, customization, troubleshooting, and enhancement.
- Experience with Sofgen ERP will be an advantage.
- Good understanding of business processes in Accounts, Quality, Production, Administration, and HR.
- Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication and cross-functional coordination skills.
- Ability to work independently, manage priorities, and deliver solutions within deadlines.
