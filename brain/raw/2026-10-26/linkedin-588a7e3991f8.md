🚨 We're Hiring: Account Manager

🔹 Role Overview



We are looking for a results-driven Account Manager to take full ownership of our client relationships.



As the primary point of contact for assigned clients, you will manage the full account lifecycle — from relationship building and performance reviews to revenue tracking and service-quality assurance — while working closely with our Operations team to ensure seamless service delivery.



📌 Responsibilities



Serve as the single point of accountability for the full client relationship.



Conduct regular client reviews to assess satisfaction, performance, and upcoming needs.



Identify and pursue upselling and cross-selling opportunities within existing accounts.



Monitor account revenue against monthly targets and proactively flag risks.



Coordinate daily with the Operations team on service delivery and issue resolution.



Ensure client SLAs are consistently met, escalating where necessary.



Resolve client complaints promptly and professionally.



Prepare and submit weekly account health reports.



🎯 Requirements



Bachelor's degree.



Minimum 2 years of experience in account management, client success, or a commercial role.



experience in the logistics sector is required.



Good command of English, both written and verbal.



Excellent relationship-building and communication skills.



Solid financial acumen, with familiarity with revenue targets, collections, and basic P&L.



Good knowledge of CRM systems and Excel.
