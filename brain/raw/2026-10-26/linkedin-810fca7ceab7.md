Hiring: HR & Operations Executive
Grahnetra AI Labs is hiring an HR & Operations Executive to join our growing team. We are looking for an organized, proactive, and detail-oriented individual who can handle recruitment, HR administration, back-office operations, documentation, and day-to-day business activities.

📍 Location: Gurugram
💼 Experience: Fresher-1 Year
📚 Qualification: Any Graduate / Relevant Degree or Diploma in Human Resources, Business Administration, Management, or a related field
👤 Employment Type: Full-Time
🏢 Work Mode: Hybrid
📅 Work Schedule: 6 Days a Week
⏰ Preferred: Immediate Joiner

🔑 Key Responsibilities
✔️ Assist with recruitment activities, including candidate sourcing, resume screening, and shortlisting.
✔️ Post job openings on LinkedIn, job portals, and other relevant platforms.
✔️ Coordinate interviews and communicate with candidates regarding schedules and updates.
✔️ Maintain candidate databases, employee records, recruitment trackers, and internal documentation.
✔️ Assist with employee onboarding, joining formalities, and HR documentation.
✔️ Handle data entry, data verification, spreadsheet updates, and record management.
✔️ Organize and maintain digital files, documents, databases, and business records.
✔️ Prepare basic reports, summaries, and operational updates.
✔️ Coordinate with internal teams to ensure smooth execution of daily activities.
✔️ Track pending tasks, follow-ups, and operational requirements.
✔️ Ensure accuracy and completeness of records, reports, and documentation.
✔️ Support routine HR, administrative, and operational activities as required.
✔️ Manage multiple tasks efficiently while meeting deadlines.

💻 Mandatory Requirements
✔️ Fresher–1 year of experience in HR, recruitment, administration, operations, or a related role.
✔️ Basic understanding of recruitment, candidate coordination, and administrative processes.
✔️ Good knowledge of MS Excel, Google Sheets, MS Word, and other office tools.
✔️ Strong data entry, documentation, and record-management skills.
✔️ Good communication and interpersonal skills.
✔️ Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and time management.
✔️ Ability to manage multiple responsibilities and prioritize tasks effectively.
✔️ Professional approach when communicating with candidates and team members.
✔️ Ability to work independently as well as part of a team.
✔️ Proactive attitude, willingness to learn, and ability to take ownership of assigned tasks.
✔️ Freshers with good communication and computer skills are welcome to apply.
