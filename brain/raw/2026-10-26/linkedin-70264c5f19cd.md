This Friday, we had Deftera '26: The Intra-Department Symposium at our college. It was quite a memorable day, I should say!

I was coordinating an event and wanted to do something different from the usual Excel sheets, endless data entry, and hectic last-minute stress. I also wanted to give participants private instructions instead of repeatedly announcing them and disturbing those busy turning their ideas into magical apps, utilities, and games. 😂

Here's where VibeForge: Vibe Code It Out! comes in. 🚀

The event had six challenges. Participants could choose one and build anything they imagined using AI coding tools like Cursor, v0, Bolt, or Lovable. A website, a game, a utility, anything! They had just 45 minutes, with a surprise task assigned after 30 minutes.

Managing participant details, timers, challenge selections, and surprise task completion using spreadsheets? Hell, nahhh. 💀

So, I built VibeForge using React and Supabase!

✨ What it does:
🔹 Collects participant details and lets them choose a challenge.
🔹 Reveals challenge instructions only after confirmation, when the timer starts.
🔹 Tracks start time, remaining time, and end time.
🔹 Tracks surprise task assignments and completion.
🔹 Provides an admin dashboard with participant details, active and completed counts, and everything needed to coordinate the event smoothly.

🌐 Try it here: https://lnkd.in/gFprrEss

The admin dashboard isn't publicly accessible, so I'll share a video showcasing it too. Hehe!

Huge thanks to OpenCode for making development so much simpler by doing most of the heavy lifting! ❤️ I still stayed up until 1 AM testing everything, but it was totally worth it.

For once, I was enjoying coordinating an event instead of breaking my bones and losing my hair over last-minute chaos. 😂

Sometimes, the best way to solve a problem is to build a tool that eliminates it in the first place.

One less spreadsheet. One less headache. One smoother event. ✨
