I knew Praper Media would be different.
 I just didn’t know HOW different!!

Having worked in #IT companies lately , I was used to working with people from different age groups, mostly with people double of my age even.

And then came Praper Media  — 
 A Video editing Agency where almost everyone is Gen Z (yes, I’m one too 😂), and many are experiencing their FIRST JOB straight out of college.

The #culture? Different.
The #conversations? Different.
The #energy? VERY different. 🥲

But honestly, it’s not about doing random things for content or making everything look cool on Instagram.

It’s about how naturally people can work when the environment allows them to be themselves. And of course, every generation has its own way of looking at work. ❤️

But the biggest change for me wasn’t the age group, the industry, or even the culture.

It was the #WAY #WE #WORK.

In my previous experiences, a lot of HR work involved manual effort — maintaining hiring trackers, employee databases, files, documentation, and endless follow-ups. I knew how to manage it all, but it took time and constant attention.

At Praper, automation, clear #SOPs, and well-defined workflows have made so much of this #smoother.

There’s clarity, flexibility, and room to get things done without unnecessary chaos.
Of course, every workplace has its challenges. But when the foundation is right, even the busiest days feel much more manageable.


And something that genuinely makes me happy? 
As an HR professional, I can usually wrap up my work by 6 PM.
- No unnecessary backlog sitting in my head.
- No feeling that ten things are still pending after logging out.
- No everyday exhaustion from trying to keep everything together manually.

I get to finish my work, log out, and actually have an evening of my own.
And honestly, this might sound like a small thing, but it feels HUGE when you’ve been used to juggling so many things at once.

Sometimes, career growth isn’t about learning to do more. It’s about discovering a better way to do what you’ve always done. 🧡


Fellow #HRprofessionals, how well-defined are SOPs in your day-to-day HR processes?
Do structured workflows help you reduce manual effort and improve efficiency?
