Black Friday is getting close. Is your order process ready? 🛒

Most retailers are preparing their webshops for more visitors and orders.

But what happens after someone clicks “Buy”?

Does the order reach your ERP? Does the warehouse receive it? Is stock updated across your sales channels?

And what happens if something goes wrong?

When orders depend on manual exports, spreadsheets or someone checking data, higher order volumes can quickly become a problem.

Here are 5 things worth testing before 27 November:

1. Place an order: Does it reach your ERP and warehouse automatically? Does the customer receive an update?
2. Change your stock: Is the new quantity updated across all your sales channels?
3. Trigger an error: What happens if an order contains an unknown product or missing information? Will someone be notified?
4. Process a return: Are the refund, inventory and financial records updated correctly?
5. Retry a failed order: Can you safely process it again without creating duplicates?

This is exactly the kind of work we do at Teknuro.

Using Celigo, we’ve helped customers connect their webshops, ERP, warehouse systems (WMS), product information systems (PIM) and more.

Instead of manually moving data between systems, orders, stock, pricing, product information, shipments and invoices can be exchanged automatically.

In one of our projects, this also helped make it possible for new customers to start placing orders within just one day.

But automation isn’t just about getting data from A to B. It’s also about knowing when something fails and being able to fix it without disrupting the rest of the process.

Better to discover these issues now than during your busiest sales weekend.

How confident are you that your order process is ready for Black Friday?
