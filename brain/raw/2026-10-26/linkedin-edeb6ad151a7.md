Chartis Research has named CleverChain a Category Leader for KYC Data and an Enterprise Solution for KYC Solutions in its KYC Data and Solutions 2026 Quadrant Update. 

This is our first appearance in this publication, placing us directly in the top-quadrants, based on completeness of offering and market potential. Our thanks to Philip Mackenzie, Ahmad Kataf and the Chartis Team for the assessment, and congratulations to the other vendors featured.

Two of the report’s takeaways matter to anyone buying KYC data. 
Chartis finds that capabilities which once distinguished specialist vendors are becoming widely accessible. It also finds that data and solutions providers alike are repositioning as risk intelligence businesses. Once registries, watchlists and ownership records stop being scarce, coverage alone gives a buyer little reason to prefer one provider over another. The choice then turns on the quality of the intelligence, and on its ability to confidently automate workflows at scale.

That is what HELIX, the contextual engine behind CleverChain’s agents, is built to deliver:

◾ Calibrated to each organisation. Policies, procedures, use cases, regulatory mapping and internal data are wired into the workflow, so each finding arrives aligned to the organisation’s own framework and risk appetite.

◾ Orchestrated across data and models. Registries, commercial datasets and open sources are selected for each scenario, and the best-fit model is drawn from an ensemble for each task on current performance. No single provider is a dependency. 

◾ Evidenced for scrutiny. Agents test each risk hypothesis against primary-source evidence and resolve it into a narrative a reviewer can defend. 

◾ Continuous, with materiality built in. Monitoring assesses each change against the organisation’s own risk profile. It then routes the change to a timestamped update, an evidenced escalation or a client outreach, so perpetual KYC reduces the review backlog without creating an alert backlog in its place. 

◾ Remediation at field level. Where specific data points are missing or out of date, our remediation agents complete those fields directly, without re-running a full investigation.

Chartis Research also expects the economics of agentic AI to become a central competitive consideration. In our view, the useful unit for that comparison is the cost of a decision an organisation can defend to an auditor, a regulator or its own board, including the analyst hours spent repairing incomplete records.
