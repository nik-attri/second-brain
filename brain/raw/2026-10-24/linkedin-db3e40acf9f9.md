3 in 4 procurement leaders say their data isn’t ready for AI.

YES 75%. I was baffled too.

But what is most baffling is that most of them buy it anyway.

Ok ok, I do get why - because there is this endless pressure to “do something with AI” in the air.

It is relentless and signing off a licence is a ton easier than admitting your spend data has the same category sitting under 20 different names (iykyk don't cha Susan Walsh)

So let me save you a lot of money:

AI will not fix that. The end.

If you are going to give an agent a supplier list full of duplicates, contracts that live as PDFs on a shared drive, and a process half your stakeholders already work around it will just do its thang...

At scale. 
Without a qualm. 

And then, to end the fun, it will charge your hard cash every single time it runs.

It's the old "garbage in, garbage out" but faster and hella more expensive.

I have worked with (MANY!) procurement teams who spent millions and millons on a platform and were back in their spreadsheets.

Why is it nobody asks them how they actually buy before they buy the tool? It's shocking

That never gets written up as a failed project, no no no. 

They call it “adoption issues” and get it buried.

We really don’t have an AI problem at all.

What we do have is two decades of boring, dull, unglamorous jobs we kept putting off, and AI has walked in and turned the lights on. Tah-dah!

When I see teams getting real returns it is because they did the most, dullest of things first. 

Cleaned the data
Agreed one taxonomy
Dragged their contracts out of PDF hell. 

And then, and only then they let AI near it.
