Case Study: From a stand-alone website to one connected ordering platform: Odoo for a healthy-food brand 🥗

Industry: Food and beverage, online ordering
Business: A modern healthy-food brand serving salads, wraps, bowls and beverages
Platform: Odoo ERP
Modules: Website, Sales, Inventory, Accounting, CRM, Contacts, Dashboards, Reporting, plus a custom Order Management

THE PROBLEM
As demand grew, the brand's website ran separately from everything behind it
• Orders were passed to the kitchen by hand
• Menus were updated manually in the backend
• Stock and accounts were updated later
• Online payments were limited and unreliable
• No single view of order status
• Leadership had little visibility into sales and performance
• It was hard to grow the digital side reliably

THE GOALS (agreed at the start)
1. A modern online ordering website
2. Website integrated with Odoo
3. Order processing automated end-to-end
4. Secure online payments
5. Menu, customer and order management in one place
6. Better operational visibility
7. A platform ready to scale

THE SOLUTION (one connected ecosystem)
1️⃣ Website and ordering experience
• Responsive website for desktop and mobile
• Menu, prices and availability update instantly from Odoo, with no re-entry
• Customer login and live order tracking

2️⃣ Kitchen, payments and inventory
• A custom kitchen order workflow that sends incoming orders straight to the kitchen queue
• Secure payment gateway integration
• Stock that updates automatically as orders are placed and fulfilled

3️⃣ CRM and executive visibility
• Customer profiles with full history, so repeat orders are easy
• Executive dashboards and MIS reporting

HOW WE DELIVERED IT (steps)
Business discovery and requirements → Website UI/UX design → Odoo configuration and custom development → Payment gateway integration → Inventory and sales integration → Testing and user acceptance → Go-live and ongoing support

THE RESULT
✅ Seamless website-to-Odoo integration
✅ Online orders processed automatically, from website to kitchen to accounting
✅ Faster kitchen operations and fulfillment
✅ Secure digital payments
✅ One platform for menus, stock, customers and orders
✅ Dashboards that give management live visibility
✅ A modular foundation ready for the next stage of growth

Tech: Python, Odoo ORM, XML/QWeb, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PostgreSQL, payment gateway APIs.

LESSONS LEARNED
• Put website, sales, inventory and accounting on one data model, so there is no gap between systems.
• Use Odoo's built-in website and ORM integration for real-time sync, not manual re-entry.
• Build custom only where the business needs it. Here that was the kitchen order flow, instead of a generic template.
• Start with discovery. Every technical choice should trace back to a real problem.
