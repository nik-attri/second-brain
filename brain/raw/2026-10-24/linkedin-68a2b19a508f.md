Healthcare GCCs May Generate 67,000 Jobs in India in 2026: The Shift Towards Specialised Talent

𝗜𝗻𝗱𝗶𝗮'𝘀 𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘁𝗵𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗚𝗖𝗖𝘀 𝗖𝗼𝘂𝗹𝗱 𝗚𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝟲𝟳,𝟬𝟬𝟬 𝗝𝗼𝗯 𝗢𝗽𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀 𝗶𝗻 𝟮𝟬𝟮𝟲. 𝗕𝘂𝘁 𝗪𝗵𝗼 𝗪𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗚𝗲𝘁 𝗛𝗶𝗿𝗲𝗱?

India's healthcare Global Capability Centres (GCCs) are evolving beyond traditional support functions into AI, digital health, scientific research, and clinical development.

According to Quess Corp's India Healthcare GCC Outlook 2026, nearly 93% of projected job demand is concentrated among professionals with 3–12 years of experience.

Three important workforce trends stand out:

🔹 AI and data analytics: Demand for specialised technology skills is rising.
🔹 Clinical and regulatory expertise: Research, quality, and compliance capabilities remain important.
🔹 The experience gap: Growing demand for experienced professionals raises questions about entry-level hiring and future talent development.

For healthcare organisations, the challenge is not just creating jobs. It is finding professionals who combine technical expertise with an understanding of the healthcare environment.

Discussion question: How can healthcare GCCs balance the demand for experienced specialists with the need to develop fresh talent?
