We're Hiring | Production Planning Manager
 Sulaymaniyah, Kurdistan Region, Iraq
Pioneer Pharmaceutical
Lead production, materials and capacity planning, converting approved demand into executable plans supporting product availability, delivery and compliance.
Key Responsibilities
• Develop production plans, MPS, MRP and capacity plans using validated demand, firm orders and approved master data.
• Support S&OP, assess demand, material and capacity constraints, and develop contingency plans using approved alternatives.
• Coordinate lines, shifts, batch sizes, changeovers and cleaning constraints with Production, which retains execution accountability.
• Verify material availability and usability, expiry/retest dates and QA/QC release timelines; communicate purchasing priorities and track supply commitments.
• Integrate institutional and tender demand, including KIMADIA where applicable, against validated contract milestones.
• Maintain ERP planning data with designated owners; coordinate new launches and approved regulatory/packaging changes with relevant functions.
• Lead planning reviews, align priorities and coordinate shipping documentation, permits and approvals within delegated responsibilities.
• Lead team development and succession; track projects, analyze forecast accuracy and plan deviations, and report KPIs, risks, corrective actions and required decisions.
Contracting, technical substitutions and approvals remain with authorized functions.
Qualifications & Competencies
✔️ Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy, Industrial Engineering, Business Administration, Operations Management, Supply Chain Management or a related field.
✔️ At least 15 years in production/material planning, including supervisory/management experience. Pharmaceutical manufacturing experience is mandatory.
✔️ Strong knowledge of MPS, MRP, S&OP, capacity planning, BOMs and GMP planning requirements.
✔️ Proficiency in ERP, advanced Excel, data analysis and planning/project-tracking tools.
✔️ Strong leadership, analytical, prioritization and cross-functional communication skills; ability to coordinate multiple planning initiatives.
✔️ Proficiency in English; Arabic/Kurdish is an advantage.
✔️ MBA, CSCP or CPIM is desirable.
