3 invoices. 6 weeks. Zero replies. 💸
That is rarely a "late customer" problem. It is usually a follow-up problem.
Most UAE businesses I see still chase payments the same way: someone in accounts opens the ageing report on a Monday, spots the overdue names, and sends another round of polite-but-awkward "just following up" emails. If Monday is busy, nothing goes out. Every forgotten reminder lets the invoice age another two weeks — cash you already earned, sitting idle.
Manual chasing has one built-in flaw: it depends on a busy human remembering to do it.
Odoo does it the boring, reliable way:
• Automated reminders that go out the day an invoice goes overdue — then on day 7, day 14, day 30 — without anyone remembering a thing
• Every reminder logged on the customer chatter, so the full history is visible to the team
• A cash-flow forecast that shows what is actually coming in, instead of guessing
When we set this up for clients at ATMAR, the phone calls don't disappear — but they go to the 3 customers who genuinely need them, not the 30 you were drowning in.
