Every onboarding required printed forms, signatures, and manual entry into the payroll system.

An automated workflow could capture data from digital forms and feed it directly to HR software.

That single change can shrink the onboarding checklist dramatically and free up admin time each month.

Error-prone manual entry would disappear.

Lesson: Automating a single data capture point can transform the whole hiring experience.
