The best AI automation doesn’t start with “What can we automate?”

It starts with: “What work is slowing our people down?”

Think about the repetitive tasks that drain time but rarely need human judgment:
• Summarizing meeting notes
• Sorting and routing support requests
• Drafting first-pass reports
• Updating records across systems

Automating these steps can free teams to focus on the work that actually needs them: solving problems, building relationships, and making better decisions.

But successful automation isn’t just a technology project. It needs:
1. A clear, measurable problem
2. Reliable data and well-defined workflows
3. Human review where accuracy or trust matters
4. A way to track results—and improve over time

Start small. Choose one frustrating workflow, test an AI-assisted solution, and measure the time or errors it saves. Then scale what works.

The goal isn’t to automate everything. It’s to make valuable human work easier to do.
