I had a lovely evening at the Hard Hats To Heels York event last night - it's the 3rd one I've been to and each time I get something different out of it.

This time it was Holly Osborn Jennings who was talking to us about thinking of the end, then setting smaller tasks and milestones to get to that goal. I am going to be dusting off my moodboard - I did one last year and it really did help with my mindset. 

The one thing I do struggle with at the moment is how hard the landscape is for freelancers and anyone in design, copywriting and marketing especially. But I am going to be more positive from now on instead of let it bring me down - and I am also going to be looking less at the news and getting to the gym more to do classes I enjoy as that's my 'thing' that brings my mood up.

Thanks to Julia Felton for organising and lovely to see Helen Reynolds, Kathryn Dunn, MFHT, Tessa Gray amongst others. 


For those who don't know me, I'm a web, graphic and branding designer. I also have a SaaS which my husband and I have developed - a facilities management and on sight reporting app which we are hoping to get into the property sector. It is currently being used by Merseyrail and Stadler and lends itself to anyone who is in spreadsheet hell!
