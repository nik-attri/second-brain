Voice-of-customer > voice-of-boardroom

Your homepage leads with “operational efficiency” when there are recordings of ICPs explaining (in exact language) why they bought your software.

Meanwhile, 6 highly-paid people in a meeting are trying to guess.

Customers have their own words for all of it. And those exact words matter.

They tell you how the problem lives in their head.

That’s what I’m looking for when I read raw VOC.

I keep the exact phrase, the situation around it, and what it reveals about why they bought. 

Then I look for that same reason in other conversations with customers we want more of. 

One person saying something memorable gets my attention. 

Several customers describing versions of the same headache gets me thinking about the positioning. 

Their language helps me answer those questions. 

Sometimes it also hands me the headline.

VOC data + positioning analysis is a hell of a starting point for a homepage.

A buyer lands there and recognizes the EXACT mess they are dealing with. 

Now they have a reason to read about the product features, integrations, controls and automations make that problem go away. 

Give them something to believe.

If I’m responsible for conversions and pipeline, I want more buyers reaching the demo button thinking, “These people understand what we’re dealing with”

There’s probably spicy VOC for that in a customer call you've already recorded.

Maybe go read it before you pay for another thousand people to scroll past “operational efficiency”...
