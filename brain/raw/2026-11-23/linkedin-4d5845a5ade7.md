I don't just report the numbers. I build the tools.

For most of my career, my work in finance has meant reconciling, reporting and explaining numbers. This year I started building the tools that do the repetitive parts for me.

Here's what I've build so far:

🔹 Treasury cash-position dashboard. It gives a live view of cash positions, with an AI assistant that answers treasury questions straight from the data. I'm testing it against a set of evaluation questions so the answers stay accurate.

🔹 Bank reconciliation app. It runs in the browser with nothing to install, and matches transactions using clear rules on amount, date and reference.

🔹 Business Clinic web app. I built it for a church's Economic Empowerment Team. It collects business owners' details, has a secure admin login and exports the data. It runs on Google Apps Script and Google Sheets at no hosting cost.

🔹 AI automation workflows. I build n8n workflows that connect systems and remove manual steps.

What I've learned: the best finance tools come from people who understand both the ledger and the code. I'm now going deeper into AI engineering (APIs, RAG, deployment) so I can connect automations to real ERP systems like Sage.

I'm open to roles and projects where finance meets automation and AI. If your team is drowning in spreadsheets and manual reconciliations, let's talk. 🤝
