Last week we looked at why a falling student loan delinquency rate can mislead. Here's the practical follow-up.

If you finance tuition yourself, national averages won't tell you how your students will repay. Your own program data will.

Three things worth tracking instead:
→ Outcomes by decision path. How do automatically approved, conditionally approved, and manually reviewed applicants perform later? That shows whether your thresholds are set in the right place.
→ Performance by plan type. Fixed payment plans, income-based or ISA structures, and hybrid plans carry different risk. Compare them on your own cohorts.
→ Performance by program and intake. Averages across the whole portfolio hide the segments that drive your losses.

None of this depends on how a credit bureau reports defaulted debt.

Which of these can you see in your data today?
