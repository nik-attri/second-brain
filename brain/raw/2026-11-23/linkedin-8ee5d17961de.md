AI is moving fast. Businesses need to think beyond the hype.

Here are 3 AI developments worth your attention this week:

1. Banks are restricting AI agents.
Major US banks are tightening controls on third-party AI agents accessing customer accounts due to security and transaction risks.
→ Business lesson: AI automation needs clear permissions and human oversight.

2. AI agent adoption is accelerating.
McKinsey's 2026 research reports that 40% of respondents at organisations with over $1 billion in revenue are scaling AI agents, up from 27% previously.
→ Business lesson: AI agents are moving from experimentation into real business operations.

3. OpenAI calls for industry-wide AI limits.
OpenAI's Chief Scientist has proposed common safety thresholds for developing increasingly powerful AI systems.
→ Business lesson: AI governance and security will be as important as innovation.

THE BIGGER PICTURE
The AI conversation is shifting from:
"How can AI save us time?"
To:
"How can AI safely run parts of our business?"

Practical Advice for Businesses: Start with repetitive tasks. Integrate AI into existing workflows. Keep humans in control of critical decisions.
