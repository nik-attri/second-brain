#QMENTAFoundation
 A governed data layer for medical imaging AI, across any therapeutic area and modality. Collect, organize, label, harmonize, and validate your data in one system:
- 50+ validated AI workflows available from day one, or bring your own models
- Vendor-neutral ingestion (GE HealthCare, Siemens Healthineers, Philips, Canon, United Imaging) with automated PHI removal

#QMENTATrials
 One governed platform for every imaging function in a regulated neuroimaging trial, from site activation to regulatory submission:
- Protocol deviations flagged within minutes of upload, while the rescan window is still open
 - Version-locked AI deployed in hours, not months
- A zero-footprint viewer with about 1-second load times, shared by sites, CROs, sponsors, and radiologists
- 510(k)-cleared platform built for FDA 21 CFR Part 11, GxP, HIPAA, and GDPR, with zero nonconformities across external pharma partner audits to date

#QMENTALegal
 Standard MRI can look normal even when white matter tracts show measurable microstructural changes. QMENTA Legal automates DTI analysis:
 -  16 fiber tracts segmented automatically, with FA, MD, AD, and RD measured for each tract
 -  Left vs. right asymmetry analysis compares each tract with its counterpart in the opposite hemisphere
 -  A structured report returned to PACS or secure download, typically within 24–48 hours
- Pay-per-scan: no subscription, no pipeline to build. The result is objective, reproducible metrics that support expert interpretation.
