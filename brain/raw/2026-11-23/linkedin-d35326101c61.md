#we #are #Hiring

Job Description: Legal Counsel
Company: Oraxel Solutions
Location: Sulaymaniyah, Kurdistan Region of Iraq
Job Type: Full-time
Reports To: Country Manager

Job Overview:
Oraxel Solutions is seeking an experienced Legal Counsel to manage legal matters, support business operations, review contracts, and ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq.
Key Responsibilities:
•	Provide legal advice to management on business and operational matters.
•	Draft, review, and negotiate contracts and legal agreements.
•	Ensure compliance with applicable corporate, commercial, and labour laws.
•	Handle company registrations, licences, permits, and governmental procedures.
•	Advise HR on employment contracts, labour disputes, and social security matters.
•	Represent the company before authorities and courts when legally authorized.
•	Manage legal claims, disputes, official correspondence, and documentation.
•	Coordinate with external lawyers and governmental departments.
•	Monitor regulatory changes and identify potential legal risks.
Qualifications and Skills:
•	Bachelor's degree in Law.
•	3–5 years of relevant experience in corporate or commercial law.
•	Valid professional authorization for applicable legal practice and representation.
•	Good knowledge of Kurdistan Region laws and governmental procedures.
•	Experience in contract drafting, legal disputes, and employment matters.
•	Excellent Kurdish and Arabic; good working English.
•	Strong communication, negotiation, and organizational skills.
•	Ability to work independently with confidentiality and professionalism.
Preferred:
Previous experience as in-house legal counsel, particularly within telecom, trading, engineering, or contracting companies.

Application Instructions:
Interested candidates should submit a resume and to hr.oraxel.iraq@oraxel.com  with the subject line: Legal Counsel
