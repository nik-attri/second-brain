Legal Manager Kuwait Regional Group Full-time On-site Languages: Arabic and English A leading Kuwait-based regional group is strengthening its leadership team. We are looking for an experienced corporate and commercial lawyer to lead the Legal function as Legal Manager. This is the most senior in-house Legal position in the company. You will report into senior management, work directly with the Board when required, and have overall responsibility for the companys Legal function. The Role Corporate & Strategic Matters Lead Legal work relating to corporate restructurings, investments, joint ventures, new entities, shareholder matters and other strategic transactions. Support the Legal workstreams required for IPO readiness and work closely with external Legal and IPO advisers. Commercial Own the drafting, review and negotiation of significant commercial agreements across the Group, including supplier, distribution, technology, services, partnership, logistics and other strategic arrangements. You should be comfortable handling difficult commercial negotiations and finding solutions that protect the company without unnecessarily slowing the business down. Corporate & Board Matters Advise senior management on Companies Law, corporate structures, authorities, shareholder matters and Board-related Legal issues. Work closely with the Governance and Compliance functions on matters relevant to IPO and listed-company readiness. Legal Function Leadership Lead the companys Legal function and act as the senior internal Legal adviser to management. Manage external counsel in Kuwait and other GCC markets, oversee significant disputes and litigation, strengthen Legal processes and templates, and provide practical Legal advice across the business. Who We Are Looking For You are likely to have 10+ years of strong corporate and commercial Legal experience, gained in a respected law firm, listed company, major private group or complex operating business. You should have strong hands-on experience in areas such as:  Drafting and negotiating commercial agreements. Advising senior management on corporate and commercial Legal matters. Managing disputes, litigation and external Legal counsel. Corporate transactions, restructurings, acquisitions or joint ventures. Providing independent, practical and commercially focused Legal advice.  Strong knowledge of the Kuwait Legal and commercial environment is important. Professional-level Arabic and English are required, including the ability to review, draft and negotiate Legal documents in both languages. Experience with listed companies, IPOs, CMA/Boursa Kuwait matters, Board matters or GCC operations is an advantage, but is not mandatory. Previous responsibility for a Legal team is preferred.
