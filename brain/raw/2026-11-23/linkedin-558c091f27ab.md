13,644.

That’s Queensland’s October settlement number... it’s the lowest month going back to early 2025, and 29% below October last year (19,289) .

October is normally one of the strongest months of the year , this one came in below the February lull of both 2025 and 2026 .

Go back to the high... December 2025: 20,616 transfers lodged . October’s number is 34% lower... a third of the market ... gone in ten months !

The slide wasn’t gradual... June 2026 was 18,694. July was 14,996, a fall of almost 20% in one single month. That’s when contracts signed after the May Budget started to settle.
July to October is down 21% on the same four months last year. That’s 15,718 fewer transactions .

The ABS says home lending fell 15% in the September quarter...  in both volume and value... that’s the largest fall since records began in 2002 !

Investor lending: down 31%
First-home buyer lending: down 9% , but don't forget the Federal Budget was sold as a policy for first-home buyers .

And the deposit they need ... new survey data from Compare the Market reveals Aussies who have the ability to save are putting away a median $250 a month . If the cost of living doesn't increase ... a 20% deposit on the average $1.1m home would take more than 70 years to accumulate .

Obviously interest rates have played a part... the RBA has now lifted the cash rate five times this year to 4.85%. The Governor says inflation is being driven by domestic capacity pressures .... but lets be honest , Government spending is part of that demand. When it keeps flowing into a capacity-constrained economy, the RBA has to squeeze households and businesses harder to make room for it .

Governments that don’t have to explain why inflation is sticky... rather just brush it off in a press conference with no accountability .

On top of that, Canberra chose to remove incentives for the investors who house Australia’s renters... a market already losing confidence lost more .

Fewer investors means fewer rental properties... tenants pay for that in higher rents ... the very people trying to save for a home deposit . 

With the banks and economists tipping the bottom of the market for mid to late 2027, these numbers are only going to get worse before they get better .

More pain for sellers . More pain for the families who rely on this industry for their income . More pain for renters .

To Canberra ... settlements are just a number on an Excel spreadsheet .

But behind the 15,718 fewer settlements is a family whose plans are on hold . A seller who can’t move on . An agent without a deal. A conveyancer / broker with a quiet desk. A tenant opening another rent increase .

That's what we're seeing at the moment ... not just a number on a spreadsheet , and they deserve a hell of a lot better from the Australian Government .
