Hard to beat working in energy, but my weekends are spent persuading people to adopt cats that have been through hell and back!🐾

By day it's kVA reviews, procurement strategy and half-hourly data.

By weekend it's a very different kind of negotiation.

"Yes, he's missing an ear. No, that doesn't make him any less of a cat."

We have Kiko's Cat Cafe in Rye, our side hustle and, honestly, the thing we're proudest of.

Every cat that comes through our doors has a story. Some came off the streets. Some were abandoned. Some were rescued from all over the world and arrived nervous, scarred and wary of every human they met.

Then someone walks in for a flat white (or Cat White) and leaves having found their new best friend.

What we've achieved so far:

✅ Over 200 cats rehomed, rescued from all over the world
✅ Ranked in the top 10% of cafes worldwide on Tripadvisor
✅ Voted number 1 in Rye for tea and coffee (in a town that's practically swimming in cafes ☕)

Running a small business alongside a full-time career is relentless. Early mornings, late nights, litter trays and spreadsheets..

But watching a cat that's been through the worst finally curl up on someone's lap makes every bit of it worth it.

If you're ever near Rye, come and say hello. Bring an open mind, and possibly a cat carrier. 😉
