🎂 5 years of Blue Green calls for a special Mendix Meetup!

On December 8, we're inviting the Mendix community to our office in Amsterdam to celebrate our 5th birthday!

And because we can't spend the entire evening eating cake, we've also prepared two interesting tech talks for our fellow Mendix enthusiasts. 🎤

🔹 Thom Hopstaken will introduce BGS-utilityTools, his own Mendix extension built using the Mendix Web API. He'll show how he built it and how it helps developers automate repetitive tasks without using AI tokens.

🔹 And apparently, we're not the only ones celebrating a 5th birthday! Mendix Workflows is turning 5 too! Our second speaker Jonathan Diehl will take the stage to talk about Workflows and celebrate this milestone with us.

Besides the tech talks, expect
- a fun birthday quiz, 
- good food, drinks, cake, 
- and plenty of time to catch up with familiar faces and meet new people from the community.

📅 December 8 | 17:30 – 22:00
📍 Blue Green Solutions | B. Amsterdam
