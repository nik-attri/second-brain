Job Vacancy: (Code 88 / 03) 

The selected candidate will be hired through International Business Services and will be assigned to our client. 

Position: Business Process Digitalization & Continuous Improvement Specialist 

Contract duration: full time 

Field: Medical devices – Multinational 

Location: New Cairo  

Working Hours:  8 

Days off: Friday & Saturday 

 

Job Purpose: 

Improves operational ways of working and translates well-defined business processes into practical, sustainable digital workflows. 

Working closely with process owners, operational teams and technology partners, the specialist uses tools such as Microsoft SharePoint and Power Automate to create low-code digital solutions. 

Key Responsibilities 

Translate approved process requirements into simple, user-friendly digital workflow concepts. 
Build or configure low-code solutions using Microsoft SharePoint, Power Automate and related Microsoft 365 tools. 
Ability to use more advanced tools eg. Python, Alteryx for more data heavy applications.  
Create practical solutions for requests, approvals, task allocation, notifications, document storage, status tracking, exception handling and reporting. 
Test solutions with users, document outcomes, manage defects and refine the design before implementation. 
Monitor solutions after launch and coordinate maintenance, improvements or escalation to specialist technology teams when required.  
Develop monitoring dashboards and reports that provide visibility into automation performance and business outcomes. 
Support data quality, traceability, access control, and auditability requirements. 
 

 

Required Qualifications 
Practical experience in an operational business function such as Customer Service (CS), order management, sales support, supply chain, finance operations or a comparable environment. 
Demonstrated understanding of how end-to-end business processes operate in practice, including hand-offs, exceptions, controls and the importance of process adherence. 
Exposure to Lean, continuous-improvement or structured problem-solving methods. 
Experience with Microsoft Power Apps, Microsoft Forms, Power BI, Teams, OneDrive or related Microsoft 365 tools. 
Familiarity with process-mapping standards or tools such as swimlane diagrams, flowcharts or Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN). 
Basic project-management capability, including scoping work, setting priorities, organizing tasks, tracking actions and delivering agreed outcomes. 
Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail and a structured approach to problem solving.
