2026 made one thing clear to me: real AI progress isn't in the demos. It's in how many of our own manual tasks we can hand off.
As a Data Scientist looking for my next role in the Stuttgart region, I noticed I was spending more time searching for jobs than applying to them.
So I built a pipeline with Apify that:
🔹 Scrapes new Data Science / AI roles in the Stuttgart region from job boards
🔹 Matches each role against my CV
🔹 Notes where I fit, and where I have gaps, for every role
🔹 Sorts them into tiers: "Best shot", "Worth a try", "Good fit, more competition"
🔹 Tracks how many applicants each posting already has
The result: one sheet that tells me where to spend my energy first.
What I did NOT automate: the applications themselves. The gap notes tell me what to address in each cover letter, but writing it is still my job.
My takeaway: don't ask "what can AI do?" Ask "what am I doing manually that I shouldn't be?"
What have you automated this year? 👇
