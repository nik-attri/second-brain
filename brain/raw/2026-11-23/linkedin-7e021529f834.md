Housing Transformation - Panoramic Associates 

Normally, I use this space to share general reflections from conversations, post live roles, and break down new sector or regulatory updates.

However, with the run in to Christmas well underway and since I haven't had the chance to message everyone individually yet - I thought it would be helpful to do a quick reset on what I actually do at Panoramic Associates. 

I partner with Social Housing and Local Government leaders to assess their systems, processes and data- helping organisations drive efficiency, and navigate transformation without relying on expensive, long-term outsourced delivery.

The 3 Core Areas I Cover:

Housing Management Systems: End to end technical and strategic leadership across HMS lifecycles - covering data cleansing, API integrations, implementations and general optimisation pieces. 

Regulatory Compliance: Establishing robust data governance to meet Big 6 requirements and Awaab’s Law, enabling proactive risk identification.

Homelessness & TA Prevention: Tackling reactive process and adoption challenges while embedding predictive data models to strip away manual admin.

The Problems I Typically Solve 

Whether I’m speaking with Housing, IT, or system leads, the root issues usually look like this:

Disconnected Systems: Core systems failing to talk to each other, creating data blind spots and stalled user adoption.

Manual workflows: Overworked teams managing critical tasks on personal spreadsheets instead of live Power BI dashboards and centralised systems - turning audits into nightmares.

Stretched Internal Capacity: Permanent teams drowning in BAU tasks with no headspace or specialist SME skills to deliver system roadmaps.

How I Do It 

I diagnose the root operational issue first, and connect housing providers with specialist talent either on an interim, or consultancy basis, depending which route is healthier for the bank balance. 

They don't just plug a temporary gap - they fix problems whilst upskilling your permanent staff, so the knowledge stays in-house long after their contract ends.

If any of those headaches sound familiar from a personal or company perspective -always happy to have an informal chat.
