Three out of four companies see a 3 to 10 percent uplift in return on sales from pricing and quoting automation. That finding comes from a Simon-Kucher study of 200 business leaders published in 2026.

Good news for anyone quoting manually. But before you go comparing tools, check what HubSpot already does.

If you're on legacy quotes, you have no automated pricing at all. If you've moved to Revenue Hub, you get price books and tiered pricing, though each price book is a separate subset of your product library with no bulk import or update. Not ideal when you're maintaining them per customer.

So a HubSpot Plugin earns its place only when it fills a real gap: automated company-based pricing, multi-currency price lists, or simpler price management than price books. And look at the cost model. Per-user pricing grows with every rep you hire. Usage-based pricing grows with the quoting you actually do.
