Why do freight forwarders and customs brokers still reconcile CARM statements manually in Excel?

It’s not because they love VLOOKUPs or spending 30 minutes on every CAD declaration.
It’s because in trade compliance, changing workflows feels far riskier than the hours being lost.
When duties, GST, and CBSA compliance are on the line, “new software” usually triggers 5 real fears:
⚠️ Fear of AMPS penalties if an algorithm miscalculates
⚠️ 6+ months of painful ERP/TMS migrations
⚠️ Handing sensitive supplier commercial invoices to a black-box 3rd party
⚠️ Resistance from already overworked customs teams
⚠️ What happens when CBSA tweaks the rules again?

So teams choose the pain they know (spreadsheet hell amp; leaking unverified GST) over the risk they fear.

1️⃣ Non-Invasive Audit Layer: Sits quietly alongside your existing TMS/ERP—no ripping, replacing, or IT downtime.

4️⃣ Sub-20-Second 3-Way Matching: Cross-reconcile CAD vs. Commercial Invoice vs. SOA with live Bank of Canada exchange rates—flagging duty overpayments and unclaimed ITCs instantly.
