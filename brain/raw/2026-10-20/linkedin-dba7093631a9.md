I’m building an AI workflow automation that can simplify sourcing for recruiters without the use multiple sourcing channels, qualifying 100's of candidates automatically and I’m sharing a PDF on how this can be done.

The workflow uses a job title, job description and location as inputs. Built using a tool like n8n, it connects APIs and authorised data sources to discover candidate profiles, organise relevant information and use an AI model to assess skills, experience, industry background, and alignment with the job requirements. 

The results can then be stored in a structured database, with potential matches and the reasoning behind each assessment ready for recruiter review.

The interesting part? We may not always need multiple sourcing platforms to improve our sourcing process. With the right workflow, we can connect existing tools, automate repetitive tasks and make better use of the data available to us. Of course, AI-generated matches still need human validation and the quality of the output depends on the data sources and workflow design. But the opportunity to spend less time navigating tools and more time engaging with talent is worth exploring.

I’m looking at sourcing not just as a search activity, but as a workflow that can be engineered.
