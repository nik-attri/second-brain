So, what exactly can AI not do at this point?

Sometimes, we spend so much time worrying about AI taking our jobs that we forget to explore how it can make us better at them.

If you're a designer, learn how to use it in your design process.
If you're a writer, explore how it can improve your workflow.
If you're a teacher, discover how it can support your teaching.
If you're a Product Manager, explore how it can help you with research, analysis, documentation, and prototyping.

Whatever your profession is, there is probably a way AI can help you work smarter.

But remember, having access to AI is not the same as knowing how to use it well. It can generate outputs, but you still need judgment, context, creativity, and the ability to recognize what is actually good.

Don't just watch AI change the world. Learn how to use it to do better work in yours.
