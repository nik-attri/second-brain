Recruiters spend significant time on repetitive activities such as data entry, interview 
scheduling, status updates, reporting, and follow-ups.

Many of these activities can be automated without removing the human element.Automation can help recruiters spend more time speaking with candidates and hiring managers.

The objective should be to automate repetitive work, not relationships.

Good recruitment technology gives recruiters more time for the work that actually requires human judgment.
