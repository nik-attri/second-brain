📢 WE’RE HIRING | Manager – Pricing Control & Competitor Intelligence

📍 Company: GoFix
💼 Experience: 5–8 Years
👥 Team Management Experience: 2–3 Years
🏢 Function: Pricing Control & Commercial Analytics

About the Role
GoFix is looking for a commercially driven and analytical Manager – Pricing Control & Competitor Intelligence to lead our pricing team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for monitoring competitor pricing, managing discount controls, protecting profit margins, and ensuring competitive pricing across our stores.
If you have strong analytical skills, retail pricing experience, and the ability to turn market insights into business decisions, we’d love to hear from you!

🔑 Key Responsibilities
- Lead and manage the Pricing Control team, ensuring timely and accurate execution of daily activities.
- Monitor and analyse competitor prices, promotions, offers, and market trends.
- Recommend pricing strategies to maintain competitiveness while protecting profitability.
- Establish and enforce discount policies, approval limits, and pricing control procedures.
- Review exceptional discount requests and identify unauthorized or unprofitable discounts.
- Track pricing compliance across stores and ensure adherence to approved policies.
- Prepare pricing reports and dashboards to monitor price gaps, discount leakage, and gross margins.
- Work closely with Sales, Store Operations, Finance, Marketing, Commercial, Procurement, and IT teams.
- Evaluate promotional campaigns and assess their impact on sales and profitability.
- Train store teams on pricing policies and discount approval processes.
- Identify pricing discrepancies and implement process improvements.

🎯 What We’re Looking For
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Marketing, Retail Management, or a related field.
- 5–8 years of relevant experience in pricing, commercial analytics, revenue management, retail operations, or category management.
- 2–3 years of experience in team management.
- Strong knowledge of pricing strategies, gross margins, promotions, and discount management.
- Experience in retail, e-commerce, distribution, or multi-location businesses.
- Advanced Excel skills; Power BI, Tableau, ERP, POS, or pricing-system knowledge is an added advantage.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder-management skills.
- High attention to detail, commercial awareness, and the ability to make data-driven decisions.

Be part of a dynamic team where your pricing strategies and market insights can directly influence business performance, profitability, and competitive growth.
