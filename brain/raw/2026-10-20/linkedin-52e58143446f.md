Last week, I had the chance to attend the Women in BIM - AI in Action conference in London.

It was a fantastic day of learning, networking and exchanging ideas around AI and its growing impact on the AEC industry. From practical tips on getting the most out of AI, to discussions around security, governance and the need to maintain human judgement at the centre of decision-making, there was plenty to take away.
I particularly enjoyed seeing real-world applications of AI in action, from agentic design workflows that can automate complex tasks and support decision-making, to autonomous robotics carrying out activities on site.

It was also a great opportunity to connect with BIM professionals from a wide range of backgrounds and organisations, as well as catch up with fellow mentees and mentors from the Women in BIM network!

Thank you to the organisers and speakers for such an inspiring event. I came away with even more excitement about how AI can support our industry 😊
