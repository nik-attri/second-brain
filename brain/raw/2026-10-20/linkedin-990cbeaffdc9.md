Can an employee still say there is no time in the era of Agentic AI?

I think Short answer: No.

Long answer: The nature of time has fundamentally changed. We have moved from reactive copilots to autonomous AI agents that orchestrate multi-step workflows.

They draft contracts, run data analysis, triage support, and execute operations across systems. So can Agentic AI perform employee tasks?

Yes, it can execute repetitive, structured, and execution-heavy tasks with speed. But it cannot replace contextual judgment, empathy, or strategic ownership. When someone claims less time today, the bottleneck is rarely operational bandwidth.
It is almost always a lack of system integration, poor process design, or fear of delegation. AI doesnt eliminate the need for humans; it eliminates the tolerance for low-leverage busywork. The real competitive advantage belongs to teams that shift from doing tasks to managing workflows. If your team is still drowning in manual friction, the issue isnt a lack of hours. Its an absence of orchestration. How is your organization redefining time and productivity this year?


My Perspective and Ideas to Increase Employee Performance

When organizations introduce autonomous AI systems without changing how teams actually operate, productivity often stalls. To genuinely elevate employee performance and banish the less time excuse in the age of Agentic AI, leaders should focus on these core pillars:

 
 >> Shift Focus from Task Execution to System Orchestration: Train employees to think like managers and supervisors of AI agents rather than solitary task-doers. Performance should be measured by the quality of the outcomes they orchestrate, not the volume of manual hours logged.

>>  Eliminate Vibe Coding and Unstructured Tool Adoption: Simply throwing AI tools at a team without alignment leads to confusion and rework. Establish clear, standardized internal protocols for how agents handle recurring workflows (such as reporting, data entry, and lead routing).

>>Automate the Administrative Drain: Actively identify and strip away repetitive friction points across departments. Give employees dedicated hours to build and refine custom automated mini-workflows using low-code tools tailored to their specific daily bottlenecks.

>>Prioritize Continuous Evaluation (Eval Loops): Implement continuous tracking mechanisms for AI-assisted outputs. When employees trust that their AI agents are running accurate, tested processes, they spend less time double-checking code or documents and more time executing high-impact strategy.

>>Cultivate Psychological Safety Around Upskilling: Address the fear of replacement head-on. Performance thrives when employees feel empowered to experiment with automation, knowing that the goal of Agentic AI is to elevate their capacity, not eliminate their roles.
