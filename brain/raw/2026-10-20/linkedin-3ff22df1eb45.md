Apparently we’ve reached the “let strangers loose inside the software” stage of building BizzCred. 😂

So that’s exactly what we’re doing.

We’ve opened up our new BizzCred platform for people to test.

Not a video.

Not screenshots.

Not a carefully controlled demo where somebody conveniently avoids clicking the button that doesn’t work.

The actual live platform.

👉 https://lnkd.in/e-29CvhT

Getting inside takes about 30 seconds.

Pick an email.

Pick a password.

Done.

No email verification.
No phone verification.
No credit card.
No salesperson hunting you down afterward.

Hell, make up the email if you want. We genuinely don’t care.

What we DO care about is whether the platform is useful.

BizzCred is being built to bring a lot of the small-business financial journey into one place.

Inside, you can explore tools and workflows for business credit, fundability, vendor/tradeline development, capital readiness, funding preparation, SBA/lending pathways, training, documents, progress tracking, and client management.

There are two sides to explore:

👤 Client Portal — where a business can actually work through the process.

🤝 Partner Portal — where financial professionals and partners can manage and help multiple businesses through that process.

The bigger idea is to stop making small-business owners piece together 17 different websites, spreadsheets, YouTube videos, lenders, vendors, and credit resources just to figure out:

“What the hell am I supposed to do next?”

That’s the problem we’re trying to solve.

So go play with it.

Click everything.

Try to confuse it.

Try to break it.

Then send me your feedback.

And please — don’t be nice just to be nice.
