🌟 Job Opportunity | Food & Beverage (F&B) Manager | Riyadh
Alma Hotel is seeking an experienced and results-driven Food & Beverage (F&B) Manager to oversee its food and beverage operations, lead service teams, maintain high hospitality standards, and ensure operational efficiency and guest satisfaction.
◾ Company: Alma Hotel
◾ Position: Food & Beverage (F&B) Manager
📍 Location: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
◾ Industry: Hospitality / Hotels
◾ Employment Type: Full-Time
🌟 Requirements & Qualifications
◾ Proven experience in Food & Beverage or Restaurant Management.
◾ Strong knowledge of hotel F&B operations, restaurant service, and hospitality standards.
◾ Experience managing daily operations, service quality, and staff performance.
◾ Excellent leadership, communication, and team management skills.
◾ Ability to manage operational budgets, control costs, and improve profitability.
◾ Knowledge of food safety, hygiene, and HACCP principles.
◾ Experience in menu planning, inventory control, and supplier coordination.
◾ Ability to monitor F&B performance indicators, revenue, and operating expenses.
◾ Strong understanding of guest service standards and complaint resolution.
◾ Experience in staff scheduling, training, performance evaluation, and team development.
◾ Familiarity with restaurant POS systems and inventory management software.
◾ Excellent organizational, decision-making, and problem-solving skills.
◾ Ability to work under pressure and maintain service quality during peak operations.
◾ Previous experience in hotel F&B management is preferred.
◾ A diploma or degree in Hospitality Management, Hotel Management, or a related field is an advantage.
