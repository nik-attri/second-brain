Nigeria is going digital. But are our records keeping up? 

A government ministry can launch digital services. A business can operate online. A hospital can use modern technology. 
Yet, behind the scenes, important documents may still be buried in filing cabinets, approvals may depend on physical files, and staff may spend valuable time searching for records. 
Here’s the challenge: Digitizing your organization means more than putting computers on desks. It means transforming how information moves.
In December 2025, Nigeria’s Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security launched an Electronic Document Management System (EDMS), while the Federal Ministry of Innovation, Science and Technology also went live on the 1GOV Enterprise Content Management System.
These developments raise an important question for both public and private organizations: Is your document management process ready for the digital future?

In our latest article, we explore how EDMS can help organizations: 
- Retrieve important records faster. 
- Strengthen document security and accountability. 
- Automate approvals and workflows.
- Reduce paperwork and improve operational efficiency.
- Preserve institutional knowledge. 
Whether you work in government, healthcare, education, finance, or business, this is a conversation worth having.
