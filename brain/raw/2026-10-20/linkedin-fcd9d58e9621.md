As soon as I turned 16 I got my first job as an evening and weekend receptionist for a car dealership. 

“Palm Chrysler Subaru, how can I direct your call?” 🚗

This was before cellphones, before everyone had a computer at their desk, and it was BOOORRRIIINNGGGG 😴

I took over the desk most weekdays for a woman who had to be 90yrs old. She showed me how to use the TYPEWRITER 🤦🏻‍♀️, use the Carbon-copy message pads, and transfer calls to the salesmen. My desk sat next to the little closet where the keys to all the cars on the lot were stored, this was how I got to know this rag tag team of Subaru and Chrysler sellers- and lemme tell ya- car salesmen are a different breed 😬🤣 (maybe this will be a series I’ll post about one day- including the infamous gallon of milk challenge weekend) 

Fun fact- not many people call a car dealership outside of service hours. Additional fun fact, I hate being bored… did I mention this job was BORING??! I mean, a 90yr old woman was doing this job during PEAK hours so what could a 16yr old possibly do to stay busy?! 

Well, I made lots of friends (who’s shocked?! 🤣). I got to know the woman who ran the service department. She was a badass- those service guys respected the hell outta her. She needed someone to call all the customers who had their car serviced to check and see how the car was and if they needed anything else. She was willing to PAY me additional money on top of my hourly rate 🤑 I would make 25¢ per call and there was a bonus for scheduling appointments I could earn as well. Well, say less… put me in coach! 🥎

16yr old me was cold calling everyone in a 100 mile radius of this dealership and cashing that check. Better believe I got really good at getting my little pitch in before I got cussed out or hung up on 👏🏻 😅

Mind you, they gave me no script, no training, nothing. Just a phone and a printed spreadsheet of names and phone numbers with a 👍🏻 and a pat on the back. 

And you know what? I killed it at that job. So much so, that I ended up having to replace myself at the desk so I could dedicate more time to being on the calls. It expanded to car buyers too- not just service. I would call everyone who ever stepped foot in our store and get them to tell me how we did, what we could do better, and have them schedule their maintenance appointment. I was a one woman customer outreach team paid by the dial. ☎️

I think about that job a lot. Were these managers putting too much faith in a 16yr old to run their customer outreach? Or were they just curious if this motion worked and figured how much could a teenager mess it up? Or maybe they saw potential in me and I rose to the occasion… I’ll never know. 

That dealership is long gone now. But I like to think that’s where my sales bug took root. I saw a need, created a playbook, and brought in revenue with a successful repeatable process. My only regret is not negotiating a commission 😜
