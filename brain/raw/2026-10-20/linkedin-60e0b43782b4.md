⭐ Rising Stars Week is here! ⭐

We're counting down the hours until the SOLD OUT ACG Denver - Young Professionals Rising Stars Awards, and we're especially excited to welcome Josh Cashman, CFA, Founder & CEO of QoEAgent AI, former ACG Denver Board Member, Leadership 20 alum, and Adjunct Professor at the University of Denver - Daniels College of Business Reiman School of Finance, as this year's keynote speaker.

In his keynote, "The ROI of Showing Up," Josh will challenge attendees to think beyond traditional metrics of success. Drawing from his experience in valuation, M&A, corporate finance, and impact investing, he'll share why some of the most valuable returns in our careers and lives can never be captured on a spreadsheet. From mentorship and professional growth to community engagement and meaningful relationships, Josh will explore how investing in people often yields the greatest return of all.
