I turned a 1-day job into 30 minutes. Heres how:Every month-end, we had the same nightmare.1,000 customer statements. Manual. Full day of printing, emailing, checking.My team was drowning and customers were waiting.I asked one question: Why are we still doing this manually?I went to our SAP Business Support team and said: Lets automate this.We built an automated distribution via SAP.

Result?
✅ From 1 full day → 30 minutes
✅ 1,000 statements sent automatically
✅ Zero manual errors
✅ Team freed up to focus on collections, not admin

Lesson:
As Finance Managers, we shouldnt just manage the numbers. We should fix the process behind the numbers.In Accounts Receivable, time is cash flow. Every hour saved in admin is an hour spent collecting.What manual process are you still doing in your finance team that should have been automated yesterday?#AccountsReceivable #SAP #FinanceTransformation #ProcessImprovement #FMCG #CreditManagement #OTC
