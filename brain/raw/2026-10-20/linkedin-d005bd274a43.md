Annual planning was built for a slower world. Locking in a budget once a year, then spending months reconciling spreadsheets as priorities change, isn't a process anymore. It's a bottleneck. Moving to quarterly updates can cut planning time in half. With the right portfolio management technology, you can update plans continuously, shift resources as the business changes, and leave spreadsheet hell behind for good.
