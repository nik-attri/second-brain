Documenting screens is a complete nightmare—especially when the UI changes every other week. ☠️

We've all been there on a project when someone says: Can you document this screen? Here’s the app, here’s Wiki, and here’s Snipping Tool. Good luck.

Why are IT pros still taking screenshots, drawing red arrows, and typing out screen descriptions by hand like it’s 1998?

Screens are essential, but keeping them up to date manually is just a waste of time.

So we decided to fix it.

Instead of pasting static images and long tables into wiki pages, we turn screen annotations into live, interactive visual models. You annotate the UI once, and it instantly syncs into Confluence as interactive documentation.
