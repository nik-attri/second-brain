Just when my DELETE Method™ trainees thought I'd finished creating resources for them… 😂

Apparently, I have a problem with leaving things alone.

My latest bright idea is a HUGE suitability spreadsheet covering pretty much everything a practitioner might encounter.

What we can use hypnosis for, how to approach different issues, when we shouldn't use it, and what we CAN do to help someone feel more comfortable when working directly on an issue isn't appropriate.

Because knowing how to use hypnosis is one thing. Knowing when to use it, when to adapt and when to leave well alone is just as important!

I'm aiming for something trainees can refer to when a client walks in with an issue and they think, Right… what the hell do I do with this one? 😂Of course, I thought this would be a quick little project.

Famous last words. Practitioners, what's the most unusual presenting issue a client has ever brought to you? I'm curious what I might have missed! 😂
