𝗢𝗽𝗲𝗻 𝗕𝗲𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝗘𝗹𝘀𝗲: AI for High Street & Rural Businesses…🚀

You've got a Shop, a Studio, or a Spare Room that doubles as a Head Office. You've got a Website, or at least a Facebook page. You know your way around Email and a Spreadsheet. What you haven't got is time.

That's not a guess, by the way. Earlier this year, Enterprise Oxfordshire ran a diagnostic with 59 High Street Businesses across the county. 88% said they were confident with everyday digital tools. Yet 49% scored themselves low on collecting and using Customer emails and reviews, and 29% on promoting the Business online. The gap isn't skill, it's hours in the day.

This Session is about closing that gap with AI Tools that are cheap or free, need no technical background, and can be running before you've finished your first coffee.
Grae Laws of Beyond Touch has spent the last two years showing #Oxfordshire Businesses how to do exactly that, and this is the condensed version. 

In the first half hour, he'll show you:
✅ How to write a month of social posts, a Newsletter, and a Google review reply in the time it takes to unpack a delivery.
✅ How to turn a rambling Customer email into a clear Quote, without the ten minutes of staring at the screen first.
✅ How to get the answer to "what should I stop doing" from your own Sales figures.
✅ The one AI habit that separates the Businesses who save time from the ones that just find a new thing to fiddle with.

Who is it for…
Independent High Street Businesses in Oxfordshire's market Towns and Villages, Rural and Home-based Businesses, and anyone running a Business on their own or with a very small Team. No AI experience needed. If you can send an email, you're qualified.
