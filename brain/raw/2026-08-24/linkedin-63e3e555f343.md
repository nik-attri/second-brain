🏙️ How Can a Smart Building Stay Open — Yet Secure? 🔍

Retail and public spaces on the lower floors. Corporate offices and restricted areas above.

As mixed-use buildings become more common, building management teams face a growing challenge: how do you manage different people, access needs, and security requirements within the same building?

When shoppers, employees, VIP guests, and contractors move through the same property every day, important questions arise:
Where do visitors go after entering the building?
Can security teams detect when someone enters an unauthorized floor?
How can different zones stay accessible without compromising security?

This is where #VisualAI can make a difference. 

🔐 Visitor Access × Cross-Zone Security
Once a visitor is registered, access can be assigned based on identity, authorized floors, and permitted time periods. If an unauthorized individual enters a restricted office or controlled area, building management can quickly identify the situation and respond.

🔍 Cross-Camera Tracking × Faster Investigation
When an incident occurs, security teams no longer need to manually search through footage camera by camera. With #airaTrack, personnel can quickly trace movement across multiple cameras and understand where an individual has been and where they are heading — shifting security from passive monitoring to proactive awareness.

📊 People Flow Insights × Smarter Building Operations
Beyond security, video can also become valuable operational data. By understanding people flow, high-traffic areas, and space occupancy, building managers can gain actionable insights for space planning and daily operations.

From security and building management to intelligent analytics and environmental sustainability, AIRA transforms existing video infrastructure from simply “seeing” what happens into actionable intelligence that helps building teams understand spaces, identify events, and make better decisions.
