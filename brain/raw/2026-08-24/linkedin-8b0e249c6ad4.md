I watched a logistics dispatcher in Bangalore lose a ₹8 lakh freight deal last week because he was in a meeting when the inquiry came through WhatsApp. By the time he replied, the shipper had already booked with someone else.

This happens constantly in logistics. Your best salespeople are drowning in manual follow-ups. A lead sits in WhatsApp for two hours. A callback gets missed because someone's on another call. And suddenly, your competitor who automated their response is winning deals you should have closed.

The problem isn't that you need fancy AI. It's that your lead response is built on heroics instead of systems. Most firms I work with lose 30-40% of qualified leads just to slow replies.

I put together a checklist for Bangalore logistics companies on what actually moves the needle here. It's not complex, but it matters.
