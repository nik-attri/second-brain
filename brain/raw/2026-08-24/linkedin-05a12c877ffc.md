I used to think the tools were the problem. They weren't.

A few years back, I sat across from a direct selling brand that had invested heavily in their tech stack. New CRM. New training portal. New compensation software. New social sharing tool.

On paper, they were digital. In reality, their consultants were drowning.

Every tool lived in a different tab. Every login was different. Every workflow was a guessing game. Their top performers — the ones who should have been selling — were spending their mornings just trying to figure out where to start.

I used to think that was a technology problem. I'd walk in, assess the stack, recommend new tools, and watch the brand feel hopeful again.

Here's what that belief cost me — and them:

Nothing changed. Not really. Because the problem was never the tools. It was that nobody had defined what a productive consultant day actually looks like before they built anything.

More than 50% of direct selling companies are still in the early stages of digital transformation — creating a growing and dangerous digital divide. (Source: McKinsey via Rallyware, https://lnkd.in/gcFgShYk, 2025)

Global retail sales held flat at $163.9B in 2024 — not because the channel lacks technology, but because the technology isn't being experienced as one coherent workflow by the person doing the selling. (Source: WFDSA via Direct Selling News, https://lnkd.in/gguu-ttb)

Rodan + Fields figured this out. Their chief sales officer said it plainly: "There is magic in simplicity in this business." They built a centralized mobile app — one place, one tap, everything a consultant needs — and the early results were strong. Not because the app was fancy. Because it removed the friction between intention and action. (Source: Direct Selling News, https://lnkd.in/gj9mmHqe)

That's the shift I made in how we build at D-SOL.

Now we start with one question before we build anything: what does a productive consultant day look like, from first login to first sale?

Then we build backward from that answer.

✅ One login. Every tool the consultant needs — back office, training, KPIs, catalog — lives in one place.
✅ One tap to shop, host, or join — no redirect chains, no manual rep ID entry, no lost momentum.
✅ One source of truth for the field — corporate pushes updates, assets, and announcements to the same app the consultant is already living in.
✅ One view of performance — consultants see their numbers in real time, so they know exactly where to focus.

The brands winning right now aren't the ones with the most tools. They're the ones who decided what the consultant's day should feel like — and then built that experience on purpose.

Simplicity isn't a design choice. It's a growth strategy.
