A three-person physio clinic was drowning in admin — and it was costing them 15 hours every single week.

Appointment bookings. Patient follow-ups. Intake forms. Billing. For a small practice, that's time that should be going to patients, not paperwork.

Here's what they changed — without hiring anyone or building custom software:

1️⃣ Online booking with AI reminders — patients book themselves, get automated follow-ups, and no-shows dropped by 35%.

2️⃣ Digital intake forms — patients complete them on their phone before arriving. No clipboards. No manual data entry.

3️⃣ AI-assisted billing — invoices are auto-generated and claims submitted based on appointment type. The practice manager barely touches it.

The result? Admin time fell from 15 hours a week to just 5. That's 10 hours handed back — now spent on patient experience and growing the practice.

This isn't a tech company story. It's a real small business that chose the right tools and set them up properly.

If you run a clinic, a consultancy, or any service business, this kind of outcome is within reach.
