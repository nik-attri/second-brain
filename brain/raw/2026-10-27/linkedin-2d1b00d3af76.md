Allow us to reintroduce ourselves.

Which feels a little ridiculous, considering NS Marketing has been around since 2019. But this is social media, where the collective attention span is roughly equivalent to a goldfish that just got promoted to Vice President of Thought Leadership.

We've been doing this for going on 20 years, in various agency and corporate roles. Long enough to remember when Facebook was actually fun, Twitter was called Twitter, and LinkedIn was... well, LinkedIn has always been a peculiar little terrarium of people congratulating themselves.

So… hello. Again.

We're NS Marketing. We handle social media, SEO, paid advertising, email marketing, content strategy, and occasionally explain why getting 40,000 impressions doesn't necessarily mean 40,000 people give a shit.
We genuinely love marketing. The psychology, the creativity, the strategy, and the beautiful, occasionally maddening process of figuring out why something works while something else crashes into a dumpster, catches fire, and somehow wins an industry award.

We love working with small businesses, partnering with agencies, and finding surprisingly simple answers to complicated problems.

What we're less enthusiastic about is the marketing industry's extraordinary ability to take a perfectly reasonable idea, wrap it in seventeen layers of corporate jargon, slap a trademark on it, and announce the birth of a revolutionary new framework.

Sir, you made a spreadsheet. Please return to Earth.

We've been here since 2019, and doing the work in other ways since 2006. We've spent those years helping businesses figure out what actually works, while periodically wondering who the hell left the marketing industry unsupervised.

We're just going to be a little louder about it.
