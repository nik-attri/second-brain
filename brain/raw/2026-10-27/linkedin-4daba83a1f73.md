🚨 WE ARE HIRING | ADMIN & SALES MANAGER
Organization: TECHNOMED Devices India Ltd.
Location: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
Experience: 2–5 Years
Qualification: MBA
Salary: ₹20,000 – ₹35,000 per month
Job Description
We are looking for a dynamic and result-oriented Admin & Sales Manager with experience in hospital or clinic administration and sales management. The candidate will be responsible for managing daily clinic operations, supervising staff, driving revenue growth, and ensuring excellent patient service.
Key Responsibilities
Administration & Operations
Manage day-to-day administrative operations of the clinic.
Supervise front desk, housekeeping, support staff, and other clinic personnel.
Monitor staff attendance, duty rosters, discipline, and overall performance.
Ensure proper clinic maintenance, cleanliness, hygiene, and patient service standards.
Maintain records, reports, documentation, inventory, and administrative expenses.
Coordinate with doctors, healthcare staff, vendors, and management.
Ensure compliance with applicable healthcare regulations and clinic policies.
Sales & Business Development
Manage and drive clinic sales, revenue, and business growth.
Develop strategies to increase patient footfall and promote clinic services.
Build relationships with doctors, referral partners, corporate clients, and local businesses.
Identify new business opportunities and generate leads for clinic services.
Monitor daily sales, revenue targets, conversion rates, and performance reports.
Follow up with potential patients and corporate clients to improve conversions.
Develop promotional activities and local marketing initiatives to increase clinic visibility.
Track sales performance and prepare regular reports for management.
Candidate Requirements
MBA in Hospital Administration, Healthcare Management, Marketing, or Business Administration.
2–5 years of relevant experience in hospital or clinic administration, preferably with sales responsibilities.
Strong leadership, communication, negotiation, and team management skills.
Ability to achieve sales targets and manage clinic operations efficiently.
Knowledge of healthcare services, patient handling, and business development.
Proficiency in MS Office and sales reporting.
