group projects are proof that communism doesn't work.



BECAUSE WHY THE HELL AM I DOING EVERYONE'S JOB????

there are four types of people in every group project:

a. the person who does 80% of the work and develops trust issues for life.

b. the person who says "guys, what can i do?" when the project is ALREADY SUBMITTED.

c. the person who disappears for 11 days and returns with "sorry guys, been busy 😭"

d. the person whose only contribution is changing the font from calibri to arial and then asking to be credited.

AND SOMEHOW EVERYONE GETS THE SAME MARKS.

the person who spent three nights researching the topic gets the same grade as someone who discovered the project existed 20 minutes before the deadline.

honestly, the real lesson isn't teamwork.

it's learning to forgive.

or learning to put everyone's contribution in a spreadsheet and present it to the teacher like evidence in a criminal trial.

(i'm not saying i've considered the second option; i'm saying google sheets has excellent documentation features. 😭)

which group project person are you?

and if you say "the leader," please provide references.
