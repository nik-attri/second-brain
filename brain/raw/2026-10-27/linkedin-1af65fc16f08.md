We create so much video content and then let it gathering digital dust, which is frustrating, because the stories don’t stop developing when a video reaches the graveyard folder - it ain't coming back out.

To repurpose and reuse any of this content usually means swapping files between tools and manually hunting for relevant footage. A job with a remarkable ability to remain on most to-do lists, right there at the bottom, below update timesheets and write annual reviews.

I've recently been searching for something that makes me want to reuse old content, but it has to be quick and easy.

This week I tried Quick Cut in Adobe Firefly’s video editor.

I uploaded 15 videos (around ten hours of footage) and created a prompt to bring related political construction stories into one focused video.

In my latest article, I walk through this process in four steps.

If you’ve got useful conversations sitting in your archive, have a read.

Try editing your own videos in Adobe Firefly. Choose a theme, upload relevant recordings and give Quick Cut a brief: https://adobe.ly/videoFFgo
