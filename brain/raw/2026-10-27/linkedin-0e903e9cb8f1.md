It’s always wild to see how people GENUINELY believe the hype around this game is anything but manufactured. No. Almost nothing will change in the industry during its release. It’s absolutely psychotic to think that GTA6 will pull players away from other titles en masse when it’s only releasing on two systems. Anyone who’s paid attention will know GTA6 will maybe be popular for a week or two at most, immediately lose all attention outside of knob slobbers and streamers the exact same way Red Dead 2 was.

GTA isn’t being made by the same people that made the original games let alone 5. Almost ZERO conversation about the game is happening beyond saying how it’s going to kill sales and play time in all other games when it releases.

To me, that screams media manufactured hype. Everyone I talk to says they may pick it up and are interested but anyone without a PlayStation or X Box isn’t even thinking about GTA6. We have a situation here where almost nobody is talking about the game and yet everyone in the industry and media is blowing it like a cheap hooker in a gas station bathroom.

I do not see a situation where ANY of this speculation comes true. It almost never does when companies do this in gaming. They bet on a situation and the players always seem to not latch on. Gaming media and creatives lately are out of touch with the actual players and it’s been known for years.

The people pedaling this crap likely aren’t actual gamers. They go off market trends and spreadsheets written by suits trying to analyze a market that actively resists that kind of speculation. I don’t care if it’s somebody with twenty years making games, they don’t have the finger on the pulse of gamers in 2026 if they buy into this notion of GTA6 being a ‘cultural moment’ or whatever.

It’s fabricated and plastic as all hell. Believe what you want but I don’t buy the hype for even a moment. I have to wonder who’s going to be right next month, the suits or me. I’ve almost never called this wrong when I see them, so let’s see who has the better idea of the 411 here. The suits banking on a release being a mega hit or the guy who has a library clocked at nearly 50,000 and puts in nearly 1000 hours in games a month.
