Jahan, General Manager at Cirrus International (our lift components business), starts almost every customer call the same way. How are you going? What are you up to? Then they get to the work stuff, and a couple of days later he'll ask how the volleyball went.

People love it when you remember. It tells them they're more than an order number.

If you're not naturally good at remembering the little things, you don't need fancy software to get better at it. A notebook, a spreadsheet or a note on their contact in your phone is enough. How they take their coffee, their kids' sport, the team they follow.
