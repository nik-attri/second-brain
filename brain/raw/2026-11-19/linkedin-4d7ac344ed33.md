Making one social media post takes 30–45 minutes: design, caption, hashtags, upload to all your social media account. I built a system that does it in one click.
Automation Build Series, #1 of 7
In real estate we post every day. Someone had to open Canva, write a caption, find hashtags and upload. Every single day.
So I built Post Studio, an AI content studio that:
→ Writes the caption and hashtags in the brand's own voice
→ Generates the image and applies the brand's logo, colours and layout
→ Lets each business design its own template by drag and drop
→ Copies the style of any post you like from a single reference image
→ Queues a whole week of posts and publishes them automatically to all your social media accounts.
→ Runs many businesses from one system, each with its own private login
How it works:
Brief → AI caption + image → branded design → queue → auto-publish
The biggest lesson: AI APIs fail more often than you'd think. Models get overloaded, free quotas disappear overnight. Adding automatic retries and a backup AI provider is what made it reliable enough to use every day.
It started as a Google Sheet workflow for our own page. Now it's a full platform I can set up for any business.
Tech: n8n · Next.js · PostgreSQL · Railway · Gemini · OpenAI · Cloudflare AI · Meta Graph API
