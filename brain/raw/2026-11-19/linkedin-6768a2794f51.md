🚀 We're Hiring: Assistant Manager - Card Operations | Dialog Finance PLC

Are you passionate about the payments industry and ready to take the next step in your career? Join Dialog Finance and be part of a team that is shaping the future of digital financial services in Sri Lanka.

As an Assistant Manager - Card Operations, you'll play a critical role in managing card operations, ensuring regulatory compliance, driving operational excellence, and supporting innovative payment solutions.

🔹 Key Responsibilities
✅ Oversee card issuance, activation, maintenance, and transaction reconciliations
✅ Ensure compliance with card scheme regulations and internal controls
✅ Prepare regulatory and management reports, including QMR submissions
✅ Monitor fraud alerts and coordinate with Risk & Compliance teams
✅ Support audits, operational controls, and governance requirements
✅ Drive process improvements, automation initiatives, and efficiency enhancements
✅ Support UAT activities and technology implementations
✅ Guide and mentor team members while maintaining high operational standards

🔹 Ideal Candidate
✔ Degree or Diploma in Finance, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field
✔ MBA or professional qualifications will be an added advantage
✔ 2-4 years of experience in Card Operations or Payment Operations
✔ Strong understanding of payment card industry practices and card schemes
✔ Analytical mindset with strong problem-solving capabilities
✔ Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
✔ Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills

At Dialog Finance, innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning drive everything we do. This is your opportunity to work with industry-leading professionals, contribute to transformative financial solutions, and grow your career in an exciting and evolving sector.
