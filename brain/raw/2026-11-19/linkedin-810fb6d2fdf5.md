TallyIra: How AI Inside TallyPrime is Saving Hours of Accounting Work

TallyIra is Tally's AI capability built into TallyPrime 7.1 and above. Its first feature, Docs by Ira, turns invoices, bills, and bank statements into draft vouchers automatically.

Just scan or upload a PDF/image from your desktop, WhatsApp, or the TallyIra mobile app . The AI reads party name, invoice number, GST, and line items, and prepares a GST-compliant entry in TallyPrime. You simply review and accept — nothing is posted without your approval.

For accounting teams drowning in manual data entry, this means up to 80% less time spent typing purchase and sales bills, fewer GST errors, and faster month-end closing. Field staff can send documents from mobile while in transit, and the back-office team can reconcile bank statements in minutes.

It's available on licensed TallyPrime with active TSS, with 100 free pages to start.
