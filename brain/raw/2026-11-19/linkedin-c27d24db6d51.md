PaperOrchestra is a multi-agent framework from Google Research that automates the end-to-end process of writing AI research papers. It transforms raw materials—ideas and experimental logs—into submission-ready LaTeX manuscripts with literature synthesis and generated plots.

→ The pipeline orchestrates five specialized agents: one generates outlines, another synthesizes literature from Semantic Scholar, a third writes sections, a fourth refines content, and a fifth generates figures from code.

→ It handles LaTeX automation to match target conference templates (CVPR 2025 included), closing the gap between scattered research notes and polished submissions.

→ Install with `pip install -r requirements.txt`, then run `python paper_writing_cli.py --raw_materials_dir /path --latex_template_dir /path --output_dir /path --use_plotting true` to generate your first paper.

→ Licensed under Apache-2.0. The dataset is not included; it will be released separately. Open issues suggest active development.

If you're drowning in research notes or tired of manual paper formatting, this saves weeks of structure and literature work.
