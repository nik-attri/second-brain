NOW THIS IS WHAT MAKES ALL THE HARD WORK WORTHWHILE! ❤️

What a day yesterday with the boys from OmniEngage!

We’ve been working with Daniel, Matthew, Ajmal and Ahmad on their strategy, positioning and rebrand, and yesterday we got to see it all come together at the Customer Experience Expo in London.

And bloody hell, it looked good! 😂

The new brand, the stand, the messaging, the conversations happening around it. Just brilliant seeing something you’ve helped create actually out there doing its job.

But that’s only half the story.

Because after a full day of talking business, technology, AI, customers and everything in between, we all headed out for a few bevvies and a cracking Chinese.

The whole squad together.

Plenty of laughs, a bit of banter, and some really good conversations that had absolutely nothing to do with work.

And I was thinking afterwards how much I love this part of what we do.

We didn’t feel like consultants working for a customer.

We felt like mates who happen to be building something pretty special together.

And that’s not something you can put in a proposal, stick on an invoice or measure on a spreadsheet.

You have to earn it.

By doing what you say you’ll do, giving a damn, being there when it matters and getting to know the actual people behind the business.

It’s how I reckon business SHOULD be done.

Massive congratulations to Daniel, Matthew, Ajmal, Ahmad and the whole Omni Engage team.

You’ve got something really exciting here, lads, and we’re chuffed to bits to be involved.

GOOD BUSINESS. GOOD PEOPLE. AND A BLOODY GOOD CHINESE.

That’ll do me. 😂❤️
