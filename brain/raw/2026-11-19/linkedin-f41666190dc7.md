Welcome to the team, Juul Nijs!

He'll also take on a timely topic for some of our clients: Box 3, the upcoming Dutch tax on investments. The Netherlands is currently reforming how it taxes #wealth, including #property, potentially including taxes on #unrealised #gains stemming form the change in value.

For Dutch owners of holiday apartments in Tyrol, that might raise a profound question: 

What is the property currently worth and how is it going to be taxed? 

As a native Dutch speaker who lives and studies in Innsbruck, Juul is well placed to help bridge these two markets. 🇳🇱🇦🇹
