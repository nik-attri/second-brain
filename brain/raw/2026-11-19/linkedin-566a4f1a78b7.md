Tomorrow in Helsinki: Let's Solve Hydrogen!

Green hydrogen is difficult enough to produce. Why should proving that it's green be so complicated?

Tomorrow, I'll be presenting CibusCell Technology approach to an interesting challenge from Helen at Let's Solve Hydrogen in Helsinki.

The challenge: RFNBO compliance and greenhouse gas intensity.
Hydrogen operators need to demonstrate where their electricity comes from, how their electrolysers operate, whether renewable energy requirements are fulfilled and what the actual carbon intensity of their hydrogen is. And they need to provide reliable, traceable evidence. Today, much of this process still requires substantial manual work.

Our idea at CibusCell is simple: Let's automate it.
By connecting operational plant data with intelligent calculations and compliance workflows, we want to make RFNBO assessments, GHG calculations and certification reporting significantly easier.

Not another isolated reporting tool, but a connected process from plant operations to auditable evidence.

For me, this is a great example of how AI and automation can create real value beyond optimizing energy costs.

Because scaling green hydrogen also means scaling the processes that prove it's green.
