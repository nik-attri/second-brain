Do you know how terrifying it is to be a single point of failure???

Imagine taking a job because you desperately want to help rural micro-entrepreneurs build their businesses. You want to be a strategic mentor. You want to look them in the eye and talk about real growth. You want to help their community grow and be self-sustaining. You want to make a difference. 

But instead... you spend hours a day answering the exact same question about fertilizer ratios. For the fiftieth time this month.

You love these people. You really do! And the organizations employing you are doing everything they can to support the communities. But expecting one human being to act as a 24/7 technical helpdesk for 200 different businesses is a recipe for complete burnout.

You are drowning in routine questions, completely trapped in reactive mode.
That is the entire heartbeat of why we built iNua.

We created a tireless, 24/7 AI assistant that knows the entire training manual by heart. It lives right on the basic offline channels your community already uses. When an entrepreneur has a routine question at 8 PM, they don't have to guiltily text their exhausted mentor. They just ask the AI in their native language, and get an instant answer.

We built this so the heroes in the field can finally catch their breath. So they can stop acting like a human instruction manual, and actually get back to mentoring.
