Imagine a company that takes five steps to approve a simple request.

An employee submits a form.

The manager downloads it, signs it, and emails it to Finance.

Finance copies the information into a spreadsheet.

Someone else updates the tracking system.

Then the employee sends another email asking whether it was approved.

Now the company decides to automate the process.

Great idea.

Except nobody asks why five steps were needed in the first place.

So the developers build a system that does exactly what everyone was doing manually.

Only faster.

And suddenly the company has a very expensive way to make five unnecessary steps happen automatically. 😂

I think this is one of the most important questions to ask before building or buying software:

If we were designing this process from scratch today, would we still do it this way?

Sometimes the biggest improvement comes before anyone writes a single line of code.

A clearer process can be more valuable than a complicated system built around a problem nobody stopped to question.
