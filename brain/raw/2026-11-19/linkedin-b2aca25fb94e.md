Hey Meta for Business if you're reading this. 👀 
As a Social Media professional, you need to make our lives easier 🙏. 
So here are 10 things I wish Meta Business Suite would improve:

1. Scheduling Stories with interactive stickers. Polls, quizzes, question boxes, the music library. I wish we could use it without having to go back into Instagram and do everything manually. 

2. The whole login and account access system. Why do I need to connect a real human Facebook profile to properly manage enterprise business accounts? And why does switching between accounts sometimes feel like solving a puzzle?

3. The verification process.  Between two-factor authentication, identity verification, business verification and random security checks, accessing an account can become a job in itself. And the support? A nigthtmare.

4. More consistent reporting data. Why the numbers can't stay consistent?  😭

5. A less chaotic Ads Manager dashboard. I shouldn't have to navigate five different places just to find the information I need.

6. More reliable scheduling. Yes, we have other platforms to do that because yours have failed.

7. The same publishing features as the apps. If I can do something directly in the app, why can't I do it through Business Suite?

Don't get me wrong, Meta Business Suite is useful, especially considering it's "free".

But when you manage multiple accounts, plan campaigns, analyse performance and report results, these little limitations quickly become hours of unnecessary work and reliability in 100 other platforms. Shoutout to Metricool and Sprout Social, love you guys!

And considering how much businesses invest in Meta's platforms, I think it's fair to expect a better experience for the people managing them.

Fellow social media professionals, what would you add to the list?

Meta, if you're listening... 👀
