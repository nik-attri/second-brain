730 Day 1s and counting. 🎉
 
Two years ago today I showed up at Amazon Web Services (AWS) with a fresh badge, a laptop I couldn't log into, and the confidence of someone who was pretty sure Day 1 ended at 5 PM.
 
Two years later, it's still Day 1. (Except now I say "two-way door" when I can't pick a show on Prime Video.)
 
Two years by the numbers: 📅 730 Day 1s (and not a single Day 2, I checked) 🤝 More customer meetings than my calendar can legally hold ☕ An amount of cold brew I'll only share with my doctor 📚 About 2,000 new acronyms, 1,400 of which I use confidently ✈️ Enough airport time to have a favorite gate 💬 Countless "quick questions" that were never actually quick
 
The real highlight has been the people.
 
To the Solutions Architects: you're the ones who say "let me draw that out" and somehow turn a messy whiteboard into a working solution. You earn customers' trust, you tell them the truth, and you stay until the problem is solved. One SA in particular was next to me for almost this whole ride. He always did right by our customers, and he taught me more than he'll ever take credit for.
 
To my leaders: thank you for the trust, the coaching, and the room to run. Leaders who hop on a plane to sit across from your customers are rare. Mine do it, and our customers notice.
 
To my team: you're basically the Avengers, just with more spreadsheets. Nobody here says "not my account." If someone posts "anyone have a minute?" in the chat, five people show up. We celebrate the wins, cover for each other on the rough days, and keep raising the bar.
 
To my customers: thank you for your trust. Consider my gratitude officially stored in S3 Glacier. It's deep, durable, and never going anywhere. 🧊
 
If you've been part of these two years, you know who you are. Thank you. 🙏
 
Year 3, let's go. Still Day 1. Still caffeinated. 🚀☕
