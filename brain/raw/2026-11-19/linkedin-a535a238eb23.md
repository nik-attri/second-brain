My quote almost killed my deal.

Here’s what happened.

The product was right. The pricing was right.

The prospect was ready to move.

Then he forwarded my quote to his finance team.

They came back with three questions.

“Why is this in English? Our invoicing needs to be in Italian.”

“The currency says USD. We pay in euros.”

“Which legal entity is this coming from?”

Nothing about the product.

Nothing about the price.

All about the quote.

The deal sat for two weeks while I rebuilt everything.

New document in Italian. New currency. New entity header.

I did it manually. It took longer than it should have.

That’s when I realized something.

The quote is the first signal that you can operate in their market.

If it shows up in the wrong language or the wrong currency, the buyer doesn’t think “admin error.”

They think “this company can’t do business here.”

Now I create quotes on DealHub.ai

I don’t rebuild anything. I select the market and it’s done.

Same pricing rules behind every version.

One quote. Their language. Their currency. The right entity.

Cross-border buyers test you before they buy from you.

The quote is the test.

What’s the smallest thing that ever cost you a deal?
