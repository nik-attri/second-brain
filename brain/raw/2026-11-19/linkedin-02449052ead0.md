Most advertisers mistake Google Ads impression share for true market presence—but they measure very different things.

Impression share only shows your performance in auctions where you’re eligible, ignoring competitors bidding aggressively outside your reach. Meanwhile, paid search share of voice tracks every advertiser’s actual ad appearances across a defined keyword set and locations, revealing who really dominates the market.

By scanning consistent keywords regularly and from multiple geographies, you can map competitive visibility trends—not just isolated snapshots.

Want to know how to build this competitive intelligence systematically (without drowning in manual searches)? This insight reshapes how we think about paid search strategy and brand defense. 🔍
