We're Hiring | Project Manager – MEP
  Company: Laith Electromechanical L.L.C. (LEM) 
 Location: Abu Dhabi, UAE
  Position: Project Manager – MEP 
 Employment Type: Full-Time 
We are seeking an experienced and results-driven MEP Project Manager to lead and manage electromechanical projects, ensuring successful execution within approved timelines, budgets, quality standards, and HSE requirements. 

 Key Responsibilities

 • Lead and manage complete MEP project execution, including Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Fire Fighting, Fire Alarm, and ELV systems.
 • Ensure compliance with contract specifications, approved drawings, project requirements, and applicable UAE regulations.
 • Coordinate technical submissions, shop drawings, material approvals, method statements, and authority approvals. 
• Prepare and monitor project schedules, budgets, manpower histograms, procurement plans, and resource allocation.
 • Supervise site operations, subcontractors, project engineers, and multidisciplinary teams.
 • Manage procurement coordination, material delivery, subcontractor performance, and project resources.
 • Monitor project costs, cash flow, payment valuations, variations, and contractual obligations. 
• Ensure compliance with QA/QC requirements, HSE regulations, and ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 standards.
 • Coordinate with clients, consultants, authorities, and internal departments to ensure smooth project execution.
 • Oversee testing, commissioning, inspections, documentation, and final project handover. 

 Qualifications & Requirements 

• Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical, Electrical, or a relevant Engineering field. 
• 10–15 years of experience in MEP construction, including a minimum of 5 years in project management. 
• Proven experience in handling large-scale MEP projects in the UAE. 
• Strong knowledge of UAE authority requirements, including ADDC/TAQA, Civil Defence, DMT/Municipality, and other relevant authorities. 
• Strong knowledge of MEP systems, project planning, cost control, contract administration, and QA/QC procedures.
• Excellent leadership, communication, negotiation, and problem-solving skills.
 • Ability to manage multiple stakeholders and deliver projects within approved budgets and schedules.
