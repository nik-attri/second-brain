If lessons learned are simply created, filed and forgotten, were they ever actually learned?

Imma say no.

Despite the multicoloured Post-its, endless workshop discussions and the beautifully formatted spreadsheet, we haven't learned a bloody thing.

Exhibit A: Corporate Karen has just walked into the EXACT same organisational car crash we spent three months documenting, dissecting and solemnly swearing - with Brownie Guide Promises - we'd never repeat.

But, alas, here we are...Same problems. Same governance nonsense. Same blind eye to what happened before.

New programme logo, though. So that's nice.

In a 3 am fever dream, I've written here about the curious and ironic organisational ritual of "Lessons Learned" - our collective ability to document failure with forensic precision, and our heroic determination to repeat it.

And why, perhaps, the first question at your next lessons learned workshop should be: "What the hell happened to the lessons we learned last time?"

If the answer is "They're on SharePoint somewhere", I fear we may have identified the problem.
