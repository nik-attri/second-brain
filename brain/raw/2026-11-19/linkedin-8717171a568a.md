💻 A customer has 10 transactions. How would you find just their latest one using SQL?

Imagine analysing customer purchase history.

A customer may place multiple orders over several months, but sometimes the business needs only the most recent transaction for each customer.

How do we solve this without manually sorting and filtering every customer?

This is where SQL Window Functions come in!

Using:
🔹 PARTITION BY – Separates records customer by customer.
🔹 ORDER BY – Arranges transactions from newest to oldest.
🔹 ROW_NUMBER() – Assigns a sequential number within each customer group.

Finally, filtering for rn = 1 gives us the latest order for each customer.

📊 Where is this useful?
🔹 Identifying the latest customer transaction.
🔹 Tracking the most recent patient appointment.
🔹 Finding the latest employee record.
🔹 Analysing recent sales activity.

💡 Sometimes, the challenge isn't finding all the data. It's finding exactly the record that matters.
