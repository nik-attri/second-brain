The fastest way to get a well paid marketing engineer job (150-300k per year) is to build one marketing system that runs by itself.

Not a certificate. One working system you can show, with some tangible results.

From 612 job ads we have analyzed. There are three places to start:

1. A campaign engine. Half the ads ask for campaign, lifecycle or email work. One member, who co-founded a two-person agency, took their website copy from a whole day to about an hour.

2. A content engine. A quarter of the ads ask for content. The part that matters is the review step before anything goes live. A senior copywriter in our community had theirs add a compliance check, and said: "I usually do that type of QA work manually."

3. AI visibility. 15% of the ads ask for SEO or AI search work. One member, a fractional CMO, checked what AI tools say about their firm: 53% right. They fixed the sources, and a week later it was 71%. 

That's what a hiring manager wants to see.
