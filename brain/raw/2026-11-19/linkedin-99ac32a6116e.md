​10 minutes fighting an automated phone menu and a useless AI bot...
Just to get dumped overseas onto a burnt-out rep who doesn't understand you...

Who you can barely understand...
Only for the line to drop—forcing you to do the whole circus over again.

They built it to protect a spreadsheet while cashing your monthly bill.

→ Three repeat callbacks
→ One expensive stateside escalation
→ One broken, revolving-door BPO floor
→ One loyal customer walking out the door forever

​The fix is right in front of their faces—they just sit too far from the phone lines to see it.
