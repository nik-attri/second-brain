The #Hiring Slop: 
High-Ticket Bait, Paid CV Arbitrage, and "Audit Clearance" Postings. 

Old school approach with new concerns. Over the past two years, a predatory trend has accelerated across the LinkedIn platform  within the #Businessdevelopment #strategy, corporate #sustainability, #ESG, and #GHG management roles. 
Having been approached directly by several unverified entities, head hunters  with requests to evaluate senior opportunities, backed by an inbox full of direct evidence the operational pattern is clear. This isn't recruitment noise; We understand the lead gen, but is this how it should be? it is structured data-harvesting exploiting a sector navigating real market pressures. 

How the pipeline operates:
1️⃣The High-Ticket "Director / CSO" Trap: Synthetic accounts post attractive leadership roles (e.g., Chief Sustainability Officer/Strategy Director) complete with top-tier compensation packages. These listings stem from micro-entities (<2 or No employees) created solely to harvest practitioner data.

2️⃣The "5-Minute Review" Funnel: Upon receiving your CV, an automated response praises your background as "impeccable," followed immediately by: "Your profile needs rework or say won't pass ATS/client filters without our professional rework."
Paid Editing Arbitrage: You are funneled to paid CV writing services. The recruiter collects a kickback, your contact details are sold to lead pools, and the role vanishes.

3️⃣"Audit-Clearance" Ghost Listings: Even  Roles posted by "established listed firms"  opening and closing within 30 minutes reflected on their career page. These are often pre-allocated internally or listed strictly to satisfy audit compliance, creating fake market liquidity. 

THIS COULD BE NORMAL. But there are several entry level candidates desperate to launch their career impacted by this trend. 

Protecting the Ecosystem:
- Enforce a Zero-Fee Rule: Legitimate MENA employers and search firms never charge candidates for applications, formatting, or placement.
- Audit the Entity: Check commercial registries and domains before releasing detailed career records.
- Validate via Network: Cross-check advertised mandates with trusted industry peers.
