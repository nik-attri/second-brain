"Tracy, I want to work with you in The Steward and Grow Group Accelerator. I see the value in what we'd do, and I'm SO SICK of this money stress. It's just it feels like a lot of money."

I nodded with compassion.

The woman in front of me was carrying so much guilt around her finances. But she'd watched her own sister get out of debt and build a six-month emergency fund after working with me. And she was hungry to lay down her old money habits, live debt free, and finally learn to manage her money God's way.

"I totally get that," I said. "It DOES feel like a lot, especially if you're not sure where your money's been going, and a budget hasn't worked before. I'm curious. What do you feel would be different about coaching than what you've already tried?"

She looked thoughtful.

"I'd have someone to guide me. Someone I could ask questions, who's done this before and knows what they're doing. A second set of eyes on my finances. And someone to teach me what I don't even know I don't know."

I smiled. "You're exactly right. So… what do you feel the Lord is asking you to do?"

She was quiet for a moment. "He's telling me I need the help. And that He'll provide."

And you know what happened?

God SHOWED UP for her. Even when unexpected expenses and car repairs hit. For the first time, she paid every bill on time. She saved a $1,000 emergency fund. She started making real headway on her debt.

But the most importantly, the first time ever, she felt HOPE in her finances the MOMENT she said yes to investing in herself and walking in obedience to what the Lord was asking of her.

And she isn't the only one.

But they knew something had to change. What they'd tried on their own hadn't worked; the thought of another year exactly like this one scared them more than the leap did.

So they made a decision: trust the Lord to provide, and trust the process. Here's what happened.

After ONE month of coaching, they had $1,000 EXTRA in their bank account. Even with the coaching payment already taken out.

Sit with that for a second.

Thirty days in, the thing they were scared they couldn't afford had already found them money they didn't know they had, without increasing their paychecks. Just from learning to steward what was already passing through their hands.
