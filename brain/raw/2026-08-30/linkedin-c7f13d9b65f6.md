Yesterday I described a facilities company drowning in manual CV screening. Here's what we built to fix it.
Candidates now land on a branded careers page, upload their CV, and have a real conversation with an AI screening agent — questions about experience, availability, salary expectations, and a competency question specific to the role.
Their CV gets read and scored by AI. Their conversation gets scored too. Once enough people have applied for a role, the system automatically ranks everyone, sends HR a shortlist of the top 10 on Slack, and emails each shortlisted candidate a confirmation.
No spreadsheet. No manual reading. No good candidate left waiting two weeks for a reply.
What used to take a full week of someone's attention now runs quietly in the background while HR does literally anything else.
This is the kind of system I build — not a chatbot for the sake of having one, but automation that removes a bottleneck that was genuinely costing the business good hires.
