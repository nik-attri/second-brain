What if Saturday morning was designed on purpose?

I cannot wait to build the Bar’N.

Not because I need another gym, another garage or another place to store bikes. The idea is much bigger than that. I want to create a place that makes it easier for families to live well together.

Picture a Saturday morning in Bella Vista. A few families show up early. We start with a neighborhood WOD, then head out for a trail run, mountain bike ride or gravel loop. When everyone gets back, the grill is hot, the music is on, the kids are around, and nobody is in a hurry to leave.

That sounds like fun, but it is also intentional development.

The place matters. The people matter. The activities matter. When those three work together, health improves, relationships deepen, memories get made and better living becomes something we actually experience instead of something we keep talking about.

That is what I want the Bar’N to become.

A place for training, adventure, hospitality, recovery, work, music, food and family. Not a lifestyle showroom. A living lab where we can learn what actually helps families live, work and play better together.

And before we ever build the ultimate version, I think the smarter move is to start testing the rhythm now.

WOD. Trail or ride. Grill and chill.

Invite a few families. See who shows up. See who brings their spouse. See who comes back. Learn what makes people healthier, happier and more connected.

Maybe that is also how we find the right families for DEVCO.

Not by inventing avatars in a spreadsheet, but by creating the kinds of places and experiences we want to share, then paying attention to the people who want to build that kind of life too.

Better living should be designed, but it should also be a hell of a lot of fun to live.
