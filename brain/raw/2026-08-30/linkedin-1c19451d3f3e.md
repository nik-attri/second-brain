Work smarter, not harder—even when it comes to your professional image? 💻✨

I recently realized that my last haircut was back in October 2025, and my hair color was done in December 2025. In the physical world, maintenance takes time. But in the digital space? Efficiency is everything. 

While I still use unfiltered photos from time to time, I’ve found myself mostly preferring subtle digital filters. The reason is simple: it completely eliminates the need for manual makeup. 

To me, this isn’t about hiding—it's a productivity hack. In a fast-paced professional landscape, leveraging smart digital tools to look polished saves precious time. We automate our workflows, optimize our calendars, and streamline our businesses. There is no reason we can't use tech shortcuts to streamline how we present ourselves online, too. 

Where do you draw the line between digital convenience and real-life authenticity on a platform like LinkedIn?
