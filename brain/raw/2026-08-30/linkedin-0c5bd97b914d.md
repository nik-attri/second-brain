At Every Inc., the media side was the top of funnel. A separate consulting arm did the paid work underneath it.

Eleanor Warnock, our latest Offcuts guest, spent years inside that model before becoming an advisor to Bek Ventures. She joined Tim Richardson this past week to talk about where journalism's business is heading, and where AI actually earns a place in her own work.

On the industry: Eleanor thinks it's splitting in two. A handful of giants like the New York Times and the FT on one side. A long tail of one-person newsletters on the other. The middle, where local and mid-size outlets used to sit, isn't coming back, and she doesn't think AI fills that gap either.

On her own workflow: she runs separate Claude projects for her newsletter, her stealth company and her Japanese-language writing, and automates transcript cleanup and headlines without a second thought. The one thing she won't hand over is research before an interview, because she's tried it and walked into a conversation underprepared.
