Dropbox and Excel are my biggest competitors (combined value of over $4 trillion 😅)

But when it comes to managing construction approvals, I reckon we smoke them.

They do the job, but it takes a hell of a lot of teamwork and effort. Not to mention everyone is working off their own versions of a spreadsheet.

But what if the builder, certifier and consultants could all work together in the same live workflow?

That's what we've built at DocuBuild. Three different views, all connected in one place.

Pretty cool seeing the difference it makes to collaboration and visibility.
