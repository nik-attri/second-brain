Corporate payments are still shockingly manual. And that's the problem AccessPay CEO Johan Jardevall wants to fix first.

In our latest conversation, Johan makes a compelling case: before we rush to overlay AI on treasury operations, we need to get the basic infrastructure right. Too many finance teams are still wrestling with fragmented systems and manual processes that should have been automated years ago.

His perspective on corporate banking? It's lagging behind consumer expectations by a mile. AccessPay is bridging that gap as the UK's leading corporate-to-bank connectivity platform, helping treasury teams finally escape spreadsheet hell.

The takeaway: transformation starts with solid foundations, not shiny tech.
