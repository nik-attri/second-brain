AI is powerful. But it can't replace the human touch.

These days, AI-generated designs are everywhere: logos, posters, social media creatives, even videos. For many of us, AI has become part of our daily workflow.

But I believe something is about to shift.

When everyone uses the same tools, everything starts to look the same. The novelty fades, and people begin to crave something real: a hand-drawn illustration with its small imperfections, a video edit with personality and storytelling instincts, a design that carries a human point of view.

I'm not against AI. We need it. It saves time, speeds up ideas, and handles the repetitive work brilliantly.

But some things simply get better when they're created manually. Emotion, originality, and craft can't be prompted into existence.

The future isn't AI or manual. It's knowing when to use each one.

Use AI for speed. Use human creativity for soul.

What do you think? Will people eventually return to manual design and editing, or will AI take over completely?
