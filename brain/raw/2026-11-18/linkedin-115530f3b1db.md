One thing I believe we need to talk about more in data protection is the human cost of processing Subject Access Requests.

Behind every SAR is often a cross-functional team of highly skilled professionals, from privacy and legal to HR and IT, all contributing time and expertise to get the response right.

But at what cost? Hours spent manually searching, reviewing and redacting information. The pressure of statutory deadlines. The fear of making a mistake. And, in some cases, the emotional challenge of handling requests involving people you know personally.

Meanwhile, the work privacy professionals are actually employed to do doesn't stop. DPIAs, breach investigations, privacy strategy, employee awareness and so much more.
I think we need to challenge the idea that this level of manual effort is simply an unavoidable part of the process.

At Aetherien, we believe technology should help organisations manage and simplify this burden more intelligently, while allowing people to focus on the expertise and judgement that really matter.

It's not just about making SAR processing more efficient. It's about making better use of the people, skills and knowledge organisations already have.

We've explored this in our latest article, and I'd be interested to hear how others are experiencing this challenge.
