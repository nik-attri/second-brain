Lets be honest.

You are not operating in the same world you were operating in 20 years ago. Hell, not even five years ago.

The way business is done has changed. The way information moves has changed. The speed at which decisions need to be made has changed.

But some construction businesses are still trying to manage millions of rands with information across different people, files, phones and spreadsheets.

And the owner is often the last person to know what is actually happening. That is not a small inconvenience. It is a competitive disadvantage.

While one business is waiting for reports, another can see its financial position in seconds.

While one owner is making calls to find out what is happening, another can open a phone and see what has been spent, earned, certified, paid, delayed and exposed in real time.

That is where construction is heading.

Every serious industry is moving towards connected, real-time digital intelligence. Construction cannot keep pretending it is exempt.

You cannot build a modern construction business using yesterday’s operating methods and expect tomorrow’s results.

The question is no longer whether technology belongs in construction. The question is how much longer you can afford to operate without it.

Because your competitors are not going to wait for you to catch up.
