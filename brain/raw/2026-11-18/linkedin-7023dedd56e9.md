Anyone who’s ever managed home collection knows the monthly payout cycle can quietly turn into a source of tension. Phlebotomists want to feel the numbers are fair, finance wants clean reports, and the lab just wants the whole thing to stop eating up so much time.
Storing the visit charge and per-km rate on the profile and letting the system do the rest is such a simple but powerful shift. Clear numbers for everyone involved really does end most of the friction.
We’d be curious for those of you still calculating this manually or in spreadsheets, what’s the biggest headache you face every month? And for anyone who’s already automated it, what changed the most once the process became transparent?
Appreciate you sharing this. Operational clarity like this makes a bigger difference than people realise.
