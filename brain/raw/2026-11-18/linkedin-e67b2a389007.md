You can write whatever the hell you like in your LinkedIn bio. 

Systems thinker.
Customer obsessed.
Commercial.
Visionary.
An AI-native commercial leader who operates at the intersection of whatever.

Lovely.

The interesting bit is whether the people who’ve seen you under pressure, behind on target, five tabs deep in a spreadsheet and trying to work out why the numbers have gone feral say the same thing.

For reasons that seemed sensible at the time, I recently asked a bunch of people I really respect to submit written evidence about what I’m like to work with.

An accidental external 360, if you will.

Unfortunately, their stories matched.

> systems thinker.
> pushes people.
> brings everyone with her.
> raises the bar.
> follows the data (even when the data is being deeply inconvenient)

That’s the bit I care about. Because you can write whatever you like about yourself.

But when people from completely different chapters of your career independently describe the same person?

That’s reputation.

Which is handy, because tonight I’m going to the awards I collected all this evidence for.

So I’ll shortly be standing in a room full of brilliant people, pretending to be a normal amount of chill about being shortlisted alongside people I really rate.

Pray for ya girl 💖
