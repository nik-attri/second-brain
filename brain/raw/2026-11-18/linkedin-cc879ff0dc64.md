As a small company, finding the right public sector tenders takes up a lot of our time as: 

a) most procurement portals are slow and outdated, so getting to what you need takes ages
b) there isn't a single consolidated procurement portal (why not??) so you have to have to maintain a number of separate logins
c) most of the useful information is then separated into different files with varying formats so you have to go through each one manually and extract the info

Although there are many other services out there that can assist with this, they tend to cost a lot of £££ per month.   Then Aleksandrs P. came to me and said, "we build lots of useful tools for our clients, why can't we do it for ourselves?".  So naturally, it got added to his to-do list 😜 

Over the next few weeks we're going to share more tools that we've built internally to "show not tell" what we do, and would be curious to see if others would find them useful!
