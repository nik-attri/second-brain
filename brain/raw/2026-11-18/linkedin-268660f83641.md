I was busy collating data for a presentation when I suddenly remembered something: I don't have to do everything the hard way. 



There was a day at the office when I was asked to prepare slides for a presentation.

I was still collating and organising the data that would go into the slides, and there were other tasks waiting for my attention.

Then it hit me.

Wait a minute! I know the tools to use. I know how to craft the right prompts. Why am I trying to do everything manually?

I immediately thought of Claude AI.

I structured my prompt, supplied the necessary information, and let Claude do its thing.

And voila! My slides were ready.

But let me emphasise something: I didn't just generate the slides and rush off to present them.

I reviewed the content, checked for errors, made necessary corrections and ensured the slides communicated exactly what I wanted.

And honestly, Claude did a remarkable job!

This experience reminded me of something I keep saying:

**AI is not here to take over your job. It is here to help you do your job better.**

As finance professionals, accountants and researchers, we have so much to do already. Learning how to use AI effectively can help us save time, improve productivity and focus more on the professional judgement that makes our work valuable.

But remember, AI can assist you; it cannot replace your responsibility to review, verify and own the final output.

So, are you still doubting the usefulness of AI?

Maybe it's time to stop seeing it as a threat and start learning how to work with it.
