🚀 WE’RE HIRING IN DUBAI (DIFC) — JOIN OLAM AGRI!

Olam Agri’s Risk Management Solutions (RMS) team is growing! We are looking for a  detail-oriented, analytical professionals to join our DFSA-regulated OTC Derivatives team in Dubai, covering commodity and FX derivatives (swaps, forwards, vanilla & exotic options, accumulators, barriers, and structured products).

Client Services Analyst (OTC Derivatives | KYC / AML / Trade Lifecycle)

📍 Location: Dubai, UAE (DIFC – DFSA Regulated Entity)
💼 Experience: 5–6 years in regulated financial services (at least 3 years in OTC derivatives operations or client services) [1]

Key Responsibilities:

Onboarding & Compliance: Own end-to-end onboarding readiness, KYC/AML reviews (CDD, EDD, UBO, PEP/sanctions screening), and DFSA Category 2 regulatory reporting.
2. Legal Documentation: Negotiate, review, and execute ISDA Master Agreements, Schedules, CSAs, Terms of Business, and give-up agreements.
3. Operations & Treasury: Manage daily trade lifecycle workflows, prepare T+1 and T+2 settlement/funding forecasts for Treasury, and execute margin and cash calls. 
4. Automation: Design and build Python and VBA tools (alongside Power Query/Power BI) to automate reconciliations, confirmation matching, and alerts under strict version control.

Qualifications & Skills:

Education: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business, Economics, Computer Science, or a related field.
 
Core Experience: Hands-on AML/KYC experience and deep familiarity with ISDA/CSA terms (close-out netting, thresholds, eligible collateral, give-up scope).
Technical Stack: Working experience in Python (pandas, NumPy, Git) and VBA (macros, Outlook automation), plus Advanced Excel.
Bonus Points: ICA/ACAMS certification (or CAMS in progress), commodities/agri-derivatives background, and DFSA rulebook knowledge (AML, GEN, COB modules).
