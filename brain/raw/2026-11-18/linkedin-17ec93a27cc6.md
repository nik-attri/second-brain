"Cinema upgrade ho gaya. Aap kab hoge?"

Saw this billboard and honestly, this question is quite interesting.

Think about it.

There was a time when we watched movies in black and white. Then came colour, bigger screens, better sound, VFX and now IMAX.

Things kept changing, and we adapted.

But when it comes to our everyday work, many of us are still doing things the same old way.

Same spreadsheets.
Same repetitive reports.
Same emails written manually again and again.

It's not that these things are difficult. It's just that sometimes we spend too much time doing things that could be done differently.

I think the bigger challenge today isn't just learning new technology. It's being willing to change the way we've been working for years.

AI is becoming a part of everyday work, whether we are ready for it or not.

Saw this creative by Be10x and it definitely got me thinking.

Maybe upgrading ourselves deserves as much attention as upgrading the technology around us.

What do you think?
