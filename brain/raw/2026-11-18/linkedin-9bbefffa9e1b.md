You should be able to request a product listing without agreeing to review emails.

We’ve added optional review-decision emails for guest founders on LaunchNest. The choice starts unchecked and sits separately from permission to publish the product. You can request a listing and follow its status through your private receipt without choosing email updates.

If you do choose them, an approval email includes the public listing link. A request for changes or a decline includes the reason and next step. You can also enable or stop future updates through your private receipt.

For teams building submission flows, that separation is worth considering. Someone may want their software listed while preferring to check the decision themselves. The request should still work for them.
