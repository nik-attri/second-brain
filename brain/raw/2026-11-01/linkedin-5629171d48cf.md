🚀 From an idea to a nationally ranked AI project — our journey at the Ziro National Agentic AI Hackathon 2026!
I'm thrilled to share that our team AUTO-PILOT secured National Qualification Rank #64, with an evaluation score of 132/180!
💡 Introducing: Competitor Changelog Scout
We built an AI-powered agent that automates competitor monitoring, helping product teams stay updated without manually going through multiple changelogs, blogs and release notes.
What does it do?
Automatically collects competitor updates.
Uses AI to classify them as Shipped, Announced, Renamed or Uncertain.
Stores structured intelligence in a central database.
Enables human approval before accepting updates.
Generates insights and weekly digests.
🛠️ Technology Stack
Frontend: React, TypeScript, JavaScript, CSS
Backend & Database: Supabase, PostgreSQL, Edge Functions
AI: Groq API
Automation: n8n
Deployment: Render | Version Control: GitHub
