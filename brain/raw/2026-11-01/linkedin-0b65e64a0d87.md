Lazy business marketers be like:

January
​"New Year, New Strategy! Is your business ready to smash its goals?"

​February
​"Fall in love with your business this Valentine’s Day!"

​March
​"Spring into success and clean up your CRM! Do it for your mum"

​April
​"No joke, our prices are ridiculously egg-cellent!"

​May
​"Bank Holiday Weekend! Time to kick back and automate your workflow."

​June
​"Sizzle into summer with hot new leads! Make dad proud."

​July
​"Don't let your sales suffer from the summer slump!"

​August
​"Back to Basics: Sharpen your skills before September!"

​September
​"Autumn leaves, autumn sales: Fall into big savings!"

​October
​"Spooktacular savings that won't haunt your budget!"

​November
​"Black Friday Countdown: The biggest deal of the century!"

​December
​"Unwrap your success this Christmas!"

Every. Single. Year.

Honestly, we need to do better. Can't we just market our businesses without all these cheesy gimmicks? Especially retail stores.

Now, before you moan.. if a season is relevant to your business (like selling flowers for Valentines Day), then go nuts. But maybe then give the 'spooktacular flowers' a miss on Halloween.

It seems people just rotate the same 12 months of adverts each year. Anyone else bored of the same cycle? No, just me?
