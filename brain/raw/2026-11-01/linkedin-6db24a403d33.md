I saw a poll GaryVee posted last week that made me stop scrolling.

The question was: "What would you do if money wasn't a factor?"

24% of people voted "Start a business."

At first I thought: how in the hell does that make sense? 

The whole reason most people put up with the stress of starting a business

is because of money.

And the funny thing is

if you changed the poll to the complete opposite

"What would you do if money WAS the only factor?"

those exact same people would still click "Start a business."

I think two very different groups of people clicked that button.

The first group is people who have never actually run a business, and they're just 

romanticizing the idea of being a founder because they watch GaryVee.

Give them $20 million tomorrow and they wouldn't touch a spreadsheet ever again.

The second group is the people who are actually wired wrong in the head

the ones who get bored after three days on vacation 

because they secretly need the uncertainty and a problem to fix just to feel normal.

For the founders in here:

 if your bank account was set for life tomorrow morning, which group are you actually in?
