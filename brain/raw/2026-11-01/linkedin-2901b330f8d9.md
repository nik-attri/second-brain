Just built and tested my first end-to-end AI voice agent workflow, and it completely changed how I look at business automation! 🚀
Here is how I set it up and how you can leverage it to create a powerful side income stream:
1. What is VAPI?
VAPI (Voice AI API) is an incredible developer platform that lets you build, deploy, and customize lightning-fast voice assistants in minutes. Think of it as the infrastructure that gives your AI a natural, conversational voice.
2. How to Build Your Voice Agent on VAPI
Set up your assistant model, prompt, and voice persona directly on the VAPI dashboard.
Connect your telephony or web client so customers can talk to the agent naturally in real-time.
Configure custom functions to let the agent trigger external workflows during a live conversation.
3. Supercharging with Zapier MCP (Model Context Protocol)
This is where the magic happens! By integrating a Zapier MCP server into the voice agent, the AI isn't just chatting—it's taking action. During my test use case, when a customer verbally confirmed an order over the call, the voice agent instantly triggered a Zapier workflow that fired off an automated confirmation email to the client's inbox in real-time. Seamless! ⚡
4. How to Turn This Into a Side Income
Local businesses (clinics, real estate agencies, e-commerce stores, service providers) are drowning in missed calls and manual booking confirmations. You can build these custom voice agents for them as a freelance service or agency offering, solving a massive pain point while building a scalable side income.
