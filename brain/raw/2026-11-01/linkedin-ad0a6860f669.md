Artificial Intelligence & The Stock Market: Where Technology Meets Financial Decision-Making

What happens when two of the most exciting areas of today’s world – Artificial Intelligence and the Stock Market – come together?

I recently had the privilege of delivering a session on “Artificial Intelligence in the Stock Market” at the Eastern India Regional Council (EIRC), Centre of Excellence, Kolkata, interacting with fellow Chartered Accountants.

It was a particularly memorable experience. I always feel special being at ICAI – my Alma Mater. Experiencing it as a faculty member at the Kolkata Branch makes it even more meaningful. There is immense satisfaction in sharing knowledge with the professional community that has shaped my journey.

AI in the Stock Market – Exploring the Possibilities

The way we analyse financial markets is evolving rapidly, and AI is emerging as a powerful tool for traders, investors and financial professionals.

During the session, we explored some practical applications of AI:

🔹 Technical Analysis: Identifying chart patterns, trends and potential trading opportunities.

🔹 Strategy Development: Converting trading ideas into rule-based strategies.

🔹 TradingView Automation: Using AI to write Pine Script, develop customised indicators and automate trading alerts.

🔹 Fundamental Analysis: Analysing financial statements, ratios and business performance.

🔹 Research & Automation: Processing market information, improving research efficiency and automating repetitive workflows.

One message I particularly wanted to emphasise was:

AI is not here to replace traders and investors; it is here to enhance their capabilities.

Technology can make analysis faster and workflows smarter, but financial judgement, discipline and risk management will always remain essential.
