🚀 Excited to share my project: AIHire – AI-Powered Smart Interview Assessment!
💻 This project focuses on building an AI-powered platform that automates and simplifies the candidate interview and evaluation process through a multi-modal assessment framework.
🤖 The system integrates multiple modules, including Resume Screening, AI-Based Interview Evaluation, Speech and Emotion Analysis, and Coding Assessment to evaluate candidates across different dimensions. It uses semantic language processing to assess interview responses, speech processing techniques to analyze communication, and automated coding assessment to evaluate technical skills.
✨ The platform provides an integrated assessment workflow with candidate performance reports, helping make the recruitment and evaluation process more structured and efficient.
