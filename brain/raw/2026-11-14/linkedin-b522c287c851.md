Your best engineers shouldn’t be maintaining an analytics layer.
 
Endpoint Clinical tried for 15 years. Then they embedded ThoughtSpot and launched Elo AI in about 3 months, with roughly 1.5 FTEs.
 
That’s the thread across our first Top ThoughtSpot Embedded Innovators (Fall 2026):
 
→ Endpoint Clinical: closed a 15-year analytics gap in 3 months
 → onlyfy by Xing: recruiters ask Mira instead of waiting on reports
 → Zencargo: customers are “addicted to Luca”
 → AskBosco: executive reporting from weeks to minutes
 → Gallus Insights: 200 dashboards replaced by one question
 → Elmo Software Group: the end of HR “spreadsheet hell”
 
Each decided what only they should build: their data, their domain logic, their customer experience. Then they embedded the rest.

Congrats to these teams, and thank you for building with us. 👏
