🚨 Before you automate tomorrow, do you actually know what’s running today?

It’s the weekend, so here’s a little FREE activity for Monday morning when you return to the office. ☕💻

And a small glimpse of what Aiology has to offer.

We’re all talking about Microsoft Copilot, AI agents and digital transformation. But there’s a question I think every CEO, Head of IT and leadership team should be asking:

How many automated processes are already running across your organisation, and does anyone have a complete picture of what they’re doing?

Think about it.

You’ve got Microsoft Teams for collaboration, SharePoint managing information, Power Automate running workflows, legacy systems still doing their thing, and now AI agents entering the workplace.

What happens when two systems start performing the same task? Or one automation triggers another, which triggers another?

Potentially duplicated work, conflicting processes, unnecessary costs and operational disruption.

Not exactly the frictionless office we were promised! 🤔

🔍 YOUR FREE MONDAY MORNING CHALLENGE

Grab a coffee, sit down with your Head of IT and try this:

1️⃣ Open the Microsoft Power Platform Admin Centre.

2️⃣ Review your existing Power Automate inventory.

3️⃣ Identify the owners, triggers and connected systems.

4️⃣ Look for duplicated workflows, failures and dependencies.

5️⃣ Ask whether your newer AI agents could be interacting with those same processes.

Start with a read-only review. No need to switch anything off.

You might discover everything is working beautifully.

Or you might uncover something nobody realised was happening.
