That renewal notice you archived in October? It's coming back as a charge.

A simple first Bot for any small business: once a month, Grok Bot (AI teammates you message like a coworker) searches your Gmail for receipts and renewal notices from the past year, then lists every subscription with its price, billing cycle and next renewal date. Price increases get flagged. The full list goes into a spreadsheet in Google Drive, and the top 5 to review land in your inbox.

Zachary Mitchell of Brainfinite AI suggests starting with one message and one rule: "Don't cancel anything." The Bot doesn't know which tools your team still opens, so it asks. The owner decides what stays.

Expect the first month's list to need a little cleanup (vendors that bill from two addresses, receipts sent to a teammate). It tightens up from there. Think of it as a starting point; every company handles bills a little differently, and fuller versions are on the way.

It needs Gmail and Google Drive connected.

Who on your team gets the renewal emails right now? Tag them.
