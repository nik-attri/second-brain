Your SACCO's loan book lives in a spreadsheet. You know this is a problem, but you haven't fixed it yet.

I built Akili Finance because I watched SACCO managers spend entire days manually entering member data, cross-referencing loan records, and stitching together compliance reports from five different places. The regulatory deadline hits, and someone's staying late to pull numbers that should have been automated months ago.

Here's what I learned: the cost of slow loan processing isn't just time. It's member frustration when approvals take two weeks. It's missed revenue because you can't see who's ready for a top-up. It's compliance risk because your PAR analysis lives in someone's head, not in a system.

Akili Finance (https://lnkd.in/d5xmB_F3) changes this. Query your member portfolios in plain English. Process loans with automated member risk scoring. Run SASRA compliance reports in minutes, not days. M-Pesa integration means payments hit your system automatically. Your entire member book lives in one place, and you can actually see what's happening.

The managers I've talked to went from processing loans in 3-5 days to same-day approvals. Their compliance team went from manual spreadsheet hell to automated reporting.

One question: How many days per month does your team spend on manual data entry and compliance reporting that a system should be doing?
