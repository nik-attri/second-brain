What happens in a NEXASEC web application test?

1. Scoping: we agree targets, test accounts and rules of engagement in writing
2. Recon: we map every page, parameter and API endpoint
3. Testing: authentication, access control, injection, business logic, file uploads
4. Validation: every finding is reproduced manually, no unverified scanner output
5. Report: clear risk ratings, proof, and fix steps your developers can follow
6. Retest: we confirm the fixes work

Testing your app before your customers' attackers do.
