“AI for travel” often brings a familiar picture to mind: a chatbot, an itinerary assembled from a gallery, or a template filled in manually—with smart features such as generating a link for the client to review, approve, or request changes.
I have the feeling that many advisors already using AI may not yet imagine what Bespoke Experiences Studio (BES) can do—or what a journey designed with it looks like. We built BES to use AI to research, create, and design. Starting with a brief, it combines services, places, and moments into a complete bespoke journey proposal, shaped around preferences, wishes, mood, and occasion.
For me, the exciting part is what a professional can do with that capability: explore possibilities, bring a personal vision to the proposal, and refine it through an understanding of the person it is being designed for.
That is what I mean when I say: AI is the advantage. Expertise is the difference. The technology expands what is possible; the advisor’s judgment gives it direction.
I believe the professionals who explore these capabilities will find new ways to distinguish their work. And that is why I keep coming back to this thought:

AI will not replace the advisor. The advisor who uses AI will replace the one who doesn’t.
