4 YEARS OF STUDY. 120 APPLICATIONS SENT. 1 TEMPLATE REJECTION. 📩

And honestly? That's the lucky outcome.

Most applications don't even get that. They get silence.

Here's what often happens behind the scenes: before a human reads your CV, an ATS parses it, filters it, and ranks it. If your file is built around course titles and buzzwords, it has nothing concrete to match against, and it sinks.

And when a recruiter does open it, they often spend only seconds on an entry-level CV. No proof of ability, no reason to stop.

So the "we've decided to move forward with other candidates" email isn't a verdict on your degree. It's what a CV with no verifiable evidence looks like from the other side.

How to break the cycle:

● Swap "Proficient in CAD and FEA" for links to public 3D assemblies and mesh convergence studies
● Swap "Passionate about propulsion" for real test data, or a documented simulation of a design you built
● Swap course lists for 2 well-documented projects showing end-to-end design trade-offs

Hiring teams don't interview promises. They interview demonstrable ability.
Stop sending silent documents. Start submitting proof.

How many applications did it take you to get your first interview? 👇
