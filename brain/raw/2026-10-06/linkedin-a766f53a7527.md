We recently integrated with RazorpayX Payouts for Naturelite Paints, and the experience was refreshingly simple. The APIs were genuinely plug-and-play and we've been able to automate our payout workflows without any hiccups.

The biggest surprise, though, was Ray on the new RazorpayX Agentic Dashboard.
I never thought AI would become a part of our banking workflow this soon. Instead of manually creating beneficiaries, navigating multiple screens, or clicking through approvals, It honestly feels like having an operations teammate built into our banking platform.

This is one of those products that makes you wonder why banking has always been so manual.

Kudos to Ayush Bansal, Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar for building something that genuinely feels ahead of its time.

Excited to see where this goes.
