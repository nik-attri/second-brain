Alcatel India needs an generalist HR, Asst Manager/Deputy Manager based at their Faridabad HQ.
Position Overview:

The incubement-Human Resources will support the end-to-end delivery of human resources activities at Alcatel HQ, Faridabad. The role will manage key HR operations, ensure timely payroll and statutory compliance, support talent acquisition, and provide reliable HR and management support. This is a full-time, on-site position.

Required Qualifications and Experience:

•MBA in Human Resources or an equivalent postgraduate qualification in HR.
•More than 5 years of relevant experience in HR generalist operations, talent acquisition, payroll coordination, and statutory compliance.
•Working knowledge of HR processes, employment documentation, payroll inputs, and compliance administration.
•Experience working with HRMS, payroll systems, recruitment platforms, and Microsoft Office or equivalent productivity tools.
•Strong written and verbal communication skills, stakeholder-management capability, and attention to detail.

Preferred Competencies:

• Ownership and ability to manage multiple HR priorities independently.
• Sound judgment when handling confidential employee information.
• Strong coordination and follow-through skills.
• Practical problem-solving and analytical ability.
• Ability to work effectively with employees, managers, vendors, consultants, and senior leadership.
• High standards of integrity, accuracy, and professional conduct.

Success Measures:

Success in this role will be measured through timely and accurate payroll inputs, effective closure of recruitment requirements, compliance discipline, reliable employee records, responsive HR operations, and the quality of support provided to management and employees.
