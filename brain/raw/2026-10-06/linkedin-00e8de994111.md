Unpopular opinion from something I am working on, the Happy path is probably the least interesting part of testing a new process or a system

On paper, most processes look simple. Then you talk to the people actually doing the work:
“Except sometimes…”
“For this customer it’s different.”
“We normally fix that manually”
“There’s an Excel for that.”
😂
I am seeing a lot of this while working on a Proof of Concept. And it’s taught me something:
Testing technology doesn’t just expose gaps in the system. It exposes how your business really works.
The unwritten rules, The Exceptions and Workarounds. The decisions everyone just knows to make.

So instead of only asking: “Does the system do what it’s supposed to?”
We should be increasingly interested in asking:
“This happened in the business yesterday. What would the system do?”

Because I am starting to think the exceptions tell you more about a business than the standard process does.
