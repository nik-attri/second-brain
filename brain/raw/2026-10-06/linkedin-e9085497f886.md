Hadrian raises $40 million, bringing total funding to $65 million!

HV portfolio Hadrian, the agentic AI offensive security platform, has announced a $40 million round which was co-led by Forgepoint Capital and Smartfin, with HV participating alongside existing investors Motive Partners and Picus Capital. The funding will support expansion across EMEA and the US.

The timing reflects where the threat landscape is heading. AI is helping attackers move faster, automate whole attack chains, and write zero-day exploits. Meanwhile:
➡️ 87% of organizations still run manual pentests
➡️ Only 0.47% of vulnerability scanner findings are genuinely exploitable
➡️ Security teams are drowning in alerts that need no action

Hadrian fixes that. Founded by Rogier Fischer, Olivier Beg and Maurice Clin, Hadrian gives security teams a hacker's-eye view of their own attack surface. Customers including TotalEnergies, Amadeus, Leroy Merlin and Damen Shipyards Gdynia S.A. have seen 10x greater visibility into critical risks, 80% faster time to resolution, and 5x ROI compared to manual pentesting.

Congratulations to Rogier, Olivier, Maurice and the entire Hadrian team!
