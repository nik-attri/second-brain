You are a highly skilled professional. 
So why are you still doing $15/hour work?

Think about it:

🩺 A physiotherapist spends 4 years studying human anatomy and rehabilitation. 
Yet, they spend 2 hours every morning playing phone tag, confirming appointments, and chasing no-shows.

💻 A web designer spends years mastering UX, conversion, and code. 
Yet, they spend their Friday nights manually formatting PDF proposals for leads that might never reply.

We romanticize the "hustle". We tell ourselves that "doing everything myself" shows dedication.

But here’s the brutal truth: 
Doing administrative tasks isn’t hustle. It’s a leak in your business model.

Every minute you spend on scheduling, data entry, or chasing paperwork is a minute stolen from:
→ Treating a patient who needs you.
→ Closing a high-ticket client.
→ Actually resting, so you don’t burn out.

The goal of modern software isn’t to replace the human expert. 
It’s to "fire" you from the tasks that drain your energy, so you can finally do the high-value work you actually studied for.

Automation isn’t about being lazy. It’s about respecting your own expertise enough to protect your time.

Question for my fellow professionals:
What is the one "$15/hour" task you are still doing manually, even though you know you should automate it? 

Be honest. I’ll start in the comments. 👇
