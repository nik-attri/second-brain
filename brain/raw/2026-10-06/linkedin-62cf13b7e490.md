Job Title: Planning and Procurement Engineer
Location: Johar Town, Lahore
Job Type: Full-time

About the Job:
We are hiring an engineer with a background in Mechatronics (or related fields) to support our solar installation projects. This role covers material procurement planning, technical sales facilitation, basic AutoCAD design, and daily system data management.

Responsibilities:
   - Procurement: Prepare accurate Bills of Quantities and Materials (BOQ/BOM) and coordinate orders with equipment suppliers.
Sales Support: Perform system sizing and prepare technical proposals and quotations for clients.
   - Data Entry: Enter project records, orders, and inventory data into our internal management system.
   - Office & Tracking: Maintain costing sheets, purchase trackers, and reports using MS Excel.
   - Design: Create basic solar layouts and schematics using AutoCAD.

Requirements:
   - Education: Bachelor’s degree in Mechatronics, Electrical, or related engineering field.
   - Experience: Fresh to 1–2 years of experience, Candidates with prior experience in the Solar Systems will be preferred.

Technical Skills:
   - Strong command of MS Excel and the MS Office suite.
   - Basic working knowledge of AutoCAD (2D drawings/layouts).
