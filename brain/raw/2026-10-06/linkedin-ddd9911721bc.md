Stop treating your ad performance review like a full-time data science project. I see so many D2C founders drowning in platforms that offer 50 different attribution models and cohort analyses when all they really want to know is one thing: is my money making money? We built AdPulse because the industry overcomplicated the basics. You don't need a tool that requires a certification to navigate. You need a single, clean dashboard that pulls your spend, ROAS, CPA, and CTR from Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn into one place. No more manual exports. No more living in spreadsheets every Monday morning. And when you have a question—like 'Which channel had the best efficiency yesterday?'—you can just ask the AI assistant in plain English instead of hunting through endless rows of data. You don't need a massive enterprise analytics suite to manage your growth. You just need to know where your ad budget is actually going. Keep your stack simple and your focus on the bottom line. #Ecommerce #D2C #MarketingAnalytics
