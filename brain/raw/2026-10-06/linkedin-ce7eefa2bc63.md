I don’t want to use AI to build a better version of yesterday’s company.

Or shall I better say "Super Intelligence" 😉 

That was probably the most important thing I wanted to say on the stage - Monday 14.09.2026 on the Handelsblatt Live stage in Düsseldorf.

Most "AI transformation" still starts with the existing company.

Here is the process.
Here is the workflow.
Here is the organization.

Now make it intelligent.

But even if we re-engineer the process, automate it, agentify it and make it autonomous...

...we are still starting from something we already know.

And the future, by definition, contains things we don’t know yet.

So at ALYN AI, with our clients, we work in the opposite direction.

We start with a "future capability" that does not exist yet.

What should this company be able to see, understand, decide or create that it cannot today?

What new product, interaction or even business model becomes possible if that capability exists?

Then we build backwards.

That is what "designing companies from the future backwards" means to me.

And what happened after this talk made me ridiculously happy.

People came to me with mathematics. Physics. Inverse problems. Intelligence. Their own visions. Their own questions.

Not "Which process should we automate?”

But:

- How do we make THAT future possible?
- What does that mean in reality?
- How are you realizing this approach with real companies of today?

That shift is everything.

I prefer the incredibly exciting unknown of the future to the comfort of optimizing what we already know.

And maybe the companies that matter most in the next decade won’t be the ones best prepared to survive the future.

Maybe they’ll be the ones "intelligently adaptive enough to choose which future they want to make real."

It will all come down to this:

Are you actively choosing your future?
Or you're just hoping you'll arrive at a good one?

There is a huge difference between the two.
It's just a different approach of looking at things at the end of the day.
