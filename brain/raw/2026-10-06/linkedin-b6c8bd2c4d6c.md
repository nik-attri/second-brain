Five years ago we bet on three founders in a sector nobody wanted to touch.

Today that bet paid off. Morphosis Capital has signed the full exit of Stay Fit Gym, which joins forces with 18GYM România, backed by Enterprise Investors. Fund I is looking at up to 4.6x net MOIC and a 40%+ net IRR.

Behind the contrarian headline, the company and the sector ticked almost every box on our investment checklist: a high-growth, highly fragmented market, very good margins by European standards, high ROIC at gym level, a sector full of M&A activity across Europe, and super ambitious founders. The timing looked scary. The fundamentals didn't.

The numbers tell the story: 7 clubs in Bucharest in 2021, now operating 77 clubs across Romania. From €1.7m revenue to ca. €34m and a 20x increase in EBITDA, in 5 years.

I didn't watch this one from the boardroom. At a critical point I jumped in as CFO, and I learned more about building a business in those months than in years of looking at spreadsheets.

But the real credit goes to Alexandru Lascar, Preodisteanu Marius and Laur Lascar – and to the team on the floor, day in and day out. When we came in back in 2021, we backed three founders with an obsessive work ethic and a very clear idea of what a Romanian fitness brand should feel like. You delivered on every bit of that and then some. You never stopped acting like founders, even when the company got 20x bigger. We were lucky to have a seat next to you for the past five years. Thank you for the trust, the energy and the ride.

A big thank you also to my Morphosis team on this deal: Andrei Gemeneanu, Dragos Petre, David Masculic, CFA and Cezar Visan. Financing, acquisitions, openings, reporting, the late nights before every opening and every board meeting. This was a team effort from day one, and I couldn't have asked for a better one.

Next up: Stay Fit Gym and 18GYM become a dual-brand platform with 120+ clubs and about 130,000 members across Romania – exactly the kind of platform this market needs.

And to Vlad Ronea, founder of 18GYM: congrats and good luck. You are getting great partners, and you and the Stay Fit guys are going to make one hell of a team. Very proud of what we built together, and excited to watch the next chapter from the sidelines.
