I said this on a call a week ago and I'll say it here. The number one thing every client asks us is where the hell they're getting value from the AI tools.

They bought the seats. They rolled out the licenses. Had the training. Measured adoption. Even then, nobody in the room can point to the return on investment, and the CFO starts asking why the bill keeps going up. Token usage for the sake of token usage is so 2026.

A lot of the time the problem is where the work runs. Emails, spreadsheets, meeting notes, all going through the most expensive model on the market. You're paying top-shelf prices for work a smaller model handles fine. You don't need a rocket scientist to tell you a recap of your previous week from your inbox.

AI in 2026 is a backend architecture problem. Most companies just have an expensive subscription habit.

Open-weight models hosted here in the US cut token costs 50 to 90% on the right work. I'd move 90% or more of most companies' workloads over and keep the big models for the stuff that actually needs them. That's how you go from "we bought AI" to being able to show what it's doing.
