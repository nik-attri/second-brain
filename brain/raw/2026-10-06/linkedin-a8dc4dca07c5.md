Recently, I got engaged in a contract job where we used Excel to manually map medical records to a Master tariff list.

During training, we were taught to do the mapping manually. But I kept thinking: “Can AI and automation make this faster?”So I applied my knowledge of AI and automation and saved myself tens of hours of repetitive work!  my team lead noticed how i work she wont stop bringing more task , she called me Chief😊. 
 I didn't stop there. I asked a bigger question: What if someone could simply upload the Excel file and the system does the mapping automatically?That question led me to build Datasens Medical Tariff Mapping  Review Software.

It is an AI-assisted Python application that:

📌 Uploads the Excel workbook
📌 Reads the required sheets
📌 Loads the Master tariff database
📌 Runs the matching engine
📌 Maps high-confidence matches (≥85%)
📌 Sends uncertain records for review
📌 Identifies records with no valid match
📌 Produces a review-ready output file.

The principle is simple:
Let the system handle the obvious matches. Let humans focus on the uncertain ones. This project reminded me that data analytics is not just about creating dashboards. It is also about looking at a repetitive business process and asking: "How can I turn this into a system? "From Data → AI → Automation → Intelligent Business Systems.

i will be adding LLM layer to it so that any unmapped  record below  85%  confidence level can also be checked with LLM .
