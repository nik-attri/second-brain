💩 No more “Spread SHIT” management

A lot of companies say they “have an ISMS.”
What they actually have is:
📊 Excel.
📄 Word.
📁 SharePoint.
📑 File copies.
📅 Calendar reminders.
🧠 And one person who somehow knows where everything is.
Until that person leaves. 👋

That is exactly what happened to one of our customers.
Their ISO 27001 / IT compliance environment had grown into roughly 1,660 files spread across SharePoint.
The real problem was not the number of files.
The real problem was that the compliance process itself was scattered across them.
❓ Which policy is current?
❓ Which control is actually working?
❓ Which evidence is still valid?
❓ Which review is overdue?
❓ Who owns the issue?
❓ What changed since last month?
If answering those questions requires opening spreadsheets and searching folders, you do not have a live compliance system.
🏺 You have administrative archaeology.

🤔 Why CISO Assistant?
Our first reaction was obvious:
Why not Vanta or Drata?
Because this customer did not need another expensive SaaS subscription just because everyone else uses one.
They needed:
💰 Control over cost.
🔐 Control over data.
🗺️ And support for the compliance roadmap they were actually planning.

That roadmap included:

✅ ISO 27001
🇪🇺 GDPR
🛡️ NIS2
🇪🇸 ENS
🤖 EU AI Act

And the customer is planning to go through ENS certification in Spain.
That mattered.
Vanta and Drata covered much of the stack, but ENS was the missing piece in the options we reviewed.
CISO Assistant gave us the full combination in one platform, with a self-hosted option.
🧪 So we built a POC.
🚀 Then we implemented the real portal.

🔧 What actually changed?
Not “we moved files into another tool.”
That would be pointless.
We changed how compliance works.
📜 Policies now have owners, versions, review dates and approval status.
🔗 Evidence is linked directly to the controls it proves.
🔍 Audits, findings and remediation are managed in the same system.
And the biggest change:
⚙️ Controls became automated.
The platform now checks areas such as:
👤 Microsoft 365 account compliance
💻 Managed device compliance
🐙 GitHub branch protection
🛡️ Snyk security findings
🌐 HostedScan vulnerability results
🎓 Personnel security training
If something drifts from the expected state, the system can flag it, create a finding and notify the person responsible. 🚨
That is a completely different model.
You stop asking:
“Did somebody remember to check this?” 🤷
And start asking:
🔥 “What is failing right now?”
That is the real move from documentation-heavy compliance to continuous compliance monitoring.
💩 No more compliance hell built on spreadsheets, copied files, and endless calendar reminders.
