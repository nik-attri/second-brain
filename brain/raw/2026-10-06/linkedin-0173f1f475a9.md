REMOTE CAREER CLASS #04 🌉
Revenue Operations Specialist + AI Automation

The person behind the revenue machine.

One thing I have been learning while building THE BRIDGE 🌉 is that some of the highest-value remote skills are not necessarily the skills people talk about every day.

Revenue Operations is one of them.

So, what exactly does a Revenue Operations Specialist do?

A company does not pay you simply because you know how to use HubSpot.

It pays you because you can help answer questions like:

Where are we losing potential customers?
Why are our leads not converting?
Where is our sales process breaking?
Is our CRM a mess?
Which activities are actually producing revenue?

That is where RevOps comes in.

Revenue Operations is the function that brings together the people, processes, data and technology involved in generating revenue.

Think of a RevOps professional as someone helping to keep the company's revenue machine running properly.

They look at where leads are coming from, whether those leads are qualified, how quickly sales teams respond, where prospects drop out of the pipeline, how long deals take to close, which marketing channels produce revenue, why customers leave and where the business is wasting time or money.

They also help management understand things like conversion rates, customer acquisition costs, pipeline value, win rates, churn and revenue forecasts.

And this is where it gets interesting.

RevOps and AI automation can work extremely well together.

RevOps asks:

“How should this revenue process work?”

Automation asks:

“How can we make this process happen automatically?”

AI asks:

“Where can technology analyse information, generate outputs or support decisions within this process?”

For example, imagine a company is losing leads because salespeople are manually entering information into a CRM and following up too late.

A RevOps professional can identify the problem in the process.

Ai automation can connect the systems so information moves automatically.

AI can then help analyse conversations, qualify or prioritise leads, generate follow-up drafts and surface useful information for the sales team.

Now you are no longer just saying:

“I know how to use ChatGPT.”

You are saying:

“I can help you reduce manual work, improve your lead follow-up and make your revenue process more efficient.”

That is a completely different value proposition.

That is why I find the combination of RevOps + AI Automation so interesting.

Don't learn technology simply because it is trending.

Learn to identify a business problem. Then learn the technology that can solve it.

That is the kind of thinking THE BRIDGE 🌉 is about.

We are not just learning tools.

We are learning how those tools create economic value.
