We have a spot open in our Finance team 📣‼️ 

We’re looking for someone who’s curious, willing to learn, and wants to experience what working in Finance actually looks like beyond the classroom.

You’ll be working closely with our Finance team and, along the way, you’ll get to be part of the everyday work that keeps things running from data entry and reconciliation to monthly closing, accounts payable, audit, and tax reporting.
There will probably be things you’ve never done before. There will definitely be new things to learn. And that’s okay.

We’re looking for someone who pays attention to details, is comfortable working with numbers and data, and most importantly, is willing to learn and ask questions.
As you grow into the role, you’ll also get guidance from the team, hands-on experience with real finance processes, and the chance to build stronger Excel and accounting skills.

We’re looking for:
• Final-year students or fresh graduates majoring in Accounting or Finance
• Basic understanding of accounting and financial statements
• Familiar with Excel (SUM, IF, VLOOKUP)
• Detail-oriented and accurate
• Able to work with large amounts of data
• Willing to learn and work with deadlines

Your first internship doesn’t have to be perfect. It just needs to be a place where you can start, learn, make mistakes, and get better.

Come grow with us, apply below! ⬇️
