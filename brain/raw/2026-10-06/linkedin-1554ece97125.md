When we released the first version of Insights Copilot in YouScan Social Listening ✨, we advocated that the ability to see original data points for any AI generated insights should be a core principle to build trust in such insights.

Today, it’s hard to imagine otherwise. Good, verifiable references are a core principle of AI Insights in Social Intelligence practice.

Another important principle for building trust in AI assisted research, in my opinion, is to make known information explicit. Don't just rely on AI to infer everything even if AI can easily do it. 

Probabilities and nuances add up. What seems obvious to an AI model in one context can be missed in another.

In Insights Copilot these principles work in the following ways: 
 - When Insights Copilot analyzes data – it uses all the known filters available in YouScan, hence there is no guessing and inferring when preparing data for deep analysis. 
 - And when Insights Copilot is answering – it not only produces the answer itself, but also communicates the answer by changing what you see in the platform. 
It answers with applied filters, charts and original data points the same way as you would do it when analyzing data manually. 

This allows to dive deeper or cross-verify analysis at any point manually, if needed, without added cognitive load.
