What a day for La French Tech !

💶 Cleavr raised an €8M seed in a round led by Varsity to become the AI coworker for accounts receivable across Europes mid-market and enterprises. 

Grab a coffee ☕ with Baptiste Nassoy ( 👉DM). He's neither Irish nor Italian, but he'll talk your ear off about cash recovery as Cleavr collects cash to your treasury, enabling your Spiko account ( 👏 ).

Because for Cleavrs clients, getting paid is existential: receivables pay salaries, cover operating costs and fund growth. Cleavr automates the entire cash collection process with AI, cutting manual tasks by 80% and payment delays by 37%.

The difference? 

Collections is still done by hand in most finance teams. Chasing, reconciling, escalating, all of it tracked on spreadsheets while the cash sits outside the business. 

Cleavr takes that work off the team and runs it continuously.That is the real story, the back office is moving from headcount that processes to software that collects, and it is already in production. 

With 100+ customers across Europe in a few months, it is one of Better Angle fastest company. This round will accelerate product development and growth across European markets.

Congrats to Baptiste Nassoy, Antoine Grenard, Arthur GUERIN and the team and to their lead Varsity and co-investors Kima Ventureses, 100IN, Portfolio Ventures, Kerala Ventures, Financière Saint James, STATION F and Clover, with strategic support from Datadog and PE funds.
