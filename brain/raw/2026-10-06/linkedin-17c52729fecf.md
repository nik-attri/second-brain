Airtable enabled the idea of AI to become a reality. 🔓 But, it didn’t start that way. 🤔 
 
Did that sound like some AI bot wrote that? I promise I did it myself! Maybe being so lost in the weeds of Airtable I myself have just become AI. 😱 🤖 
 
Anyways, the story goes like this ➡️ When we initially took on Airtable it was simply a cost-efficient means to consolidate accounts from Trello to something else another team at my company was already using. This other thing, Airtable, was then described as a supercharged spreadsheet.
 
However, Airtable is so much more than that! It’s a relational database first and foremost, but it’s also an open sandbox that gives you full autonomy to control any data that you could possibly imagine:
 
🏗️  Data structures and data pipelines that help make sense of the chaos between individuals, teams, and organizations you might work with.
🏆  Autonomy to have full ownership of workflows that otherwise would take a team to manage.
💬  A skillset and language that allows you to unlock the full potential of AI and automated workflows.
 
I've been a huge fan of Airtable ever since we first brought it on, and I’ve been fortunate enough to save many teams within my organization from Google Form and Spreadsheet hell with it… well, maybe there’s still a bit of Google Form and Spreadsheet hell but we’re working on that.
 
As part of the #Airtable MVP program I hope to use this opportunity to return the favor I received from Gareth Pronovost and others (with their many GREAT YouTube videos on the platform) to share my own knowledge and skills that I’ve now gained with others.
 
So, if you’re curious about Airtable, or you’re already in deep and getting sweaty with it, please reach out and let’s chat anytime! I’d love to pick your brain and learn something new in return too! 🤗
