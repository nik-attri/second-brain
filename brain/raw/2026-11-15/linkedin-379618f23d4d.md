Building ProcureHub -> AI-Powered Smart Procurement Platform

Excited to share the latest development progress of ProcureHub, a B2B procurement management platform we are building to simplify and automate business purchasing workflows.

Recent development milestones:

Employee dashboard and purchase request management.
Purchase request submission and approval workflow.
Procurement officer workspace.
RFQ (Request for Quotation) creation and management.
Vendor and quotation management modules.
MySQL database integration with multiple service databases.
Docker-based microservices deployment.
Backend health checks and service monitoring.
SI-Talker — our planned AI-powered procurement assistant.

Technology Stack:
React | TypeScript | Python FastAPI | Oracle MySQL | Docker, Inc | Microservices | Groq | Google Gemini | Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Our vision is to reduce manual procurement work, improve transparency, simplify vendor management, and make intelligent procurement accessible to growing businesses.
This is an ongoing journey, and we're continuously improving the platform, testing workflows, and building toward a scalable product.

Proud of the progress our team is making!
