We built Sirraya Finance from scratch. Now, we're opening it for early access.

We have always believed that financial modelling shouldn't be limited by spreadsheets, broken formulas, or complicated workflows.

Founders need to understand their numbers, explore possibilities, evaluate decisions, and anticipate what lies ahead. They shouldn't have to spend hours maintaining spreadsheets to do that.

That's why we built Sirraya Finance.

Not another spreadsheet wrapper, but a financial modelling engine designed from the ground up.

The idea is to bring sophisticated financial modelling into a system where founders can:
Build integrated financial models without manually managing complex spreadsheet formulas.

Model revenue, expenses, cash flow, and financial projections.

Run what-if scenarios to understand the financial implications of business decisions.
Evaluate hiring, pricing, growth, and operational strategies.

Understand their company's financial position and plan ahead with greater clarity.

What makes this particularly interesting is the engineering underneath it.

Sirraya Finance is powered by a purpose-built Rust financial engine, designed around deterministic computation, modularity, and a clean separation between financial logic and presentation.

We believe financial infrastructure should be reliable, understandable, and accessible, not unnecessarily complicated or prohibitively expensive.

We are now opening Sirraya Finance for early access through a limited free trial.

We're onboarding early users, gathering feedback, and improving the platform based on real-world financial modelling requirements.
