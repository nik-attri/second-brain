Mention "Digital Transformation" to a traditional construction company, and watch them put up a wall.

I recently spoke with a construction firm managing 300+ contract workers and 30+ project engineers. Their biggest headache was a dedicated back-office team drowning in manual attendance tracking and payment reconciliations.

They clearly needed workforce monitoring, but they were hesitant to embark on a massive, disruptive automation journey.

Instead of pitching a complex, end-to-end tech overhaul, we reviewed their existing process flow and proposed a targeted pathway for one immediate win: automating the link between site attendance and back-office payment processing to deliver fast ROI with minimal disruption.

They loved the approach, and it’s now with their board for a final decision. My biggest takeaway is simple: clients don’t want a "digital journey"—they want a practical fix for one painful problem that saves time and money right now.

Solve the immediate bottleneck first; the rest of the transformation follows. Looking forward to taking this to the next level!
