AI is a powerful tool for drafting, but relying on it entirely for your professional presentation is a trap.

AI is an incredible assistant, but making it do 100% of your professional presentation work usually backfires.

When you let a tool generate everything from scratch without your own touch, three things happen:

‣ Your voice gets completely lost behind generic corporate jargon.

‣ Critical nuances specific to your career, project, or audience get missed.

‣ It becomes obvious to anyone reading it that it was auto-generated.

Use AI to break through writer's block, organize your structure, or polish your grammar. But always inject your own narrative, review the details, and make sure it actually sounds like you.

But how do you strike the balance between using tech and keeping your authentic voice?

You look at examples around you. You look for a mentor. You look for something that can help you, something that is NOT AI.

While I regularly leverage top AI tools for what they do best, it’s just as important to look at their limitations—especially with this one.

The command was simple : "Build and Refine a resume based off the details, and make sure it's professionally and aesthetically pleasing." Notice how I did not ask it to add flowers, or colors, or designs. I may have been at fault, but it cluttered the whole resume. As a test, I applied to 10 different places, and offered them this AI generated resume, and almost all of them rejected on grounds of "Resume Rejected". I reached out to one of them, and I told them about the test phase, and all they told me was "Hardik, if this is what AI can do best, we might as well abandon it."

I'm not saying that AI cannot do anything. It can make presentations in seconds that would take us hours to finish. It can build webpages, and websites within minutes, which would require us to manually code thousands of lines with scripts and connectors. It can generate posters, coverpages, and images from our imaginative prompt. But letting it completely handle things for us that we can do better is just us being lazy.

Find a mentor. A peer. A professional. Someone who knows something better than you. Acquire it to grow yourself. And then watch the creeping success help you reach the sky you once thought was impossible for humans.
