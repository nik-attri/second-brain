I built an AI system that does what most freelancers spend 5+ hours a day doing manually.
Finding leads. Researching companies. Writing cold outreach. Following up.
Here's what it does 👇
🔍 Finds companies showing buying signals → Job boards, Google, Maps — it catches who's hiring for AI/automation roles or drowning in manual work.
🔬 Researches each company with AI → Scrapes their site (politely), detects tech stack, finds pain points, identifies contacts.
🎯 Scores fit (0–100) → AI decides which leads are worth your time — so you focus on the top 20%.
✉️ Writes personalized outreach → Not templates. Real, specific cold emails and LinkedIn notes that reference their actual problems.
📅 Runs a multichannel follow-up sequence → LinkedIn engage → Connect → Email → Follow-up → DM → Close the loop.
💬 Handles replies, call prep & proposals → Paste their reply, get a suggested response. One click for call prep notes or a full proposal.
The tech stack: • Python + FastAPI + Streamlit • Gemini / Groq / OpenAI / Anthropic / Ollama • SQLite, BeautifulSoup, Serper, Hunter, Adzuna • Zero frontend frameworks — plain HTML/CSS/JS for the original UI
Built it end-to-end. Open source on GitHub.
