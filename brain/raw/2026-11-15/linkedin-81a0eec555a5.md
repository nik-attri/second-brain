DCUD Development is Hiring!
Position: Resort Manager
Location: Delta Region, Egypt
DCUD Development is seeking an experienced and dynamic Resort Manager to lead the overall operations of Delta Region, ensuring exceptional guest experiences, operational excellence, and sustainable business performance.
Key Responsibilities:
-Oversee the overall resort operations, ensuring smooth and efficient daily management.
-Develop and implement operational strategies to achieve business objectives and maximize profitability.
-Ensure the highest standards of guest satisfaction, hospitality, and service quality.
-Manage and supervise all resort departments, including Front Office, Housekeeping, Food & Beverage, Maintenance, and Security.
-Monitor operational budgets, control expenses, and optimize resource utilization.
-Lead, motivate, and develop the resort management team and operational staff.
-Establish and monitor KPIs to evaluate operational performance.
-Ensure compliance with health, safety, security, and hospitality standards.
-Coordinate with commercial and marketing teams to enhance occupancy rates and revenue generation.
Job Requirements:
-Bachelor's degree in Hospitality Management, Hotel Management, Business Administration, or a related field.
-8–12 years of relevant experience in hotel or resort operations, with at least 3–5 years in a senior management position.
-Proven track record in managing large-scale resorts or hospitality establishments.
-Strong leadership, operational, financial, and problem-solving skills.
-Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
-Strong knowledge of hospitality operations and guest experience management.
-Ability to work under pressure and manage multiple operational functions.
