Managing GST compliance for e-commerce sellers on Amazon, Meesho, and Flipkart shouldn’t mean drowning in messy Excel sheets every month.
Recently, I built GST Desk—an end-to-end GST automation and reconciliation solution designed specifically for online sellers and tax professionals.
Instead of spending hours manually formatting marketplace reports and worrying about portal mismatches, the workflow is now completely streamlined:
✅ One-Click Marketplace Uploads: Simply upload raw sales and return reports from Amazon, Meesho, Flipkart, or custom B2B/B2C templates—the system auto-detects the platform and processes the data. 
✅ CBIC Rate & HSN Master Validation: Automatically cross-checks every SKU and HSN code against the latest CBIC rate notifications (factoring in invoice dates and per-piece value rules). 
✅ Instant Error Detection & Corrections: Flags mismatched GST rates or wrong HSN codes upfront, calculates exact tax differences, and lets you fix or override them in a click. 
✅ Complete GSTR-1 Readiness: Generates clean, ready-to-file GSTR-1 summaries, B2CS (Table 7), bill-wise HSN summaries (Table 12), and Document summaries (Table 13). 
✅ Tally Integration & Visual Dashboards: Automates accounting entries directly for Tally and gives a clear month-wise/quarterly dashboard of net taxable value, state-wise sales, and tax liability.
Building tools that eliminate repetitive manual reconciliation while ensuring 100% statutory accuracy is always rewarding.
If you're an e-commerce seller, accountant, or CA managing multi-platform GST filings, I’d love to hear how you currently handle your monthly HSN and rate reconciliations! 👇
