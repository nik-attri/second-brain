🚨 "We've Always Done It This Way" – The Most Dangerous Sentence in Food Safety

🛑 You ask why a step is done a certain way.
❌ The answer: "That's how we've always done it."

👉 This is how bad habits become SOPs:
⬅️ A shortcut becomes standard practice.
⬅️ A workaround becomes "the way we do things."
⬅️ No one remembers WHY — they just remember HOW.

🚨 Why is this dangerous?
👉 Processes change. Equipment changes. People change.
👉 What was safe 10 years ago may not be safe today.
👉 Without understanding the "why," people can't adapt when things change.

✅ How to break the cycle:
👉 **Ask "Why?" five times** — get to the root of the practice.
👉 **Review SOPs annually** — challenge every step.
👉 **Document the rationale** — not just the steps, but the reason.
👉 **Empower new employees to question** — fresh eyes see what veterans miss.

🚨 Real-Life Example:
A bakery had a step where operators manually added a preservative. 
✅ A new hire asked: "Why do we add it before the oven? Doesn't heat destroy it?" 
👉 The team realized the preservative was losing 80% of its efficacy. They moved the addition step and solved a shelf-life problem they'd "lived with" for years.

👈 Tradition is not a food safety control. Science is.
