The cutest guy I had ever seen walked into the bank where I was working.

He was wearing Levi’s, a UPS T-shirt, and a big grin.

He walked over to my manager and asked, “I’m looking for Robynn. Is she here?”

My manager pointed at me.

“Hi, I’m Chris. I wanted to thank you for helping my mom.”

For probably one of the only times in my life, I was speechless.

“Um, you’re welcome. Who’s your mom?” 😂

A few days earlier, I’d helped his mother with a problem with her checking account.

We chatted, and he left.

A few days later, he came back.

“I’d like to take you to dinner. 

I said yes.

I was 24. He was 26.

I’d already been married and divorced. That marriage lasted about five minutes.

I was raising a little girl, buried in student loan debt, and in the middle of a custody battle.

I remember wondering what on earth this guy wanted with me.

But we went to dinner.

And we laughed. A LOT.

More than three decades later, we’re still laughing.

Yesterday, Chris turned 60. ❤️

He’s a corporate controller. And for more than 30 years……..

He’s been giving me something very special……

Spreadsheets.

SO. MANY. SPREADSHEETS. 😂😂😂

Buying a car?

He’ll research every model, compare prices, calculate financing, negotiate discounts, and present his findings.

Going to Disney World?

Spreadsheet for flights, hotels, tickets, meals, and fast passes. 

Dishwasher breaks?

He can’t fix the damn thing.

But he can write one hell of a check.

And he’ll have a spreadsheet explaining which replacement makes the most financial sense.

But here’s why I married him.

A few months after we started dating, he got off work late and let himself into my apartment.

It was around one o’clock in the morning.

My little girl was asleep in her crib. When she heard him come in, she called his name.

I was sound asleep until I heard noise from the living room.

I got up to investigate.

And there they were.

My 26-year-old boyfriend, who’d worked all day, sitting on the floor.

Playing Barbies.

With my daughter.

At one o’clock in the morning.

He didn’t wake me up.

He didn’t tell her to go back to sleep.

He just sat down and played.

I stood there watching them.

And I knew.

This was the man I wanted to marry.

I needed someone who would love my little girl, too.

And he did.

He became her dad.

Later, we added a son to our family.

We’ve raised two kids, built careers, started businesses, and made it through some wonderful and difficult years.

And Chris is still that same guy.

Solid. Reliable. Kind.

And funny as hell.

Happy 60th birthday to the UPS guy who walked into my bank all those years ago.

Thank you for taking a chance on a 24-year-old single mom with a complicated life.

And thank you for sitting on that floor at one o’clock in the morning and playing Barbies.

Of all the things you’ve done for me, that’s still the one I remember most.

I’d choose you again.

Even if it meant another three decades of spreadsheets. ❤️
