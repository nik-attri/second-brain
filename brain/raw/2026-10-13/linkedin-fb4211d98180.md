What if welcoming a new employee didn't require HR to handle every task manually? 

🤖 Imagine a company hiring several employees and having to manually process their information, prepare onboarding details, update spreadsheets, and send welcome emails. These repetitive tasks consume time that HR teams could spend helping employees settle into their new roles. So, I decided to build a solution! 

🔥 Introducing my AI-Powered Employee Onboarding Automation, built with n8n. This project demonstrates how AI and workflow automation can simplify the employee onboarding process from the moment a new employee submits their information. 

⚙️ What does the system do? 

✅ Automated Data Collection: Captures new employee information through a form. 
✅ AI-Powered Processing: Uses an AI agent to process onboarding information and generate relevant employee details. 
✅ Employee Records Management: Stores employee information in Google Sheets. 
✅ Automated Welcome Emails: Sends personalized onboarding emails to new employees. 
✅ Instant Notifications: Integrates Telegram notifications into the workflow. 

🔗 Technologies used: n8n — Workflow automation AI Agent — AI-powered information processing Google Sheets — Employee records management Email integration — Automated employee communication Telegram — Workflow notifications 

💡 Why does this matter? Employee onboarding involves more than welcoming someone to a company. It requires accurate record-keeping, timely communication, and coordination between different processes. By automating repetitive steps, businesses can reduce manual administrative work, improve consistency, and create a smoother onboarding experience for new employees.
