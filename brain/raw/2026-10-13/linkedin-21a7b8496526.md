93% of SAP professionals say competitive pay is essential. 

87% say remote or hybrid. Then it drops...

Flexibility 64%. The right role 52%. Everything else under 50%. 

That’s what SAP candidates told us they need from a job in 2026/2027 — nine factors, ranked by the share calling each one essential. It’s the page I’d put in front of any hiring manager before they write a job spec. 

Three things that stand out 📊 

Pay and remote aren’t “nice to haves”. They’re the entry ticket. Miss either one and the other seven never get a hearing. 

Only 45% call a quick process essential. But the best SAP profiles are gone within two weeks, so the slow client loses them whether they rate speed or not. 

Modern SAP exposure — S/4HANA, BTP, AI-ready data — sits at 38%. Lower than most CTOs expect. People want to be paid properly and trusted first. The tech comes after.
