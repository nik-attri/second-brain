At Homersham Ltd we sell a hell of a lot of of the Additel Corporation 680A, 20-Bar, logging pressure gauge.  How come?  Civil folk (I mean those that work with civic ammenitys as opposed to well spoken, freindly people!) must test a pipe they've installed to hold it's pressure 1.5 X rated pressure for hours.  The Additel 680AD can log these measurements and some of my customer e-mail a certificate direct from their cell phone to the end-user customer!  Complete with graphs, spreadsheet and statistics!  Forget about stop watches and multiple visits or, heaven forfend! a paper chart recorder.  The Additel 680AD does it all while one sits in the Ranger having a coffee, smoke etc while viewing live data by Bluetooth in between rounds of Candy Crush!
