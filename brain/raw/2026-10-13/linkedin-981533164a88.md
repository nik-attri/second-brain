93% of procurement professionals have had a supplier ignore their sustainability questionnaire 😫 

The old process with spreadsheets, email chains, manual follow-ups etc. breaks down fast at scale:
→ Response rates stay low 
→ Follow-ups take 6-8 weeks and multiple attempts 
→ Suppliers are simultaneously fielding the same requests from 47 other customers 
→ And your board still wants that ESG report by Q4

Is this really what procurement teams should be spending their time on? 🧐 

On the other side, the suppliers aren't ignoring these questionnaires out of bad faith. They're drowning in the same requests from 47 other customers, asked to track data they've never measured, in formats they don't use, for a customer representing 0.5% of their revenue.

The companies getting real supply chain transparency are automating the entire supplier engagement layer. That’s what we help them do at Responsibly.
