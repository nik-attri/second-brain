Picture a work order taking two days to get processed.

That's where The Grout Guy was.

A WA tile and grout restoration business, growing fast, and drowning in the admin that came with it. Work orders arriving, getting read, getting retyped into the system, sometimes twice because someone missed one.

Then they put AI agents on it.

Work orders now go from inbox to system in two minutes. That is over 20 hours of manual data entry saved every single week. And the business grew from 30 field techs to 130 without adding admin staff to match.

Two minutes. Not two days.

Here's the part most people miss. They didn't start with an AI strategy.

They started with their worst process. The one that ate the most hours and caused the most errors. They mapped it, measured it, then automated that one thing.

That's the order that works. Find the process, measure the hours, automate the worst one first.

The result isn't a fancy tool. It's 20 hours back in the week and a business that can grow without breaking.

What's the worst manual process in your business right now? The one everyone quietly hates. Write it down this week and count the hours it takes.

That number is your starting point. And usually the best one to fix first.
