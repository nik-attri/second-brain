One of the biggest struggles with running a travel blog wasn’t creating the content. It was keeping old content up-to-date. 

I’ve been writing travel content for around 20 years, and I have thousands of articles sitting on Travel Dave covering destinations, hotels, road trips, experiences, adventures, and everything in between.

Going back through thousands of blog posts to manually find hotel mentions, create affiliate links, check whether those links still worked, update them and then do it all again was simply impossible.

There were always more articles than hours in the day.

That meant I knew there were potentially valuable affiliate opportunities sitting inside my existing content, but I struggled to keep up with them.

Then I installed the STAY22 script with Spark enabled, and honestly, it has changed my affiliate game.

Spark automatically identifies accommodation opportunities within my existing content and adds relevant Stay22 affiliate links. Instead of manually trawling through thousands of articles, it does the heavy lifting.

That means I can finally monetise parts of my archive that I simply hadn’t ventured into before. 

It isn’t just about adding a few more links to new articles. It’s about unlocking the value of the content I’ve already created.

An old destination guide can suddenly have relevant hotel links. A road trip article can create accommodation opportunities. A post I wrote years ago can continue generating affiliate revenue without me having to remember to go back and update it.

And then there’s the maintenance problem. Hotels change. Booking pages change. Links disappear. Properties close. Affiliate URLs get outdated.

Manually updating thousands of individual links is a full-time job.

Spark takes away a huge amount of that headache, helping keep hotel links working and relevant instead of leaving me with a graveyard of dead affiliate links buried throughout the site.

So instead of:
Find article → find hotel → create link → add link → check link → update link → repeat thousands of times.

I’m much closer to:
Publish great travel content → let Spark find the opportunities → earn from them. That’s a pretty compelling change, and I'm very happy about it. 

For travel bloggers and publishers with a large archive, I think the real opportunity lies here. We’re often told to create more content to make more money. 

But sometimes the answer isn’t creating more. It’s finding a better way to monetise the content you’ve already created.

For me, STAY22 Spark has opened up affiliate revenue in places where I simply couldn’t keep up before. And best of all, it’s working in the background.

Twenty years of travel stories suddenly have another opportunity to earn.
