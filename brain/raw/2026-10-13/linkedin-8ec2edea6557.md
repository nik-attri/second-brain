Hiring a loan officer assistant is the usual move when an LO’s volume picks up. 
But look at what the job actually is on a Tuesday. 
Building the 1003 from a pile of documents. Chasing missing docs. Checking a scenario against guidelines. Keeping an eye on TRID dates so nothing slips a closing. 
A good LOA is worth far more than that. They’re the second set of eyes on a tricky file, and the person who keeps a nervous borrower calm while you’re on another call. 
If most of their week goes to data entry and deadline-watching, you’re not getting the best of the role. And they aren’t either. 
Before the next hire, ask what you want this person doing all day. We’re launching Mooric soon for the paperwork half of that list. Happy to show what it does, and what it doesn’t.
