The AI game is changing. And small businesses have a huge opportunity. 👀

Hot take: The next 6 months won't be about who uses the most AI tools. It'll be about who builds the smartest systems.

Here's what I see coming:

→ AI agents handling more repetitive tasks, from lead follow-ups to client onboarding.

→ Solopreneurs doing the work of a much bigger team without necessarily hiring more people.

→ Small agencies competing on speed, efficiency, and results — not team size.

But here's the catch: Having AI tools isn't the advantage. Knowing how to connect them into a system that actually works is.

Stop chasing every new AI tool.

Start building workflows that save time, reduce manual work, and help you scale.

Because the real opportunity isn't just using AI to work faster.

It's building a business that doesn't need you to do everything manually.

What's your take? Are we moving toward AI-powered businesses or is the hype still ahead of reality?
