🎥Turn Manual Data Notification Processing into an Automated Workflow

CJC’s Data Notifications (DN) Hub helps market data teams move away from spreadsheet-heavy checks and automate the workflow from exchange notification to permissioning.

In our latest fireside chat, our Project Team Lead, Jack Woodford, shows how DN Hub processes notifications from LSEG and Bloomberg, checks affected RICs and tickers against client usage, and identifies the users, applications, PDP codes, field IDs and pricing changes that require action.

Instead of downloading data, running manual lookups and cross-checking multiple files, teams get a structured view of what has changed and what needs to be permissioned within a few minutes.
