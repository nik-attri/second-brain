There’s something quite special about being a marketer. One minute you’re sat in a meeting saying, “I’ve got an idea…” and the next you’re wondering how on earth that idea has turned into a full-scale production involving 47 spreadsheets, 300 emails, a few minor meltdowns and the occasional “whose idea was this anyway?” 😂

But then comes the moment when it all starts coming together. The plans become something real. The endless conversations, the problem-solving, the creativity, the last-minute changes (because there are always last-minute changes 🙃)… suddenly, you can see what everyone has been working so hard towards. And honestly, there’s nothing quite like it.

The best part isn’t even seeing the finished result. It’s getting to work alongside a bunch of exceptionally brilliant people who take an idea, challenge it, improve it, add their own magic and somehow make it better than you ever imagined. 

People who care just as much as you do. Who roll their sleeves up, laugh through the chaos and make the seemingly impossible happen. A special shout out to David Deacon who puts the D in Digital and David Handley, Sinead Willetts and the amazing folks at Mosaique. 

Marketing might look like the glamorous bit from the outside… but behind the scenes, it’s a wonderful combination of creativity, teamwork, caffeine actually lots of caffeine and controlled chaos. I wouldn’t change it for the world. Because the magic isn’t really in the idea. It’s in the people who bring it to life.  And sometimes, you just have to stand back and think… bloody hell, look what we did. Really proud of this one 🥹👏
