Stop treating your ad data like a research project. If you are a D2C founder spending more than two hours a day in spreadsheets just to figure out your blended CPA, you are losing the only thing that actually matters: time to focus on your product.

We built AdPulse because we saw founders drowning in 'all-in-one' analytics suites that require a degree in data science just to run a report. You don't need another attribution model, a cohort analysis tool, or a creative heat-map platform.

You just need to know where your money is going.

AdPulse pulls spend, ROAS, CPA, and CTR from Google, Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn into one clean dashboard. No manual imports, no broken formulas. And if you have a specific question about your spend? Just ask our AI assistant in plain English, and it gives you the answer.

Stop overcomplicating your ad stack. Sometimes the most powerful tool is the one that simply works.
