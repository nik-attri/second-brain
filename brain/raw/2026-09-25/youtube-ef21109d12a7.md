How do you keep building a product when AI capabilities change so quickly? At the Lenny and Friends Summit, Every co-founder and CEO Dan Shipper makes the case for a small research lab that can explore new ideas while the product team improves what already works. He explains how to run fast experiments, test them in real work, and bring the most promising results into the main product.
