You're awake at 5am answering customer messages because billing gaps have become your job.

But what if Tuesday at 4pm looked completely different?

Right now, you're reviewing an AR aging report. Again.

$47K sitting past 30 days. Another client asking for more time. You already know how this conversation goes.

Here's what most businesses in your revenue range ($500K–$5M) don't realize: those billing gaps aren't just cash flow problems. They're operational anchors.

They force you to:
- Chase payments manually (stealing your Tuesday afternoons)
- Miss undercharging until it's too late to recover
- Spend mental energy on receivables instead of growth
- Watch your team avoid AR work because processes are broken

The businesses we work with see a different Tuesday.

Instead of reviewing aging reports, they're reviewing growth opportunities. Instead of sending follow-up emails, they're onboarding new clients. Instead of calculating how much cash is sitting uncollected, they've already recovered it.

On average, they find $127K in the first 30 days alone. Not through aggressive collection tactics. Through fixing what was broken in the billing and delivery tracking process.

The gap between where you are and where you want to be isn't about working harder.

It's about fixing the operational leaks that make you the only person who can do this right.

Your Tuesday at 4pm should feel different.
