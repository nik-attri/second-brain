AI has made it easier than ever to ship software, but how do you know what’s worth building? In her talk at the Lenny and Friends Summit, Claire Vo argues that traditional feature roadmaps can push teams to clear backlogs, copy competitors, and keep shipping without learning what matters. She makes the case for bigger ambitions, stronger convictions, and experiments grounded in real customer evidence.

Recorded live at Lenny and Friends Summit on September 10, 2026, in San Francisco.
