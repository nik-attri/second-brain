POV: you spent 4 hours building a lead list and somehow found everyone except your actual customer.

Painfully real.

And I fear some of you are using Prospeo for the most boring possible reason 

You open it.
Find someone’s email.
Copy it.

Leave.

Meanwhile Prospeo is sitting there like:

“girl I can literally find your entire target market??”

Because finding the email is NOT the part of outbound that makes me want to close my laptop.

It’s finding the right freaking people.

Anyone can say:

“We’re targeting SaaS founders.”

Okay lovely.

There are approximately 900 billion of them on LinkedIn.

Which ones???

THIS is where Prospeo gets kinda insane.

You can get painfully specific with 40+ filters.

Like:

→ what the company actually sells
→ keywords
→ funding
→ website traffic
→ Products & Services
→ even AI Lookalikes

That last one???

Very very clever.

Basically, you can give Prospeo examples of companies that already look like your ideal customers…

and ask it to find more companies like them.

So instead of building another gorgeous spreadsheet called:

FINAL_LEADS_v7_ACTUALLY_FINAL.xlsx

filled with 8,000 people you’re never going to contact…

you can build a much smaller list of people who actually make sense.

And THEN Prospeo finds the contact data.

→ 90M triple-verified emails.
→ 99% email accuracy according to Prospeo.
→ And the entire database gets refreshed every 7 days.

Which is kinda important when half of LinkedIn apparently changes jobs every Tuesday.

This is why I keep Prospeo in my outreach stack.

Not because I need another email finder.

God knows the internet has enough of those.

But because it helps with the bit BEFORE the email:

Who the hell should we actually be contacting?

If you do outbound for yourself or clients, genuinely have a play with the filters.

You might realize you’ve been building lead lists the hard way this entire time.

Try Prospeo → https://prospeo.io
