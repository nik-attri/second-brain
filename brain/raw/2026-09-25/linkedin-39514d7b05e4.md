We're excited to invite you to our next webinar in the Barreleye Current Series.

A Smarter Way to Model System Hydraulics for Water: Plan, Validate, and Optimize Faster

📅 October 14, 2026
🕘 9:00 AM MT | 10:00 AM CT | 30 minutes
Presented by: James Burke

Register here: https://lnkd.in/eWTWcKeK

James Burke, Founder and CEO, will introduce Barreleye's System Hydraulics capability and show how producers and water midstream companies can model water infrastructure, validate hydraulic feasibility, compare scenario costs, and optimize water movement in one connected workflow.

He'll walk through a live demo, designing a new pipeline that ties a new pad into an existing, hydraulically constrained system.

What you'll see:
• Build a hydraulic model within a broader water infrastructure planning workflow
• Evaluate a proposed pipeline and its pumping requirements against existing system constraints
• Validate what can physically happen, including required lines, pumps, and operating conditions
• Compare scenario costs without rebuilding spreadsheets or switching between disconnected tools
• Optimize water movement while accounting for physical constraints, commercial agreements, and costs
• Built for facilities, water management, planning, engineering and commercial teams at producers and water midstream companies.
