AI agents are changing what we should expect from a website.
A traditional website waits for a human to operate it.
An AI-powered web application can understand an objective and initiate the work itself.
Imagine a sales platform where you don't manually:
Search for prospects
Update the CRM
Prepare follow-ups
Generate reports
Move data between systems
Instead, you tell an AI task agent what outcome you want.
The agent uses APIs, databases and business tools to execute the workflow, verify the results and report back.
That's where web development + AI automation + agent systems become much more than another software feature.
The interface becomes the place where humans set direction.
The agents become the systems that execute.
The future of software may be less about giving people more buttons — and more about giving them fewer things to do.
