And thats a wrap on Camp re:Connect 2026  🏕️ 

I’m not sure there are words to fully describe what it feels like to see months (and months) of planning finally come to life.

The behind the scenes of an event like this isn’t always glamorous. It’s spreadsheets, numbers 😡,  emails, phone calls  😵‍💫 , last minute changes, fighting with printers, running around, loosing your laptop, very little sleep and probably other things I can't think of.  But then you get to look around and see people laughing, learning, connecting, trying something new and genuinely enjoying themselves and suddenly, the hard work feels worth it.

Camp is always a wild ride, and I’m very grateful to have so many incredible people along for it with me.

To the Orchestra Marketing team quite literally could not do this without you. From the bottom of my little heart thank you for showing up, jumping in when I needed you, lifting me up when I needed it and most importantly, letting me yell at all of you for a week. 😂 I hope you know how much I appreciate every single one of you.

To our speakers, sponsors, partners, volunteers and campers, thank you for trusting us with your time and bringing so much energy to Camp. You are what makes this crazy little conference between the pines so special.

And to my crazy partner in crime, Jocelyn Butler, thank you for throwing this crazy idea out into the world and then trusting me to bring it to life. Thank you for giving me the space to learn, figure things out, make mistakes, solve problems and make Camp my baby. 

Over the past three years, Camp re:Connect has taught me more than I ever could have expected. Its taught me about leadership, problem solving, people and myself. Somewhere along the way, it stopped feeling like just another event. 

Camp has truly become a part of who I am, and I’m incredibly proud of what we’ve built.

Camp re:Connect 2026, you were one hell of a ride. Thank you to everyone who was on it with me.
