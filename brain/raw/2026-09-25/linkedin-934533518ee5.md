“SQL can do much more than SELECT *”
SQL isn't just about fetching data.
With SQL, we can:
🔹 Find hidden patterns in millions of records
🔹 Detect duplicate or suspicious transactions
🔹 Identify customers who are likely to stop purchasing
🔹 Find products that are losing sales
🔹 Compare this month's performance with last month's
🔹 Rank employees, products, or customers within groups
🔹 Calculate running totals and moving averages
🔹 Find the first and latest activity of each customer
🔹 Detect gaps in dates or missing transactions
🔹 Turn raw transactional data into business insights

And the interesting part?

All of this can be done without manually going through thousands of rows.

That's what makes SQL powerful —
it doesn't just retrieve data; it helps us ask better questions of the data.
