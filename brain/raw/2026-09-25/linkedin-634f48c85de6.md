🚀 We’re Hiring: #Zendesk Administrator | #Hyderabad
📍 Location: Hyderabad, India
💼 Experience: 6–8 Years
👨‍💻 Role: Zendesk Administrator
🏢 Function: IT / #Service Desk / #Zendesk #Administration
We are looking for an experienced Zendesk Administrator to manage and optimize our Zendesk platform, support user access and administration, and ensure efficient ticket routing and workflow configuration.

🔹 Key Responsibilities
Manage employee onboarding and offboarding within Zendesk.
Add/remove employees from appropriate Zendesk groups and distribution groups.
Manage user profiles, roles, permissions, and access.
Troubleshoot ticket access, views, groups, and permission-related issues.
Create and maintain service types through #Zendesk #Admin Center.
Create, modify, and maintain Zendesk groups based on business requirements.
Develop and maintain macros to improve ticket-handling efficiency.
Configure and maintain triggers and automations.
Manage views to ensure tickets are routed and displayed correctly.
Support ticket routing and workflow configuration.
Perform regular checks to ensure Zendesk configurations and workflows are functioning as expected.
Implement configuration changes based on approved business requirements.
Maintain documentation, SOPs, and administration procedures.
Identify opportunities to improve workflows and automate repetitive activities.
Collaborate with #Service Desk, IT, and other internal teams to resolve Zendesk-related issues.
Support testing and validation of new or modified Zendesk configurations.
Follow appropriate access, security, and compliance practices.
Provide knowledge transfer and support to team members when required.

🔹 Required Skills & Experience
6–8 years of relevant experience, with strong hands-on Zendesk Administration experience.
Good understanding of Zendesk #Admin Center.
Experience managing users, groups, roles, and permissions.
Hands-on experience with macros, triggers, automations, and views.
Strong understanding of ticket routing and workflow management.
Good troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.
Ability to translate business requirements into effective Zendesk configurations.
Strong communication and documentation skills.
Ability to work independently and manage multiple administration requests.

🌟 What We’re Looking For
A detail-oriented professional who can take ownership of Zendesk administration, troubleshoot issues efficiently, maintain platform stability, and continuously identify opportunities for workflow improvement and automation.
