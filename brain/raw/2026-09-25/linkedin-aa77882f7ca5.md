Recently done with Malaysia Conference 🎉

What was mine Research paper topic? 🤔
Ans- My research paper topic was Neurosumbolic Cognitive for  predictive mitigation of supraharmonics ( 2 to 150khz)  in Ev charging clusters. ⚡⚡

Tha main theme of my research paper was to address the issue of *passive filters* in current industries , which is fixed depending on cable length and filter and have  to be changed manually but in EV station the 🚗 🚗 cars come  and go every 10 minutes so it becomes very hard for passive filters in today industries to catch or match the frequency ,speed of cars.


How my  research work can help to sort out industrial problems?  🤔🚗⚡

Ans-    I took 10 chargers, each 22kW, switching at 50kHz, and connected them with 100 meter LV cable. Then I increased to 50 chargers to see crowding effect. To make it real like Tata Steel plant, I also added 5 solar inverters and 20 LED lights whose noise is spread from 45 to 55kHz.

I used Simscape Electrical to run it and FFT block to see noise at each frequency like 8.3kHz. For prediction test I used Python and ran the same case 100 times.

To check result I used THD-SH, which is total buzz from 2kHz to 150kHz divided by main 50Hz power. If its 0.8% its safe, if 14% meter will fail.

Results show one charger is safe with 0.8% noise, but 10 chargers together make 6.2% and 50 chargers make 14.1% with 58V peak at 8.3kHz, which kills smart meter.

My twin detected this 8.3kHz problem 4.2 seconds before danger with 94.7% accuracy.

For PLC jamming, 5 solar + 20 LED made noise 134 dBuV, limit is 120, so 27% meter messages failed. With my twin, noise came down to 109 dBuV in 0.8 seconds, 25dB reduction, and failure only 1.2%.
 100% is not possible in real plant because grid always changes, but we can go near 100% if we do three things.

First, train twin with real Tata Steel feeder data, not only simulation, so it learns actual cable length and noise. Second, use faster DSP chip and more sensors at PCC so prediction is every 5ms not 20ms. Third, make twin self-learning, so after each car plug-in it updates its grid impedance automatically..
