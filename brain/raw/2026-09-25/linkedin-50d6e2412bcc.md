What should we keep from our work with AI so we can pick up where we left off in a new conversation?

I have been exploring this question by building an LLM Wiki in my personal AI workspace. The aim is to carry forward what we worked on, why we chose a particular direction, and what we need to check next.

For each task, the Wiki records its purpose, key decisions and their rationale, current results, unresolved issues, and next steps. It also links to source documents and work logs so the supporting evidence can be revisited when needed.

The structure has two levels. An overview helps locate the relevant task, followed by a detailed record for that task. I designed the workflow so AI can use these records in a new conversation to understand the existing direction and suggest next steps that fit the current request.

For example, when resuming a weekly research task, the starting point includes what was reviewed last time, why adoption was deferred, and which conditions need to be checked this time.

Another goal is to connect these records to recurring task automation. Clear objectives and decision criteria make it easier to specify what should be repeated, which sources to consult, and where to record the results. As a first step, I have scheduled a weekly review and update of the Wiki itself.

In the records, I distinguish completed work from planned work, and my decisions from AI suggestions. Since these records become the starting point for future tasks, I want them to make clear what has actually been confirmed.

Next, I plan to assess how well context carries over into new conversations, reduce the need to explain the same background repeatedly, and identify further tasks to automate.

How do you keep track of decision rationale and next steps in your work with AI?
