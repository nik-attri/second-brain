🚀 We’re Hiring: Onboarding Intern | Gurgaon

If you’re the kind of person who figures things out instead of waiting to be told what to do, we want to hear from you.
We’re looking for a smart, enthusiastic & street-smart Onboarding Intern to join our team for a 3-month paid internship.
And no  this isn’t a “sit around and do data entry” internship. 👀

⚡ What you’ll actually do:
-Work closely with our Onboarding team and help keep things moving.
-Manage and update sheets, trackers & onboarding data — with the context behind the numbers, not just copy-paste work.
-Get a real understanding of how leads move through the funnel and what makes them convert.
-Learn how customer psychology and behaviour influence business decisions.
-Jump in wherever the onboarding team needs support and take ownership of getting things done.
-Get hands-on exposure to how a fast-paced startup actually operates.

🧠 What you’ll learn:
Think of it as a crash course in:
Leads → Psychology → Conversion → Onboarding → Business Operations
You’ll see how things connect instead of just being assigned one small piece of the puzzle.

👀 You might be our person if you:
-Are street-smart and know how to get things moving.
-Ask “why?” instead of blindly following a process.
-Pick things up quickly and aren't afraid to figure things out.
-Are organised and comfortable working with spreadsheets.
-Can juggle multiple things without losing track.
-Bring good energy, curiosity and a “let’s figure it out” attitude.

📍 The Details
Duration: 3 Months
Stipend: Paid
Location: Gurgaon
Work Mode: Work From Office
Days: Monday – Friday (Flexible timings)

If you're looking for an internship where you can actually learn how leads, customers, conversion and business operations work, this could be a pretty good place to start. 🚀
