The ERP product goes live.
Spreadsheet hell creeps back in.

Companies pull financial statements and inventory plans out of the ERP and into Excel. And when they have questions, they turn to ChatGPT. And frankly, that's the AI adoption reality of many companies.

Now AI agents built into ERP systems promise to change that.

And they can. They can query ERP data, answer questions, generate reports, run analyses and automate workflows. They may even eliminate some of the spreadsheets.

But here’s what I think is still missing.

Getting information out of the ERP is one thing. Resolving the work that information reveals is another.

A supplier delay isn’t just a late purchase order. It may affect material availability, production schedules and customer commitments.

Knowing the inventory number isn’t the same as knowing which orders are at risk, what alternatives are available, who needs to make a decision and whether the problem has been resolved.

AI agents can make ERP systems easier to access and use. With the right integrations and configuration, they can do much more.
