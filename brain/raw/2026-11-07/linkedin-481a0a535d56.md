Founded in 2012, First Call Recruitment is an HR consulting firm that helps organizations attract, develop, and retain the talent that drives performance. For over a decade, we have earned the trust of leading institutions across Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI) and Wealth Management.

Our Foundation

These industries operate under strict regulatory and reputational expectations, where one hiring decision can affect client trust, compliance, and revenue. We understand the skills, certifications, and cultural fit needed for roles from relationship managers, credit and risk professionals, and wealth advisors to senior leadership.

Core Capabilities

Talent Acquisition and Executive Search: Targeted sourcing, structured assessment, and shortlisting for specialist and leadership roles.
Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO): End-to-end hiring, from workforce planning to onboarding, with transparent reporting.
HR Advisory and Operations: Policy design, onboarding, compensation benchmarking, performance management, and compliance support.
Workforce Analytics: Data-driven insights on attrition, hiring efficiency, and talent trends.

A Dedicated Model for Every Client

Each client is served by a dedicated team that learns its business, hiring standards, and culture. This lets us manage multiple engagements in parallel without compromising quality, with consistent communication and focused attention.

Expanding Our Reach

Building on our strength in BFSI and Wealth Management, we are expanding into Information Technology and other growth sectors, guided by the same principles: rigorous market research, precise candidate evaluation, and long-term partnerships.

Why Organizations Partner With Us

Over a decade of proven domain expertise
Dedicated teams aligned to client objectives
Structured, compliant, data-informed hiring
A culture of transparency and accountability
