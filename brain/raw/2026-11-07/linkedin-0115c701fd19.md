Perspective.

I'm not sure how many of my contacts this will reach, I rarely post so I'm sure the algorithm will bury this post deep...hope not though.

Today I had a initial conversation with a new client.  He is 89, and his lovely Mrs. is 92.  I started with my intro conversation to get to know them and for them to get to know me.  Within minutes I was astounded by them.  He is 89, physically and mentally probably the most amazing 89 year old I have ever met.  Without getting into details combined they have over 20 Financial Accounts (different locations), and my client spreadsheets everything and tracks his balance at the end of every year.  Yes, he knows the balance/location/tax coding of every account he has.  Among the 20+ account (only 2 of which I am responsible for) at least 15 ROTH accounts which he established years ago understanding the tax benefit.  When I brought up taxes and asked who handles all this he chuckled, so I said let me guess pen and paper right?  He laughed and said yea I'm embarrassed to say.  Please I said, I respect the hell out of that.  

I know there is a lot of work for me to do in order to help these new and amazing clients.  But before I can do that I need to get to know them as people.  I spent more time talking about his family and asking about his life.  He has been married approaching 70 years, 2 children and 4 beautiful grandchildren.  Amazing.

Having just reached 20 years with my amazing wife Erica I was compelled to ask him what the secret was to 70 years of marriage was.  He didn't hesitate, he immediately said "take care of each other".  

I won't add extra paragraphs on what that means to Erica and I personally but each of us have situations that we can relate to.  As a father and husband my family takes care of me, and likewise.  

No matter how bad the situation, we all need to take care of each other.  This simple conversation with my client reminder me to tell Erica how much I appreciate her.

The world in general has divided us.  What we need to do is just learn to understand that behind every face, every word spoken is a history.  When people get angry at us and we don't understand it there is probably something else going on.  When we get mad at another driver on the road for doing something really dumb there may be a reason.  It is easy for us to judge without knowing the whole story, it makes us feel satisfied and almost happy with ourselves pointing out what they did wrong.  That is an empty happiness, how do you feel a short time later?

Moral of my rant is we all need to be better to each other.

Hold the door for someone.  Smile.  If someone is having trouble lifting something help them. When you walk by someone smile, and maybe say hi.  

For business professionals treat your clients the same as family.  Get to know them personally, and perhaps you will learn something about them and yourself along the way.
