The future of #smart #waste #management is being redefined by #IoT and advanced tracking technologies. Our goal at Innovation4Waste is to empower frontline operators, making door-to-door collection more efficient, precise, and seamless.

Our plug-and-play IoT ecosystem integrates directly into municipal waste services without changing existing operations, supporting workers every day:

• Wearable & Vehicular RFID Solutions: Wearable RFID readers and vehicle-mounted systems automate data collection during door-to-door pickup, eliminating manual tracking errors and streamlining the operator's workflow.

• Fill-Level Monitoring: Smart sensors track container capacity in real time, allowing for dynamic route optimization and ensuring collectors only empty bins when necessary.

• Traceability for Pay-As-You-Throw: By accurately capturing disposal data, our technology improves recycling quality and provides the data required for fair, volume-based waste taxation.
#Innovation is not about replacing human effort—it is about providing the right digital tools to accelerate the green transition and optimize urban collection.
