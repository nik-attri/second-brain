WE'RE HIRING | PRODUCTION DEPARTMENT

Electropolymers Private Limited (EPL) is expanding its Production team and has three exciting career opportunities available in Karachi!

We are looking for motivated, technically sound, and results-oriented professionals to join our team.

Open Positions:
Executive – Production
Fresh graduates to 1 year of relevant experience.

Senior Executive – Production
2–4 years of relevant experience.

Assistant Manager – Production
4–5 years of relevant experience.

Key Responsibilities:
- Oversee daily production and assembly operations.
- Ensure achievement of production targets and quality standards.
- Monitor production planning, manpower allocation, and workflow.
- Improve productivity and minimize rejection, rework, and downtime.
- Monitor OEE and drive continuous improvement initiatives.
- Implement Lean Manufacturing, 5S, and TPM practices.
- Coordinate with Quality, Maintenance, Stores, and other departments.
- Ensure compliance with safety procedures and operational standards.

- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Industrial, or Polymer Engineering, or a related field.
- Understanding of assembly line operations, OEE, 5S, TPM, quality control, and workplace safety.
- Strong teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, and adaptability skills.
- Good communication skills and proficiency in English and MS Office, particularly Excel.
- Willingness to work in shifts.
