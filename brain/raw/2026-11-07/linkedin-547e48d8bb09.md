I keep losing deals.
And honestly, it's all my fault.

A founder comes in wanting a custom build. Budget ready. Excited.
20 minutes later I'm telling them to use a tool that costs $30 a month.

My sales pipeline hates me. 😅

But AI made building software cheap, and now everyone wants to rebuild everything from scratch.

Please don't.

Most businesses don't need new software.
They need the tools they already pay for to actually talk to each other.

Here's when I tell people NOT to build:

→ A good tool already does 80% of it. Your "unique workflow" is usually the 20% you can adjust.
→ You haven't run the process manually long enough to know what it should be.
→ You want a custom CRM because HubSpot "feels clunky". That's a setup problem, not a software problem.
→ The goal is a report. A dashboard on top of your existing data takes days. A rebuild takes months.
→ Nobody on your team will own it after launch.

And here's when building makes sense:

→ You've tried the tools, properly, and they genuinely can't hold your business rules.
→ Per-seat pricing stops making sense. 200 people on a $40/seat tool is $96k a year, forever.
→ Your core ops are stitched across 6 tools and a WhatsApp group, and every handoff leaks.
→ The thing you'd build is the thing that makes you different.

A lot of what we do is helping people find the right tool, or the right alternative, and wiring it into what they already run. Only when nothing fits do we build.

Rebuilding from scratch isn't always the right answer.
Getting you to the outcome fastest is.

Reinventing the wheel isn't innovation. It's just an expensive wheel.

Founders and ops leads: what's the tool you almost rebuilt and then didn't?
