I don’t need an AI agent. I’ve got nothing for it to do. 

Everywhere I look, people are building AI agents, automating workflows, connecting 15 apps and streamlining their businesses.

Brilliant. Good luck to them.

But what about the rest of us?

We know AI is the future. We’re curious. We want to understand it. We don’t want to get left behind.

But we’ve got no business to streamline, nothing much to automate and we’re not particularly keen on connecting our entire digital lives to something we barely understand.

I’m 60. I use AI pretty much every day.

I’ve never built an agent. I don’t automate anything. And I certainly don’t have 15 apps talking to each other.

Yet I find AI incredibly useful.

It helps me solve problems, understand things, make decisions, explore daft ideas and occasionally tells me complete bollocks.

And that’s got me thinking.

What if millions of people don’t actually want to become AI experts?

What if they just want to discover how this remarkable technology can make their everyday lives a bit easier, better or more interesting?

Maybe we don’t need to catch up with the AI experts.

Maybe the AI conversation needs to catch up with us.
