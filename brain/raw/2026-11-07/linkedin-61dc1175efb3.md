How the bloody hell have we done that?

That was my first thought when I realised we'd built a whole strategy and forgotten to mention money.

Every goal had a number on it. Keeping more customers, higher satisfaction, more people converting online. But nobody had mentioned revenue or profit.

Looking at who was in the room, it made sense. We had three directors, covering IT, marketing and digital. They all cared about their own areas, but nobody was looking at the business as a whole.

I flagged it in the write-up, and it changed how I run these sessions. Now, for every goal, I ask what it's worth to the business. I also think much harder about who's in the room. Finance often gets left out of strategy sessions because they're seen as the creative bit. In my experience, finance people keep things grounded, and they often come up with the ideas nobody expected. Let them out of their spreadsheet cells for the day. They've earned it.

Who's the person most often missing when big decisions get made in your business?
