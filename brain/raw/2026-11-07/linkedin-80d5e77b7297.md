They care far more about balance, survival and your ability to perform over time.

Following my keynote, “Perform When It Matters – and Enjoy the Journey,” I’ve received considerable interest from companies curious about what business can learn from elite sport about leadership, culture and performance.

And in conversations with clients, one thing keeps coming up:
Many companies build high-performance environments around the WRONG FOCUS and It’s time for a SERIOUS UPDATE!

When I work with people, elite athletes and teams, I ALWAYS start with the individual.
Where are they today? Where do they need to be tomorrow, next week or at their next competition?

We support this with insights from biology, neuroscience and recovery.
Yet in business, we often respond to performance challenges by pushing harder rather than working smarter.

To me, HIGH PERFORMANCE IS NOT ABOUT PRESSURE. IT’S ABOUT BALANCE, HUMAN UNDERSTANDING AND THE RIGHT FOCUS.

That requires knowing which mental and physiological state the next task demands, and how to get ourselves into exactly that state of mind. 

Elite athletes know and understand the difference between two essential states:

COMPETITION MODE: where the brain and body are activated, focused and ready to perform.
&
RECOVERY MODE: where the nervous system settles, allowing recovery, adaptation and rebuilding.

The same principles should apply at work and in life in general.

If we constantly operate in “competition” mode, surrounded by deadlines, meetings and KPIs, without sufficient recovery, we seriously risk that our stress-response systems goes into overdrive and is chronically activated.
Prolonged stress will decision-making, reduce performance and undermine creativity.

Neurologically, the brain can activate similar threat-response systems whether we face actual physical danger, a threatening spreadsheet or overwhelming deadlines, even though these threats are very different.

TO ME SUSTAINABLE HIGH PERFORMANCE IS NOT ABOUT SQUEEZING MORE OUT OF THE SYSTEM. 
It’s about understanding how the system works.
Knowing how to activate it when performance matters, and how to recover and rebuild when it doesn't.

One of the most underrated skills for modern leaders, ambitious individuals and teams is STATE REGULATION, The ability to clear task/mental residue between meetings, consciously shift between focus and calm, and prepare your brain and mental state of mind for the next challenge.

Because how well you recover mentally reset and adapt your state of mind to the task ahead is directly proportional to your success in the situation and will make a hell of a difference when it truly matters!
