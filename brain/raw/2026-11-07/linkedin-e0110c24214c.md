Free pizza on Fridays will not fix veterinary staff burnout.
Your team doesn't need perks. They need operational relief.
Pizza doesn't solve:
❌ Technicians drowning in manual prescription refill requests
❌ Doctors writing medical charts well past 8:00 PM
❌ Front-desk staff managing angry phone calls over a 3-week backlog
❌ Experienced nurses spending half their shifts on administrative data entry
Veterinary medicine is mentally and physically demanding enough on its own. Adding unnecessary administrative friction drives great clinicians out of the profession.
If you want to protect your hospital's culture and retain your staff, invest in systems that clear the operational runway so they can simply practice medicine.
