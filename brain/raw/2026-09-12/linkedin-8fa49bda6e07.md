After 3 years and 5 months, it’s time to say goodbye to TikTok and take on a new challenge. 🤍

Working at a big tech had always been a dream of mine, and I can definitely say it was a dream worth chasing.

The Guilherme in this picture had no idea what the next few years would bring. I joined to work with Brazil Payroll, but somewhere along the way my world became much bigger than that.

I had the chance to work with people from different countries and cultures, be part of a global team, go through major changes and implementations, and work on projects I probably wouldn’t have imagined when I first joined.

There were challenging days, countless calls, deadlines, spreadsheets and probably more payroll reconciliations than I can count. There were also achievements I’m really proud of, so much learning and, most importantly, amazing people.

I’m grateful to everyone who trusted me, taught me something, challenged me, helped me when I needed it or simply made the crazy days a little more fun. I’m not going to mention everyone here, but you know who you are and you rock!!! 😍

Looking at this picture now feels special. That version of me was just happy to have made it into a company he had dreamed of working for. He had no idea how much he was about to learn and grow.

I leave very different from the person who walked in, and I think that’s the best thing I could have asked for.

Thank you, TikTok. It was a hell of a ride. 🫰🏻

Excited for what comes next. 🍀
