🚨 WE’RE HIRING | JUNIOR HR GENERALIST
📍 Location: Pune – Navale IT Park
💼 Experience: 2–4 Years
⏱️ Joining: Immediate Joiners Preferred
👩 Preference: Female Candidates
🔹 Job Summary
We are looking for an experienced and proactive HR Generalist with strong hands-on experience in Talent Acquisition, Campus Recruitment, Payroll, HR Compliance, and Zoho People.
The ideal candidate should have excellent communication skills, be fluent in English, and be capable of independently handling day-to-day HR operations.

🔹 Key Responsibilities

Talent Acquisition
Manage end-to-end recruitment activities for various positions.
Source candidates through job portals, LinkedIn, referrals, and other channels.
Conduct candidate screening and initial interviews.
Coordinate interviews with hiring managers and ensure timely closure of positions.
Maintain recruitment trackers and MIS.

Campus Recruitment
Handle end-to-end campus hiring activities.
Coordinate with colleges, placement officers, and recruitment teams.
Organize campus drives, interviews, and selection processes.
Build and maintain relationships with educational institutions.

HR Operations
Manage employee lifecycle activities including onboarding, documentation, confirmation, transfers, and exits.
Maintain accurate employee records and HR databases.
Handle HR-related queries and employee coordination.
Manage HR processes through Zoho People.

Payroll & Compliance
Coordinate and support monthly payroll processing.
Maintain attendance, leave, and employee-related payroll inputs.
Handle statutory HR compliance activities including PF, ESIC, PT, TDS, and other applicable compliances.
Maintain compliance records and support audits when required.

🔹 Required Skills
2–3 years of relevant experience in HR Generalist / HR Operations / Talent Acquisition.
Strong experience in Talent Acquisition and Campus Recruitment.
Hands-on experience with Zoho People.
Good understanding of Payroll and HR Statutory Compliance.
Excellent verbal and written English communication skills.
Strong interpersonal, coordination, and problem-solving skills.
Good knowledge of MS Office / Excel.
Ability to manage multiple HR activities independently.
Immediate joiners preferred.
