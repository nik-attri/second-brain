🌹 Today’s Friday Flowers goes to my Swedish love, Irma Holmgren Holm
 
I always think it’s mad that little Irma and little Jamie grew up on separate continents, completely unaware that one day this Swedish powerhouse would have such a huge impact on this little South African’s life.
 
Irma is quite literally one of the reasons I’m on this wonderful career trajectory. She opened the first door that led to so many more.
 
At a time when I was starting over, navigating life as a single mother and figuring out what the bloody hell came next, Irma was there. The cheerleader I didn’t know I needed, seeing something in me before I knew what to do with it myself.
 
Naturally, we fell in love over colour-coordinated spreadsheets, problem solving, fresh stationery and an unhealthy appreciation for organised fun.
 
We were basically Monica Geller’s wet dream.
(Can I say that on LinkedIn? Anyway, I have now.)
 
Irma bloody LOVES a challenge. She adds colour, texture and approximately 14 levels of organisation to everything she touches, and you’ll thank her for it!
 
If we were stranded on a desert island with only sticks to eat, Irma would have a map locating them, a spreadsheet for the most efficient stick-acquisition strategy and, by day three, somehow they’d taste better.
 
Her precision and attention to detail are honestly unlike anything I’ve ever seen hey.
 
And somehow, amongst all that brilliance, I get to call her my friend. Gosh am I proud of her.
 
She’s also the only person in the world I could walk 26.2 miles with and not want to kill by the end, which says everything about our friendship. 😂
 
Before this starts sounding suspiciously like one of those… what do you call them… references 😂, a little insight into my Irma:
 
🌹 She’s been asking “WHY?” since kindergarten. Tell Irma “that’s just how it’s done” at your own risk hey. She wants to know why, how, who decided that and whether we should rethink the whole thing. Because you probably bloody should.
 
🌹 A recent reminder? Life is what you make it. If something isn’t serving you anymore, let it go and trust what comes next. It's most likely better! 
 
🌹 Her problem-solving brain starts with: Where are we now? What does everyone think? What are we trying to achieve? Give her A and B and she’ll work out the road between them.
 
🌹 Apparently some people find her intimidating. Hilarious to those of us who know the warm, deeply caring human underneath that formidable Swedish aura.
 
🌹 A lot of the dreams she had when she was younger, she’s actually gone and DONE. She’s systematically ticked off the life she once imagined. Yes Queen!
 
And the best bit? Younger Irma would look at her now and think, “She’s quite cool.” And I bloody agree.
 
Irma, thank you.
 
For opening that first door.
For your kindness.
For believing in me. 
For cheering me on when I needed it most.
 
Without you, I genuinely wouldn’t be where I am today, and I’ll forever be grateful for you.
 
My Swedish love. 🌹
