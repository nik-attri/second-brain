So here’s some news. After nearly 18 years with Honda, I've decided it's time to change models.
That's 18 years of meetings, launches, spreadsheets, strategies, flights, deadlines, achievements, presentations, challenges, friendships... and approximately 47,000 conversations about cars.
Honda has been an incredible company to work for. It has given me opportunities to learn, develop, grow, and work alongside some truly exceptional people. Looking back, I feel incredibly grateful for everything the company and its people have given me.
And they often say that work becomes family, which in my case is quite literal as it’s where I met my wife. Together, we then welcomed my son, my most successful product launch to date.
So, whilst I couldn’t be more excited for the next chapter, I owe this incredible company so much and leaving will be bittersweet.
Thank you to everyone who has been part of the journey. It's been one hell of a ride.
