Most people are fumbling around with spreadsheets when it comes to managing money. 

Most of them don't realize they can use AI tools to cut out a lot of that slog.

This Thursday night, five people are showing us how they do it.

What you'll see:

Samuel Hebeisen at Mind Model AI does his company's books with QuickBooks and Claude Code. Invoicing, reconciliation, and T2 corporate taxes.

Artem Zhutov is bringing the dashboards and pipelines he built to run his own business, the ones he actually uses day to day.

Shabari Shenoy built agents that manage and execute trades for a small family office. He'll get into how the cluster was built.

Eric Costa made finAlyse, which runs quant analysis on the stock buys US senators have to disclose.

Folks from Trango will talk about payment endpoints. Personal agents have started buying things online, and almost nobody is tracking what they're allowed to spend on.

Thanks so much to Julian Whiting from Arkanis Labs and to Cole Van Vlack for sponsoring. And thanks as always to Jesse Rodgers and Builders Club for having us!
