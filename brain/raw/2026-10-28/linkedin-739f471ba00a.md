As a technical founder, you spend months obsessing over positioning.

How do you explain the problem, the architecture, and the business value without sounding like a buzzword generator?

I searched 3650HQ on Google today, and the AI Overview caught me completely off guard:

"3650HQ is an African conversational commerce startup that has been featured in BusinessDay Nigeria and other major West African publications for its tech platform designed to help businesses reduce the cost and complexity of managing customer communication."

Seeing an LLM synthesize our entire mission so cleanly and cite our recent BusinessDay Nigeria feature in the same breath was surreal.

When we set out to build 3650HQ, our goal wasn't just to make another messaging tool. We saw African merchants drowning in scattered DMs, manual bank transfers, and expensive recurring software subscriptions that ate into their margins.

Google’s overview captured the three pillars we spent months building to solve that:

Unified Inbox: Bringing WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, and email into one command center.

Commerce Automation: Automating order flows, payments, and delivery tracking directly inside the chat.

Pay-As-You-Go Pricing: Ditching rigid monthly subscriptions so businesses only pay for active usage.

Getting featured in BusinessDay Nigeria is huge validation that conversational commerce isn't just an informal workaround - it is the backbone of retail across the continent, and it deserves robust infrastructure.

Massive credit to the entire team shipping behind the scenes. We're just getting started. 🚀
