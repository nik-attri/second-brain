Your company subsidizes employee meals. But can you track where every rupee goes? 
Think about this.
500 employees. ₹50 meal subsidy per person. 22 working days.
That's a potential ₹5.5 lakh monthly subsidy commitment if every employee uses the benefit every day.
Now comes the bigger question:
How do you track eligibility, actual consumption, employee contributions and vendor settlements without drowning in spreadsheets?
This is where digital meal subsidy management changes the process.
Here's how it works 👇
✅ HR/Admin defines subsidy policies and eligibility.
✅ Employees order meals through a digital platform.
✅ The system applies the eligible company contribution.
✅ Employees pay only their remaining share.
✅ Every transaction becomes part of the reporting and reconciliation process.
The real advantage isn't just cashless dining.
It's connecting employee benefits with actual cafeteria consumption.
For HR: Better visibility into benefit utilization.
For Finance: Clearer subsidy spending records.
For Admin: Less manual reconciliation.
For Employees: A simpler, more transparent dining experience.
