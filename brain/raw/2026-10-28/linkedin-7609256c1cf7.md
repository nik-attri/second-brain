The future of data analytics isn't just about dashboards. It's about asking better questions. 📊🤖

AI is changing how we work, but here's what I believe matters for aspiring Data Analysts:

→ Knowing which questions to ask
→ Understanding whether the data is reliable
→ Identifying meaningful patterns
→ Translating insights into business decisions
→ Using AI responsibly without blindly trusting its answers

Tools will continue to evolve. The ability to think critically about data will remain essential.

As someone building my career in data analytics, I'm focusing on more than just learning tools. I want to understand the business problems behind the numbers.
