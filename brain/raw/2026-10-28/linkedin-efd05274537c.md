Pulled the devil horns out of my tickle trunk this week. 😈

They suited the conversation.

A client and I were knee-deep in measurement hell. 

Three systems that don’t talk to each other.

Hours spent cutting & pasting to build a report that LOOKs like it came out of a simple export.

I call it "triangulating" - you pull what you have, correleate it into the most complete picture possible, and let experience and judgement carry the rest.

It's a necessary 'waste of time' because it's how Marketing stays credible while the fix is being built.

And the real fix is rarely the BI tool.

Plenty of companies have already paid for a BI dashboard while marketing data still lives in a spreadsheet beside it. 

Because the BI builders aren't fluent in marketing measurement so we get folded in last.

What moves it is a data vision the CEO, CFO and BI analyst can say yes to. What needs to connect, what changes for the business, and what visibility you need to get there.

That document is ours to write. No one else at the table can.

The horns go back in the trunk eventually. 

But not by waiting for the export to get smarter.
