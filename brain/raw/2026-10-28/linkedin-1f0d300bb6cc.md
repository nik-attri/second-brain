The only businesses I'd seriously consider building in 2027:

1. AI automation agencies for businesses drowning in repetitive work.

2. Micro-SaaS products solving one expensive problem for one niche.

3. Distribution businesses (media, newsletters, communities, personal brands).

4. AI-powered lead generation systems for service businesses.

5. Vertical AI agents for real estate, healthcare, law, and accounting.

6. Productized services that use AI to deliver results faster.

7. Niche directories that connect buyers with hard-to-find services.

8. AI tools that turn existing business data into actionable insights.

9. Customer acquisition systems for businesses struggling to get leads.

10. AI implementation firms helping traditional businesses automate operations.

11. Industry-specific software replacing spreadsheets and manual workflows.

12. Founder-led media businesses monetized through services, products, and subscriptions.

13. AI-powered research tools that help businesses make better decisions.


The opportunity isn't just building with AI.

It's solving problems people already pay to fix, then figuring out how to reach those people consistently.
