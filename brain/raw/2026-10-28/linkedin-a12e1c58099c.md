📢 WE’RE HIRING: JUNIOR PROJECT SUPPORT – JAPANESE SPEAKER

Dayalima Group is looking for a Project Support team member to support sourcing and recruitment for job opportunities in Japan. You will actively search for potential candidates through various channels, assess their suitability, and coordinate the recruitment process with candidates, LPK partners, clients, and TSK.

Employment Status: Contract (Partnership) 
Placement: South Jakarta

Key Responsibilities
- Source potential candidates for job opportunities in Japan through LPK partners and other relevant channels.
- Review candidate CVs and documents, and assess each candidate’s qualifications against the job requirements.
- Conduct initial candidate screening and evaluate whether candidates are ready to be recommended to clients/TSK.
- Coordinate with candidates and LPK partners on CV submissions, document completion, interview schedules, and follow-ups.
- Prepare and submit suitable candidate profiles to clients/TSK, and monitor their progress through the recruitment process.
- Maintain accurate candidate records, recruitment reports, and sourcing data.
- Handle recruitment administration, including contracts, Confirmation Letters, invoicing, and data entry into spreadsheets.

Qualifications
- Minimum Diploma (D3) or Bachelor’s degree (S1), any major.
- Proficient in spoken and written Japanese; JLPT N4 minimum, with N3 preferred.
- Detail-oriented, disciplined, and comfortable reviewing candidate information and handling administrative documents.
- Able to communicate and coordinate effectively with candidates, LPK partners, and other stakeholders.
- Able to manage multiple tasks and work well in a team.
- Fresh graduates are welcome to apply.
