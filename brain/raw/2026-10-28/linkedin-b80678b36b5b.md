The single most pointless 10 minutes in a job search: retyping your own work history into a form that will never improve your chances of getting hired.

We timed it. A typical employer application portal — not even an advanced one — takes 8-12 minutes of pure data entry before you even get to a cover letter or screening questions: name, address, every past role re-typed field by field, education, references, the works. None of that effort differentiates you from another candidate. It's just friction that has to happen before the part that actually matters.

That's the one piece of the process we think shouldn't require a human at all — which is why autofill across application portals exists inside AutoApplyMax, so that 10 minutes goes to zero and the time goes toward the parts of an application that actually move the needle.

Curious how this lands for others: what's the most absurd or repetitive step you've hit in an application portal — the kind of thing that made you question why it was even being asked?
