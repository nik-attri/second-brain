We’re Hiring: Financial Manager

Company: Mamoun Group
Location: Marghani, Heliopolis, Cairo
Job Type: Full-Time
Experience Required: Minimum 10 years

About the Role

Mamoun Group is seeking an experienced Financial Manager to join our Egypt office and lead back-office accounting and financial support for a U.S.-based construction company.

The successful candidate will work closely with U.S. management and external CPAs to maintain accurate financial records, deliver timely reports, and support budgeting, cash flow management, and project financial performance. Excellent written and spoken English is essential.

Key Responsibilities

Manage daily accounting operations, including accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger, and bank reconciliations.
Lead monthly and year-end closing and prepare accurate financial statements and management reports.
Prepare budgets, cash flow forecasts, and financial analyses.
Monitor project costs, profitability, billing, and collections.
Establish and maintain effective financial controls and reporting procedures.
Coordinate with U.S. management and external CPAs on financial reviews, audits, and tax preparation.
Supervise and develop the Egypt-based accounting team.
Required Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or Finance.
Minimum 10 years of relevant accounting and finance experience, including experience managing an accounting team.
Excellent command of English, both written and spoken.
Strong knowledge of financial reporting, budgeting, cash flow management, and internal controls.
Advanced Excel skills and experience with accounting software.
Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills, with close attention to detail.
Preferred Qualifications

Experience supporting U.S.-based companies is a strong plus.
CPA certification is a plus.
Familiarity with U.S. GAAP and U.S. accounting practices.
Construction accounting experience, including job costing and work-in-progress reporting.
What We Offer

Competitive salary based on experience and qualifications.
Professional development and career growth opportunities.
A collaborative environment working directly with a U.S.-based business.
To Apply

Please send your updated CV to hiring@mamoungroup.com with the subject line “Financial Manager – Egypt Office.”
