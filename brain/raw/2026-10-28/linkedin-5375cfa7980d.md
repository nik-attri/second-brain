Something I find fascinating about the current AI + payroll shift:
A few years ago, we were asking:
“Can AI help payroll?”
Now we're asking:
“What should AI be allowed to do?”
That's a completely different conversation.
AI can already help detect anomalies, investigate payroll issues and prepare actions.
The next challenge is deciding:
What can it do alone?
What needs approval?
What must always stay human?
I think the future payroll professional won't be the person who does everything manually.
And they won't be the person who lets AI do everything.
They'll be the person who knows where to draw the line.
