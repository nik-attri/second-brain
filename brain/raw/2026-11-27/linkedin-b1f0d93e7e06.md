Six months ago, I joined Owen Thomas, and what a six months it’s been!

I’ll be the first to admit I probably didn’t fully appreciate what I was getting myself into. Coming into a startup, with probably the least amount of experience in the team, learning a new market whilst also helping to grow and develop it has been one hell of a learning curve.

I remember plenty of Friday nights when my friends were in the pub and I’d take myself to Nando’s, get a table for one and sit there until 10pm working through Excel spreadsheets, mapping out projects and doing everything I could to set myself up for the following week.

In true Louise style I had plenty of moments thinking, what is going on? Am I actually going to be able to do this? 🤣

But when I want something, I give it everything I’ve got. I made a conscious decision to really throw myself into this, prioritise my routine, focus on my fitness and spend a lot of my spare time doing reformer Pilates. Because looking after your headspace is just as important as putting the hours in.

I’m also incredibly competitive (ask my sisters lol) so naturally I wanted to win, and I wanted to win straight away. Learning that building and growing something takes time has probably been one of my biggest lessons.

We’ve also had plenty of laughs along the way, which definitely helps when you work with a great team!!! 

Harry and Nathan you guys are pretty great. So proud to be at Owen Thomas and building what we’re building.

Six months down. This is just the beginning. ❤️
