The old me would have cut him off to list features. This time I shut the hell up.

I was on a call with a prospect when he started describing a tool he wished he had: a way to re-engage old leads who went quiet. He had a spreadsheet for every listing from the past few years, with every lead who ever inquired about it. 

The old me would have jumped in with "we're building that!" and rattled off a list of features, as this tool is already something on the Evitas docket.

Instead, I let him finish. Then he got specific. He wanted the first message to mention the address of the listing they originally asked about, so they'd remember who he was. That detail only came out because I wasn't talking.

When he was done, I told him it was already on the roadmap. He wanted in.

When a prospect is describing the problem, they're writing your pitch.

He talked for two minutes and I said almost nothing. Best pitch I gave all week.
