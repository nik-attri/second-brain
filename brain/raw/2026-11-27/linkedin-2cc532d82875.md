Last week LinkedIn proudly informed me my posts had received 15,285 impressions.
It's also the week I started talking bollocks in between talking about work.



Two weeks on, here's where we are.
I've posted 24 times:
12 text posts
4 videos
4 photos
A couple of carousels
And, would you believe, an actual article.
13 were bollocks.
10 were about work.
(Yes, that's only 23. I make pictures, not spreadsheets. Now is clear why I used to work in banking )

The bollocks posts beat the work posts by roughly 8 to 1.
Best bollocks post: 12,209 impressions.
Work posts: 2,202 combined.

I've also commented a shit tonne. 
One comment apparently reached nearly 29,000 people.
Clearly, I've found my calling.

But wait, there is more to this...
My profile views have doubled. A handful of people have joined the conversation. I can sadly count the DMs on the fingers of one hand.

And not one person has asked how I can help them get noticed, create better content or feel more comfortable in front of a camera.
Not one.

Several days after asking whether LinkedIn is the new Tinder, I'm beginning to think I've become quite good at getting right-swiped but haven't actually managed to get a date.


Apparently it would seem, being interesting gets you noticed.
Being noticed doesn't necessarily mean anyone knows what the hell you do.
I could carry on talking bollocks and watch the numbers climb.
Or I could remember that I'm actually here to run a business.
Perhaps I'll just do both.

Keep people entertained, give them something worth remembering, and occasionally remind them why I'm here.
12,000 impressions are lovely.
But they don't pay the mortgage.

Right. Back to talking bollocks.
What could possibly go wrong?
