If a real hands-on skill doesn't match the exact keyword syntax your ATS filter expects, to your company that engineer doesn't exist.

Boards often assume automated screening brings discipline and scale to hiring. In practice, keyword-driven AI filters turn recruiting into an arms race where candidates use LLMs to mirror job-description buzzwords while strong builders who write plain, honest resumes get auto-rejected.

At the same time, hiring managers buried under 50-plus applications per opening lean on rigid proxies because manual triage does not scale. That bottleneck does more than hurt talent quality. Threat actors such as DPRK-linked Famous Chollima and Jasper Sleet—documented by Mandiant and Microsoft—rely on polished, keyword-tailored personas and laptop-farm operations to slip into remote engineering and IT roles.

For CISOs and CHROs, this distorts both P&L and security posture. False negatives starve defensive teams of real builders, while false positives place unvetted or synthetic identities inside privileged environments, turning a recruiting shortcut into an insider threat.

Replacing keyword-density auto-rejections fixes the funnel without drowning managers in noise. Pair an early, asynchronous 30-minute practical log or architecture triage exercise with a live, identity-verified troubleshooting walkthrough where candidates explain real trade-offs without AI assistance.

An automated hiring filter that rewards keyword mimicry over proof of craft invites synthetic risk inside.
What is the first keyword filter your CISO and CHRO will retire this quarter in favor of real diagnostic proof?
