Sales team setups in 2027.

AI has changed the way sales teams work.

Working firsthand with hundreds of sellers & marketers and building our own outbound motion, we're seeing where this is heading.

Despite the uncertainty, the jobs of BDRs & AEs won't be replaced. 

Their objective is still the same.

But the outreach volume they can manage has massively changed.

An old-school BDR could do:
- 50 manually researched calls/day
- 10 personalized emails/day
- 2-3 demos booked/week

A new-school BDR can do:
- 200-400 calls/day with power dialing
- 30-50 hyper-personalized emails/day (AI-drafted)
- 7-10 demos booked/week

This is only possible with proper RevOps & GTM Engineering infrastructure.

We always had tools.

AI just brought better ones:
• Clay + Claude Code to orchestrate data and run automations
• AI Ark, Ocean.io, DiscoLike to find the right accounts
• Nooks to power through calls with pre-researched lists
• Instantly and HeyReach to automate email and LinkedIn campaigns
• Ergo to record every conversation and turn it into data intelligence
• HubSpot, Qwilr and Mutiny to close deals

The best teams in 2027 won't just hire more people.

They'll get more out of every person they hire.

The opportunity has never been bigger for the teams that move first.
