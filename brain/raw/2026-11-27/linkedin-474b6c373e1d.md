Black Friday is here.

Today, one of the most useful things to measure may not be traffic or conversion.

It may be: How many manual interventions did it take to keep orders moving?
Re-keying orders.
Checking stock.
Updating systems.
Chasing status.
Resolving exceptions.

Higher volume doesn’t create those processes. It makes their cost easier to see.

So once today is over, don’t only ask: “How many orders did we process?”

Also ask: “What did we have to do manually to process them?”
