Most companies buy AI expecting it to replace strategic thinking.

That is why 80% of corporate AI budgets burn cash with zero return.

AI is not a substitute for human judgment on narrow, high-stakes decisions. Its real commercial leverage appears when you have to understand complex data across a wide operational footprint:
→ 50,000 SKUs moving across multiple warehouses with hidden spoilage leaks.
→ Millions of customer transactions hiding subtle churn patterns.
→ Fragmented regional pricing data where manual spreadsheet audits take months.

If you have 12 enterprise clients, a sharp operator with a spreadsheet beats an expensive AI model every time.

If you are drowning in wide, messy operational data across dozens of markets, AI is the fastest way to spot where your margins are quietly bleeding.

Stop buying AI to look innovative. Deploy it only where the volume of data exceeds human visibility.
