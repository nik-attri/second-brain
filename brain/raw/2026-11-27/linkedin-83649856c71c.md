Imagine someone asking what you do, and you knowing EXACTLY what to say to get them asking for more info, nodding along and wanting to buy.

Inside your head, you know what you do, and your clients obviously love working with you.

But however hard you try to explain it to new people, it doesn't really feel like they "get it". They ask what feels like really silly questions, their eyes glaze over, and the conversation dies a death before it even started.

Does that just mean they aren't your ideal client? Does it mean nobody wants to work with you?

Absolutely not.

All it means, in the nicest way possible, is that you need to be a hell of a lot more clear in the way you speak.

The temptation is to jump into explaining what we "do", our background, why our work is important...

But the reality is, we all just care about ourselves and so you need to grab the attention of the listener right away, and make it about THEM (your ideal client).

This goes for how you introduce yourself at a networking event, the words you use in your social media content, how you speak on a discovery call or in the DMs... EVERYWHERE.

Let's take your answer to "so, what do you do??" as an example here.

These three sentences light people up and get them taking action:

1⃣ The very first sentence has one and only one job 👉 to grab attention in a way that makes them think "oh hey that's me"

Don't give them your job title or start explaining what you do. Instead ask them a question or make a statement about the situation they are in right now.

For example, a bookkeeper could say: "You know that feeling when your accountant asks for receipts and you open a shoebox?"

2⃣ And then your second line should be the thing that fixes that problem they are currently facing. Not describing how you're going to help them, but describing what the outcome is going to be for them (get them excited!).

The fix could be: "I turn that into a five minute monthly task."

3⃣ And then finally, we end with a short bonus line. This helps you stand out, be remembered, and handles objections before they even come up.

For our final line, we could add "without 15 new apps or yet ANOTHER spreadsheet"

And then... you STOP TALKING 🤣

I know it's hard. But more words does not make people understand it better.

The more you understand who your client is, how your work is different from competitors, and the words you need to use to speak about it... the easier this becomes.
