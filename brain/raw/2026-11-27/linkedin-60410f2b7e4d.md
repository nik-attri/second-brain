Your CRM says John Doe lives in Berlin. Your billing system says he’s in Munich. Your support tool has three "J. Does" and none of them have an active subscription.
Welcome to data sprawl. And it’s quietly breaking your analytics.

When companies scale, systems multiply—and the "single source of truth" turns into five conflicting versions of reality. Everyone looks at the same customer, product, or vendor, yet sees completely different numbers.

That’s where MDM (Master Data Management) comes in.
Think of MDM not as "just another database," but as the referee for your data. It pulls fragmented records across your stack, strips out duplicates, resolves conflicts, and creates one Golden Record that every team can actually trust.

Why it’s a game-changer:
AI & BI without hallucinations. You can’t train reliable AI agents or trust dashboards when one customer exists five times under different IDs.
Zero operational friction. Logistics gets the right address, finance gets the right billing info, and manual CSV reconciliations disappear.
One shared language. Paired with a semantic layer, MDM ensures every department talks about the exact same entities.

Who actually needs MDM?
Growing & enterprise teams drowning in a zoo of ERPs, CRMs, and app databases.
Companies in M&A mode trying to merge customer bases and product catalogs without losing their minds.
Teams building Data Mesh or AI agents—because decentralized data domains only work when the core entities underneath are rock-solid.
When to skip it: If you're an early-stage startup with one database and a few dbt models, full-blown MDM is overkill. Stick to clean transformations and basic data hygiene until the pain is real.

How are you taming duplicate records right now—duct-taping it with SQL scripts, or running dedicated MDM?
