Hiring: MIS Executive – Telecom Warehouse & Inventory

📍 Location: Bhubaneswar, Odisha
💰 Salary: ₹22,000 – ₹25,000 In-Hand
💼 Industry: Telecom

Job Description

We are looking for an experienced MIS Executive to manage warehouse data, inventory tracking, ERP/WMS operations, reporting, and reconciliation activities within the telecom industry.

Key Responsibilities

- Inventory & Asset Tracking: Manage end-to-end inventory records and ensure data accuracy for serialized telecom equipment.
- ERP & WMS Management: Handle Oracle ERP and Warehouse Management System (WMS), including GRN, MIN, and stock transfers.
- MIS & KPI Reporting: Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly reports and dashboards for inventory accuracy, order fill rate, aging stock, dispatch TAT, and space utilization.
- Audit & Reconciliation: Conduct cycle counts, physical stock verification, system reconciliation, and audit documentation.
- Reverse Logistics: Track RMA, faulty equipment returns from field sites/towers, and refurbishment processes.
- Team Coordination: Coordinate with warehouse teams, data entry operators, supply chain teams, and finance departments.

Required Skills

- Strong knowledge of MS Excel and MIS reporting.
- Experience with Oracle ERP and WMS.
- Knowledge of inventory management and stock reconciliation.
- Understanding of GRN, MIN, RMA, and stock transfers.
- Good analytical, communication, and coordination skills.
- Previous experience in the Telecom Industry is preferred
