LODET AI | Building Our Own Advertising Intelligence
At LODET, we are developing more than advertising campaigns. 
We are building the operational intelligence to plan, 
configure, 
test, 
monitor, and progressively automate our own advertising workflows.
Our latest internal case study focuses on a Meta Ads campaign for Grand Tunis, 
built around a Messenger conversion strategy.
What we are engineering:
Geographic targeting: 
Restricting delivery to Tunis, 
Ariana, 
Ben Arous and 
Manouba.
Campaign architecture: 
Configuring Meta Sales campaigns with Messenger as the exclusive conversion destination.
🎀Creative management: 
Preparing and validating video advertising assets and messaging.
Conversational commerce: Designing Messenger greetings, automated responses, and order-confirmation workflows.
🎀Budget governance: 
Establishing spending limits, delivery assumptions, and financial scenarios before activation.
🎀Risk management: 
Auditing payment status, account restrictions, pricing consistency, and delivery commitments.
🎀Performance intelligence:
 Defining measurable indicators, including cost per conversation, conversion rate, delivered orders, and return rates.
🎀Our engineering principle: 
Draft. Validate. Simulate. Authorize. Execute.
The current Grand Tunis campaign remains in draft mode. No advertisement has been published, 
and no advertising budget has been spent.
We deliberately separate preparation and automation from financial authorization. 
Publishing remains an explicit owner-controlled action.
We are also working toward extending regional campaign management directly through the LODET agent, rather than relying exclusively on manual Ads Manager configuration.
Why does this matter?
Because advertising should become an accountable operational workflow, not simply a button that spends money.
At LODET, we are developing our own capabilities across AI, marketing technology, conversational commerce, and agentic automation.
