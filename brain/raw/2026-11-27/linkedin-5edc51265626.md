🚀 Linear is now integrated with Boilerplate Builders!
You can now connect Linear to your automated workflows and AI Agents, making project management an integral part of your automation processes.
With our new integration, you can manage issues, sub-issues, projects, cycles, labels, comments, attachments, and more directly within your workflows.
You can also automatically trigger pipelines whenever an issue, comment, or attachment event occurs in Linear using an OAuth connection.
Every supported Linear operation can be independently enabled as a tool for your AI Agents, giving you full control over how they interact with your projects and tasks.
Whether you want to automate repetitive project management activities, streamline issue tracking, or build more autonomous AI-powered workflows, you can now do it with Boilerplate Builders.
