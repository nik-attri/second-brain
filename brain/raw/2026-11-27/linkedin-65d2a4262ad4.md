It took years to build your customer database. An AI agent could destroy it in seconds.

As marketers, we increasingly use AI to analyze customer lists, segment audiences, automate CRM workflows, and personalize campaigns.

But are we protecting our most valuable business asset?

In April 2026, a Cursor AI agent powered by Claude reportedly deleted PocketOS’s production database and its associated backups in just nine seconds.

This incident should be a wake-up call for every business leader.

Before connecting your customer database to any AI agent, consider these seven safeguards:

🔹 Read-only access: Allow AI to analyze data without modifying or deleting original records.

🔹 Independent backups: Maintain recoverable copies outside the AI agent’s reach.

🔹 Data anonymization: Remove unnecessary personal and sensitive customer information.

🔹 Human approval: Require authorization before critical database operations.

🔹 Restricted permissions: Give AI access only to what it needs.

🔹 AI safety skills: Configure dedicated instructions governing how AI handles customer data—but never rely on instructions alone.

🔹 Data governance: Establish clear policies for privacy, security, retention, and accountability.

AI can make your marketing smarter, faster, and more efficient. But uncontrolled automation can put years of business growth at risk.

The future belongs not just to businesses that adopt AI, but to those that know how to control it.

Before giving AI access to your customer database, ask yourself:

If something goes wrong tomorrow, can we recover everything?
