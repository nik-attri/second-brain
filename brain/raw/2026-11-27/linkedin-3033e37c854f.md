This is pretty brutal, no denying it.

Lightbulb moment yesterday, on a call a NETER, who called out the need to belong. 

Yet are companies where people still belong?

The waves of inevitable layoffs, as vendors slash costs and automate process with agentic workflows. 

In 1-2-3 years, will we look back on the rule of 40 as easy street? Smart boards are planning for faster growth, massively reduced cost, and a corporate engine run by digital robots.

When Fabiola Enciso and I founded paceGTM, our vision included creating a collective of on-demand experts who can drive commercial outcome, fast. "No strategy theater": best-in-market AI and GTM agents and processes, to push growth upwards.

Another call I had yesterday: a friend who took a year to get a role, after redundancy, only to be back on the market again, six months later.

Corporate structure x the human need to belong, to a tribe, a team and a mission, may no longer work for many professionals.

If you can build agents, generate genuine demand, and/or close deals, get in touch. We're building the collective. Demand is strong.

Taking the leap into being your own entity, might just be the best next step you can take.
