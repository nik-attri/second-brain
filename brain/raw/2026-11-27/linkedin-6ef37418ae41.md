DAY 12/30: Imagine having to do all of this manually for every new customer. 

A potential customer visits a business website and fills out an enquiry form.

Someone now has to collect their details, create a contact record, send a welcome email and notify the sales team.

Now imagine doing that for dozens or even hundreds of enquiries.

This is where automation becomes particularly interesting!

During our class at TS Academy, we explored a practical example called Lead Capture Automation.

The idea is to create a workflow that automatically:

1. Captures information submitted through a website form.
2. Creates a contact in a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system.
3. Sends a welcome email to the potential customer.
4. Notifies the sales team about the new lead.

Think about it: one form submission can set several activities in motion without someone having to manually handle every step.

What I find fascinating is how different applications can work together to create one connected process.

It also helped me appreciate that automation isn’t limited to eliminating repetitive tasks. It can improve how businesses respond to people, manage information and organise their operations.

And honestly, I’m becoming more curious about how to build these workflows myself.

Understanding the concept is one thing. Actually creating one? That’s something I’m looking forward to!
