EU hiring sits 26% below January 2019 levels.
It fell a further 15% in the last year alone.

LinkedIn's own Economic Graph data, published 8 June 2026, finds hiring across the EU still 26% short of pre-pandemic levels and the UK 24% short, with entry-level roles tracking the same rate rather than falling faster. LinkedIn is explicit that AI is not the cause: roles with higher AI exposure are not declining faster than others. The driver is tighter finances and lower business confidence.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. A structural decline from automation would be close to permanent. A cyclical one, caused by confidence and financing conditions, reverses when those conditions do, and conditions always eventually turn.

So plan your search on cyclical timing, not permanent scarcity. This is a worse market than 2019, not a broken one, and worse markets reward whoever kept their pipeline running through them. Keep applying, keep the conversations warm, and be ready to move fast the moment confidence starts to return. It always does.
