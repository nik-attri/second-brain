The more data a company has, the easier it can become to lose sight of what’s actually happening.

A growing D2C business can have Shopify, Meta, Google Analytics, CRM, ERP, payment gateways, inventory systems, finance tools—and 20 different dashboards.
The data is everywhere. 📊

But revenue drops.
CAC moves.
Ad spend goes up without improving customer value.

And suddenly the most important question is:
“Why?”

Answering it often means jumping between systems, reconciling numbers, opening spreadsheets, and manually connecting dots that were never designed to connect. 🧩

I’ve been thinking about this a lot over the past week while building around decision intelligence.

And one thing is becoming increasingly clear:
The problem isn't always a lack of data.
It’s a lack of context.

For founders like Vineeta Singh, Varun Alagh and Ghazal Alagh, who have built and scaled businesses across multiple channels and increasingly complex operations, this problem only gets harder with scale.

Because eventually, the question changes from:
“Do we have the data?”
to:
“Can we actually see the business clearly?” 🔎

A true single source of truth shouldn’t just tell you what happened.
It should connect the fragmented pieces, explain why it happened, expose what’s changing and show what deserves attention next. 🧠

Because better decisions don't come from having more dashboards.
They come from seeing the whole picture. ⚡
