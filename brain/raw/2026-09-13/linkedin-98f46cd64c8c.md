Excited to share ClaimPilot, our project for the Agents Everywhere hackathon.
Insurance claims can involve a lot of repetitive work — reading emails, checking information, identifying duplicates, creating tasks, preparing documentation, and waiting for approval.

We built ClaimPilot as an AI agent that fits directly into the existing communication workflow.

Here’s what it does:
📩 Reads incoming claim emails
🔎 Verifies the claim and detects potential duplicates
📝 Creates tasks and supporting documentation
👀 Posts a structured review card for human approval
📤 Sends the response automatically once approved

The important part is that the agent doesn't simply automate everything blindly. Human approval remains part of the workflow when a decision matters.

Built for the #AgentsEverywhere hackathon with AI Tinkerers, OpenAI, CopilotKit, OpenRouter, Exa, Auth0, Ambiguous AI, Trigger.dev, Mozilla.ai, and Google Cloud.
