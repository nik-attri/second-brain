AML compliance is not just a fraud problem. It's a problem with documentation.

At Limaz, we built Naiza to solve both at once.

Here's what happens behind the scenes.

Every individual or entity gets screened, automatically, against three categories: sanctions lists, Politically Exposed Persons, and watchlists.

Not manually. Not on a spreadsheet someone updates when they remember to.
Every check returns structured match context, not a vague "flagged" status with nothing behind it.

Your team sees exactly what matched, why it matched, and what to do next.

That's the difference between a compliance process you can actually defend to a regulator, and one you're just hoping never gets tested.
Naiza. Built for real, auditable AML review, not only for catching fraud.
