We automate processes. We don't automate relationships.

One of the most common reasons clients tell us they came to Linx is surprisingly simple:

They wanted to be able to reach someone.

Many of them already had an EOR provider.The technology worked. The countries were covered. Employees could be onboarded.

But when something went wrong — a payroll issue, a benefits question, a difficult termination, an urgent compliance situation — getting the right person to actually own the problem became the problem.

That has shaped how we've built Linx.

We believe in automation. In fact, we've automated much of the work behind global employment — onboarding workflows, documentation, payroll checks, compliance monitoring, reporting and repetitive administration.

And we're continuing to invest heavily in AI.

But we've been very deliberate about what we automate and what we don't.

We automate the process. We don't automate the customer or employee relationship.

Because the purpose of automation should be to give our teams more time for customers and employees, not less.

If an employee has a payroll problem, they shouldn't feel like ticket #8472.
If a customer has an urgent issue in Brazil, India or Poland, they shouldn't have to navigate a chatbot, a knowledge base and three levels of escalation before finding someone who can make a decision.

They should know who to call.
And the person they call should own the problem until it's resolved.

This matters because EOR is fundamentally different from most SaaS.
We aren't just providing software.We are responsible for people's employment, pay, benefits and some of the most important moments in their working lives.

That's why I believe customer support and employee support aren't functions sitting alongside the product.They are part of the product.

Country coverage is becoming easier to replicate. Technology will continue to improve. AI will automate more of the administrative layer of employment.

But trust, responsiveness and accountability are much harder to automate.
And increasingly, they're exactly why clients come to us.
Automate the work. Never automate the relationship.
