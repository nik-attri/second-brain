Something a little different today. Some may say a bit out there. Some may say, you're on to something, let's explore this some more.....  

Why Kowa is Leaning Into "Vibe-Based Evaluation"

Western evaluation argues that 'real evidence' is logic models, rigid data indicators and neat percetages.

That data is numbers, fact and figures, charts and spreadsheets.

But what if it was something that wasn't so neatly collected or quantified?!

What if.... it was what was embodied, what we see, hear, feel and collectively sense about an initative, program, service...

This IS data, in real-time, and it's often a hell of a lot more accurate than a questionnaire that someone filled out just to get a free lunch. 

We want to lean into Vibe-Based Evaluation as a respected way of measuring change. 

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander ways of knowing, have been guiding us, since time immemorial. This is that. A knowing, instinct, sense...

We don't have all the answers yet, but we're ready to find out. It’s time to stop over-complicating things with jargon and start trusting the vibe. 

We'd love to know what you think about Skye's paper, let's get the conversation started and see where this takes us.....
