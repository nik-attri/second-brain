One thing I’ve learned working in integration:

Most companies do not have an integration problem.

They have a “how the hell are we supposed to keep track of all this?” problem.

APIs over here.
Cloud stuff over there.
On prem systems that refuse to die.
Three different teams managing five different tools.

Absolute chaos.

That’s why I actually think IBM Integration Hybrid Control Plane is pretty cool.

Best way I can describe it:

Air traffic control for your integration environment.

You are not ripping everything out and starting over. You are just finally getting one place to see what is running, where it is running, and who is doing what.
Basically, less integration spaghetti. 

More knowing what the hell is going on.

And when your environment starts looking like a group project where nobody knows who owns what, that becomes pretty valuable pretty fast.

Curious how other teams are handling this today.

Is everything actually centralized, or are we all just pretending the spreadsheet is under control?
