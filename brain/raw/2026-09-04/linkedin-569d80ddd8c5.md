AI isn’t making companies more efficient. It’s exposing how inefficient they already were.
Give someone AI and they can write an email in 30 seconds.
Great.
But then they copy the information into a CRM.
Update a spreadsheet.
Message someone on Slack.
Wait for somebody else to update another system.
And on Friday, someone manually creates a report explaining what happened.
We’ve put a Ferrari engine inside a car with square wheels.
The interesting part of AI isn’t generating things faster.
It’s asking a more uncomfortable question:
Why does this task exist in the first place?
Why does a human need to move that information?
Why are we producing that report?
Why do three systems need the same data?
Why does somebody need to remember to follow up?
Before adding AI to a workflow, I think businesses should delete, simplify or redesign the workflow itself.
Otherwise we’re not transforming work.
We’re just automating the mess.
