Before buying another dashboard, ask what happens before the dashboard exists.

Where do the numbers originate?

Which definitions are consistent across the business?

What is changed manually?

Where does reconciliation happen?

Who owns each handoff?

What decision must the final report support?

A dashboard is the final layer. It can display information clearly, but it cannot repair disconnected source systems, inconsistent definitions or a reporting process that depends on spreadsheets and key person knowledge.

AlignIQ starts by mapping the reporting reality. Only then do we determine what should be standardised, integrated, automated or redesigned.
