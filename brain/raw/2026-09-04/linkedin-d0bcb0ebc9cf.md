I spent a lot of time in labs during my biotech undergrad, where every data point had to be defensible. No hand-waving, no 'trust me'.

Now I watch companies bolt AI onto old products and call it strategy.

That's not strategy. That's feature warfare. Faster, cheaper and the same.

If you want uncontested market space, apply Blue Ocean Strategy to AI. Don't apply AI to the strategy you already have.

1. Change the question

Stop asking "how do we beat competitors?"

Start asking "which problems is nobody solving at all?"

Blue oceans come from noncustomers - people who avoid your industry because it's too complex, too expensive, or too slow. People improvising fixes in spreadsheets and WhatsApp because they don't trust existing players with their data.

AI turns strategic the moment you serve the people the industry ignored, not when you automate what incumbents already do.

2. Run the ERRC grid on your AI, not your feature list

Eliminate - jargon-heavy UX, manual config hell, black-box decisions with no explanation

Reduce - cognitive load from endless dashboards, time to first value

Raise - transparency, provenance, context-aware personalization

Create - AI-native workflows that never existed before

That's value innovation: cost down, buyer value up, far enough that you stop being comparable.

3. One test that cuts through the noise

If your product vanished tomorrow, would customers go back to the old way or would the old way now feel unacceptable?

You're in a blue ocean when buyers stop comparing you to your category at all.

4. The 30–60 day AI Blue Ocean Sprint

→ Map the red ocean (speed, accuracy, UX, transparency, price)

→ Interview noncustomers to find what's actually blocking them

→ Build your ERRC grid across capability, UX, pricing, trust

→ Prototype one AI-native workflow that makes the old way feel painful

→ Launch into a niche and own it first

5. The mindset shift

Red ocean question: "How do we add AI so we don't fall behind?"

Blue ocean question: "How do we use AI to make the current game irrelevant?"

The only way to win an AI arms race is to stop entering it.
