PMs, your customer just gave you 50,000 problems. And 30 days to solve them. 👀


Imagine you’re working at a company that helps e-commerce businesses make their product catalog and listings more AI-enabled.

One of your customers has around 50,000 SKUs.

And they come to you and say:

“A lot of our products aren’t performing well. Can you help us fix this?”

You discuss it with your manager.

And your manager says:

“We have 30 days. What are we going to do?”

Now you’re the PM. 😅

Where do you start?

Do you start looking at the data?

Do you talk to the customer first?

Do you look at the listing quality?

Do you check pricing, traffic, conversion, inventory, search ranking, categories, images, descriptions…?

Or is there something else you’d want to understand before even touching the problem?

And there’s another catch.

There are 50,000 SKUs.

You obviously can’t manually go through all of them in 30 days.

So how would you figure out where the actual problem is?

And once you find it…

How would you decide what is worth fixing in those 30 days?

I’m deliberately not putting my approach here because I’m genuinely curious about how different PMs would think about this.

If you were in this situation:

👉 What would you do first?
👉 What questions would you ask the customer?
👉 What data would you look at?
👉 How would you narrow down 50,000 SKUs?
👉 And what would you not spend time on?

Would love to hear how you’d approach it — especially if you’ve dealt with something similar in the real world.

No textbook answers please. 😄
Tell me what you’d actually do.
