SnowGeek Solutions + ServiceNow: the AI governance gap public-sector agencies cannot delegate away.

I now track UAH: Undocumented Agent Handoffs: the number of agent/tool interactions discovered during an architecture review with no recorded owner, bounded protocol, version authority, execution trace, or retirement path.

That indicator matters when an AI agent can discover an MCP server, invoke a tool, or delegate to another agent. Without a controlled registry and bounded interaction protocols, a simple workflow can become an unbounded mission path: with unclear authority, data-residency exposure, weak audit evidence, and difficult incident response.

For mission-critical agencies, digital sovereignty is not only where the platform runs. It is knowing which agent, model, provider, tool, and connected service can act: and proving why, under whose policy, and with what trace.

FedRAMP High authorization of the ServiceNow platform does not automatically authorize every individual agent, model, capability, tool, provider, connected service, or agent-to-agent path.
