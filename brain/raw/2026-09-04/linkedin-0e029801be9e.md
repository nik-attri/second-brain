A year ago, MSPortal launched at CanITCon. On September 9–11, we’ll be back for #CanITCon2026, and it’s pretty wild to think about how much has changed in twelve months.

When we launched last year, I was confident in the idea and passionate about the problem we were trying to solve for MSPs, but you never really know how launching a business is going to go. 

If I’m being fair, the app wasn’t exactly ready for prime time. Some of those early demos were... interesting. Bugs appeared at the worst possible times, things broke that had worked perfectly five minutes earlier, and there were definitely moments where I was building the plane while flying it (Some of my old colleagues know that's where I thrive most!)

Thankfully, a few MSPs believed in what we were building and took a chance on a tiny startup. We onboarded our first customers in late December and early January.

Today, we have more than 30 MSP clients and we’re onboarding another 1-2 every week. We’ve expanded into Europe, where we already have around 10 clients, and MSPortal now sees upwards of 2,000 daily active users.

More importantly, I get to watch MSPs use something I built to fundamentally change how they handle account management, client service, projects, reporting, QBRs, onboarding and the countless other workflows that used to live across disconnected tools and spreadsheets.

The last year has also been one hell of an education. Learning how to sell. Building a pipeline. Talking to prospects. Supporting customers. Prioritizing an endless stream of great feature requests. And somehow maintaining our slightly ridiculous goal of shipping meaningful updates every single week. Somehow we haven't missed a week!

It’s been a lot. It’s also been incredibly satisfying.

MSPortal is almost unrecognizable compared to the product I nervously demoed at CanITCon last year, and I’m incredibly excited to bring it back to the place where this whole thing really started.

If year one was about proving this could work, I have a feeling year two is going to be about seeing just how big we can make it, and those who know me know that I dream BIG. 

See you at CanITCon 2026. 🇨🇦
