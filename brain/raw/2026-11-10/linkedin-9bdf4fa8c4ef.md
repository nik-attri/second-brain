Moving an assessment online is an opportunity to improve the process around it.

If administrators still have to enter the same candidate details into several systems, reconcile bookings manually or guide candidates through disconnected steps, those difficulties remain part of the assessment experience.

Understanding that workflow should come early. How do candidates register and book? How do they access their assessment? Where is invigilation needed, and what information must move between systems?

At Certify Assessment Solutions, these are the practical questions behind our assessment technology. Nexus supports registration and scheduling, Examiner supports assessment authoring and delivery, and Invigilate supports remote invigilation.

The starting point is understanding which parts of the process need support and how they should connect with the organisation’s existing systems. A professional certification programme and a university examination may need different arrangements, even when they use similar technology.

A well-designed workflow gives administrators clearer oversight and candidates a more straightforward route through their assessment.
