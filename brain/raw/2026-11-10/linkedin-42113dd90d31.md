𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗖𝗵𝗮𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗻𝗴𝗲

One of our clients was facing a financial discrepancy of approximately PKR 20 million.

The financial records weren't matching, creating concerns about transaction accuracy and internal financial control.

𝗢𝘂𝗿 𝗔𝗽𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗮𝗰𝗵

Rather than simply correcting the figures, our ERP technical and support team investigated the issue at its source.

We:
✔ Analysed financial records across relevant ERP modules
✔ Traced transaction entries and identified inconsistencies
✔ Investigated how different departments were recording transactions
✔ Identified the root cause of the discrepancy

Our investigation revealed that an employee in the accounts department had missed recording certain transactions in one section of the ERP, even though the relevant information was available in other sections.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻

Using the interconnected modules of our customized ERP system, we traced the missing transactions, identified the source of the mismatch, and helped the client reconcile their financial records.

The issue was identified through the system, without relying on guesswork or manually reviewing every business transaction.
