Building an AI agent is one thing. 
Can your team test it, decide what it’s allowed to do, and show that it improves a real workflow?
That’s what we’ll work on in Sepenta Pro. 
I’m sharing the full day-by-day syllabus for our next cohort, starting 4 January 2027.

Over four weeks, you’ll work with a team to take one AI-enabled workflow from business discovery to a release decision.

Week 1 · Plan : Define the problem, practise PI Planning and turn the work into a Jira backlog.
Week 2 · Build : Use Claude Code and GitHub to build a working agent, with clear permissions and a human approval step.
Week 3 · Test and deploy : Test how the agent behaves when things go wrong, automate the checks and deploy to cloud staging.
Week 4 · Demo and decide : Show what works, review the remaining risks and decide whether to release, run a limited pilot or hold.

Sepenta Pro is for experienced Product Owners, Product Managers, Scrum Masters, Agile Coaches, delivery leads and engineers. You’ll contribute from your own role and practise for one of four SAFe® certification pathways.
