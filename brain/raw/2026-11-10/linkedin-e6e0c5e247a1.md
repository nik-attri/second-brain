We are seeking to hire a motivated and results-driven #Recruitment_Specialist to join our HR team and support our recruitment operations by attracting, assessing, and hiring qualified candidates for both White-Collar and Blue-Collar positions.

Key Responsibilities:

- Develop, monitor, and update the annual #manpower_Plan in coordination with department heads.
- Manage the full #recruitment_cycle for White-Collar and Blue-Collar positions.
- Create and publish #job_advertisements across relevant recruitment channels.
- Build and maintain a database of qualified candidates to meet current and future #hiring_needs.
- Source, screen, and shortlist candidates based on job requirements.
- Conduct initial HR interviews and coordinate the #interview_process with hiring managers.
- Prepare and maintain #recruitment_reports, including hiring status, recruitment progress, and recruitment KPIs.
- Follow up with selected candidates to ensure completion of #hiring_documentation and confirm joining dates.
- Maintain accurate interview records and update #candidates_statuses (Accepted, Rejected, On Hold).

Job Requirements:

- Bachelor's degree in any discipline (HR Certification is a plus).
- 1–2 years of experience in Recruitment or Talent Acquisition.
- Good understanding of the full recruitment cycle for both White-Collar and Blue-Collar positions.
- Excellent communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills.
- Proficiency in MS Office applications, especially Excel.
- Very good command of English (spoken and written).
- Ability to work under pressure and manage multiple vacancies simultaneously.

Location: 10th of Ramadan City, Egypt.
