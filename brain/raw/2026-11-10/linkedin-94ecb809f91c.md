November in Finland: the month that makes you question every life choice that led you to this latitude. Dark, wet, and approximately 758 days long. 

Since hibernation apparently isn’t an option, here’s my survival tips:
- Go outside during daylight. You have three minutes. Roughly 11:57–12:00. Don’t be late.
- Let AI do the boring stuff. You have no energy left anyway. Three hours of spreadsheet misery can take ten minutes with the right prompt.
- But also, stop optimizing everything. You’re barely functioning. Spend twenty minutes talking nonsense. With actual humans. Spontaneously. Out loud. Yes, even in Finland.
- Find your thing. Mine is apparently spending my free time knee-deep in mud at the horse stable in horizontal rain and winds from hell. I can’t explain it. I don’t need to. Somehow, it’s where I find my peace. ❤️
- And for Pete’s sake, wear reflectors. Or a headlamp. You’re a dark lump in a dark coat on a dark road. Nobody can see you. Nobody wants to hit you either, so help them out.

See you in December. Which is also dark, but at least it has Christmas lights. ✨
What’s your November coping mechanism? Bonus points if it’s unhinged.
