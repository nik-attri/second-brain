Every post-event workflow used to trap me in the exact same operational nightmare.

Event badge scans dumped into a messy CSV.
Salesforce open in another tab to check account ownership and deal stages.
Gong open in a 3rd tab to see what the prospect actually cares about.
Reachdesk open in a 4th tab trying to find an appropriate follow-up gift.

I would spend 4 hours cross-referencing tabs just to figure out 3 simple things:

- Which accounts we scanned actually have active opportunities
- What specific pain point came up on recent calls or at the booth
- Who deserves a high-touch follow-up vs standard nurture

That operational tax is why event follow-up drags on for 2 weeks until the lead goes cold.
Reachdesk MCP changes that entire workflow because it connects gifting directly into Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot alongside your CRM and conversation intelligence.

Instead of manual spreadsheet hell, you can run multi-tool queries in plain language:

1. Cross-reference booth scans against CRM pipeline. You can ask Claude to scan your event attendee list against Salesforce, flag every open opportunity over $50K, and filter out existing customers.
2. Pull context from Gong call notes. You can prompt the model to review recent call snippets for those accounts, identify personal interests or project milestones, and recommend a curated gift from the marketplace that fits the conversation.
3. Prepare the send for approval. The model stages the gift send directly inside your conversation, respects your user permissions and budget caps, and waits for your confirmation before anything ships.
4. Track pipeline attribution without building custom reports. You can prompt: Show me every gift claimed from last quarter's field events that moved to closed won.

You go from half a day of manual data entry to a 5-minute review process.
