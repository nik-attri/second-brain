CHANGING INDUSTRIES as a PRICING EXPERT, brings a FRESH perspective, new methods, and best practices into your company. 

Every heard of the saying: What got you HERE won't get you THERE?
Yup, that one. It's true, especially in a world of ai.

1) ⚡COPPER WIRE 
2) 🍗CHICKEN
3) 🔩NUTS & BOLTS 
3) 🧀CHEESE
4) 🫐BLUEBERRIES  

Guess what they all had in common? 
🪙Fluctuating Cost hourly/daily/weekly

🪙VOLUME is the goal of the PROFIT $$$

Pricing Algorithms are a SKUS, data, market trends, and ultimately a floating matrix.
Bringing in an "outsider" is sometimes the BEST THING you can do in a stale PRICING environment, because industries have different trends, methods, and ideas - that OFTEN WORK across the industries. And yet "this is how we have always done it" seems to keep people STUCK in their ERP matrix or even worse: pricing spreadsheet hell.

Remember the Ted Lasso episode when the "NEW GUY" was brought in, and they watched new method, mindset, and plays make the MAGIC? A NEW playbook was born.

MINDSET is huge in pricing, and bringing in a NEW PERSPECTIVE is how you win championships. 

⚽PRICING IS A TEAM SPORT.
