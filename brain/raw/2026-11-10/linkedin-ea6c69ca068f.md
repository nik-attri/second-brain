Building a rocketship globally is one hell of a ride.

Endless flights, airport food, meetings, pitch decks, spreadsheets and emails. You can’t exactly let go of the steering wheel.

So this weekend in Bilbao, we combined business with some very serious tortilla research.

Runniness, egg-to-potato ratio, caramelisation, seasoning and structural integrity were all rigorously assessed.

Pictured here, catching up on emails while picking my winner.
