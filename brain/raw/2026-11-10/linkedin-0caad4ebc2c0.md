I spoke with three Indian SME founders last week who are still doing ITC reconciliation manually.

They have no idea what hit them on November 1.

India’s GST Council reforms went live, and compliance changed overnight.

Refunds are now automated, tax credits match in real time, and seller sign-ups now take minutes. 

(If your ERP system relies on manual entries, you're directly locking up your own cash)

Automated competitors are getting their GST refunds back almost instantly. So if your team is still matching invoices by hand, your cash stays trapped every time someone makes a typo.

That said, the divide between automated companies and manual ones is about to get brutal.

When we set up FinanceOS for clients, we build real-time tax matching right into the daily workflow. That way cash flows back into the business automatically instead of sitting in end-of-month review hell.

If your team is still doing this on spreadsheets, you're just leaving your own money sitting on the table. Automate the setup once and keep your cash moving.
