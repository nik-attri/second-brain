i’m not a developer. i can’t write a line of code. i’m not a cfo either, and i’ve never built a cash forecast.
and honestly, i think that’s part of why i’m good at my job.
my job is to talk to people. find out what’s slowing their business down. and bring the right experts into the conversation.

sometimes it’s a company drowning in manual work.
sometimes it’s a design agency that wants to say yes to bigger projects but has no development team behind it.
sometimes it’s a cfo who builds the cash forecast in a spreadsheet on sunday night.

different problems, same question:
who in the room understands the work well enough to fix it?

in two years at cody, i’ve learned that one of the most useful things i can do on a first call is stay out of the technical details and ask better questions.
the experts take it from there.
not a pitch. just how the job works.

what’s the first question you ask on a first call?
