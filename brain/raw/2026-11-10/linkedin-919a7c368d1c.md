AI + Data: The Foundation for Smarter Business Decisions.

As organisations accelerate their AI adoption, there is a growing focus on AI models, emerging technologies, Agentic AI and identifying new business use cases.
While AI is opening up tremendous opportunities for businesses, having the right data foundation will play an equally important role in realising its true potential.

The combination of AI capabilities with reliable, accessible and contextual data is what can drive meaningful business outcomes.
This becomes even more important in the FSI industry, where use cases around underwriting, claims, risk assessment, fraud detection and customer experience depend on accurate data and a strong understanding of business context.
I believe the next phase of enterprise AI adoption will be about bringing together three important elements:

Trusted Data: Building a reliable and consistent data foundation.
Business Context: Connecting data with business processes, rules and domain knowledge.
AI Capabilities: Leveraging AI to automate workflows, generate insights and support better decision-making.

The real opportunity lies in bringing these capabilities together to make AI more meaningful, reliable and scalable across the enterprise.

AI has tremendous potential. The stronger our data foundation, the greater the value organisations can unlock from it.
