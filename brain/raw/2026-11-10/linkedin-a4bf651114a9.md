I've been lying about inventory and didn't even know it.

Not on purpose. Shopify said one number. The co-man's spreadsheet said another. The 3PL said a third and quietly changed it on a Tuesday. Once a quarter I'd pick one, type it into the model, and call it the count. 

Everybody nodded. Nobody checked. Including me.

A few months ago I got annoyed enough to do something about it. Not gonna lie, I'm not a developer. Claude Code doesn't care. So I built a thing that pulls all three every night and tells me where they don't agree.

One of the first things it flagged was a part showing 30x the stock we actually had. The co-man's daily report was in cases of 30. We'd been reading it as units. For how long? Honestly I don't want to know. I'd looked at that file a hundred times.

The other morning it told me 1,243 orders went through, 1,240 tie out, three don't, here's the three and here's why.

Takes less time to read than this post.

I used to think inventory reconciliation was an accounting thing you suffered through at quarter end. It's not. It's the only time your inventory is actually real. Everything else is a vibe.
If you're still doing the quarterly count and calling it truth... I did it forever . No judgment. But the tools are sitting right there and they're basically free.

Who the hell knows what ops looks like in two years. But I'm never typing a guess into the model and calling it the count again.
