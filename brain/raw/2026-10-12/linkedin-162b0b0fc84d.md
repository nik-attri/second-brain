Waaa, today we’re launching buyer personas in Bubble 🎉

As a product marketer, creating personas always frustrated me. Good ones took so much research. Digging up customer calls in tools not made for PMMs. Analyzing them manually without bias, or hoping AI would spot the right patterns. By the time I was done, the market had moved and sales stopped opening my beautiful persona decks. Because they weren’t valuable anymore. No sh*t.

So we’re building Bubble, the tool I wish I had. A PMM hub where you create product marketing fundamentals from customer insights, not guesswork. Select your customer calls and interviews, and out comes the PMM asset you need. New today: buyer personas!

How buyer personas work:
1) Bubble analyzes your calls like a researcher would, ranking every insight by how much evidence backs it
2) Then it builds your persona using Adele Revella's 5 Rings of Buying Insight best practices

You see why buyers buy, what holds them back and how they decide, all backed by real customer quotes. And it’s not another deck collecting dust in a Drive. With our Claude integration, your persona lives where your team works, ready for messaging, landing pages and sales briefs.
