Airtable becomes powerful when it starts responding to your data automatically.

Over the weekend, our September Cohort Elites explored how Airtable can do more than store information.

They learned how to build workflows that respond to real business events, including:

• Sending different welcome emails to different groups using Zapier

• Triggering an automation when a record enters a filtered view

• Creating a “Paid Students” view to identify learners based on payment status

• Automatically sending emails, updating records and notifying a team

• Using Airtable automation to calculate grades and performance remarks

• Using AI tools to generate JavaScript scripts for Airtable automations

• Creating scheduled reminders based on dates and deadlines

One of the key lessons was simple:

Automation is not just about connecting tools.

 It is about understanding a process and deciding what should happen next.

The Elites also learned that they do not need to memorise every line of code. 

With the right prompts and a basic understanding of what the script is doing, they can use AI to support their workflow-building process, test the result and debug errors.

This is the kind of practical, process-focused learning we encourage at Beyond-Age Tech Solutions.

The journey is still ongoing. 

The next class will continue with Airtable Interfaces, the course project and the transition into Zapier.
