Before we built Mundaprice, our team used to give commodity information through two WhatsApp communities 6 days a week. In an unstructured fashion and quite a cumbersome thing to do daily. A farmer in one of our WhatsApp communities, Mr Mwiya, would write down (in a book) prices of his commodities way before harvest, consistently each day.
The excitement in us when he made the comment publicly during an interaction within the community.

The process of daily sending price info, tho tiring, gave us a little sense of fulfilment but sadly, that sense of fulfilment was short-lived. In addition to this, the burden of using WhatsApp communities for such a system was that, the member sometimes would have to scroll through multiple messages for them to find the necessary info they were actually looking for and second, unless they were writing down commodity prices in some book, it will be some difficult task to observe historic patterns or discover exactly what the price movement meant.

After many months, we eventually built a digital dashboard called 'Mundaprice.com' and we took away the need to manually write down prices in a book for farmers in the WhatsApp communities.
On the mundaprice dashboard today, any farmer can now observe historic price movement going back 2 weeks and up to a month on selected packages. Getting on the platform today does not mean one starts from Zero, they can start from up to a month back of commodity prices so they can easily catch-up.

As we work upwards towards onboarding of other fitting markets, the farmer will soon be able to compare prices and trends all in not more than 5 clicks on mundaprice.com

No calculators or experience in data analysis needed to understand the dispayed info, all the graphes are an easy-to-follow type of design.

Today, we're actively covering three markets within Zambia and over the next 28 days, we have made all markets on mundaprice accessible to users even on the lowest tier.
