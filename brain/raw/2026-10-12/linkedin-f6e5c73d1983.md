Turns out I have room for one more fractional data leadership engagement this year.

Hi everyone, and especially all the new people who started following me recently after my posts about the non-technical side of data problems. 

I recently finished a short engagement with an e-commerce startup in Bavaria, helping them understand where their data setup and processes were holding them back and what they could realistically improve in the short term.

Right now, I have one advisory role and one fractional engagement, leaving capacity for one more. The top three problems I usually help with:

• Data setup that stopped scaling
Manual processes, unclear ownership, unreliable numbers, and a data team drowning in requests.

• Data teams that need structure
Priorities, responsibilities, stakeholder communication, hiring, and focusing on problems that actually matter.

• Delivery and transformation
Making sure important data initiatives move forward with the right architecture, quality, and speed.

My background is hands-on. I spent many years as a data engineer before moving into leadership, but most difficult data problems I see today are not primarily technical. I’ve worked across health and life sciences, automotive, e-commerce, publishing, SaaS and consumer businesses.

My ideal client is a scale-up whose data setup isn’t scaling with the business, or a larger company that needs experienced data leadership to get an important initiative delivered. Usually I join for 3-9 months, improve the setup, team and processes, and ideally make myself replaceable again.

If this sounds familiar, or you know someone dealing with these problems, happy to have a chat.
