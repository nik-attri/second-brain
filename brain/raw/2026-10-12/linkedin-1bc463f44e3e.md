The way we work just changed.

Anthropic integrated Claude directly into Google Workspace—and it's not just another AI feature bolted on the side.

Claude now lives inside Docs, Sheets, and Slides. No more switching tabs. No more copy-paste hell. No more context-switching that kills your flow.

Here's what actually matters:

**The Problem It Solves:**
Most AI tools force you to leave your work. You're in a spreadsheet, you need help with a formula—so you open ChatGPT in another tab, copy your data, paste it back. Friction everywhere.

Now? Claude is right there in the sidebar.

**What This Enables:**
• Write and edit documents with AI suggestions—without leaving Google Docs
• Create complex formulas in Sheets with natural language
• Reformat presentations in Slides instantly
• Approval modes so you stay in control (AI doesn't just do—it suggests)

**Why This Matters:**
This isn't about AI doing more. It's about AI working *where you already are*.

The companies winning right now aren't the ones with the fanciest AI. They're the ones who understand that friction kills productivity. Every extra step, every tab switch, every context break—that's where momentum dies.

Anthropic got this right. They didn't build another standalone tool. They embedded intelligence into the tools you already use every day.

This is the future of AI adoption: not replacement, but integration.

The question isn't "Will AI change how we work?" anymore.

It's: **How quickly will your team adapt to AI that actually fits into your workflow?**
