Introducing Level 4 AI & Automation for Marketing.

Marketing has changed. The way teams research, create, analyse, automate and make decisions is changing with it.

So we’re launching a brand new programme built around the skills modern marketers increasingly need.

Our new Level 4 AI & Automation for Marketing programme is designed to help marketing professionals build the practical skills to apply AI and automation to real marketing processes and challenges.

Learners will develop the capability to identify worthwhile opportunities, improve workflows, build AI-enabled solutions, manage risk and measure the impact of the changes they introduce.

For employers, it’s a way to develop marketers who can work more effectively with AI, and who understand the work, the systems and the processes they’re looking to improve.

Our first induction takes place this December.
