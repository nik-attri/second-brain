Dear Practice owners,  this is why your claims are getting rejected ❗️❗️❗️

Are you struggling to handle the revenue side of your clinic/practice❓️

I have observed a number of NPs, DPC owners, and private practice owners, and there seemed to be a persistent discussion about their difficulties with billing and insurance in each of those observations.

In my interactions with them, I helped them realize that their lack of understanding of the procedures involved in the patient billing cycle—also known as revenue cycle management (RCM)—is the reason they are encountering claim denials and rejections.

These procedures include:

1. Patient Registration: Only after the patient's data has been collected can billing start. This contains insurance details, patient demographics, etc. Mistakes made now could lead to problems later on in the billing process.

2. Insurance verification, Eligibility and Prior Authorizations. 

3. Patient Encounter Documentation: At every stage of a patient encounter, accurate medical documentation is required. Think of it as the proof that shows the insurance company the services rendered, the diagnosis, the procedures, etc.

4. Coding and Entry: The diagnosis, the service rendered, and the medical equipment used are all communicated when the appropriate medical codes are applied.

5. Submission of the Claim: The claim is prepared and submitted to the patient's insurance. How well you have adhered to the earlier stages will determine how successful your claims are.

6. Processing insurance. What occurs once the insurance company receives the claim? After reviewing the claim, they decide how much is paid. Another name for this process is "claim adjudication."

7. Posting and appealing payments.

8. Patient Billing: Keep track of copays, deductibles, and co-insurance while the claim is being processed.

9. Follow-up: Not all claims are paid out without incident. Certain claims might be rejected outright, denied, suspended, or even underpaid.

This is why hiring an insurance coordinator and billing specialist like me is important 💯❗️because managing patient care while juggling all of these responsibilities can quickly become wearisome.
