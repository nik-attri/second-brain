HOUSE CONNECTION 
Another tool that solves a very common problem in Civil 3D.

If you’ve ever worked on a gravity network, you already know how many house connections can be included in a single project.
Their locations are usually defined in the design drawings, so the question is: do we really need to draw every pipe and structure manually, one by one?
Of course not.
With the new House Connection tool in ZI Tool, you can create house connections simply by selecting their locations and setting the design constraints you need.
You can use [ ROTATE STRUCTURE ] to adjust the rotation of them .
[ DARK MODE ] is added to all tools in ZI TOOL .

Instead of spending hours repeating the same steps, you can get the job done much faster while keeping the design requirements under control.
It’s all about making everyday Civil 3D tasks easier and spending less time on repetitive work.
