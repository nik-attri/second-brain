Kampala, thank you. 🙏
On Saturday we hosted the first n8n Meetup Kampala, and the Kampala tech community showed up in a way I won't forget. Developers, school IT teams, educators and first-time automators filled Inspire Coffee Cafe, asked sharp questions, and stayed long after the last demo to swap ideas on what to automate next.
We built and walked through real n8n workflows together: WhatsApp messages routed to the right action, invitations sent across channels in one go, and attendance summaries delivered to parents automatically. The lesson that stuck: automation isn't about replacing the tools people use. It's about connecting them.
A special thank you to Grok, our AI build partner. In the long nights before the meetup, it worked alongside our team, opening pull requests, running checks and fixing issues, so that what we showed on Saturday was real, live and working. Building with humans and AI side by side is no longer the future. It's how we shipped this weekend.
Thank you to our speakers, to Inspire Coffee Cafe for a wonderful venue, and to the n8n team for supporting community meetups around the world.
This community is just getting started. If you're automating anything in Kampala, or want to start, come to meetup #2 and bring a workflow. 🚀
