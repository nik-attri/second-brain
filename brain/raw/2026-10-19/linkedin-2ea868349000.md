The global CPA and accounting talent shortage is hitting an all-time high. 🚨

Industry data shows a steady drop in accounting graduates globally over the past few years, while compliance, tax laws, and reporting requirements keep getting more complex every single quarter.

Working in the global accounting space, I see this daily: local teams are stretched thin, burnout is at a record high during tax season, and hiring locally for entry-to-mid level roles is taking 2–3x longer than it used to.
This is exactly why the hybrid model combining local leadership with integrated global teams is no longer just an option; its becoming the standard operating model across the industry.
How is your team handling the current talent shortage this year?
