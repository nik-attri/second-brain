PoC: Before We Build, We Need to Prove.

At Envision Lab, We’ve learned that one of the most important steps in software development is often overlooked, Proof of Concept (PoC).

When we have a new idea, especially around AI, LLM, RAG, automation or complex system integration, the first question shouldn't always be:

“How quickly can we build the product?”

Sometimes the better question is:

“Can we actually make this work?”

That’s where a PoC comes in.

A PoC allows us to test the most critical technical assumptions before investing significant time and resources into a full product.

For example, before building a complete AI Agent, we may first validate:

🔹 Can the LLM understand the required context?
🔹 Can RAG retrieve the right information?
🔹 Can different APIs communicate reliably?
🔹 Can the AI automate the intended workflow?
🔹 Is the expected accuracy achievable?
🔹 What are the technical limitations and risks?

If the PoC succeeds, we have greater confidence to move toward an MVP.

I see the difference this way:

PoC → “Can we build it?”

MVP → “Will people use it?”

As a technology company, our goal at Envision Lab is not simply to write more code. It is to reduce uncertainty, validate ideas, solve real problems and build technology that creates measurable value.

Sometimes, spending a few days proving an idea can save months of development.

Build smart. Validate early. Scale with confidence.
