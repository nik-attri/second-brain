The plant manager calls the office. The office calls the site. The site guy says he'll check and call back.

That's a Wednesday morning on most multi-site jobs.

Somewhere there's a list. Could be a spreadsheet, could be a folder on someone's desktop, could be in the head of the bloke who's been with the company twelve years. The problem isn't that nobody cares. It's that the information lives in the wrong place.

When something moves, breaks, or gets disputed, you need to know where things were and when. Not tomorrow. Not after three phone calls.

What I've been building lately (for construction and technical companies specifically) is a simple way to put that information somewhere everyone can actually reach it. No special training. No new habit to form. Just the right answer in the right place when someone needs it at 7am on a site gate.

If that sounds like something worth a conversation, message me.
