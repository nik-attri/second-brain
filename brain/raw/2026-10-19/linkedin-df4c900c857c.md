Every recruitment team wants to use AI. But how many are fixing the workflow around it?



AI wont fix your recruitment process. It will expose how broken it already is.

But here is a question worth asking:

Are we using AI to solve recruitment problems, or simply making inefficient processes run faster?

Think about it.
→ Automating a confusing application process doesn't make it candidate-friendly.
→ Screening CVs faster does'nt guarantee better hiring decisions.
→ Sending automated emails doesn't improve candidate experience if the information is meaningless.
→ Scheduling interviews faster doesn't help if feedback still takes weeks.

The real opportunity is not just automating tasks. 
Its redesigning the workflow around them.

Start with the basics:
• Identify where recruiters lose the most time.
• Remove unnecessary steps and repetitive data entry.
• Automate follow-ups, scheduling reminders and routine updates.
• Keep human judgement where it matters like evaluating people, understanding context and making hiring decisions.

AI should give recruiters more time to recruit, 
not just more tasks to manage.

I am particularly interested in how AI can improve Talent Acquisition Operations without losing the human element of hiring.

One question for recruiters and HR professionals:

If you could fix ONE inefficiency in your recruitment process before introducing AI, what would it be?

I would be interested to hear what's actually slowing teams down.
