My mother-in-law Ingrid has kept a handwritten guestbook in Tegernsee since 1988. 

System uptime: 100%. Data loss: 0%. User compliance: absolute. 

Compare that to my team. Last month, our department workflow stalled simply because #flex-ops-v1 couldn't be renamed, forcing us to migrate 40 people to v2 manually. Half the team is still lost in the legacy architecture.

Over coffee at 07:10 after my Sunday Bergtour, watching Ingrid hand a pen to a guest, it hit me. 

In enterprise tech, we spend millions eliminating friction. But friction is where commitment lives. When you physically write your name, you own your presence. In a seamless channel, you just stream.

I’m not saying we destroy our cloud infrastructure.
I’m not saying paper beats enterprise software.
I’m saying frictionless tools default to frictionless accountability.

We need Tactile Governance.

I could not rename #flex-ops-v1.

Audit your stack for artificial ease today and tell me below: where do you keep your analog friction? 👇
