Catching up after an action-packed start to October, still 🏄‍♂️ the AI wave!

Earlier this month, I visited 🇬🇧 London for Google Enterprise Summit 2026 to share the stage with Decathlon & Worldpay, moderated by Lauren Miskelly, on 'Redefining work in the AI era'. Where I shared how we are consolidating our security landscape to enable safe AI adoption, and shared a look behind the scenes of our global devices strategy.

Incredibly proud that Randstad was also recognized with the 'Contributor Champion Award'! 🏆 🎉 A great testament to our active partnership with Google, helping shape product development through our 'Ambassadors program' and advocating for innovation and change. We couldn't have done this without Vivek Kumar Alessandro Franza Winona Chatzimpyrrou and many more ! 💙

Fittingly, while in London we also launched our global "Working Smarter with AI & Responsible AI" training at Randstad. It focuses on daily AI workflows and introduces agentic AI, helping our people build personal agents to automate routine tasks so our people can focus on human connections.
