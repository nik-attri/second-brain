☕ Project Update: Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard in Excel I’m excited to share my Coffee Shop Sales Analysis project, where I analyzed 149,116 transaction records covering January to June 2023 and built an Excel-based dashboard to explore business performance. 

🛠️ Tools and techniques: 
 • Microsoft Excel 
• Power Query for data preparation 
• PivotTables and PivotCharts 
• Interactive dashboard filters / slicers 
📊 A few findings from the dataset: 
• Total revenue: $698,812.33
 • Coffee was the highest-revenue category at $269,952.45. 
• Coffee and tea together contributed approximately 66.8% of revenue.
 • 8:00–10:00 AM stood out as a key sales period.
 • June recorded the highest monthly revenue in the six-month period. 

💡 This project helped me practice turning raw transaction data into business insights, comparing product and store performance, and communicating findings through a dashboard.
