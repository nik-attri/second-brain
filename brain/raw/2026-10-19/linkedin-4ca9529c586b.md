We’ve started working on a new project at Kroma, and we’re really excited about this one. We’re integrating AI-generated shots into a motion design video, continuing to explore the possibilities of combining realistic footage with motion design.

The level of realism we can achieve with AI today is becoming hard to ignore, and I think choosing not to explore these tools would be a mistake. We’ve already had the chance to experiment with AI, and projects like this allow us to keep developing that part of our work. 

The goal ISN'T to replace the creative process, but to combine AI-generated footage with manually crafted 2D animation and art direction to expand what’s possible.
