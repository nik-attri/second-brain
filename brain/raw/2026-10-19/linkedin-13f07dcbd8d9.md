I think we're celebrating the wrong things in fintech.

A customer taps their phone. Payment successful. Everyone's happy.

But what happens after that?

A recent UAE study found that 96% of surveyed SMEs use digital payments, yet only 39% have integrated payments with their business management tools.

That's a massive gap.

Think about a offee shop owner who accepts hundreds of payments a day but still needs different systems to match orders, reconcile transactions, track invoices and understand cash flow.

We've made paying easier for customers. Have we made running a business equally easier for merchants?

Having spent years building in the payments industry, I believe we've focused too much on the transaction itself.

The real opportunity isn't just helping a business collect money.

It's helping that business operate better, waste less time and grow.

And I think that's where the next big chapter of fintech in the UAE should be written.

I'm curious to hear from business owners here.

What's one frustrating task you still do manually every day that technology should have solved years ago?
