You use AI. But how good are you at using it?
I decided to put myself to the test this morning.

I asked ChatGPT to assess my capabilities out of 100. Then I asked Claude to do exactly the same.

And I gave them one very clear instruction.
Be brutally honest.

The results were interesting. But what really caught my attention wasn’t the scores. 

89 for ChatGPT which I’ve used for almost 3 years and 62 for Claude used for only 3 months.

It was discovering where I could improve and which capabilities I wasn’t making full use of.

And it got me thinking.

So many of us are now using AI regularly. Some every single day.
But how often do we actually stop and assess how effectively we’re using it?

Are we exploring the capabilities available to us? Are we making the most of the tools we’re paying for? Are we still doing things manually that AI could help us accomplish?
Or have we simply become comfortable with what we already know?

So here’s my challenge to you. 👇

Open ChatGPT, Claude or whichever AI tool you use most.

Ask it:

“Assess my AI capabilities out of 100. Be rigorous and critical. Tell me what I’m doing well, where I’m underusing the platform and the three most important things I could do to improve. Be clear about what you can and cannot verify.”

Then ask one more question:

“What would I need to demonstrate to achieve 90 out of 100?”

Remember, this isn’t a scientific assessment. Your AI may not have visibility of everything you do. But it can be an excellent starting point for challenging your habits and identifying opportunities.

📊 Now vote in the poll!

I’d love to see where people are on their AI journey.

And if you’re comfortable, share your actual score in the comments. Better still, tell me what you discovered you could be doing differently.

There are no prizes for the highest score. The real value is recognising how much more we could achieve.

Because having access to AI and knowing how to use it effectively are two very different things.
