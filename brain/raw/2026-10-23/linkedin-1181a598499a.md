The body keeps the score, even when your calendar says you don’t have time for it.

To the physicians and corporate executives reading this:

You’ve gotten damn good at overriding your own discomfort to deliver results.

The 14-hour days.
The high-stakes decisions.
The endless alerts.

“That’s just what the role requires.”

Meanwhile, your jaw clenches during rounds or while you stare at another spreadsheet.

Your shoulders live up by your ears.

Your heart starts racing before you’ve even opened your inbox.

And that knot in your stomach? You’ve had it so long you call it “normal.”

You keep saying, “I’m fine. I can handle this.”

I believe you can handle it.

What is handling it doing to you?

What are you angry about that you keep swallowing because you have to remain “professional”?

What grief have you had no room to feel?

How much responsibility have you taken on because everyone assumes you’ll be the one to carry it?

Your physical symptoms deserve attention. So do the emotions you’ve had to push aside to keep performing.

You can unclench your jaw and go right back to the same conversation you’ve been avoiding.

You can drop your shoulders and pick up everything you feel responsible for again.

But at some point, don’t you want to ask what the hell you keep having to brace yourself for?

That’s where my work with clients begins.

We get honest about what they’ve been carrying, what they’re afraid might happen if they put any of it down, and what actually needs to change.

Then we figure out a next move that accounts for their paycheck, their responsibilities and the life they still want to have outside of work.

If you’re reading this and thinking, “Damn, that sounds like me,” send me a message. 

We’ll set up a call so you can learn how my coaching works and see if I’m the right person to help you make that next move. 💛
