Welcome to Excel Hell!

It's something I talk about regularly in my workshops and webinars, because it's often a symptom of a much bigger productivity problem.

I have a love-hate relationship with Excel.  I love Excel for modelling, prototyping and short-term projects. It's incredibly powerful and flexible.

And when spreadsheets become the backbone of important business processes, that's when I start to worry. Particularly when nobody is checking whether the numbers are right.
Spreadsheets are notoriously easy to get wrong. A missing formula, an incorrect reference, a forgotten row. And suddenly we're making decisions based on inaccurate information or spending hours fixing a broken reference.

With AI, it's becoming easier to check spreadsheets for potential errors. And we still need human scrutiny.  And that includes triangulation - checking the information against other sources to make sure it's complete, accurate and makes sense.

Because a spreadsheet can be mathematically correct and still be completely wrong if the underlying data is incomplete.  And that's where Excel Hell really begins.

How confident are you in the spreadsheets your business relies on?
