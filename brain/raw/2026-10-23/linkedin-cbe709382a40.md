Build AI boldly. Put boundaries around what it can do.

AI is moving beyond answering questions.

Today, AI systems can interact with tools, access information, automate workflows, and increasingly make decisions that affect real business operations.

That creates an important question:

As we give AI more capabilities, how do we ensure it stays within the boundaries of its authority?

Security cannot be an afterthought added only after something goes wrong. As AI becomes more autonomous, the mechanisms for controlling, monitoring, and auditing its actions deserve just as much attention as the intelligence itself.

At Librae AI Labs Sdn Bhd, we're developing LIA — Librae Immune Agency.

LIA is designed as an additional AI security and control layer to help monitor and constrain unauthorised or potentially malicious AI-driven actions, with auditability built into its architecture.

Our approach is straightforward: rather than asking organisations to abandon the AI systems they already use, we want to explore how an independent control layer could complement their existing infrastructure.

We believe this could be especially relevant to AI solution providers, software companies, system integrators, and organisations deploying AI agents into real business environments.

We're interested in connecting with teams that want to explore:

- AI security, control boundaries, and adversarial testing.
- Technical evaluations of AI control mechanisms.
- Integration opportunities with existing AI products and platforms.
- Potential technology partnerships that create value for end customers.

We are continuing to develop and evaluate LIA, and we welcome serious technical discussions, constructive challenges, and opportunities for independent validation.

Because the future of AI should not be measured only by what it can do, but also by how responsibly its capabilities can be controlled.

Build AI boldly. Put boundaries around what it can do.
