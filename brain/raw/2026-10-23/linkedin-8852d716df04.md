Next week at AME Milwaukee, I'll spend four days with people who've built their careers on lean and operational excellence.
⠀
It's the Association for Manufacturing Excellence's 42nd international conference, with keynotes, practitioner sessions, and best-practice tours. I expect a lot of discussion about where a plant loses time.
⠀
Our contribution to this topic is the skills matrix. A team leader at a food manufacturer once told me he spends three days a week in Excel keeping his matrices up to date.
⠀
That's more than half his working week, spent organizing spreadsheets instead of working with his team on the floor.
⠀
And none of that time shows up on a value stream map because it's chalked up to admin.
⠀
I'll be there with Rick van Echtelt. He'll be on stage showing what changes when manufacturers bring AI into skills management, from filling a line at short notice to training and cross-training operators faster.
⠀
