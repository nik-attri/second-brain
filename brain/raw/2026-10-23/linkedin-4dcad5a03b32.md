📢 WE ARE HIRING | ACCOUNTS EXECUTIVE – PURCHASE & VENDOR COORDINATION

Company: Surbhi Satcom Pvt. Ltd.
📍 Location: Noida, Uttar Pradesh
🏭 Industry: Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) / PCB Manufacturing

Surbhi Satcom Pvt. Ltd. is looking for a detail-oriented and organized Accounts Executive with experience in purchase accounting, Purchase Order (PO) processing, and vendor coordination. Candidates with prior experience in the EMS or PCB manufacturing industry will be preferred.

Key Responsibilities:

- Prepare, process, and maintain Purchase Orders (POs).
- Handle purchase entries in Tally and maintain accurate accounting records.
- Coordinate with vendors regarding purchase orders, invoices, quotations, and delivery schedules.
- Verify purchase invoices and resolve discrepancies in coordination with relevant departments.
- Maintain records of electronic components, PCB materials, and vendor-related documentation.
- Manage and update purchase data using Tally and MS Excel.
- Ensure accuracy in data entry, documentation, and purchase-related accounting activities.

Required Skills & Qualifications:

- Experience in purchase accounting and PO processing.
- Practical knowledge of Tally and MS Excel.
- Experience in vendor coordination and invoice verification.
- Familiarity with electronic components and PCB materials is preferred.
- Strong data-entry, documentation, and coordination skills.
- Prior experience in EMS, electronics manufacturing, or PCB manufacturing will be an advantage.
