2 years ago today I traded the drill rig for a desk at Raglan Drilling.

After years on the tools, Gus and Hugh gave me the chance to move back into business development and get back to city life. I haven’t looked back.

Years on the rigs taught me what a good operation looks like. This role has taught me that it also has to stack up on a spreadsheet. Bringing those two together for clients is the best part of my job.

Any BD or sales person will tell you it’s a hell of a lot easier to sell a great product, and in that respect I’m lucky. The boys have built a fantastic business with a unique culture, and I’m incredibly proud to represent it to the industry.

Behind the scenes, we’ve changed a lot of how we work. We’ve tightened up our systems, cut out the clutter, and made the business more commercially robust.

The result? The most successful year in Raglan’s history.

None of that happens without a crew that turns up every day, a workshop that keeps the rigs turning, and owners who aren’t afraid to back new ideas.

Thanks to the whole Raglan team. Here’s to what next year has in store.
