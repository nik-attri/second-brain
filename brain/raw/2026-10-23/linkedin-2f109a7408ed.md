✨ WE’RE HIRING | LUXURY FASHION BRAND ✨

📍 Location: Mumbai

We are looking for exceptional talent to join a luxury fashion brand across retail leadership, operations, production, and creative functions.

Experience in couture and/or luxury retail is mandatory. We are keen to connect with professionals who understand the standards, craftsmanship, pace, and excellence expected in the luxury fashion industry.

Open Positions

1. Retail Head
💰 Salary: Up to ₹50 LPA

* Strong leader capable of managing, mentoring, and driving performance across multiple Store Managers.
* Experience in couture and retail is mandatory.
* Experience managing a large-scale business, ideally with a turnover of ₹1,000 Cr, is preferred.

2. Operations Lead – Production
💰 Salary: Up to ₹50 LPA

* Lead production coordination and execution, working closely with stores.
* Strong stakeholder management and follow-up skills.
* Must be assertive in driving timelines, ensuring accountability, and pushing back on unrealistic store requirements.

3. Production Head
💰 Salary: Up to ₹25 LPA

* Lead end-to-end production operations, ensuring quality, efficiency, and timely delivery.
* Strong expertise in production planning, execution, and team management.
* Ability to resolve bottlenecks and maintain high-quality standards.

4. Embroidery Head
💰 Salary: Up to ₹30 LPA

* Strong expertise in embroidery techniques and execution.
* Exceptional eye for aesthetics, with the ability to provide valuable creative inputs across products.
* Seeking someone with strong technical and aesthetic sensibilities, beyond a purely product- or design-focused approach.

5. Illustrators – 4 Openings
💰 Salary: ₹30,000 per month per role

* Strong drawing and illustration skills.
* Experience in fashion, couture, or apparel illustration is preferred.
