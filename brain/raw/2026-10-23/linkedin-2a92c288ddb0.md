Invoices. Purchase orders. Contracts. Claims. Onboarding forms. Handwritten notes. Every week, thousands of hours go into reading documents and keying data into systems. It’s slow, costly, and error-prone.

That’s the problem we built DoCapture at CognitBotz to solve. 👇

What is DoCapture?
An intelligent document capture and data extraction platform that turns unstructured documents into accurate, structured, ready-to-use data. It combines AI, OCR, Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) and NLP.

What makes it different?
🔹 Understands, not just reads: it grasps the structure and context of each document, not just the text. It picks out names, addresses, invoice numbers, dates, amounts, tax details, account data and signatures.
🔹 Any format: scanned images, PDFs, invoices, receipts, POs, contracts, forms, ID documents and handwritten notes.
🔹 Smart classification: it identifies the document type and routes it to the right workflow automatically.
🔹 Built-in validation: extracted values are checked against business rules and reference records. Errors, missing fields and duplicates are flagged before they cause problems.
🔹 Seamless integration: secure APIs connect to ERP, CRM, HRMS and DMS, so data flows straight into your systems.
🔹 Searchable, secure repository: find any document by metadata, keyword or category, with role-based access, encrypted storage and full audit trails.
🔹 Real-time dashboards: track volumes, accuracy, validation status and workflow progress.
