“We already have Microsoft 365. Why would we need help?” 😎

We don’t need a consultant. We have Teams, SharePoint, Power BI, and Copilot. Our digital transformation is pretty much done!”

Famous last words. 😅

Three months later… 👀

✅ Teams has 500 messages, but nobody can find the important one.
✅ SharePoint has all the files, except the one everyone needs.
✅ Power BI has dashboards, but the team is still asking for Excel reports.
✅ Copilot is ready to help, but nobody knows what to ask it.
✅ Power Automate was supposed to save time. Somehow, the team is still doing everything manually. 😅

And suddenly, having Microsoft 365 and actually making it work for the business feel like two very different things. 😂

Don't get me wrong. Microsoft 365 is powerful.

But buying the tools is the easy part. Connecting them to real workflows, getting people to use them, and making them solve everyday business problems is where the real work begins.

So, here's a question for everyone already using Microsoft 365:
What's the one thing your team still struggles with, even after investing in Microsoft 365? 👇

I'd love to hear your stories. What happens behind the scenes!
