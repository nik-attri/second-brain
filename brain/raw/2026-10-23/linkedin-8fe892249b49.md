📢 WE ARE HIRING!

Position: Business Finance & Accounts Manager 

📍 Location: Bhubaneswar, Odisha
💰 CTC: Up to ₹50,000 per month
💼 Employment Type: Full-Time | Permanent
🏢 Work Mode: On-site
📌 Experience: 5+ Years

🔹 Job Overview:

We are looking for an experienced Business Finance & Accounts professional to manage day-to-day accounting, financial reporting, statutory compliance, payroll, and business finance activities. The ideal candidate should have strong knowledge of GST, PF, ESI, TDS, and financial analysis, along with hands-on experience in accounting software.

🔹 Key Responsibilities:

- Manage daily accounting operations, expense booking, and month-end financial closure.
- Prepare Profit & Loss Statements, Balance Sheets, Cash Flow Statements, and MIS reports.
- Handle GST returns, ITC reconciliation, TDS, PF, and ESI compliance.
- Manage monthly payroll processing and statutory deductions.
- Prepare revenue forecasts, budgets, variance analysis, and project cost reports.
- Handle bank, vendor, customer, and general ledger reconciliations.
- Work on Zoho Books/Zoho Finance implementation and administration.
- Coordinate with auditors, tax consultants, banks, and internal departments.
- Ensure accurate financial records and timely statutory filings.

🔹 Required Skills:
✅ Strong knowledge of Accounting & Financial Reporting
✅ GST Returns & ITC Reconciliation (GSTR-2B)
✅ PF, ESI, TDS & Payroll Management
✅ Zoho Books / Zoho Finance
✅ Tally Prime and/or SAP knowledge
✅ Advanced MS Excel, MIS Reporting & Financial Modelling
✅ Budgeting, Forecasting & Variance Analysis
✅ Project Costing and Financial Analysis

🔹 Eligibility Criteria:

- Bachelor's degree in Commerce, Finance, Accounting, or a related field is mandatory.
- CA-Inter, CMA-Inter, or MBA in Finance will be an added advantage.
- Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in Finance & Accounts.
- Experience in the IT, Telecom, or project-based industry will be preferred.
- Candidates with immediate to 30 days' notice period are preferred.
