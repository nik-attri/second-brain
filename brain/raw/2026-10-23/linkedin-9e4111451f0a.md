#ValueMomentum | DevOps Engineer First Round Interview Questions

Interview Questions:

Tell me about yourself.
Which university did you complete your graduation from, and where is it located?
What is your team size?
What is your role and responsibility in the team?
You mentioned monitoring dashboards. What kind of dashboards do you monitor?
Your experience is mainly in AWS. Do you have experience with Azure?
Do you have experience with Azure DevOps?
How are production deployments handled? Do you deploy directly to production?
What is the biggest production issue you have handled?
What AWS services have you used in your current project?
Where do we use Load Balancer in AWS?
In which AWS services can a Load Balancer be attached?
In AWS Fargate, does the Load Balancer get enabled automatically, or do we need to configure it manually?
Can you explain Azure Service Bus?
What is the equivalent of Azure Service Bus in AWS?
How many ways can we publish messages?
Have you used SQS/SNS in your project?
When is a message deleted from the queue?
Why are you looking for a change before completing three years?
Do you have hands-on experience with Python?
How would you rate yourself in Python?
Everyone is talking about AI. How has AI helped in your daily work?
Before CI/CD pipelines, how were deployments done?
Which programming language did you learn first during your college?
Do you know about GitHub Copilot? What does it do?
Do you have knowledge of IoT? What is IoT?
Why did you choose software after studying Electronics and Communication Engineering?
How long was your training before getting assigned to a project?
What is a generator in Python?
What is a constructor in Python?
Which Python data structures have you used?
Explain Linked List.
Have you written any AWS Lambda functions?
Why do we create Lambda functions?
How is Lambda used in your current project?
Can you explain AWS Redshift?
Can you explain Stream Analytics?
What is Azure Event Hub?
Do you have any questions for us?
