( 3 ) WHAT I’M THINKING ABOUT

Software is dead. And it won’t be pretty.

Last Friday, I worked until 10 PM building a dashboard for Erretres: business forecasts, a sales tracker, new business leads, candidates in our recruitment pipeline… All in a single Claude artifact. Connected to my email, Slack and Google Drive. All of it. Live.

Keeping everything up to date used to mean hours wrestling with spreadsheets from hell. The best part? I had a lot of fun building it. Just Claude and me, in my spare time, without having to wait for a designer or developer.

It’s so good I’m seriously considering turning it into a product. If you run an agency, this thing could make your life a whole lot easier.

But here’s the uncomfortable part: if I can build this in a weekend, what does that say about the entire project management software industry? About all those SaaS companies charging per seat, per month, for features nobody uses?

Software as we know it is dying. The question is how many subscriptions you’ll cancel before you realise it.
