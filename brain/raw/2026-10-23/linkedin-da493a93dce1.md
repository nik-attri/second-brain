We’re proud to see our Smart Building project recognised with the Impact Positif Award at the Prix des Sociétés de Services, organised by Napta in partnership with La Lettre du conseil. 

The project started with a concrete operational challenge: information on energy consumption, temperature, air quality and occupancy came from different sensors and systems that used incompatible protocols. 
Our teams often had to identify anomalies manually, making it harder to get a clear picture of building performance. 

So, four teams, Mantu Lab, Sustainability, Real Estate, and Office & Facility Management, built one shared platform. It brings into a single view IoT and AI bring energy, temperature, air quality and occupancy.  

AI models forecast consumption, detect anomalies and provide recommendations that help teams decide what to do next. 

The platform is now running at two pilot sites in Paris and Levallois-Perret, with a foundation designed to support expansion across Mantu’s offices in more than 60 countries.   

This project shows how we can put data, technology and operational expertise to work on a concrete challenge, with measurable benefits for building management and energy performance.

Timothée Masurel and Jean-Rémy Jacono accepted the award on behalf of Mantu at the ceremony in Paris.
