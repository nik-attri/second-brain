Day 10 of turning my LinkedIn into a diary, and I don’t think I’ll ever get over the feeling that I am in over my head.

Next Friday, October 30, 2026, I’ll be hosting my very first in-person event as a Base44 Ambassador.

On a rooftop.

With actual people.

Who have voluntarily registered to attend something I am responsible for.

I’d like to thank everyone who ever told me I could do anything I put my mind to. I’d also like to ask why none of you specified that I didn’t have to do EVERYTHING.

The funny thing is, I used to be SO confident about public speaking. Give me a microphone and an audience, and I’d be delighted. Getting me to shut up was historically the bigger problem.

But this is a whole new problem.

Because now I’m not just speaking. I’m organising, hosting, representing something I genuinely believe in, and apparently responsible for making sure other human beings have a good time.

And I care. SO MUCH.

I want people leaving inspired, excited, and wondering why they spent three years sitting on an app idea when they could’ve just started building it. I want people leaving as not just Base44 users but builders! 

Unfortunately, my nervous system has interpreted this enthusiasm as an imminent threat to national security.

My resting heart rate is no longer resting. My cholesterol has requested a meeting with HR.

Anyway.

We’re bringing together builders, founders, creatives, and curious people to explore what’s possible with AI and Base44.

There’ll be builders showcasing their work, conversations, networking, and because apparently I cannot organise anything without being a little extra, we’re capping the night with food, drinks and a DJ.

Am I excited? Absolutely.
Am I terrified? Profoundly.
Do I have spreadsheets? Several.
Do they make me feel better? NEXT QUESTION.

But I’ve learnt that sometimes the difference between being out of your depth and doing something extraordinary is having the audacity to show up anyway.

SO, SINGAPORE. OCTOBER 30. 6.30PM. COME THROUGH.

You don’t need to code, have a startup, or know what the hell you’re doing. Evidently, neither do I. 

Just bring your curiosity, your ideas, and maybe that friend who’s been threatening to launch a billion-dollar startup from their Notes app and get to know more friends who got out of their Notes app.

I’ll be the woman on the rooftop looking like she’s got everything under control.

Please don’t check my pulse.
