【Speaker Spotlight | HerRise Program #4】
Money Strategy to Accelerate Growth
— A Complete Guide to Financial Planning, Fundraising & Capital Use

Our speaker: Kazumasa Ito, CXO of DEV.TOKYO Inc.

Kazumasa led game console development projects at Sony Computer Entertainment (now Sony Interactive Entertainment). He then co-founded a SaaS startup with an American partner, which was selected for an overseas accelerator and raised investment. With work experience in Italy, he brings broad expertise in business models, strategy, finance, and product development.

In this hands-on workshop, you'll build a simple P/L and cash flow statement in a spreadsheet — and learn how to read a company's performance and cash flow.

He'll also join the office hours as a core mentor for 1:1 support.
