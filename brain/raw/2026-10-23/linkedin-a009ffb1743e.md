#FinanceOps folks — this one's for you!

📢 Hiring: Sr. Analyst, Global Client Payments 
📍 #Pune | Hybrid ⚡ 6+ years

At Avalara, this role owns daily bank reconciliations and month-end close for payments we make on behalf of clients, with a mandate to convert manual recon effort into AI-supported process.

What this role is: 

✅ Real ownership of reconciliation, exception resolution, and month-end close, not just task execution 
✅ A seat with leadership weight — mentoring peers, driving fixes, keeping the team unblocked 
✅ AI applied to real reconciliation work (exception trends, SOP drafting, variance narratives) 
✅ Judgment calls on presented items and stop-payments within defined controls

What this role is not: 

❌ Pure data-entry or instruction-following recon work 
❌ "I used ChatGPT once" level AI exposure 
❌ A role where mistakes get quietly patched instead of flagged and fixed

We're looking for someone with strong experience in: 
🔹 Bank / Cash Reconciliation & Month-End Close 
🔹 ERP systems (NetSuite or similar) 
🔹 Advanced Excel 
🔹 Mentoring or coaching junior team members 
🔹 AI-driven process improvement

If you like turning reconciliation chaos into a clean, well-documented, AI-assisted process, this role is worth a look.
