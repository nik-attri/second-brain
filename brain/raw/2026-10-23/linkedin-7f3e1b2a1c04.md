Think about the hours spent managing emails, following up with leads, answering customer calls, preparing marketing content, onboarding employees, or screening CVs.
What if these tasks could become one connected, intelligent workflow?
At OpeXa Automation, we build practical AI agents and customised automation workflows around the way your business actually works.

📩 Email Assistant
Organise incoming emails, categorise requests, draft replies, and route important messages to the right person with approval where needed.
🎙️ AI Voice Agents
Handle routine customer enquiries, capture lead details, support appointment booking, and route complex calls to your team.
📣 Real Estate & Marketing Kit
Turn a single brief into listing descriptions, social media content, WhatsApp messages, campaign ideas, and follow-up sequences.
👋 Employee Onboarding Kit
Connect welcome emails, document collection, task assignments, and onboarding reminders into a structured workflow.
📄 HR CV Parsing
Extract candidate information, organise CV data into a structured sheet, and support initial screening against job requirements.
🔗 Custom Business Automation
Already have a process that consumes hours every week? We can explore how to connect your existing tools and turn repetitive steps into a complete workflow.
How do we approach it?
Understand your current process.
Identify repetitive tasks and bottlenecks.
Design the right workflow using automation, AI, and your existing tools.
Test the process, add safeguards, and refine it.
Measure the time saved and improve where needed.
The goal isn't to automate everything just because AI is available.
It's to reduce unnecessary manual work, minimise avoidable errors, improve consistency, and give your team more time for meaningful work.
