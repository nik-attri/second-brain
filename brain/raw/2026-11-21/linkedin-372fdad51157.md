“Do your creatives actually convert?”

We hear this question quite often when talking to potential customers.

And here's how I'd answer:

Just like any other creatives.

In fact, once they're created, they're no longer ours. They're yours.

But there's a much bigger concern behind this question.

Businesses want to know:

- Will their ad performance drop?
- Will their agency clients start leaving?
- Will they actually save money, time, and operational headaches?
- Will switching to CreometriX disrupt a process that already works?

These are perfectly reasonable concerns.

So let's clarify what CreometriX actually does.

We don't promise that a machine will produce creatives guaranteed to convert. Nobody can honestly promise that.

What we've built is a system that helps you produce creatives without starting from scratch every time or manually stitching your entire workflow together across multiple tools.

You provide the project context, use your own prompts, build briefs from templates and references, generate variations, and refine them.

But the final decision always belongs to the person who understands the product, the audience, and the advertising objective.

They're the ones who decide whether a creative is ready to launch.

CreometriX doesn't take responsibility for your advertising results. It helps you organize and improve the production process behind them.

So when someone asks us, “Do your creatives convert?”, we'd like to ask:

Who actually decides which creative gets launched — us or you?

Because once you subscribe, it's your production system. And your creatives.
