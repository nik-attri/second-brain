Garbage, trash, waste. What ever you want to call it, we are eventually drowning to it. Now I'm concentrating on what comes from the construction site. To be more specific, collecting it.

At many construction sites there is one big problem: there is no room for the needed containers. Because it's not just the containers that need the space, there is a lot of different things coming that must be stored, not just for the contractor but also the subcontractors have all kind of material, and there has to be room for the vehicles, which can be big, safe passageways for the workers and during the winter snow is adding some challenge to this giant puzzle. 

It's great that these leftovers are collected and recycled but it's not possible to fit dozens of containers to limited space and because of schedules I can't expect guys to have a little piece of something on their left hand and a manual of 100 pages on the right hand wondering to which of these 50 containers I put this??!! I have designed quite good (approved by our supervision) system which is for collecting main groups of different types of waste.

Now we get to the point where the waste management company wants more detailed sorting... I'm sorry but we don't have the space or resources to do it, but you have and your staff is (hopefully) educated to do it. 

 What is the point of this text? Too often can still be seen that there is only 3 containers, stone, wood and all the rest. Law is demanding that 11 different kind of demolition and construction wastes to be sorted and collected at the site and we have 14 but sometimes it's a real merry-go-round when the demands for the limited space increase... So far we survived!  😅 Also law (Finland & EU) is demanding that 70% of demolition and construction waste should be recycled. L&T waste management company has estimated that average recycling percentage is between 30-35%.

So far all the suggestions for improving are planning, calculating the costs and other theoretical solutions. Theory and practice are two different things. We need more space (chic!) and real life solutions instead of increasing demands from people who have never even been at the demolition or construction site. On paper everything is easy.  😉 

These were my Saturday morning thoughts, enjoy your weekend people!
