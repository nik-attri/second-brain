YouTube just makes up views now. 

in the last 3 weeks the definition of a view has meant 3 different things.

it used to be: a view was someone who watched your video with intention. By clicking on your video. 

then they changed it to: if someone scrolls and they accidentally pause for too long on your thumbnail, or hover past it…. Now that is a view. 

now it’s: “we’re going to guess how many people are looking over your shoulder while you’re standing in line at start bucks, and we’re going to count that person as a view.”

and as dumb as that is, there’s something even dumber. 

the people paying for ads don’t care, they’ll pay more, even if the views are fake. 

why? Because the money advertisers spend isn’t anyone’s real money. 

the influencer marketing kid who just got out of school, whose jobs is to talk to influencers doesn’t care wether views are real or fake. They care about reporting a number to their supervisor. 

their supervisor doesn’t care if that number is real or fake, because they have a quota on how many influencers they need to report to the brand. 

the brand’s contact point doesn’t care because they see the number of views they were able to get for the brand.  

and no one above that person cares because it’s a number on a spreadsheet that they can report to whatever VC firm is investing in them. 

it’s all a giant scam. 

I’ve never seen an optimized sponsorship on influencer content. Not one. 

and it doesn’t go without trying. I’ve begged to help make things actually functional and effective and brands just… they don’t care. 

this is the sign of a bubble burst, but I don’t think it will. Maybe these companies will turn to ai creators and that’ll do it. Idk. 

but honestly, what the hell am I going to post about on here at this point? 

since 2020 I’ve been posting about conversion rate optimization - and those posts - the posts that will double your revenue if you could just improve a conversion rate from 2 to 4%…. Crickets. 

because it’s just not that sexy. No one really cares about making simple changes. 

wether it’s a big corp, or a small business like ourselves, everyone likes the vanity metric.

because conversion rates, look fake but are real. Vanity metrics are fake but look real. And we all fall under these illusions cause we’re all monkeys any way.
