Generating 500 leads at ₹15 each looks incredible on Meta Ads Manager until your sales team realizes only two people are actually buying.
In India, cheap volume often masks low intent, making the distinction between WhatsApp inquiries, website forms, and realized revenue critical.

Day 14 of my 100-Day Performance Marketing Journey 🚀

💡 What I Learned Today:
- Leads vs Conversions vs Sales: A lead is merely contact information, a conversion is an on-site milestone (like Add to Cart or Form Submitted), but a sale only occurs when real money hits the bank via UPI or delivered COD.
- WhatsApp Leads (Click-to-WhatsApp): Provides exceptionally low cost-per-lead (CPL) and low friction, which works well in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. However, it demands an active calling/chat team to filter out window shoppers.
- Website Leads & Conversions: Carries a higher upfront CPL because the user must read landing pages and fill forms. This extra friction naturally filters out casual clickers and yields higher buyer intent.

🛠️ How This Applies to Indian Campaigns:
- For service businesses and high-ticket consulting in cities like Pune or Ahmedabad, run Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns, but integrate an automated qualification message to keep qualified CPL under ₹80 instead of drowning in junk chats.
- For D2C brands selling products over ₹999, optimize for Website Purchases instead of lead forms. Paying a ₹250 Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) on your Shopify store is far more sustainable than chasing twenty ₹15 unverified WhatsApp leads.

🔑 Key Takeaway:
Optimizing for the cheapest lead is a vanity metric; true performance marketing optimizes for the lowest cost per settled transaction.
