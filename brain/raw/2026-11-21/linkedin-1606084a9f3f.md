📍 Location: Mumbai
🏢 Industry: Manufacturing / Renewable Energy
💼 Experience: 4–5+ Years
🎓 Qualification: CA / CPA / MBA (Finance)

We are looking for a dynamic and analytical finance professional with relevant industry experience in Manufacturing and Renewable Energy.
The ideal candidate should have strong expertise in Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), budgeting, forecasting, financial modelling and business performance analysis, with the ability to support strategic business decisions.
Key Responsibilities:
▪️ Manage Annual Operating Plans (AOP), budgeting and forecasting processes.
▪️ Develop and maintain financial models using MS Excel for business planning and scenario analysis.
▪️ Analyse financial performance across business units and provide actionable insights.
▪️ Prepare monthly and quarterly financial reports, dashboards and variance analysis.
▪️ Develop strategic presentations for Co-Investors, Bankers and Senior Management.
▪️ Collaborate with Operations, Strategy and Business teams to align financial objectives.
▪️ Support capital allocation, cost optimisation and SG&A analysis.
▪️ Drive improvements in financial planning tools, reporting systems and processes.
Required Skills & Experience:
✅ CA / CPA / MBA in Finance or equivalent qualification.
✅ 4–5+ years of experience in FP&A, Corporate Finance or Investment Analysis.
✅ Candidates with experience in the Manufacturing and/or Renewable Energy industry are preferred.
✅ Strong proficiency in MS Excel, financial modelling and data analysis.
✅ Experience with ERP systems such as D365, SAP or Oracle.
✅ Excellent communication, presentation and stakeholder management skills.
Preferred Skills:
▪️ Working knowledge of IFRS / Ind AS.
▪️ Exposure to Power BI / Tableau.
▪️ Knowledge of SQL / Python or automation tools.
▪️ Experience in investment analysis, valuation and strategic finance projects.
