Your AI agent will break your month-end financial close. Here is why it happens. (Breakdown below 👇)  

"We want AI agents running our close"; this is what we hear from finance leaders every day. However, the more critical question is: "Where do we start?" The answer is often tedious and complex, which most people prefer not to hear.  

There is no shortcut; finance transformation is like a ladder. You can start on any rung, but you cannot skip one. 

Here are the five layers, from the bottom up:
1. Data: Unifying ERP, bank, sub-ledger, and contract data within a single finance data cloud.
2. Connected processes: Running O2C, P2P, and R2R on that unified data.
3. Rules and controls: Integrating approval limits, segregation of duties, and audit trails.
4. Intelligence: AI that matches, predicts, and provides explanations.
5. Autonomous agents: Handling routine operations and flagging exceptions for human review.

Identify your rung and climb in order. Be honest about your current standing. For example, if someone must manually build a file to provide a receivables position, you are still at layers one or two, regardless of the AI tools you have purchased. 

If approvals are still managed via email after the fact, layer three is not yet complete.  Most companies occupy different rungs for different processes, collections may be at layer four, while inter-company reconciliations remain at layer one. 

What happens when you skip a layer? Failures typically manifest one or two layers higher, which is why they are often misdiagnosed. If you deploy agents on unrefined data, a pilot might succeed on a clean sample, but the process will fail once month-end arrives.

 Why use one platform instead of five tools? Using different vendors for each rung creates fragmented data models. These gaps are filled with exports and spreadsheets, forcing agents to stall at every hurdle while waiting for human intervention. 
 This is why I advocate for connected finance operations. Your chosen platform must transform raw finance data, possess inherent finance knowledge, and run agents on the same underlying data and controls.  

Where should you start? Wherever you are today. Progress one layer at a time. Every layer built on an unfinished foundation inherits its flaws, and agents deployed on top will simply produce messy outcomes at machine speed.  Clean up your current layer, connect it to the one above, and then proceed. It may feel slow at first, but your progress will eventually accelerate.
