Claude Autopilot For Job Search Is Here.
A complete search workflow, with approval built in 👇

A useful job-search assistant needs to connect the steps:

→ Find vacancies that match the target role
→ Read the requirements and flag missing evidence
→ Tailor the CV using real experience
→ Draft a cover letter for that specific opening
→ Prepare the application for a final review

The approval step matters. A polished application still needs accurate facts, relevant examples and a human decision before it goes out.

Our rule is simple: automate the repetitive work, keep control of the claims and the applications.

A better search starts with better matches.
