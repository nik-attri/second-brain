OpenAI just launched ChatGPT for Accounting. What does it mean for junior accountants and auditors? 🤔
In short, it drafts journal entries from invoices, gathers records, helps with reconciliations and flags possible duplicates. 
A person still has to review and approve.

What may change for juniors:
• Less time on manual data entry and matching
• More time on reviewing, checking and explaining the numbers
• Higher value on judgment and professional scepticism

What stays the same:
• Understanding why an entry is right
• Catching what the tool gets wrong
• Communicating with clients and teams
• Responsibility. You sign your work, not the AI.

It's very new, so the real impact isn't clear yet. The time savings reported so far come from the company itself.

My take as a recent graduate: learn the logic first, then the tools.
