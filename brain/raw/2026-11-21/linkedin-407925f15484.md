At most schools we see teachers mark an assessment, enter those marks into a spreadsheet, apply their own grade boundaries to convert those marks into grades, and then manually transfer the grade into the MIS for the Data Manager then import the grades into whole school analytics for SLT to then analyse.

Even writing that out felt time-consuming and created opportunities for mistakes.

Here's how Pupil Progress works:
- Teachers enter marks once
- Grades are calculated automatically exactly as the exam boards do
- SLT can access the data centrally for analysis and see the interventions planned by middle-leaders (and why)
- The Data Manager has access to the grades 2-4 weeks earlier

This is for every subject and qualification across KS4 and KS5.



Less time managing data and more time acting on it.
