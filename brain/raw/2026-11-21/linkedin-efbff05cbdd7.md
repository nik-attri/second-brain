"All humans will become permanently unemployable".
To those preaching this, do you understand the difference between "closed system" tasks & "open system" tasks? Take this example,

Autonomous vehicles - it takes a lot for them to work.

Planned cities, refined GPS systems, real time traffic updation systems, adequate law enforcement, fail safe mechanisms, & a respectful driving culture within the city.

It took years to develop these things - that's why only a limited number of cities have autonomous vehicles.

To put simply - it took years to make "driving" a closed system. With predictable rules & agent behaviours.

In how much time can we expect the same level of development to reach let's say India's villages & towns?

Even cities!

Not just India - a lot of countries have a major chunk of the population living in unorganized & organic districts.


You must have looked at videos robots working in warehouses (eg. amazon) where tasks are limited to the confines of the entry & exit gates of the facility.

That's a closed system.

Even the hazard detection in the robot is trained on accidents happened in the past. There's no way to predict hazards that haven't happened yet - only scenarios that humans can imagine;

Or you overload the intelligence by asking it to imagine countless scenarios & be prepared for that - I leave it to you to decide who'll pay for that activity's resources.


Thing is, machines have replaced/assisted only the tasks which happen either in closed loops, or tasks which humans are manually incapable of doing (eg. running millions of calculations at once, lifting a 1000kg load, travelling across the seas, etching 92 billion transistors on a 750 mm² chip).

People preaching "end of employment" still look at the world through the tech world's eyes - which itself is a "closed system" of technology & investment.


The world & its cultures are more dynamic than we expect a data bank to understand.

Dynamic meaning ever changing, evolving, adapting, responding to its environment.



If you don't want to be replaced by a robot, it's time you become dynamic too.
