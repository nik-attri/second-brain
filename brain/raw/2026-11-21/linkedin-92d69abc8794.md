3 Weeks Done! - BMC Graduate Bootcamp
1.0: Excel Training

As we conclude the third week of the BMC Graduate Bootcamp 1.0 - Excel Training, I am really grateful for how much I have grown in just three weeks.

What I learnt:

Week 1 & 2: Excel basics, Cell Formatting & Freeze Panes, Conditional Formatting, Logical Functions (IF, AND, OR), Sorting, Filtering, Validation & Tables, Date Functions, SUMIF, COUNTIF, VLOOKUP, and Pivot Tables.

Week 3: This week took it to another level.
1. Data Cleaning- Text Functions - How to clean messy text using functions like TRIM, UPPER, LOWER, PROPER, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, and LEN to fix extra spaces, wrong cases, and inconsistent names and codes.
2. Advanced data cleaning - How to handle duplicates, blanks, errors, and inconsistent formats using Text to Columns, Find & Replace, Remove Duplicates, and Data Validation to make data clean and ready for analysis.
3. VLOOKUP - How to quickly look up and return data from a table using a lookup value, without searching manually.
4. Xlookup, index & match Functions - How to do more powerful and flexible lookups than VLOOKUP, including looking left and handling data that is not in order.

What I found most interesting:
Building the interactive lookup tool and the depreciation register. Seeing Excel go from just a table to a working system that prevents errors and posts real accounting entries was eye-opening.

How it is improving my ability to work with data:
I can now build clean, error-free reports, look up data in seconds instead of searching manually, and handle real accounting tasks like asset management and depreciation with confidence. My work is faster, more accurate, and easier for anyone to understand.

My challenge:
At first, remembering the syntax for INDEX/MATCH and getting the columns right was confusing. I overcame it by practising step-by-step, breaking each formula into small parts, and asking questions. Now it clicks.

Thank you to Brattle Multiconcepts Consult for this free, practical training.
