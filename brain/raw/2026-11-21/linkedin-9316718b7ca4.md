🌖 The Larger Half is back this Monday, November 23! Two speakers are going to show us how they actually use AI: one at work, one in the job hunt.

Nikki K.: What Comes Next After Prompting?

Nikki is a full-time field marketer at a tech company. She went from prompts to routines to an app that made her the best she's ever been at her job. Now she's handing it over to her entire sales team.

Ben Miller: How AI Runs My Job Search

Ben's system finds roles that fit, picks the right leader to write to, and drafts a short note for him to send himself. 1 in 6 senior leaders replied. He'll show a path for every level of AI use, from the free version of Claude chat all the way to Claude Code.

⚡ Then we close with a lightning round: AI best practices from the room. Bring one thing that's working for you!
