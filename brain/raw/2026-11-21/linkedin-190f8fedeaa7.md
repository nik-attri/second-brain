My entry for the ZoomCharts 4U Reports Challenge is done!
The brief was to turn a recruitment dataset into a report that shows which hiring channels deliver real value, not just the most applicants. So I built the Recruitment Performance Tracker in Power BI, in three pages:
📊 Channel Performance: where the money goes. €3.16M spend and €2,411 per hire, split by channel and cost component. 
🧭 Candidate Journey: the funnel from 47,586 applicants to 1,310 hires. You click a stage, then drill by Channel Group → Channel → Provider, and the whole page follows. 
✅ Quality & Retention: 180-day success, probation pass rate and retention.
💡 One thing the data shows: Paid advertising brings about 75% of applicants, but only 14.9% qualify and 54.5% are still successful at 180 days. Employee networks reach 69.7% and 80.5% on the same measures.
🎨 Design goals: a dark "Emerald Night" theme, a minimal layout, and drill-downs and reset that anyone can use without instructions.
🛠️ Built with: ZoomCharts Combo PRO, Pie/Donut PRO and Combo Bar PRO, with Deneb and a few HTML visuals for the finishing touches.
Thanks to ZoomCharts for the challenge and for visuals that make drilling down so smooth. 🙌
