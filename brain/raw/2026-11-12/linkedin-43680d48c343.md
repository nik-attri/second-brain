You’ve been “looking for a business to buy” for two years.

But you haven’t bought anything.

And that's the whole problem. Looking and FINDING are different things.

Let me guess what your current M&A strategy looks like:

You spend your evenings scrolling business-for-sale websites like you're browsing Rightmove.

You find something vaguely interesting. You sign an NDA. You wait two weeks. You finally receive a 40-page broker teaser featuring an "Adjusted EBITDA" that somehow includes the owner's dog's vet bills and his wife's leased Range Rover.

You put in a sensible, structured offer. You get outbid by a private equity firm paying 100% cash.

Or you just get completely ignored and never hear back from anyone.

Rinse and repeat.

If this is your strategy, you aren't an active business buyer. 
You are just playing at it.

In most cases, if there is ever a decent business for sale that is listed with a broker, there is huge interest. 

Said broker will likely then choose favourites and people that they know have deep pockets to increase the likelihood that they will sell the business.

(That's not a dig at brokers, it's just good business sense for them to do that.)

Serious business buyers who are looking to build portfolios don't just sit around and wait.

They get proactive. 

They reach out to the business owners even before they consider a sale and just plant a seed.

They then nurture that seed until it grows into an idea of selling the business.

The savvy business buyer then lends a sympathetic ear on "how they would likely structure a deal if they were to sell."

Deals like this happen over a cup of tea, discussing legacy and staff protection, rather than fighting over a spreadsheet in a boardroom.

We had a client come to us after spending 24 months stuck in that exact broker-led NDA hell. He was exhausted.

We completely bypassed the open market. 

We built a targeted off-market prospecting campaign and went straight to the retiring owners in his sector.

Within 3 months, he had a signed Heads of Terms. No bidding wars. No inflated multiples.

Stop waiting for brokers to send you bloated deals. Go directly to the source.

We're looking for 3-5 business buyers to work with and find deals for.
