"The world only makes sense if you force it to." Bruce Wayne.

Well, I guess it's time I force the world to make sense. Or face being sued for unoriginal thinking while an army of David Ellison's lawyers marches up to my front door!
Bring it on!

Anyways, to get a bit more serious. I've had this profile for months. Never put anything on it because of "reasons". Which I won't go into. At least not now.
So how do I force the world to make sense, you ask? The answer is mundane. By not shutting up. By being that constant, nagging voice that you just can't ignore. You try, you put your hands over your ears, you start using earplugs, but you still hear it.

By writing.

If there's one thing I've always been good at — and, without ANY modesty, still am — it's writing. Been doing it from an early age. My "CV" says 25 years. Yes, technically. Honestly, it began even before that.

Elementary school. High school. University? Not so much. For some strange reason I opted for law school (I know, I know!), and there wasn't much writing there. Well, minus the dry, God-awful legalese we had to memorize.

But writing was always my passion. Take an existing character from an established IP and put my own spin on it? Hell yes. I was writing Batman back when some of the people writing him today were but a glint in their father's eye. :D James T. Kirk. Same deal. I think I even tried writing Star Wars, but didn't get far?

Anyways.

The real passion was in creating something from scratch. [Now, from scratch doesn't mean from nothing.] Yes, just like any writer out there, I take an existing "trope" (I call it a concept, by the way) and put my spin on it. Make it mine. It feels familiar. You squint and go: "Hang on, I've seen that in... no... wait, I actually haven't." And you just keep reading. Page, after page, after page.

I've got stories that need to be told. Stories that scream to be let out, even if they don't fit into spreadsheets or market analysis or aren't everyone's cup of tea. Characters that are hounding me to be set loose into the world. (Knock it off, you two!) See? Even now they're elbowing me!
