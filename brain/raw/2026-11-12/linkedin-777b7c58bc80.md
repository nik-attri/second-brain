Large banks are already proving what AI can do with documents. The bigger question for NBFCs is: do you need a large enterprise budget to achieve similar results?

Consider what is already happening in Indian banking. DBS Bank India has used AI-powered document processing across loan files, KYC forms and financial statements. AI-driven identity verification and OCR have helped reduce customer onboarding timelines from days to minutes. Its GenAI-enabled content capture has also reduced data-entry time per transaction by nearly 50%.

At Axis Bank, AI is increasingly taking over the traditional “maker” role in document processing. The bank reports that 80–90% of documents no longer require a manual touch, with operations productivity improving by approximately 15%. These are not just technology stories. They address a business problem that every Bank and NBFC CXO understands.

How much time is your team still spending reading documents, entering data, validating information and moving it between systems?

For many financial institutions, the challenge is not a lack of opportunity to automate.
It is the assumption that meaningful automation requires a massive technology investment.  It doesn't always have to.

The opportunity goes well beyond KYC:
