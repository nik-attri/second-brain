🇬🇧 Vestor Club : UK Investment Spotlight

Tano Raises $4M Seed Round to Turn Creator Marketing into a Scalable, AI-Powered Advertising Channel

📍 Location: London, United Kingdom
💰 Funding: $4M (£3M approximately)
💼 Sector: Artificial Intelligence / Marketing Technology
🌱 Stage: Seed
👥 Founders: Will Caplan (CEO) and Sagar Shah (CTO)
🤝 Lead Investor: Cherry Ventures
Other Investors: Seedcamp and The Players Fund (earlier backers)

🚀 What does Tano do?

Tano is building an AI-powered creator advertising platform that helps brands manage influencer and creator partnerships at scale.

Its AI agents work alongside human creator strategists to identify suitable creators, manage outreach and communication, coordinate campaigns, handle payments and measure performance.

Instead of relying on large teams to manually manage hundreds or thousands of creator relationships, brands can set their campaign objectives and budgets while Tano handles much of the operational work.

The company already works with major brands, including Unilever, HelloFresh, Bloom & Wild and Skin + Me

💡 Why does this investment matter?

Creator marketing is becoming an increasingly important advertising channel, but managing campaigns at scale remains labour-intensive.

Tano is addressing that gap by combining AI automation with human expertise, making it easier for brands to work with more creators while measuring the commercial results.

Its reported customer results are encouraging. For example, Skin + Me, grew creator-driven sign-ups and revenue by 3.5 times, while HelloFresh recorded a 93% increase in conversion rates.

The investment signals growing confidence in AI platforms that can automate complex marketing operations.

📈 What's next?

-Invest in computing infrastructure and engineering talent.
-Expand its team of experienced creator strategists.
-Scale its AI agents to manage significantly more creator relationships.
-Build towards its ambition of making creator advertising as measurable and scalable as traditional digital advertising.
