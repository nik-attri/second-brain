Sales is not one job. It is an entire career ecosystem.

Day 3 of my Sales Enablement Academy (SEA) training and we went through the core sales roles that exist in the market right now. I 
had no idea just how many doors this one skill set can open.

There is the:
• SDR who handles prospecting and outbound. 
• The BDR who focuses on new business development. 
• The cold caller and appointment setter who live on the phones. 
• The DM setter who works through social and direct messaging. 
• The inbound account 
executive who converts warm inbound leads. 
• The sales virtual assistant who supports the whole operation from behind the scenes.

And then there are the adjacent roles. Sales operations and 
enablement. Account and client management. Sales customer 
support. Leadership and strategy. Even industry-specific sales 
careers.

Here is what this map showed me.

Sales is not a dead end. It is a launchpad. Each of those roles 
builds a skill that feeds the next one. And for me, every role on that list is a window into a problem I will eventually build a system to solve.

• The SDR who cannot find the right prospects. 
• The appointment 
setter drowning in manual follow-ups. 
• The sales ops person 
trying to get their CRM to actually work. I am learning all of this from the inside so I can build the solutions from the 
outside.

Which of these roles are you in right now? And what is the one problem in that role you wish someone would just fix?
