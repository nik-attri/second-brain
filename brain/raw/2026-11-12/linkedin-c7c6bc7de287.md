Honestly, this is such a clever marketing idea! 😄

I love how Be10x  used the Ramayana billboard to make its own message stand out.

"Some stories deserve to be repeated. Your office tasks don't."

Simple line, but it makes so much sense. We don't mind watching our favourite stories again and again, but doing the same office work every single week? That's a different story!

We spend so much time on repetitive tasks that could be handled much faster with AI and automation.

What I liked most is that Be10x  didn't need a complicated ad to get the message across. Sometimes, a little creativity does the job better than a big campaign.

A really fun and thoughtful way to promote smarter working! 👏
