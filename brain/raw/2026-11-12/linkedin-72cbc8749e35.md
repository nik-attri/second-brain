Your team spends the first hour of every day sorting email. How many deals are already cold by then?
Most owners think the problem is volume. It is triage.

When I audit a sales inbox, I check five things before I talk about AI or automation.

These are the signs that manual sorting is costing you deals:

→ A manager spends the first hour of the day deciding which emails matter

→ Complaints and urgent requests look the same as newsletters in the list

→ Two people reply to the same inquiry because nobody marked it

→ A quote request that arrived at night is still unread days later

→ When someone is on leave, their emails sit untouched or the whole team drowns

If you recognise three of these, you are losing deals to delay.

The fix is not hiring another person to sort faster.

It is teaching the system to read, route and flag before a human even opens the inbox.

Which of these five do you see in your business?
