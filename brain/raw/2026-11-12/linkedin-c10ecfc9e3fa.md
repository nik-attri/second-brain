How can #ArtificialIntelligence transform the way businesses operate? This was one of the key themes explored during Day 3 at Sage Future for Partners, with contributions from several Sage leaders, including Gretchen O'Hara, Aaron Harris and Anand Swaminathan.

A key message : AI is becoming an integral part of the Sage ecosystem, with new opportunities for businesses and partners alike.
✅ 𝗧𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗺𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁. As AI becomes more deeply embedded in business software, security, transparency and accountability remain essential. Sage highlighted the importance of explainable and verifiable AI, with clear governance, human oversight and control over how agents operate.
✅ 𝗔𝗜 𝗶𝘀 𝗯𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗽𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝗼𝗳 𝗘𝗥𝗣. With solutions such as #SageX3 and #SageIntacct, the ambition is to bring AI closer to everyday business and financial processes, helping users access information, gain insights and automate tasks more naturally.
✅ 𝗔 𝗻𝗲𝘄 𝗿𝗼𝗹𝗲 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗽𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗻𝗲𝗿𝘀. Through the Sage AI Gateway, MCP and APIs, partners will have new ways to connect AI tools to Sage solutions and build intelligent agents and applications. Sage provides trusted access to data, governance and the platform, while partners bring their business and industry expertise to create solutions that address real customer needs.
✅ 𝗙𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗶𝗱𝗲𝗮𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝗯𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝘃𝗮𝗹𝘂𝗲. The AI Maker Pathway opens up possibilities to build workflows, connect ecosystems and develop AI agents on trusted Sage data, whether for specific customer projects or solutions to bring to market.

For Deveho Consulting Group, these developments are particularly exciting. With our expertise in Sage X3 and Sage Intacct, we see real potential in combining the power of AI with our knowledge of business processes and the specific needs of our customers.

The goal is not simply to introduce more AI, but to make it useful, reliable and relevant to the way businesses operate.

The next chapter of ERP is taking shape, and partners will have an important role to play in building it!
