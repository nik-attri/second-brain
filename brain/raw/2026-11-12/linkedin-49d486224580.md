I was at the bus stop today with an open umbrella over my laptop.

Pouring rain.
Debugging active safety logic.

People stared like I had lost my mind. 

And you know what? They are right. If you are comfortable, you are not moving fast enough. 

While legacy aerospace and automotive companies spend 8 months drowning in manual paper checklists for a single safety assessment, I am writing code under a transit shelter in a downpour. 

Safety engineering is broken. It is bloated, bureaucratic, and stuck in 1995. Fixing it does not happen inside a quiet glass boardroom with a latte. It happens in the trenches.

If your feed is full of polished corporate fluff today, share this so someone wakes up. 

To the normal people at the bus stop: sorry for the weird show.
To the engineers who are sick of legacy bureaucracy and endless spreadsheets: let us grab a water and fix this industry.

Btw: If you have seen me riding my pennyboard or bombing down a street on my bike with an open laptop... yes, the meme is real. My brain never turns off.
