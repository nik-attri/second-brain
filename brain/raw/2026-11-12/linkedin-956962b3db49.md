I actually stopped for a second when I saw this billboard.

Be10x placed its billboard directly below the Ramayana one, and honestly, the placement itself caught my attention before I even read the full message.

The line said:

“Some stories deserve to be repeated. Your office tasks don’t.”

That’s a clever use of context.

The Ramayana billboard is already something people are going to notice. Instead of trying to compete with it, Be10x used the attention it was already getting and made its own 
message work as the follow-up.

And the contrast is what makes it memorable.

We don't mind watching the same story again and again.

But doing the same Excel work, reports, emails or repetitive office tasks every single week?

That’s a different story.

What I liked most is that the billboard didn’t try too hard. No complicated explanation. No overloaded design. Just a simple line that becomes more interesting because of what’s sitting right above it.

As someone who works around content and marketing, this is the kind of advertising I find interesting  when the placement becomes part of the idea itself.

Be10x basically didn't just buy billboard space.

They found the perfect billboard to sit underneath.

And honestly, if AI can take a few repetitive tasks off my plate, I’m all for it.

What’s one office task you’d happily never do manually again?
