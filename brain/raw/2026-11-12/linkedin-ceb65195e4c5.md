Today marks 100 days in my new role as Consulting Partner at PwC Trinidad & Tobago! 🇹🇹

Having helped many clients develop their first 100-day plans, of course I had to take a dose of my own medicine.

Looking back at my rather ambitious Excel spreadsheet, did I complete every activity? Hell no! 😅

But what stands out isn't the number of boxes ticked. It's the outcomes achieved, relationships built and lessons learned.

🎯 Outcomes: From listening and learning to setting a clear strategy, aligning the team and building commercial momentum. We've laid some important foundations for growth.

🤝 Relationships: Getting closer to our clients, connecting expertise across the Caribbean and wider PwC network, and getting to know a talented team with so much potential. Thank you to all who have welcomed me so warmly. 

💡 Learnings: The best plans provide direction, not a rigid script. Leadership is about knowing when to lean in, when to step back and how to empower others to take ownership.

This week, we took time as a team to reflect on our first 100 days, celebrate the wins and challenge ourselves on what comes next.

And that's perhaps my biggest takeaway: sustainable change isn't something one leader drives. It's something a team owns.

Here's to many more days of driving change, creating opportunities and making an impact across our team, firm, market and region! 🚀
