No dev team. No big budget. Just one agent drowning in leads from three different sources: the website form, a Facebook ad, and walk-in inquiries typed into a spreadsheet by hand.
Here's the build, step by step, if I were starting from zero:
Every lead source feeds into one place. Website form, ad platform, and manual entries all route into a single intake point instead of three disconnected inboxes.
A qualification layer runs automatically. Budget range, timeline, and area get checked against a few simple rules, not to reject anyone, just to flag who needs a call today versus who can wait for tomorrow's follow-up.
The first reply goes out in under a minute, and it's not a template. It references the actual property or question the lead brought up, because that context was already captured at step one.
Everything gets logged automatically. No one has to manually retype a name into a CRM three separate times.
The agent only gets pinged when a lead is actually worth interrupting their day for. Everything else queues quietly for the next check-in.
None of these five steps require the agent to be more organized, more disciplined, or better at follow-up. The system carries that weight instead.
Total build time for something like this: usually a single afternoon, with tools most teams already have access to.
