Rubber and metal: how do we ensure the perfect bond in our anti-vibration mounts? 🛠️

One of the key factors in the quality of anti-vibration mounts is the adhesion between the rubber and metal.

The components are subjected to forces in different directions depending on the application. They offer the best performance only if there is no detach between the rubber and metal sections.

These two materials, completely different in mechanical and physical characteristics, are joined together thanks to a surface treatment of the metal part consisting of two layers: gray (primer) and black (cover).

This process is extremely delicate and can compromise the quality of the final product. For this reason, Patrini has been carrying out metals preparation process directly in its Fizzonasco facility. Only in this way can we control and guarantee consistent quality, offer maximum flexibility, and remain competitive with a product entirely Made in Italy.

Since we produce components with very different dimensions and geometry, we are equipped with three different systems:

• 🤖 Fully automatic system: for preparing flat surfaces (ideal for bobbins).

• ⚙️ Manually loaded 2-station machine: for all deep-drawn metal components and tube sections (for machine feet, cones, bushings). 

• ✍️ Manual station: for components with irregular geometries or for applications with very demanding requirements.

Even in this area we follow the philosophy that distinguishes us: keeping the majority of the production process in-house.
