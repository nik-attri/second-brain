Exporting heavy machinery overseas is a massive win—until an operational fault occurs thousands of miles away and the local field technician can’t diagnose. 🚚🌍
An automotive integrator based in Pune faced this exact challenge after delivering heavy vehicle to international customer sites.
When an unexpected fault popped up during operation, field engineers hit three major friction points:
Language Barriers: Local technicians struggled to communicate with offshore support team due to language barrier. (English is not always the option.) :-)
Costly Downtime: Engineers spent up to 25 minutes searching through multi-page PDFs while equipment sat idle.
Support Strain: The technical team in India was constantly pulled into late-night support calls to translate basic manual steps over phone or messaging apps.
They solved this operational friction using CENS SOP Agent.
Instead of hunting through endless PDFs, overseas field technicians now simply speak or type the fault description into their phones in their own working language.
The agent searches their uploaded equipment manuals—and only those manuals—delivering a clear, step-by-step action plan in seconds, backed by exact page-level citations.
The Impact:
⚡ 60% Faster Diagnosis: Field issues resolved without waiting on HQ.
⏱️ Down to 2 Minutes: Resolution time dropped from 25 minutes to under 2 minutes.
📚 10 Manuals Deployed: Seamless multilingual support across their entire exported fleet.
