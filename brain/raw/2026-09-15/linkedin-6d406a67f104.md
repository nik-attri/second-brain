🤖 AI VS. EXCEL HELL IN LOGISTICS

Excel itself is not the real problem.

The problem begins when shipment information is scattered across emails, PDFs, spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

🔎 DISCOVER THE PROBLEM

The same booking details are entered repeatedly. Different teams save different versions, while important updates remain buried in inboxes.

⚠️ ANALYZE THE IMPACT

Manual copying creates errors, missing information and delayed decisions. Exceptions may remain invisible until a shipment misses its cut-off—or the customer asks for an update first.

✅ APPLY AI TO REAL OPERATIONS

AI can extract shipment data from emails and documents, match bookings with containers, validate key fields, detect missing information, summarize shipment status and issue exception alerts.

In the future, connected data could also help predict ETA risks, recommend alternative sailings and identify recurring operational bottlenecks.

AI should not replace logistics professionals. It should remove repetitive work and reveal problems earlier—while people review exceptions, communicate with partners and make the final decision.

The best starting point is one repetitive, high-friction process—not replacing every system at once.
