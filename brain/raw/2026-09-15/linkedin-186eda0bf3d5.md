If you were given the chance to hand off all your work today to someone else, would you do it?

Manually verifying business expenses? You’d probably hand that over in a heartbeat.

And what about deciding your overall business strategy? Probably not. (I know I wouldn't.)

There are things only you can handle, like deciding who you work with.

There are things you simply love doing, like coming up with new product ideas.

Then there are the tasks you dislike and aren't great at, like organizing SOPs. Those are the ones you should pass off without thinking. Even if you could "do it better and much quicker" than somebody else.

For example, I’d never hand off reading stories online, learning where people struggle, and coming up with ways to help them. But would I hand off copy-pasting 100 lead details into a spreadsheet? Hell yes!

Having total clarity on which task belongs in which bucket is the difference between founder burnout and scaling a thriving business.

On the other hand, it's absolutely fundamental not to delegate the core stuff. If you pay a consultant to set your strategy, an agency to handle a crisis, and an AI agent to handle employee comms, you're driving your business toward a dead end.

Successful founders protect their time for what actually matters. Even on their busiest days, they carve out space to focus on those high-impact areas because they know that's where they move the needle most.

What are the top 3 things you love doing that you contribute to your business better than anyone else?
