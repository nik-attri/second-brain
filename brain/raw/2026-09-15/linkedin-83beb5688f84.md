Your next query shouldn't require a 200-page PDF.

Engineering teams are drowning in proprietary schematics and legacy manuals. Standard LLMs hallucinate on specific part numbers. RAG solves this by grounding responses in your actual documentation.

It turns static blueprints into interactive knowledge. No more digging through revision histories.

Key operational benefits:

- Instantly retrieve specific torque specs or wiring diagrams.
- Cross-reference mechanical and electrical constraints in one prompt.
- Reduce onboarding time for new engineers.
- Maintain audit trails by citing exact document pages.
- Handle multi-modal data including text and vector images.

Imagine a scenario where a field engineer asks, 'Why does Module B overheat at 40°C?' The system pulls the thermal schematic and the latest maintenance manual. It returns the root cause and the specific fix, citing page 42 of the 2023 manual. No guessing. No email chains.
