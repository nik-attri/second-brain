MEET US AT THE CADFEM WORLD CONFERENCE 2026

Meet us on October 20-22 at the Darmstadtium Congress Center in Darmstadt. At the DESIGNTEC booth, we will showcase real-world projects in simulation-driven engineering, CAE process automation, machine learning, and Digital Twins for critical infrastructure.

✅  ANSYS ACT extensions
✅  PyAnsys applications and automation tools
✅  APDL scripting and customization
✅  User Programmable Features (UPFs)
✅  Custom material model development and implementation
✅  AI and machine learning integration into engineering processes
✅  Digital Twin solutions for infrastructure and industrial assets

We also develop our own specialized engineering software, including A-BOLT for advanced bolted joint analysis.

Visit us to discuss how simulation, automation, AI, and custom software development can accelerate your engineering processes.
