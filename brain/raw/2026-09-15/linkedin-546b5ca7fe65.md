Managing dual-authority compliance between EASA and FAA shouldn't mean drowning in endless spreadsheets, manual text comparisons, and scattered email threads.
As my ITU Aerospace Engineering graduation project, I am thrilled to finally share AeroRyth—a platform I've been developing that is currently in active WIP! 🚀
Designed to make life easier for CAMOs, Part-145 organizations, and airworthiness engineers, AeroRyth brings EASA CS-25 and 14 CFR Part 25 requirements together on a single screen, optimizes MRO workflows, and centralizes audit-ready documentation. My goal is to build an end-to-end ecosystem ranging from interactive IPC integration to live Kanban work orders.
Of course, there are many other modules and features running in the background that couldn't fit into the presentation. One of them is an integrated communication network where not only internal departments, but also all external and internal stakeholders—such as customers, OOC, CAMO, and MRO/Hangar teams—can coordinate instantly and transparently through work orders.
