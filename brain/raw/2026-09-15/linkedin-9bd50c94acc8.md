Three problems many companies try to solve with AI…
when, in reality, they may not need AI at all.

Something isn’t working properly, someone suggests “adding AI”, and suddenly it feels like half the problem is already solved.

But sometimes, you really don’t need to go that far. In fact, at MindShore, we don’t believe every problem should be solved in the same way.

👉 Your team spends far too much time looking for information.

Yes, the quick reaction might be: “Let’s add an AI assistant to answer questions.”

But if that information is spread across five different tools, folders, emails and spreadsheets, the real problem starts much earlier.

Shouldn’t you begin by connecting your systems properly and deciding where each piece of data should actually live?

👉 Preparing a report takes hours.

Again, it’s tempting to think AI could simply generate it automatically.

But if someone first has to copy figures from three different platforms, correct data manually and check that everything matches, we’re pretty sure there’s a much simpler solution: automate that process first.

👉 Each department is working with different numbers.

AI isn’t going to magically decide which figure is the right one either.

First, you need to agree on where each data point comes from, who updates it and which version is going to be treated as the correct one.

Then, if it makes sense, you can look at what AI can actually do with that information.

Yes, we know artificial intelligence is incredibly useful. Very useful. We’d never say otherwise.

But it shouldn’t become the automatic answer to every business problem.

Sometimes, what you really need is something much less “cool”, like:

🔵 Organising your data
🔵 Connecting two systems
🔵 Removing a manual process
🔵 Stopping five different versions of the same information from circulating

And although that may sound a lot less exciting than saying “we’ve implemented AI”… it’s often exactly what makes the work start running better.
