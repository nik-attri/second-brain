Excel is the greatest engineering scratchpad ever invented.
It is also the most dangerous pre-CMMS staging tool in existence.
When you manage 80,000 asset tags across 14 spreadsheets:

1. Foreign keys are an illusion: Change a parent system code in sheet 1, and 400 child instruments in sheet 4 become instant orphans.
2. No data type validation: A technician enters "150#" in a pressure rating column, another enters "150 PSI", and a third enters "Class 150".
3. No coordinate link: The row in Excel cannot take you back to the bounding box on the original vector P&ID.
4. Concurrency hell: Engineer A updates revision 7; Engineer B overwrites it with revision 6.3.
