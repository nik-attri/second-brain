🚀 2-Day Sales Operations (SDR Training Event) | WizCommerce
 
On 11th & 12th September 2026, I had the opportunity to be part of a 2-day Sales Operations training at WizCommerce in Bengaluru.
 
It was much more than a training session. These two days gave us a fresh perspective on how we work, understand our customers, and connect the right processes, product knowledge, insights, and technology to create meaningful impact and drive sustainable growth.
 
A few things that stood out for me:
 
🔹 Understanding the Customer & Industry Better
Different industries have different buying behaviours, challenges and operational complexities. Understanding that context helps us approach conversations with much more relevance.
 
🔹 Thinking Beyond Activity
Sales growth isn't simply about doing more calls or sending more emails. It's about identifying where friction exists and understanding how that friction impacts business outcomes.
 
🔹 Selling by Persona
Different stakeholders care about different outcomes. Understanding their responsibilities, KPIs, challenges and priorities helps create more meaningful conversations.
 
🔹 Research Before Reaching Out
Good research helps identify relevant signals, form a hypothesis and start conversations around a potential business problem instead of simply pitching a product.
 
🔹 RevOps, Data & HubSpot
Another important takeaway was understanding how Revenue Operations and HubSpot can support a more scalable revenue process.

HubSpot can help bring customer and sales data together, improve data visibility, track activities and pipeline movement, automate repetitive workflows, and help teams make more informed decisions.

From a RevOps perspective, the real value comes from connecting People + Process + Data + Technology to reduce manual work, improve visibility, and identify where revenue is getting stuck.
 
🔹 Connecting Technology with Business Problems
Understanding the WizCommerce product ecosystem also helped us think beyond features and focus on how technology can simplify processes, improve customer experience, and create opportunities for growth.
 
Looking Ahead 🚀
The biggest takeaway for me is that continuous improvement is what creates sustainable growth.
Going forward, there is an opportunity to become even more data-driven, customer-focused, and process-oriented — using the right technology and insights to make better decisions and create more value.
 
The questions I’m taking forward:
 
Where can we remove friction?
What data can help us make better decisions?
How can we make our processes more scalable?
And how can we turn better insights into business growth?
 
Two days of learning, discussions, collaboration, and new perspectives.
 
Small Steps. Big Impact. 🚀
