I Reduced Manual Data Entry by 80% for an Aesthetic Clinic in the US.

A clinic owner came to me drowning in admin.

Her staff was spending hours every day manually entering patient data, updating records, chasing forms, and copying information from one system to another. It had become so normal, nobody questioned it anymore.

I spent time mapping every touchpoint where data was being entered, moved, or duplicated by hand. What I found wasn't a staffing problem. It was a workflow problem. The systems existed. They just weren't talking to each other.

I redesigned the workflow and connected the right automation layer on top of it.

The result 80% of what her team was doing manually every single day, gone. Not cut. Automated. Her staff stopped being data processors and started doing what they were actually hired to do.

The clinic didn't hire more people. It finally let the right systems do the work.

If your team is spending hours on tasks that should take minutes, the problem isn't their speed. It's the system they're working inside.
