94% of Copilot usage isn't what you think it is →

Microsoft just dropped the real data on how 100,000+ professionals use AI at work.

The results? Everything we thought about AI adoption is wrong.

📊 Here's what people are ACTUALLY doing with AI:

• 49% → Analyzing, reasoning, and making decisions
• 19% → Collaborating and communicating
• 17% → Creating and producing work
• 15% → Gathering information

The biggest surprise? Content generation isn't even close to the top.

🧠 Inside that analysis category:
→ 28% decision-making
→ 9% information processing
→ 6% data analysis
→ 5% creative thinking
→ 2% strategic planning

Most teams are still thinking about AI wrong.

They're asking: "How can AI make this task faster?"

But the real question is: "How can AI redesign our entire workflow?"

🔄 The shift from task optimization to workflow transformation:

❌ Old approach: Use AI to write the memo faster
✅ New approach: Use AI to reshape how decisions get made

❌ Old approach: Automate one step in the process
✅ New approach: Eliminate unnecessary handoffs entirely

❌ Old approach: Speed up information gathering
✅ New approach: Move expertise to the moment it's needed

💡 For founders and leaders, here's your action plan:

1. Pick ONE recurring workflow this week
2. Map every input, decision, handoff, and output
3. Identify where information gets lost or work stalls
4. Redesign around AI's strengths in analysis and reasoning
5. Test the new workflow end-to-end
6. Measure decision quality, not just speed

The companies winning with AI aren't just using it as a productivity tool.

They're using it to fundamentally change how work gets done.

Decision-making is eating the world. And AI is the engine.

Which workflow will you redesign first?
