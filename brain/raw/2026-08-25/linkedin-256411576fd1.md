🏁𝐕𝐌𝐀𝐏 𝐃𝐢𝐚𝐫𝐢𝐞𝐬 : 𝐕𝐀𝐋𝐈𝐀𝐍𝐓 𝟐𝟎𝟐𝟔 🔚

It’s a wrap for Valiram VALIANT 2026🥹❤️

6 campuses in 6 weeks, followed by 4 days and 3 nights for the VALIANT Finals 

I decided to write this post the day after everything ended , while the memories are still fresh, the emotions are still here and the tiredness hasn’t fully left yet.

Looking back, it’s honestly a mixture of feelings. Just last September, I was on the other side of things , as one of the participants at the VMAP Assessment Centre 2025. And now, almost a year later, I’m standing here as someone who will soon be a senior in this programme🥹

Never in my wildest imagination did I think I would be part of the team behind VALIANT 2026, working on Recruitment & Logistics. From handling 6,000+ applications at the very beginning, going through countless shortlists, coordinating candidates, solving one logistical problem after another and eventually welcoming 60 finalists into the Finals … it definitely wasn’t easy.

There were moments where I genuinely wondered how we were going to pull everything off.

But somehow, we did.

And somewhere between the flow and agendas, spreadsheets, WhatsApp calls, checklists, last-minute changes, sleepless nights and endless “any casualties?” moments, I realised something - projects like this aren’t just about getting things done. They’re about the people you meet, the people you struggle with and the people who somehow make all the chaos worth it.

Time really flies. Something that once felt like it was only just beginning suddenly ended with what feels like one giant fast forward button (the X2 version ones) . And now, we’re left with memories that I know I’ll look back on someday and miss terribly.

To the OCs of VALIANT 2026 — WE MADE IT, GUYS 🥹
(Tze Yuh Shu Nur Rashidah Z. Liaqilah K. Sofia Elena Abdul Muhaimin Syahir Aminuddin Sharmine Qistina Shawn Tham Amanda Tan Sher Lyn Lee Aqil Raziq Bin Mohamad Abdullah Rosefaye Ku)

We chose the harder road, but we soared together side by side and somehow survived every “business disruptions” that came our way.

Thank you for making the sleepless nights a little more bearable, the late lunches a little more enjoyable, the stressful moments a little less stressful and this entire journey so much more meaningful.

And maybe the most bittersweet part of all is knowing that this is the end of something that once occupied almost every corner of our lives.

Most importantly, thank you Fong-Tuan CHEN, Chartered Fellow CIPD for having this project and giving me the opportunity to showcase my “big heart personality” , to care deeply, connect genuinely and laugh through all the chaos along the way. 

Goodbye to my biggest project in VMAP.
Goodbye to the logistics & recruitment girl. 🔚

And goodbye to a chapter that I know I’ll always look back on with a very full heart.

VALIANT 2026 — you were one hell of a ride ❤️
