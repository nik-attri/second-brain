Liaise with Operations to ensure adherence and elevate concern areas to immediate line manager. Follow-up on pending/deficient documentation vis à vis credit operations/transactions with Trade Finance, Operations and other relevant functions to ensure timely resolution. Raise day-to-day system issues/bugs and follow-up for their resolution. Take note and report policy breaches to immediate line manager. Carry out record keeping and filing as required.
