Our advisor walks in expecting discovery. Client walks him through everything — brokerages, WL policies, retirement timeline, gaps. Then says: "I don't need income yet. But when I do, I want it to be you."
And offers to send over his full eMoney spreadsheet before the meeting even ends.
This is what happens when you're positioned as a specialist, not a salesperson. The client isn't buying. He's hiring you for the role he'll need in 7 years.
Wild first meeting.
