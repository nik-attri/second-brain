Network, I need your help!

I’m hiring an HR Manager to join my team - think seasoned HR Generalist meets HRBP.

I’m hoping to have the role officially posted by the end of the week, but I’d love to get ahead of it. If there’s a solid HR professional in your network who comes to mind, send them my way or make an intro.

The TL;DR for candidates:

* We work across multiple agencies, which means different businesses, competing priorities and a lot of stakeholders. You need to be comfortable managing up, exercising good judgment and figuring out what actually needs your attention first.

* We’re in the office 3x per week in Liberty Village, Toronto.

* You’ll wear multiple HR hats. Some days you’ll be deep in employee relations or advising a leader; other days you’ll be working through HR operations, administration or leading a People project. You need to be comfortable doing both the strategic and the hands-on work.

* We move fast. We care about quality, but we also value efficiency, ownership and momentum. If you need a lot of structure or struggle when priorities shift quickly, this probably won’t be the right environment.

I’ll prioritize candidates who bring:

* At least 2–3 years of experience independently managing full-cycle HR
* Strong generalist fundamentals with the confidence to operate as an HR partner to leaders
* Experience with HiBob and ADP - a huge plus
* Experience in advertising, experiential marketing, agency or another fast-paced professional services environment - also a big plus
* Strong project management skills and the ability to take something from “we need to fix this” to done

I can tell you about the perks if we make it there. 😉

One caveat: I probably won’t be able to personally connect with everyone who reaches out, but if you see this post and decide to put yourself forward, I genuinely appreciate it.
