How do you build software for a client when no one on your team speaks their language?

That was the challenge on Magdalena Grau first project with us. Here's how she pulled it off.

Maggie is a team of one, she's one of our best AI Delivery Architects).  In three weeks, she built and promoted two custom ServiceNow applications to production, plus an integration, herself with Echelon.

The client had a business critical process living in spreadsheets and email: manual, uncentralized, impossible to audit. No single source of truth. No way to know who changed what, or when. She replaced it with two custom apps and an integration that pulls in data from a legacy database, now centralized, governed, and auditable inside ServiceNow.
How she did it in 3 weeks:

1) Live mockups. These were first time ServiceNow users. Maggie mocked the solution up live and presented it in real time, and for the first time they could see their forms come to life inside ServiceNow. They ideated on the spot, suggested changes, and saw their ideas built in front of them in minutes, not days.

2) Across a language barrier. The client's team works primarily in Portuguese. We leveraged AI notetakers to capture and translate every conversation, Echelon analyzed their Excel sheets written in Portuguese, and Maggie built against them. We met our client in their language without ever needing to speak it ourselves.

3) Knowledge transfer and documentation. She didn't just deliver and leave. Maggie taught the client's team how to use ServiceNow and documented the build, so they can maintain and extend it themselves.

Magdalena Grau, this was a hell of a first project. Watching you do this solo, across a language you don't speak, teaching as you built, made every one of us proud to have you on the team.

And this is just one example of the many ways Echelon is changing how ServiceNow work gets done in 2026. AI native, 100%, through and through.
