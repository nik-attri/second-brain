I used to really enjoy playing ping pong at the first software developer job I had; someone had installed buttons into the table and hooked them up to an ELO tracking / office leaderboard application.

This summer I started playing pickleball with my friends and it's been a whole lot of fun. Someone was using a spreadsheet to track our game history and naturally I couldn't help myself; I ended up building an app we could use to track our games and overengineered the hell out of it. I added leaderboards, session planning, configurable seasons, achievements, milestones, court booking cost tracking tools, and a whole lot more.

I ended up turning it into a real product and soft launched it a couple months ago which has been pretty exciting and a good learning experience.
