🚀 Just wrapped up a new database development project: A Real Estate Market Analytics & Sales Tracking System!

As I continue to build my backend and data skills, I wanted to tackle a real-world business problem: How do real estate agencies track agent performance and prevent stagnant property inventory?

Here is what I designed and built using standard SQL: 
📊 Database Architecture: Designed a normalized 3-table relational schema (Agents, Properties, Transactions) enforcing strict data integrity. 
🏆 Performance Analytics: Wrote queries using multi-table JOINs and aggregations to generate agent revenue leaderboards. 
⚙️ Troubleshooting Logic: Implemented automated business logic using DATEDIFF and CASE statements to flag "stale" listings (active for >90 days) and output targeted price drop recommendations.
