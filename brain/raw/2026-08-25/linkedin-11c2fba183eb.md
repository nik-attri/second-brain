We’re not competing with a hire.

We’re competing with the limits of human bandwidth.

Hire the AR person.

Let them handle disputes, relationships, escalations and exceptions.

But don’t make them spend their day:

Sending the same reminders.
Calling customers who simply forgot.
Tracking promises manually.
Checking whether payments arrived.
Updating spreadsheets after every interaction.

Let software do what software is good at.

Let humans do what humans are good at.

That is the economics of DemandPay.

Not replacing one AR executive.

Giving one AR executive the ability to manage 200 customers without losing track of anyone.

The comparison was never:

DemandPay vs employee.

It is:

one employee alone vs one employee with leverage
