Nine years ago, I remember spending weeks onboarding a single client. It was a painful, 100+ step manual process. 

Sound familiar?

Internal client onboarding can be a HUGE operational bottleneck. We were drowning in manual steps, and frankly, it was unsustainable. Recognizing this wasn't just a problem for one team, but a systemic inefficiency, I decided to tackle it proactively.

Before it was even a formal product requirement, I initiated an automation project.

I architected and engineered a robust Modular Application Provisioning Framework. Using Ansible, Bash, and Jenkins, we automated that entire 100+ step deployment lifecycle for internal use. It was all about standardization and efficiency.

Then, the magic happened. 

Our Product Owner identified the need for a "one-click" customer-facing provisioning feature. Because we'd already built the underlying framework, we were able to seamlessly integrate it into the product's landing page via REST APIs.

✨ The result? 

We leveraged this pre-built automation to significantly reduce the time-to-market for the new product feature. This saved an estimated 6 FTEs of operational effort annually. 

The transition from a clunky internal manual process to a slick, customer-facing, self-service solution drastically improved deployment consistency and accelerated global client onboarding.

Sometimes, the best way to solve a big product problem is to start by fixing the internal inefficiencies nobody else is looking at. 😉
