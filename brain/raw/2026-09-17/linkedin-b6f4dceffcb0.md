What if ten of the biggest problems inside a Google Business Profile agency weren’t actually ten different problems?

That sounds unlikely, because on the surface they have almost nothing to do with each other.

One agency owner is worried because too much knowledge lives inside the head of one employee. Another can’t understand why every new client seems to bring almost the same amount of new work. Somewhere else, an agency is doing plenty for its clients but still getting asked, “What have you actually done this month?”

Then there are the everyday problems. Different team members managing profiles differently. Staff chasing clients for photos, approvals and customer information. Important information scattered across email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets and Drive.

As the agency grows, maintaining the same standard gets harder. Then something unexpected happens, perhaps a verification problem, an ownership issue or an unwanted profile change, and suddenly everybody is trying to work out what to do.

Meanwhile, you’re trying to make GBP management feel like an ongoing service rather than a one-off optimisation.

And when any of those things go badly enough, they tend to have one final destination:

The agency owner’s desk.

Those look like ten separate problems. You could probably find ten separate tools to help with them. You could write more SOPs, add more spreadsheets, schedule more meetings and hire more people.

But there’s something strange about that approach.

Because if you keep solving each problem separately, you keep adding more moving parts to an agency that already has too many moving parts.

So perhaps these problems have more in common than it first appears.

The knowledge problem. The workload problem. The client communication problem. The consistency problem. The reporting problem. The “why the hell is this back on my desk?” problem.

They all point towards the same thing.

You don’t have ten problems.

You have one systems problem showing up in ten different places.

And what if there was one solution designed around all ten?
