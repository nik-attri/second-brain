Ode to the Broker
 
They'll tell you your business is a diamond in the rough,
 The multiple's fair, the numbers are enough.
 He'll pull an SDE from somewhere near the moon,
 Then vanish with commission the second the deal's done.
 
He calls himself a matchmaker, a guide, a trusted friend,
 He's paid the same whether the story has a happy end.
 He'll walk you through "the market" like he built the thing himself,
 Forgetting every number came from a spreadsheet on a shelf.
 
Twelve percent for paperwork, twelve percent for spin,
Twelve percent to tell you what you could've googled in.
He's not a villain, not exactly — just a man who's found his lane:
Get paid on the way in, gone before the pain.
 
So here's to every buyer who signed with stars in their eyes,
And the SDE that shrank the moment reality arrived.
The broker's already gone, cashed out, out of range —
Turns out the only guaranteed return was his.

Stop paying brokerages and brokers 10%. Cavemen went extinct too.
