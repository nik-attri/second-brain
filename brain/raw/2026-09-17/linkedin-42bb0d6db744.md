Life is like a box of chocolates - you never know what you're going to get. In some cases a reorganization. 

So it happened. You are impacted. Roughly knowing about some others, who also were in the same room. And even though you all had a feeling this would happen (the VP being suddenly in your city without any prior announcements? Sending an invitation late in the evening for a meeting tomorrow?), but the blow was still utterly painful.
You were a high-performer. Recognitions, impact awards. A plaethora of success stories. What the hell happened?

Slowly, day by day, week by week you started to go through the stages of grief until you reach acceptance and understanding. You are a cell in an Excel spreadsheet, and it (along with some others) was in the red.

Vendor CWs are being onboarded and knowledge transfers start. That is part of the deal, not that hard. Maybe. What is hard is to find the motivation, the willpower to do what is necessary. To use your suddenly freed calendar to do good, productive things. You turn into the Narcos meme.

God knows it is one of the hardest periods in my life. Disillusioned, demotivated, destabilized. But I think I have finally started to understand, that I need to seek meaning in this event - what can I take out of it, to make me stronger in the future?

Here's to change. Cheers!
