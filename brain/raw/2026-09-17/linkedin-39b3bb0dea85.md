Mark Zuckerberg and Jensen Huang are right to push back on AI pauses.

The entire safety debate acts like real testing happens when 50 researchers in SF run synthetic benchmarks behind closed doors and write a paper.

Models look invincible on academic tests. Then an operator feeds them a corrupted spreadsheet with weird merged cells and the entire workflow falls apart.

Synthetic evals test if a model can solve a theoretical puzzle. They tell you basically nothing about surviving everyday operational mess.

When you lock models up inside elite labs, you just engineer massive blind spots.

Real safety comes from crowdsourced chaos. You let thousands of builders and everyday operators beat the hell out of these systems in the wild until the fragile parts show up.
