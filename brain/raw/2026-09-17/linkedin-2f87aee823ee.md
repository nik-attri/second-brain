I’m rebuilding my website from scratch.

Manually.

At a pace that can only be described as humbling. 😅

I’m also relearning HTML and CSS, which I last touched before I graduated in 2003.

So basically, the Ice Age of the internet.

Back when getting online in the Philippines meant either going to an internet shop or buying a prepaid internet card and hoping nobody picked up the landline.

My current workflow is very “PM-builder on a budget”

Claude helps me create some of the design components, like typography, buttons, and states.

Then I use GPT to help me with the HTML and CSS. Most of it is still copy-paste, but I ask it to explain what I’m doing so I actually learn while I build.

It’s slow.

It takes way longer than just asking AI to do everything.

But I’m learning again.

As a Product Manager, I understand that even small changes can take more work than they look. But there’s something different about experiencing it firsthand. “Just make the button behave like this” hits very differently when you’re the one staring at the screen wondering why it won’t.

I’m not trying to become a developer.

I just don’t want to get left behind while the way we build products keeps changing.

So yes, I’m going back to basics.

Websites for now.

Maybe a usable app later.

And if you’re also relearning something you left behind years ago, it’s okay to start again from scratch.

Even if your version of scratch involved dial-up.
