Most “takeoff” posts on here are selling counts.

Counts are not the job.

A takeoff that doesn’t catch the spec override, the detail that never made the bid form, the note that says “at the contractor’s expense,” or the utility that sits in the same corridor as the foundation is just a spreadsheet waiting to blow up in the field.

That’s the work we actually do.

GRIND Construction Services is my company. Estimating, project management, and construction management support out of Hudson Valley, NY. We are not an overseas takeoff mill. Every project gets reviewed in-house by people who have priced work and built work.

GRIND Ops is the system I built behind the company.

I designed the workflows, rules, and checks around how an experienced contractor and estimator actually looks at a job. It reviews plans, specs, addenda, and bid documents for scope, quantities, conflicts, constructability, risk, exclusions, and missing information.

What comes out is not a chat summary.

On a recent package I ran it from nothing but the drawings and specs. No estimate. No scope sheet. No list of problems. In under 30 minutes it produced the project review, the estimating/constructability package, and produced line items to complete the job.

How it breaks down:

WHO — Built around how an estimator actually reviews a job, not how a software company thinks he should.

WHAT — Plans, specs, and bid documents. Scope, quantities, conflicts, constructability, risk, exclusions, missing information.

WHERE — Next to an existing estimating department. Front of the bid, during the estimate, or as a last set of eyes before it goes out.

HOW — RECON reviews the project. DEPLOY builds the estimating and constructability side. CITADEL audits the results and challenges anything that isn’t supported by the documents.

WHY — Time. The estimator still makes the decisions. The system eats the document-heavy work so he can price, plan, and think about how the job gets built.

I’m not trying to replace estimators. I’m trying to give a good one a tool that makes him a hell of a lot more efficient.

If you’ve got a set on the board and you want to see what that looks like on your documents, send it. I’ll run it and send the reports back.
