UK Energy & Heat consultants are burning 50%+ of their billable hours fixing broken CSVs.

While engineering teams should be solving complex decarbonization challenges, they are increasingly trapped in Excel meter-reconciliation hell.
With HNTAS compliance and tightening Ofgem regulations, the sheer volume of half-hourly interval data is overwhelming traditional consultancy structures. Highly paid engineers are being forced to act as data janitors.

Because of this operational bottleneck, many UK M&E and heat network consultancies are turning away high-margin projects. They simply have zero remaining operational capacity.
The most forward-thinking firms are fundamentally restructuring how they handle compliance data. They are separating the data operations from the engineering sign-off.

Instead of forcing engineers to hunt for missing meters, orphan data points, and network heat loss (Delta T) anomalies, they run raw backend data through specialized computational pipelines first.
By the time the data reaches the engineering lead, it is already a clean, normalized, audit-ready CP1 evidence pack.

Engineers get to be engineers.
Consultancies unlock the capacity to take on more projects.

Compliance risk drops to zero.
If your senior engineers are still manually scrubbing raw utility bills and sub-meter hierarchy sheets, your operational model is holding back your growth.

How is your team currently handling the HNTAS data load? Are you building tools in-house, or just brute-forcing it with more spreadsheet hours?
