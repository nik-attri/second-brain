The biggest trap in software development isn't writing bad code. It's automating your customers' worst habits.

One of the biggest challenges while building for healthcare has been figuring out which of our customers' processes to automate in the product and which ones to simply discard.

When an industry goes digital, there is a major risk of carrying over suboptimal manual processes into systems where they get locked in long-term. This cements poor workflows into digital tools, making them much harder to change down the line.

For us, the key has been to have a Customer Success team where everyone is a healthcare professional. They know what actually matters from a clinical perspective and what can be safely thrown out. 

To help healthcare take a major leap forward, we’ve combined the CS team’s insights with modern SaaS expertise and a healthy dose of creativity and problem-solving. That is exactly how the product has evolved.

To an outsider, someone who isn't a clinician, it's tough to figure out what are strict requirements, what's flexible, and where workflows can be completely reimagined. But for healthcare professionals who can think both innovatively and commercially, it’s completely manageable to sort out.

Then comes the challenge of getting customers onboard to  modernise and automate. 

It’s truly impressive how Ingmar Veinberg managed to find the playbook for doing just that, and successfully spread this mindset across our amazing CS team. They are part of the company’s core and enable us to grow and explore new innovative ideas on a daily basis.
