Build the Future with Us
ISSM.AI, headquartered in Islamabad, builds AI solutions that transform how large organisations operate — spanning finance, government, manufacturing, retail, railways, and defence. Our systems run on real operational data inside customer environments, meeting strict compliance standards. Reliability, scalability, and security are at the heart of our engineering challenge.

Position: DevOps & Cloud Engineer 
Department: Engineering - Platform & Infrastructure
Level: Mid-Level (typically 4–7 years of hands-on DevOps/Cloud/SRE experience with production ownership)
Location: Karachi & Islamabad (5 Days/Week) on-site presence expected 
Mode: Full-Time Employment
Salary: Negotiable (based on experience)

About the Role
You’ll design, build, and operate infrastructure powering our AI products. Expect hands‑on coding, reviewing infra changes, and influencing platform architecture. Work across Azure, GCP, AWS, Huawei Cloud, and customer‑managed environments with focus on Linux, networking, containers, IaC, and automation.
 
Key Responsibilities
- Design, provision, and maintain cloud infrastructure across Azure, GCP, AWS
- Write and maintain infrastructure as code (Terraform, Bicep/ARM, CloudFormation)
- Build repeatable environments for dev, staging, and production
- Support hybrid and customer-hosted deployments
- Build and improve CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI)
- Automate deployments with blue/green, canary, and rolling strategies
- Implement rollback-friendly release practices
- Partner with application and ML teams for model/data pipeline deployment
- Operate production Kubernetes clusters (AKS, GKE, EKS, self-managed)
- Containerise applications with secure, lean images
- Manage deployments with Helm, Kustomize, Argo CD, or Flux
- Support GPU-backed and batch workloads
- Instrument systems with metrics, logs, and traces (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Datadog
- Define SLIs/SLOs and meaningful alerts
- Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews
- Design and test backup, restore, and disaster recovery procedures
- Apply least-privilege access controls across IAM and RBAC
- Manage secrets securely (Key Vault, Secret Manager, Vault)
- Integrate security scanning into pipelines
- Maintain network security posture and support audits
- Monitor and optimise cloud spend
- Investigate and resolve performance/capacity issues
- Automate recurring tasks with scripts/tooling
- Maintain runbooks and documentation

Requirements
- Strong Linux fundamentals and networking knowledge
- Production experience with at least one major cloud (Azure, GCP, AWS)
- Practical IaC experience (Terraform preferred)
- CI/CD pipeline experience in mainstream tools
- Docker & Kubernetes production experience
- Scripting ability (Python, Bash, PowerShell, Go)
- Git collaboration workflows
- Monitoring/logging/alerting experience
- Strong communication skills in English
