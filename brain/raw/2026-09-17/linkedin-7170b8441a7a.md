I built an AI agent that reads a lead's message and tells you, in seconds, whether it's worth your time.

Most sales teams still qualify leads by hand — reading the message, guessing at intent, deciding who to call first. It's slow, and the good leads sit in the same queue as the tire-kickers.

So I built a Lead Qualification & Routing Agent: submit a lead, and the AI extracts the pain points, sizes up buying intent, scores it 0–100, tags it Hot/Warm/Cold, and drafts a personalized first reply — all before a human even looks at it.

A few things I wanted to get right:
→ Real reasoning, not a hardcoded scorecard — every score comes with an explanation
→ A live "agent activity" feed so you can actually see it thinking, not just a spinner
→ Auto re-analysis on a schedule, smart enough to skip leads that are already Lost or Converted
→ One-click email send straight from the lead's AI-drafted reply

Demo video below 👇

Built with React, Node/Express, and Groq for the AI layer. This is part of the work I'm doing at Leverage Executive Labs, where we build automation systems like this for companies drowning in manual lead follow-up.

Would love feedback from anyone doing similar work in sales automation.
