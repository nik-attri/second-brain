I'm hiring: Solutions & Account Manager – Construction Technology

We're looking for someone who loves understanding how businesses actually work.

This isn't a traditional Account Manager role. You'll sit with construction companies and deeply understand how they manage their projects, field teams, timesheets, quoting, variations, subcontractors, compliance, documentation, invoicing and day-to-day operations.

Then you'll help us answer how Fieldr should be configured, integrated or customised to work better for this particular business.

You'll work with prospective customers through discovery and tailored demonstrations, then continue owning the relationship once they become a Fieldr customer, coordinating their onboarding, configuration, integrations, customisation and ongoing requirements with our product and engineering teams.

I'm particularly interested in people with experience across construction technology, construction operations, SaaS, ERP/business systems, solutions consulting, implementation or customer success.

Experience with platforms such as Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, HammerTech, Buildertrend, Simpro, AroFlo, ServiceM8 or similar would be highly regarded.

You don't need to be a developer, but you do need to be commercially minded, technically curious and genuinely interested in understanding how construction businesses operate.

If this sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.
