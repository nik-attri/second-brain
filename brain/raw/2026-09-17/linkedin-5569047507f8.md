We have access to more tools than we can ever use.
 
But the real problem is, we don't know what we don't know.
 
Most of us are still doing things manually. Not because AI can't do it. But because we never thought to try.
 
Somewhere out there, someone has already built an agent for that exact problem you think "only a human can solve."
 
The gap isn't skill. It's not even access.
 
It's just - not knowing to try.
 
Start trying things you think AI can't do. You'll surprise yourself more often than not.
