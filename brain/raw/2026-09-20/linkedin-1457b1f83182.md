Tomorrow I turn 50.

And tonight, as I close out my first half century, I’m feeling incredibly grateful.

Today was about as perfect as I could have asked for. I spent the day at a community festival, surrounded by people, conversations, laughter, and a few beers. I wouldn’t have wanted to spend it any other way.

At one point, as I was enjoying a few of the last beers of my 40s, I ran into some clients.

But after doing this for nearly 10 years, I’ve realized how inadequate the word “client” can feel.

These are people who have trusted me with some of the most important decisions in their lives. Their health coverage. Their retirement. Their families. Their businesses. Their financial security.

Over the years, thousands of people have placed that trust in me and in Benefit Advisors Group.

I don’t take that lightly.

Ten years ago, I hung my own shingle and decided to build something of my own. I had no idea exactly where it would lead.

And yes, at times it has been an absolute shit show.

There have been long days, sleepless nights, mistakes, disappointments, risks that scared the hell out of me, and plenty of moments when I wondered what I had gotten myself into.

But there have also been incredible relationships, friendships, opportunities, lessons, and moments that I never could have imagined when I started.
Somewhere along the way, this became about much more than building a business.

It became about building something that matters.

It became about people.

It became about families who call us when they are confused, scared, retiring, turning 65, dealing with a health issue, helping a parent, or simply trying to make the right decision.

To the thousands of people who have entrusted me with those decisions, thank you.

Truly.

Your trust has allowed me to build a business, support my family, create opportunities for other people, and do work that I genuinely care about.

That is something I will never take for granted.

So today, running into members of the Benefit Advisors Group family and getting to share a beer with them felt incredibly fitting.

It reminded me that what we are building is bigger than policies, plans, premiums, and spreadsheets.

It is relationships.

It is trust.

It is community.

And I feel incredibly fortunate to be part of it.

The first 50 years have been one hell of a ride.

I’m proud of what I’ve built, grateful for everyone who has been part of the journey, and incredibly excited about what’s still ahead.

Here’s to the first half century.

And to making the second half count.
