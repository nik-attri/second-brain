Why India for U.S. Recruiting?
India has a large pool of recruiters experienced in U.S. staffing, including:
Sourcing and screening
LinkedIn Recruiter and Boolean searches
Dice and other job portals
Database mining
Candidate follow-ups and pre-screening
ATS, VMS and recruiting workflow support
W2, C2C and other US staffing requirements

The time-zone difference can also work in your favour. While your US team is finishing its day, your India recruiting team can continue sourcing, screening and building the candidate pipeline. The next morning, your US Manager can start with fresh candidates and updated submissions.

Offshore Doesn't Mean Outsource Everything:
The most successful offshore model is built around integration. Your US team remains closely connected with the India team through regular communication, clear KPIs, ATS workflows, client-specific requirements and defined responsibilities. The India team becomes an extension of your recruiting operation — working toward the same hiring goals.
