Are these 3 daily bottlenecks slowing down your freight brokerage?

Moving high-volume freight requires constant check-calls and clear lines of communication. When you're stuck putting out operational fires, you lose time to close new loads.

Here are 3 clear signs it’s time to bring in a Freight Broker Assistant:

1️⃣ You’re drowning in manual track & tracing: Spending half your morning calling drivers for status updates leaves shippers guessing on ETAs. A dedicated assistant provides proactive, real-time tracking across every load.
2️⃣ Shipper communication is lagging: Delayed responses to shipper check-in emails damage trust. Prompt updates keep your clients happy and confident in your service.
3️⃣ Carrier follow-ups are falling through the cracks: Slow carrier updates lead to missed detention claims, lost rate confirmations, and communication breakdowns during transit.

You don't need more hours in the day—you just need focused track, trace, and communication support.
