A new lead comes in.

Someone reads the email, copies the details into the CRM, writes a reply, sets a reminder to follow up, and tells the team.

Five small tasks. For every single lead. Every day.

None of them look like a problem on their own. That's exactly why they never get fixed.

The manual work in most businesses isn't one big job. It's dozens of tiny ones hiding inside the workflow:

 • Reading and replying to emails
 • Copying data between tools
 • Qualifying and routing leads
 • Remembering to follow up
 • Pulling reports together

And the common mistake when people try to fix it: they automate one task.

An auto-reply here. One zap there. The lead still gets handled by hand.

What works is automating the workflow:

Lead arrives → AI qualifies it → CRM is updated → a personalised message goes out → the follow-up is scheduled → the team is notified.

One trigger. Six actions. Nobody has to touch it.

The goal isn't to automate everything. It's to automate what shouldn't need a human every time:

• Repetitive — the same steps, again and again
• Rule-based — predictable logic
• Time-consuming — the work that slows your team down

People stay on decisions, relationships and growth. The system handles the rest.
