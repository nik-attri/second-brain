After years as a Chairwoman, I’ve concluded that every boardroom is basically a wildlife documentary.

You just need to know what you’re looking at. 😂

🐿️ THE “JUST ONE THING” SQUIRREL

“Can I just add one thing?”

No. No you bloody cannot.

Because “one thing” is never one thing.

It’s an anecdote.

Then a clarification.

Then a slightly related point.

Then a story about something that happened in 2017.

Then:

“Actually, that reminds me…”

NO.

By this point, the original agenda item has died peacefully of old age. 😂

And somehow they’ve still got “just one final thought.”

⸻

🦜 THE AGENDA PARROT

Repeats the same point.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Different words.

Same point.

We heard you the first four times, Kevin.

⸻

🦅 THE SILENT EAGLE

Sits quietly for 90 minutes.

Says nothing.

Looks mildly bored.

Then suddenly:

“I think we’re looking at the wrong issue.”

Silence.

Everyone stares at the table.

Bloody hell. They’re right.

⸻

🐙 THE SPREADSHEET OCTOPUS

Laptop open.

Three screens.

47 tabs.

Charts everywhere.

Nobody knows what any of them mean.

But apparently Slide 38 changes everything.

⸻

🐌 THE “FIVE MINUTES” SNAIL

“This will only take five minutes.”

It never takes five minutes.

It takes 35 minutes, a coffee break and occasionally the rest of your natural life.

⸻

👑 THE CHAIRWOMAN

Listens.

Watches.

Lets people debate.

Then eventually says:

“Right. What are we actually deciding?”

And suddenly everyone remembers why they came. 😂

Because the best boardrooms aren’t the quiet ones.

They’re the ones where people challenge, debate, disagree, laugh occasionally and still leave pulling in the same direction.

After enough years around board tables, I’ve learned something important:

The agenda is only a suggestion.

The real skill is getting 10 highly opinionated adults to finish on time.

Now THAT is governance. 😂
