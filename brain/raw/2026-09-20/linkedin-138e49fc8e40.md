Six months ago, I joined Verkada. On the exact day I hit my six-month mark, VerkadaOne 2026 officially began.

When I joined, I remember being SO excited to jump back into event demand gen. And then I saw the goal. 😂

The challenge was simple on paper: fill the room and grow VerkadaOne bigger than ever before.

What initially felt like a daunting goal turned into 2,600+ attendees and 31% YoY growth.

And somewhere along the way, my role became much bigger than demand gen.

I stepped deeper into event logistics and operations, helping lead the thousands of details it takes to actually bring an experience of this scale to life.

And I couldn’t have asked for better partners in crime. Lexi Horwitz (Harvey) , Serene Kim  and Alex A.  — we buckled up and went for it. There were late nights, impossible asks, last-minute pivots and plenty of moments where we wondered how the hell we were going to pull it all off. 

And our fearless leader, Gaby S., who trusted us to run, pushed us to think bigger and somehow kept us moving forward through it all.

What this small but mighty team accomplished was truly next level. There is no other group I would have rather been in the trenches with.

To the extended Verkada team who jumped in wherever and whenever we needed it: thank you. An event of this scale truly takes a village and so many of you were on this list. Rebecca L. , Julia Chi , Heather Delzio , James McNeely plus so many more.

And a very special shoutout to our event partners teams who truly went above and beyond to help us bring VerkadaOne to life. ❤️ Nate Siblock Jaycee Wallace Shon Welsh Denya Wertz WilsonEvents. 

There’s something surreal about watching thousands of people walk through an experience you spent months building. Seeing packed rooms, conversations happening, customers + partners connecting and everything finally come together makes every spreadsheet, late night, fire drill and sleepless night worth it.

But my favorite part comes now. I get to unwind and spend time with the people I work so hard for. ❤️

A little beach, Bruno Mars and celebrating my bonus kid’s 15th birthday. 🏝️🎶🎂

Six months down. 31% YoY growth. 2,600+ attendees. One unforgettable VerkadaOne.

And a whole lot to be grateful for. ❤️
