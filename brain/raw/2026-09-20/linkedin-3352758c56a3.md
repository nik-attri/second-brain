AI can generate a UI in seconds.
But generating a UI is not the same as designing a good product.
As a UI/UX and Product Designer, I see AI as a tool that can help me explore ideas faster, generate directions, reduce repetitive work, and experiment with possibilities I might not have explored manually.
But the important decisions still require understanding.
Why does this screen exist?
What is the user trying to accomplish?
What information matters most?
What should the user see first?
Where might they get confused?
And most importantly,  does this solution actually solve the problem?
I believe the future of design is not about choosing between AI and designers.
It’s about designers learning how to work with AI while keeping human thinking, empathy, creativity, and product understanding at the center.
AI can help us create faster.
But we still need to know what is worth creating.
That’s something I’m continuously learning and exploring in my own design workflow.
What do you think will become the most important skill for designers in the AI era?
