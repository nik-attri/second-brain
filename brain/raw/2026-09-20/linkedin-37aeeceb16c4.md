📢 We’re Hiring | Assistant Manager – Human Resources

We are looking for an experienced and dynamic Assistant Manager – Human Resources to join our Human Resources team and play a key role in managing HR operations, employee relations, talent management, engagement, and organizational development.

🔹 Key Responsibilities

• Develop and implement HR strategies, policies, and initiatives aligned with organizational objectives.
• Lead recruitment and selection, including manpower planning and hiring for operational and mid-level positions.
• Manage employee relations, disciplinary matters, grievances, and conflict resolution.
• Oversee HR operations, including recruitment, onboarding, employee documentation, and HR reporting.
• Manage performance management, employee evaluations, and development planning.
• Lead training and development, career development, and succession planning initiatives.
• Analyze HR reports, workforce analytics, and HR metrics to support business decisions.
• Ensure compliance with labor laws, HR policies, regulatory requirements, and best HR practices.
• Drive employee engagement, employee retention, and organizational culture initiatives.
• Develop and implement employee wellbeing and wellness programs.
• Design and manage rewards and recognition programs.
• Evaluate HR team performance and provide coaching, mentoring, and development support.
• Drive continuous improvement of HR policies, systems, and processes. 
• Plan and coordinate employee engagement activities, company events, and organizational initiatives. 

🔹 Requirements

🎓 Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field. Master’s degree preferred.
💼 Minimum 3 years of relevant HR experience.
📌 Strong knowledge of HR principles, policies, procedures, and labor laws.
🤝 Excellent communication, interpersonal, and employee relations skills.
📊 Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
💻 Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Microsoft Excel and Word.
⭐ Ability to manage multiple HR functions and work effectively with employees and department heads.
