Industrial maintenance powered by AI! 🏭

We are proud to introduce TechAssist Pro, an innovative application developed by ARSoft, designed to redefine how technicians diagnose and resolve equipment failures on production lines.

In the industrial environment, downtime means major losses. Manually searching for errors in dense technical documentation and losing expertise during shift changes are real challenges. TechAssist Pro solves these exact problems by putting AI directly into the hands of maintenance teams.

🔧 Key features of TechAssist Pro:

🤖 AI diagnostics assistant: A smart chat-based assistant that helps technicians diagnose issues and explains error codes. The AI provides answers based on equipment manuals, past resolution history, and available online solutions.

🌍 Advanced multilingual capabilities: Communication barriers disappear! Technicians can interact with the app in their native language. You can ask questions or report issues in Romanian, English, German, or many other languages, and the AI assistant will formulate responses and solutions in that same language even if the original technical manuals are written in a completely different language. An essential feature for multinational work environments.

📚 Smart defect logbook: A knowledge base where the team can quickly log local defects and repair methods. The app includes an "AI Auto-Suggest" feature: based on a brief description of the issue, the AI analyzes the history and automatically proposes an error code (Tag) along with the optimal resolution steps.

📄 Contextual PDF manual integration: Users can upload technical documentation (PDF manuals) directly into the app. The system processes these documents and loads them into the AI's memory, giving it the exact context needed to generate hyper-personalized assistance.

💾 Secure data management: Integrated export and import functionalities for knowledge base backups, ensuring that your valuable maintenance history is always safe.

With a modern, intuitive, and fully mobile-adapted interface, the application eliminates the barriers between raw information and field intervention.

Developed by ARSoft, this system doesn't just accelerate repairs; over time, it builds a collaborative ecosystem for all technicians.
