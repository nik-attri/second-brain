Before I joined this training, I thought being good with a computer just meant typing in Word and browsing Facebook.

I was wrong.

During our training on Digital Work Tools at Work Place Academy, we were given a task to plan a budget and create a presentation as a group. My first instinct was to do it manually. Then our trainer showed us the power of Google Sheets and Canva.

With Google Sheets, I learned how to organize data, use formulas to calculate automatically, share the same sheet with my team and see everyone working on it in real-time. No more sending "final, final2" on WhatsApp.

With Canva, I discovered that you don't need to be a graphic designer to create professional designs. I learned how to design presentations, posters, and social media content that actually looks professional.

Work Place Academy didn't just teach us tools, they taught us a smarter way of working — how to collaborate without confusion, save time, and deliver quality work from anywhere.

I'm truly grateful for this practical and eye-opening experience. The future of work is digital, and I feel more prepared for it.

Thank you, Work Place Academy!
