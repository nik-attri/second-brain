Mental fog, burnout, brain on vacation at the most awful times of day. Is this you? 

By the time most investors call me, they've already closed the biggest deal of their lives and they're falling apart doing it.

Picture this: 5 a.m. flight to check on a construction site. Back-to-back calls until midnight. Coffee for breakfast, a protein bar for lunch, whiskey to "unwind." Sleep is whatever's left after the spreadsheets. He tells himself this is just the season. Next quarter he'll slow down. But next quarter never comes; because the portfolio keeps growing and so does the tab his body is running up in silence.

The portfolio is appreciating. The owner is depreciating. Most never recognize the crossover until it becomes expensive. 

This isn't burnout. Burnout implies an ending. What I see in high-performing investors, executives and athletes is something more dangerous: a high-functioning breakdown.
