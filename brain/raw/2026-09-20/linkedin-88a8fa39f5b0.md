You are spending half your work week gluing together apps that refuse to speak to each other.

Most teams think they have an organization problem.

In reality, you have an automation bottleneck that legacy software simply cannot fix.

Look at your daily calendar.

How much of your actual time goes toward creative strategy, big-picture thinking, or revenue-generating work?

If you are being honest, it is barely twenty percent.

The rest disappears into a black hole of moving text from one browser tab into another.

You copy data out of an email.

You clean up formatting inside a spreadsheet.

You re-enter customer notes into your CRM, and then you draft the exact same update message in Slack for the fifth time today.

We were promised that the software boom would free us from tedious digital busywork.

Instead, it just fragmented our attention into forty open tabs and a endless pile of repetitive manual tasks.

Legacy automation tools made a noble effort over the past decade.

They let us trigger basic actions like sending an alert when a form was submitted, or appending a new line to a Google Sheet.

Yet the moment a task required actual reading, nuance, summarizing context, or making a subjective editorial decision, traditional rules-based automation shattered instantly.

You had to step back in and manually bridge the gap.

This friction is precisely what Max Brodeur, co-founder of the Y Combinator-backed startup Gumloop, set out to solve.

Brodeur and his team recognized that businesses do not need another brittle if-this-then-that pipeline.

Teams need genuine AI-first automation that can read messy text, understand intent, synthesize unstructured information, and make intelligent routing choices without demanding an engineering degree to configure.

Gumloop is built so that anyone - not just technical developers writing custom Python scripts - can assemble modular AI workflows visually.

Imagine taking the messy unstructured data coming from customer tickets, sales calls, or vendor invoices, running it through intelligent reasoning models, and outputting polished deliverables straight to your team.

The pain points we deal with every single day are not your fault.

You are not disorganized, and your team is not lazy.

The tools we were given were simply designed for an older internet where data was strictly structured and mechanical.

When you introduce autonomous intelligence directly into the pipes connecting your apps, the invisible work that drains your team's energy finally evaporates.
