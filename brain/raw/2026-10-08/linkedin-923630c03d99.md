5 AI job titles, explained for freshers 🧠

"AI engineer" on a job board means 5 different jobs now. Here's what each one actually does:

1. GenAI Engineer — builds chatbots, search and agents on top of LLMs. Fastest-growing tech role in India right now.
2. Agentic AI Engineer — AI that plans and takes multi-step actions using tools and APIs. Demand in India grew 260% this year.
3. Prompt Engineer — designs prompts that make AI outputs actually reliable. The most entry-friendly of the five.
4. AI Product Manager — owns AI products and bridges business with technical teams.
5. Data Scientist — analyzes data and integrates AI solutions into real products.
