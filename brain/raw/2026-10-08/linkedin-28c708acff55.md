🏠 WE’RE HIRING: REAL ESTATE ACQUISITIONS & DISPOSITIONS VA

🌎 REMOTE | FULL-TIME
We’re looking for an experienced Real Estate Acquisitions & Dispositions VA to support our off-market real estate operations.

⚠️ This is NOT a basic administrative VA position. We need someone with hands-on experience in real estate outreach, buyer research, lead follow-up, CRM management, and deal support.

🔑 What You’ll Do
🏘️ Buyer Research & Dispositions
Build and qualify a strong Baltimore buyer network for off-market properties.
Research cash buyers, investors, developers, property managers, multifamily owners, and real estate investment companies.
Follow up with existing buyers and identify opportunities that match their buy box.
Maintain accurate buyer information and activity in the CRM.

📞 Seller Outreach & Acquisitions
Work seller leads and gather information about their motivation, property condition, timeline, and selling situation.
Qualify leads and book appointments with the acquisitions team.
Conduct consistent follow-ups through calls, emails, and text messages.

💻 CRM & Lead Management
Keep all buyer and seller information organized in GoHighLevel.
Record calls, emails, texts, notes, follow-ups, and next steps accurately.
Make sure no qualified lead or opportunity falls through the cracks.

🔎 Prospecting & List Building
Use Apollo to research and build targeted lists of:
Real estate investment companies
Property management companies
Multifamily owners
Cash buyers
Real estate developers
Other potential investment partners

🛠️ Rehab & Project Support
Research material pricing for active rehab projects.
Collect and compare supplier quotes.
Help organize pricing information so projects can be evaluated efficiently.

✅ What We’re Looking For
▪️Previous experience in real estate acquisitions, dispositions, wholesaling, investing, or real estate lead generation
▪️Strong experience with buyer/seller outreach and follow-up
▪️Experience using GoHighLevel or similar CRM platforms
▪️Experience with Apollo or other prospecting/list-building tools
▪️Strong research and lead qualification skills
▪️Excellent written and spoken English
▪️Highly organized and detail-oriented
▪️Able to manage multiple leads, conversations, and follow-up tasks
▪️Comfortable working independently and taking ownership of tasks
▪️Strong understanding of real estate terminology and investor buy boxes is a major plus

⭐ Ideal Candidate
You’re someone who can find the right buyers, qualify leads, follow up consistently, keep the CRM clean, and help move real estate opportunities forward.

If you have real estate VA, acquisitions, dispositions, wholesaling, or investor outreach experience, we’d love to hear from you!

📩 Interested? Send us your updated CV to kaye@vame.io and a brief summary of your real estate experience.
