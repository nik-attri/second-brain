A healthcare group was paying three people to chase reimbursement claims.

Not process them. Chase them.

When they came to us, one employee expense claim took 11 days to clear. Finance was drowning in WhatsApp photos of crumpled bills. 🧾

Here's the uncomfortable part: they didn't have a finance problem. They had a software problem they'd been solving with headcount for four years.

We rolled out Cling Invoice across 420 employees. Receipt capture on mobile, policy rules built into the approval flow, automatic duplicate flagging.

Result:
11 days to 2 days for claim approval.
73% drop in processing time.
Zero new finance hires in the 12 months since.

The ROI wasn't the software. It was the three salaries that stopped going into data entry.
