We just launched KAI in Future Driver! 🤖🎉

Over the years, we've seen the same challenges across hundreds of transport companies.
Not enough time. Not enough people. Not always the right expertise. And way too much manual work.

Large transport companies have entire teams managing their fleets. Smaller companies often have one person doing it all.

We want to change that.

Meet KAI, your future virtual equipment and fleet manager. 🤖

Our goal is ambitious: within 3–6 months, we want KAI to handle every task our customers currently do manually in Future Driver. And much more.

We want a company with 10 trucks to have the same capabilities as one with 1,000.

After months of building and great feedback from our beta users, KAI is now available to all 
Future Driver customers!

Still early days, but I genuinely believe this could be one of the most important things we've ever built.

Let's see how far we can take this! 🚀
