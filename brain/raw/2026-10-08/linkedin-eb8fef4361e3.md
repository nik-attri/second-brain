Great to be exhibiting at the The Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine and connecting with colleagues who share our ambition to advance prenatal care. 👋

A highlight was the successful talk by Dr. Thomas Day during the pre-congress course, “Pre-natal Diagnosis and Therapy” bringing valuable clinical insight to the use of AI in Ultrasound.

At Fraiya, we’re developing AI tools to support clinicians in their everyday scanning workflow. FraiyaScan helps identify standard views, capture images, automate measurements and track examination completeness and giving clinicians more time to focus on the patient.
