“Using the EasyLoad enables us to transport up to 𝟰𝟬 𝘁𝗼 𝟱𝟬% 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝗸𝗶𝘁𝗰𝗵𝗲𝗻 𝗰𝗼𝘂𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿𝘁𝗼𝗽𝘀, unload easily on site, and—most importantly—do it all with minimal effort.”

Made from cement infused with dense materials—like glass, marble, and granite—a solid terrazzo kitchen countertop can easily weigh between 𝟭𝟬𝟬 𝘁𝗼 𝟮𝟱𝟬+ 𝗸𝗴. Handling them manually is not only difficult but can also be very physically demanding. Furthermore, high-end materials require delicate handling to prevent costly damage.

”Before EasyLoad, we used to pack our terrazzo pieces individually on a cart. Together, we could only transport a limited number of countertops because the carts took up so much space.” says Ramon Ruesink, founder of Terrazzo Ruesink BV “The main issue was the amount of equipment we had to bring to each job site in order to install the countertops.”

“We came across EasyLoad through one of our customers, who also used an EasyLoad system—one that was built into a company van. Since compact transport is our biggest advantage, we decided on an EasyLoad system mounted in a trailer. Using the EasyLoad enables us to transport up to 40 to 50% more kitchen countertops, unload easily on site, and—most importantly—do it all with minimal effort.”
