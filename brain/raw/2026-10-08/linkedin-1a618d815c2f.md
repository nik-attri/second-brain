Turning Customer Feedback Into Actionable Business Data with Automation At Elans Infotech, we’re building automation systems that help businesses do more than simply collect data — they help businesses understand it automatically. One of our recent projects is a Customer Feedback Sentiment Analysis Automation designed to simplify the way businesses collect, analyze, and organize customer feedback. The workflow starts with a simple customer feedback form, where users can submit their name and share their experience or feedback. Once the form is submitted, the automation takes over. 🔹 Step 1 — Collect Feedback Customer details and feedback are captured automatically through the online form. 🔹 Step 2 — Analyze Sentiment The submitted feedback is passed into a Sentiment Analysis stage, where the system evaluates the customers message and determines the overall sentiment. The feedback is categorized into: Positive ✅ | Neutral ➖ | Negative ❌ 🔹 Step 3 — Store Structured Data After the sentiment is identified, the workflow automatically creates a record in Airtable, storing important information such as: Customer Name | Feedback | Sentiment | Date This means the business doesnt have to manually read every response, determine the sentiment, and enter the information into a database. Everything happens automatically. Why this matters for businesses Customer feedback contains valuable information, but manually reviewing hundreds or thousands of responses can be time-consuming. With this type of automation, businesses can create a structured feedback pipeline that makes it easier to: ✅ Monitor customer satisfaction ✅ Identify negative feedback quickly ✅ Understand positive customer experiences ✅ Organize feedback in one place ✅ Reduce repetitive manual work ✅ Build better customer insight dashboards ✅ Make faster, data-driven decisions What makes this project valuable is the combination of automation + AI-based analysis + structured data storage. At Elans Infotech, we focus on creating practical systems like this that can fit into real business operations and turn everyday information into something much more useful.
