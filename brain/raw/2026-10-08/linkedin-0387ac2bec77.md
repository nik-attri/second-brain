One application should not feel like an obstacle course.

Create an account.

Verify your email.

Upload your CV.

Correct everything the system copied incorrectly.

Then enter the same employment history manually.

Two hours later, you finally press submit.

And sometimes, nothing comes back.

Professionals do not lack motivation.

The process demands too much repetitive work from them.
