Let’s goooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo! 🐄

If you ever worked in an agency, you'll know that there is always a big beef between the Creative and the Financial departments. All those big ideas requiring big budgets and then a shoestring budget or a client who wants "something viral" for the price of a Subway sandwich.

I've sat in those meetings; hell, I've been holding the budget spreadsheet. But I've also been the one proudly holding the BIG IDEA. Neither chair is comfortable. 

Because there is a very tricky question here: when creativity has to do more than win attention, does it still deliver?

Australian Marketing Institute NSW is putting that question on Tuesday 20 October to people who've answered it with trophies. 

Simon Banks is back as host and keynote, and I know you all love him. Creative, innovation and design nerd-in-chief, he taught me how to draw a humanoid from sticks and I'll never forget this.

And you should see the panel! The Australian Marketing Institute's CMO of the Year 2026 Award winner Caroline Raj, Agency of the Year winner Hamish Stewart, and the two serial awards winners Charlie Dejean and Andrew Bao.

The questions on the table: How do you move beyond the brief? How do you challenge convention without losing sight of the business objective? How does a bold idea become real-world impact, with the finance team still speaking to you afterwards?

I'm on the NSW State Committee that put this together, so consider this my shameless plug. It's also the event I'd be buying a ticket to if I weren't already waiting for you at the door. 🚪 Not alone, of course, but with Zeina Khodr, Nicole Papoutsis, Stefanie Bruzze, Adrian Sandy 🗝️, Emily Taylor, Deborah Dolan, Peter Pavisic and of course Eyob Yesus from Icon Corporate Events.

Register here 👇 and don't forget to seat your Creative Director and your CFO together. 😆
