When AI automation needs access to sensitive financial data

A financial advisory and asset management client came to us with a familiar challenge.

Their advisers were spending too much time preparing for client reviews - pulling together portfolio data, previous recommendations, client notes, risk profiles, and recent market activity from several systems.

The company wanted to automate this process using AI.

The workflow would:
- gather relevant client and portfolio information
- summarise recent activity
- flag changes requiring adviser attention
- prepare a structured meeting briefing
- generate draft follow-up notes

The business value was clear: less manual preparation, more time with clients.

But the security challenge was equally clear.

The AI needed access to highly sensitive information, including financial data, portfolio positions, investment history, internal notes and suitability information.

At RT Consult, we started by defining exactly what information the AI needed at each stage of the workflow.

The system was designed around controlled data retrieval, existing user permissions and clearly separated actions.

Advisers could only retrieve information they were already authorised to access.

The AI could analyse and summarise data, but it could not independently execute investment decisions or modify core portfolio records.

Higher-impact actions remained behind explicit approval points.

We also introduced traceability across the workflow, so the client could see what information had been retrieved, how the AI had used it and where human review had taken place.

The result was not simply an AI assistant. It was a controlled automation layer around an existing advisory process.

For financial organisations, the challenge is not proving that AI can automate the workflow.   

The challenge is designing that automation so it can operate inside the same control environment as the rest of the business.

That means keeping access proportionate to the task, preserving existing permission structures, separating analysis from execution and ensuring higher-risk actions remain governed.

At RT Consult, our focus is on making that transition practical: connecting AI to real operational processes while maintaining the control, traceability and resilience expected in regulated financial environments.

That is what allows AI to move from experimentation into production workflows that advisory and asset management teams can rely on.
