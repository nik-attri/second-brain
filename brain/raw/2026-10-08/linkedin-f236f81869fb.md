Some of y’all are still working like AI was never invented.

And at this point...

IT’S STARTING TO SHOW. 🤣🤣🤣

I’m not even talking about the people proudly announcing:

“I don’t use AI.”

Oh, we know.

WE BEEN KNEW. 🤣🤣🤣

You’re taking DAYS to rewrite a job description.

DAYS???

Oh my brother...what are we doing? 🤣

You spent your entire afternoon creating 10 PowerPoint slides, and half that time was spent trying to get the pictures to line up. 🤣

You sat through a 60-minute meeting, took four pages of notes and STILL don’t know what the action items are.

You’re staring at a spreadsheet with 2,000 rows like it personally disrespected you. 🤣

You’re reading a 37-page document from beginning to end just to find the three things that actually matter.

You’re doing the same repetitive task for the 47th time because:

“That’s how I’ve always done it.”

And my personal favorite...

You’re BUSY as hell.

Just not necessarily PRODUCTIVE. 🤣

Here’s what some people are missing:

AI isn’t making everybody smarter.

It’s making people who know how to USE IT faster.

And when two equally talented people are doing the same job, but one has learned how to automate, summarize, research, analyze, draft and execute faster...

That productivity gap starts getting REAL obvious.

Your old way may still WORK.

But so does MapQuest. 🤣🤣🤣

Eventually, saying:

“I don’t use AI.”

is going to sound a lot like:

“I don’t use Excel. I prefer to calculate everything myself.” 🤣

You don’t have to LOVE AI.

But you might want to learn how to USE it before somebody starts wondering why everything takes you so damn LONG. 🤣

In 2026, refusing to learn how to use it isn’t protecting the way you work.

It’s protecting the way you USED to work.

And everybody can see the difference. 🤣

“The difference between WORKING HARD and WORKING SMART is getting harder to HIDE.” 🤣

— Murph
