🚀 WE’RE HIRING | IT Monitoring Specialist

tradias is looking for an IT Monitoring Specialist to join our team.

💻 What You’ll Do
• Monitor applications and systems using Grafana, Prometheus, Loki, and Alertmanager
• Build and maintain dashboards, alerts, PromQL/LogQL queries, and YAML configurations
• Investigate incidents, troubleshoot issues, and continuously improve alert quality and monitoring coverage
• Develop and maintain scripts, APIs, webhooks, and monitoring automations
• Create workflows and integrations using Power Automate, Microsoft Teams, and Jira Service Management (JSM)
• Manage monitoring configurations and changes through Git/GitHub
• Collaborate with technical and business teams to understand monitoring requirements and their impact

🔎 What We’re Looking For
• Degree in Computer Science, MIS, IT, or a related field
• 1–3 years of professional IT monitoring experience
• Hands-on experience with Grafana and Prometheus; familiarity with Loki and Alertmanager is a plus
• Basic knowledge of Python/scripting, YAML, REST APIs, and webhooks
• Familiarity with Git/GitHub and automation tools such as Power Automate
• Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills
• A collaborative mindset with a strong sense of ownership and accountability
• Curious, proactive, and motivated to learn new technologies

📩 Interested? Send your CV to: p.nahlous@tradias.de

⚠️ Please note: This position is intended for candidates with 1–3 years of professional IT monitoring experience. Applications will be reviewed based on the required experience and core technical qualifications.
