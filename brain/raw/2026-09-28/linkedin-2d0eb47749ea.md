Week 1 Recap: From Forms to Full Automation with n8n! 🚀

Last week we kicked off with hands-on automation and it was amazing! We had our first test and our first practical assignment on building real business workflows.

Our Assignment: Automating Customer Journeys

We were given 3 scenarios to automate:

1. Customer Enquiry Automation for a Digital Marketing Agency
The challenge: When a client submits an online form, automatically capture their name/email/service, save to Google Sheets, send a confirmation email, and notify the sales team on Slack.

2. Online Store Order Automation
The challenge: When a customer places an order, capture the order details, record it in Google Sheets, send an order confirmation email, and notify the store owner.

3. Job Application Automation
The challenge: When an applicant submits a form, capture their details (name, email, phone, position), save to Google Sheets, send an application-received email, and notify the HR team.

The Solution Pattern (for all 3):
Form/Webhook Trigger → Capture Data → Google Sheets (Storage) → Gmail (Customer Communication) → Slack/Email (Business Notification)

This taught me how one simple trigger can power an entire business process without manual work.

We also had our first test:
We were asked which n8n trigger to use for (a) quickly creating a new form that feeds into a workflow = Form Trigger, and (b) connecting an EXISTING website form to n8n = Webhook Trigger.

So excited to keep learning and building smarter, automated systems!
