My goal for AI in my Customer Success team? More time with customers.

I’m currently working with my teams on exactly that.

We’re developing an AI driven approach to identify churn risks earlier by bringing together customer signals that would otherwise require a lot of manual analysis.

But detecting churn is only part of the objective.

The real goal is to give our CSMs more time with their customers.

Less time analysing data, building reports and looking for signals manually.

More time understanding customers, building relationships, driving adoption and creating value.

I don’t want AI to create another dashboard for CSMs to monitor.

I want it to do the heavy lifting behind the scenes, identify the signals, surface the risks and help the team know where to focus.

AI is everywhere in the Customer Success conversation, but when I speak with people in my network, surprisingly few have actually implemented AI solutions within their CS teams.

So I’m curious:

What AI initiatives have you implemented within your Customer Success teams?

I’d love to exchange ideas and experiences, hear what’s actually working, and learn how others are using AI to make their teams more effective and give CSMs more time with customers.
