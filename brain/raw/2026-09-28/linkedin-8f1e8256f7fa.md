Building a multi-location appointment scheduler on Salesforce shouldn't mean drowning in boilerplate XML, blowing through governor limits, or reinventing standard CRM objects.

In our hands-on engineering series, we break down how to architect and build a scalable Salesforce Appointment Scheduler from the ground up—with a clean, developer-first approach:

🔹 Maximizing Native Capabilities: Leveraging standard compound address geocoding and SOQL DISTANCE() to eliminate 3rd-party mapping latency.
🔹 Least-Privilege Security from Day 1: Scaffolding dedicated permission sets instead of building as System Administrator.
🔹 Governor-Limit-Conscious Testing: Enforcing strict separation between in-memory unit test fixtures (build) and persisted records (create) to keep test suites blazing fast.
🔹 High-Velocity CLI Automation: Using sf paired with hob to eliminate manual XML authoring and click-fatigue across schemas, permission sets, and test factories.
