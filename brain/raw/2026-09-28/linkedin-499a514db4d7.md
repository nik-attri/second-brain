A few months ago, I took on a side project that changed how I think about my own work.
A Saudi inspection company asked me to help them get ready for ISO/IEC 17020 accreditation — from a standing start. No quality manual. No documented procedures. No management system at all, really. Just a good team and a hard deadline.
What started as "can you help us with some documents" turned into building an entire QMS from the ground up: policies, procedures, forms, internal audits, management review, the works. Watching it come together — and watching the team start to trust the system instead of firefighting — taught me something worth sharing: the hardest part was never the paperwork. It was getting people to actually run their business through a system instead of around it.
So I'm formalizing what I learned. I'm launching QMS Blueprint — a consulting practice focused on helping small and mid-sized companies build management systems that actually hold up under audit, without drowning their teams in bureaucracy they'll never use.
If you're a business owner or quality manager staring down an accreditation deadline with no idea where to start, I'd love to talk.
