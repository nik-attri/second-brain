WHY SHOULD MONTH-END CLOSE FEEL LIKE AN EMERGENCY EVERY MONTH?

If the same reconciliations, accruals, adjustments and reporting activities happen every month, why do many finance teams still experience the same month-end pressure?

Sometimes the problem is not workload.

It is the design of the process.

A strong month-end close should have:

✓ Clearly assigned responsibilities  
✓ Cut-off deadlines communicated before month-end  
✓ Standard reconciliation templates  
✓ Automated recurring entries where appropriate  
✓ Outstanding-item tracking  
✓ Pre-close activities completed before the final day  
✓ Clear review and approval responsibilities  
✓ Escalation of unresolved items  
✓ A close calendar visible to the entire finance team  

But there is another question finance teams should ask:

Which activities are we repeatedly doing manually that our ERP could already be doing for us?

Automation does not necessarily mean purchasing expensive new software.

Sometimes it begins with properly using functionality already available in the organisation's ERP—recurring postings, automated reports, workflows, reconciliations, alerts and standard templates.

The objective should not simply be to close the books faster.

It should be to achieve a faster, more controlled and more accurate close while giving finance professionals more time for analysis.

Finance transformation sometimes starts with a very simple question:

“Why are we still doing this manually?”
