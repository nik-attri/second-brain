Can a fire sprinkler design get rejected even if we follow every single NFPA 13 requirement to the letter?
The short answer is yes!
 
Meeting spacing rules and balancing hydraulic calculations is only half the battle. If our drawing is cluttered, hard to navigate, or visually overwhelming, reviewers and fitters won’t waste time trying to decipher it. A design that isn’t easily readable simply isn’t usable in the field.
