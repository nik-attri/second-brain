I have been working in payroll for over 20 years now and somehow, I’m still here! 😂

I’m sharing this because, when people hear “payroll”, they often think it’s just about numbers, spreadsheets and pressing a button on payday, but there’s so much more to it than that.

It’s problem-solving, juggling deadlines, dealing with the unexpected on a regular basis’ and then checking the same thing for the 50th time because you suddenly don’t trust your own eyes 👀can anyone else relate to this? 

And yes, there are definitely moments when you wonder why you chose this career in the first place! 😂 although as most people will say in payroll is that it chose me 🫣 

Today as a Payroll Team Leader working for Deel, the main part of my role is that I am supporting other people on their payroll journey.

This means that I’m helping my team grow, keeping everyone calm when things get a little chaotic and reminding them that, yes… we WILL get through payroll! And it’s such a sigh of relief at the end of the month but then it all starts again at the beginning of the following month 😂

But over the years, I have learned hell of a lot about UK Legislation , made many, many mistakes, fixed my many, many mistakes aswell as others many, many mistakes and still have lots of “WTH” is going on moments.

But one thing hasn’t changed: I still care about getting it right. Because when payroll goes well, nobody notices. But when it doesn’t… EVERYONE notices! 😂🤔😂

So I’m sharing this as a little reminder that payroll isn’t just about processing pay. It’s about responsibility, teamwork, problem-solving and making sure people are paid accurately and on time.

And after more than 20 years, I’m still proud to be part of it. ❤️
