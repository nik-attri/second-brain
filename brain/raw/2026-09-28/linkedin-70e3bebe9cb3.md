How do you sum up a chapter that gave you so much more than just a job?
Last Friday I finished with Ray White Commercial CSR.
And I'm finding this one a little harder to write than I expected.

I came into this role ready to work hard, build, improve things and make a difference. What I didn't expect was how much I would grow along the way.

I've had the opportunity to be part of an incredible team, take on some seriously challenging situations, build systems and processes from the ground up, solve problems that sometimes felt impossible and work with clients who trusted me with assets, businesses and decisions that genuinely mattered.

There were long days, difficult conversations, plenty of fires to put out, a ridiculous number of spreadsheets and more than a few moments where commercial property management decided to keep me humble.

But there was also so much laughter.
So much learning.
And a team I became incredibly proud of.

I'll miss the things that never make it into a job description:
+ The communal supply of my energy shots and drinks
+ Passing around the Celtic salt like we were running some kind of highly questionable wellness clinic
+ The moments some of us ended up lying on the office floor because apparently that was the only reasonable response to whatever fresh challenge had landed in our inbox
+ And laughing at situations that somehow became perfectly normal parts of our working week

Some days were stressful.
Some days were absolute bedlam.
And some days all we could do was look at each other, laugh and get on with it.
Those are the moments I'll probably remember most.

Watching people grow in confidence, capability and knowledge has been one of the most rewarding parts of this role. I'll miss the conversations across the office, the problem solving sessions, the random humour, the Monday madness and being surrounded by people who genuinely cared about doing a good job.

To our owners, tenants, contractors and clients... Thank you for trusting me. Some of you challenged me, some taught me and some became people I genuinely enjoyed hearing from.

And to the RWC CSR team.. Thank you.

Thank you for trusting me to lead, for letting me challenge the way we did things, for embracing new systems and ideas and for tolerating my obsession with processes, audits, AI and "there has to be a better way to do this" conversations.

I'm incredibly proud of our journey.
Walking away from this team is without question the hardest part.

But sometimes a chapter can be wonderful and still be ready to end.
I'm leaving grateful, proud and genuinely excited about what comes next.

This role reinforced something I already knew about myself... I love people, I love building things, I love solving problems and I love the journey that comes with bringing people along for the ride.

Thank you, RWC CSR, for the lessons, the trust, the madness, the laughs and the people.
It's been one hell of a chapter. ❤️
