Ever tried selling on a #marketplace and ended up #drowning in double data entry, retyping products, updating stock twice, copying orders by hand?

That's the headache a sports retail client of ours came to us with. A solid online store, but reaching customers on a popular marketplace meant manual work, or hiring someone just to keep the two in sync. Not realistic for a catalogue of 70,000+ product variations.

So we built the #bridge between the two.

One click sends products, prices and stock to the marketplace. Orders land automatically in their store, ready to prepare. The moment something ships or gets cancelled, the marketplace knows within minutes, no manual updates, and it even talks to their accounting software.

The numbers: stock refreshed every 5 minutes, orders picked up every 15, three systems working as one.
Selling in more places shouldn't mean more manual work. Sometimes it just takes the right integration.
