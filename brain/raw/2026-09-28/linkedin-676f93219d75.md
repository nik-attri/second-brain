An AI decision wiped off my 16 year old account.
And there is no human in a 1.9 trillion dollar company who could do anything about it.

Recently, I got hit by the AI ban wave on Meta and Instagram.
Late in the night, I receive a mail that my Facebook account is suspended because the account doesn't follow Community Guidelines. 

It must surely be a mistake by some AI agent. I clicked on Appeal.
2 minutes later, I got another mail, saying that my account is permanently banned. 
I checked. All of my accounts were gone. Facebook. Instagram. My company account. Everything.

That cant be true. I never received any warning in the past. 
It's my personal account. I don't pretend to be someone I am not.
There must be some way to get a human to manually check it.

I spent the next 2 hours talking to Meta AI Support who made me run in loops without giving anything concrete. 

Fed up, I went to Reddit. 
What a sigh of relief to see that I was not the only one.
There is a massive group of people who were wrongly terminated in the past 6 months. 
All across the world. 

They tried every way possible. But there is no way to reach a human.
Their last resource: File a case against Meta.

Now I have 2 options how this can go for me. 
Also a third bonus option for Meta. 

1. I focus on other channels. I still have a LinkedIn account. YouTube. X. I have no time to go through the legal route. I can get clients via these channels as well. 

2. I wait patiently. I have mailed the Grievance Officer. I have opened reports with AI chat agents. But the chances of anything meaningful happening are very low.

3. (Bonus): I have an idea for Meta to go from 1.9 trillion dollar to a 2.5 trillion dollar company. Fast. Just have an option for people whose accounts were disabled to pay for a human agent. $100 - $500 for a human check, depending on the size of your account. This is such a huge market. There are so many people just waiting to get their accounts back. 

Right now, I am a bit clueless. 
Do I have anyone in my contact list who is connected to someone at Meta? 
Do they know about this problem?
Can social media do its thing to connect to the right person?

Final thought:
I hate being a fearmonger but what in the actual sci-fi world are we living in, where an AI agent decides something, but the decision is final irrespective of its chances that its decision was wrong. 
And no human is out there to check.
In a 1.9 trillion$ company. 
The irony.
