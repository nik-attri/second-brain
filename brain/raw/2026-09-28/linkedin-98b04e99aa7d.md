There are a few things I swore I’d never do when I was an engineer.
Then I became CTO.
Now I say things like:
“Do we really need to build this?”
“Can we ship the scrappy version first?”
“Let’s not touch that system right now.”
“Who is this actually for?”
“Can we solve this manually for 10 customers before automating it?”

4 years ago me would probably hate working with current me.

Back then, a hard technical problem was exciting by default. Now I’ve learned that the hardest problem isn’t always the one worth solving.

Sometimes the best engineering decision is a shortcut. Sometimes it’s deleting something. Sometimes it’s telling an excited engineer, “Cool idea. Not now.”

Turns out becoming CTO didn’t make me more obsessed with technology.
It made me much more suspicious of building things :)
