PSA on AI:
You don't HAVE to use AI in every single thing you do.
Your business doesn't HAVE to incorporate AI in all business processes and workflows.
AI is NOT a must have.
AI is purely optional.

The world is not going to come crashing down if you don't have the newest AI model or if you are out of tokens or even - wait for it... if you just don't use AI.

Saying it again:
The world is not going to come crashing down if you do not use AI.
It will probably just come crashing down for all of the investors who have invested trillions of dollars (yes, TRILLIONS) and sure as hell won't ever see that money ever again 😂 

People can function completely normally, if not better, in today's world without AI, or at least those who haven't outsourced all their critical thinking and skillsets to AI.

AI is NOT the next smartphone, the next WiFi, the next car, the next printing press.
Take away smartphones, Wifi, cars, etc. the world would probably be a mess in the tech age.
Take away AI... is it really going to cause a global meltdown and mess up everything?

Majority of people use AI to either summarize information or organize information.
Sure, that's useful to a degree, but is it really necessary and JUSTIFIABLE to put all of our resources into advancing AI and building data centers on every street corner just for AI summaries or that AI can do a spreadsheet?

Furthermore, is it justifiable to ruin our economies, our environment, our psychological and mental well being, and everything that makes us human just for convenience and profit and "productivity"?
