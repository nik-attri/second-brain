One thing I’m trying not to do when speaking to businesses about AI is start with AI.

It’s very easy to walk into a meeting and immediately start talking about chatbots, automation, voice agents and software.

But most business owners don’t really care about any of that.

They care about:
- Where are we wasting time?
- What are we paying people to do manually?
- Where are enquiries being missed?
- What causes mistakes or delays?
- How can we improve customer experience? 

Those are much better questions to start with.

- Sometimes the answer is AI
- Sometimes it’s better software
- Sometimes it’s improving an existing process
- Sometimes the biggest opportunity is somewhere else entirely

The more conversations I have with SMEs, the more I realise the value isn’t in trying to put AI everywhere.

It’s in identifying the few areas where better systems can genuinely save time, reduce costs or help the business grow.

The technology comes second.
