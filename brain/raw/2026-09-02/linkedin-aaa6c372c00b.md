WE AUTOMATED THE WRONG THING.
BUT FASTER.

Every growing company has that one process.
> A lead arrives by email
> Someone copies it into a spreadsheet (Zapier it)
> Someone else asks about it in Slack
> The CRM says the deal died 3 months ago
> Martin Riggs from 2nd floor knows the real status

Then management says: “Let’s add AI guys!”
Excellente. Now the wrong customer gets the wrong follow-up automatically, 24 hours a day, with a beautifully written summary.

That’s progress. Anti-perfection. AI doesn’t fix a broken process.
It gives it sunglasses, a badge and permission to move faster.

Before you automate anything, answer these questions:
. Where does the truth live ? (in how many places)
. Who makes the decision ?
. What happens when something goes wrong ?

If the answer is “ask Martin” >>> you don’t have an AI problem. You have an operations problem wearing a software costume. Find out what works best manually 100% then AI/automate the hell out of it.

Don’t give a broken process a robot partner. Fix the process first.
