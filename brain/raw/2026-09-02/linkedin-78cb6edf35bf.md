Most businesses don't have a software problem.
They have a process problem.
I’ve seen businesses use WhatsApp, spreadsheets, emails and multiple tools to manage one process.
Then they decide
“We need an app”

Before building anything, look at the process
What is repetitive?
What gets delayed?
What gets done manually?
Where do mistakes happen?
Sometimes you need custom software
Sometimes you need automation
Sometimes you just need to remove one unnecessary step.
Good software starts with understanding the business.
Not writing code
That’s one of the biggest lessons building real systems has taught me

If your business process feels harder than it should, look at the process before looking for another tool
Would you change the process before building new software?
