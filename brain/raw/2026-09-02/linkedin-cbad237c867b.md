A copywriter messaged me, exhausted.

Booked solid. Genuinely good at her craft. Still felt like she was drowning.

Every new client meant setting up the same thing from scratch, a new intake form, a new invoice template, a new onboarding email, manually, every single time.

She thought that was just what being busy looked like.

I asked how many hours a week that setup actually cost her. She'd never counted. Once she did, it came out to almost six.

Six hours a week, disappearing into repetitive setup, for someone whose actual skill had nothing to do with forms and invoices.

We built her one system. New client fills a form once, everything else, invoice, welcome sequence, contract, generates automatically behind the scenes.

She got six hours back without raising her rates or taking on less work.

Most creatives think being overwhelmed means they need to slow down or charge more.

Sometimes it just means the boring parts were never built to run without them.

If setup is quietly eating your week too, that's the part I build for people.

PS: she used those six hours to actually take a full Saturday off. first one in months.
