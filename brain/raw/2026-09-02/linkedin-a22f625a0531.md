AI WhatsApp Customer Support Bot

" Responds to every WhatsApp inbound message with a context-aware ChatGPT reply, maintaining full conversation history per customer via Make.com's Data Store. "

You know that moment when customer support is drowning in 200+ WhatsApp messages a day, and your team spends 40 hours a week copy-pasting the same replies?

Manual support on WhatsApp always breaks the moment volume scales.
Most companies try to fix this by hiring more reps or dropping in a basic chatbot.

But generic chatbots treat every message as an isolated event — forcing customers to repeat themselves until they leave for a competitor.

We built an AI WhatsApp support engine using Make.com, ChatGPT, and 360dialog that maintains full conversation memory for every single customer.

Here's how the workflow executes in seconds:

→ 360dialog webhook catches the incoming message instantly
→ Make.com queries a Data Store using the customer's phone number to pull full interaction history 
→ Intelligent Router loads past context for returning users or initialises the business knowledge base for new contacts 
→ ChatGPT generates a natural, brand-consistent reply referencing previous exchanges 
→ 360dialog delivers the response straight back to the WhatsApp chat within seconds → Data Store appends the new exchange to keep memory seamless across future sessions

Why conversation memory is the critical differentiator:

Standard chatbots fail because they have zero memory. By persisting the full interaction history in a Data Store, the bot can reference previous order details, understand the ongoing context, and only escalate to human agents when complex situations arise.

The real business impact:

→ 80% reduction in manual support workload (from 40 hours weekly down to 8) 
→ 90% improvement in customer satisfaction due to instant replies 
→ $50K+ annual savings compared to adding support headcount 
→ 550% ROI by converting after-hours enquiries 24/7
