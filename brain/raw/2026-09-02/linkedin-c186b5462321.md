For most of my career, I worked in software, publishing, and technology.

I was trained to solve problems with logic. Gather the information. Analyze it. Make the decision.

But there were also times when I knew something before I had enough information to logically know it.

A feeling about a person. A hesitation about a decision. A solution that seemed to come out of nowhere.

For years, I dismissed those moments as coincidence. Now I understand they were another form of information: intuition.

And intuition isn't something you have to leave at the door when you walk into work.
It can help you navigate relationships, make decisions, recognize when something isn't quite right, and understand information that hasn't made its way onto the spreadsheet yet.
