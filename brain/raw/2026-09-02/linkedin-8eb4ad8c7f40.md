Meet our CCO, Igno 👋

Environmental monitoring has become more connected, but for many teams, it is still fragmented.
Different devices. Different platforms. Different workflows. More time spent bringing everything together.

That is one of the challenges Igno wants to help solve.
He joined Omnidots as CCO in January, with a clear focus on the next step: making environmental monitoring easier to manage across hardware, data, and software.

“Customers are tired of managing fragmented systems. The opportunity is to bring trusted field data and automated software workflows together in one environment.”

Honeycomb brings vibration and sound monitoring together, with the goal of reducing manual work and giving teams a clearer view of what is happening on site.

At INTERGEO, Igno is looking forward to discussing Omnidots vision with customers, partners, and monitoring professionals.
His question for visitors:
“How can Honeycomb automate the manual work in my current monitoring and reporting process?”

If that sounds familiar, meet Igno and the Omnidots team at INTERGEO in Munich.
