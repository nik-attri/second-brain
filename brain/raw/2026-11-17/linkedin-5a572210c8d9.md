🤖 When we talk about automation, we often celebrate the hours saved.
But lately, I’ve been thinking about a different question:
What happens to those hours?
If we automate one repetitive task only to replace it with another, have we really improved the way we work?

For me, the more interesting question isn’t:
“How much can we automate?”
It’s:
👉 “What can people do better because we automated it?”

Think about the time we spend chasing updates, compiling information, checking routine activities or preparing recurring reports.

Now imagine giving some of that time back to people.
Time to solve a difficult problem.
Improve a service.
Learn something new.
Collaborate with a colleague.
Or simply pause and make a better decision.

That’s where I believe the real opportunity with AI and automation lies.
Let technology take care of more of the repetition.
Let AI help uncover patterns and insights.
And let people bring the context, curiosity and judgement.

Because good automation shouldn’t make people less relevant.
It should give people more opportunity to do the work where they add the most value.
Maybe the real measure of automation isn’t simply the work it removes.
💡 It’s the better work it makes possible.
What’s one repetitive task you’d happily never do manually again? 🙂
