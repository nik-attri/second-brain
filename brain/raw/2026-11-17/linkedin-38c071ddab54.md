A great hotel concierge reads you in nine seconds. Rooftop or the quiet place around the corner.

Now imagine that concierge was written by IT from a product spreadsheet.

That's most brand chatbots. And this week, they became advertising.

OpenAI is letting brands publish "business agents" inside ChatGPT. Priceline put its agent Penny inside Snapchat ads, and says Penny users convert at about 2x.

The bot is no longer a support widget. It's the first thing customers meet.

"Great question! Here are some options that may suit your needs" isn't a voice. It's a hold message.
