I didn’t choose the name “Automation Fairy.” 🧚‍♀️

A client did.

For a long time, my headline said things like:

“GoHighLevel Specialist | CRM Automation | Workflow Builder”

All true.
None of it memorable.

Then I finished a project for a client who’d been drowning in manual follow-ups.
Leads came in, replies went out, reminders sent themselves.

A week later she messaged me:
“I don’t know what you did, but it’s like a fairy is running my business now.” ✨

I laughed.
Then I realised she’d explained my work better than my entire headline ever had.

Clients don’t remember tools.
They remember how you made their work feel.

So I kept the name.
And people started remembering me. 😄

Sometimes your best positioning isn’t something you invent.
It’s something your clients already say about you.

What’s the nicest thing a client has ever said about your work? 👇
