Some might be aware that I got made redundant a few months back, and have been frantically job-hunting since.

In aid of that, I used Lovable to build myself a job-tracker tool.

https://lnkd.in/gN2NMaGZ

It has google-based authentication and stores user's data on a simple database, which is great for easy portability across platforms.

It beats the hell out of spreadsheets anyway.

Any time I make a new job application I fill in a quick form with company, recruiter, job-title and URL, plus a few useful bits like salary and Remote/Hybrid, and whenever there's new info, I can add an update with status and description.

There's even a History page with stats and graphs, so I can tell how many new applications I made each week.

Anyway, if you're in my position and want a tool to help manage your job-hunting, feel free to make use of it!
