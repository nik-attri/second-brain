Most Project Managers are still drowning in status updates, scattered spreadsheets, and “Can you just quickly check…” messages.

Funnily, I used to be one of them... 😂

But not anymore! I have stopped treating tools like Airtable, Google Sheets, n8n, Make, and Zapier as “nice-to-haves” and started treating them as the actual operating system of my projects.

Here’s what changed:

- Airtable became the single source of truth, not another spreadsheet that dies in a week.

- Automations in n8n/Make/Zapier turned repetitive follow-ups, status syncs, and handoffs into invisible background processes.

- AI + structured data meant I stopped chasing information and started directing it.

Now, you might wanna know what result I got... 😂 
Well, I got less time to coordinate and more time to actually manage outcomes.

Project management isn’t about being the person who knows where everything is. It’s about designing systems so everyone already knows.

If you are still manually updating trackers and chasing people for status, you are not managing the project, you are managing the chaos the project creates.
