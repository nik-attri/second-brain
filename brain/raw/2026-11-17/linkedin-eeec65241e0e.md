The repetitive follow-ups. The endless copy-pasting. The spreadsheets you keep updating manually.

Imagine getting that time back.

On November 23, Women of SaaS Milan and Women+ in Automation are bringing you Build Your First Automation — a hands-on workshop where you'll go from doing everything manually to building your very first automation.

And the best part? You don't need to know how to code.

✨ Here's what you can expect:

→ Learn the basics of n8n and see an automation built live, step by step.

→ Bring your laptop and a repetitive task you'd love to automate.

→ Build your own workflow with mentors in the room to guide you.

→ Share what you've created and connect with other women over networking and aperitivo.

Meet the experts guiding the session:

🎤 Giovanni Andreose — n8n Ambassador for Italy
🎤 Srishti Gupta — Data & AI Engineer, Euronext
🎤 Eithar Yassin — AI Developer, Adler Ortho

Whether you work in marketing, product, operations, HR, sales, or you're simply curious about automation, this workshop is designed for beginners.
