Booking a hotel day pass in Cyprus by hand turned out to be a part-time job 

We wanted something simple: one day at a hotel, all inclusive, at a good price. Doing it manually meant checking sites, refreshing pages, comparing offers and forgetting where I saw what.

So I stopped refreshing and wrote a scenario in Claude Code instead. I described the task and the conditions and asked it to watch for day pass offers.

Three days later it sent us a list of hotels. I asked Claude to email the hotel, and then we just called and agreed on everything.

What surprised me was where the effort actually sat. The booking itself took one phone call, while everything else was waiting and watching.

As someone who works on products, I see a familiar pattern here. When people build their own workaround, they show you a job the product doesn't do for them. Booking platforms are great at helping you search, but what I needed was someone to wait for me 

Every user workaround is a feature request nobody filed.

What task have you handed to an AI agent just because no app does it the way you need?
