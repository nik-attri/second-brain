Everyone is queuing at the wrong door, but there is a reason why

The largest enterprises in the world are all using AI, but many are getting far less value from it than they could.

As lead AI adviser to Fortune 500 and FTSE 250 companies, I start with the foundation:
How people work, who owns the result and what leaders are willing to change.

Many companies tend to skip that.

They run more and more pilots, roll out licences across the business, and add agents to processes nobody has questioned in years.

It all looks like progress, but the operating model underneath stays exactly the same.

This is what real AI transformation looks like:
1. Redesign the process first.
→ Remove the steps that should not exist before you automate the ones that should.
2. Fix the data the work depends on.
→ AI is only as reliable as the information it runs on.
3. Name an owner for the result.
→ One executive accountable for the value it delivers.
4. Change how people are managed.
→ If appraisals still reward the old way of working, people will keep working the old way.
5. Add AI and agents where they create value.
→ Only once the four steps above are in place.

None of this is as exciting as a demo, but it is how AI ends up on the P&L.

So start with one workflow that matters.

Redesign it end to end, name its owner and prove the value before moving to the next.
That is how transformation stops being a slide in the board pack and becomes the way the company runs.
