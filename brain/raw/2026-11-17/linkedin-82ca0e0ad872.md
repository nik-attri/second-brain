A note for Founders: You’re trying to grow GTM, close a round, and build a team, all before your runway runs out. “Cut costs” doesn’t begin to cover it.

The gap is rarely “should we use AI,” it’s finding the time. You’re heads-down on GTM, fundraising, and the next 10 hires, and AI adoption keeps slipping down the list. We get it, exploring tools and building the case for adoption takes time most founders don’t have spare.

But the upside shows up in a few places at once:
- Complements your hiring strategy: automate investor research during a raise instead of adding a junior analyst to do it manually.
- Saves revenue: draft your financial model and deck in-house with AI, instead of paying a fractional CFO or consultant.
- Saves time: automate due diligence prep and data room organisation, so you walk into investor meetings prepared, not up all night buried in folders.

Fundraising only adds to the pressure. Gartner research shows AI is already saving sales teams close to 5 hours a week, time that’s even harder to spare mid-raise, when every hour should go to investors, not admin.
