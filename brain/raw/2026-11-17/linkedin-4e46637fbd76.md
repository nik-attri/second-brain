AZ-104 Tip!

To tag onto the last post I had. When we look at Policies, we can apply them at the subscription or resource group level. A clear policy definition helps to know what the policy should do, and what it applies to as well.

For example, we can have all NSGs within a Resource Group (RG) have a deny port 80 rule across all subnets within that specific RG. It's much faster than manually applying these rules in each NSG.
