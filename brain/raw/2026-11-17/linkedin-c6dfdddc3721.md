Walking into a client meeting with "remind me who's on this call?" is a rough way to start.

Grok Bot gives you AI teammates you message like a coworker. Zachary Mitchell of Brainfinite AI has been testing a two-Bot meeting prep setup for owners and sales teams.

The night before any meeting with an outside company, a Client Meetings Bot checks tomorrow's calendar, hands each company to an Account Research Bot (their website, news from the last 30 days), pulls the account history from the CRM, and emails a one-page brief by 6 PM: who's attending, what changed, where things stand, and two questions worth asking.

After the meeting it asks for three quick notes and drafts the recap email and the CRM note. Both wait for a person to read and approve them. Nothing goes to the client on its own.

What the owner still decides: the real goal of the meeting. A Bot can surface that a client just opened a new location. Only you know whether that's a chance to grow the account or a sign they're shopping around.

Good habit from day one: keep the CRM read-only at first, and sign in on the Bot's computer yourself instead of sharing passwords in a chat.

The example uses Outlook. If the team runs on HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel or a shared spreadsheet, the same idea works with what's already in place.

It's an early version of the idea. Every sales process is shaped a little differently, so this can go a few directions, and more complete builds are on the way.
