Bridging Tongji engineering roots with industrial practice in Shanghai. 🌉⚙️
​Today, I organized a small-group visit for 8 同济大学 alumni and students to the 西门子 Intelligent Manufacturing Innovation Center in Shanghai. 🏢 As a Tongji alumnus currently working as a Working Student at Siemens Mobility in Germany, it was a great opportunity to step back from my daily simulation models and explore how Siemens’ broader industrial software and AI ecosystem is applied on the ground in China. 🇨🇳🇩🇪
​Instead of a high-level marketing tour, we dove straight into engineering workflows and real-world deployment challenges. Here are a few technical takeaways that stood out from our session: 💡
​🔄 Closed-Loop Digital Twins (Siemens Xcelerator): Moving far beyond static 3D visualization. It connects virtual commissioning and multi-domain system simulation directly with shop-floor PLC data, edge diagnostics, and cloud analytics.
​🧠 Pragmatic Industrial AI: Unlike generative consumer AI, industrial AI prioritizes determinism and physical accuracy. Seeing how AI surrogate models (especially following the Altair integration) cut down thousands of heavy CAE simulation runs, and how upcoming MCP/SDK integrations will automate CAD/CAE workflows, was particularly relevant to my own engineering work.
​🥽 Immersive Line Validation: Testing a virtual battery production line via VR to evaluate ergonomics, worker fatigue, and layout bottlenecks before committing to physical hardware.
​A huge thank you to our guide, Lei Zhao (赵雷), for walking us through the labs and answering our candid questions on ROI hurdles, solver convergence, and software licensing models. 🙌 Special thanks as well to my fellow Tongji peers, including 施驿捷, for making the discussions so sharp. 🤝
​Always energizing to connect with fellow engineers across borders. Looking forward to bringing these perspectives back to my work in Germany! 🚀
