How many times does your restaurant team answer the same guest questions every day?

“Do you have a table available tonight?”
“Can we sit near the window or somewhere quiet?”
“Is parking available?”
“Can I change my reservation?”
“How long is the waiting time?”
“Can you remind me about my booking?”

These are everyday conversations in the restaurant business.

But managing every enquiry manually, especially during busy lunch and dinner hours, isn't easy.

Your team is already busy welcoming guests, managing tables, coordinating orders and delivering great service.

What if more of these conversations could be handled before they reach your staff?

That's where DineIQ AI comes in.

Imagine connecting the entire guest reservation journey through one intelligent conversation layer.

With DineIQ AI, restaurants can support guest interactions such as:
→ Voice Reservations: Answer calls and help guests with booking requests.
→ Seating Preferences: Capture requests for window seats, booths, quiet corners, indoor or outdoor seating.
→ 3D Seating Experience: Give guests a clearer view of seating choices where an interactive 3D floor-plan feature is available.
→ Parking Information: Answer questions about parking and nearby facilities using restaurant-approved information.
→ Reservation Management: Support booking changes, cancellations and confirmations.
→ Smart Reminders: Send reservation reminders through configured messaging workflows.
→ Waitlist Management: Capture waiting guests and notify them when tables become available through connected reservation workflows.
→ Menu & Dietary Enquiries: Help guests understand menu options and communicate dietary preferences.
→ Human Handoff: Bring restaurant staff into the conversation whenever personal assistance is needed.

And here's something worth thinking about.

A table becoming available after a cancellation is an opportunity.
A guest waiting for a table is an opportunity.
A phone call outside business hours is an opportunity.

The challenge is making sure these opportunities don't get lost in day-to-day operations.

The objective isn't to replace restaurant staff. It's to help them spend less time answering repetitive questions and more time looking after guests.
