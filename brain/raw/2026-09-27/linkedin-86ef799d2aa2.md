𝙁𝙧𝙤𝙢 𝙖𝙡𝙚𝙧𝙩𝙨 𝙩𝙤 𝙖𝙘𝙩𝙞𝙤𝙣 — 𝙥𝙤𝙬𝙚𝙧𝙚𝙙 𝙗𝙮 𝙅𝙤𝙪𝙡𝙚. 🚀
What an incredible experience presenting at SAP Inside Track Bengaluru (#sitBLR) at SAP Labs Innovation Park, Devanahalli! 📍

I had the privilege of speaking on a topic close to my heart:
"Joule-Powered Situation Handling: Conversational AI for Intelligent Exception Management." 🤖

Here’s the challenge we all know too well — critical business exceptions often get lost in the noise, and by the time someone notices, valuable time (and money) is already gone. ⏳

Situation Handling in SAP S/4HANA Cloud flips this script. It proactively delivers the right alerts, to the right users, at the right time — helping teams address issues before they escalate. ✅

But the real game-changer? The Joule integration. 💡
Instead of manually hunting for exceptions, users simply ask in natural language:
🗣️ "Show me my situations?"
…and Joule responds instantly — surfacing relevant situations on demand and guiding users straight to the right Fiori app to take action. No searching. No guessing. Just conversational, intelligent exception management. 🔹

During the session, we explored:
🔹 SAP-delivered use cases for Situation Handling
🔹 How to customize situations for unique business needs
🔹 How Joule transforms exception management into a natural conversation
🔹 Real impact — saving time, reducing costs, and sharpening decision-making

What stayed with me most wasn’t just the tech — it was the energy of the community. 🌱 The curiosity, the questions, the hands-on enthusiasm from developers, partners, and SAP professionals who genuinely love what they do.

A huge thank you to the organizers, speakers, volunteers, and the entire SAP Community for making this event so memorable. 🤝

Proud to be part of a community that continues to learn, share, and innovate together.
Keep learning. Keep sharing. Keep connecting. 🚀
