Ignoring your online reviews is like leaving a patient waiting in your reception area. Not responding can cost you more than you realise.

For many dental practices, review responses are still handled manually, whenever someone finds the time. That usually means delays, inconsistent tone, and positive feedback receiving attention while more difficult reviews are left unanswered.

Patients do not expect every response to be identical. They do expect the practice to be present, professional and willing to listen.

A well-designed system can monitor new reviews, alert the right team member, suggest an appropriate response and ensure nothing is missed. Sensitive or complex feedback should still receive thoughtful human input, but automation removes the administrative gap between a review being posted and the practice responding.

This also supports a stronger local presence: active, relevant engagement helps show prospective patients that your clinic takes communication seriously.

It is the kind of system we build and refine for dental clinics at Rankdent—not to replace the human relationship, but to protect it at scale.
