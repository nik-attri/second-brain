Some jobs in your business aren't difficult.

They're just really annoying to have to remember. 😂

Send that email.
Copy those details over.
Update that spreadsheet.
Send the booking information.
Follow up with that person.

None of them are particularly complicated.

And individually, they might only take a few minutes.

But first you have to REMEMBER to do them.
Then stop whatever you're doing.
Find the right thing.
Do the job.

And repeat the whole process again next week.

Or tomorrow.

Or every single time somebody books.

That's the bit I like getting rid of.

Not by automating every inch of your business until nobody can work out what the hell is happening.

Just by looking at the repetitive bits and asking:

Does this actually need me?

Could I set this up ONCE instead?

Because there are enough things rattling around in my head already. 😂

I made a free guide with 10 things you can set up once rather than doing them every week.
