With the spring equinox behind us here in Aotearoa, and the clocks now sprung forward, it feels like a good time to pause, clear out what no longer serves us, and make space for what comes next.

I've been fairly quiet on LinkedIn lately.

There is already so much being said about AI, automation and transformation. Yet in many of the conversations I'm having, I'm seeing something more fundamental.

Boards and leadership teams are under increasing pressure to invest, modernise and respond to AI. But before we ask whether our organisations are ready for AI, perhaps there is another question worth asking:

“Is the mindset we are bringing into the boardroom ready for the age of AI?”

I'll come back to that another time.

For now, a simple spring-cleaning thought:

📍Before you automate a process or introduce AI, pause and ask what you may be carrying forward with it. And be clear about who is responsible for which clean-up.

✔️In the business, the spring clean is operational.
→ An unclear process does not become clearer because it is automated.
→ Information that is inconsistent, outdated or poorly governed does not become more trustworthy because AI can access it.
→ Unclear ownership does not disappear when technology enters the decision.

That work belongs with the people who own the processes and the data.

✔️In the boardroom, the spring clean is different. It is not the Board's role to tidy workflows. It is the Board's role to examine what it asks for, what it rewards, and what it assumes.
→ Are we debating which AI tool or brand to choose, before agreeing what problem it solves and how it advances our strategy?
→ Are we asking "how fast?" before "how ready?"
→ Are we judging AI by what was deployed, or by the value it protected?
→ Are we reviewing reports, or testing the assumptions behind them?

Management cleans what it runs. The Board cleans how it governs. When only one side does the work, the clutter simply moves.

Sometimes, before adding something new, the most intelligent thing we can do is look carefully at what we are about to carry forward.

So, as we step into spring:

❓What would your Board, and your business, prefer not to carry forward into an AI-enabled future?
