Most intake forms are built by asking, “What information could we collect?”

A better question is: “What does our team actually need to make the next step clear?”

Extra fields create extra friction. Poorly ordered questions create confusion. And a form without a thoughtful follow-up process simply moves the admin problem somewhere else.

This six-slide checklist covers:

• defining the form’s purpose
• removing questions nobody uses
• grouping fields in a logical order
• explaining sensitive requests clearly
• setting response expectations
• routing submissions to the right person

The aim is not to automate clinical judgment. It is to make the administrative journey clearer for patients and staff while keeping appropriate human oversight.
