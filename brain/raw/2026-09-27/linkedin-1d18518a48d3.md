14 hours of studying. Piles of 100+ books. And a lingering, terrifying question: "Am I even studying what actually matters right now?"

I lived this exact nightmare as a JEE aspirant. And I realized something broken: millions of students aren’t failing because they lack hard work. They are failing because they are drowning in information overload, fighting a blind war with zero direction.

So, I stopped complaining about it and started building the fix.
Introducing PrepMove 🚀 (Starting with PrepMove.JEE, and expanding into PrepMove.NEET, PrepMove.BITSAT, and beyond).

PrepMove isn't just another generic study timetable. Think of it as a dedicated personal spectator built directly for your preparation:

👁️ It Watches Your Blind Spots: PrepMove actively spectates your specific journey. Every single mock test you upload is dissected down to the micro-level, isolating your exact personal weaknesses.

⚡ Dynamic Percentage-Split Engine: It marries macro exam shifts with your individual test performance, automatically recalibrating your roadmap in real-time so your study energy always flows where it yields the highest rank.

🛑 Zero Noise, Pure Execution: No more 10,000 confusing options. It hands you absolute clarity: the exact chapter to master today, the precise book to open, and the exact verified resource to use.

🎯Automated Pipelines: Connect web data to every user's personal weakness and run an adaptive priority list for every user, connected seamlessly so updates happen with zero manual effort.

My 30-Day Execution Sprint: 
1️⃣ Build the core dynamic percentage-split engine for PrepMove.JEE. 
2️⃣ Ship our high-retention web interface for our first wave of beta testers. 
3️⃣ Run live student feedback loops to lock down hyper-precise study fixes.

I’m building this completely in public. If you had an intelligent spectator fixing your prep data, how many hours would you save?
