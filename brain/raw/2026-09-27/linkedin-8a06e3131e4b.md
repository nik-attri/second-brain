We’re hiring: AI Content Operations Intern 🎬

We’re looking for someone to join Trinetra AI  × Bullet Microdrama OTT and work hands-on with our AI content production and localization workflows.

This is an execution-heavy role. You’ll be working with actual content every day: generating, dubbing, syncing, checking, organizing, exporting and moving files through the production pipeline.

What you’ll actually do:

→ Run AI-assisted dubbing and localization workflows across languages
→ Generate and manage voice, audio, subtitles and localized video versions
→ Manually sync, review and fix outputs where AI doesn’t get it right
→ Perform QC checks on dubbing, lip sync, audio, subtitles and final videos
→ Organize source files, generations, dubs, subtitles and final exports
→ Maintain trackers and make sure every asset moves through the right production stage
→ Prepare and hand off clean, correctly named files to internal teams
→ Test AI video, audio, dubbing and localization models
→ Document workflows and help us make repetitive processes faster and more automated over time
→ Support ongoing AI content production wherever needed

You’ll probably enjoy this if you:

Are extremely organized, patient with repetitive production work and obsessive about getting details right.

You’re comfortable working with lots of files, spreadsheets, folders, versions and deadlines.

And you’re curious about AI, but understand that making AI useful in production requires a lot more than prompting.

You don’t need years of experience. Execution, attention to detail, speed and willingness to learn matter more.
