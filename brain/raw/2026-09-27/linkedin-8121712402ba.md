Just got home from Victoria, and now that I’ve finally had a chance to sit down, I’m starting to process just how incredible yesterday was.

BSides Vancouver Island 2026. What a day. 💚💜

A huge THANK YOU to everyone who came out yesterday, supported the event, filled the rooms, listened to some incredible talks, asked great questions, and most importantly, helped make BSidesVI the community-driven event that it is.

The talks were fantastic, but what I love most about BSides is what happens around them — the hallway conversations, old friends reconnecting, new people meeting for the first time, researchers comparing notes, and those completely unexpected conversations that you know will continue long after everyone has gone home.

To our amazing planning team — you are the reason this happens. Months of work, countless meetings, spreadsheets, emails, last-minute changes and probably more than a few moments of “why do we do this to our selves”

You pulled it off.

And to our wonderful volunteers, thank you for giving up your time and being everywhere yesterday. Registration, rooms, speakers, attendees, microphones, problems that nobody else even knew existed… you handled it all.

BSidesVI doesn’t happen without you.

A few very special thank-yous:

🍻 Horizon.io & Cycode, thank you for an amazing after-party and for giving everyone a place to keep the conversations going after the conference.

🍽️ Cafe Malabar, thank you for an incredible speakers’ dinner and for taking such amazing care of our speakers.

🏢 Victoria Conference Centre, your entire team was fantastic. Thank you for helping us turn all of our planning into an actual conference.

🎛️ Encore, thank you for the incredible technical support and for keeping everything running behind the scenes. You made our jobs a hell of a lot easier.

And to our speakers, thank you for travelling here, sharing your research, your experiences and your knowledge with our community. The quality of the talks yesterday was incredible, and I know I wasn’t the only one walking away with a long list of things to go research.

But most of all…

Thank you to everyone who showed up.

BSides isn’t a corporation putting on a conference.

It’s a community of people who care about this industry getting together and building something themselves.

Yesterday, that community was pretty damn awesome.

I’m incredibly proud of our planning team, our volunteers, our speakers and everyone who came out to support BSidesVI.

Now, I’m going to get some sleep…. Because apparently getting home from BSidesVI on Saturday and flying to BSides Saskatoon tomorrow seemed like a perfectly reasonable idea when I agreed to it. 😂

The weekend isn’t over yet. 💚💜

See you in Saskatoon!
