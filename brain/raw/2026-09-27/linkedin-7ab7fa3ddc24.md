I was asked to review 10% of the tickets.
I reviewed all of them.
Around 500.

Not because I was trying to be exceptionally thorough. I simply missed the "10%" part of the instruction. 😅

By the time I realised it, I had already gone through the entire month manually: opening tickets one by one, checking how the issue was investigated, how the answer was communicated, whether the resolution was correct, and evaluating each case against our quality criteria.

It was definitely more work than necessary.
I had a deadline, so I asked my team lead for two more days to finish the review. He was confused, but agreed.

Then we had a call with him and my colleague, and I started with:

"So, I reviewed around 500 tickets and noticed these things..."

Pause.

Both my team lead and my colleague stared at me with big eyes.

"Did I do something wrong?.."

"No... I just asked you to review 10% of the tickets for the month, not all of them."

"Ahhhh, really?.. Well, anyway, I noticed that..."

And that was the conversation. 😅

Later, my team lead told me that even our lead was wondering why I had asked for two more days to finish the review.

Because, you know, 50 tickets and 500 tickets are slightly different things)

And yes, I reviewed almost 500 tickets in roughly the amount of time that was originally planned for 50.
