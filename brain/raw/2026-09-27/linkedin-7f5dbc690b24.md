EL DIRECTOR DEL SABOR | EPISODE VIII: MAY THE FORECAST BE WITH YOU — THE COUNCIL OF PENDING QUESTIONS

Four festive concepts. Four critical paths.
Then the Council opened the P&L.

El Director arrived at the budget review with the F&B forecasts, the 2027 budget and a coffee that understood the assignment.

The Council had four questions:

What if? What happens? What the hell? And finally… what the F&B?

Revenue entered wearing a cape. Cost arrived with receipts. Profitability waited to see if anyone remembered it.

El Director pointed at a cell:
“What is the assumption behind this number?”
The spreadsheet requested legal counsel.


Across the festive kingdoms, the concepts met their critical paths.

At Octavious  Kingdom in the Sky, WonderScape saw a countdown. Operations saw four floors, pricing phases, payment paths, staffing and a rain plan.

At La Calle Cincuenta y Siete, Crossroads prepared its buffets. Early bird prices arrived first. Standard prices followed. The children’s packages demanded their own chair.

Inside The Grand Republic of Grill & Dramatic Lighting, District Times put steaks in negotiations with fire. Its event P&Ls waited nearby with considerably less flair.

At Laguna Azul’s Lagoon of Eternal Sunset, the  After and the glow pictured a live band beside the water.

The critical path asked about contracts, lighting, the floor plan and a wet-weather alternative.

The pool asked: “Am I still a pool?”
“Yes,” said Operations. “That’s why we need a safety plan.”

Then the Council’s oldest adversary appeared: I THOUGHT WE AGREED.

“I thought the price was confirmed.”
“I thought the client knew the terms.”
“I thought someone else owned the deadline.”

The ruling was brief:

Confirm the price. Clarify the terms. Get the approval. Name the owner.

The timelines, previously admired for their colors, began behaving like actual timelines.

The budget faced its questions. The festive programme moved closer to execution. Beautiful ideas acquired the details needed to deliver them.

THE WEEK’S FINAL RECIPE

One part imagination. Two parts commercial discipline. Add four critical paths and a Council that reads the small print.

Serve when the price, promise and owner agree.

And may the forecast be with you.
