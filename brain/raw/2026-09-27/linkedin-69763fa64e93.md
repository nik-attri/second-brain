🚀 My Hackathon Experience – CITTA RISE 🤖
I’m happy to share that I participated with my team in the CITTA RISE – 10-Hour AI Agent Building Hackathon at KIET, Kakinada.
For the hackathon, our team worked on an idea called “Intelligent Meeting Coordinator & Summarizer.” 🧠🤖
Our idea was to build an AI agent that can help manage the complete meeting process — from coordinating and scheduling meetings to understanding the meeting discussion, generating a summary, identifying important points, and creating action items for follow-up.
During the hackathon, I got hands-on experience with:
🔹 Understanding AI Agents and Agentic AI
🔹 Breaking a real-world problem into smaller tasks
🔹 Building and experimenting with an AI-based solution
🔹 Working with my team under a limited time
🔹 Learning from mistakes and improving our idea
🔹 Understanding how AI can automate everyday workflows
The 10-hour challenge was definitely intense, but it was a great learning experience. I got to understand that building an AI agent is not just about using an AI model — it is about designing a workflow where the agent can understand, decide, and take actions.
Thank you to Citta AI and KIET for providing this opportunity to learn, build, and collaborate. 🙌
Looking forward to building more AI and agentic systems! 🚀
