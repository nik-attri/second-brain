THE PEOPLE CLOSEST TO THE FOOD ARE NOW THE BEST PLACED TO BUILD WHAT IT NEEDS 🧑‍🍳🤓👩‍💻

When we change 1 ingredient in 1 salad, it has to be right in 18 places: the tills, 4+ delivery apps, our catering platforms, the website, the app, menu boards, screens and line sheets, the allergen matrix, the prep guide, and the head of every person on the line.

For what's coming up to 7 years of developing products for TSP, keeping all of that in sync came down to TERRIBLE spreadsheets and a lot of late nights before every menu launch. In food, a missed update isn't just a typo, because it can mean an allergen someone wasn't told about - or a nutritional mistake that goes against my philosophy of transparency.

This time last year I started playing around with Lovable thanks to my AI wizard fiancé Gordon Tveito-Duncan. I had the idea to build something to fix my and my team's biggest pain point - a HORRIFIC spreadsheet called SP ABSOLUTE COPY. 

I started building, and within a few days I had the framework for what would go on to change the next 6 months of my team's ways of working, and my ability to launch a menu into a French-speaking team on a new continent.

That became ABSOLUTELY SALAD PROJECT - 1 live system behind everything we serve. Allergens, dietaries and nutrition are calculated from verified supplier specs, and every change goes through the right owner (food safety, brand, marketing, finance) before it's signed off. From there it reaches our kitchens, our platforms and our customers at the same time. HELL YES.

We're launching our new menu with it right now, and I can honestly say we've solved the biggest problem in launching a product: for my team, for our customers, and for our store teams across 16 sites, two countries and two languages. Newly developed dishes move from the test kitchen to live on one pipeline, our site teams have live recipes, line maps and garnish guides instead of printouts, and customers can customise their bowl and see exactly how it stacks up on allergens and nutrition as they go, in English or in French. I can sign off everything to ensure accuracy and brand standards across every platform and my team no longer want to kill me when a supplier spec changes. [I don't think.....?]

I built the first version from my own frustration, but it's my team who got it over the line and launched it across our sites and to our customers, and I'm so grateful to them: Affan Shaikh Patrick Baggaley Sophia (Saphy) Buchanan

2 or 3 years ago I couldn't have dreamt of building something like this, and 7 years ago, when I wrote our first menu, it would have sounded utterly mad. 

The expectation has always been that people in food make the food and the tech is someone else's problem, but I don't think that holds any more. The people closest to the food are now the best placed to build what it needs, and I'd bloody love to see more of us doing it.
