🔭 𝗔 𝘀𝘆𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗺 𝗰𝗮𝗻 𝗯𝗲 𝟭𝟬𝟬% 𝘂𝗽 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗯𝗲 𝗶𝗺𝗽𝗼𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗯𝗹𝗲 𝘁𝗼 𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝗱.
That's the difference between monitoring and observability. 
Many organizations have:
✅ Infrastructure monitoring
✅ Service dashboards
✅ Availability alerts
✅ Health checks
And that's necessary. 
But during a major incident, a harder question appears:
"Why did the system produce this outcome?"
That's where many architectures struggle. 
Each team can see its own service.
Each dashboard looks healthy.
Yet nobody can explain what happened across the entire transaction path.
The problem isn't visibility inside components.
The problem is visibility between them. 
When reviewing architecture designs, I ask:
1️⃣ Can we trace a business transaction end-to-end?
2️⃣ Are requests correlated across service boundaries?
3️⃣ Can we explain a decision after it happens?
4️⃣ Do logs tell a story or just record events?
5️⃣ Who owns cross-service visibility?
6️⃣ Could we diagnose a failure without rebuilding the timeline manually? 
Every service was monitored.
The architecture wasn't observable.
