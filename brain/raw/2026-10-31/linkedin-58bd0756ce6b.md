“I spent as much time living in these tools chasing notifications as I am actually getting work done.”

If that sounds like your typical Tuesday, you’re not alone. Most founders don't lose momentum to actual client work - they lose it to “copy-paste hell” and playing administrative firefighter for tools that refuse to talk to each other.

By 3 PM, context switching between scattered apps leaves you completely exhausted before the real work even gets started.

People always ask us how we manage building a studio and scaling multiple products like Plexo and Spark without losing our minds.

Truth is, we don't start with a flashy feature list.

Every custom tool or workflow we build starts because our team at Charisol hit that exact same wall in our own day-to-day operations and thought, “there has to be an easier way to do this.” We were tired of watching the "spreadsheet owner" become the ultimate bottleneck.

When your background workflows are running quietly behind the scenes, you actually get to focus on your clients and moving your business forward - not just keeping the machine running.

What’s one clunky admin task in your business you wish you could automate before the week runs out?
