The no.1 thing you learn from talking to founders is that most GTM and marketing teams are a huge mess.

We're travelling around Europe at the moment, currently in Berlin, working with companies in person and setting Revnu (P26) up on site 🇪🇺

They're paying for 20+ overpriced subscriptions, locked into yearly contracts for product they do not use, and a lot of them have tried to build their own tools as well.

Others don't realise how good things are now. We spoke to recruiters & brokers who are still paying for lead lists, getting humans to send every message and tracking everything in spreadsheets 

Large orgs can't just sign up to a new ai product, they're stuck in onboarding hell.

So we take the burden, turn up fully forward deployed, fix the integrations, onboard the team onto revnu and leave with their marketing automated.
