He loved me first… ❤️

...and the legacy he left behind is tremendous and most precious to me: integrity, strong values and life principles, character, leadership, perseverance, compassion, empathy, love, forgiveness — and that typical Romanian humour (those who really know me, know what I’m talking about😁).

But mostly, he left me the great talent to feel the music, his poetry specially written for me or my daughter, the dancing, and the ability to move on and enjoy life, no matter how hard it got.

There is no secret that I am sucker for love and romance, on top of my very technical and analytical brain layers. I love writing love stories. 🥰😍 And today, after watching this video on repeat for more than an hour and almost one year since he passed away, I decided to honour his memory by adding something new to my BridgePoint Management services:

**Wedding planning. 💍**

After 20+ years of living and working in four different countries, learning five languages and currently studying two more, managing global and complex projects, people, timelines, unexpected problems and a trillion moving parts, I figured I could probably manage a wedding or two. 😄

One thing is for sure: I successfully organised my own at 25 — with no fancy pants budget (we all know that business constraint, don’t we? 😁), fast (we know that one too 😅), and no Smartsheet, Jira, Confluence or ServiceNow…

Just regular Tuesdays, a spreadsheet and a very strong determination to get married.

N.B.: at THAT time! 😂

I love the idea of helping people create one of those moments they’ll remember for the rest of their lives.

So… why not? Let’s give it a go and marry the hell out of you. I say I DO! 💍

Do you? 😊

Cheers to my Dad in heaven… I hope you’re still dancing somewhere out there, you old devil! I know I am. 😇❤️
