AI can do more in recruitment than ever before. But it still needs the right foundations.

I recently had the opportunity to speak at The Rise of Digital Workers: What It Means for Recruiters, where we explored what AI and digital workers actually mean for recruitment businesses in practice.

A big part of the conversation was around Bullhorn Amplify and how digital workers can take on some of those repetitive, everyday tasks that eat into recruiters' time.
But there's another side to this.

If Bullhorn is going to become your system of action, it needs to remain a reliable system of record.

And recruiters don't spend all day working inside Bullhorn.

They're on LinkedIn, sourcing candidates, researching the market and finding those "hidden gems" that technology won't necessarily uncover for them.

That's where Coral Starfish fits into the picture.

We help recruiters keep Bullhorn connected while they're working within LinkedIn – whether they're using Amplify, working alongside other AI tools, or simply carrying out tasks manually.

One of my biggest takeaways from the RecConnect event?

AI doesn't remove the need for good recruitment processes. It makes them even more important.

AI can automate. Integrations can remove friction. But recruiters still bring the judgement, relationships and ability to spot the people that technology might miss.
