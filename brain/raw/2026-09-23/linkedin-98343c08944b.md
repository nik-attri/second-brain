Anupriya Bhatt has been elevated to Associate Vice President – Human Resources at HDFC Life, bringing 20+ years of experience across HR Business Partnering, talent acquisition, talent management, capability building, employee engagement, retention, performance management, workforce planning, and leadership development. Her career with HDFC Life spans roles including Associate Vice President, Chief Manager Human Resources, and Regional Executive HR, complemented by leadership experience at Exide Life Insurance and Indiabulls.
Earlier, Anupriya held recruitment and HR leadership roles at IBM, IBM India Limited, HMV Saregama India Ltd, Saregama India Limited, and Descon, building expertise across strategic HR, senior leadership hiring, business partnering, talent development, employee engagement, and HR operations. Her focus on building future-ready talent, developing leaders, creating inclusive workplaces, and enabling business growth through people continues to shape her leadership journey at HDFC Life.
