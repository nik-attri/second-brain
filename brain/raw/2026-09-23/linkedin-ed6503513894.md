Identifying a problem in an organisation and solving it is not just about finding a quick fix.

There is a process to it, and knowing how to navigate that process matters.

There are problems, and then there are root causes.

Imagine manually calculating a frequency using several numbers and arriving at the wrong answer.

The wrong answer is the problem.

But why is the answer wrong?

That is the question that leads us to the root cause.

So, we investigate. We go through the calculation and eventually discover: Ah! I entered the wrong figure at some point.

Now we know what happened. The calculation needs to be corrected, and the answer recalculated.

The lesson?

Solving a problem without understanding its root cause may only fix the symptom.

In business, the real value is not just in asking, “How do we fix this?”

It is also in asking: “Why did this happen in the first place?”
