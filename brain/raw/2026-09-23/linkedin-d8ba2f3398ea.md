The Meta Ads update we have all been talking about is finally here, and it is a massive shift for media buyers. 🚨

If you run campaigns, you probably know that Meta is moving towards fully automated ad delivery, making Advantage+ Placements the default.

The biggest change? We are losing the ability to manually exclude specific placements, platforms, devices, and operating systems directly at the ad-set level.

Honestly, as a Performance Marketer, losing that manual control felt a bit scary at first. Nobody wants their lead-generation budget wasted on accidental clicks in the Audience Network.

But fighting the algorithm never works. We just have to adapt and focus on strategy. Here is how I am adjusting my campaigns to keep the lead quality high:

1. Moving to Account-Level Controls
Since we can't uncheck boxes at the ad-set level anymore, I am setting up hard exclusions at the Account level to block low-quality publisher networks before the ads even launch.

2. Using Value Rules
We might not be able to completely turn off a placement, but we can tell Meta it is worthless to us. I am setting negative bid adjustments to drop bids aggressively on placements that usually bring junk leads.

3. Letting Creative Do the Filtering
The best way to control where your ad shows up is through your creative dimensions. If I don't want my graphic awkwardly squeezed into an Instagram Reel, I simply don't upload a 9:16 asset in that specific campaign.

Meta wants to find the best places for our ads automatically. Our job now is to feed the system better data and cleaner creatives, rather than micromanaging the checkboxes.

Fellow marketers, how are you handling this Advantage+ update? Are you changing your campaign structures yet? 👇
