𝗩𝗼𝘆𝗮𝗱𝗼 𝗵𝗲𝗹𝗽𝘀 𝗿𝗲𝘁𝗮𝗶𝗹𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗻 𝗱𝗮𝘁𝗮 𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗼 𝗯𝗲𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿 𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀.
We helped its Finance team do the same.

We are proud to share the successful completion of Phase 1 of our Planful implementation with Voyado.

Voyado supports more than 500 retail brands across Europe through its agentic customer experience suite. As the business grows internationally, Finance needs clear and timely information to understand performance, manage costs and plan ahead.

Working closely with the Voyado team, Pryzm Advisory delivered:

✔ 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗮𝗻𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗥𝗲𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴
Budget vs Actual reporting enables Finance to identify variances quickly and focus attention where it matters. A common financial structure also provides a clear group-level view across entities without manually combining separate data sets.

✔ 𝗥𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗻𝘂𝗲 𝗣𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴
Voyado can plan revenue using consistent assumptions and compare its latest outlook directly with actual performance. As expectations change, Finance can update the forecast and assess the impact on profitability, expenditure and investment.

✔ 𝗘𝘅𝗽𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗲 𝗣𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴
Cost centre-based templates provide budget owners with a clear and consistent way to plan expenditure. This improves cost visibility, strengthens ownership and shows how spending decisions affect the wider forecast.

✔ 𝗪𝗼𝗿𝗸𝗳𝗼𝗿𝗰𝗲 𝗣𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴
For a people-led technology business, workforce forecasting involves much more than modelling headcount. Voyado’s model calculates employee-level labour costs, including complex pay structures, regional tax caps and benefit tiers.

It also assesses the impact of extended PTO and parental leave, giving Finance and HR a detailed view of employee costs, capacity and future recruitment needs across teams and regions.

Together, these capabilities show how revenue, expenditure and workforce decisions affect the overall financial plan.

𝗣𝗵𝗮𝘀𝗲 𝟮 𝗶𝘀 𝗮𝗹𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗱𝘆 𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘄𝗮𝘆.

Pryzm Advisory is now integrating Kleer and Hailey HR directly with Planful.

The Kleer integration will automate the transfer of Actuals, reducing manual extracts, uploads and reconciliation.

The Hailey HR integration will bring employee data into Workforce Planning, keeping headcount, compensation and workforce assumptions aligned with Voyado’s HR records.

Together, these integrations will provide a more complete and up-to-date view of financial performance and workforce costs.

A huge thank you to Tola Lakavivat, Robin Nordblad, Benjamin Ekelund, Ellen Rodman and the wider Voyado team for their trust, expertise and collaboration. We are proud of what both teams have achieved and look forward to continuing our work together in Phase 2.
