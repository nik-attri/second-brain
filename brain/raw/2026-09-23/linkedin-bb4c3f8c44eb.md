After YEARS of building and testing this, I finally get to teach it publicly for the first time.

And if you've been watching me build it while thinking, "Emilia, what the hell IS it?" 😂 Fair question.

Here's the shortest version:

I spent years in big data and software watching companies use patterns to understand what works and make smarter decisions.

Then I stole the useful thinking, left corporate, and built a way for the rest of us to use it on our OWN lives. 😈💚

No math. No spreadsheets. No giant reserve of willpower.
