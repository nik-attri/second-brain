Why is our website down? Why can't we logon?

TL;DR: Certificate lifetimes have already shrunk from 398 days to 200 days, they will shrink again to 100 days and eventually 47 days within 2 years, organisations need to stop relying on manual renewals and start automating them.

At some point in my career I stopped getting excited about new gadgets and started getting excited about certificate renewal automation. Apparently that's where I'm at now. Not buying the latest iPhone that looks gorgeous but costs twice what my laptop does.

The other thing I realised in IT is that some of the biggest outages don't come from cyber- attacks, hardware failures, or complex software bugs.

Sometimes it's just a certificate that wasn't renewed. Certificates are something no one really wants to know about, so it’s good that at It’s Us we do.

For years you could treat SSL/TLS certificates as an annual housekeeping task. That world is disappearing quickly.

A certificate that could previously be issued for 398 days is now limited to 200 days. The roadmap takes that down again to 100 days in March 2027 and eventually just 47 days in March 2029.

The thing is this isn't about certificates. It's about business resilience.

The question for leaders isn't:

"When does this certificate expire?"

It's:

"What happens to our customers and staff if it does?"

The lesson isn't that IT teams need more calendar reminders.

The lesson is that manual certificate management is becoming unsustainable.

At It's Us we've seen first-hand how something as simple as an expired certificate can take 
down business-critical services. The answer isn't more spreadsheets or more reminders.

It's automation, visibility, monitoring, and removing single points of failure.

·       Because Bob will answer the phone at 5am when everything breaks.
·       He'll probably charge overtime too.
·       And a callout fee.
·       Far better to automate the pain away.

The organisations that adapt now will barely notice these changes. The ones that don't may discover their next outage isn't caused by technology failing at all.

It's caused by technology doing exactly what it was designed to do.

Next week: domain validation timelines. Another change likely to catch a few
organisations by surprise.
