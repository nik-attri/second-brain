Twenty years ago, Intelligent Waves (IW) began with an idea, a spreadsheet, and a young entrepreneur sitting in Baghdad with no idea where the journey would lead.

Today, I’m proud to share that IW has been acquired by B&A.
I started IW to solve hard technical problems for the people serving our country, risking everything for our country. I then used what it became to create, create opportunities for others, create new technologies, to build something with impact. What began with two people grew into an extraordinary team supporting some of our nation’s most critical missions. Along the way, we took risks, made mistakes, learned a hell of a lot, and built something far bigger than I ever imagined.

IW gave us the foundation to do more... We launched Warriors Ethos to help veterans, transitioning service members, and military families find their next chapters. We built products and solutions, one that led to spinning out Hypori, carrying our commitment to innovation and national security into a new company.

None of that happened because of me, or any one person.

To every IW teammate, past and present: thank you. YOU built this company through your commitment to our customers, the mission, and each other. This milestone belongs to you.

To Tony Crescenzo and the IW leadership team: thank you for your partnership and for taking what we built to new heights. One of the most important lessons of entrepreneurship is to surround yourself with exceptional people, trust them, and give them room to lead.

To Jonathan Evans and everyone at B&A: thank you for recognizing what makes IW special. I’m excited for what this combination means for our people, our customers, and the missions we serve.

And to my wife, my family, mentors, partners, and friends who believed in me long before there was much reason to: I’m forever grateful.

I’ve said before that I don’t measure success by money. Money is a tool; impact is the measure. Looking back, I see warfighters supported, careers created, veterans helped, and new ideas given a chance to grow.

Twenty years ago, General Ray Odierno put his hand on my shoulder and told me that the opportunity to start this company, having come where I had come from, was the American dream. I wish he were still here so I could tell him how the story turned out.

I hope we made him proud. I know I’m proud of every person who helped make it happen.

To the IW family: thank you for twenty extraordinary years. 

To our new B&A family: here’s to the next chapter. 

I cant tell each of you who helped shape the company, its success, and me personally thank you... But I must say thank you to Marqus Hutchinson, John Hammes, Amy Wood, Craig Janus, James W. Howell, John Carmin, Matt Stern and so so many more. You did this... well done, and thank you.
