Things I’ve learned after one month of full time podcasting as the Executive Producer, Host, Researcher, Guest Booker, Creative Director… and apparently everything else…..

1. Recording the podcast is actually the easy part.

2. “I’ll just record one episode a week” was adorable. 

3. Every guest needs a little project management.

4. Researching an episode takes much longer than saying, “I’ll just prepare a few questions.”

5. Being the host means remembering the questions while simultaneously trying to look natural on camera. 

6. Your storage will become your enemy.

7. A 29GB video file can humble you very quickly.

8. Finding a reliable editor is a full-time side quest.

9. There is always one more file to upload, send, download, rename, transfer or somehow find.

10. Being the Executive Producer apparently means becoming the unofficial IT department, production coordinator, content planner, editor manager and quality-control person too. 

11. I thought I was making a podcast. Somewhere along the way, I realised I was basically running a small production operation.

12. And yes… my carefully planned release calendar is now looking increasingly questionable. 🤨 

But here’s the biggest thing I’ve learned:

There was no amount of preparation that would have made this easy.

I could have planned for months, made spreadsheets, mapped out every detail and tried to anticipate every problem.

At some point, the only way to learn was to start.

Start recording.
Figure it out.
Make mistakes.
Fix them.
Build the systems as you go.

You can love the thing you’re building and still occasionally look at it and think…

“What the hell have I done?” 🤷🏾‍♀️

But honestly?

I’m learning. I’m figuring out the systems. I’m getting better.

And Tales of a Modern Woman is slowly becoming what I imagined… with a LOT of patience, of course.

There are nights when I genuinely say, “I’m done. I’m not doing this anymore.”

Then I wake up the next morning and it’s like, “No way. You are not stopping now.”🙄

Now… excuse me while I go cry over the 17 HOURS still remaining on this upload.🥲🤦🏾‍♀️
