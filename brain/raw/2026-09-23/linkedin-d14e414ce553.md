🎓 The Problem: Admission Teams Are Drowning in Manual Verification
Every admission season, universities receive thousands of student applications. Behind every application is a checklist that needs to be verified:
✔️ Does the student have the required marks?
✔️ Is the student from the required academic stream?
✔️ Is the previous board/institution recognised?
✔️ Does the student meet the eligibility criteria for the selected course?
When this verification is done manually, hours of staff time go into checking documents, comparing data, and identifying eligible students. With a large number of applications, even a small error or missed detail can slow down the admission process.
What if universities could validate all of this in minutes?
🚀 The Solution: docXplor by Vixplor Analytics
With docXplor, universities can upload student application data in a ZIP file and let AI handle the verification workflow.
Upload → Extract → Validate → Identify Eligibility
docXplor: 📂 Extracts required information from student documents
🔍 Checks the data against university-provided master data and admission norms
✅ Validates marks, streams, boards and other eligibility criteria
🎓 Identifies students eligible for the applied course
Less manual verification. Faster decisions. A smoother admission process.
Because admission teams should spend their time managing admissions — not manually checking thousands of documents.
