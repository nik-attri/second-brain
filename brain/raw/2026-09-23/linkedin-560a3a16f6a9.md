You didn’t pour your savings and sanity into a business just to become an unpaid data-entry clerk at 11:30 PM.

Whether you run a pizzeria, an auto repair shop, or a boutique hotel, you know the drill:
- Copying numbers from your POS into a spreadsheet.
- Double-checking supplier invoices against bank payouts across 5 open browser tabs.
- Manually pinging staff when a booking shifts.

Tech advice always screams the same thing:
"Just download this new app! Add another AI widget!"

The result?
You now pay for 6 SaaS subscriptions that don't talk to each other.
You became the "Human API" — the fragile, exhausted glue holding your software stack together.

The Alternative: Invisible Digital Plumbing

When you step into a boutique hotel or a great restaurant, you don't admire the pipes inside the walls. You just turn the faucet and clean water flows.

Your business operations should work the exact same way.

That’s what PRaaS (Process-as-a-Service) actually is:
Invisible digital plumbing running quietly behind the walls.

Think of it like GitHub for real-world operations:
Instead of hiring expensive software teams or trying to build brittle automations from scratch, you choose a battle-tested bundle:

* Restaurant Order & Invoice Reconciler
* Hotel Guest Booking & Payout Sync
* Auto Repair Parts Procurement & Job Billing

"Fork and Run" in 1 Click:
1. Fork the verified bundle into your private workspace.
2. Connect your accounts (POS, bank, accounting, WhatsApp/SMS).
3. Forget about it.

It runs silently like electricity in the background.

The only time it pings your phone is when an actual human decision is needed:
"Tomato invoice is .20 higher than agreed pricing. Approve or dispute?"

One tap. Done. You go back to running your business, greeting guests, or sleeping through the night.

Stop being the human glue between disconnected apps. Which routine workflow in your business is burning your evenings right now?
