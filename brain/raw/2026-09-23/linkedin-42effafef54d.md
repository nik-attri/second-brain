AI isn't coming. AI is here. 🚀

Last week, our team — Josip Kustić, Ana Tekic, Mirta Bujanović, Ivan Barać and Kristina Šeparović — headed to Zadar for Infobip Shift, one of the region's biggest tech conferences. 

It was Josip's first Shift, so we asked him what stuck with him. Here are his key takeaways:

🤖 Is AI still "coming"?

 AI was, unsurprisingly, the main topic, and for Josip the answer is clear: it's already here. It's no longer just hype. We're already using it, and the real question is how to automate, improve efficiency and free ourselves from repetitive work. The idea isn't new. We've always looked for ways to automate the "boring" parts of work. What has changed is the toolbox, now complemented by Generative AI.

 His advice: "If you are not already using AI for this purpose, start!"

🧩 Are AI agents really everywhere?

 It certainly felt that way at Shift. People are already using agents for both professional and personal tasks. Josip admits some use cases were a bit too much for him: "At some point, you may spend so much time organising your agents that your 'free time' is no longer free." 🙂 Still, it's an area we need to keep exploring.

🔌 Could MCP and APIs change how we build applications?

 Josip believes they could. MCP combined with APIs can help modernise existing applications and should be considered when designing new architectures.

🛡️ Is AI governance keeping pace?

 This topic got less spotlight than expected, or, as Josip puts it, perhaps he missed the right sessions. Either way, governance remains critical as organisations adopt and scale AI, and it's a conversation we want to see more of.

🏢 From AI hype to real enterprise use cases

 A highlight for Josip were the panels on how large software companies and enterprises are actually using AI. AI is moving beyond experiments and individual productivity into complex, large-scale enterprise environments, and we'd love to hear even more about real use cases, challenges and results.

💡 What does this mean for us at Digacon?

 Shift was another confirmation of something we already strongly believe: AI isn't coming. AI is here.

 Our focus on AI, both internally and in customer solutions, is becoming increasingly important. And it's not only about AI itself. Through our UiPath partnership, we work with business process orchestration and (test) automation, combining automation, orchestration and increasingly AI-powered capabilities.

 The real potential lies in bringing AI, automation, APIs, applications and business processes together into end-to-end intelligent workflows that turn AI into business outcomes.

Great weather, great people, interesting discussions and plenty of food for thought. Thank you, Infobip, and see you at the next Shift! 👋
