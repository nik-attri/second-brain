Every time a student fills out an online college admission form these days, there is a good chance an eight-year-old Gurugram startup is quietly running the backend, and it is now heading toward the stock market.

NoPaperForms, the company behind the enrolment automation platform Meritto and the payments product Collexo, has filed an updated draft prospectus with SEBI for an IPO worth up to 375 crore rupees through a fresh issue, alongside an offer for sale of nearly 3.84 crore shares by its long-time backer Info Edge. Founded in 2017 by Naveen Goyal and Suraj Sapra, the startup began by solving a painfully manual problem: institutions drowning in paper forms during admission season. Today it serves over 1,180 customers, including names like Manipal University and PhysicsWallah, and has expanded into the UAE and Malaysia.

The numbers behind the filing tell their own story. Operating revenue grew 25 percent to 115.6 crore rupees in FY26, but profit after tax jumped a striking 534 percent to 11.9 crore rupees in the same period. Info Edge, which first backed the company with a modest seed cheque in 2017, still holds close to 48 percent of the business on a fully diluted basis, making this IPO as much a payday for one of India's earliest internet investors as it is a coming-out moment for an edtech-adjacent SaaS company.

The fresh capital will largely go toward technology, cloud infrastructure and customer acquisition, positioning Meritto for its next phase as a public company.
