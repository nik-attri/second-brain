The 2-Hour Question

If AI could solve one challenge in your business today, what would it be?
For most small business owners I talk to, the answer isn't "I need a chatbot" or "I want to sound smarter in emails." It's much simpler:
"I want my time back."

Here's what that usually looks like in practice:
→ Manually copying data between spreadsheets and systems
→ Re-typing the same client follow-up emails every week
→ Chasing invoices, bookings, or form submissions by hand
→ Updating the same report in three different places

None of these tasks require creativity or judgment. They just require consistency — which is exactly what automation is good at.
With simple, no-code tools like Make or Zapier, tasks like these can run quietly in the background: a form submission automatically creates a task, a new client automatically gets a welcome email, a completed order automatically updates your records.

No coding. No overhaul of how you work. Just fewer things falling through the cracks.
The businesses that benefit most aren't necessarily the biggest — they're the ones willing to ask: what repetitive task takes up too much of my time?
That one question is usually where the real efficiency gains start.

What would your team do with two extra hours a day?
