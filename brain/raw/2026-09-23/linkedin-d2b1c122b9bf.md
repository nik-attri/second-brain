Recruitment is a long game. This one took six years.

📅 2020 - I met with Stephen right as COVID was kicking off. It was a tricky time for everyone, we spent a lot of time going through his career history and what he wanted to do next.
📅 2021-2024 - We stayed in touch with calls every so often. Often he was simply someone I'd call for advice on a role I was working on, or to ask who he knew for a role I was working on. Every single time, he picked up the phone and helped - with nothing asked in return.
📅 2025 - I called him for a BD call. He'd just stepped into a Site Manager role and was hiring for a very niche skillset, so this time he was my client.
📅 2026 - I was brief on a new Operations Manager search, and Stephen was one of the first names that came to mind. The timing was perfect - he just became active on the market and open to a move.

Yesterday, he signed with the client and accepted the role.

Recruitment isn't always the easiest game. If you're just starting out and finding it tricky, just know that your value is your network and your time in market. It's the relationships you build over years, where both sides show up for each other long before there's anything in it. When the timing's finally right, it will play out in your favour.

Congratulations on the new role Stephen!
