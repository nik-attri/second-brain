“PPC is easy these days, isn’t it?”

Smart Bidding does the bidding.

Performance Max does the targeting.

Automation does most of the heavy lifting.

So… what are PPC specialists actually doing?

It’s a question Sean Healy, Senior PPC Strategist at Journey Further, hears surprisingly often.

And his answer is simple: PPC hasn’t become easier. The work has changed.

Yes, PPC specialists are spending less time making manual bid adjustments than they did in the past.

But that time hasn’t disappeared.

Today, PPC teams are managing more campaign formats, more features and increasingly complex accounts.

Conversion tracking has become far more advanced — and far more critical. When something breaks, fixing it quickly can be the difference between maintaining performance and watching an account head in the wrong direction.

Then there’s the constant stream of new Google Ads features.

Every new feature creates another question:

Should we use it?

Will it actually improve performance?

How does it interact with everything else in the account?

And how do we know?

We test.

That’s the part of modern PPC that often gets overlooked.

Automation hasn’t removed the need for PPC specialists.

It has shifted the job away from manually pulling every lever and towards deciding which levers are worth pulling in the first place.

As Sean explained during his PPC Live talk, modern PPC is increasingly about testing, measurement and making sense of an advertising platform that has more features and automation than ever before.

So no, PPC isn’t just “set Smart Bidding, launch Performance Max and let Google handle it.”

The manual work may have changed.

The thinking certainly hasn’t. Well for those who are good at their jobs! 😉😁
