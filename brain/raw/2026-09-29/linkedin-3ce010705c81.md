I had another realization about AI today. I was given a task to clean up a Google Docs template... and it turned out to be 180+ documents.

Of course, my first thought was: “AI can help me finish this faster.” And yes, it helped.
But eventually, we ran into issues. Formatting changed, some things had to be corrected again, and at one point, doing it manually was actually easier.

Then I remembered something our Pastor often teaches us:
Before you replace something with a system, you first need to understand how to do it manually.

And now I understand why.

AI can make our work faster, but we still need to know the work behind it. Because when the tool fails, you need to know what went wrong. You need to know what “correct” looks like. And you need to be able to continue even without the tool.

So now, while I’m manually fixing these documents, I don’t see it as going backwards. I’m actually learning the process better. And maybe that’s the lesson for me: Learn the process first. Then automate what you truly understand.

AI should help us become better at what we do... not make us helpless without it.
