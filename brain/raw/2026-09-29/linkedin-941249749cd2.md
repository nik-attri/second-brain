We raised a Series A 🔥

When Wayfarer was started in 2019, the idea was to build a hotel company that could drive operational uplift by being efficient.  Many hotels later, we've learned an unreasonable amount about all the things that make hotels super complex to operate.  

We started building Materia years ago to solve this.  Long before the current AI wave, we painstakingly architected infrastructure around how hotel teams actually work - connecting workflows, systems and data so teams can operate without drowning in everything day to day manual processes.  That architecture is also pretty well suited for what's coming next.

We imagine a world where hotel teams can work with AI agents across this infrastructure - whether they're ours, yours, or one of the many great options being built out today.  Today, I'm very happy to welcome Canon Marketing Japan and Global Brain as partners as we take the next step in building it.

Operating hotels is hard.  Building technology for physical spaces is hard.  Naturally, we decided to do both 😁
