I can't believe I made my own app obsolete inside Grok Bot. That's just the pace of AI right now, and honestly, I'm embracing it.

A few weeks ago Grok Bot launched a marketplace. Think of it as an app store for AI assistants: specialist bots with their own preset skills, tools and workflows that live inside your Grok Bot chat. Anyone can build a custom bot.

So I rebuilt Nomad Pro as one. And it does things my app never could.

Quick context. Nomad Pro started because I hate admin. If you've left the UK, the rules on how much time you can spend back home run to 143 pages of HMRC guidance. Staying on top of it means drowning in a sea of spreadsheets, and general admin hell.

The Grok Bot version handles all of that like a personal PA. It connects to your Google Calendar, Gmail, Sheets and Drive, checks in with you each evening (or week, or quarter - it's yours to customise!), and keeps every day documented in one place.

Then you just talk to it:
• "Show me my year." It builds you a travel analytics dashboard.
• "Can I spend 2 months in Lisbon from next month?" It works out your Schengen days so far and gives you an answer.
• "Suggest somewhere to stay in October." It researches places based on your preferences and budget.

And you don't even have to ask. It watches your upcoming travel and tells you what you should know, like when visa rules or HMRC guidance change.

It's record-keeping, not tax advice, but you're armed with knowledge based on your real travel history and plans.

The app's still there if you want it, making admin fun, one day at a time. But this is the AI-native Nomad Pro I always dreamed of, and it's free for Grok Bot users.
