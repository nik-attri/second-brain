Job Title: Senior Data Analyst – IT Infrastructure, Security & Compliance
Location: North Holland & Randstad accessible
Contract Type: Full-time / Interim / Payroll (No ZZP)
Language Requirement: Fluent English & Dutch mandatory

About the Role
Are you an experienced Data Analyst with a background in IT Infrastructure, Cyber Security, or Risk Management within the Dutch banking sector?
We are seeking a proactive, go-getter Data Analyst to join a high-impact, multidisciplinary Agile/DevOps team at a leading Dutch Financial Institution. In this role, you will bridge the gap between complex infrastructure datasets and strategic decision-making—translating infrastructure, vulnerability, and compliance metrics into actionable insights that safeguard operational resilience.
Key Responsibilities
Data & Risk Analytics: Extract, model, and analyze large volumes of operational, security, and infrastructure data using SQL, Power BI, and Databricks.
Lifecycle & Vulnerability Management: Monitor Lifecycle Management (LCM) risks (hardware/software end-of-life) and analyze vulnerability management scan trends to prioritize remediation.
Compliance & Governance: Build dashboards and KPI frameworks supporting internal audit, risk officers, and regulatory compliance requirements.
Process Automation: Partner with DevOps teams to streamline reporting workflows using Microsoft Power Platform (Power Apps / Power Automate).

What You Bring
Degree: Bachelor’s or Master’s in Data Analytics, Computer Science, or Information Management.
Experience: 3+ years as a Data Analyst within IT Infrastructure, Cyber Security, or Regulated Banking Environments in the Netherlands.
Technical Stack: Advanced proficiency in SQL, Power BI / Tableau, and experience with Agile / DevOps / Scrumban.
Domain Knowledge: Hands-on understanding of Vulnerability Management frameworks.
Languages: Bilingual fluency in Dutch and English is required.
Employment Eligibility: Candidates must be eligible for direct employment/payroll (ZZP is not allowed for this assignment).
What is Offered
Opportunity to make a direct impact on the cyber resilience of key Dutch financial infrastructure.
Dynamic, modern Agile/DevOps environment with hybrid working arrangements.
Attractive compensation package with professional growth and training programs.
