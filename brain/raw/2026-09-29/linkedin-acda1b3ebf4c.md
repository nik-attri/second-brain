I have a question.

How many “temporary” tech workarounds in your business are now approximately three years old? 😂

You know the ones.

👉 “This form doesn’t connect properly, so I’ll manually move the info.”

👉 “This checkout doesn’t tag people correctly, so I’ll fix it once a week.”

👉 “This automation broke, so I’ll add this spreadsheet JUST IN CASE.”


And individually, none of these seem that bad.

Ten minutes here, one extra click there, a manual check every Friday, but eventually the workaround gets another workaround.

And THAT workaround gets a backup process and then nobody is entirely sure what the original system was supposed to do anymore. 😂

Now changing ANYTHING feels terrifying because:

What if I remove this random step and something three platforms away stops working?!

This is one of my favourite things to untangle.

Because very often the answer is not:

“Add another automation.”

It’s:

Why the hell are we doing this step in the first place?

Inside my Tool Overwhelm Rescue Session, we trace the actual flow of your business and figure out:

Where things are unnecessarily complicated.

Where your tools aren’t talking.

Where manual steps can disappear.

And where you can remove entire chunks of the process instead of automating a process that shouldn’t exist.

Sometimes the smartest automation is deleting six steps. 😉
