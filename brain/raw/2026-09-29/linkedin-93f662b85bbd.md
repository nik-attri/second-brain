Starting your own industrial automation company? 

Are you a controls technician, controls engineer, or industrial electrician? 

Have you thought about "hanging your own shingle"? 

On top of all your systems integtration knowledge now you will need to learn or do the following:

You need a website domain with email addresses attached to it. Using your Gmail is not reccomended here. I highly reccomend 2FA as my company paid a hacker $30k once when he posed as my own project manager asking us to change his bank information...yeah we fell for it. 

You need an EIN from the IRS which you can get through a registered agent online. They will make your business filing legitimate. 

You will need to register for a business licence in the states where you will be doing business. 

You will need a lot if different kinds of insurance. Business Liabiltiy, general business, medical, cybersecurity, commercial auto insurance.

You will need a CRM or atleast spreadsheet to track your customer interactions, Purchase orders and invoice templates. You should have a sales funnel. 

You will need accounting software like Quickbooks which I personally HATE. 

You will need an accountant and a CPA to file your taxes. Remember that state taxes vary and especially excise taxes can kick your ass like they kicked mine. 

You SHOULD have a linkedin page for your business and consider making pages on other social media sites. 

You will need softwares on softwares like docusign, Autocad, Eplan, Studio5000, TIA Portal and Ignition, Canva, Claude, Meeting summary software etc. 

You will need company laptops and an IT person/company. I sure as hell am not my own IT. They can spin up virtual machines for you as well. 

If you want to learn from my science experiment called Process & Controls Energy follow me! This was my first try at my own business. 

Stay tuned for my new business...
