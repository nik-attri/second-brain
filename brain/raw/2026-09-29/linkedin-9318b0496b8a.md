• Working knowledge of Articulate, Captivate, Adobe Creative Cloud, video and audio tools, and AI-based authoring tools.
• Good understanding of LMS platforms and SCORM, AICC, or xAPI standards.
• Proficient in MS Office and Google Workspace.
• Strong client-facing, presentation, proofreading, and content evaluation skills.
