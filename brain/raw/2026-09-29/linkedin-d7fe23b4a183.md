This is what I want technology to give me more of. ☀️🌊

Not more time staring at another dashboard.

Not another AI tool to learn simply because everyone on LinkedIn is talking about it.

Time.

That’s what good systems, automation and now AI should ultimately create in a business.

The exciting thing about AI is just how much potential it has to help us work smarter and scale what we do, but I think we need to be careful that we don’t become so obsessed with USING AI that we forget why we wanted the efficiency in the first place.

I want a business that can generate leads, nurture relationships and move potential clients through a clear journey without everything relying on me manually making it happen.

Then AI can come in and make those already strong systems even more efficient.

For me, that’s where the real opportunity is.

Use the technology to support the business you want to build, not create another full-time job managing the technology. 😂

More views like this and fewer unnecessary hours behind the laptop sounds pretty good to me. ❤️
