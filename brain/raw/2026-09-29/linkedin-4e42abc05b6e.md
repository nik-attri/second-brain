If the FDA asked your team to produce an end-to-end traceability spreadsheet for an ingredient lot received 18 months ago, could you deliver it in 24 hours?
For food processors and cold-chain distributors, the FDA FSMA 204 mandate changes the baseline from "recommended traceability" to a legally enforceable standard with strict electronic reporting deadlines.
Most legacy ERPs and manual paper-based logs break down across three specific operational areas:
🔴 Critical Tracking Events (CTEs) & KDEs: Capturing receiving, transformation, and shipping data without drowning warehouse teams in manual data entry.
🔴 Dual-Unit Catch-Weight Precision: Meat, seafood, and fresh produce vary in weight from carton to carton. Costing and billing by certified scale poundage while managing inventory by case count requires real-time dual-unit synchronization.
🔴 Automated FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out): Manual warehouse picking inevitably leads to stock spoilage, short shelf-life deliveries, and painful retail chargebacks.

In Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, these workflows can be fully automated using native lot tracking codes, expiration calculation formulas, and automated electronic CTE/KDE log extraction.
