A mediocre employee with automation could outperform an entire department stuck in 2015.

Not because they're smarter.

Because the department is drowning in outdated processes.

Manual reports.

Endless approvals.

Pointless meetings.

The uncomfortable truth?

Companies don't always have a talent problem.

Sometimes they have a management problem disguised as a staffing problem.

Technology won't magically fix bad leadership.

But it can make the cost of incompetence impossible to ignore.
