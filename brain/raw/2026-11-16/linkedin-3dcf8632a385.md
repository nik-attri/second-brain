🐄 The Sacred Cow of Success

Your biggest career risk isn't what you don't know. It's the sacred cow you refuse to challenge: "I've always done it this way."

Most of us approach career decisions the same way we've approached business decisions for decades: Analyse the options. Assess the risks. Build the shortlist. Choose the smartest move. It worked before. 

That's precisely why it might fail now. Because the challenge facing many of us today isn't a decision problem. It's an identity problem. But the belief that "how I've always done it" is making us irrelevant.

Promotion. Board role. Portfolio career. Consulting. Semi-retirement. Another corporate move.

What if every option on that list is just a more sophisticated form of staying the same?

That question makes high achievers uncomfortable. Because we've been rewarded our entire careers for making better choices. Rarely for questioning the assumptions behind them. 

The leaders building extraordinary second, third, and fourth acts aren't obsessing over which option to choose. They're confronting a far more uncomfortable question: "What truth have I been ignoring because it threatens who I've become?" 

That's where the brilliant Dr Suzanne Henwood's bWise® Coaching Academy, Multiple Brains, and 12 Intelligences framework completely changes the conversation. Most senior leaders like you have spent decades strengthening analytical intelligence. But analysis is often useless when you're standing at the edge of reinventing yourself. The breakthrough usually arrives somewhere else:

✅ Creative Intelligence Challenges futures nobody else can see, only you.
✅ Relational Intelligence Reveals who you're becoming, not just what you're doing.
✅ Somatic Intelligence Exposes the opportunities your body says Hell Yes! to without any data to back it up.
✅ Intuitive Intelligence Detects emerging patterns long before a spreadsheet can validate them.

The future that changes everything may require you to create it. And you can't optimise your way into a life that doesn't exist yet. The future doesn't need a better version of who you've been. The world needs a creative, inspired, aligned YOU 🫵 Get brave enough to design an extraordinary next chapter.

👇What's one belief about success, leadership, or career progression that you've had to let go of in the last five years, and what did that do for you?

That's where the real conversation starts. 

This is the work I do - the raw, uncomfortable, career-altering work of rebuilding the internal compass you've outsourced for far too long.
