Job Description: ServiceNow ITOM Developer
Position
ServiceNow IT Operations Management (ITOM) Developer, India
Hiring  for contractual position of ServiceNow ITOM Developer  5-6 Years 
Reach 7531883312
Follow Ravi Chauhan

We are seeking a skilled ServiceNow ITOM Developer to support the implementation of Discovery, Service Mapping, CMDB, integrations, and automation solutions. The candidate will configure and develop ITOM capabilities, build integrations with infrastructure platforms including Microsoft Azure and VMware vCenter, support CMDB data quality and reconciliation, and help deliver stable deployments under the guidance of the Technical Lead and Architect.
Key Responsibilities
Discovery & Service Mapping
•	Configure Discovery schedules, credentials, and IP ranges.
•	Install, configure, and maintain MID Servers.
•	Support Service Mapping implementation, including entry points and pattern configuration.
•	Troubleshoot Discovery and Service Mapping errors.
CMDB
•	Configure CMDB classes, attributes, and relationships.
•	Support data reconciliation activities using IRE rules.
•	Support CMDB health and data quality remediation.
Integrations & Automation
•	Develop integrations with Microsoft Azure and VMware vCenter and good to have Amazon Connect, PagerDuty, SCCM / Intune and Microsoft 365.
•	Build automation using IntegrationHub, Flow Designer, and scripting.
•	Develop and maintain REST/SOAP API integrations.
Delivery & Support
•	Develop solutions as per approved technical designs.
•	Resolve defects and support testing activities.
•	Support deployments and go-live activities.
•	Follow ServiceNow best practices and OOB capabilities.
Required Skills
ServiceNow
•	3 to 6 years of ServiceNow development experience.
•	Hands-on experience with: 
o	Discovery
o	Service Mapping
o	CMDB
o	MID Servers
o	JavaScript
o	IntegrationHub
o	REST/SOAP APIs
•	Integration experience with Microsoft Azure and VMware vCenter.
•	Exposure to Amazon Connect, PagerDuty, SCCM / Intune and Microsoft 365 integrations (preferred).
Technical & Professional
•	Understanding of infrastructure technologies (servers, networks, storage, virtualization, and cloud).
•	Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
•	Good communication and teamwork skills.
•	Agile delivery experience.
Certifications
•	ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA): Required
•	Certified Implementation Specialist: Discovery (Preferred)
•	Certified Implementation Specialist: Service Mapping (Preferred)
