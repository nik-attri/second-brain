META was fined €805 million for accessing the photo galleries of Android users in Italy. And it’s not even a GDPR violation. 

Photos and videos, stored on Android smartphones, have been accessed by the company through the Facebook application without specific consent and without adequate prior information. 

What happened?

Since 2023 the app has required a general single permission for access to the gallery, valid for the entire app and for multiple functions. Instead of asking for a specific consent on the feature “Get suggestions from gallery when browsing Facebook”, it was set as enabled by default and users had to manually disable it. 

Now, as announced on 14 November, the Italian authority has imposed the fine and ordered META, within 60 days, to introduce a separate and specific request for the feature and to keep it switched off by default. 

Something interesting from a legal perspective:

The Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante) found that the rules transposing the e-Privacy Directive (not GDPR!) were violated. These are the rules, protecting smartphone as part of a person's private sphere and requiring free and informed consent to accessing any information contained on the device. 

This is the reason for not referring the case to Ireland, where META’s EU headquarters is located. The Italian authority found that the one-stop-shop mechanism applies for GDPR cases but not for e-Privacy violations. 

What to expect next?

This procedure does not concern the potential use of content to train AI, which Meta denies. The Italian DPA may assess the new feature, launched in 2026, that allows cloud processing of gallery content, in a separate procedure.

META intends to appeal the fine. However, if the competent authority in every EU country that has implemented the e-Privacy Directive follows Italy’s example, META could face considerable regulatory pressure, especially if the fines are of a similar size. 

What can Android users do now?

If you still see your photos in posting suggestions, you can disable this feature from the app menu: Settings & Privacy -> Settings -> Camera roll sharing suggestions.
