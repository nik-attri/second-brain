48 hours, 1500+ registered teams, and a Top 16 finish at Deviathon 2.0! 🚀🔥
​I am thrilled to share that our team, PRAVAHA, recently competed in Deviathon 2.0, a national-level hackathon hosted at GLA University, Mathura, from October 29 to 31.
​Out of 1,500+ registered teams nationwide, we earned a spot among the 80 offline finalists. After 48 continuous hours of prototyping, integrating workflows, and defending our build across multiple evaluation rounds, we advanced to the final stage (Round 3) and secured 16th place overall out of 78 on-site teams! 🏆

​💡 The Challenge & What We Built:
We tackled problem statement DEV-C01: Employee IT & HR Helpdesk Agent.  

​In corporate setups, internal support desks face recurring ticket backlogs for routine tasks—ranging from password resets and hardware provisioning to leave policies and payroll inquiries. To automate this, we built a full-lifecycle virtual assistant that resolves common inquiries against institutional knowledge documents and escalates unhandled edge cases directly to human support:  
​Conversational AI Layer: Built an intelligent assistant embedded into Microsoft Teams powered by policy document indexing across key HR and IT workflows.  
​Automated Ticket Pipeline: Integrated Power Automate workflows to automatically log tickets and pass context-aware handover payloads to human support teams whenever a request requires escalation.  
​Live Deployment: Deployed a working instance for external demonstrations and validation.
