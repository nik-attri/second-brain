In 2021, a friend asked me to manage the social media for his business.

I said yes.

I thought it would be easy.

It wasn't.

Every day, I opened one tool to schedule posts, another to check performance, and another to respond to comments.

None of them talked to each other.

At the end of each week, I manually copied the numbers into a Word document so my friend could see what was working.

I eventually realized something was wrong.

I was spending more time reporting on the work than actually doing the work.

So I started looking for a better tool.

I found plenty of social media management platforms, but most focused on doing one thing really well. Others were built for larger companies with dedicated marketing teams.

But my friend had a small business.

I was one person.

We didn't have a marketing team.

We just wanted to show up consistently online and understand whether our efforts were working.

That experience changed how I looked at social media tools.

Small businesses and creators care about their online presence just as much. The difference is that they don't have large teams to manage it.

I kept hoping someone would build the tool I needed.

Then I realized I could try to build it myself.

That's one of the reasons we started building Novetiv.

Our goal is simple: help SMEs and creators manage their social media from one place, while using AI to turn scattered social media data into insights and actionable recommendations.

Not just telling you what happened, but helping you understand what it means and what you could do next.

We're still building, testing, learning, and getting things wrong.

The new version launches on December 20.

Between now and then, I'll share some of what we've learned along the way.

But I'm also interested in learning from you.

What's the most annoying part of managing social media for you?

Creating content? Scheduling? Analytics? Responding to people? simply knowing what to do next? Or managing same account with others?

Tell me below.
