We started Cortena with zero interest in the hype.
It was about solving a problem.

So a session called “Beyond the Hype” felt like home.
On 3 December, I’m joining the Female Finance Circle for a Lunch Session on agentic finance workflows in a multi-entity setup.
 
The topic came straight from their community. 
When FFC polled their members, AI-ERP and AI workflow tools came out on top. 
What makes this session different: it’s not me pitching.

Jana Granig, Team Lead Finance at FoodLabs, will share how her team handles payables across multiple entities. She spent months testing what works and what doesn’t, and she’ll be honest about both.

Together, we’ll tackle three questions:
→ How do you process incoming invoices without your team drowning in them?
→ How do you keep the books clean in a multi-entity setup?
→ How do you get ready for Germany’s e-invoicing mandate without adding manual work?

I’ll show live what that looks like in practice, including where a human still makes the call.
Hosted by Jana Scharfschwerdt, Co-Founder of the Female Finance Circle.

 📅 Thu, 3 Dec, 12:00 CET, online
 👉 Register: https://luma.com/psxlzb75
