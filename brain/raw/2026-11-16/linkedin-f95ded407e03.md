Nectar Launches Creator Agent to Automate Creator Marketing Programs.

Read more on our website: https://lnkd.in/dEXFdpnN

Nectar has launched Creator Agent, powered by its AI agent Ren, to help brands automate creator discovery, vetting, outreach, engagement and campaign reporting.

The platform uses a database of over 300 million creator profiles to help brands find relevant creators and manage relationships more efficiently.

“Creator Agent gives every team the system to do that,” said Misbah Uraizee, Co-founder and CEO of Nectar.

Farah Uraizee, Co-founder and CTO of Nectar, highlighted how the platform uses deeper creator insights to identify authentic brand fits.

Matthew De Zilwa, Brand Marketing Manager at David Protein, also shared how Creator Agent has become a core part of his team's influencer marketing workflow.
