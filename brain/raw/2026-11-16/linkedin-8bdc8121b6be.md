We’re Hiring: Decision Science Manager 🚀
Are you an experienced AI and consulting leader ready to shape the future of customer service? We are looking for a Decision Science Manager to drive enterprise AI transformation programs.   


📍 Locations: Bengaluru | Gurugram | Mumbai | Kolkata | Pune | Chennai | Hyderabad 📈 Management Level: 07 - Manager   

🔍 What We’re Looking For
Must-Have Skills: Agentic AI, Generative AI, LLMs, Contact Centre AI, GCP/Azure/AWS, Data Science, Consulting, RFP/RFI, SOW, Program Delivery.   

 


Good to Have: UI/UX, CI/CD, DevOps, Enterprise AI Solution Delivery.   


Experience: 14+ years overall; 6+ years in Data & AI, Contact Centre AI, and Consulting.   


Educational Qualification: B.Tech/BE or M.Tech/ME or MBA or equivalent.   


📋 Job Summary
As a Decision Science Manager within the S&C GN Customer Service Analytics & Contact Center AI practice, you will manage enterprise AI transformation programs across strategy, solution design, RFP/RFI responses, SOW development, delivery execution, and program management. You will drive business development and client engagement while delivering Agentic AI, Generative AI, Conversational AI, and LLM-based solutions on GCP, Azure, and AWS.   


🎯 Key Roles & Responsibilities
Manage end-to-end Contact Centre AI workstreams from discovery and use-case definition through solution delivery, adoption, value tracking, and transition to operations.   


Translate business challenges into prioritized AI use cases, requirements, roadmaps, business cases, and measurable improvements in containment, handle time, resolution, quality, customer satisfaction, and cost to serve.   


Design and deliver scalable Agentic AI, Generative AI, LLM, Conversational AI, speech/text analytics, agent assist, automation, and knowledge solutions across cloud platforms.
