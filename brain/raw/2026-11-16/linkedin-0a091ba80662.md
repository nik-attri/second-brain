You’ve got AI. Great. Does anyone actually know how your business works?

Apparently, every business needs AI. I see every second post says so and be careful don’t like anything because the gates of hell will open 🤦🏻‍♀️ or is it the gates of opportunity? 

AI agents. AI automation. AI-powered everything. You go for goodness sake what about my customer they need me too. But everywhere Claude, Grok and the next hot tip from someone you never heard of says so. 

But can I ask a slightly awkward question?

Do you actually know how your business works today?

I mean REALLY works.

How many times does someone touch an invoice before it’s paid?

Why does month-end take 12 days? And not 3🧐 or 5😳. What do you need to make great strategic decisions? 

Why does the same customer have three different account numbers?and a COD account🙄🤔

Why does finance spend half its life chasing information from operations? Instead of supporting operations make the best moves to grow your business? 

Why are your people building spreadsheets to compensate for the systems you’ve already paid millions for?  FYI I have a more hate then love relationship for excel. How much of your business runs on excel? Excel is one failed formaula away for disaster 

And here’s my favourite…

Why are we talking about automating processes that nobody has properly understood in the first place?

AI is incredibly powerful. 

But if you don’t understand your business, you might just end up making expensive mistakes at the speed of light.

Before you buy another AI solution, spend some time getting to know your business.

It might be the best investment you make.
