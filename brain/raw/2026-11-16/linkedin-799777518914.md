During freight bid season, carrier pricing analysts spend days buried in massive shipper spreadsheets—manually reformatting messy files from Emerge, Jaggaer, and Coupa, fixing broken VLOOKUPs, and guessing at lane rates off generic market boards. One bad spreadsheet formula or unvalidated ZIP-code pair can lock a truckload carrier into an unprofitable contract for an entire year. Meanwhile, software vendors claim generative AI will solve every logistics problem, even when large language models fail basic multi-constraint routing math.

In this episode, we sit down with Neil Fernandes, Founder of EnrouteAI, to unpack how truckload carriers and delivery fleets are replacing manual spreadsheet chaos with purpose-built pricing and routing intelligence.

Neil explains how EnrouteAI ingests messy, 10,000-lane shipper bid files directly as received—validating bad geo-pairs, flagging backhaul and triangle network fits, layering fuel-normalized DAT/SONAR benchmarks, and exporting back into the shipper’s exact template in under an hour. We also dive into Neil’s candid take on freight AI: why LLMs excel at parsing messy bid sheets while traditional mathematical optimization engines still crush AI on network routing, and how building "bid memory" gives carriers a compounding edge across every mini-bid and contract cycle.

Key Topics Covered in This Episode:

Escaping Bid-Season Spreadsheet Hell: Eliminating days of manual template reformatting, broken VLOOKUP errors, and cell-by-cell lane cleanup.

Pricing for Network Fit, Not Market Averages: Flagging backhauls, triangles, and deadhead-filling lanes against a carrier's real operating costs and fuel programs.

Compounding "Bid Memory": Retaining every lane priced, won, lost, or skipped so mid-year mini-bids and rate refreshes take hours instead of weeks.

AI vs. Traditional Optimization Algorithms: Why generative AI belongs on messy data parsing while proven math engines (MILP/metaheuristics) win on routing and network constraints.

Granular Final-Mile Route Intelligence: How Enroute Final Mile captures real-world delivery nuances—like exact dock entrances and verified time windows—to cut fleet miles by 20%.
