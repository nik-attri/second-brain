57% of QA tests are now automated, according to Sembi's 2026 Software Quality Pulse Report.

Between 2020 and 2025, that number hovered around 40%. So this is good news, mostly.

The catch is what's left. The 43% still running by hand isn't the easy stuff. It's the business-critical workflows, dynamic UIs, and cross-platform scenarios that broke the last time someone tried to automate them.

That's where automation programs stall. It's also where our updated ebook spends most of its time: why teams get stuck there, and the progression mature teams use to get moving again.
