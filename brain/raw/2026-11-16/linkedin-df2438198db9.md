Experienced Sales & Marketing Representative (Remote)
We are seeking experienced candidates on behalf of our clients for a Sales & Marketing Representative role. If you communicate well, are comfortable on calls and can work accurately in ERP systems, we’d like to hear from you.

What you’ll do
Make outbound calls to prospective clients to introduce services, qualify leads and arrange meetings
Handle inbound calls and enquiries professionally, and follow up on them promptly
Make follow-up calls on quotations, proposals and pending leads to move them through the pipeline
Create, update and maintain accurate entries in the ERP software, including customer, lead, quotation and order records
Log call outcomes, notes and next steps in the ERP right after each conversation
Keep sales and marketing data complete and up to date, and check entries for errors before they are finalised
Support the sales team by following up on leads, enquiries and quotations
Reach out to prospective and existing clients by email to introduce services and arrange meetings
Help prepare sales and marketing materials such as proposals, presentations, email campaigns and social media content
Track follow-ups and conversion status, and update the pipeline regularly
Coordinate with internal teams to make sure client requests are handled on time
Prepare basic sales and marketing reports from ERP data when requested
Keep client information confidential and communicate in a professional manner at all times
What we’re looking for
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with confidence on phone calls
Attention to detail and accuracy in data entry
Comfort working with ERP or similar software
Ability to work independently in a remote setup

Details
Location: Remote
Working hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST (7:00 PM – 3:00 AM PKT)
Salary: Market competitive
Joining: Immediate joiners preferred
How to apply: Send your CV to hr@virtuousgrp.com or DM me your cv.
