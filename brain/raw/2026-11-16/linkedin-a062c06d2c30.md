Before I knew it, my time in Sydney was fast coming to an end. But I had one more stop to go before dashing back to the airport to get home and give my wife and girls a big hug.

For over 10 years, I’ve worked closely with BD to bring automation, workflow enhancements, and a world-class patient experience to our pharmacies - including my Australian-first designs at TerryWhite Chemmart Mt Gravatt Plaza.

So catching up with Ed Murphy who is steering the ship across Australia and New Zealand for BD Rowa and so much more, was the perfect way to wrap up the trip.

Right now, having the right conversation about tech in healthcare matters more than ever. After last week’s global Microsoft outage disrupted healthcare services (including our own), alongside US flight cancellations and Coles cyber incidents at home, it’s easy to see why uncertainty creeps in around over-relying on tech.

But here is what people aren’t talking about.

Everyone moans when things crash - but this isn’t new. I remember when dispensing systems crashed and we went back to handwriting labels and repeats with pen and paper, then manually processing PBS scripts for reimbursement. Let me know if you remember this too!

Despite all the repeated tech challenges over our lifetimes, here’s what will always hold true:

Used right, tech isn’t about replacing what we do - it’s about changing how we do it.

And that’s what BD Rowa brings to the table: a fundamental shift in how we operate.

Here’s a reality check though…

Buying a Ferrari doesn’t magically make you a high-performance racing driver, and having the latest pharmacy tech doesn't automatically make you a world-class pharmacy business owner.

That’s why we’re cooking up something really cool with Ed and his team - helping owners become high-performance drivers who know how to drive their Ferraris without the limiters keeping them from unleashing their true power, while building a world-class pit crew to keep the garage running at the highest levels.

And if you wish you owned a BD Rowa robot, but need to fine-tune your business to invest in one, that’s where we come in too! DM me to find out more.

It was epic seeing you Ed and I can’t wait to reveal what we’re up to soon!
