You signed up for Xero because everyone said it was the best cloud accounting software for Irish businesses.

Six months later, the bank recs are a mess, the VAT coding is inconsistent, and you still can't tell whether last month was profitable.

This isn't a Xero problem. It's a training problem.

Here's what proper Xero training actually fixes:
- Invoicing mistakes that distort your debtor list. Credit notes posted as negative invoices. Repeating invoices set up wrong. Payment links never enabled. Training covers the full lifecycle from quote to paid - so your Aged Receivables report actually means something.
- VAT rates applied incorrectly. Xero doesn't know whether a supplier invoice should be at 23%, 13.5%, 9% or exempt. It only knows what your staff tell it. Mixed-rate invoices coded at a single rate. EU purchases with reverse-charge VAT set to "no VAT". These errors build quietly and surface at filing time.
- Bank rules that break the books. A rule that auto-codes everything from a supplier to one account sounds efficient. Until it isn't. Training teaches when to automate and when to review manually.
- Owners who can't read their own reports. Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Aged Receivables. You don't need to become an accountant. You do need to know what each report is telling you - and what to do when something looks off.

One thing that's often skipped: if your Xero file is already a mess, training on top of broken data just teaches your team to make errors faster.

Clean up first. Train against a tidy file. Order matters.
