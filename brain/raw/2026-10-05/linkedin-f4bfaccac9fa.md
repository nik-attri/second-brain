On September 30, our team took part in the International Forum “Financial Sector in the Era of Digital Innovation” in Dushanbe as a Silver Sponsor.
The event brought together government representatives, central banks from across Central Asia, international financial institutions, banks, fintech companies, and business leaders.
The discussions focused on payment integration, modernization of financial services, cross-border solutions, digital ecosystems, and the future of banking.
For us, the forum was also a great opportunity to present our new platform for financial institutions, which brings CRM and Core Banking together in one system.
The platform includes a digital lending workflow and more than 15 modules that automate key banking processes. Since all components work within one integrated environment, financial institutions can avoid using multiple third-party systems, simplify integration, and reduce additional implementation costs.
What we especially appreciated was the practical nature of the discussions. The focus was not just on trends, but on real challenges, current needs, and the role technology can play in making financial institutions more efficient and connected.
Many thanks to the Association of Financial Organizations of Tajikistan (AFOT) for bringing the financial community together and creating a valuable space for open discussion, exchange of experience, and new ideas.
