The business case for MBD is clear. Yet adoption across the supply chain remains challenging. Many suppliers pragmatically convert MBD back into drawings to keep working with their existing processes.

So, how do we break this pattern? A key element is information exchange across the supply chain: moving from manually processing documents to fully digital and automated information flows.

In this article, I explore how we can move MBD from obligation to motivation. A Dutch version of the article, 'MBD: van moeten naar willen', is available in an earlier post.
