❗️Warning: obligatory, boring reintroduction post incoming….

I’ve had quite a few new connections recently, so I thought I should probably explain who I am and what the hell I actually do.

It’s been a bit of a crazy ride over the last 18 years.

I’ve gone from running my own little one-woman business to building the systems that run other people’s businesses.

And it definitely wasn’t planned!

When I first started working for myself, proper job like, I was a PA / remedial sports massage practitioner in Johannesburg.

I had clients to book in, appointments to manage, people to follow up with, payments to keep track of and all the other stuff that comes with running a business.

So I created systems.

Back then, most of them were on paper.

Then spreadsheets.

Then bits of software.

And somewhere along the way, I realised I really quite liked the systems bit.

Over the years I’ve worked in care, started umpteen side hustles, built websites, funnels, CRMs and automations, moved countries, started again and spent an unreasonable number of hours figuring out how to make technology do things it really didn’t want to do.

Gradually, the systems moved from paper to computers.

And I went from creating systems to run my own business to hooking up systems that run other people’s.

Which, after 18 years of working for myself, has somehow brought me here.

I’m Emma Holler. I build HighLevel systems, but only for one kind of business: the ones where several people each run their own clients under one brand.

Multi-practitioner practices, clinic groups, franchises.

I’ve been the practitioner seeing my own clients.

I’ve run the business.

And now I hook up the technology that sits behind it.

That’s where Behind the Booking has come from.

I build one master system, prove it with a single practitioner or location, then hand it over so the business can roll it out without me.

I build the system. You control it.
