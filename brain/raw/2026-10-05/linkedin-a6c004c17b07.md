The #fieldservice management market is expanding rapidly, driven by the push for operational efficiency and the increasing complexity of deployed assets. 

Organizations are deploying connected sensors, real-time tracking, and cloud-based scheduling platforms, promising a shift from break-fix to predictive service. Yet the reality lags behind intent. 

Many companies collect data from technicians, equipment, and customer interactions, but the gap between data collection and actionable insight remains wide. The core challenge is not acquiring more data, we are drowning in data lakes - but making data meaningful. 

#PredictiveMaintenance requires clean, consistent, and timely data, yet many organizations rely on manual entry, paper forms, or disparate systems. #DirtyData leads to false alarms and missed signals, eroding trust in predictive models. 

While the market pushes toward #IoTIntegration and advanced analytics, progress is stalling at the point of entry. 

This article, by Kris Oldland examines how field service organizations grapple with data-driven operations, where predictive intelligence is making real strides, and where it is falling short.
