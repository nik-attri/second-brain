🚨 WE ARE HIRING! 🚨
📌 Position: Telesales Executive
📍 Location: Lagos
💼 Employment Type: Full-time

We are currently seeking a results-driven and customer-focused Telesales Executive to join our client’s sales team.The ideal candidate will be responsible for engaging existing and prospective customers via telephone, promoting products and services, building strong customer relationships, and consistently achieving sales targets.

🔹 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
• Contact existing and prospective customers to promote and sell company products and services.
• Clearly communicate product features, benefits, pricing, and value propositions.
• Respond to customer inquiries and provide accurate product and service information.
• Record customer interactions, purchase orders, feedback, and other relevant information accurately.
• Process customer purchases in line with established company procedures.
• Generate qualified leads and opportunities for the outside sales team.
• Manage customer accounts and build relationships that encourage satisfaction, retention, and repeat business.
• Use approved sales scripts to handle objections effectively and close sales.
• Develop strong knowledge of the company’s products and services to make suitable recommendations based on customer needs.
• Consistently achieve or exceed daily and monthly call-volume and sales targets.
• Perform other sales-related duties as required to support business growth.


🔹 REQUIREMENTS 
• Minimum of 1 years’ proven experience in a telesales role.
• B.Sc/HND in Business Administration, Marketing, or a related field.
• Proven ability to meet and exceed sales targets.
• Strong communication, persuasion, and negotiation skills.
• Excellent customer service and relationship management skills.
• Good data-entry and record-keeping abilities.
• Ability to handle objections, work under pressure, and remain persistent.
• Proficiency in basic computer applications, CRM systems, Microsoft Office, etc.
• Strong organizational and time-management skills.

🔹 KEY COMPETENCIES
✓ Sales-driven and target-oriented
✓ Customer-focused mindset
✓ Strong negotiation and persuasion skills
✓ Resilience and persistence
✓ Excellent communication skills
✓ Proactive and organized
✓ Strong team player
