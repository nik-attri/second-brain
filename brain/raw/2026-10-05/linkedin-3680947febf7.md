I keep wondering if I’m looking in the wrong places, because I cannot seem to find a company in Ohio that checks all the boxes I’m looking for. I’m talking about a company that does something genuinely interesting, creates experiences that people actually remember, gives a damn about both its employees and its customers, and pays good people well enough that they can build a life instead of constantly calculating what they have to give up to take the job.

I can find pieces of it. There are companies with great cultures that don’t pay enough. There are companies that pay well but seem to have forgotten that employees are human beings and customers are human beings. There are companies that talk about employee experience, engagement, development, culture and customer experience until the words practically lose their meaning, but then the actual job turns out to be another desk, another spreadsheet and another Friday counting down the hours.

Maybe the company I’m looking for is out there and I simply haven’t found it yet. Maybe there is an Ohio company doing something completely different, where the work itself is interesting, the people matter, the customer experience matters, and the people running the place understand that you don’t attract exceptional employees by offering them the same thing everyone else does and calling it a culture.

So here’s my challenge to the Ohio business community: where is that company? Where is the organization that can look at someone with experience in education, business, real estate, people development, customer-facing work and a pretty damn strong belief that work should mean something, and say, “Yeah, Travis. You might actually belong here."

I’m not looking for someone to convince me to take a job I already know I don’t want. I’m looking for the company that makes me wonder how the hell I managed to overlook them.

If you work for that company, own that company, lead that company, or know somebody who does, I’d genuinely like to hear from you.

Prove me wrong. Show me that the company I’m looking for actually exists in Ohio.
