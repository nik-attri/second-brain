Last week’s AI Dinner in Singapore was a blast! Our CEO, Marc Gamet , and Head of Revenue, Razak SIT , joined new friends from across industries for an evening of good food and thoughtful conversation. We found plenty of common ground in how we’re thinking about AI at work.

Over dinner, we explored questions many teams are asking: How much time are we spending fixing problems instead of moving the business forward? What makes adopting new technology, and changing the way people work, so challenging? And is compliance slowing automation down, or helping organisations adopt AI more responsibly?

The conversation was open, thoughtful, and full of perspectives we’ll keep thinking about. Here are four takeaways from the evening:

Start with impact. The hard part isn’t spotting manual work; it’s deciding which process to automate first. Prioritize opportunities by business impact, effort, and measurable ROI.
Adoption isn’t transformation. Employees using AI tools is a start. Real change happens when AI becomes part of the workflows that keep the business running.
Plan for what happens after launch. Automations need someone to monitor performance, handle exceptions, maintain integrations, and adapt them as needs change.
Make AI practical for SMEs. Smaller businesses need clear starting points, the right expertise, and solutions that fit their budgets and scale.

The takeaway: successful automation is about more than deploying technology. It’s about choosing the right problem, embedding AI into everyday work, and keeping it useful over time.

Thanks to everyone who joined us last week and made the evening such a great one. We’d love to keep the conversation going, what AI and automation topics should we explore next?
