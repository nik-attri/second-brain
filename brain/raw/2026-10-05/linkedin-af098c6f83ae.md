Reviewing our physical retail portfolio across the UK and Ireland is one of my favourite aspects of my role. 

Which locations are performing. Which need attention. Where the gaps are. And where we want to be next.  So many aspects all rolled up into one. The commercial implications. The community context. The cost of a lease versus the opportunity it represents. What a location says about us and who it puts us in front of. Whether the footfall is there today or whether we're backing somewhere because of the potential.

Property decisions in retail are long ones. You're not just committing to a number on a spreadsheet. You're committing to a team, a community and a trading environment that will look different in three years to how it looks today.  The question I keep coming back to when I'm looking at a potential new location isn't just "will this work commercially." It's "do we belong here and can we serve this community well."  When the answer to both is "yes", that's when you know it's a "hell yes".  

What do you look for when you're evaluating a new location or market? I'd love to hear how others approach it.
