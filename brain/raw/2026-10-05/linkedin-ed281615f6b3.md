🚨 WE ARE HIRING | ASSISTANT MANAGER / DEPUTY MANAGER – PPC/OMC
🏢 Titan Laboratories
We are looking for experienced professionals to join our Production Planning & Control (PPC) team and contribute to efficient production planning, inventory management, and manufacturing operations.
📌 POSITION DETAILS
Designation: Assistant Manager / Deputy Manager
No. of Positions: 2
Department: PPC / Operations
Qualification: D.Pharm / B.Pharm
Total Experience: 7–10 Years
Industry: Pharmaceutical Manufacturing
🔹 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
• Plan and schedule production activities to ensure smooth and timely manufacturing.
• Create, manage, and monitor manufacturing orders.
• Forecast inventory requirements based on production plans and customer demand.
• Review sales forecasts and customer requirements to develop effective production plans.
• Schedule production batches based on raw material availability, production capacity, and lead time.
• Coordinate procurement planning based on customer demand and production requirements.
• Monitor raw material availability, inventory levels, and warehouse stock.
• Coordinate with Stores/Warehouse for receipt, movement, and delivery of materials.
• Prepare production schedules considering routing, quantities, lead times, and customer commitments.
• Monitor machinery utilization and optimize machine productivity.
• Identify and resolve production planning issues in coordination with Production, Procurement, Marketing, Stores, and other departments.
• Analyze production capacity and ensure availability of manpower, machinery, materials, and other resources.
• Monitor raw material and finished goods inventory to ensure uninterrupted production.
• Provide accurate inventory and planning data to the Accounts/Finance team.
🎯 DESIRED CANDIDATE PROFILE
• D.Pharm / B.Pharm qualification.
• 7–10 years of overall experience with relevant experience in PPC / Production Planning / Manufacturing Planning.
• Experience in the Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Industry will be preferred.
• Strong knowledge of production scheduling, inventory management, material planning, and capacity planning.
• Good communication, analytical, and cross-functional coordination skills.
• Knowledge of ERP/SAP will be an added advantage.
🌟 WHY JOIN TITAN?
Join Titan Laboratories as we enter an exciting phase of growth and contribute to strengthening our manufacturing capabilities, operational processes, and business growth.
