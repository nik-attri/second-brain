Well, that officially wraps up my 2026 WNBA regular-season tracking.

465.6 units wagered

I started posting my numbers publicly on LinkedIn this season because I wanted there to be an actual record of what I was doing instead of showing up at the end of the year with a record nobody had ever seen before.
So here it is.

I’m attaching the full EDGE Tracker from June 2 through September 24.
Every tracked wager is included.

The public version shows:

Game

Bet placed

Result

I intentionally left My Line, Market Line, Edge and my internal notes out of the public ledger.
That doesn’t mean those numbers didn’t exist.
My numbers on the games were posted here on LinkedIn throughout the season, before the games were played. Anyone who has followed along this year has seen those posts in real time.
I just don’t think I need to publish every piece of the actual handicapping process and every internal note I use to arrive at those numbers.

The point of this document is to show the complete betting record.
No highlight reel.
No deleting losses.
No showing only the good weeks.
All 231 tracked bets are there.

For consistency, the record was graded using -110 pricing, and I only tracked lines that were broadly available across multiple major sportsbooks and/or prediction markets rather than grabbing some random number that existed at one place for five minutes.
And just to be clear about what this season was: this was my proving ground.
I wanted to spend an entire WNBA season building this process, putting my numbers out publicly, tracking the results, finding out where I was right, finding out where I was wrong, and seeing whether this could actually hold up over a meaningful sample.
