Excuse me, but having a stack of skills is definitely bragging rights. 😌

Good morning and Happy new week 😊

And I’m not talking about collecting skills just to add them to your CV, I’m talking about being able to bring multiple useful skills to the table and knowing how they can work together to solve problems.

For example, my skill set cuts across e-commerce operations, customer support, sales, virtual assistance, data entry, administrative support, and project coordination and honestly, I’ve come to see this as an advantage because businesses don’t always have problems that fit neatly into one job description.

You may need someone who can handle customers today, update your e-commerce operations tomorrow, organize data, follow up on sales, support administrative tasks, and coordinate a project when needed. That’s where having a stack of skills becomes valuable.

From a business owner’s perspective, instead of hiring five different people for five different responsibilities, having one capable person who can comfortably work across multiple functions can help reduce hiring costs, improve flexibility, and make day-to-day operations easier to manage.

Of course, this doesn’t mean one person should be expected to do the work of an entire department, It means that having someone with complementary skills gives a business more flexibility in how it assigns work and solves problems.

For me, I’m not trying to be boxed into one skill or one job title, I’m building a stack that allows me to contribute in different ways.

Your skills are not “too many.” Sometimes, your range is exactly what makes you valuable.

What skills make up your stack? 🤔
