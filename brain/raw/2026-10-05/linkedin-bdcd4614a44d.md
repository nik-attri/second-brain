Most engineering teams I talk to are drowning in AI tools that promise automation but end up being glorified spreadsheets with a chatbot on top. Here’s the thing: if your "AI automation" still requires someone to log in, click through menus, or debug failed workflows every week, you’re not automating, you’re just adding a layer of complexity.

We had a client in Lagos whose "AI-powered" lead routing system was actually a chain of Zapier automations that broke every time a field name changed in their CRM. Their sales team spent hours every morning fixing what should’ve been invisible. The real fix? Not another AI tool, but a simple n8n workflow that watched for new leads, checked their fit, and routed them automatically, no clicks, no debugging, just done.

Here’s the difference: spreadsheets with AI stickers still need human oversight. Real automation runs in the background, handles edge cases, and doesn’t require a PhD to keep it alive. If your "automation" feels like a part-time job, it’s not working.

What’s the one process in your team that’s supposed to be automated but still needs manual fixes? Drop it below, I’ll tell you what’s really going wrong.
