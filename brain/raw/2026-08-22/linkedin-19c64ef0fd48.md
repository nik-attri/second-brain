Raw data to live dashboard in 6 minutes.

Most teams spend a week on this. Power BI does it faster than your morning standup.

Here's the 5-step framework that ships dashboards while others are still cleaning Excel tabs:

**Step 1: Get Data (40 seconds)**

Top left button. Excel, CSV, SQL server, SharePoint — pick your poison. Preview and load. No copy-paste hell. No version control nightmares.

**Step 2: Clean (2 minutes)**

Transform Data opens Power Query. This is where cleaning happens — not in Excel spreadsheets with a dozen hidden tabs. Duplicates gone. Errors flagged. Column names fixed. Data types locked in.

Every action lands on the right side. All reversible. Close and apply.

**Step 3: Model (the make-or-break step)**

This is where it gets real. Drag the key field onto the other table. That's a relationship. Then build your measure: Total Sales = SUM(Amount).

Skip this step and you ship the wrong number. Model first, visualize second.

**Step 4: Visuals (4 minutes in)**

Drag a field, pick a chart. Bar, line, tree map — whatever tells the story. Add one slicer and watch the numbers change by region: Central, East, South, West.

The data responds in real time. No refresh button needed.

**Step 5: Publish (6 minutes total)**

Push it to the service. Send the link. Schedule automatic refresh. It never needs you again.

Get. Clean. Model. Visuals. Share.

That's the entire workflow. Six minutes from raw spreadsheet to stakeholder-ready dashboard.

The magic isn't in the tool — it's in the process. Power Query handles the mess. The data model ensures accuracy. The visuals make it scannable. The service makes it scalable.

While most teams are still debating which columns to include, you're already in production.
