☁️🤖 GDG Cape Town Google Cloud Summit Recap Eduvos

Sip And Cloud Tech had the opportunity to attend the GDG Cape Town Cloud Summit Recap at Eduvos Tygervalley Campus
A big thank you to Andrew Sithole for opening the event & acknowledging JetBrains & Eduvos for supporting the tech community. 🙌🏽

From Notebooks to Production
Thamu Mnyulwa challenged us to think beyond experimentation and ask: What happens when we take our notebooks into production?
We explored Papermill, Bash, Docker, Container Registry and Vertex AI, as well as #AIcoding tools such as Antigravity, Gemini CLI & Claude Code.
The big lesson for me: building a model is only one part of the journey. We also need structure, automation, deployment & scalability.

🤖 Hands-On with Google Antigravity
Hands-on session with Antigravity CLI. It was a great reminder that AI coding is not simply about asking AI to write code, but understanding how these tools can fit into our development workflows and the solutions we are building.

📊 From Natural Language to Data Agents
Another highlight was Nomthandazo T. Tshuma’s session on Google Cloud Data Agent Kits, conversational analytics and the Agentic Data Cloud ecosystem.
We explored how natural language can help us interact with data from sources such as PDFs, CSVs and other unstructured data, with BigQuery helping turn information into useful insights.
One statement stayed with me:
There is no AI without Data.
But I would add:
There is no trustworthy AI without trustworthy data and proper guardrails.
We touched on row-level security, tested instructions and responsible data access - reminding us that AI agents must be useful, secure and responsible.
☁️ Cloud Run also went straight onto my “I need to explore this properly” list! 😄

❤️ A Special Moment
One of the highlights of the day was finally meeting Nomthandazo T. Tshuma in person.
She has been an inspiring sister to me online, and I’ve followed her journey and contributions to the tech community with so much admiration.
Sometimes people inspire us from a distance without even realising the impact they have. So being able to finally meet her, learn from her session and connect beyond the screen was truly special. 🙏🏽✨
From online inspiration to an in-person connection — I’m grateful for the moment. ❤️☁️

🚀 My Biggest Reflection
What I loved about this summit was that it wasn't simply about another AI tool. It was about the journey:
Experimentation → Automation → Containerisation → Deployment → Data → AI Agents → Responsible AI
That is exactly where I want to keep growing across Cloud, AI, Data and Cybersecurity.

The question I keep asking is:
How do we take an idea, build it properly, automate it, deploy it, secure it & create real value for people and organisations across Africa?
