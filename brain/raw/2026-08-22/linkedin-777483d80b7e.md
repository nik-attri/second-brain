We stopped selling “AI automation.”
We started building business operating systems.
Most companies don't actually need another AI tool.
They need the 15 things their employees currently do manually to talk to each other.
So at AutoFlow, we build systems like:
→ Real Estate: inbound lead → AI qualification → hot-lead routing → CRM
→ Sales: prospecting → validation → personalized outreach → follow-ups → tracking
→ Dental: incoming call → AI receptionist → qualification → appointment → CRM
→ Finance: bank statement PDF → OCR → categorization → QuickBooks
→ Documents: thousands of documents → deduplication → OCR → AI processing
→ Property: thousands of images → quality matching → automated uploading
→ Manufacturing/Trading: enquiry → data extraction → quotation → order pipeline
And we're not just connecting Zapier boxes.
We're building the actual system around the business process.
CRM + AI agents + APIs + dashboards + databases + automation + human handoffs.
That's the difference between:
“Here's an AI tool.”
and
“Your business process now runs without someone babysitting it.”
We're looking to work with businesses where there's a lot of:
• repetitive data entry
• lead qualification
• manual follow-ups
• document processing
• appointment handling
• CRM updating
• reporting
• repetitive communication
If that's happening inside your company, there's probably a system we can build around it.
