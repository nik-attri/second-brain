Here's the lesson I learned in Amazon..

Competitive niche while working on client Account..

The product quality was good.

The images were good.

The listing was optimized.

But the main thing was not optimized… 

I realized it later when I tried this thing…

YES.

Let me explain what happened in a few minutes.

I was optimizing the PPC campaigns, and I had optimized the listing as well. However, we weren’t seeing much improvement in sales or ACOS.

So, I sat down and started thinking about what I could do differently.

Then I got an idea:

What if I discovered more keywords, even those with only 100 search volume (SV)?
I decided to give it a try.

Here’s what I did.

We were selling a product with multiple variations, so I wrote the content again. This time, I included more keywords, even keywords with 100 and 80 SV.

Yes, I’m talking about those highly long tail keywords.

Instead of using only short tail keywords, I merged long tail keywords with short tail keywords throughout the listing.

I did all of this manually because I wanted to test the strategy properly for my client and increase sales on the account.

It took a lot of time and effort.

But the results were unexpected.

After just 2 to 3 days, I started seeing an increase in sales without changing the bids or budget.

And this is where I realized something interesting…

We don’t know when a keyword with 80 or 100 SV will suddenly start trending and move to 200, 300, or even higher search volume.

By targeting these keywords early, we can potentially start capturing additional sales as their search volume increases.

You can also check the keyword trends in Helium 10 to understand how a keyword is performing over time.

The main thing is….

Don’t ignore low-search-volume, long-tail keywords.

Just think about it, almost no one is using this keywords trick…
