Your expertise may be scalable.

Your hours aren’t.

I’ve been thinking about this a lot lately because I keep seeing the same problem in expert-led businesses.

A coach creates every client strategy manually.

An astrologer spends hours preparing every reading.

A consultant repeats the same analysis for every new client.

A practitioner has years of knowledge, frameworks and methodology sitting in her head, documents and notes.

And every time a new client arrives, she starts working again.

We call this a business.

But very often, we’ve simply built ourselves a highly specialized job.

I realized I had exactly the same problem with one of my own methodologies.

Over the years, I had accumulated a huge body of knowledge around numerology and personality analysis.

The obvious way to use it was to sell more readings.

Which would also mean doing more readings.

Instead, we tried something different.

We took my methodology, calculations, interpretations, rules and years of accumulated expertise and turned them into a system.

Today, a client enters their information.

The system performs the calculations, uses my methodology to create the analysis, generates the personalized report and delivers it automatically.

No generic AI-generated reading.

No AI inventing my methodology.

My expertise is still the product.

I just don’t have to manually reproduce it every single time someone buys it.

And that experiment changed the way I look at expert businesses.

Everyone is talking about using AI to write posts faster.

I think that’s probably one of the least interesting things AI can do for an expert.

The much bigger question is:

How much of what you currently do manually could become a system without removing the part that makes your expertise yours?

I’m particularly curious about coaches, consultants, astrologers, numerologists and other practitioners who create personalized work for clients.

How much of your client delivery are you still doing manually?
