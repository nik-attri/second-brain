After LA Commons’ upcoming gala in October, I’m turning the page on nonprofit development and looking for my next adventure. 👀

I’m excited to get back into a creatively stimulating professional environment, particularly in gaming, entertainment, advertising, or another creative industry.

I’ve worn a lot of hats throughout my career, from freelance writer and creative to video game producer and nonprofit fundraiser. Along the way, I’ve built a toolkit that includes project management, cross-functional collaboration, stakeholder management, creative problem-solving, and large-scale event planning. I’m highly organized, good with people at every level, and have a borderline unreasonable love of a color-coded spreadsheet.

I’m especially interested in Creative Project Management, Game Production, Creative Producing, or Community Management, but I’m open to the right opportunity!

So, if you know a team I should talk to, a role I should see, or a company doing something interesting, I’d love an introduction.

And before I dive into whatever comes next, I’m looking forward to going out with a bang at this year’s gala, celebrating the designation of the Historic South LA Black Cultural District and the incredible work LA Commons is doing in the community. I’m very grateful for my time there, and if there’s one thing I’ve learned from nonprofit development, it’s how to throw one hell of a party. 😉

Here’s to the next chapter. 🎮✨
