From a problem statement to a product — that was the real hackathon. 🚀
Participated in the Online Round of Automate India Hackathon 2026, powered by HackBriven, where our team built SANCTION — an AI-powered Expense Operations & Approval Platform designed to make organizational expense management smarter, faster, and more transparent.
🧠 What we brought to the table:
 → AI-assisted expense request processing
 → Automated approval workflows
 → Duplicate & anomaly detection
 → Budget-limit and risk analysis
 → Real-time dashboards & analytics
 → Centralized request tracking and reporting
The online round wasn't just about writing code — it was about understanding a real-world problem, making product decisions, building under constraints, and continuously iterating until the idea started feeling like a real product.
From ideation → UI/UX → development → analytics → refinement, every stage pushed us to think beyond “making it work” and focus on making it useful.
Grateful for the experience, the recognition, and the lessons that came with it. 🏆
One round. One product. A lot of iterations. And plenty more to build. ⚡
