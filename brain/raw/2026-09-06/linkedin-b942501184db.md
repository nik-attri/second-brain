How we saved a growing agency 18 hours a week (without hiring a single person). 📉 ➡️ 📈

Here is what their workflow looked like last month:
1. A new lead fills out a contact form.
2. The owner sees the email 4 hours later.
3. The owner manually texts the lead to find a time to talk.
4. Back-and-forth messages for 2 days trying to schedule a call.
5. Lead gets cold, goes to a competitor who replied faster.

Here is the Forge Flow upgrade we built:
1. Lead fills out the contact form.
2. An automated workflow instantly logs them in the CRM.
3. The system automatically sends a personalized text with a calendar link.
4. The client books their own slot within 90 seconds.

Zero manual work. Zero delayed replies.
