Prop firm news trading rules 2026 explained: Which prop firms allow NFP and FOMC trading? This guide compares The5ers, FTMO, FundedNext, FTM, and other prop firms to break down news trading restrictions, blackout windows, profit deductions, funded-account rules, and unrestricted news trading options.

If your trading strategy depends on NFP, FOMC, CPI, or other high-impact economic releases, one overlooked prop firm rule can turn a profitable trade into a rule violation.

And the biggest mistake?
Assuming that the rules you followed during the evaluation are the same rules you'll face after getting funded.

They often aren't.
Our latest guide breaks down:
• The5ers - Which programs allow unrestricted news trading and where the 2-minute High Stakes restriction applies.
• FTMO - How the Standard funded account differs from the unrestricted Swing account.
• FundedNext - How the 5-minute news window and 40% News Reward Share Rule can affect funded CFD traders.
• FTM - What its current published rules indicate and why traders should verify the policy directly before trading major releases.
• Other news-friendly prop firms- Including firms that publish unrestricted news trading policies.

⚠️ One detail traders often miss:
A manually placed trade isn't the only thing that can count as execution.
An automatic stop-loss or take-profit trigger during a restricted news window can also have consequences, depending on the firm's rules.

Before choosing a prop firm for news trading, ask:
✅ Are NFP and FOMC trades allowed on the funded account?
✅ Is there a blackout window?
✅ Do automatic SL/TP executions count?
✅ Are profits deducted or is the account breached?
✅ Does the rule apply to CFDs, futures, or both?
✅ Does the firm publish a clear list of restricted events?

News trading isn't just about predicting the market. It's about understanding execution risk, slippage, spreads, drawdown limits, and the exact rules governing your funded account.
