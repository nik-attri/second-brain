AI in supply chain isn't about replacing people—it's about removing routine data noise so planners can focus on strategy, vendor relationships, and risk. 

With the adment of AI each individual role is shifted from operational to strategic implements.

Let AI do the repeated tasks and you be in control of decisions.

Holding tens of millions in inventory while planners spend 70% of their time on manual min/max updates, long PR-to-PO cycles, and quote chasing is a silent killer of working capital. 

When looking to scale operations, the old playbook says: “Hire more people.”

Let us take a different route: AI Workforce Augmentation. 🤖

By deploying a 9-Agent AI Squad directly alongside our team, we automated low-tier data execution across inventory planning, supplier tracking, and spend analytics: 

Material Availability: Scaled to 98%+. 

Working Capital: Unlocked millions by liquidating dead stock and optimizing reorder points. 

The financial impact? An initial software investment in Microsoft Copilot, Power Automate, and Azure OpenAI generated an annual value yield of up to 1,662% ROI
