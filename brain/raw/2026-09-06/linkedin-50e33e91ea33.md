Is there an "ideal trading diet"? I would think so, however I think the ideal diet is different for everyone because our bodies and nutritional needs are different. Even for the same person, it changes with time as well.

For me, I have 4 staples. Fish, egg, vegetables, fruits. Regardless of where I am, I have 3 out of 4 of them every day. I deliberately left 1 one out because I think not having something occasionally will help absorption when we do have it.

I avoid meat totally. Eating them makes me very sluggish and sleepy. I avoid dairy and soy products totally, having them makes me sleepy and tired as well. Same for gluten, which is a broad category of wheat flour products. I avoid. Sometimes I miss the taste of bread, I'll take a few bites, enjoy the taste and spit most of it out.

I also do not drink alcohol for years already. The last that I had was a couple of years ago at Wimbledon's Prince of Wales pub where I had a pint of Guinness to celebrate a good year of investments. Drinking is such a rare event that I even remember where was the exact seat I sat.

Over the past 2 months, I tripled my capital through swing trading, achieving over 90% win rate over more than 100 trades. I'm competing with the cream of the crop traders in the world, many young sharp quick, and the best systematic automated trading. While I make all trades manually by hand.

I genuinely do not think feeling sluggish and sleepy or having handovers could let me perform as well as I did. Some work days are 20h days. It takes efforts to find out what food works best for us and it takes tremendous discipline to commit to that. No alcohol - can you imagine a life without alcohol?

Well, to me, that's part of the job to win in trading. To bring forth the best version of ourselves to the trading desk. Every day. In every trade, 1 side wins and 1 side loses. At that ultra competitive level, the optimization to win happens beyond just the professional experience.
