A website can look perfect today... and be trash 3 months later. 🚨

I've seen it happen. A lot.

Found a great site for a SaaS client last quarter.

Niche fit. Good traffic. Solid authority.

Added it to the outreach list immediately.

Checked it again last week before sending the pitch.

Completely different site.

Traffic tanking.

Publishing random AI posts about "10 best dog toys" on a SaaS blog.

Every 2nd article screaming "Sponsored Post."

Link farms everywhere.

Would I still pitch this to my client today?

Hell no.

That's why I never trust a spreadsheet from 3 months ago.

Before any guest post or link insertion, I re-check:

→   Is traffic still stable or dying?
→  What are they publishing this month?
→  Are the articles actually helpful or just SEO filler?
→  Has it turned into a "Write for us" link farm?
→ 👀 Would I personally read this site?

Metrics tell you what a site WAS.

They don't tell you what it BECAME.

For SaaS link building, I'd rather take 1 relevant, healthy site today than 10 "high DR" sites from an old list.

Don't ask:

"Was this a good website?"

Ask:

"Would I recommend this to my client TODAY?"

Because your client's reputation is on the line, not just a backlink.

Agree or disagree? 👇
