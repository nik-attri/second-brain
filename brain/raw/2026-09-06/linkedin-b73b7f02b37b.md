A conversation I have with brokers almost every week:

Them: "Man, I'm just drowning right now. Every week it's the same thing — someone messages about a property, I drop everything, send the OM, answer 20 questions, hop on a 30-minute call... and two weeks later I find out they don't even have verified liquidity."

Me: "How much of your week does that actually eat up?"

Them: "Honestly? More than I want to admit. And the worst part — while I'm busy chasing that dead-end lead, I've got three warm off-market prospects from last month just sitting there. Nobody followed up. They probably think I forgot about them."

Me: "You didn't forget. You were playing administrator instead of broker. That's the real problem — it's not that you don't have leads, it's that you don't have a filter."

Them: "A filter? Like what, just... ignore people?"

Me: "No — automate the first conversation. Let AI handle the initial triage before it ever hits your calendar. Someone reaches out, it asks the right questions upfront: budget, timeline, financing status. Window shoppers get filtered out before they waste your time. Serious buyers get an instant response with exactly what they need. And your long-term pipeline? It stays warm on auto-pilot, following up automatically, so nobody slips through the cracks again."

Them: "So the AI is doing the deal?"

Me: "Not even close. AI isn't closing a multi-million dollar transaction — that's still you, your relationships, your negotiation. This business will always be human. But AI stops your day from getting eaten alive by manual admin. That's it. That's the whole point."

Them: "...Alright, I need this."
