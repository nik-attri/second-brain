🌟 Ready to take your HR knowledge from the classroom into the real world?

A great HR career is built through experience. If you’re looking for an opportunity to develop your skills, work on real HR projects, and gain hands-on experience with an international team, this could be your next step. 🌎💼

We're looking for a Remote Human Resources Assistant Intern to join the youbloom team!

This is an opportunity to gain practical experience in recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, HR administration, and employee support while working with a company dedicated to improving the live music experience worldwide. 🎵

📍 Location: Remote

📅 Start Date: Flexible (Fall, Spring, Summer, or Winter)
⏳ Duration: 3–6 months
🕒 Schedule: Monday–Friday
⌛ Hours: 10–20 hours per week
💼 Job Type: Part-time Internship
💰 Compensation: Unpaid internship

📌 Job Description

youbloom is seeking a highly motivated Human Resources Assistant Intern to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will support the Human Resources department with various administrative tasks, including recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, maintaining records, and updating HR files.
Exceptional interns who perform very well may be considered for future full-time or part-time opportunities with compensation as the company continues to grow.

💼 What You'll Do

✅ Assist with HR administrative tasks, including processing applications, onboarding, and offboarding
✅ Support recruitment and selection processes, including screening resumes and scheduling interviews
✅ Assist with data entry and record-keeping
✅ Maintain accurate Human Resources files and documentation
✅ Support HR inquiries and team needs

🎯 What We're Looking For

✔ Bachelor's degree in Human Resources or a related field
✔ 0.5–2 years of experience in a Human Resources administrative role
✔ Proficiency in Google Suite
✔ Excellent written and verbal communication skills
✔ Strong organizational and time management skills
✔ Strong attention to detail
✔ Ability to work independently and as part of a team
✔ Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines

🎵 About youbloom

Our vision is to make live music performance easier and better for all.
Our mission is to expand the delivery of amazing live music experiences by increasing the connectedness of fans, artists, promoters, and venues.
