On Facebook, I had a friend post something about asking chat GPT to describe them as if they came with a warning label. I wanted to know what it would say about me. If you're interested....

Oh, you absolutely came with a warning label. 😂
⚠️ WARNING: MAY CONTAIN EXTREME LEVELS OF CHAOS
Handle with caution. This woman is simultaneously capable of being wildly practical, aggressively ambitious, deeply caring, and completely unhinged in the most entertaining way.
Known side effects include:
Will turn a minor inconvenience into a 17-step business opportunity.
Cannot simply have an idea. She must immediately ask, “Okay, but how can we make money with this?”
Performs best when everything is on fire. Literally and metaphorically.
Has a suspiciously high tolerance for chaos and an unusually low tolerance for incompetence.
May quit the corporate world in her head approximately 14 times before lunch.
Will research an idea at 2 AM and somehow end up considering an LLC, insurance policy, website, marketing strategy, and commercial kitchen by sunrise.
Can go from “I’m exhausted” to “WAIT—I HAVE AN IDEA” with absolutely no transition period.
Will question authority, corporate logic, workplace policies, capitalism, and possibly the structural integrity of the building.
Has a natural instinct for operations: “This is a disaster. Give it to me.”
Dangerously resourceful when money is tight.
Will turn leftovers into dinner, dinner into a product, and the product into a potential side hustle.
Possesses the rare ability to be simultaneously “I have no idea what I’m doing” and “I have already developed a five-year strategy.”
Deeply protective of the people she loves.
May overthink something she cares about for 72 hours and then casually make an enormous life decision in 11 minutes.
Is not particularly interested in being told something “can't be done” until she has personally investigated approximately 47 ways to do it.
DO NOT mistake kindness for weakness.
DO NOT mistake exhaustion for surrender.
DO NOT give her access to a spreadsheet, Google, or an entrepreneurial thought after 10 PM.
Final warning:
Once she decides she wants something better, she's extremely difficult to stop.
She may complain.
She may spiral.
She may say, “I don't know what the hell I'm doing.”
And then somehow…
she does the damn thing anyway.
