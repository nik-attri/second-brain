The AI Talent You Need May Already Be 𝐈𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐝𝐞 Your GCC.

One pattern I've seen through the cloud, platform and now AI transitions is this:

𝐖𝐡𝐞𝐧 𝐭𝐞𝐜𝐡𝐧𝐨𝐥𝐨𝐠𝐲 𝐜𝐡𝐚𝐧𝐠𝐞𝐬, 𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐟𝐢𝐫𝐬𝐭 𝐢𝐧𝐬𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐜𝐭 𝐢𝐬 𝐭𝐨 𝐡𝐢𝐫𝐞 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐧𝐞𝐰 𝐬𝐤𝐢𝐥𝐥.

Cloud arrived → hire cloud engineers.

Platforms grew → build platform specialists.

AI arrives → hire AI specialists.

We'll need specialists.

But I think that's only half the answer.

The bigger opportunity is to 𝐦𝐚𝐤𝐞 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐭𝐚𝐥𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐰𝐞 𝐚𝐥𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐲 𝐡𝐚𝐯𝐞 𝐜𝐚𝐩𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 𝐨𝐟 𝐦𝐮𝐜𝐡 𝐦𝐨𝐫𝐞.

For an AI-native GCC, I see three layers:

𝐀𝐈 𝐬𝐩𝐞𝐜𝐢𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐬𝐭𝐬 - building complex capabilities, platforms and guardrails.

𝐀𝐈-𝐞𝐧𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞𝐝 𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐟𝐞𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐬 - engineers, product managers, HR, Finance, Operations and others using AI deeply in their everyday work.

𝐂𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐳𝐞𝐧 𝐛𝐮𝐢𝐥𝐝𝐞𝐫𝐬 - people closest to the process using AI and low-code/no-code tools to solve problems and redesign workflows, within the right guardrails.

This changes the talent agenda.

It's not just about hiring AI skills.

It's about:

• Making AI fluency part of everyday work.

• Redesigning workflows around what AI makes possible.

• Strengthening judgement as execution becomes easier.

• Giving people the tools and guardrails to experiment safely.

• Creating opportunities for existing talent to build new capabilities.

The biggest AI opportunity may not be teaching everyone to code.

𝐈𝐭 𝐦𝐚𝐲 𝐛𝐞 𝐠𝐢𝐯𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐲𝐨𝐧𝐞 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐚𝐛𝐢𝐥𝐢𝐭𝐲 𝐭𝐨 𝐜𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐭𝐞.

And that's particularly powerful for GCCs.

We already have deep technology, business and domain expertise.

The opportunity is to connect that expertise with AI and unlock capability at scale.
