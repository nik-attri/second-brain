AI training can teach your employees how to use AI.
But it doesn't automatically change how your company works.

-This is where many AI initiatives get stuck.
-A company conducts a one-day AI workshop. Employees learn prompts, explore tools, and leave excited.
 
Two weeks later?
 
-Most people are back to their old workflows.
-Not because the training failed.
-Because training and adoption are two different things.
-AI training answers:
“How can I use AI?”

-An AI adoption program answers:
“How do we make AI part of the way we work?”
 

I’ve seen this difference repeatedly.

Employees may know how to use ChatGPT, Copilot, or other AI tools, but still spend hours doing repetitive reporting, documentation, research, customer support, and data work manually.

That’s an adoption problem. 
️
So which one does your company need?  
Choose AI Training when: 
-Employees have little or no AI experience
-You need basic AI awareness
-Teams need practical prompting skills
-You want to introduce approved AI tools
-Employees need confidence before experimenting
Choose an AI Adoption Program when: 
-Employees already use AI but inconsistently
-AI usage isn't connected to business workflows
-Different teams use different tools and methods
-Leadership can't measure business impact
-You want AI to improve productivity at scale
 
-The strongest approach is often both.
-Start with training.
-Then identify high-value workflows.
-Build repeatable AI workflows.
-Create usage guidelines.
-Measure time saved, costs reduced, quality improvements and productivity gains.
-Then scale what works. 

For example, instead of simply teaching a sales team how to use AI, help them build an AI-assisted workflow for:
Lead research → qualification → CRM updates → follow-ups → reporting
 
-That's when training starts turning into business value.
-The same applies to HR, finance, customer support, operations and marketing.
 
The goal isn't to have employees say:
“We learned AI.”
The goal is to have them say:
“This process is now faster because of AI.”

It's helping organizations move from AI awareness → AI usage → AI workflows → measurable AI adoption.
