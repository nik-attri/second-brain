Your ERP can process a sales order. But can your automation recognise a bad one before it gets there?

A duplicate purchase order. An unmatched customer.
A product missing from the catalogue. 

These aren't merely data-entry errors. They're business decisions that can affect downstream operations. 

In a recent sales order automation project for a Global industrial equipment manufacturer, SigiloTech addressed this challenge using Microsoft Power Automate, AI Builder and Dynamics 365 Business Central.

The workflow follows three stages:
CAPTURE — Extract purchase order information from incoming PDFs.
VALIDATE — Check for duplicates, verify customer details and identify missing catalogue items.
EXECUTE — Create valid sales orders, log records in SharePoint and notify the relevant teams.

The critical part is the validation layer that determines when automation should proceed and when human intervention is needed.

This is an important distinction for business leaders evaluating automation: faster processing means little if errors move through the system just as quickly.
