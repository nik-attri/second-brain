Don't Automate a Bad Process

There's a mistake businesses can easily make with AI:

Taking a broken process and making it faster.

That's not transformation.

That's faster inefficiency.

Before introducing AI, ask:

• What exactly is the business process?

• Where are employees spending the most time?

• Which steps are repetitive?

• Which steps require human judgment?

• What information does the process depend on?

• Where do errors currently occur?

• What should AI be allowed to do?

• Where must a human remain involved?

• How will success be measured?

For example, don't start with:

“Let's introduce an AI chatbot.”

Start with:

“Our customer-service team spends 30% of its time searching internal information. Can AI reduce that workload while maintaining response quality?”

That's a business problem.

And AI becomes the potential solution.

The strongest AI implementations won't necessarily come from companies using the most AI tools.

They'll come from companies that understand their workflows well enough to know where AI actually belongs.
