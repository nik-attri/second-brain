🚨 YOU COLLECTED ALL THAT DATA… NOW WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING WITH IT? 👀📊

Observations. Assessments. Attendance. Documentation. Family engagement. Staff data.

ECE programs are collecting information every damn day.

But collecting data and using data are two very different things.

If the data is sitting in a spreadsheet, buried in a file, checked off on a form, or entered into a system—and nobody is looking at it, analyzing it, or using it to make decisions…

WHAT. IS. THE. POINT?

This week, MBC is taking a closer look at Data Management and asking the questions that matter:

📌 What data are you collecting?
📌 Why are you collecting it?
📌 What patterns are you seeing?
📌 What gaps are showing up?
📌 How is your data influencing classroom and program decisions?
📌 And most importantly… WHAT ARE YOU DOING WITH WHAT YOU FIND?

Because data without action is just paperwork.

The goal isn't to collect MORE data.

The goal is to make the data you already have work harder for your children, educators, families, and program.

📊 Collect it. Organize it. Understand it. Use it.
