DAY 4/30 — Don’t build a chatbot because everyone is building chatbots.

I like AI.

But I don’t think every business problem needs a chatbot.

Sometimes a business doesn’t need another conversation window.

It needs a better workflow.

For example:

A company receives customer orders through WhatsApp.

Employees manually read the messages.

They check inventory.

They copy information into a spreadsheet.

Someone follows up with the customer.

Another person updates the records.

At first, the business may think:

“We need an AI chatbot.”

But do we?

Maybe the real solution is:

WhatsApp → API → Inventory → Order workflow → Dashboard → Team

No chatbot required.

Or perhaps the problem is completely different.

If managers cannot see what’s happening across the business, they may need a dashboard.

If two systems don’t communicate with each other, they may need an API integration.

If customers struggle to find the right product, they may need better search or a recommendation system.

If employees need to complete a process while away from their computers, a mobile application may make more sense.

If a repetitive process keeps consuming staff time, workflow automation may be the answer.

And yes, sometimes the right answer really is AI.

The point is:

Don’t choose the technology first. Understand the problem first.

That’s actually why I built a small working product called Workflow AI.

I wanted to turn this idea into something people could interact with.

You describe a business problem.

Workflow AI analyzes the workflow and suggests the type of digital solution that may fit — whether that’s automation, a dashboard, an API integration, AI, a recommendation system, a mobile app or search.

It doesn’t automatically say:

“You need AI.”

Because AI is a tool.

It is not the business strategy.

For me, good product development starts with a much simpler question:

“What is making this process harder than it needs to be?”

Then we decide what technology belongs there.

That’s the approach I bring to business automation, custom web applications, SaaS products, mobile applications, API integrations and AI-powered digital products.

Technology should fit the workflow.

Not the other way around.

I built Workflow AI as a simple demonstration of that principle.
