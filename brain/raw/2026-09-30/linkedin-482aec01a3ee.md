📢 We’re Hiring: Operations Manager – Staffing & Payroll
Location: Noida
Experience: 4-5 Years
Industry: Staffing / Recruitment / Payroll
Employment Type: Full-Time
 Salary- As per Market Standard

We are looking for an experienced Operations Manager to manage end-to-end staffing operations, client coordination, employee lifecycle processes, and operational delivery across multiple locations.

🔹 Key Responsibilities
Manage day-to-day staffing operations across multiple clients and locations.
Lead and manage the operations team to ensure smooth and timely execution.
Handle end-to-end employee onboarding, joining, attendance, payroll coordination, and exit processes.
Coordinate with clients on manpower requirements, employee deployment, attendance, payroll, and operational concerns.
Monitor employee headcount, attrition, absenteeism, and deployment status.
Ensure timely resolution of client and employee escalations.
Coordinate with HR, Recruitment, Payroll, Finance, and Compliance teams.
Monitor PF, ESIC, statutory compliance, payroll inputs, and documentation in coordination with the respective teams.
Prepare and review daily/weekly/monthly MIS and operational reports.
Track operational KPIs, TATs, SLA adherence, and client satisfaction.
Identify operational gaps and implement corrective actions.
Develop and maintain SOPs to improve operational efficiency.
Conduct regular client meetings and provide operational updates.
Support business expansion and ensure smooth onboarding of new clients and locations.
Manage and resolve critical operational escalations within defined timelines.

🔹 Required Skills & Experience
5–8 years of experience in Staffing / Recruitment / Payroll / HR Operations.
Experience managing large-scale manpower operations will be preferred.
Strong knowledge of employee lifecycle and staffing operations.
Experience in client management and escalation handling.
Good understanding of payroll and statutory compliance processes.
Strong leadership, communication, and stakeholder-management skills.
Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities.
Strong knowledge of MS Excel / HRMS / Payroll systems.
Ability to manage multiple clients, locations, and priorities simultaneously.
🎓 Qualification
Graduate / MBA in HR, Operations, or a related field preferred.

Interested candidates can share their updated CV at kaashikarastogi@olivegreenconsulting.com or apply directly through LinkedIn.
