𝐎𝐧𝐞 𝐨𝐟 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐦𝐨𝐬𝐭 𝐞𝐱𝐩𝐞𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐬𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐞𝐧𝐜𝐞𝐬 𝐢𝐧 𝐛𝐮𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐬𝐬 𝐢𝐬:

“That’s how we usually do it.”

It sounds harmless.

Until you realize that “how we usually do it” is also why:

Customers keep waiting.

Staff keep repeating the same tasks.

Sales opportunities keep getting lost.

The same complaints keep coming back.

Managers keep solving problems that should have been solved once.

And everyone is busy fixing yesterday’s problems instead of building tomorrow’s opportunities.

𝐒𝐨𝐦𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐦𝐞𝐬, 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐛𝐥𝐞𝐦 𝐢𝐬𝐧’𝐭 𝐭𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐭𝐞𝐚𝐦 𝐢𝐬𝐧’𝐭 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐡𝐚𝐫𝐝.

The problem is that the system has become comfortable with the way things are.

And comfortable systems can quietly become expensive systems.

That’s why the end of a month is a good time to ask uncomfortable questions.

What are we doing simply because we have always done it?

What process creates unnecessary friction?

What do our customers keep telling us?

What does our team keep complaining about?

What are good people compensating for manually?

And perhaps the most important one:

If we were building this process today, would we design it the same way?

If the answer is no, September has already given you something valuable to work on.

𝐃𝐨𝐧’𝐭 𝐜𝐚𝐫𝐫𝐲 𝐚𝐧 𝐨𝐥𝐝 𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐜𝐞𝐬𝐬 𝐢𝐧𝐭𝐨 𝐚 𝐧𝐞𝐰 𝐦𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐡 𝐣𝐮𝐬𝐭 𝐛𝐞𝐜𝐚𝐮𝐬𝐞 𝐢𝐭 𝐬𝐭𝐢𝐥𝐥 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤𝐬.

Ask whether it still deserves to exist.

That is one small question that can unlock a much better way of working.

As September closes, what is one process, habit, or “normal” way of doing things you think deserves another look?

September ends today.

The opportunity to build better doesn’t.
