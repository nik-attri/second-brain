WE ARE HIRING – Senior IT Service Management / ITSM Engineer | Experience – 3–6 Years

**About the Job –**

We are looking for a Senior IT Service Management / ITSM Engineer to support and improve enterprise service management processes and platforms. The role requires practical experience across incident, problem, change, request, configuration and service management. You will work with infrastructure, application, security and business teams to improve IT service workflows, reporting, automation and operational consistency.

**Minimum Qualification –**

A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business or a related field, with 3–6 years of experience in ITSM, IT operations or IT service management platform administration. Strong understanding of ITIL practices and enterprise service management workflows is required.

**Roles and Responsibilities –**

* Administer and enhance enterprise ITSM platforms and service workflows.
* Support incident, problem, change and service request management processes.
* Configure and maintain ticketing workflows, queues, assignments and notifications.
* Monitor SLA performance and identify recurring service management issues.
* Support CMDB processes and configuration item management.
* Maintain service catalogues, request forms and approval workflows.
* Develop operational reports and dashboards for service management teams.
* Analyse incident trends and identify opportunities for service improvement.
* Support problem management activities and root-cause analysis.
* Assist with change management workflows, approvals and implementation tracking.
* Automate repetitive ITSM workflows using platform capabilities and scripting.
* Integrate ITSM platforms with monitoring, identity and enterprise technology systems.
* Maintain ITSM platform roles, permissions and configuration settings.
* Support platform upgrades, enhancements and configuration changes.
* Ensure ITSM processes follow established organisational policies and standards.
* Prepare service management documentation, procedures and operational runbooks.

**Recommended –**

Strong experience with ServiceNow, BMC Helix, Jira Service Management, Freshservice or similar platforms is preferred. ITIL 4 Foundation certification is recommended, while ITIL Specialist or platform-specific certifications are valuable. Knowledge of CMDB, discovery, service catalogues, SLA management, workflow automation, REST APIs and reporting will be an advantage.
