Everyone's asking if AI will replace IT jobs. 💻

Here's my take: it won't replace us, it'll free us up.

The manual, repetitive stuff (ticket triage, routine fixes, endless troubleshooting) is exactly what automation should be handling. That leaves room for what actually needs a human: the complex problems, the strategic work, the stuff that scales with the business instead of drowning in it.
