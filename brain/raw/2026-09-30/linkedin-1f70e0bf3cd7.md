🤖 Today is National AI in Work Day.

And perhaps the most interesting thing about AI in the workplace is that it doesn't always feel like "AI" anymore.

It's becoming part of the everyday workflow.

Businesses are using AI to:

• Draft and refine emails, reports and documents
• Analyse data and identify patterns
• Summarise meetings and information
• Research markets, customers and competitors
• Support customer service
• Generate ideas and content
• Automate repetitive administrative tasks
• Help teams learn new skills and work through problems

The benefits are becoming increasingly practical: less time spent on repetitive tasks, faster access to information, support with creativity and more opportunity to focus on higher-value, human work.

UK research published this year suggests around 44% of workplaces are now using AI every day. At the same time, adoption isn't the whole story — organisations also need to make sure people have the skills, confidence and judgement to use AI effectively.

And that's the bit I find most interesting.

AI isn't necessarily becoming a separate thing we "do". It's becoming part of how we do things.

The question for businesses isn't simply "Should we use AI?"

It's increasingly:
"Where can AI genuinely make our people better, our processes better and our customer experience better — while keeping human judgement at the centre?"

So, on #NationalAIInWorkDay, I'd love to know:👉 Where is AI already making a difference in your working day?

👉 And where do you think there's still too much hype and not enough practical value?
