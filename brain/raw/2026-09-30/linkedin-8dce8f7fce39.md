One of India’s largest LPG & industrial gas distributors was drowning in paper. Every bulk tanker, mini tanker, cylinder truck, and empty return required manual logging at every plant, during every shift. Gate bottlenecks were rising, and reconciliation took days and sometimes weeks.

To streamline operations, Incresol built a digital truck-movement platform natively integrated with SAP S/4HANA + SAP TM.

Here’s what changed:

🚛 End-to-End Tracking: Full visibility for tankers and cylinder trucks
⚖ Direct Weighbridge Capture: Automated weighbridge-to-app data flow
📝 Zero Department Paperwork: Seamless, digital handoffs
✅ Real-Time Approvals: Instant deviation sign-offs on the go
📊 Digital Evidence: Automated plant-level day-end reconciliation

The Impact:

⚡ Reconciliation Time: Reduced from hours/days to minutes
📄 Manual Logs: 100s of registers completely eliminated
🛡 Compliance: 100% audit-ready digital trail
⏱ Turnaround Time: Drivers back on the road, not waiting in long queues
🌐 Scalability: Standardised and deployed across 22+ plants

This same field-tested technology is now packaged into PLMS - Plant Logistics Management System by Incresol.
