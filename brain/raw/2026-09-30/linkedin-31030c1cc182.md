WE ARE HIRING – Senior Identity & Access Management Engineer | Experience – 3–6 Years

**About the Job –**

We are looking for a Senior Identity & Access Management Engineer to implement and support enterprise identity, authentication and access management solutions. The role requires hands-on experience with identity lifecycle management, SSO, MFA, directory services, RBAC and privileged access controls. You will work with security, infrastructure, application and business teams to improve identity security while ensuring users receive appropriate access to enterprise resources.

**Minimum Qualification –**

A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Engineering or a related field, with 3–6 years of experience in IAM or identity security engineering. Strong knowledge of Active Directory, Entra ID or equivalent identity platforms, authentication protocols and access management concepts is required.

**Roles and Responsibilities –**

* Administer and enhance enterprise identity and access management platforms.
* Manage user identity lifecycle processes across onboarding, role changes and offboarding.
* Implement and maintain SSO integrations for enterprise applications.
* Configure and troubleshoot MFA and modern authentication mechanisms.
* Manage RBAC models, groups, roles and access policies.
* Support Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID environments.
* Implement identity integrations using SAML, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect and LDAP.
* Support privileged access management and administrative account controls.
* Investigate authentication, authorisation and identity-related incidents.
* Conduct access reviews and assist with remediation of inappropriate permissions.
* Automate identity provisioning and access management processes using scripts or workflows.
* Work with application teams to onboard applications into enterprise IAM platforms.
* Monitor identity systems and investigate suspicious authentication activity.
* Support IAM-related security assessments and compliance requirements.

**Recommended –**

Experience with Microsoft Entra ID, Active Directory, Okta, Ping Identity, SailPoint, CyberArk or similar technologies is preferred. Knowledge of SAML, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, SCIM, RBAC, PAM and PowerShell will be valuable. Relevant Microsoft, Okta, SailPoint or security certifications are an advantage.
