⚠️ Seeing a sudden drop in your keyword rankings or SEO score? 

Don’t panic just yet.

There is currently a widespread issue affecting keyword ranking data across the SEO industry.

At the moment, all four of the keyword data suppliers we use are experiencing problems, and from what I can see, this isn’t isolated to one platform. 

A number of SEO tools appear to be experiencing similar issues.

In simple terms, Google seems to have changed something in the way ranking data is being returned or collected, and it is causing some tracking platforms to report inaccurate keyword movements.

The result?

Keywords that are actually still ranking perfectly well in Google may temporarily be shown as having dropped significantly or even disappeared altogether.

That can then have a knock-on effect on the SEO scores and visibility reports you are seeing.

So, if your report suddenly shows a large fall in rankings, it does not necessarily mean your website has actually lost those positions.

We have already seen examples where the tracking software is reporting lost keywords, but when we manually check Google, the rankings are still there.

The impact also varies considerably between websites.

Some customers are barely affected at all, while others are currently showing much larger apparent drops simply because more of their tracked keywords are being affected by the data issue.

I completely appreciate that seeing a sudden decline in an SEO report can be worrying, particularly when you have invested a lot of time and money into improving your Google visibility.

But at this stage, there is no reason to assume that these reported drops represent a genuine loss of rankings.

Our expectation is that the keyword data providers will resolve the problem and ranking reports will return to normal shortly.

For now, the most important thing is not to make any rushed SEO changes based purely on these temporary ranking reports.

Changing pages, rewriting content or altering an SEO strategy because of inaccurate tracking data could potentially do far more harm than simply waiting for the reporting systems to stabilise.

We will continue monitoring the situation closely and will notify our customers as soon as the keyword tracking data is back to normal.

SEO reporting tools are incredibly useful but this is also a good reminder that SEO software is reporting what it can see. 

It isn’t Google itself.

Sometimes the rankings are fine.

It’s the data that’s having a bad day. 😄
