We’re Hiring | Data Entry Operator – Inventory Section

Bore and Bore (Private) Limited is looking for a detail-oriented and organized  Data Entry Operator – Inventory Section to join our  Local Supply Chain  team.

Position Details

* Job Title: Data Entry Operator – Inventory Section
* Department Local Supply Chain
* Location: Maymar Karachi Head Office
* Experience: 6 Months – 1 Year
* Salary:  PKR 60,000 – 70,000

Job Summary

The role is responsible for converting authorized physical warehouse documents into accurate ERP/Google Sheets records  by recording receiving, issuance, transfer, return, and adjustment transactions. The position will ensure accurate data entry, proper document verification, record maintenance, and timely updates of inventory transactions.

Major Duties & Responsibilities

1. Enter  GRN, material receipt, issuance, transfer, and adjustment transactions  into the ERP/Google Sheets.
2. Update  receiving, issuance, and summary reports accurately and on time.
3. Maintain inventory records based on authorized documents  and verify documents before entering records.
4. Coordinate with the Inventory Office to resolve incomplete or missing records.
5. Maintain electronic and manual records wherever required.
6. Support inventory reconciliation between physical inventory and ERP records.
7. Prepare basic transaction and periodic inventory reports.
8. Ensure timely posting of warehouse transactions.
9. Maintain data accuracy and proper traceability of transactions.
10. Analyze inventory data and provide timely updates to the Warehouse Lead regarding discrepancies.

Minimum Qualification

* Graduate

Required Skills

* Advanced MS Excel
* Google Sheets
* Data Analysis
