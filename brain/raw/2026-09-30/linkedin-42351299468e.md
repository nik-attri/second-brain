WE ARE HIRING | JUNIOR MERCHANDISER – RIDING DIVISION

 Location: Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh
 Department: Merchandising – Riding Division
 Experience: 0–5 years
 Employment Type: Full-time
 Salary: ₹15,000–₹18,000 per month
 Reporting To: Senior Merchandiser

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Junior Merchandiser – Riding Division to support product sampling, merchandising activities, documentation, costing, data management and coordination with various departments.

Key Responsibilities:
1-Monitor the product sample process according to buyer requirements.
2-Maintain merchandising files, timelines and action plans.
3-Assist Senior Merchandisers in maintaining buyer accounts.
4-Prepare sampling schedules and trim cards.
5-Prepare POs and invoices for product samples and maintain related documentation.
6-Prepare costing and estimate profits for each order.
7-Coordinate with sampling and material departments for product development, approvals and shipment schedules.
8-Assist in preparing costing, work orders and BOMs.
9-Perform data entry, data management and data analysis related to merchandising activities.
10-Prepare and maintain MIS reports, trackers and other required data reports.

Requirements:
1-0–5 years of relevant experience in merchandising; freshers with relevant skills may also apply.
2-Good data entry and data management skills.
Strong analytical and numerical skills.
3-Good working knowledge of MS Excel and report preparation.
4-Ability to prepare and maintain POs, invoices and sampling-related documentation.
5-Good communication, coordination and follow-up skills.
6-Ability to coordinate with multiple departments and manage timelines.
Knowledge of costing, BOM, work orders and merchandising documentation will be an advantage.
