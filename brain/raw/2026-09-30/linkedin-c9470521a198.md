How my client reduced hiring time by 60% in 14 days

(with RecruitFlow OS)

They were struggling to hire fast enough to keep up with growing demand across multiple roles, especially admin and operations positions.

But they encountered a consistent problem.

Too many applications. Too little time. And inconsistent screening decisions across the hiring team.

They said:

"We're getting candidates, but we're drowning in CVs and still not confident in who we choose."

I suggested they implement RecruitFlow OS to automate the early-stage screening process and standardise candidate scoring across all applications.

Here's why it worked:

→ It removed manual CV sorting from the process.

→ It created consistent scoring criteria across all applicants.

→ It surfaced the strongest candidates first instead of last.

Here's what we did next:

↳ Strategic step 1: We integrated RecruitFlow into their application intake process to centralise all candidate data.

↳ Strategic step 2: We built structured screening filters aligned with role-specific requirements.

↳ Strategic step 3: We automated candidate ranking so hiring managers only reviewed pre-qualified profiles.

The result is that they reduced screening time by over 60% within two weeks and significantly improved hiring confidence across the team.

What changed wasn't just speed.

It was clarity.

They stopped guessing who to hire and started operating from a structured decision system instead of volume pressure.

P.S. If you're looking to reduce hiring time and improve candidate quality without increasing headcount, RecruitFlow OS is built for exactly that.
