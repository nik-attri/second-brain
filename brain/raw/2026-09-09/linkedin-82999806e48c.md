🚀 Top 12 out of 63 Teams — SIH Internal Hackathon!

Excited to share that our team has been selected among the Top 12 teams out of 63 in our college’s Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Internal Hackathon Round! 🎉

We proposed an AI-powered Task Automation Solution that transforms meeting conversations into actionable outcomes.

💡 Our solution can:
🔹 Generate meeting summaries from audio/transcripts
🔹 Identify tasks, responsibilities, and deadlines
🔹 Automate task organization and follow-ups
🔹 Create and schedule events on Google Calendar

We explored Generative AI, speech-to-text, agentic AI workflows, FastAPI, and Google Calendar API to bring the idea to life.

Being selected among the Top 12 teams has been an amazing experience and a great opportunity to work on a real-world problem with an amazing team. 🤝

A huge shoutout to my teammates Tanvi Bhageshwar Rutuja Gawade Poonam Salunkhe Archana Waybase Khushi Deshmukh and our mentors for their constant support and guidance!

Excited for what’s ahead! 🚀
