A few months ago, I was sitting with the executive team of a major enterprise prospect. 


Our team had just spent 20 minutes presenting our solution, architecture, strategic position & benefits etc…

The room was completely polite. And completely cold. 😅

I asked a simple question: "Forget about the AI for a second. What is the actual roadblock stopping you from getting your second workload into production this year?" 

The Chief Data Officer leaned forward and immediately sighed. It wasn't their model or accuracy. It was the exact same bottleneck I’ve been watching enterprises struggle with for over two decades: their proprietary data was trapped across disconnected, legacy systems, documents, and their teams were drowning in manual preparation work.

That meeting was a powerful reminder of a rule we live by: the market does not buy technology. They buy the resolution to a painful problem. 🚀 AI has simply amplified a two-decade-old data problem left over from the big data era. 

When we stopped trying to pitch "shiny AI features" and focused 100% on how Iris’s platform could establish a unified ontology that served as a context layer on all fragmented data sources, without forcing them into a massive, multi-year infrastructure migration, the entire energy in the room shifted.

We stopped talking about AI features and started mapping out how to remove their data-readiness bottleneck. That deal didn't just get unstuck; it expanded into a strategic, multi-year digital transformation initiative.

When you are building the plane while flying it during a company turnaround, it is so easy for tech teams to fall in love with their own “deep-tech.” But executive buyers don't fund elegance. They fund the removal of the specific friction standing between them with proven ROI.

Now, I'm off to jump on another customer call (with a fresh cup of tea to save my voice after talking WAAAY too much today 😅)!

When you evaluate new software or AI for your organization, what is the #1 underlying bottleneck you are trying to remove right now?

Share what you are seeing with your vantage point?! 👇
