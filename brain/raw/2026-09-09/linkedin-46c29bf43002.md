💡I was spending hours writing implementation plans for new projects. Until I realized there was a much better way. 

Drafting structured plans for every emerging initiative (outlining goals, tactics, budget, and scope) is critical, but manually building them from scratch every time became a massive time sink.

So, I turned to Claude to build a custom skill to automate the heavy lifting. 🤖

I set up a prompt framework that walks through the key parameters:
- Product name and core objective
- Key tactics and channels
- Budget allocation
- Deliverables and timeline

The First Iteration? It got us about 80% of the way there: a solid baseline, but it needed fine-tuning to truly hit the mark. 🎯

After running a few test rounds and sharpening the inputs, we refined the skill. Now, it hits the bullseye every single time. Best of all, now that it is built, it is available to everyone on our team so anyone can generate structured, actionable plans in minutes. 🚀

AI shouldn't replace strategic thinking. It should clear the path so you can focus on execution.
