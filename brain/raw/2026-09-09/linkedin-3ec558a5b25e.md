Imagine running a restaurant where nobody knows what was ordered, what was cooked or what was paid for.

A customer orders:

“One steak, chips and a Coke.”

The waiter writes it down.

The kitchen makes a steak, chips and two Cokes.

The customer gets charged for a burger and three Cokes.

Finance then spends Friday afternoon going through 47 spreadsheets trying to work out what the hell happened.

And when the customer wants another Coke, they have to email the restaurant and wait 3 days.

Sounds completely ridiculous, right?

Yet this is how managing software subscriptions can feel inside some MSPs.

A customer needs 10 Microsoft licences.

➡️ Someone takes the order.

➡️ Someone else provisions them.

➡️ Finance has to work out what was actually purchased.

➡️ The customer gets billed.

➡️ Then someone has to reconcile the supplier invoice against what the customer has.

➡️ And when the customer needs another licence, another email lands in someone’s inbox.

Why are we still doing this manually?

This is where Infiterra comes in.

Think of Infiterra as the system running the restaurant behind the scenes.

🛒 Customer orders → Customer self-service

⚙️ Order comes in → Provisioning

📦 What was actually supplied? → Subscription management

🧾 What did we buy vs what did we bill? → Billing reconciliation

💰 Are we getting the best commercial value? → Subscription optimisation

And it does this alongside the PSA and CRM your MSP already uses.

So instead of your team spending hours chasing licences, fixing billing discrepancies and moving information between systems…

the process actually flows.

And here’s the bit that should make MSP owners stop and think:

If you’re managing hundreds or thousands of recurring subscriptions, even tiny inefficiencies can become a very expensive problem.

You didn’t start an MSP to employ people to manually reconcile software licences all day.

You started it to make money and grow.

Stop running the restaurant from a pile of receipts.
