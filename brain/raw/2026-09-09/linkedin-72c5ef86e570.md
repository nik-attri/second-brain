The hardest part of building a digital product isn’t always building it.
Sometimes it’s explaining what the hell it actually is.

When I launched the Turasona Toolbox, I realized screenshots could show the design—but they couldn’t prove that this is a real, working web app. Not a printable. Not a spreadsheet. Not a Notion template.

So I built a 30-minute live demo.

You can click through all six planners, add and edit sample records, test the features and make a complete mess before deciding whether it’s right for you. The sample data resets after 30 minutes and never touches anyone’s private account.

It reinforced something I believe about both brand and UX: sometimes the strongest marketing isn’t another claim. It’s removing uncertainty and letting people experience the product for themselves.

This project has also become a very real exercise in bringing brand strategy, product design, UX, copy and ecommerce together—which is exactly the kind of work I love doing.

Serious organization. Colorful language. Zero shame.
