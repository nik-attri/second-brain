#Hiring #Reservation #Associate #Fresher #Hospitality #Graduate #Hotel Management 

Hiring Freshers with good communication skills

Role : Reservation Associate
  
Job Summary
  
The Reservation Associate supports the reservations team by handling guest bookings, responding to inquiries, and ensuring accurate data entry. This role focuses on delivering excellent guest service while assisting in maximizing occupancy and revenue in line with brand standards.
  
 
Key Responsibilities
  
Handle room reservations via phone, email, and online channels 
Enter and update booking details in the system accurately (e.g., Opera PMS / other      tools)
Assist guests with reservation queries, changes, and cancellations 
  
Handle basic guest concerns and escalate complex issues 
  
Generate daily reservation reports and forecasts 
  
Support team with group bookings and special requests
Assist in preparing arrival lists and daily reports 
  
Promote hotel services and upsell when possible
Follow pricing and availability guidelines 
Support revenue goals through efficient booking handling 
  
  
Required Skills & Qualifications
  
Good communication skills in English 
Basic computer knowledge (MS Office, email handling) 
Strong attention to detail 
Positive attitude and willingness to learn 
  
  
Preferred Qualifications
  
Diploma/Degree in Hospitality/ Tourism/ Hotel Management  
Familiarity with hotel booking systems (training provided)

Interested candidates can share resume to hr6@phoenix-cg.co.uk
