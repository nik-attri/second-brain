🚀 Hiring: Infrastructure Automation Engineer | Pan India

We’re looking for an experienced Infrastructure Automation Engineer to join our team and help build enterprise-grade automation that improves operational efficiency, consistency, security, and speed of change.

📍 Location: Pan India
 💼 Experience: 8+ Years
 💰 Budget: Up to ₹12-18 LPA

🔧 What You’ll Work On
Design, develop, test, and maintain Ansible automation using playbooks, roles, inventories, and reusable modules.
Automate critical Day-2 infrastructure operations, including patching, configuration drift, health checks, compliance validation, and remediation.
Support OpenShift platform operations, configuration updates, cluster processes, and platform hygiene.
Build automation with strong guardrails — idempotency, validation, error handling, rollback, logging, and audit evidence.
Integrate automation with enterprise ecosystems such as IAM, secrets/certificates, networking, monitoring, CMDB, and ITSM.
Troubleshoot automation failures across Ansible, OS, network, and platform dependencies.
Integrate automation into CI/CD pipelines, including testing, linting, packaging, versioning, and releases.
Enable self-service infrastructure capabilities through pipelines, templates, and catalog-based workflows.

⭐ Good to Have
Python / FastAPI for building RESTful infrastructure APIs.
Docker + Kubernetes/OpenShift experience.
Practical knowledge of AI/LLMs, RAG, and AI-enabled developer/infrastructure tooling.

🎯 What We’re Looking For
✅ Strong hands-on Ansible experience in enterprise environments
 ✅ Working knowledge of OpenShift and platform operations
 ✅ Enterprise infrastructure experience with governance, controls, change management, and dependencies
 ✅ Strong production/operations mindset
 ✅ Experience building automation that is safe, reusable, auditable, and reliable
The ideal candidate is someone who can go beyond simply writing automation — someone who understands production operations, enterprise controls, reliability, and measurable business impact.
