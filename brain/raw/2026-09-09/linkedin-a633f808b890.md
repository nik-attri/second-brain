Most fintech ops teams I talk to are drowning in the same three problems.

Reconciliation that still runs through a spreadsheet someone exports from three different systems every morning. Compliance checks that depend on one analyst manually cross referencing transactions against a rules document. Customer onboarding that stalls because a KYC document sits in a queue waiting for a human to read it.

None of this is a data problem. It's a plumbing problem. The systems already hold the answer. Salesforce has the customer record, the core banking platform has the transaction, the document sits in a shared drive somewhere. Nobody built the connective layer that lets an AI agent actually look across all three and act.

That's the piece we build at Avlys. Not a new CRM, not a new core system. An agent layer that sits on top of what a fintech team already runs, reads across those systems, flags what needs a human, and clears the rest on its own.

The teams that move fastest on this aren't the ones with the biggest engineering headcount. They're the ones willing to admit that a smart layer over existing infrastructure beats a multi year platform migration every time.

If your reconciliation process still depends on someone's Tuesday morning ritual, that's usually the first place worth automating.

Fintech folks, what's the one manual process in your stack that everyone quietly hates?
