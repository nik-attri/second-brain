Your operations lead shouldn't be spending four hours a week manually comparing supplier quotes for compliance.

Most "AI implementation" stops at a chatbot that answers FAQs. That is a toy. A real agent doesn't talk to you; it works for you.

Take the standard procurement process. In South Africa, many businesses require three quotes that must meet specific technical specs, B-BBEE levels, and delivery lead times.

The manual way: An ops lead downloads three messy PDFs, highlights the prices, checks the expiry dates, and cross-references them against a checklist.

The agentic way:
1. An AI agent monitors a specific procurement email alias.
2. It extracts data from incoming PDFs—even the ones that are poorly scanned.
3. It scores every quote against your specific internal procurement matrix.
4. It flags non-compliant quotes and drafts a rejection email for your team to hit 'send'.

The shift is moving from asking an AI "What does this document say?" to "Does this document meet our five-point criteria for this project?"

Stop treating LLMs like a search engine. Treat them like a junior analyst with a very specific SOP and a clear mandate to move data between systems.
