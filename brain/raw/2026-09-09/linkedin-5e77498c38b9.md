Your next coworker might not be human.
 
And that is not science fiction anymore.
 
AI is moving from:
 
“Ask me anything.”
 
to
 
“Give me a goal. I’ll get it done.”
 
That shift is much bigger than ChatGPT.
 
AI agents can now work across tools, handle multiple steps, and increasingly operate inside real business workflows.
 
India is already moving quickly in this direction — Microsoft reports that 32% of India’s AI users qualify as “Frontier Professionals” who are redesigning work around AI agents.
 
But here's what I find more interesting as a product builder:
 
The biggest change won't be AI replacing people.
 
It will be people managing digital workers.
 
Imagine a sales team where:
 
→ An agent researches prospects
 → Another qualifies leads
 → Another prepares proposals
 → A human reviews the important decisions
 → Another agent updates the CRM
 
The human doesn't disappear.
 
The workflow changes.
 
And this creates a completely different question for founders:
 
Don't ask:
 
❌ “Where can we add AI to our product?”
 
Ask:
 
✅ “Which part of this workflow should no longer require a human to do manually?”
 
That's where the real product opportunities are.
 
But there is another side.
 
More autonomy means more responsibility.
 
Security.
 Trust.
 Human oversight.
 Data quality.
 Accountability.
 
The companies that win won't simply have the most AI agents.
 
They'll have the best-designed human + AI systems.
 
I believe we're moving from:
 
Software → AI software → AI agents → AI-powered organizations.
 
The interesting part?
 
We're still very early.
 
If you could give one AI agent a full-time job in your company today, what job would you give it?
