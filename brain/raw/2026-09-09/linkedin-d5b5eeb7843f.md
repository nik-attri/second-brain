I AM LOOKING FOR GENIUSES! The kind of people who've been told their entire lives that they're “too much,” “too distracted,” “too sensitive”. Basically, I'm recruiting #neurodivergents who aren't designed for the factory settings of society.

THIS IS THE IDEA 💡...

We buy a house next to a beach. Not because we're trying to escape responsibility, but because our brains function better when nobody is asking us why we forgot to reply to a WhatsApp text.

Then we hire disciplined & loyal  neurotypicals, the implementors. People who'll remind us about appointments, do admin work, answer the phone & remember where we put our keys, phones, spectacles. 

Meanwhile, we'll be in our #innovation room trying to figure out how to solve a problem that nobody else has noticed exists. Since we'll be dollar billionaires, we'll also hire a psychiatrist who understands neurodivergence. We can peacefully own 47 notebooks containing structured plans that we abandoned on last year on Tuesday.

We'll also hire a Psychologist who understands #ADHD, #Autism, sensory differences, executive dysfunction, hyperfocus, burnout, #RSD & the chaos that happen when several high IQ geniuses are in the same house.

In our Innovation Room, when one of us gets an idea at 2:17 a.m., we don't say, “That's crazy.” We say: What if...?” Then we all brainstorm as a team. One genius sees the business model. Another sees the #tech. Another sees the problem we are solving. Another asks why the hell nobody has done it before. Another has already opened a spreadsheet to analyze. Another has already designed the logo. And our business person  is standing in the corner saying, “Guys, I think this could become a billion-dollar company.”

Our brains work extremely well in environments that allow them to work differently. For thousands of years, humans survived by hunting & gathering. We were the  #hunters! Unfortunately, modern civilisation decided that the measure of intelligence is sitting quietly under fluorescent lights for 8 hours & answer emails/calls. 9 to 5. Btw, Congratulations to neurotypicals who can do that. Neurodivergents just love innovation & creativity that 💡. 

SO THIS IS THE DRAFT PLAN:

Build wealth. Solve problems. Invest. Create opportunities for other people. And build a centre for neurodivergent kids who don't  grow up believing that their brain is broken simply because school doesn't know what to do with it. A "school" where curiosity & unconventional thinking is encouraged.

I don't want a community where neurodivergent people like me, sit around discussing how the society doesn't understand us. I want a community that's okay being misunderstood yet UNSTOPPABLE!

Bring your BIG ideas, weird obsessions, unfinished projects, ridiculous business concepts and your ability to see patterns nobody else sees. We'll bring the beach house & people who'll remember to pay the mortgage. I love ❤️  you all....
