Keeping it Simple with Scott - Week 8 💡

This week: Microsoft Power BI. 

You've probably heard the name. But what actually is it? 🤔 

So, lets keep it simple. 

Every business has data spread everywhere. Spreadsheets. Finance systems. CRMs. ERP platforms. Reports that take hours to pull together. 

Everyone knows the information is there. Nobody can see the full picture. 

That's where Power BI comes in. 

Instead of spending time pulling data from different systems and building reports manually, Power BI brings everything together into one dashboard. 

Think: 
📊 Sales performance
💷 Revenue and profitability tracking
📦 Stock and inventory visibility
🚚 Logistics and operational reporting
👥 Staff performance and productivity metrics
📈 Executive and management dashboards 

Your data is connected automatically and refreshed in real time. No more chasing spreadsheets, no more manually updating reports, and no more relying on yesterdays information to make todays decisions. 

Two things surprise people:

1️⃣ Power BI can connect to hundreds of different systems, including Microsoft 365, Excel, SQL databases, accounting software, ERP systems and CRMs.

2️⃣ Its not just for large enterprises. Businesses of all sizes use Power BI to turn raw data into meaningful insights that help them make better decisions, faster.

At Flyte, we typically do this in two ways: either by enhancing and modernising existing reporting or by creating completely bespoke dashboards tailored to how your business operates. Whether its operational reporting, financial performance, project tracking or executive-level insights, the goal is always the same: getting the right information to the right people at the right time. 

The result?
⏱️ Less time building reports
✅ Better decision making
📊 Real-time visibility across your business.
