Ask a CFO where the group's revenue numbers live, and you'll get an answer in seconds. Ask where the tax filings stand across every entity, and the answer usually starts with "let me check."

That difference is what we're talking about today at the CFO Leadership Summit in Riyadh. Finance runs on systems of record. Tax, in most groups, still runs on email threads and a spreadsheet, while the authority on the other side works with real-time data.

At 10:30 AM, Eoin Fitzgerald takes the stage with "Tax: a changing landscape". He'll show how finance leaders can bring tax up to the same standard, so insight, control and audit readiness come from how the work runs every day, not from a scramble when the questions arrive.

Eoin Fitzgerald and Ruben Van Aarle are at the summit all day. If you're here too, come find them. We'd love to meet you.
