A company can have modern technology and still operate in very old ways.

New systems.

Cloud platforms.

AI tools.

Connected infrastructure.

But decisions still happen through email.

Reports are built manually.

Teams work in silos.

Approvals take days.

That isn't digital maturity.

Technology maturity asks:

“What tools do we have?”

Digital maturity asks:

“How does the organization work differently because of them?”

That's a much better question for leadership.

Look at decision speed.

Process consistency.

Data usage.

Cross-team collaboration.

Ability to learn from outcomes.

Those are signs of real transformation.

Digital maturity is visible in behavior, not in the number of systems deployed.
