There’s something nobody tells you about leaving employment to start your own business.
You don’t really know what you’re signing up for.
You can research it.
You can plan.
You can make spreadsheets.
You can have a business plan and a very convincing conversation about how it’s all going to work.
But you can’t prepare yourself for what it feels like when your regular wage disappears.
When every pound coming in matters.
When a quiet month isn’t just disappointing — it affects your life.
When you realise that being your own boss doesn’t mean you work less.
It means there’s nobody else to pick up the pieces.
And the learning curve?
Bloody enormous.
We went from knowing our jobs to suddenly needing to understand things we’d never had to think about before.
Some things we picked up quickly.
Some things took a few expensive mistakes.
And some things we’re still figuring out.
There have definitely been moments when we’ve looked at each other and thought:
“What the actual hell have we done?” 😂
If someone had sat us down before we started and told us exactly how much time, money, stress, uncertainty and sheer determination this would take…
Would we have done it?
Honestly, probably not.
But here’s the strange thing.
Knowing all of that now…
Would we go back?
No.
Because amongst all the uncertainty, we’ve gained something we never had before.
We tried.
We took the risk.
We built something that was ours.
And even when it’s been hard, there’s a pretty amazing feeling that comes from knowing you didn’t just wonder “what if?”
You actually found out.
And maybe that’s what starting your own business really is.
Not knowing whether it will work.
Just being brave — or possibly stupid 😂 — enough to find out.
