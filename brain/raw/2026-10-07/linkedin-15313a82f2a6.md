We’re Hiring : General Manager – Commercial
Location: Gurgaon | Function: Procurement & Contract 

We are looking for an accomplished Contracts and Procurement function specialist to join our team as General Manager – Commercial. This is a high-impact senior management role for a career professional with experience of working in Oil & Gas E&P sector, who can combine strategic sourcing, commercial discipline and contract governance to deliver cost, performance and risk outcomes across projects and operations.

What you’ll lead
·      Procurement strategy, annual plans, category sourcing and cost-optimisation initiatives
·      End-to-end tendering, bid evaluation, commercial negotiation and contract award
·      Administration of high-value supply, service, EPC, drilling, O&M and logistics contracts
·      Contract changes, claims, disputes, performance monitoring and close-out
·      Supplier development, due diligence, governance, compliance and ESG/HSE alignment
·      Logistics, inventory optimisation, material availability and supply-chain risk mitigation
·      ERP/e-procurement adoption, spend analytics, dashboards and process automation
·      Team leadership, capability building and stakeholder management

What we’re looking for
·      20–25 years of progressive experience in procurement, supply chain, contracting  and contract administration
·      At least 5 years in a senior management role managing large procurement portfolios
·      Proven success in strategic sourcing, high-value contracts and complex commercial negotiations
·      Experience in Oil & Gas/CBM, Energy Infrastructure or EPC is essential
·      Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Supply Chain Management, Commerce or a related discipline; MBA/PGDM or relevant professional qualification preferred
 
Interested? Send your CV to hr@geecl.com with the subject line “Application – GM - Commercial”.
