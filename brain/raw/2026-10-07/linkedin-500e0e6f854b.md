Last night, I attended my first Women of SaaS event and had a great time meeting and chatting with so many new people! 

Women of SaaS's theme for the year is: The Year of Taking Power.

And... girl, did I use my power.

Before I get into it, some context. The panel discussion for the evening was "The AI-Native Company: What Tech Orgs Look Like in 2026". We heard from three female leaders about how AI has changed their roles, how they hire, how entry-level/junior employees can stand out from the crowd to get hired, and one company's strategy for differentiating themselves in a market saturated with AI-everything.

The discussion made it clear that AI is not going anywhere, and to stay relevant, we have to be plugged in: know what the latest tools are, explore and experiment, bring new AI-enabled/augmented ideas and solutions to the table.

To be honest, the discussion stirred fear in me as I also heard how user researchers and data analysts, were no longer needed at one company^, how experienced candidates are being hired (no junior hires), how investors and hiring managers alike are looking for companies and candidates who bring "fire" for AI.

Here comes my power move: I raised my hand to ask how the panelists are reconciling all this talk about AI with the disappearance of roles, layoffs, and the impact AI has on the environment.
 
I can't quote the responses verbatim, but here are my takeaways:
- The responses weren't reassuring. Don't get me wrong, I wasn't looking for reassurance and was just curious how the panelists were reckoning with the state of things as they are, in their own work and lives.

-  And, I say this with respect: it appears that everyone—from investors, founders, senior management, to employees—are still figuring it out. There are no nice, neat answers.

When I returned home, I thought to myself, where does this leave me/us?

Option A: "I gotta pay the bills, so I gotta play the game." ¯\_(ツ)_/¯

Option B: If I believe there is a better way (there were many of you in the room*), keep at it. Channel that fear, that energy. I'll look for companies that are building products I believe in and/or that are committed to using AI responsibly, and hold the leaders to account in my own small way.
ᕙ(  • ‿ •  )ᕗ

Option C: A + B

Option D: All possible combinations of A, B, and C (because we can hold complexity: we can recognise that we need jobs to pay our bills, be intentional about where we apply our talents, recognise that often our best is not perfect, realise that some days everything will feel like it's falling apart, continue believing that there is a better way, go about our days doing our very best to be kind to ourselves and others) 

Which option would you pick?  😬 

*So many event attendees thanked me for asking the question. Clearly, it struck a chord. Thank you to everyone who came up to me. It's so encouraging! ❤️
