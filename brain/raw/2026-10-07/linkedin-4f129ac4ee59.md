Employee advocacy usually slows down after the first few weeks.

This happens because either the system doesn't exist, or it seems too complicated.

Find topics, write drafts, check the brand rules, chase the team for approvals and remind people to post.

If done manually, this isn't sustainable. So most teams lose consistency.

The better approach is to make the system simple before asking more people to participate.

Here's the playbook we see working for B2B marketing teams 👇

𝗦𝗲𝘁 𝘂𝗽
→ Pick 5–10 people your buyers already trust
→ One workspace per team, brand rules built in
→ Invite everyone. Live in under an hour.
→ Map each person's voice so posts sound like them

𝗖𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁𝗲
→ A 15-minute interview. They talk, AI asks the follow-ups.
→ 3–5 drafts in their own voice
→ Comms and legal approve in one queue
→ Schedule the whole team's week on one calendar

𝗚𝗿𝗼𝘄
→ Run challenges with leaderboards and streaks
→ Weekly report in Slack every Monday
→ Show leadership the ROI: EMV, reach, adoption
→ Repeat. New topics, same engine.

And all of this can be done using one app.

Yes, you guessed it right, Supergrow 

Onboard your team, start capturing ideas, create posts, approve drafts, and track what is working.

Do it once, and your team posts every week.
