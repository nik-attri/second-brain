I started as an engineer.
Found my calling in design.
Today, I'm bringing both together at Super Major.

Many of you know my work through branding, 2D design, Rotaract and pitch decks.

That foundation still shapes everything I do.

My design education from Shillington in New York sharpened it.
12+ years with more than 100 international brands taught me to connect creative decisions to business outcomes.

Then Zealous opened a new dimension.

As Creative Director, I moved into 3D and visual production for global brands, including Fortune 100 companies.

I led Project, R&D, QA and L&D teams, and helped build a workforce of 800 artists.

That taught me what scale really demands:

→ Pipelines that hold under pressure
→ People who grow
→ Quality that doesn't slip across volume

It also showed me where AI genuinely helps creative work, and where it doesn't.

I'm grateful to the leadership, colleagues and clients who trusted me with that.

Now, I've started Super Major as Founder & CEO.

We build how products are seen, understood and experienced.

→ Digital Platforms
Websites, e-commerce, product experiences

→ Visual Production
3D, CGI, product animation, launch films

→ Immersive & AR
3D viewers, configurators, AR experiences

AI runs through our process.
It helps us explore faster, automate the repetitive and support quality checks.
The creative decisions stay human.

We're also building Super Major Labs, our R&D arm. Where we develop plugins, AI-assisted tools and workflows across 3D modelling, animation and QA.

The goal is practical:
- Less repetitive effort.
- Faster turnaround.
- Consistent quality.

Design foundation.
Engineering curiosity.
Production at scale.

That's Super Major.

I'll share the work, the experiments and the lessons as we build.

Led by Humans. Scaled by AI.
