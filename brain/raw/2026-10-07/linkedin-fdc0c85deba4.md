We design without Figma. At first, it was weird. How do we organize ourselves? How do we make the agents follow rules? How can we deal with some things being weird to type them in a Terminal window?

Over time, we refined our set of tools and called it simply studio. It's not a product, it's not the next "Figma, but with Code". It's a set of skills, processes, gates, automations – and very opinionated interfaces for the things we like to do manually. We design in the browser, but use a browser extension to do quick changes. We craft components and tokens manually in our Foundry, if we want to. It also helps with spotting and fixing issues by hand. The Showcase keeps it all connected with developers.

Nothing ins mandatory. Everything is helpful. Our clients integrate what they want, and it becomes theirs.
