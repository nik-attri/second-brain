For the CRE brokers out there

Turns out the visualisations we do are a nice to have.

The market data, plus visualisation may be cool, but seems y'all got bigger fish to fry

I decided to do some digging. Some foraging for pain. 

Where are CRE professionals experiencing real, expensive friction?

I researched the broker side and found some interesting problems

Thought I'd share my findings and see if they ring true

So, here's the 5 most surprising findings:
1. The primary revenue bottleneck is post-offer, not lead generation: Agency profitability is hurt more by post-offer legal delays and a ~23% fall-through rate than by a shortage of instructions.
2. Commercial data aggregators rely on manual phone surveys: Multi-billion-pound data platforms gather commercial comp data by having junior researchers manually call listing brokers, selling that data back to the same brokers.
3. Enterprise management software is often abandoned for Excel: Commercial property firms managing 250,000+ sq ft avoid specialised software because standard platforms cannot handle unique lease CAM allocation rules.
4. Planning conversion requires 18 Months of refusal logs: Local authorities require detailed evidence of every rejected enquiry and reason over 12–18 months before permitting change of use.
5. Vendors dislike evening viewings due to high flake rates: While buyers complain about 9–5:30 viewing hours, landlords and agents restrict evening viewings because evening applicants have the highest cancellation rates.

And, the 5 pains with the highest commercial consequence?
1. That post-offer conveyancing gridlock: Directly defers revenue and causes a ~23% deal fall-through rate, wiping out earned commission.
2. The data aggregator subscriptions + verification overhead: Creates a dual cost impact through expensive subscription fees and billable surveyor hours spent verifying data.
3. Vague Heads of Terms (HoTs): Causes legal back-and-forth that extends transaction timelines past six months and increases deal fall-through risk.
4. Service charge amp; CAM reconciliation in Excel: Limits the property portfolio scale per manager and exposes firms to tenant audit challenges.
5. Vetting unqualified leads in confidential sales: Consumes fee-earner hours on non-buyers while risking business disruption for operating assets

These are veeerrrrrrrrrrrrry very interesting.

And, theres work to be done.
