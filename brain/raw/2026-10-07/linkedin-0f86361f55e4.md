You should never tell someone to "go to hell.

Instead, you can tell them:

"I hope your investor asks for the cap table and it lives in three conflicting spreadsheets."
"I hope you find out the ESOP pool was never formally approved."
"I hope your first due diligence reveals that no one signed the IP assignment."
"I hope the Series A term sheet arrives and your annual filings are two years behind."


Very much possible. Very much painful.
