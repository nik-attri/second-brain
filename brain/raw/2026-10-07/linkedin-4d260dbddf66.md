Thank-you to everyone who joined us at our online event Let AI Agents Do The Work While You Sleep hosted by Jason Tan last night.
A key takeaway for me was about the importance of making sure people are at the centre of AI Adoption.
Successful AI adoption starts with the work, not the technology.  A key priority must be identifying repetitive tasks, mapping the workflow and automate only where it makes sense, and test thoroughly. Human judgement remains important, especially where there may be an impact to customers. 
The strongest message was that AI agents should amplify people, not replace them. Used well, they reduce burnout, create capacity to recognise and retain strong performers, and free leaders to focus on improvement and growth, so teams can spend more time solving problems and serving customers.
