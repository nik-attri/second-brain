I remember a time we rolled out a new security policy that basically put a brick wall in front of half our services. The intentions were good, but the aftermath was a nightmare of broken pipelines and frantic calls. It's easy to get tunnel vision trying to lock everything down, but real security comes from building it in, not bolting it on as an afterthought. You want compliance without crippling your operations, which usually means starting small, testing often, and iterating. Otherwise, you're just trading one problem for another.

#cloudengineering #awscloud #cicd #terraform #kubernetes #cloudsecurity #devsecops #observability

SECURE DEVOPS DONE RIGHT

▸ THE "BOLT-ON" BLUNDER
  • Security as a final checklist item
  • Disrupts existing workflows
  • Leads to last-minute fixes & outages

▸ INTEGRATE EARLY
  • Embed security checks in CI/CD
  • Use Terraform for secure infrastructure
  • Implement IAM least privilege from day one

▸ AUTOMATE & MONITOR
  • Automate compliance with Ansible
  • Monitor with Prometheus & Grafana
  • Use CloudWatch for anomaly detection

▸ OPERATIONALIZE SECURITY
  • Kubernetes (EKS) network policies
  • VPC segmentation & RDS encryption
  • ArgoCD for secure GitOps deployments

💡 Build security in, don't just add it on.
