We are Hiring!!

Role: Investment Portfolio Manager

Location: Lekki, Lagos

Industry: Investment Banking

Salary: Highly Competitive

Job Summary:

The Portfolio Manager will work to ensure effective management of assigned investment portfolios, developing and implementing investment strategies, identifying investment opportunities and delivering superior risk-adjusted returns in line with clients’ investment objectives, approved mandates and regulatory requirements.
The ideal candidate must demonstrate a genuine passion for investments and the ability to combine investment discipline with commercial awareness.

Duties and Responsibilities

•	Manage and monitor assigned portfolios in line with approved mandates and investment objectives.
•	Develop portfolio strategies and asset allocation recommendations.
•	Conduct in-depth analysis of equities, fixed-income securities, money-market instruments and other approved investment opportunities.
•	Monitor market developments, economic indicators, interest rates, inflation and other factors affecting investment performance.
•	Make investment recommendations and present proposals to the Investment Committee.
•	Monitor portfolio performance against agreed benchmarks and targets.
•	Identify portfolio risks and recommend appropriate mitigation measures.
•	Ensure compliance with investment mandates, internal policies and applicable SEC regulations.
•	Work closely with the dealing, risk, compliance and operations teams to ensure effective execution and monitoring of investment decisions.
•	Prepare periodic investment and portfolio performance reports for management and clients.
•	Contribute to the growth and retention of Assets Under Management (AUM) through strong investment performance and effective client engagement.
•	Maintain a strong pipeline of investment opportunities and continuously identify opportunities to enhance portfolio returns.

Requirements:

•	A Bachelor’s degree in Business/Economics, Finance, Accounting or any other related discipline.
•	Professional qualification such as CFA, ACA, ACCA, CIBN or equivalent will be an advantage.
•	Should be a SEC Sponsored Individual.
•	Strong understanding of the Nigeran financial and capital markets.
•	Demonstrable knowledge of portfolio construction, asset allocation, valuation and investment analysis.
•	Good understanding of risk management and regulatory requirements.
•	Excellent communication, presentation and report-writing skills.
•	Minimum of 5 years’ relevant experience in portfolio management, investment
