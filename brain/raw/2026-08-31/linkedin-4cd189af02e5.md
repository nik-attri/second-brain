Data centers are pulling insane power right now, but nobody’s talking about the wall they’re hitting: supply chain emissions.

We can build a data center worth hundreds of millions of dollars.

But figuring out the emissions sitting across its supply chain can still involve human inaccuracies and weeks of chasing suppliers.

That feels backwards.

Last month, we spent weeks helping map the footprint around a data center expansion. I assumed the math would be the hardest part. But it was a hidden load.
The electricity was easy to find. The messy part was everything around it - equipment, cooling, construction, vendors and the data sitting in different places.

That’s where the real Scope 3 problem showed up.

The infrastructure is scaling at AI speed.
But the way we audit what it takes to build it is still stuck in the 1990s (spreadsheets and lengthy reports).
So we hooked automated agents directly into their vendor data streams to audit the whole chain in real time and capital cleared immediately after.

The surprising part?
Once the footprint became clear, the financing conversation moved much faster.
