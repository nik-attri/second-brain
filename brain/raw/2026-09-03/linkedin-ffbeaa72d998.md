Your team is drowning in customer emails. So I built an AI agent to fix it.
 
Every growing business hits the same wall: support inboxes pile up, urgent emails get buried under routine ones, and replies take hours instead of minutes.
 
So I built Pepe AI, an AI email agent that reads, triages, and drafts responses to customer emails automatically.
 
In one pass, it:
 
Classifies the email type
Detects urgency
Analyzes sentiment
Drafts a professional, ready-to-send reply
 
No bloated multi-agent chain. No unnecessary complexity. Just a fast, single-pass pipeline, built with Python, FastAPI, and Groq, designed to actually hold up in production.
