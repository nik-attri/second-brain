Half a year year ago, Covenant Desk was two people, a shared doc, and a stubborn hunch.

Today I'm officially wearing two hats — Co-founder and Sales Lead.

We started in fintech, building a loan management system for lenders drowning in spreadsheets, legacy portals, and manual reconciliations. The response was clear: the pain isn't just software — it's the workflow around it.

So we widened the lens. Covenant Desk now ships custom software, automations, and SaaS for teams who need tools built around how they actually work — not the other way around.

Being on the sales seat as a founder is humbling. You can't hide behind a deck. Every "no" is a lesson, every "yes" is a promise you have to keep. It's forced me to listen harder than I ever did as a builder.

To our earliest clients — thank you for trusting us before the logo was dry.
