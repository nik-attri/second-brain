Well, I asked for podcast guests.

Apparently everyone decided it would be funnier if I had to do the first one by myself.

So here we are.
Episode 1 of Well, Damnit. is officially live.

The first topic:
Do More With Less Is Not a Strategy

Healthcare is under very real pressure to become more efficient. Labor is expensive. Resources are finite. Demand is not slowing down.

I don't have a problem with any of that.
I have a problem when “do more with less” becomes the entire operating plan.

Because if the work increases, the resources decrease, and absolutely nothing about the system changes...
the difference doesn't magically disappear.

People absorb it.
So in Episode 1, I get into the difference between a workforce problem and a work problem.

The expensive people doing cheap work.
The processes held together by one indispensable employee and a spreadsheet.
Why accountability can't become organizational duct tape for bad process design.
Why some burnout conversations completely miss the operational problem underneath them.

And the question I think more healthcare leaders should be asking:
If we were designing this work today, would we actually build it this way?

There are some serious points.
There is a fair amount of sarcasm.
There are a few words that probably won't make it into an executive town hall.

And since this is Episode 1, there is also me figuring out how the hell to host a podcast in real time.

But it's out.
And I'm pretty damn excited about that.

And yes, I am still looking for guests.
Physicians. APPs. Nurses. Healthcare executives. Operators. Recruiters. Locum leaders. Credentialing professionals. Technology people. Finance leaders.

You do not have to agree with me.
Honestly, that might make for a better episode.

Don't demand more from people until you've demanded less bullshit from the system.
