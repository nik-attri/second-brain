Why do multi-agent systems often collapse when integrated into complex industrial tech stacks?

We recently deployed a three-agent framework to automate regression testing, expecting a 40% efficiency gain. Instead, the agents created a feedback loop that overwhelmed our API endpoints within two hours.

The issue was not the individual agent logic, but the lack of a centralized orchestrator to manage state synchronization between independent nodes. When scaling AI workflows, autonomous agents without a shared context layer act like uncoordinated workers in a factory. They prioritize local task completion over system-wide stability.

How do you design state synchronization layers to prevent agent-based feedback loops in your own distributed systems?
