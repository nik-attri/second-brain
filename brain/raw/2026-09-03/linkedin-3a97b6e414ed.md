🚨  HIRING | Junior Accountant

AME Storage Solutions is looking for a skilled Junior Accountant to join our team.

Job description:
Junior Accountant
Location: Ajman, UAE
Employment Type: Full-time
Department: Accounts
Reports To: Finance Manager

Experience: 0-1 years
Gender: Female preferred
Languages: English & Hindi (Preferred)
Immediate Joiners Preferred

Job Summary
We are looking for a detail-oriented and organized Junior Accountant to support our finance and accounting operations. The successful candidate will assist with day-to-day accounting activities, maintain accurate financial records, process invoices, receivables & payables, bank reconciliations, support the finance team with reporting.

Key Responsibilities:
Assist with day-to-day accounting and finance activities.
Maintain daily sales report.
Record and maintain accurate financial transactions in the accounting system.
Prepare, verify, receipts, payments, and expense claims.
Assist with accounts payable and accounts receivable activities.
Perform bank reconciliations.
Maintain and organize financial documents and records.
Assist with preparation of monthly financial reports.

Requirements:
1) Bachelor's degree or diploma in Accounting, Finance, Commerce, or a related field.
2) Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Office.
3) Ability to work independently as well as part of a team.

Preferred Skills:
Knowledge of accounts payable and accounts receivable processes.
Experience with bank and account reconciliations.
Basic understanding of VAT/tax requirements is an advantage.
Strong Excel skills, including formulas and data management.

To Apply: Interested candidates can apply through indeed or email your resume and cover letter to hr@ameracking.ae
