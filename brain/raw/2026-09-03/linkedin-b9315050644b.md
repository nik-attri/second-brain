DocuWare vs. “We’re already automated.”

“We’re already automated.”
I hear this quite often.
And I understand why.

Businesses have invested heavily in technology and automation. They have ERP systems, CRM systems, HR platforms, accounting software and specialist applications.

So it can feel like:
“We're automated already.”

But here's the questions I would ask:

What happens between the systems?
What happens when an invoice arrives by email?
What happens when someone needs to approve something?
What happens when information has to move from one system to another?
What happens when someone has to search for a supporting document?
What happens when an employee needs to submit a form?
What happens when an approval is delayed?
What happens when someone has to send a reminder because the next step hasn't happened?

You can have highly sophisticated systems throughout a business...
and still have people holding the process together manually.

That's because automation often exists inside individual systems, while the work that connects people, documents and systems can remain manual.

This is where DocuWare can add value.

It helps connect documents, information, people and workflows — automating processes that can cross departments and work alongside the systems already in place.

So instead of asking:
“Are we automated?”

perhaps the better question is:
“Where are our people still doing work that our systems could be doing for them?”

That question can uncover some very interesting opportunities.
