👀 What if you could brief an entire creative team… with just one message? 

One of the most rewarding parts of building software is seeing an idea💡 go from something on a screen to something people can actually create with.✨ 

Meet Creative Studio - inside Galleri5 (now, part of Collective Artists Network) AI Studio.

Instead of jumping between AI tools, writing endless prompts, choosing models and manually organising generations, you can simply describe what you want.

“Create images of goofy crocodile playing international football.”

And Creative Studio takes it from there.
It can reason about the task, choose the right tools/models, generate multiple variations, organise the outputs, and keep everything together in one creative workspace.

One idea → multiple creative directions → one workspace.

And this is just the beginning.

We’re building AI not to replace the creative process, but to remove the friction between an idea and bringing it to life.

You bring the idea. Let the studio do the heavy lifting.

Try it: aistudio.galleri5.com

And yes, the video in this post? It was created using Creative Studio itself. 🎬
