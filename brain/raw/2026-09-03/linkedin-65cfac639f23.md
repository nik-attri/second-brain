I bought this car to do 1 "big shop"! ... OK and more BUT ... 

After 2 weeks of online searching and 10 days (which felt like a year) of actually looking ...  I bought the car I couldn't afford and which was over budget - so that I can get the retreat shopping in one go next week! (I know.. it sounds ridiculous .. but) 

It got me thinking, as I head to Birmingham for the ECA 2-day sales retreat with Matt Elwell, about those annoying eternal questions:

"What do your ideal clients want? Why do they buy?"

I've realised something is missing it's actually "Why do they buy.... NOW"

OK - so there WERE other reasons I bought - after living car-free for 4 months the spreadsheet told me I wasn't saving much money on public transport and hiring when needed, and I was missing out on both spontaneous excursions (by the time I'd got to an affordable train I'd have a 60 min turn around) and enjoyable experiences (because what would have taken 25mins in a car would have taken 2hrs, 2 trains and a taxi!) 

BUT the reason I finally bought when I did (I already had the hire car booked for next week and was going to take the cat to my mum's by train) was the little thing - the shopping. 

My caterer has pulled out for the retreat that starts a week tomorrow - so I'm doing the shopping and the cooking and the prep in advance and I couldn't face the idea of all the time wasted having to do 2 trips to the supermarket and schlepping everything back up the hill.

Yes I could've done it with a taxi. But I am also over taxis.

I don't know what you're waiting for to buy,  what you've been contemplating (or who you're waiting for to buy off you ... )

But the question is "What will this do for you NOW?"

If right now you could really do with getting the hell out of your life, to step back from 'everything' that's going on because you can't see the wood for the trees; if you want the time and space to get away from the persistent stuckness of going round in circles about the next step for your work, or the pressure of knowing there's something "just the other side" of whatever 'this' that is going on for you and creating resistance and exasperation 

Maybe your NOW is my 7-Day Resonate YOU retreat
