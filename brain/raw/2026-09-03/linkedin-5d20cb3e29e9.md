🚨 WE ARE HIRING – ASSISTANT MANAGER IE

Agility Textile (Pvt.) Ltd. is looking for a dynamic and experienced professional for the following position:

Assistant Manager – Industrial Engineering (IE)

Location: Lahore

Key Responsibilities:

Conduct Time & Motion Studies and calculate SMV/SAM.
Prepare operation breakdowns, line layouts and capacity plans.
Manage line balancing and manpower utilization.
Monitor daily production, efficiency and productivity.
Identify bottlenecks and implement productivity improvement plans.
Drive Lean Manufacturing, Kaizen, 5S and continuous improvement initiatives.
Coordinate with Production, Planning and Quality departments.
Prepare daily, weekly and monthly IE reports and performance analysis.
Support new style development and production planning.

Requirements:

BS/BSc in Industrial Engineering, Textile Engineering or relevant field.
3–5 years of relevant experience in apparel/knitwear manufacturing.
Strong knowledge of SMV/SAM, Time Study, Method Study, Line Balancing and Capacity Planning.
Good command of MS Excel and IE reporting.
Strong analytical, communication and leadership skills.
Candidates with hands-on knitwear/apparel IE experience will be preferred.

laeeq@agilitytex.com.
