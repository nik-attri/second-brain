Why do students waste hours formatting study notes instead of actually studying? 📚⏱️ Following up on my Class 1 Founder Roadmap for StudyStack AI, I took on Class 2s challenge for BuildX by Bosscoder School of Technology: The Problem Hunt. Before writing code or building our WhatsApp MVP, I investigated why active recall fails right before exams: 🔍 01. The Problem Students spend up to 3 hours manually turning dense notes into flashcards on apps like Anki or Quizlet, leaving them mentally drained before retrieval practice even begins. 👥 02. The User High school and college students managing 100+ pages of handwritten notes and PDF slides before major exams. 💡 03. Key Research Insight After interviewing 3 classmates, the biggest surprise was that students WANT to test themselves, but give up due to the formatting tax. If a revision deck isnt ready in under 30 seconds where they already hang out (like WhatsApp), they resort to passive PDF skimming. 🧬 04. Root Cause (5 Whys) Current study platforms act as static destination apps rather than instant extraction tools. They force students to act as manual data processors before learning can start. 🔄 05. Reframing the Problem ❌ Old Question: How can we build a faster flashcard app? ✅ Better Question: How might we eliminate content creation time entirely so students can start testing takes place within seconds of snapping a photo of their notes?
