You have a free automation tool sitting inside Airtable right now. Most people close the tab and go back to doing things manually.

Airtable's built-in automation layer can handle more than you'd expect before you connect a single external tool. Status changes that trigger email notifications. Form submissions that auto-tag records, write in dates, and send acknowledgement emails. Linked records that create themselves when conditions are met. Real workflows. Already available. No extra cost.

The setup is straightforward once you know where to look: pick a trigger, configure an action, test it, turn it on. Your first automation could be live in under fifteen minutes. And yes, there are limits to what the native layer can do, so we cover those honestly too, because the goal is to help you use what you already have before reaching for something more complicated.
