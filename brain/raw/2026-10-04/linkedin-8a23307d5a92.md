Managing 10,000+ engineering drawings across 5 disciplines shouldn’t feel like living in Excel spreadsheet hell. 
In major EPC and infrastructure projects, a single outdated drawing reaching the construction site can cost hundreds of thousands in rework.
Yet, most project teams still struggle with:
❌ Version chaos (Was it Rev C or Rev D approved yesterday?)
❌ Bottlenecked reviews sitting in someone's inbox with zero SLA tracking
❌ Manual transmittals assembled by copy-pasting filenames
❌ Disconnected contractor RFIs buried in email threads
We built a modern, high-density Engineering Document Management System (DMS) built specifically for fast-paced project delivery.
Here is what it changes on Day 1:
🎯 Master Document List (MDL) Control: Real-time planned vs. actual milestone tracking with automatic overdue alerts.
🔒 Strict Revision Locking: Prevent simultaneous overwrite conflicts with cryptographic audit trails and revision histories.
📦 1-Click Transmittal Packages: Generate formal transmittal packages and auto-branded PDFs ready for client dispatch.
⚡ Automated Escalation Matrix: Approvals that breach SLA deadlines automatically escalate to the next level of authority (DOA).
💬 Integrated RFI Engine: Design queries linked directly to drawings with SLA countdowns and reminder triggers.
☁️ Hybrid Storage Freedom: Store natively on your on-premise servers, Microsoft SharePoint (Graph API), or Google Drive.
Built with an ultra-clean, high-density UI designed for engineers who need fast data, zero clutter, and instantaneous lookups.
