We run an AI agent that books LTL freight for our customers at freightandlogistics.ai. Here's something we learned building it.

AI will build anything you ask for. What it won't do is tell you what you should be asking for. We were manually checking our agent's chat logs before realizing the whole thing should just run nightly and ask us yes or no. Now we ask it every time: what should this automate that we haven't thought of?
