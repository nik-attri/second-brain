I used to work on campaigns running on a Vici-dial.

When we started Right Space, we said hell no and built our own. On paper, the old way looked cheap. Then we opened the spreadsheet. It wasn’t.

After that, I wrote about STIR/SHAKEN: why your prospect’s phone judges you before you say hello, and how A-level attestation helps your call get through.

But I skipped the uncomfortable part. A-level gets you to the door. It does not prove Dave works there.

If the list is dead, recycled, wrong title, wrong company, wrong timezone, you do not have a sales campaign. You have a very trustworthy way to interrupt strangers.

I have seen teams buy 100,000 numbers for $500 and call it pipeline. That is not pipeline. That is a prayer with area codes.

The reason I am talking about the list now is FullEnrich.

Not because they sell magic numbers. Because they remove the bad ones before they reach you: 20+ sources, waterfall enrichment, and over 30% of the data they buy fails verification and never gets delivered.

That changed the first five seconds of the call.

Less “Am I speaking to John?”
More “Hi John.”
And if John says he is not John, I will respectfully tell him, "Yeah, you are" (kidding).
