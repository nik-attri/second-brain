Recruitment Invitation | Batch 2023–2027

The Placement Cell, Sambalpur University Institute of Information Technology (SUIIT), Burla, Sambalpur, invites organizations and recruitment teams to participate in our Campus Recruitment Drive for the Batch 2023–2027.

We offer access to a diverse pool of students from CSE, CSE-AI/ML, CSE-ICS, ECE, EEE, and MCA, with academic and practical exposure across a range of technology and business domains.
