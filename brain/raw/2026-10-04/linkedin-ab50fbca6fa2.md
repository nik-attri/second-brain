Building a motorcycle is one challenge. Building everything around it is another.

At Raptee.HV, the product extends from the vehicle to the rider app, connected vehicle software and services, internal operations, customer experience, and everything our teams use behind the scenes.Making all of these pieces work together is a journey of its own.

Last week, I got to share a little bit of that journey on the Zoholics Events stage.

I spoke about how we use different Zoho services across customer touchpoints and internal operations, and how we connect them into one digital stack where everything talks to each other.What makes it even more interesting is that this stack doesnt stop at the office. Some of our motorcycle systems also interact with Catalyst by Zoho, creating an interesting bridge between the physical product and the digital ecosystem.

One thing I particularly enjoyed at Zoholics was seeing how Zoho is approaching the AI wave focusing on meaningful features that genuinely improve products and workflows, rather than simply adding AI everywhere to keep up with the competition.

Then came a thought from Sridhar that stayed with me
What will humans do?
The answer: An infinite number of things.

I completely agree.
Computers changed how we work.
The internet changed how we connect.
Digital payments changed how we transact.
AI is changing what we can create. With every major technology shift, there has been a fear of what it might replace. But eventually, we found new things to build, new problems to solve, and better tools to solve them with.

I think AI will be much the same. It will change what we do. It will automate some things, accelerate others, and make us rethink many roles.
But it can also give us something incredibly valuable like More clarity. More leverage. More time to think. And more room to innovate.

Because the question isnt only What will AI do? It is also What will humans choose to do with it.
