🔥 If ABN AMRO plans to reduce around 20% of its workforce by 2028, how much manual back-office work can we still defend in the Netherlands?

The Next Web reports that ABN AMRO plans this reduction as automation takes over back-office tasks.

That is not a story about machines replacing every employee. It is a signal to audit the work itself.

🔧 In many teams, the most obvious automation targets are predictable operational loops:
- moving data between systems
- checking documents against fixed rules
- routing requests and status updates
- preparing repeatable internal reports

For a small business, the question is not: “Who can we remove?”
It is: “Which manual steps create no customer value — and what can our people do once those steps disappear?”

✅ My practical view: automate the routine, then redeploy capacity into exceptions, customer conversations, process improvement, and ownership. Those are the skills that become insurance when workflows change.

For back-office staff and manual operations teams, waiting is the risky option. Learn the process end to end. Learn to work with automation. Become the person who can improve the system, not only operate it.

For SMB owners, do not wait until a competitor automates first.

❓ Which manual process would you audit first?
