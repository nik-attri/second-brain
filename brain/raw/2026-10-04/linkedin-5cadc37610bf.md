Sunday morning reflections…..I’ve seen a lot of posts from HubSpot partners on LinkedIn about the move towards seats, credits and consumption-based pricing, and I wanted to share my perspective. Mostly because I think theres a few parts of the story missing. I understand some fear but I genuinely think there’s a huge opportunity here for partners to build even more trust and value with customers. 

We all need to move away from thinking about value being purely through the lens of the initial transaction and the implementation. In the new world I think of that first sale as the pebble hitting the water.

The real opportunity is in the ripples. A customer starts with the outcome they need. Maybe it’s generating demand, improving conversion, increasing productivity, building a CRM or getting more from AI. A HubSpot partner helps them activate the software needed at that point in time and to achieve that FIRST outcome.

But successful businesses don’t have ONE outcome. Success creates the next challenge. And the next opportunity. Generating demand  moves to improving conversion, which moves to improved onboarding, then increase retention, that drives expansion, perhaps the introduction of new agents that requires redesigning another process. Outcomes happen in sequence and out of sequence. 

Every ripple can create a new services opportunity. There’s another important shift here too, efficiency becomes part of the service. In a world of credits and consumption, “like-for-like” comparisons become much harder. The goal shouldn’t be for a customer to consume as much as possible. It should be to get the greatest possible return from what they consume. Like-for-like in my opinion doesn’t mention the partner that restructured the customer’s data, added context or  redesigned their process to save them credit spend on individual sales and instead increased the number of sales for the same amount……meaning that customer stayed and expand with Hubspot. 

The better we understand a customer’s data, processes, context and goals, the better we can help them reduce waste, optimise agents and workflows, and focus their investment on what actually delivers an outcome. What if a customer had a service budgets behind all of that inefficiency? 

Hubspot partners will uncover this from conversations, trust and genuinely understanding a customer’s business. Our partners are very good at talking! Trust me 🤣

I and partners know AI transformation creates human friction. Technology can automate a process. It can’t automatically bring an organisation with it. That requires humans who understand humans. Also something partners are good at. 

So when I look at the changes, I don’t see the services opportunity shrinking. I see it evolving. And I see the winners being our jointed customers. The initial sale is the pebble. The opportunity for HubSpot partners is in the ripples.
