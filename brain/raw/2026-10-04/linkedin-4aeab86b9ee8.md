Today was one of those days that reminded me exactly why Tertulia exists.

Not just for coffee. Not just for pastries. But for community.

Seeing people show up, kids having fun, families connecting, familiar faces returning, and new ones finding us — THAT is the part of small business nobody can fully capture in a spreadsheet.

Building something outside of your 9–5 teaches you a lot. About leadership. About resilience. About people. About yourself.

And yes, I’m incredibly proud of it.

I’m proud to talk about it.
I’m proud to share it.
I’m proud that my kids get to watch me build something rooted in community and belonging.

So here it is, proudly and publicly: I own a small business. It’s hard as hell. It’s imperfect. And today, it was absolutely worth it.

Take the screenshot. 😉

Life is what happens entre café y café. ☕️
