🎓 Happy to share a new learning milestone!
I’m excited to share that I’ve successfully completed the AI Tools & ChatGPT Workshop by Be10X and received my Certificate of Completion.
As a QA Tester, my key takeaway from this workshop was understanding how AI can support and improve different stages of the Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC). AI is not just about content generation—it can be a powerful assistant in our day-to-day QA activities.
During the workshop, I explored several AI tools and looked at how they can be applied from a QA & Software Testing perspective:
💻 Claude — Requirement analysis, coding assistance, automation script reviews, debugging, test scenario generation, and understanding technical documentation.
🤖 ChatGPT — Requirement analysis, test case generation, edge-case identification, bug reporting, API testing assistance, automation support, and documentation.
📚 NotebookLM — Understanding large requirement documents, summarizing specifications, extracting key information, and preparing testing notes.
🎨 Google Stitch — Creating UI prototypes, identifying UI/UX testing scenarios, and planning UI testing before development is fully completed.
🧠 Google AI Studio — Exploring Gemini models, prompt testing, API-based AI workflows, and understanding how AI-powered features can be tested.
🎙️ Wispr Flow — Capturing exploratory testing observations, bug reproduction steps, meeting notes, and QA updates using voice-to-text.
📊 Julius AI — Analyzing test execution data, defect trends, pass/fail statistics, and creating visual reports from QA data.
🎬 Higgsfield — Generating images and videos that can be used as sample test data for media-related application workflows.
🔊 ElevenLabs — Generating AI voice/audio for testing applications involving speech, audio, accessibility, and multilingual voice workflows.
🤖 AI Agent Marketplaces — Exploring specialized AI agents and understanding how agent-based workflows can help automate repetitive QA activities.
💡 My biggest takeaway:
AI is not a replacement for the QA mindset—it is an accelerator. 🚀
By using AI effectively, QA professionals can analyze requirements faster, identify more test scenarios, improve test coverage, assist with automation, analyze failures, generate test data, reduce repetitive work, and focus more on delivering better product quality.
🙌 A big thank you to Be10x for organizing this wonderful and insightful workshop and providing practical exposure to various AI tools.
I’m looking forward to applying these learnings in my QA & Automation Testing journey and continuing to explore how AI can make software testing smarter, faster, and more efficient. 🚀
Keep Learning. Keep Exploring. Keep Improving. 📚
