SaaS is undergoing its biggest paradigm shift since moving to the Cloud: The move from "Software" to "Workflows."

For the last 15 years, the B2B SaaS model has been simple:

Build a software tool with a nice UI.
Charge a monthly fee per user seat.
Require humans to log in, fill out forms, click buttons, and manage the data manually.

In this legacy model, software was just an efficient filing cabinet. The human still had to do 90% of the cognitive work.

Now, we are entering the era of Agentic SaaS (Service-as-a-Software).

The shift isn't just about adding a chatbot to your dashboard. It is a fundamental evolution in how value is delivered:
🔹 Old SaaS (Seat-Based): You buy a tool ➔ Your team logs in ➔ Humans do the work. 
🔹 Agentic SaaS (Outcome-Based): You define the objective ➔ Autonomous AI Agents execute the workflow, handle edge cases, and maintain data integrity ➔ Humans simply review and approve high-stakes outcomes.

Instead of paying for software that requires your team to spend hours doing manual data entry, you are deploying intelligent agents that work for you in the background.

This is why traditional seat-based pricing is dying. When an AI Agent does the work of 5 manual workflows, charging "per seat" makes no sense. The future of SaaS pricing is Outcome-Based and Value-Based.

We aren't just building faster tools anymore. We are building autonomous systems that handle the heavy lifting while keeping humans in control of strategy and trust.
The companies that master this transition won't just win their category—they will redefine it.
