Levanta is hiring a GTM Engineer (Remote)

What You'll Do:
Outbound Infrastructure
Build and maintain automated lead generation systems: enrichment pipelines, ICP scoring models, and sequencing logic
Own email deliverability infrastructure: domain setup, warm-up, authentication, inbox rotation, and ongoing monitoring
Design personalization and dynamic copy logic that drives reply rates, not just sends

Revenue Automation
Build automated workflows connecting our GTM stack (CRM, sequencers, data sources, and enrichment tools) via APIs, webhooks, and automation platforms
Identify and operationalize buying signals to trigger outbound at the right moment for the right contact
Maintain HubSpot as the source of truth: clean data, meaningful properties, functional workflows

Tooling & Operations
Evaluate and pilot new AI and GTM tools, integrating what works into the core stack
Own reporting on pipeline influenced by automated systems — meetings booked, sequences launched, lead-to-opportunity conversion
Partner with AEs and SDRs to identify workflow gaps, broken handoffs, and opportunities to automate manual work

What We're Looking For:

This role weighs demonstrated skills over resume credentials. We'll ask for a portfolio and evaluate hands-on ability during the interview process.

1. Proven experience in GTM engineering, RevOps, growth engineering, or a technical demand gen role, or a sales background where you've built your own systems
2. Hands-on experience building data enrichment workflows and multi-step automation in production
3. Proficiency with CRM platforms (we use HubSpot) and email automation tooling
4. Comfortable making API calls, working with webhooks, and connecting tools via no-code/low-code automation platforms
5. Commercial instinct. You think in pipeline generated and demos booked, not automations shipped
6. Able to move fast, work autonomously, and communicate clearly with non-technical stakeholders
