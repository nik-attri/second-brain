🔗 Ever notice how a handful of disconnected tools can turn a smooth workflow into a daily headache?  
When sales, inventory, and customer support live in separate silos, a single data entry error can ripple through the entire organization.  
Take a mid‑size retailer that uses an e‑commerce platform, a separate ERP for stock, and a marketing automation tool. Orders placed online never update inventory in real time, and the marketing system still sends out promotions for out‑of‑stock items. The result? Frustrated customers, lost sales, and a team drowning in manual spreadsheets.  

Key pain points in this scenario are:  
• Data mismatches between systems  
• Duplicate work for staff  
• Inability to get a unified view of business health  

A practical way to break the cycle is to design a set of lightweight, well‑documented APIs that let each system read and write only the data it needs. Combine that with a middleware layer—think Zapier‑style logic or a custom orchestration service—to schedule syncs, validate data, and surface errors in a single dashboard.  

Problem technology ki nahi, workflow ki hoti hai.  
When the integration is built around the business process, the tools stop fighting each other and start collaborating. That means fewer mistakes, less manual effort, and a clearer picture for decision‑makers.
