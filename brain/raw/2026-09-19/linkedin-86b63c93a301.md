Budget season always cracks me up.

All year long:

“We can probably push that to next year.”

Then September hits and suddenly everybody is staring at a spreadsheet like:

“Alright… what the hell did we push to next year?” 😂

Painting. Repairs. Exterior work. Maintenance. Capital projects.

All the stuff that was easy to ignore when the sun was shining and nothing was actively falling apart.

Here’s what I’ve learned:

A project is a whole lot easier to deal with when it’s a line item in a budget than when it turns into an emergency.

You don’t have to be ready to do the work tomorrow.

Sometimes the smartest move is just getting somebody out there, figuring out what actually needs done, putting real numbers to it, and deciding where it fits.

No pressure. No “sign today” nonsense.

Just figure out what you’re dealing with before it figures it out for you.

So I’m curious:

What’s the project at your property that everybody knows needs done…

…but somehow keeps surviving another budget meeting? 😂
