I’ve spent a lot of time trying to find a problem worth solving. I keep looking for ideas SaaS, boring businesses, developer tools, enterprise problems, infrastructure, anything that might turn into something real. I read about founders who started by building something they found interesting not because they were trying to come up with a startup. Then I look at myself and realize I’m doing the opposite. I’m trying to find the idea first. And honestly it’s exhausting. When you spend enough time looking for ideas everything starts looking like a business. A spreadsheet becomes a SaaS product. Some annoying manual work becomes an automation tool. A boring industry becomes a startup opportunity. But most of the time I’m not really finding problems. I’m looking for problems because I want to build a startup. That’s what makes it so frustrating. The more I search the more confused I get. Maybe I should search harder. Maybe I should stop searching. I don’t know. Maybe good ideas come from actually doing things. You build something because you’re interested in it you use it you run into problems and you fix them. Then you run into more problems. After a while you start seeing things other people don’t. Maybe that’s where good ideas come from not from sitting around asking “What should I build?” but from building something and eventually asking “Why the hell is this so difficult?”
