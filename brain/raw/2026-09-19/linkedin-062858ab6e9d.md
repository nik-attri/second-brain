Everyone is talking about what AI can do.
But I’m more interested in what AI is actually doing inside a real business.
Here’s a simple example from our agency.
We built an AI automation that sends a daily performance report directly into each client’s WhatsApp group — at the same time every day.
No one has to manually prepare the report.
Every morning, the client gets:
→ Spend
→ Leads
→ CPL
→ Landing page conversion rate
And this solves a surprisingly big problem.
Clients stay updated without asking the team.
Owners and managers can quickly see what’s happening across accounts without opening complex dashboards.
And there’s another interesting benefit 😆 :
When performance is visible to everyone in the group, accountability naturally increases.
If a media buyer knows the numbers are being reported every day, there’s a much stronger reason to stay on top of the KPIs.
This is what I mean when I talk about using AI in business.
Not just collecting information about AI.
Actually putting it into workflows that save time, improve transparency, and help teams perform better.
Sometimes, a small automation can solve a very big operational problem.
