📢 We’re Hiring | Junior Accountant – Arrmeelon Uniforms

Arrmeelon Uniforms is looking for a Junior Accountant to join our finance & accounts team at our Bhiwandi office.
We are looking for someone who is detail-oriented, comfortable with numbers, understands accounting fundamentals, and is willing to take responsibility for day-to-day accounting activities.

💼 Role: Junior Accountant
📍 Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra
🕐 Employment: Full-time
Key Responsibilities
• Purchase invoice accounting and data entry
• Sales invoicing and billing entries
• Journal entries and ledger maintenance
• Bank reconciliation and month-end reconciliation
• Debtor reconciliation, set-offs and follow-ups
• Preparation of MIS reports
• GST-related accounting and support for GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B
• TDS-related accounting and compliance support
• Assist in audit and statutory compliance requirements
• Maintain proper accounting records and documentation
• Coordinate with internal teams for accounting-related matters

🎯 What we're looking for
✅ Graduate in Commerce / B.Com preferred
✅ 1–3 years of experience in accounting preferred
✅ Good understanding of basic accounting concepts
✅ Working knowledge of GST & TDS
✅ Good knowledge of MS Excel
✅ Experience with accounting software/Tally is preferred
✅ Good attention to detail
✅ Strong follow-up and reconciliation skills
✅ Good communication and willingness to learn
✅ Experience in a manufacturing/trading environment will be an advantage

About Us
Arrmeelon Uniforms is a 35+ year-old textile and uniform fabrics business, specializing in school, corporate and institutional uniform fabrics.
We work with customers and distributors across India and operate in a fast-paced manufacturing/trading environment.
This is an opportunity for someone who wants to build strong practical experience across accounting, GST, TDS, MIS, reconciliations and audit-related work.

Interested candidates can apply through LinkedIn or send their CV via DM.
