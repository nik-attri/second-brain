A year ago, I told someone I felt threatened by AI. That I might lose my job to it.
Today, I'm sitting in a coffee shop — laptop open, Claude on screen — getting more done in a morning than I used to in a week.
It didn't replace me. It replaced the version of me that was drowning in the wrong work — manual documentation, repetitive tasks, hours spent figuring out where to even start.
Now I build faster. I think clearer. And I show up better for the people I work with.
A year makes a big difference when you stop being afraid and start being curious.
Happy weekend everyone. 😄
