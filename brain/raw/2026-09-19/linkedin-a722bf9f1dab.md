If you have to ask for a report to see your current exposure, you are already behind.

In trading, speed is more than just execution. It is awareness. 

If answering "what are we exposed to right now?" requires a manual pull or a wait, you do not have a reporting delay—you have a visibility crisis. 

Waiting for a spreadsheet to load while the market moves is a special kind of hell. Real-time clarity is the floor, not the ceiling. 

If your visibility isn't instant, your risk is infinite. How long is your blind spot?
