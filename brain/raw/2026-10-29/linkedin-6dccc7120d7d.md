WE ARE HIRING: Import Officer  📦✈️
📍 Location: Ikeja, Lagos
🏢 Department: Procurement & Supply Chain
👤 Reports To: Supply Chain / Procurement Manager
Key Responsibilities
 Import Operations: Manage the entire import cycle from purchase orders to final delivery, resolving logistics delays proactively.
 Compliance & Documentation: Prepare and manage trade documents (PAAR, SONCAP, Bills of Lading, Form M, Customs Clearance, Permits*).
 Customs & Regulatory Liaison:Coordinate with customs agents, shipping lines, and regulatory bodies to ensure smooth cargo clearance.
 Cost Control:Monitor duties, taxes, demurrage, and freight charges to minimize costs and optimize lead times.
 Vendor & Inventory Management:Partner with international suppliers, freight forwarders, and warehouse teams for timely material receipt.
Qualifications & Requirements
 Education: B.Sc / HND in Supply Chain, Logistics, Business Administration, Economics, or related fields.
 Experience:3–5 years handling import documentation, customs clearance, and logistics in Nigeria (Manufacturing, FMCG, or Commodities experience preferred).
 Skills:Strong knowledge of Incoterms, customs duty calculations, ERP systems, and advanced Excel.
