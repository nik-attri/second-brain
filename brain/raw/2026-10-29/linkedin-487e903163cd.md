I genuinely thought logistics was mostly about moving things from point A to point B. 

Then I worked at a sunglasses brand. 

I was doing customer service + logistics, so at a very basic level, I was living through maybe 10% of the hell a logistics coordinator deals with every day. 

Where’s this order?
Follow up with the lab
Can you check with the carrier?
Can you update the customer by email and whatsapp pls?
Yes I'm sure, we got robbed, the delivery guy isn’t answering, do we have another one in the warehouse?

Then I joined Cartage and started connecting the dots. 

Someone has to read the email, get the quote, compare carriers, book the load, track it, follow up, fix the document, check the invoice… Over and over AND OVER again. 

Logistics teams already have TONS of emails, dashboards, TMSs, spreadsheets and carrier portals. Someone still has to do something with all of it. 

That’s when Wilson finally clicked for me. 

He's not “another logistics software.” 

More like: what if some of that work could just get done? 

Still learning a lot about this industry, but what’s your take on AI in logistics?
