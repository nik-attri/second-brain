🔀 What if your AI knew exactly which specialist should handle every customer request?

Customer support isn't one-size-fits-all. A technical issue, billing question, and subscription inquiry require different expertise.

🚀 Meet Dynamic Router — a Multi-Agent Workflow template in Nymrio!

Instead of sending every request through the same sequence, Dynamic Router analyzes the user's intent and directs it to the most appropriate AI agent.

🎯 Real-World Example: Intelligent Customer Support

In our video, we build a support workflow with one routing agent and three specialists:

🧭 1. Customer Support Router

Identifies the request category and selects the right specialist.

🛠️ 2. Technical Support Specialist

Investigates API errors, authentication issues, and integration problems, providing troubleshooting guidance.

💳 3. Billing Support Specialist

Explains subscription charges, invoices, and billing questions while highlighting issues requiring human review.

📈 4. Sales & Subscription Advisor

Analyzes team requirements and recommends suitable subscription options and next steps.

🔄 How does Dynamic Router work?

1️⃣ A customer submits a question.
2️⃣ The Router classifies the request.
3️⃣ The matching specialist handles it.
4️⃣ The specialist's response is returned to the customer.

📊 What happens in our demo?

We test three distinct requests in the Nymrio Playground:

🔧 Technical: API calls suddenly return 401 Unauthorized → routed to Technical Support.

💳 Billing: A customer questions a charge after upgrading → routed to Billing Support.

🚀 Sales: A team of 20 developers wants to integrate AI agents → routed to Sales & Subscription.

The execution trace shows the routing decision and specialist response for each scenario — without running unrelated agents.

💡 Why use Dynamic Router workflows?

✅ Match requests to the right expertise.
✅ Handle multiple customer needs through one entry point.
✅ Avoid unnecessary workflow steps.
✅ Make routing decisions visible and testable.
✅ Build scalable, specialized AI support experiences.
