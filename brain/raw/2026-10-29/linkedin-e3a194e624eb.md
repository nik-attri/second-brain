We're Hiring – For a Leading Sri Lanka-based 4-Star Hotel in Dhaka! 📢

📌 Position: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)  

💰 Salary: BDT 200,000 + Other Benefits  

📍 Job Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh  

📅 Hiring Status: Immediate Hiring

A reputed Sri Lanka-based 4-star hotel in Dhaka is looking for an accomplished and commercially driven Chief Financial Officer (CFO) to lead its financial strategy, governance, and operations.

Key Responsibilities:

- Lead the overall finance function, including accounting, budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, and reporting.

- Ensure accurate and timely financial statements, management reports, and performance analysis.

- Manage cash flow, working capital, revenue, operating expenses, and financial risks.

- Establish and strengthen internal controls, financial policies, and procedures.

- Ensure compliance with Bangladesh's applicable tax, VAT, statutory, audit, and regulatory requirements.

- Advise the General Manager and department heads on financial performance, cost optimization, and strategic business decisions.

- Evaluate investment proposals and contribute to long-term business planning and sustainable growth.

- Lead, mentor, and develop the finance team.

- Present financial results, forecasts, and strategic recommendations to senior management and ownership.

Education & Experience:

- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline from a recognized university.

- Professional accounting qualification such as CA, ACCA, CMA, CFA, or CPA. Candidates must clearly mention their qualification and membership status.

- 10–15 years of progressive finance experience, including substantial experience as a Finance Director or in a comparable senior finance leadership role.

- Experience in Bangladesh, internationally branded hotels, or multi-property operations will be an added advantage.

- Strong expertise in financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, cash flow, working capital management, and internal controls.

- Practical knowledge of Bangladesh's tax, VAT, company, and statutory reporting requirements, along with familiarity with IFRS.

- Excellent communication skills in English.
