Career Opportunity: Pilot Manager (COO)! 

Our strategic partner is a multinational technology company specializing in automated industrial equipment and integrated solutions for industrial and environmental applications. With an established international presence, the company is expanding its operations in Vietnam and seeking qualified professionals to support its local operations and long-term growth. The company is currently seeking a Pilot Manager (COO) to take overall responsibility for planning and delivering a Deposit Return System (DRS) pilot in Phu Quoc in consultation with the company Director. 

Career Growth Opportunity: The Pilot Manager will have the opportunity to progress to a Country Manager role, subject to the successful implementation of the pilot project and individual performance. 

Location: Phu Quoc, Vietnam (Hybrid) 

Key responsibilities include: 

✔️ Take overall responsibility for planning and implementing the DRS pilot in consultation with the company Director. 
✔️ Lead local project activities, including staffing and stakeholder relations. 
✔️ Lead negotiations with material buyers and coordinate technical and business matters with internal teams. 
✔️ Establish the Central Consolidation Hub and identify and negotiate with redemption centers. 
✔️ Collaborate with key stakeholders, local authorities, the development partner, and internal Public Affairs, Marketing, and Legal teams. 

If you have at least fifteen years of experience in business management and senior leadership, strong P&L understanding, and the ability to lead negotiations and take ownership of complex operations, we welcome your application at minh@peoplewise.vn, thanh.nguyen@peoplewise.vn or application@peoplewise.vn.
