Thursday Thought: What if your restaurant’s menu knew what the weather was doing? 

The weather influences what customers feel like eating and drinking. So why do so many restaurants show exactly the same promotions, whatever the weather?

Imagine walking into a restaurant on a cold, rainy afternoon and seeing a steaming hot chocolate, a warming soup or a freshly prepared hot meal featured on the digital menu boards.

Now imagine the same restaurant on a hot summer’s day, automatically promoting iced coffees, cold drinks, salads and refreshing desserts.

Same restaurant. Same screens. Smarter marketing.

With weather-responsive digital signage, restaurants can make their promotions more relevant to the moment.
When it’s hot: Promote cold drinks, ice creams, salads and refreshing meal deals.

When it’s raining: Highlight hot drinks, comfort food and delivery offers.

When it’s cold: Feature warming breakfasts, soups and hearty meals.

When the sun comes out: Encourage customers to enjoy outdoor dining, takeaway lunches and seasonal specials.

And here’s where it gets interesting…

Imagine combining weather conditions with the time of day, local promotions and live menu pricing.

A rainy Monday morning could trigger a breakfast-and-coffee promotion, while a sunny Friday afternoon could showcase a completely different offer.

All without staff having to manually change the screens.

It should help you sell the right product, to the right customer, at the right moment.
