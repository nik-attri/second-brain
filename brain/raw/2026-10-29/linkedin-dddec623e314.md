90% of businesses fail not because of a lack of ideas, but because they crack under the weight of the messy middle.


I think about that statistic a lot.


In rooms where millions are on the line, people talk endlessly about strategy. But strategy is cheap when the pressure mounts. What separates the ones who make it from the rest isn't just a spreadsheet; it’s the quiet, unglamorous things.


It’s integrity when nobody is looking. It’s accuracy down to the single decimal place when chaos is screaming around you. And above all, it’s grit—that raw, relentless refusal to quit even when your feet feel like lead and your mind tells you to lie down.


That kind of grit cannot be bought, and it certainly can't be faked. It’s forged in the trenches, quiet nights, and heavy moments.


If you’re in the middle of a hard season right now, get out of your head and back on your feet. Remember who the hell you are.


You've got all it takes. Let’s get to work.
