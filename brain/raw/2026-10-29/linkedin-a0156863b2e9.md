Milwaukee. World Series champions.

For the first time. Ever.

Nineteenth in payroll. Last team standing.

The Brewers swept the Dodgers, then beat the Rays in five. Some very expensive October plans got canceled along the way.

Three of baseball’s six lowest-payroll teams made the postseason. And this was the first World Series since 1991 featuring two teams in the bottom half of spending.

Moneyball, with a champagne finish. 

Especially when the celebration belongs to a town that knows what to do with a cold beer and a very good dinner.

Milwaukee is a hell of an eating and drinking town. Neighborhood taverns, Friday fish fries, cheese curds, frozen custard, and restaurants with James Beard recognition. A place where deciding where to eat deserves considerably more thought than whether to have another.

Tonight, I suspect that second decision has made itself.

Somewhere, someone is hugging a stranger. Someone is calling the person who took them to their first game. Someone is thinking about who should have been here to see this.

That’s the part the payroll spreadsheet doesn’t capture.

Congratulations to the Milwaukee Brewers  Brewers and the City of Milwaukee .

You’ve waited a long time to order this round.

Make it a good one.
