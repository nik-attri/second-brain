🚀 What if restaurants could turn food waste into actionable insights using AI?
Food waste is one of the biggest hidden costs in the restaurant industry. But most restaurants don't have a clear picture of what they're wasting, why it's happening, or how much it's costing them.
That's why I've been working on a new Food Wastage Management System inside Automation Restaurant, our AI-powered restaurant management SaaS.
The goal is simple: Track every loss. Understand the cause. Make smarter decisions.
🔍 Here's what we've built:
📊 Real-Time Waste Dashboard
Track food wastage costs, waste rates, pending approvals, and ingredients that need attention.
📈 Waste Analytics & Trends
Visualize daily, weekly, and monthly wastage patterns and identify which ingredients contribute most to losses.
📝 Smart Waste Recording
Record wasted ingredients with automatic unit conversions, reasons, quantities, and responsible staff members.
✅ Approval-Based Inventory Control
Kitchen staff can report waste, while managers review and approve entries before inventory is adjusted.
⏳ Expiry Monitoring & History
Identify potentially expiring stock and maintain a history of waste records, approvals, rejections, and reversals.
🤖 AI-Powered Waste Insights
This is where things get interesting.
Instead of manually analyzing reports, restaurant owners can simply ask:
"How much stock did we waste in the last 7 days, and which 3 ingredients cost the most?"
Our AI Guide reads the restaurant's data and provides an instant, data-driven answer.
📌 An example from our demo dataset:
Over 7 days, the system tracked Rs 2,097.80 in food wastage, with the highest losses coming from:
🥇 Chicken — Rs 1,020
🥈 Burger Buns — Rs 447.10
🥉 Lettuce — Rs 270
These insights help restaurant managers identify where money is being lost and take action before small inefficiencies become expensive problems.
💡 The bigger vision
We're building Automation Restaurant to go beyond traditional restaurant management software.
Not just recording transactions, but helping restaurants understand their operations, reduce avoidable waste, and make better decisions with AI.
This is another step toward building a smarter, more efficient restaurant ecosystem.
