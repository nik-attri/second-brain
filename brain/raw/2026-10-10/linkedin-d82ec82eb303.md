Most software engineers think becoming a Manager is a promotion.

It’s actually a career reset to Junior status.

Here is what nobody tells you.

Yesterday, I spent 6 hours in meetings about org charts, budget approvals, and performance reviews.

Zero lines of code written.

Zero architecture diagrams drawn.

Just vibes, spreadsheets, and calendar Tetris.

As an IC, my biggest stress was a tricky race condition or a failing CI/CD pipeline. 

I could put my headphones on, lock in, and fix the bug by lunch.

As an EM, your code is now human beings.

And humans don't come with documentation.

They have bad days, conflicting PR opinions, and burnout.

You go from being judged by your own output to being judged by everyone else's.

Don't get me wrong.

Unlocking a senior engineer's potential or protecting a team from toxic scope creep is deeply rewarding.

But if you only love building software?

Stay an IC. You'll make more money and keep your sanity.

If you love building people?

Welcome to hell. Grab a coffee, your day is all meetings.
