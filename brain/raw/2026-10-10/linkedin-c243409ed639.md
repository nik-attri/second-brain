Happy to share that I've joined Prophix as Senior Manager, Business Development. Thank you to Laraib Sultan, Chris Walker and Krishan Chauhan for giving me such a good sense of the landscape prior to joining.

FP&A and corporate performance management are relatively new to me as a sector, but the need is easy to see. Finance teams are expected to plan, budget, forecast, and report with accuracy and speed, often while still relying on spreadsheets and manual processes. 

Having worked and spoken with finance leaders before, this is clearly an area where better tools can free up time for analysis and help businesses stay financially healthy for the long run. Prophix has a long track record of doing exactly that, and I'm looking forward to learning and growing here.

Building structure, putting systems in place, and beating targets is what I enjoy doing, but what motivates me even more is helping people grow to their best potential. Seeing my direct reports and colleagues develop and succeed has been the main motivational driver throughout my career, and I would love to continue doing that at Prophix.
