Federal Student Work Experience Program (FSWEP) Jobs in Canada 2026

Looking for student employment opportunities with the Government of Canada? 🇨🇦 The Federal Student Work Experience Program (FSWEP) helps eligible students gain practical work experience across federal departments and agencies.

From administration and finance to IT, science, research, and public service, this program offers opportunities in different fields throughout Canada.

Job opportunities may include:

- Federal Administrative Assistant
- Government Office Assistant
- IT Support Assistant
- Finance and Accounting Assistant
- Research Assistant
- Data Entry Assistant
- Communications Assistant
- Science and Laboratory Assistant
- Human Resources Assistant
- Environmental and Field Research Assistant

📌 JOB OVERVIEW

FSWEP is a federal government student employment program offering eligible students full-time and part-time work opportunities. Applications are added to a recruitment inventory, and hiring managers may select candidates whose qualifications match available positions.

🎯 WHO CAN APPLY?

- Full-time secondary or post-secondary students at accredited institutions
- Students who meet the minimum working age requirements
- Applicants who will return to full-time studies in the next academic term, subject to the program’s eligibility rules
- Candidates whose education and skills match specific job requirements
- International students with valid Canadian work authorization may be considered when no eligible, qualified Canadian students are available
