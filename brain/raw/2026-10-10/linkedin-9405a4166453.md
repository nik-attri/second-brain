Strengths and Weaknesses

How do we choose a job or a career path?

The logical answer would be: wherever we can earn more money.

But nowadays, more and more people are choosing a profession or career path based on what they actually enjoy, taking their abilities and skills into account.

Of course, you can learn almost anything. But!!! Will it bring you joy?

Joy is one of the key indicators that you’re on the right path and making the right choice.

After all, one person can absolutely love working with spreadsheets and analytics, while for someone else, it’s pure hell. 

I love learning and developing new skills. When I’m passionate about something, I dive deep into it, exploring every little detail until I understand it down to the molecular level. And I can talk about it for hours! (One of my favorite things to do )
