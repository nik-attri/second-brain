YOUR NEXT CAREER OPPORTUNITY IN TEXTILES, GARMENTS & EXPORTS STARTS HERE!

Are you ready to take the next step in your career with a growing textile export-import business?
Maruti Exim is hiring!

Established in 2002, Maruti Exim is expanding its team across fabric merchandising, garment exports, sourcing, finance, warehouse operations and HR. We are looking for skilled, committed professionals who want to contribute to business growth and build their careers in the textile and garment industry.

📍 Job Location: Surat, Gujarat

OPEN POSITIONS | MARUTI EXIM

1. Senior Merchandiser – Garments
Department: Garment Merchandising
Experience in garment export merchandising, buyer coordination, costing, sampling, production follow-up and shipment execution.

2. Godown Executive
Department: Godown / Warehouse Operations
Experience in finished fabric inventory, physical stock verification, ERP, FIFO, packaging and dispatch management.

3. Junior Merchandiser – Fabric
Department: Fabric Merchandising
Suitable for candidates with 0–3 years of experience in fabric coordination, vendor follow-up, order tracking and textile processing.

4. Costing Executive – Data Entry
Department: Finance & Accounts
Experience in product/order costing, Excel, data accuracy, cost sheets, variance analysis and MIS reporting.

5. Senior Accountant – 2 Positions
Department: Accounts
Looking for accounting professionals with knowledge of general accounting, bank reconciliation, accounts payable/receivable, GST, TDS, financial records and MIS reporting.

6. HR Professional
Department: Human Resources
Experience in recruitment, manpower planning, attendance, payroll coordination, statutory compliance, PMS, KRA/KPI and HR operations.

7. SBU Head – Garment Sourcing
Department: Strategic Business Unit (SBU)
Senior professional with strong garment sourcing, supplier development, costing, commercial negotiation and production execution experience. Exposure to Bangladesh and/or China sourcing is mandatory.

WHY EXPLORE THESE OPPORTUNITIES?

Work with an established textile export-import business.
Take ownership of meaningful responsibilities.
Collaborate across merchandising, sourcing, finance and operations.
Contribute to business expansion and process improvement.
