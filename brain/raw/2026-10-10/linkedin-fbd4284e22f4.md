University research labs and public policy departments are drowning in unstructured qualitative data.
Every week, researchers spend dozens of manual hours reading policy PDFs, extracting case metrics, and formatting summaries before actual analysis can even begin
To solve this bottleneck, I built an automated research and document-processing pipeline
Here is the exact 5-stage architecture I use:
1️⃣ Data Ingestion: Automated webhook triggers capturing structured metadata and document payloads from storage tables.
2️⃣ Document Synchronization: Programmatically parsing and indexing text chunks from incoming research PDFs.
3️⃣ Internal Alert Routing: Immediate status notification dispatching to key project stakeholders.
4️⃣ Centralized Audit Logging: Real-time tracking and performance metrics logging into structured data warehouses.
5️⃣ Multi-Channel Distribution: Automated formatting and clean output generation for final human-in-the-loop review.
The system handles the heavy data extraction, but the researcher maintains 100% control over the analytical conclusions
