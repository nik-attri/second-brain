Pranay Yadav is our CEO. He also looks exactly like Turtle from Entourage (we have added a picture for comparison) 

Yes, he knows. We've told him.

He and Anchit started Iden back in 2024, and the tech world has moved faster than ever since. So I asked him what major shifts he's seen in IT in the last two years. He said - 

• AI didn't shrink IT. It multiplied it.
Every "automate IT away" pitch assumed AI would mean less work. What actually happened: every team started adopting AI tools, every tool needs access to something, and every one of them is another app to onboard, secure, and audit. The IT team didn't get smaller. The surface area they're guarding got 10x bigger.

• Authorization is far harder than authentication.
Authentication is the front door: "are you who you say you are?" Every tool has solved that. Authorization is everything after: what can you touch, should you still have it, and who signed off? People change teams, projects end, access piles up, and nobody circles back. Most companies nail the login and quietly skip the rest, hoping it'll sort itself out. It doesn't.

• Workflows shouldn't be built around tools. Tools should be built around workflows. 
When you build your workflow around a tool, your team ends up working for the software: new steps, workarounds, spreadsheets on the side to cover what the tool can't do. Adoption drops, shadow processes pop up, and the tool you bought to fix the mess becomes part of it.

That's the philosophy behind Iden. We adapt to you and how you work, instead of asking you to work around us.
