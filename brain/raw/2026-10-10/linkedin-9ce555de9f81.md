Unpopular opinion: the most impactful thing a company can do for employee mental health isn't a free lunch. It's fixing broken daily processes.

Today is #WorldMentalHealthDay, and while wellness initiatives and open conversations matter, mental health also has an operational side that rarely gets talked about.

In almost every business, from frontline teams and contact centre reps to plant floor operators, burnout happens not because they lack resilience, but because they fight their tools every day.

When people are trapped in spreadsheet hell, constantly duplicating manual data entry, or navigating disconnected systems, daily friction drains talent. That frustration doesn't stay at work — it bleeds into everything else.

At Infinite Green, we believe being curiously different means looking at operational execution as an act of human empathy. Creating a strong culture and sense of purpose isn't just about big vision statements.

It's about listening to the frontline and uncovering where manual tasks and broken handoffs are quietly draining energy. It's about simplifying workflows and re-engineering processes before throwing complex technology at them. And it's about empowering standout talent with modern tools and clear paths so they can focus on meaningful work that drives real outcomes.

If you're a leader reading this, what's one daily operational headache in your business that, if solved, would make your team's day immediately better?
