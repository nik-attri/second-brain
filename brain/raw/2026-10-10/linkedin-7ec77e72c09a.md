Were Hiring |  Associate Manager-Quality Analysis
📍 Location: Noida, Sector-62
💼 Role Type: Full Time
📅 Working Days: 6 Days a Week
⚡ Immediate Joiners Preferred

About the Role: : 
We are looking for a proactive and result-oriented Vendor Management professional to manage and strengthen relationships with external vendors/partners supporting business operations. The role will be responsible for vendor onboarding, performance monitoring, commercial management, operational governance, issue resolution, compliance, and ensuring timely delivery against agreed SLAs/KPIs. 
The ideal candidate should have strong stakeholder-management skills, be comfortable working with data and dashboards, and have the ability to manage multiple vendors and field-level requirements simultaneously.

Key Responsibilities 
Vendor Onboarding and Management and Performance Management
Identify, evaluate, onboard, and manage vendors as per business requirements. Coordinate vendor documentation, agreements, compliance, and operational readiness.
Maintain an updated database of active, inactive, and newly onboarded vendors. 
Ensure vendors understand business processes, SOPs, deliverables, and performance expectations.
Define and track vendor-wise KPIs, SLAs, productivity, quality, TAT, and business outcomes.
Prepare periodic vendor performance reports and dashboards for management review.
Validate vendor invoices against agreed commercials, deliverables, and actual performance. 
Coordinate with Finance for timely and accurate vendor payments.Track vendor commercials, payouts, deductions, penalties, and incentives. 
Identify commercial leakages and ensure adherence to approved commercials.
Support negotiations and commercial discussions with vendors. 

Compliance, Governance and Stakeholder Management
Ensure vendors adhere to company policies, SOPs, statutory requirements, and contractual terms. 
Conduct periodic audits or checks of vendor processes and documentation.
Ensure proper maintenance of vendor records and supporting documents.Identify operational/compliance risks and escalate them proactively.
Work closely with Sales, Legal, Finance, and other relevant teams. 
Act as the primary point of contact for assigned vendors.
Conduct regular review meetings with vendors and internal stakeholders. 
Ensure timely communication and closure of operational issues.
