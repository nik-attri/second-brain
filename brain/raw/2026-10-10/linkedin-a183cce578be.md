𝐑𝐞𝐠𝐮𝐥𝐚𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐲 𝐓𝐞𝐜𝐡𝐧𝐨𝐥𝐨𝐠𝐲 (𝐑𝐞𝐠𝐓𝐞𝐜𝐡) 𝐀𝐮𝐭𝐨𝐦𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐝 𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐥𝐢𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞

RegTech is no longer the quiet support function. In 2026 it has become the operating system of financial services. 

Agentic AI is rewriting KYC and AML. Firms are moving from alert fatigue to systems that take action, investigate, and escalate with human oversight. Funding tells the story: RegTech deals captured more than a third of global fintech capital in recent windows, with AI spend projected at nearly 30% of total RegTech budgets this year. 

DORA’s first real enforcement quarter is underway. Regulators are demanding real-time evidence of operational resilience, not just policies. The “agentic gap” is real — many large institutions pilot AI, yet only a small percentage have fully embedded it. Trust, explainability, and audit trails remain the bottlenecks. 

The winners are building compliance as code. Automated obligation mapping, continuous monitoring, and reusable KYC are turning cost centers into strategic assets. Smaller firms that once lagged are closing the gap by adopting purpose-built platforms instead of generic tools. 

Regulations keep multiplying. Manual reviews cannot scale. Smart systems now stand guard so humans can focus and prevail. 

If your compliance team is still drowning in spreadsheets and delayed reports, 2026 is the year to change course. The technology is ready. The question is whether your organization is.
