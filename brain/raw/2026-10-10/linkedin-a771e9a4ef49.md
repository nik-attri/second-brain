Learning about cooling systems and preparing for a job interview… that’s what set a whole wave of changes in motion. For over two years, I’d wanted to learn to use ChatGPT and other AI tools and feel their value in everyday work. I remember the Milkyway pharmaceutical project for Fujifilm. Every report prepared manually, every detail, translations through Google Translate… looking back, my own little Stone Age 😅 My first ChatGPT questions, courses, learning to write prompts… I picked things up, but lacked something that would really draw me in. Then came a clear goal: understanding cooling systems and preparing for an interview. Mechanisms, connections, one question leading to another… a reason to return every day. And things started moving. I personalised my assistant, refined my questions and checked the answers. The more I understood, the more I wanted to know. Learning led to ideas, materials and projects of my own. I feel I learned more in two months than in the previous two years of trying. I don’t believe in coincidences. This was part of the changes I was already working towards. When you take care of yourself, build discipline and get clearer about what you want… a spark can set a lot in motion. Mine met a readiness that had also taken time to build. Now, mornings mean learning and developing ideas with AI. I’m exploring Claude and Gemini, creating, experimenting… university starts soon, and I’m glad to take this skill with me. I know this satisfaction. On Milkyway, I walked around the site with a tablet, exploring 3D models in Navisworks and BIM 360. My makeshift desk in the boiler room held a laptop with documentation… digital models, real construction and information I could use to improve the work. Even then, I called myself Android 😎 A human, a computer, a knowledge base… fully in sync. AI gives me a similar buzz. Again, I’m running out of screens… two laptops and a phone aren’t enough, hahaha 😂 But I’m managing. My suggestion for starting with AI? Choose something you care about. A subject, a problem, your own project. Wanting to understand something specific opened more doors than I expected. Change itself deserves another post. It can be difficult. We want to grow, yet struggle to leave the familiar. I’ve felt that, and still do. Over time, though, you recognise the feeling… and trust me, accepting change becomes easier than accepting stagnation. Everything changes, everything flows… I’m learning to move with it and shape what I can influence. That’s what draws me in: learning, creating and watching my own life change 🐲🤖#AI #ChatGPT #Claude #Android #HumanAndTechnology #Gemini
