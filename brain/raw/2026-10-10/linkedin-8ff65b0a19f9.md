Most biopharma companies are sitting on a 10-year gold mine.

Past regulatory filings. Batch records. Clinical deviation logs. Stability test results.

It is all sitting there.

Trapped in scanned PDFs, legacy ERPs, and dead shared drives.

With a 12-year-old specialty generic pharma company facing a brutal bottleneck: preparing a single ANDA dossier took 14 to 18 months.

Teams were drowning in manual data entry.

Solution was to built a Data-to-Outcome Engine to unlock their legacy data graveyard.

Here is what changed in 12 months:

1. Accelerated ANDA Filings
Instead of taking 6 months to draft a dossier, the AI auto-populated 70% of Module 3 regulatory submissions using past successful filings.
Result: Dossier drafting dropped from 6 months to 3 weeks. Hello, First-to-File advantage.

2. 505(b)(2) Drug Repurposing
The AI analyzed 10 years of patient side-effect logs and surfaced an unexpected pattern. A specific extended-release polymer reduced GI side effects by 40%.
Result: A brand new patent-protected product line without starting R&D from scratch.

3. Instant Clinical Support for Physicians
Instead of doctors waiting 48 hours for medical affairs to answer queries, an internal AI agent trained on validated past data now delivers compliant answers in 5 seconds.
Result: Brand loyalty jumped 300%.
