I overheard a meeting this morning that felt like it was from 1995.
Sadly, it was not.

I wasn’t trying to listen. I overheard it on a speaker call. 

The leader was giving the team a demo of their new management tool:
A color-coded spreadsheet! What the hell?! 

Here’s the thing. I’ve overheard this same meeting many times. Same group, every morning, at least 30 minutes. 

They go around and tell each other what they’re up to, who’s doing what, and which customers are coming in that day. 

Every bit of that already lives in a calendar or a system somewhere. They’re reading it out loud to each other. 30 minutes a day is 125 hours a year. Per person. 

The scary part is how normal this is. Most companies still run exactly this way. 

AI can run that entire process right now. An agent reads the calendar, the CRM, and the job list overnight. Everybody has the plan for the day before they sit down. Nobody recites anything. 

You don’t need a big project to get there. Pick one simple process. Build a simple AI workflow around it. Then pick the next one. 

Your competitors are going to. Some already have, and their teams had a 30-minute head start over you this morning. 

If a color-coded spreadsheet is the new technology you’re rolling out to your team, you are losing. Fast. 

That’s how you go the way of the dinosaur. Change or die. It really is that simple now. 

What is your team still saying out loud every morning that a system already knows?
