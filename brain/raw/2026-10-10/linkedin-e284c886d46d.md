AI is making employees faster.

Why are so many organisations still struggling to turn that productivity into enterprise value?

New McKinsey & Company research highlights a significant disconnect.

80% of respondents report individual productivity improvements from AI.

Yet only 37% report positive enterprise-level EBIT impact.

The problem may not be the technology.

It may be the organisation around it.

AI can accelerate analysis, automate administration and reduce the time required to complete individual tasks.

However, the work may still pass through the same departments, await the same approvals and encounter the same management bottlenecks.

Faster work does not automatically mean faster decisions.

For CEOs, CIOs, CFOs and transformation leaders, this raises a fundamental operating-model question.

Are we redesigning how decisions are made, how accountability is assigned and how value moves across the enterprise?

Or are we simply automating activities within structures that remain unchanged?
