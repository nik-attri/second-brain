Since I joined Valeria HR in May, our team has more than doubled: from 22 to 52 people.

30 new teammates since June, 19 of them in Q3 alone, across Sales, Customer Success, Payroll, Tech, Product, Marketing and Finance. Along the way we went from 7 to 21 women on the team, with women making up 47% of our hires since June.

Why does this matter? Because of who we build for. Millions of people in Spain work in hospitality, retail, logistics, events, agriculture... High-turnover sectors where contracts, payroll and compliance are still largely manual, and where errors land directly on someone’s paycheck. A late contract or a wrong payslip isn’t an admin problem for them, it’s rent. Valeria exists so that every frontline worker gets hired, registered and paid right, on time, every time, and so the companies employing them can stop drowning in paperwork.

Building that takes a team that cares about getting people’s pay right, starting with our own. 
So we’re building our People strategy on #transparency. At Valeria, everyone knows what everyone earns, across the whole company. Every job post includes its salary range, and every role now sits on a leveling framework with market-based bands across 7 job families. Next step: opening those bands to the whole team, so it’s clear not just what people earn, but why, and what it takes to grow.

And we’re not done: more roles to fill before year-end 👇
