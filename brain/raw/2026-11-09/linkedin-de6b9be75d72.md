Yesterday wrapped up Froth & Fury Fest's first emerging Artist Development Program at Thebarton Theatre Sat 7 November 2026. 

It's a hard one to put into words how I feel about this first year of our emerging artist program - the Path to Fury. It was emotional, it was charged, it was furious, and the vibe was electric! 

The result was 18 South Australian Artists showcased across 4 venues and 1,394 people show up to support them. Blowen away with this result for free all ages gigs. 

A HUGE CONGRATS to TV Therapy, Hell Machine & Theurgy for making it through to play at the main event - Froth & Fury Fest - Saturday 30 January 2027. 

To the remaining finalists Sørrøwstate, Biome & Macey Adl you smashed it and put on one hell of a fight. So proud of you all! 

Supporting emerging artists is one of the most wholesome and amazing feelings. 
I'm so honoured to be able to provide that opportunity to alternative and heavy local artists that often to forgotten about by major events and other organisations. 

We are onto our youth program now New Breed - for Young People 10 - 18 years details here: https://lnkd.in/gbhFHiSc 

Now, back to the computer, my spreadsheets and onto the big show!
