How did I train new content writers for link insertion tasks?

This is one of the most satisfying and hardest things I did at SayNine.

So, maybe if you are in that position, these tips will help you.

First, I show how to do link insertion manually and share all the tricks that they might not need.

Why? Because we have a link insertion platform that automates the whole process.

So, the second thing I do is share how it works.

This process can be overwhelming, so don’t share all the information in one day; keep it easy.

Last but not least, human connection is very important. ​

So, suggest a coffee to your new teammate and start a simple conversation.
