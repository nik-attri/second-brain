A growing business wants to get new customers on board, verify the customers and get payments. Leadership agree to use 6 third-party SaaS tools.

This looks great when everything goes live.

When you come to audit or have technical issues then you can't answer any of the questions of where the customer data is held, if they've secretly added any sub-processors, or anything related to doing it manually.

This is still your issue under the NDPA. As the Data Controller you're still responsible for what your processors do and need to ensure they all highlight any new processors they work with.

I'm not a fan of vendor issues but the problem comes when you chain software together without really thinking about who owns the data. When you assume you're protecting privacy you end up with operations logs in someone elses silo. You've also got many unvetted integrations which are access points.

At Datori Consulting Limited we help companies decide who will own the data, what the rules are and how they will handle API failures.

If the businesses operating model relies on the vendor then they're renting their operations.

How many vendors do you have holding your customers data?
