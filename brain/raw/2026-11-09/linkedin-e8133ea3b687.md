I'm excited to share one of my recent Data Analytics projects, an HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel.
The goal of this project was to transform employee data into meaningful insights that can support better HR and business decision-making.
Using Excel, I analysed a dataset of 500 employees and developed an interactive dashboard to explore key workforce metrics, including:
1. Total Employee Headcount  
 2. Employee Turnover (Leavers)  
 3. Average Salary and Tenure  
 4. Workforce Age and Gender Distribution  
 5. Employee Headcount by Department and Location  
6.  Hiring Trends by Year  
 7. Average Salary by Job Level  
 8. Employee Performance and Training Hours  
Tools and skills applied:
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Data Analysis
- Dashboard Design and Visualisation
- Interactive Slicers
One important lesson from this project is that data becomes more valuable when it is organised and presented in a way that makes patterns easier to understand and business questions easier to investigate.
