It's been a hell of a week, right? Finding out I'm going to be gone in a few months a week after signing an extension on my lease at my place, talking to others on my team that were also affected by the role reduction, and seeing all sorts of experience being kicked out the door because of where we came from. In my mind I see an empty-headed corporate stooge looking at a spreadsheet, seeing that we were officially located in Utah, we had a Discover employment DNA track, and that was the formula used to cut us loose. But that isn't the reason I'm back on my computer typing. 

I had a conversation with my manager on Wednesday, he asked how I was doing, and then said "that was a stupid question, sorry." I said it wasn't and said I was actually doing fine. He mentioned that I was taking it better than he probably would have, "I'd probably have said F off." I explained that while this is a setback and not what I would've preferred, the truth is I can make a choice. Not quite Matrix red pill or blue pill choice, but a choice mentally. I can either let being pissed off negatively affect me and drag me down, or I can continue to be a professional and do my work until my time is over. And if I find something in the interim, all the better. It doesn't mean I'm not angry, it doesn't mean I don't have one hell of a promo ready to unleash when the time is right, and it doesn't mean I'll ever forgive or forget, but as long as I'm being paid to do a job that's what I'm going to do. My manager said he appreciated that honesty, my willingness to keep working for the betterment of the company, and while the powers that be may not recognize it, he would. I'm not trying to change anybody's mind that makes decisions, that ship has sailed. I am trying to remain in the proper headspace for my future. 

There is a time and a place for everything, and right now that place is to pull a movie off of my DVD wall to use as background noise to help me go to sleep. And since I brought up doing voiceovers in my last post, I'm thinking I'll go with Pump Up The Volume. Wasn't shortwave radio a type of podcasting before anyone with a mic, an iPhone, and an opinion could start one? "I got my Wild Cherry Diet Pepsi, and I got my Blackjack gum here, and I got that feeling. Yeah, that familiar feeling that something rank is going on out there."
