30 seconds. 

That’s all it took to complete a payment using ZonePay by Zone. 

As a chronic transfer-holic, there are few things in the world that are more frustrating than having to manually type account numbers into my banking app, or listen to a merchant dictate them, only to end up with an “Invalid Account Number” error because of a typo or a hearing mistake.

Luckily, ZonePay heard my heart’s cry! Now, all I have to do is take a picture of the account details, and they’re automatically inputted.

As a Product Manager and end user, while I’m still in the camp that believes we need to be cautious about trusting AI with end-to-end financial transactions, it’s incredible to see it applied to everyday payment experiences to take away customer frustrations and make simple tasks even simpler.

With ZonePay, you can link your bank accounts in one app and complete payments in seconds.

I’m genuinely proud to be part of the team that shipped this. Fewer errors, no “did you say 572?”, more EASE. The best part is we’re only getting started!

I really can't wait for you to see what comes next!
