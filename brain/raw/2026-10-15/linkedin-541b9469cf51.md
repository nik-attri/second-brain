DIY bookkeeping can seem like the cheaper option. But have you considered what it could actually be costing you?

When you mix personal and business expenses, miscategorize transactions, or miss things in your books, the consequences can go beyond a messy spreadsheet and hours in numbers hell.

You could be:
→ Missing legitimate business deductions 
→ Making financial decisions based on inaccurate numbers 
→ Overlooking cash flow problems until it's too late
→ Wasting time and energy fixing mistakes instead of running your business

And that's before you factor in the time you're spending trying to figure it all out yourself.
