We're Hiring: Client Success Coordinator | Sri Lanka

Are you organized, detail-oriented, and passionate about helping clients succeed? Looking to grow your career while working with an international team?

Walkingspree is looking for a Client Success Coordinator to join our team in Colombo, Sri Lanka!

At Walkingspree, we help organizations across the U.S. build healthier, more engaged workplaces through our employee wellbeing platform. We're looking for someone who enjoys working with people, managing projects, and supporting meaningful client experiences.

What You'll Do
• Support our U.S.-based Client Success Managers with daily client activities and program coordination.
• Assist with client onboarding, wellness challenges, and engagement initiatives.
• Prepare reports, monitor participation, and track program performance.
• Coordinate client communications, program launches, and registration campaigns.
• Collaborate with internal teams to ensure smooth operations and excellent client support.

What We're Looking For
• Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, IT, or a related field.
• 1-3 years of experience in client support, customer success, operations, or a similar role.
• Strong English communication, organizational, and multitasking skills.
• Attention to detail and confidence working with reports, spreadsheets, and data.
• A proactive attitude and willingness to learn.
Experience with Salesforce, Excel, or Power BI is a plus!

Why Join Walkingspree?
• Work with U.S.-based clients and international teams.
• Gain experience in the SaaS and employee wellbeing industry.
• Enjoy a supportive, collaborative work environment.
• Build your career in client success and account management.
• Receive competitive compensation paid in U.S. dollars.

Position Details
Location: Galle Road, Bambalapitiya, Colombo
Working Hours: 6:30 PM - 3:30 AM (Sri Lanka Time)
