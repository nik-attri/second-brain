Ask a marketer how their week went, and they'll rarely say "I had too many good ideas."

They'll say they were busy. Busy formatting, chasing, copying, rechecking, rescheduling.

The strange part is that none of it feels like a problem on its own. Each task is small and reasonable. Together they quietly take over the time that should go to thinking.

That's why we don't see social media as a content problem. It's a time-allocation problem. The work that needs a human (taste, positioning, knowing what to say and when to stay quiet) gets squeezed by work that only needs to get done.

At Quevora, we're building AI teammates to take on that second kind of work, so marketers can spend more of their week on the first.

If you manage social for a startup or a client, I'd like to know: what's the one task you'd happily never do manually again?
