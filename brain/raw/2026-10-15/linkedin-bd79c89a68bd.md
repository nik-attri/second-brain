🚀 *KAMI SEDANG MEMBUKA LOWONGAN: SYSTEM ENGINEER – NETWORK & SECURITY (Redhat Openstack)*

*PT Karya Solusi Prima Sejahtera (KSPS)* sedang mencari kandidat profesional dan berbakat untuk bergabung bersama tim kami!
Apakah Anda memiliki ketertarikan terhadap keamanan jaringan, teknologi firewall, dan solusi keamanan tingkat enterprise? Apakah Anda ingin mengembangkan keahlian teknis sekaligus mendapatkan pengalaman bekerja dengan teknologi keamanan terkini.

Di *PT Karya Solusi Prima Sejahtera (KSPS)*, kami mencari kandidat yang termotivasi untuk bergabung sebagai *System Engineer – Network & Security (Redhat Openstack)* dan berkontribusi dalam proses implementasi, konfigurasi, serta dukungan solusi keamanan jaringan dan sistem perusahaan.

Key Responsibilities:
- Deploy, configure, and integrate security solutions across firewall, DDoS protection, networking, application delivery, identity, and endpoint security.
- Work with industry-leading technologies, including Palo Alto, Fortinet, NETSCOUT/Arbor, Cisco, F5, SailPoint, and Trend Micro.
- Support firewall implementation, configuration, policy management, and migration activities.
- Perform implementation testing, solution validation, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
- Monitor deployed solutions, troubleshoot incidents, and escalate complex issues when necessary.
- Execute configuration changes, patching, and system health checks in accordance with change-management procedures.
- Maintain technical documentation, configuration records, test results, and operational runbooks.
- Collaborate with senior engineers, project managers, and customer operations teams to ensure successful project delivery.

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Telecommunications Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum *1 year of hands-on firewall implementation or delivery experience*, preferably with Palo Alto, Fortinet, or equivalent technologies.
- Working knowledge of networking and security technologies such as Cisco, F5, Trend Micro, NETSCOUT/Arbor, or SailPoint.
- Solid understanding of TCP/IP, routing, switching, firewall concepts, and network security fundamentals.
- Strong troubleshooting, documentation, and communication skills.
- Customer-oriented, proactive, and comfortable working collaboratively within a technical delivery team.
- Willingness to support deployments and operational activities outside regular working hours when required.
