A regulator asks: "Show us exactly how you handled this data request."

What do you hand them?

For a lot of privacy teams, the honest answer is a pile of email threads, spreadsheets, and redacted PDFs.

That's the problem we're tackling at our IAPP webinar next Tuesday.

Data subject requests (DSRs) sound simple. Someone asks to see or delete their personal data, and you respond.

In reality:
→ The data is scattered across dozens of systems
→ The legal clock is running: one month under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA
→ Employee requests can pull in privileged emails and other people's data
→ "We did our best" isn't proof

Having worked these requests from the legal side, I've seen how quickly a "simple" DSR turns into a privilege review.
