At The Nordic AI Agency, we're built on something different: pragmatism over hype.
Most AI discussions in the construction sector end in one of two extremes:
Either vague hype promising overnight magic, or abandoned pilots that burned budgets on chatbots that hallucinate technical specs.

Who We Are
We are an industrial AI integration team based in Oslo, Norway. We build deterministic, production-grade AI agent systems engineered specifically for the Nordic construction, infrastructure, and energy sectors.

⚙️What We Do
We bridge the gap between fast-moving AI capabilities and complex, legacy engineering workflows. We help non-developer teams become AI-native in days, not years. We:
- Eliminate operational grind: Automating tender analysis, parsing technical handbooks (such as Statens vegvesen / NPRA), and extracting actionable BIM/IFC data.
- Accelerate design & bidding iterations: Cutting iteration cycles by up to 80%, allowing teams to evaluate 5x more design and tender options without linearly adding headcount.
- Guarantee deterministic reliability: Zero guesswork, zero unverified data. We safely and predictably connect your proprietary domain data with state-of-the-art models.

🤝 Whom We Partner With
We partner with SME construction businesses, engineering consultancies, material producers, and contractors across Norway (typically 15–100 employees) who:
- Compete in demanding public and private procurement tenders where bidding accuracy makes or breaks margins.
- Want the operational superpower of AI without the overhead of hiring an entire in-house software division or burning capital on trial-and-error pilots.
- Value dependable, hands-on collaboration: We don’t just throw software over the fence. We visit your offices, sit with your engineers, and guide your workflows step by step.
