When your flight is cancelled at 2am, McCurdy Hamilton answers.

Usually Patrick. Deborah sleeps through the first ring.

We're McCurdy Hamilton Travel. Two of us run it, and family-run gets said so often it's gone a bit soft as a phrase. So here's what it actually looks like from inside.

The person who books your trip is the person who fixes it when a hotel double-sells your room. Same phone number, same voice.

Deborah keeps a paper list of hotels she'd never send anyone back to. Patrick keeps saying he'll put it in a spreadsheet. It's been four years.

If we recommend somewhere, one of us has stood in the lobby, or grilled someone who has, usually over dinner because we can't help ourselves.

And when a call gets hard, refunds, delays, a honeymoon that started with lost luggage, it lands on us. There's nobody further up the chain to pass you to.

That sounds heavier than it feels day to day. Mostly it just means the person deciding has to face you afterwards.

We quite like that, most days.
