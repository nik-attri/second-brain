Having read the article by #Antropic engineers, few things caught my attention and sounded quite controversial:

1. They mention that the quaility of code produced by the flagship models is very high, and in many cases is better than even the senior engineer can write. Also, apparently, AI managed to optimise the code even after it was reviewed.
2. They mention that they read less of the code itself, but more an "intention" 😀 (which I understand is just a PR description)... Understandable, because the amount of generated code is insane, and that's unbearable to pay proper attention to every detail in a long term. Technically, that's not a decent review, just quickly scroll past the changes (I know how it works, as that's how long PR's are being often reviewed).
3. They bet more on post-merge and pre-deploy deterministic automated checks in the pipeline.

This brings me to the point:
1. Why do we even need to review the code if it's reasonable, or even better than what we can create manually? Moreover, amount of code generated by AI is insane, and can't be reviewed properly.
2. Why even bother opening PR's instead of following trunk-based development (pushing to the main branch directly), and still fully rely on automated testing and checks?
