Pushing their order book to RM10.4 billion is a massive corporate achievement.
But for the HSE Manager on the ground?

A project of this scale means managing safety across hundreds of third-party subcontractor crews you’ve never met.

When a client safety audit lands, "we couldn't get the data" won't protect your record. Yet your daily reality is a constant uphill battle:

• Chasing subcon supervisors across active zones just to collect missing CAPA evidence.

• Ground workers abandoning digital safety forms because the sub-urban site has zero mobile signal.

• Wasting your afternoon hand-holding crews through app logins instead of inspecting critical field hazards.

At the end of the day, you are the one held accountable for unclosed tickets and unverified Permits-to-Work (PTW).

Can your site safety workflow actually keep pace with a project this massive?

High-level safety policies don't prevent incidents, practical field tools do. Keeping sites audit-ready under real site conditions requires:

• Zero-Signal Hazard Reporting: Enabling workers to snap photos and record voice logs offline, syncing automatically once they reach signal.

• Native Bahasa Malaysia Interface: Removing language barriers so subcon crews log observations independently without needing constant supervision.

• Automated CAPA & PTW Tracking: Linking observations directly to permit closures, eliminating the manual paper chase before an audit.

Safety compliance shouldn't mean drowning in paperwork or babysitting digital forms.
