💻 You know how to handle customer issues. But what's your next career move?

You resolve customer complaints.
You manage tickets.
You troubleshoot problems.
You help keep customers satisfied.

But have you ever wondered what comes next?

In today's IT service environment, resolving an issue is only one part of the process. Organizations also need people who understand how incidents, service requests, workflows, and business processes are managed behind the scenes.

That's where ServiceNow comes in. 🚀

ServiceNow is an enterprise platform used to manage IT services and automate workflows. For customer service representatives, help desk professionals, and call center specialists, learning the platform could open a pathway to explore roles such as:

🔹 ServiceNow Administrator — platform configuration and service management
🔹 ServiceNow Developer — scripting, business rules, and workflow customization
🔹 ITSM Analyst / Consultant — understanding service processes and helping organizations improve them
🔹 Service Desk & ITSM Specialist — building on existing support experience with platform knowledge

Your customer-handling experience isn't wasted. Understanding tickets, SLAs, escalations, and customer pain points can give you useful context when learning IT Service Management (ITSM).

Imagine moving from simply resolving tickets to understanding how the entire service workflow operates.

You don't need to become an expert overnight. Start by learning the basics of ITSM and ServiceNow, then gradually build your platform and scripting skills.
