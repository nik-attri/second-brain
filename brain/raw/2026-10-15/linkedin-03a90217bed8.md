Every time a major AI feature drops, it's always met with the exact same reassurance from industry experts: "It cannot replace humans."

Pov: Claude Dashboards

It is clear that AI labs are in an aggressive, competitive sprint. They are coming for every workflow worth optimizing.

I think of it this way: If we can automate all the processes, optimize the pipelines, and have agents build live dashboards on the fly, what is left? What happens to our saved hours?

Yes, many argue that core decisions still must be made by humans; but a team of 100 people cannot all just be high-level decision-makers when there's little to no manual execution left to do.

Something has to change in coming years/months...me or you or them.

I loved a comment by "SQLGene" on a Reddit thread on Claude dashboards: "BE THE LIGHTHOUSE WHEN THE STORM IS COMING!"

The storm is here. How are you adjusting your role to weather it?
