4 months, 121 client jobs.

From January to May 2026, I interned as a Project Coordinator in the Business Development team at IdeasLabs, a digital marketing and MarTech agency in Bangkok. 

My job was to keep content work with more than 50+ brands, moving between clients, account executives, our media team and external partners. 

What the work looked like: 

📋 121 content jobs coordinated end to end. The busiest month had 38 jobs, and 112 were completed during my placement. 

📊 46 post-campaign performance reports went to clients, each within a 7-day SLA. 

🗂️ One master tracker. I built it in Google Sheets and ran every job through it myself. It was linked to the per-page stock sheets and the intake brief form, so every job had a single source of truth. 

🤝 Capacity before commitment. I checked stock and media team capacity before confirming any timeline. When the team was full, I offered clients a paid express slot to sign off on, so rush jobs still landed on time and the team didn’t get overloaded. 

💼 Beyond the pipeline. I analysed client spending and historical sales data for a publisher business unit and designed a B2B loyalty membership structure from it. That work reached the executive team. 

Three things I learned:

1. A clear brief saves more time than a fast draft. Most feedback rounds come from a brief that wasn’t clear in the first place. 

2. Coordination is translation. Clients, AEs and the media team each talk about the same job in different terms, and my job was making sure everyone meant the same thing. 

3. Data makes hard conversations easier. When I could show the stock sheet and the team’s capacity, “not this week” became a decision we made together instead of a refusal.


Thank you to the IdeasLabs team for trusting an intern with real clients and real deadlines. 

I graduate from Thammasat in this November and I’m looking for entry-level Project Coordinator and Project Manager roles. If your team is hiring, I’d love to connect.
