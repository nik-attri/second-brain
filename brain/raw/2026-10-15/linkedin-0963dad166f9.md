One workflow was costing £690,000 a year.

That's how much annual spend one of our team helped remove in a large organisation, by redesigning a single process.

The AI piece was the least interesting part.

What made the difference was the order things were done in:
1. Understand how the work happens today
2. Measure what it costs, how long it takes and where it goes wrong
3. Redesign it, and cut the steps that shouldn't be there
4. Automate what's left, using AI only where it improves the numbers
5. Measure it again

Most AI projects jump straight to step four. That's how businesses end up with a faster version of a process that never worked well in the first place.
