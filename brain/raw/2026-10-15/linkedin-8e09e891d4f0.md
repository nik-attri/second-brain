CHURCHLADY: Well. Isn’t that special.

Two men who promised to think outside the box—and apparently had it fitted.

She studies the stitches.

Such beautiful workmanship. You can hardly see where “connecting humanity” was sewn onto “selling its attention.”

SPOCK: The image is symbolic.

CHURCHLADY: Of course, dear. When we invent a ridiculous story, it’s misinformation. When they do it, it’s a keynote.

And look—little metal staples. Apparently even the future of humanity needs a few things held together manually.

BARON: You are being rather severe.

CHURCHLADY: Oh, I admire the ambition. God took six days to make the world. These gentlemen would have launched it on Tuesday and charged extra for daylight.

She folds her hands.

Now, who approved those heads? Was it… the algorithm?

No. How silly of me.

The algorithm is only responsible when something goes wrong.


BARON: Gentlemen, we appear to have confused thinking outside the box with moving into it.

SPOCK: What do the stitches signify?

BARON: For this little allegory? The labour of making a living person fit a finished model. Trim the uncertainty. Flatten the contradictions. Stitch everything together and announce a seamless experience.

DATA: These are manufactured images. They establish nothing about the actual individuals.

BARON: Precisely! And notice how quickly we wanted them to establish something. A square head, a familiar face, a few stitches—and suddenly we feel we understand an entire human being.

SABINE: So the picture is manipulating us while accusing them of manipulation?

BARON: Now we are getting somewhere.

KIRK: Then who is on trial?

BARON: Our judgment, Captain. The screen supplies the monster. Our expectations supply the confession.

SPOCK: Category drift. A visual metaphor begins to function as evidence.

BARON: Exactly. “This picture expresses my suspicion” quietly becomes “This picture confirms my suspicion.” Nobody hears the hinges turn.

DATA: What should the viewer ask?

BARON: First: “What does this image actually show?” Then: “What did I bring to it that made its story so persuasive?”
