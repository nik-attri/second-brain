136 Fucking Things to Do Before, During & After an Event to Keep Your Budget in Check

Event Ops Series, Week 2: Budget

You know what will fuck up an otherwise beautifully executed event?

A budget that looked great on paper but was never managed in real life.

The vendor quote didnt include the service charge. Somebody added twelve guests. Production needed another piece of equipment. The client wanted a last-minute upgrade. Three invoices came in higher than expected, and suddenly youre moving money around like youre playing financial Jenga.

And everybody is looking at YOU to explain what the hell happened.

I've spent more than 20 years working across the vendor side, the property side, and the event operations side. Here's what I know: the budget doesn't manage itself just because you built a spreadsheet. You need a process. You need accountability. 

You need to know what's estimated, what's contracted, what's been paid, what's still owed, and how much money you have left when the unexpected inevitably shows up.

That's especially true when you're managing large-scale conferences like CES or the F1 Investors Conference, where vendors, production, technology, transportation, staffing, hospitality, security, and last-minute changes all have the potential to affect your bottom line.

So for Week 2 of my Event Ops Series, were talking BUDGET

Ive put together 136 tasks to work through before, during, and after an event, plus a separate list of expenses and details you dont want to overlook.

Were covering everything from getting the approved budget in writing and establishing contingency to tracking vendor payments, managing scope changes, reconciling final invoices, and figuring out where every damn dollar went.

This isn't about making a spreadsheet look pretty. Its about making financial decisions before somebody else makes them for you.Lets get into the list.
