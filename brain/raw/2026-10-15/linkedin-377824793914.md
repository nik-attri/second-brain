𝗧𝗛𝗘 𝗠𝗢𝗦𝗧 𝗩𝗔𝗟𝗨𝗔𝗕𝗟𝗘 𝗧𝗛𝗜𝗡𝗚 𝗬𝗢𝗨 𝗢𝗪𝗡 𝗠𝗔𝗬 𝗕𝗘 𝗧𝗛𝗘 𝗣𝗥𝗢𝗕𝗟𝗘𝗠 𝗬𝗢𝗨 𝗦𝗘𝗘 𝗘𝗩𝗘𝗥𝗬 𝗗𝗔𝗬.

You might think you're “just an employee.”

But after 10 or 20 years in an industry, you may be sitting on something incredibly valuable:

Pattern recognition.

The banker who knows exactly where a payment process breaks.

The consultant who sees every client rebuilding the same spreadsheet.

The lawyer watching the same compliance headache repeat across dozens of clients.

The healthcare operator who knows which workflow everyone hates.

The salesperson who keeps hearing the same customer objection.

The engineer who knows the workaround nobody outside the industry understands.

You've effectively spent years doing customer discovery without calling it customer discovery.

You know:

What breaks.
Who complains.
What people tolerate.
What they pay for.
What they work around.
What the existing software gets wrong.

That may be your unfair advantage.

Not the idea itself.

Your understanding of the problem.

The mistake is assuming that because you've identified a problem, you now need to quit your job, raise money and build a company.

You don't.

First, turn that experience into a hypothesis.

Then test it.

PROBLEM → CUSTOMER → EVIDENCE → PROTOTYPE → MARKET

That's where Midas comes in.

We want to help domain experts turn years of accumulated knowledge into something testable — using AI to move faster, experts when judgment matters, and real-world evidence to determine what should happen next.

Because sometimes the next great company doesn't begin with someone asking:

“What startup should I build?”

It begins with someone saying:

“Why the hell do we still do it this way?”

What problem do you see every day that outsiders probably don't even know exists?
