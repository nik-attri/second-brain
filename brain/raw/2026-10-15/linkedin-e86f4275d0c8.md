The faces behind the organised chaos. 

Figured we should probably introduce ourselves properly before you start trusting us with the behind-the-scenes sh*t in your business.

We're Lauren + Jayden. Married, raising twin girls, wrangling two dogs, running two businesses and occasionally wondering who the hell thought adding another business to the mix was a good idea. 

But here we are.

I love a good process, a beautifully organised system and fixing things that make absolutely no bloody sense.

He's a Chartered Accountant with the qualifications, financial knowledge and real-world business experience to back it up.

Bookkeeping, cash flow, reporting, forecasting and making sense of the numbers without making you feel like you need an accounting degree to understand them.

He's the calm to my chaos. Usually.

But here's the bit that actually matters.
We don't just have qualifications on paper.
We own and operate two very real, very busy businesses. We employ people, manage payroll, juggle expenses, navigate compliance and make the same tough decisions other business owners face every bloody day.

We've built things, broken things, fixed things and learnt plenty of lessons the expensive way.

So Haus isn't about pretending we've got business ownership completely figured out.

It's about taking everything we've learnt, everything we're qualified to do and everything we genuinely love doing, and using it to make life easier for other business owners.

Good with admin. Good with money. Pretty bloody good in your corner. 

We're Lauren + Jayden. Welcome to our Haus. 👋
