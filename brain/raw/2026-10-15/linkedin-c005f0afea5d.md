Excited to announce the launch of the Slack AI Launchpad Programme in partnership with IMDA at SMEs Go Digital Day 2026 this week!  🚀 

Special thanks to Minister Josephine Teo, and Cher Pong Ng and Johnson Poh from IMDA for partnering with us to empower local businesses.

Singapore’s SMEs are the backbone of our economy - agile, innovative, and full of potential. But lean teams often get bogged down by manual, repetitive admin work and scattered data. The agentic AI era gives SMEs an unprecedented opportunity to punch above their weight, automate routine tasks, and scale across the region.

Through this programme, participating SMEs receive: 

 🔷 60-day trial of Slack Business+ (up to 10 users) 
 🔷 2 hands-on workshops with our partners to build real AI workflows 
 🔷 Dedicated community & resources for ongoing support

My advice to SMEs? You don’t have to overhaul your business overnight. Start small, tackle one friction point in your daily workflow, and build momentum from there. Slack gives you the perfect entry point.
