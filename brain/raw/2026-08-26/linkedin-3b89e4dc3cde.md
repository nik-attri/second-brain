🚨 1 in 5 Ad Impressions Is Fake. Are You Getting Paid for Bot Traffic?

Q1 2026 data: global IVT (Invalid Traffic) rate = 18.12%. That means roughly 1 in 5 programmatic impressions shows signals of fraudulent or non-human activity.

But here's what's wild: many publishers don't even know they have an IVT problem — until buyers start pulling spend.

📊 The numbers by region (Q1 2026): 
• Asia-Pacific: 27.85% IVT rate (HIGHEST globally) 
• North America: 20.37% 
• Europe: 7.80% (lowest — GDPR enforcement correlates with cleaner traffic)

By device: 
• Desktop: 18.60% 
• Mobile: 18.16% 
• CTV: 25%+ (and as high as 46% in UK CTV!)

🔍 What counts as IVT? 
• Bot networks (40% of all fraud) — automated traffic simulating human behavior 
• Click farms (15%) — low-wage workers manually generating clicks 
• Domain spoofing (12%) — fraudsters pretending to be premium publishers 
• Ad stacking (8%) — multiple ads layered on a single placement 
• Pixel stuffing (5%) — ads rendered in 1x1 pixel iframes 
• SDK spoofing (35% of mobile fraud) — fake ad interactions injected via mobile SDKs

💰 Why publishers should care:
It's not just advertisers losing money. Publishers with high IVT: 
✗ Get filtered out of premium DSP allow-lists 
✗ See CPMs drop as buyers discount "risky" inventory 
✗ Lose direct deal opportunities (brand safety teams flag high-IVT domains) 
✗ Train optimization algorithms on garbage signals → worse performance → lower bids

📌 What to do about it:
1️⃣ Monitor IVT in real time — use HUMAN, Pixalate, or DoubleVerify 2️⃣ Implement ads.txt + sellers.json + supply chain object (schain) — verify every seller in your chain 
3️⃣ Filter pre-bid — block suspicious traffic before it enters the auction 
4️⃣ Audit traffic sources — if a traffic partner sends 40% bot traffic, cut them 
5️⃣ Be transparent with buyers — share your IVT reports proactively, build trust
