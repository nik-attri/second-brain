#hiring Supply Chain Manager Al-Futtaim Automotive - BYD Riyadh

Supply Chain Manager Al-Futtaim Automotive - BYD Riyadh The Supply Chain Manager will lead the end-to-end Parts supply chain function for BYD & Denza brands across KSA, owning forecasting, inventory planning, replenishment, and operational efficiency across the Central Distribution Center and all branch locations. The role carries direct commercial impact on Aftersales profitability, parts availability, and customer experience, and acts as the primary KSA supply chain interface with the OE principal. This is a senior leadership role critical to scaling BYD & Denzas Aftersales footprint in the Kingdom. What You Will Do  Define and execute the Parts supply chain strategy for BYD & Denza in KSA, covering forecasting, replenishment cycles, stocking norms, and obsolescence management. Build and maintain demand forecasting models aligned to vehicle parc growth, seasonality, campaigns, and warranty cycles. Set inventory targets across CDC and branches to optimize fill rates, availability, and working capital.  Inventory Management & Operations  Govern core inventory KPIs including stock turn, first-pick fill rate, back-order ratio, aging stock, and obsolescence provisions. Drive inventory accuracy through cycle counts, governance routines, and ERP discipline. Standardize branch parts operations, replenishment cycles, VOR processes, and emergency order management. Act as the primary KSA counterpart to BYD HQ supply chain teams for ordering, allocations, and supply commitments. Manage OE order cycles, escalation routes, and resolution of shortages or supply disruptions. Negotiate supply terms, pricing reviews, and service-level commitments with OE and third-party vendors.  Warehousing & Logistics  Oversee inbound and outbound logistics, customs clearance, freight optimization, and last-mile distribution to branches. Drive warehouse productivity, layout optimization, and adherence to safety, quality, and HSE standards.  Reporting & Analytics  Deliver monthly supply chain performance reviews to Aftersales and brand leadership. Build dashboards covering inventory health, OE order performance, and branch availability.  Stakeholder Management  Partner with Aftersales, Service, Sales, Finance, and IT to align supply chain decisions with commercial priorities. Support new branch openings, brand expansions, and product launch readiness from a parts perspective.  Required Skills to Be Successful  Strong commercial and analytical mindset with deep understanding of automotive parts economics. Hands-on inventory planning and demand forecasting capability. Operational excellence orientation across CDC and branch operations. Advanced ERP and BI tools usage
