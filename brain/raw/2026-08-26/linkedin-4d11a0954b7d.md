Manual Access Reviews in SAP? It’s time to let AI do the heavy lifting. 🛠️

If you’re an SAP Security or GRC Consultant, you know the pain of end-of-quarter access reviews, mitigating SoD conflicts, and chasing down terminations.

The integration of AI into SAP Security and GRC is a game-changer for practitioners. Here are 3 immediate impacts AI is having on our daily workflows:

1️⃣ Dynamic SoD Rule Sets: Instead of manually updating rule sets in SAP GRC every time a new T-Code or Fiori app is released, AI can read process changes and auto-suggest updates to your SoD rules.
2️⃣ Behavioral Access Governance: Moving beyond "what access does this user have?" to "is this user using their access in a normal way?" AI baselines user behavior and flags outliers instantly.
3️⃣ Self-Healing Provisioning: AI learns from past access requests and approvals, allowing it to auto-provision baseline access safely, leaving only the high-risk requests for human review.

The result? Less time spent on manual data crunching, and more time spent on strategic risk advising.

However, AI won't replace GRC professionals. GRC professionals using AI will replace those who don't.
