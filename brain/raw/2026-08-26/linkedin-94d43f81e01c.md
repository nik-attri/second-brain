📍 Placement: Tanah Abang, Jakarta Pusat
We are looking for a detail-oriented and responsible Finance Officer to join our team. If you have experience in finance and accounting, enjoy working with numbers, and are committed to accuracy, we'd love to hear from you!

Bachelor's Degree (S1) in Finance, Accounting, or a related field
3–5 years of experience in Finance or Accounting
Good understanding of basic accounting and finance, especially Account Receivable, Bank Reconciliation, and Invoice Management
Experienced in data entry and document management
Familiar with accounting software

Perform bank reconciliation accurately and on time
Prepare, verify, and maintain invoices and financial documents

Organize and maintain finance-related documentation
Support daily finance operations and prepare financial reports as needed
