Welcome to Beyond AI | Season 1 — Episode 7

Wednesday QuickByte

Don't automate the task. Automate the handoff.
Here's an interesting way to look at AI workflows.

A lot of time isn't spent doing the actual task.
It's spent moving information from one step to the next.

Read this → summarise it → decide what matters → draft something → review it → send it.

AI can become useful in those transitions.

Try this simple workflow:

INPUT
 What comes in?
↓
PROCESS
 What needs to happen to it?
↓
DECISION
 What still requires you?
↓
OUTPUT
 What should come out?
↓
REVIEW
 What must a human check?

That last step matters.

A workflow without review is automation.
A workflow with thoughtful review is responsible automation.

💡 Quick challenge:
Pick one recurring task today and draw these five boxes on paper.

You may discover that the problem isn't the task.
It's the way the task moves.
