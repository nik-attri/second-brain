A junior HR job in Hong Kong pays around HK$18,000 to HK$21,000 a month. You won't find that number in a single salary guide, because none of them publish it.

That's the thing about salary guides. They're written by recruitment firms, from the roles recruitment firms actually place. Which means MNCs, banks, regional mandates, and people with five years behind them.

So the ladder has a hole at both ends.

✅ At the bottom, the guides are silent. The entry-level HR job is the one most people in this city actually hold, and it isn't in the data at all.

✅ In the middle, the guides sit about 20 to 25% above what employers are really advertising. Compare the two columns on HR Officer and HR Manager and you can see the gap.

✅ At the top, only the guides speak. Head of HR is almost never advertised with a salary, so their number is the only one that exists, and nobody ever checks it.

None of this makes salary guides useless. It makes them partial. Worth knowing which part you're standing in before you walk into a pay conversation holding one.

Save this one. It's the version you'll want the next time someone tells you what the market rate is.

Checked: job ads from Jobsdb Hong Kong employer-disclosed ranges, August 2026. Guide figures from the Robert Half 2026 Hong Kong Salary Guide. Deliberately not like-for-like, and the card says so. 🔍
