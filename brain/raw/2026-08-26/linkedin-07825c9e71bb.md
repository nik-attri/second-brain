I’m curious what other accountants think about this.

When you’re at capacity, is the answer to hire another person?

A few years ago I probably would have said yes. More work means you need more people. Pretty straightforward.

I’m not so sure anymore.

Lately I’ve been looking at the work itself before deciding I need more help. How much of it are we doing manually simply because that’s how we’ve always done it? How much could be standardized across clients? How much time are experienced accountants spending pulling information together, manipulating spreadsheets or doing repetitive work before they ever get to the part that actually requires their experience?

I’m not suggesting we can automate an entire accounting department, nor would I want to. There is still a lot of judgment involved in what we do.

But I do think technology has changed the point at which we need to hire.

My thought right now is to clean up the process first, automate the pieces that make sense, and then see where I truly need another person.

I’m curious how others are approaching this. Are you still hiring as workload increases, or are you finding that automation is allowing you to push that hiring point out further?
