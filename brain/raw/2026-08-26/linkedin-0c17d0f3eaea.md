Identify risks, resolve issues and make informed decisions to support successful change outcomes.

✅ Change Management certification

✅ Experience delivering change across Technology and Workforce Operations initiatives
✅ Strong stakeholder engagement and influencing skills
✅ Excellent written, verbal, presentation and facilitation skills
✅ Experience developing communication and change strategies
✅ Understanding of project management and project lifecycle
✅ Ability to work with and influence senior leaders
