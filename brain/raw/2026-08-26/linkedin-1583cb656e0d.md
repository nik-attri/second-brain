📡 HELLOGEO POINT | PRECISION CAPTURE
HelloGeo Point is our compact, AI-ready RTK GNSS hardware built for professional field operations. Connected to MetaSync through Bluetooth, it turns a smartphone into a powerful precision-survey terminal—delivering RTK Fixed positioning accuracy up to ±2 cm with real-time NTRIP corrections.

📲 METASYNC | VALIDATE, ORGANIZE & SYNC
MetaSync brings field intelligence into one seamless mobile experience:

✅ Instant switching between mobile GPS and RTK positioning
✅ Live accuracy, altitude, signal and fix-quality monitoring
✅ GCP and professional field-point collection
✅ WGS 84, UTM zones and EPSG-based coordinate systems
✅ Bubble-level guidance for precise measurements
✅ CSV point import and standard-format export
✅ Geo-referenced photo and video evidence
✅ Interactive map visualization
✅ Secure field-to-cloud project synchronization
