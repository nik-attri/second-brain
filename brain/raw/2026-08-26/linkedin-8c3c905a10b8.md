We’re #Hiring: Frontliner / Front Office

📍 Placement: Jakarta Pusat

Qualifications:
Bachelor’s Degree (S1) from any major
Fluent in English, both spoken and written
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Willing to work in a 3-shift system
Working hours: 7 hours/day
Previous experience in Frontliner, Front Office, Customer Service, or hospital/healthcare services is a plus

What You’ll Do:
Serve patients and their families in a friendly, professional, and responsive manner
Handle patient registration and administrative processes according to hospital procedures

Direct patients to the appropriate clinic, room, or facility
Handle patient inquiries, complaints, and requests professionally
Escalate patient concerns or issues to the relevant department when necessary
Coordinate with Customer Service, Admission, Cashier, Medical Records, Nursing, and other related departments
Ensure patient data and administrative documents are recorded accurately
Maintain service quality and patient satisfaction according to hospital standards
Perform administrative tasks and data entry using Microsoft Office or the hospital’s system
Maintain the confidentiality of patient data and information
Work according to the hospital’s operational schedule, including 3-shift rotations
