Well… here it is: I’m getting back into freelancing 🤠 

After just over two years working “client side” at Parks New Brunswick, I’ve realized that I actually really miss being the person who gets dropped into a project, figures out what the hell is going on, gets everyone organized, and somehow gets the thing across the finish line 🤷‍♀️

Before working with Parks NB, I spent 8+ years working agency-side and 2+ years as a freelance/white-label project manager for small and mid-sized agencies. And honestly? That was probably my sweet spot. I love the part where a team has a big project coming up and needs someone experienced to just… take it and run with it.

A six-month website project? Yep.

A rebrand that needs someone to keep all the moving pieces together? Absolutely.

Someone going on mat leave and you need a senior PM to jump in for a few months? Sure.

A project that has gotten a little chaotic and needs rescuing? I’m on it 🫡

I’m not looking for a traditional full-time role right now. I’m looking for fractional project management work, where I can come in, integrate quickly with a team, bring some structure, get the work moving, and be there for as long as I’m actually needed.

I’ve worked in marketing for over 10 years now. Everything from websites, digital and traditional campaigns, paid media, email/CRM, rebrands, events, apps, analytics, UX, QA, strategy, process development… basically, if it lives somewhere in the marketing ecosystem, I’ve probably had my hands in it. 

I’m also a designer by training, bilingual, slightly obsessed with making unnecessarily complicated spreadsheets useful, and very comfortable jumping into whatever project management software a team happens to be using.

So yes. I’m officially opening the doors again 😁

This time, though, I’m being a little more intentional about the kind of work I take on. I’d LOVE to work more with clients/organizations in eco tourism, outdoor recreation, health & wellness, hospitality and other purpose-driven spaces—or with companies building products and services for those industries.

I’m looking for interesting projects, good people, remote work, and enough autonomy to actually do what I’m good at.

If you’re an agency owner, marketing lead, former colleague, former client, or just someone who knows a team that could use a senior marketing PM for a few months (or a bigger project)… Hi, I’m available 🙋‍♀️

I’m pretty good under pressure, I’m not afraid to climb high, and I seem to have developed a surprisingly high tolerance for projects that are on fire. So maybe the climbing thing checks out 🧗‍♀️😉
