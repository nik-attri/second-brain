How I reduced a 3 hour data input task into a 15 minute process using Claude and Airtable.

If you're a marketing pro still hand-keying data from PDFs into your systems, this is worth 20 minutes of your time to try. The setup is quite simple, and multiplied across a network the size of ours, the time saved adds up fast.

Here’s my first post of automating workflows using AI. In this write up I use Claude, Airtable (Airtable is a cloud-based tool that blends a spreadsheet with a database) and Asana.

If your current problem is having to manually input data into some form of platform such as Airtable. Stick around.

In my current role as a campaign specialist at ACG Schools (part of Inspired Education Group - 125 schools, 95k students across 30 countries) my job is crm/lifecycle marketing and campaigns, but I also enjoyed efficiency and the elimination of tedious work.

So our head office rolled out a new process, and I really did not want to input more data into another workflow.

We used Asana for briefing projects. The majority of these were for open day and always on campaigns which equated to 100+ a year. Everytime I created a brief we had to manually copy our brand plan info into the platform This included info like customer demographics, USP’s positioning etc

This is where Airtable drops in. 

The task, manually input 4 schools and 4 ELS’s brand plan into Airtable so Asana could pull the data automatically without having  manually input into each brief.

However, that data needs to go into Airtable.

So naturally, I jumped in Claude up to see if he was up to the task.

The situation: Asana pulls the brand plan from Airtable into every brief, cutting out 25 manual write-ups a quarter. The data still needed rebuilding in Airtable across our 7 NZ sites.

The task: Get the brand plan data for 4 schools + 3 ELS centres into Airtable, accurately, without losing a day to it.

The action: I asked Claude "can this be done?" and as always, he said yes. I connected Airtable through the connector, dropped in a few instructions, and let it read the brand plan PDF straight into the base.

The result: First time it took 30 minutes total, start to finish. What used to be 2-3 hours of manual entry. The remaining 6 schools? 15 mins each.

Here's the TLDR:
Asked Claude if it could pull PDF data into Airtable. Yes
Connected the Airtable base via the connector app. (Surprisingly easy.)
Gave it a few plain-language instructions on what to extract and where it goes.
Let it do its thing for 5 or so minutes.
Went and checked the output for accuracy, fixed a couple of missing fields manually.
Done.

No scripts. No custom integration. No IT ticket. Just a connected tool and clear instructions.
