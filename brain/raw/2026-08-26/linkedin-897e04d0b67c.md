I’m beginning to realize data privacy is basically asking: “Wait… where the hell is all our data?” 😂

Used to think it was JUST about keeping customer data safe.

The more I learn, the more I realize the problem is knowing what you even have in the first place. 

CRM.
Emails.
Spreadsheets.
Marketing tools.
Old databases.
That random SaaS tool someone signed up for 3 years ago.

Kinda wild how quickly important data can end up everywhere. And, uh… that’s not good. 

Still learning a ton, but I’m starting to understand why this stuff gets complicated FAST. 🤓
