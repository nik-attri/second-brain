Meta is slowly taking the “manual” out of Meta Ads.

And this might be one of the biggest changes yet.

Meta has started notifying advertisers that placement exclusions at the ad-set level are going away.

That means advertisers are set to lose the ability to manually exclude:

Individual placements  
Entire platforms  
Devices  
Operating systems

So strategies like “Instagram only” or manually removing placements that consistently bring poor-quality traffic will become much harder at the ad-set level.

Meta's answer?

Advantage+ Placements.

Essentially: give the algorithm more control and let Meta decide where your money should go.

There will still be some account-level placement controls and placement value rules, so this isn't literally zero control.

But the direction is very clear.

Audience targeting became more automated.

Campaign structures became more automated.

Creative optimization became more automated.

And now placement decisions are moving the same way.

I've always preferred controlling placements when the data gives me a reason to.

Especially in India, where a cheap result from one placement doesn't necessarily mean a quality result.

So I'm very interested to see what happens when advertisers have less ability to make that decision themselves.

Meta believes its algorithm knows where your ₹1 should be spent better than you do.

Soon, we may have very little choice but to test that theory.
