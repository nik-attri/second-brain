You don't scale by hiring. You scale by removing steps.

The instinct when volume grows is to add people. But look at what actually slows things down: a lead comes in, someone has to manually triage it, then route it, then wait for whoever's free to pick it up. Add ten more leads and you don't just need more work done — you need more coordination, more managing, more onboarding.

Now remove step 2 and 3. The lead gets auto-routed and handled the moment it arrives. Same team, same headcount, and suddenly they're not drowning in triage — they're doing the actual work.

Scaling isn't about adding capacity to a broken process. It's about fixing the process so the capacity you already have goes further.

What's the one manual step in your workflow that, if automated, would let your current team handle way more?
