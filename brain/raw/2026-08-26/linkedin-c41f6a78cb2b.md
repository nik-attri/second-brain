"Did I already send this to them?"

Ah yes. The traditional independent-musician CRM: your inbox, your sent folder, a spreadsheet you haven't opened since March, three sticky notes, and the astonishingly confident belief that you'll remember.

You will not remember.

You'll remember something.

"Didn't she like that track? Or hate it? Wait… was that Jennifer? Who the hell is Jennifer?"

Meanwhile, somebody asked you six weeks ago for something "a little darker but still hopeful" and is presumably still waiting for the thing you said you'd "absolutely send tomorrow."

The opportunity usually isn't lost because you forgot the song. It's lost because you forgot the conversation.

So — do you track your pitches, or is it all stored in the same part of your brain responsible for remembering why you walked into the kitchen?
