Inspecting offshore wind farms without costly crew, climbing equipment and large vessels? Nowadays, it is already partly a reality for inland wind parks, where turbine parts are inspected by manually operated drones with standard cameras. But offshore is a different story.

The ITEA 4 ADVISOR project strives for autonomous multimodal inspection of maritime wind parks not only above, but also below the water level. Partners from the Netherlands, Denmark, Portugal, Turkey and South Korea are joining their forces to develop the solution with autonomous carriers and multi-modal sensing.

 🚤 The Dutch consortium, supported by the Netherlands Enterprise Agency, is focusing on unmanned inspection of wind turbines above water level. Here, a mother-USV (unmanned vessel) launching her UAV drones with electro-optical, thermal and acoustic sensors is tasked to arrive to an offshore wind farm and perform NDT inspectiond autonomously.

 🛸 Few days ago, thanks to the support of Tsjerk Kooistra, we have visited one of the Dutch wind park and performed a set of UAV test flights. The flights were operated by the project partners Avular and Dutch Drone Company. The most important part was the data collection by the Sorama acoustic array sensors and high-speed RGB cameras. 

 🎥 At AIMS lab TUe, we use this data to train multi-modal sensor fusion models for defect and anomaly detection. These models close the cycle of autonomous transportation, inspection and analysis to enable cost-saving automated maintenance.

 😈 Sounds all easy and smooth, but the 'devil' is in the details:
- how to solve the blur effect in images of high-spin blades?
- how do we account for yet unseen defects and anomalies?
- how to land a drone on an unmanned vessel bobbing on the waves?
- drone stabilization in the turbulent vortices,
- drone control in the GNSS-denied windpark environment, etc. 

For our research team at Eindhoven University of Technology this was especially important day, since available data on blade defects is very scarce. If you know a way to such data - let me know!

The team of the day: Tsjerk Kooistra, Carlos Kasteel, Leroy van Staveren, Corentin Monat, Camile Lendering, Umesh Kumar and Ruben Koets.
