🚀 We're Hiring | HR Manager

We are looking for an experienced and results-driven HR Manager to lead our Human Resources function in a manufacturing environment.

The ideal candidate will have strong HR leadership experience, a solid understanding of manufacturing operations, and the ability to align HR strategies with business objectives.

Key Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field.
- Minimum 10 years of progressive HR experience, including 3–5 years in an HR Manager role.
- Previous experience in a manufacturing environment is mandatory.

Key Responsibilities

- Develop and implement HR strategies aligned with organizational goals.
- Lead workforce planning and talent acquisition, particularly for technical and production positions.
- Oversee performance management, employee development, and succession planning.
- Manage compensation, benefits, and employee relations.
- Ensure compliance with Jordanian Labor Law and company policies.
- Develop and maintain HR policies, procedures, and organizational development initiatives.
- Monitor HR KPIs and prepare periodic management reports.
- Identify training needs and support employee competency development.

Technical Requirements

- Hands-on experience with (KAYAN HR) System.
- Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.
- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
- Knowledge of Power BI is an advantage.

Manufacturing & Technical Knowledge

- Workforce planning based on production requirements.
- Shift scheduling and overtime management.
- Understanding of Lean Manufacturing and Continuous Improvement concepts.
- Knowledge of ISO 9001:2015, competency management, and audit processes.
- Good understanding of Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) practices.

Leadership Competencies

- Strong leadership and team management skills.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
- Strategic thinking and business acumen.
- Strong negotiation and conflict resolution skills.
- High level of integrity, professionalism, and confidentiality.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making abilities.
- Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively.
