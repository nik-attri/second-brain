🚨 We run an AI automation agency.
We charge companies to automate their entire workflow.
And yet, inside our own business? We only use AI for roughly 40% of the work.
Not 90%. Not 100%. Just 40%.
We don't let AI reply to client emails. We don't let autonomous agents handle sensitive outreach. And we never let an LLM touch final decision-making.
Why? Because lazy founders automate everything to save 10 minutes, and end up destroying client trust in 10 seconds.
Here is our exact operating rule:
• AI handles the brutal grunt work (40%): Data scraping, syntax formatting, parsing messy sheets, and initial research summaries.
• Humans handle the high-leverage leverage (60%): Context, critical thinking, final communication, and closing deals.
AI is an intern with infinite stamina—not the executive.
The moment you hand over your personal brand, client interactions, and nuance to a bot, you aren't running an efficient agency. You’re just broadcasting that you don't care enough to show up.
Use AI to build leverage, not to become absent.
