I used to push warehouse trolleys. Now I'm building software to solve a problem I experienced while doing that job.

This isn't a story about having a perfect business idea. It's about experiencing a problem firsthand and deciding to do something about it.

While working in a wholesale warehouse, I noticed something frustrating.

Sometimes the inventory system showed that products were available, but when we physically checked the warehouse, the quantities didn't match.

And every time goods arrived or left, workers had to spend time manually entering product names and quantities from delivery notes and invoices.

I kept thinking: Why are we still entering everything manually when the information is already on the document?

That question inspired me to build Stockistry.

With Stockistry, you can:

- Take a picture or upload a delivery note or invoice.
- Automatically extract product names and quantities.
- Review and confirm the information.
- Process inventory increases or decreases.

My goal is to make inventory management simpler for wholesale businesses and reduce repetitive manual work.

And here's something important:

Stockistry is available to try for free, without requiring a credit card.

I'm sharing it because I want real wholesale businesses and warehouse teams to experience it, test it, and give me honest feedback.

I didn't build this because I wanted to create another ERP.

I built it because I experienced a problem and wanted to solve it.
