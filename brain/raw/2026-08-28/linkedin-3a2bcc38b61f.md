I spent an evening turning a Kaggle dataset into a Fantasy Premier League dashboard with Luzmo. Building it took about 20 minutes. Picking my actual team will still take me all week and go wrong anyway, when Maresca has had a few drinks the night before and decides to bench Haaland describing my luck I've had in GW1.

There was no code, no data team, no spreadsheet hell - just messy football data in and clean insight out.

The dashboard breaks down form, price, points per million, ownership - everything you'd need to make a smart, data-backed captaincy call this week.
