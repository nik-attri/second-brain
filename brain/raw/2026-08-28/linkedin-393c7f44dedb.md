A business can change so gradually that its finance operation never realizes it’s supporting an entirely different company.

More employees. More locations. More transactions. More complexity. More responsibility.

But somehow, the approvals, spreadsheets, workarounds, reporting processes, and controls still look remarkably familiar.

At some point, there’s a question worth asking:
Who the hell are you, anyway?

Our latest AxLG Insight looks at what happens when the business has evolved—but finance operations haven’t caught up.

And I’m especially curious to hear from business owners and finance leaders:
What was the first sign that made you realize your company had outgrown the way its finance operation was working?
