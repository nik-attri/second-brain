150 boxes a day. Six months. $89,100 saved.
Here's how a record label did it.
Before Ship Store, they were drowning in complexity.

150 shipments daily meant constant decisions about carriers, rates, and routing. Manual processes were eating time and bleeding money across 39,000 annual shipments.

The challenge was clear: too many moving parts, not enough visibility, and shipping costs that kept climbing.

Here's what we did:
↳ Integrated Ship Store between their operation and carriers
↳ Automated carrier selection using their existing rates and rules
↳ Eliminated manual decision-making on every single box
↳ Optimized service levels without disrupting workflows
The results hit fast.

Average shipping cost dropped 12%. At their volume, that translated to $2.25 saved per shipment. Do the math on 150 boxes daily and you're looking at $337.50 per day, $7,425 monthly, and a projected $89,100 in annual savings. But the real win wasn't just the money.

The shipping team stopped thinking about every individual box. Complexity vanished. Carrier utilization improved. The operation ran smoother with less effort, not more.

One team member told us: "We didn't have to reinvent anything. It just works in the background and saves us money."

That's proof that results don't require revolution, just the right system making better decisions automatically.
