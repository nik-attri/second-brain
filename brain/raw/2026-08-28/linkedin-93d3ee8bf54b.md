Ever have a life event that isn’t directly happening to you, but somehow rearranges everything inside of you?

Yeah. That.

Lately, I feel like I’m ten steps behind while simultaneously being pulled ten steps ahead. Make that make sense.

There’s this strange place in life where you can be deeply grateful for everything that was and still know, with every part of you, that you’re not supposed to stay there.

And whew… that part will mess with you.

Because what happens when you finally meet your people? The people who see you. Get you. Root for you. Laugh with you. Hold you down. And then you realize that loving people doesn’t always mean they’re meant to come with you into every next chapter.

Nobody prepares you for that kind of growth.

Then there’s the calling.

That damn calling.

The thing that keeps tapping you on the shoulder when you’re trying to be responsible. The idea that has you waking up at ridiculous hours because apparently sleep is optional when you’re building something you believe in. The project that makes you so excited you could scream and puts so much pressure on you that five minutes later you’re wondering, “Whose idea was this?”

Mine.

It was my idea.

That’s where I’m at.

Grieving some things. Celebrating others. Holding people close while loosening my grip. Feeling behind. Feeling called. Feeling pressure. Feeling excited as hell.

I have been so happy for everything that was.

But I think I’m even more excited about what’s next.

And here’s the wild part: I don’t fully know what “next” is yet.

For someone who likes a strategy, a framework, a timeline, a backup timeline, and probably a spreadsheet somewhere… that is RUDE.

But maybe this season isn’t asking me to have all the answers.

Maybe it’s asking me to trust the woman I’ve become enough to walk toward what feels right, even before I can see where it leads.

So I’m going.

A little tired. A little emotional. A lot excited.

And still dancing to my own damn beat.

If you’re standing somewhere between what was and what’s next too, tell me: What are you being called toward, even if you don’t have it all figured out yet?
