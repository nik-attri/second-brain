Hiring a Front Desk Office Administrator / Office Administrator to be based out at: Albany, NY (100% On-site / No Travel) for a 2+ years contract.
  
About the Role: 
  
The client is seeking a highly organized, proactive, and polished Front Desk Office Administrator to join the team. In this key role, you will serve as the primary face of our office, welcoming visitors, supporting facility operations, and ensuring smooth daily business workflows. 
  
The ideal candidate possesses exceptional communication skills, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and stays calm and composed under pressure—whether assisting external guests or redirecting high-priority inquiries from Senior Executives and Elected Officials.
  
Key Responsibilities: 
  
Front Desk & Visitor Management: Greet all visitors, manage front desk operations, and guide guests through required on-site security and sign-in protocols.

Communications & External Relations: Direct incoming calls, triage requests, and      manage organization-wide announcements related to building and facility updates. Confidently handle inquiries from Senior Executives, Elected Officials, and key stakeholders by taking precise messages or routing them to the appropriate back-up contacts.

Mail & Logistics: Accept, sort, and deliver incoming mail; process outgoing shipping transactions via UPS and FedEx.

Facility & Kitchen Support: Assist with office space maintenance, including      kitchen organization and managing office compost programs (including      handling compost buckets). Support additional facility team initiatives as assigned.

Administrative  Operations: Manage room schedules, coordinate office events, and      perform accurate data entry and reporting.
  
Qualifications & Requirements:
  
Education:      High School Diploma or equivalent.
Experience:    2+ years of administrative, receptionist, or office support experience.
Technical Proficiency: Hands-on experience with Microsoft Office 365 (Outlook,      SharePoint, Word, Excel).
  
Core Competencies:
  
Organizational Mastery & Attention to Detail: Self-motivated with a high degree      of precision in executing tasks and managing multiple shifting priorities simultaneously.

Composure  & Crisis Management: Proven ability to remain calm, professional, and efficient during high-stress situations.

Clear Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills to deliver status updates, ask clarifying questions, and interact professionally with internal leaders and external guests.
