Yesterday a client told me I'm good at staying grounded in her larger vision while also meeting her in whatever moment she's in and helping her figure out the best next step. 

When I talk about helping creative visionaries do the thing that matters, this is what I mean. 

Not #hustleculture. Not, "here is a 7-month project plan that fills 6 Excel spreadsheets and I will push you and you will do every single task on time or there will be hell to pay."  

Instead: "Ok, we've gotten clear on the big picture of what you're trying to create or bring into being. We know what success will feel like and look like. Maybe we've created a loose sense of key steps. Now, let's arrive in this present moment. What is happening right now? There are competing priorities. Given your limited time and energy, given your deep inner wisdom, what is the next right thing?"

Strategy and improvisation are both in my DNA. So is a deep commitment to the power of getting still and connecting to the wisest part of ourselves. 

When you work with me, it's not about a grind. It's about a softening, a relaxation into who you truly are, so you can make the work you truly want to make.
