Camphouse is heading to Chicago.

We're excited to be the Title Sponsor of MediaPost Planning & Buying Insider Summit, happening September 8 to 11 at the Pendry Chicago. For three days, we'll join 100 top practitioners who make key decisions about media spending.


This year's theme, "Precision in the Plan," is something we talk about with our customers every day. Budgets are tighter. There are more channels than ever. AI is changing how we build and buy plans. Precision is no longer optional, but it's hard to achieve when your plans, budgets, and results are spread across different spreadsheets.

Sean Mooney, our Senior Director of Brand & Agency Growth, will give a keynote on this topic. He'll talk about how planning and buying teams can work differently when every plan is backed by a single source of truth.

If you're attending, please stop by and say hello. We'd love to hear how you approach precision in your work.
