Automation isn’t “coming soon” to independent pharmacies — it’s already in the building, reorganizing your workflow, judging your sticky notes, and filling 200 vials before you’ve finished your first sip of coffee.

Across the U.S., only 12–18% of independent pharmacies** currently use meaningful automation. But over the next 12–24 months, that number is expected to jump to 20–30% — and in Florida, where burnout is basically a weather pattern, automation adoption is projected to hit 25–35%.

Let’s be honest:
Pharmacists aren’t scared of robots.
Pharmacists are scared of being left alone with 400 scripts, 2 techs, and a printer that jams only when you’re already behind.

Here’s the real story:
Chains automate to cut labor.
Independents automate because they actually want you to stay alive and maybe even smile once in a while.

What’s being automated?
Dispensing robots (tiny coworkers with perfect attendance)

Workflow automation (because sticky notes are not a system)

Inventory automation (no more scavenger hunts for Lisinopril)

Telepharmacy (remote verification = remote sanity)
