Many companies or clients ask for WFH (Work From Home) experience when hiring.

But I have a question:
How can we gain WFH experience if no one gives us the first chance?

Not everyone is strong in verbal interviews. Some people feel nervous while speaking. But that does not mean they cannot do the work well.
I may not be very confident in verbal interviews, but I perform well in tasks such as:

• Customer Service Representative
• Order Entry Specialist
• Document and file organization
• Calendar Organizer
• Data Entry
• Live Chat handling and customer communication

I believe skills, dedication, and willingness to learn are more important than speaking abilities.
I hope companies will give opportunities to people who are ready to work hard and grow, even if they don't have previous WFH experience.
Everyone deserves a first chance. Do you agree with me?
