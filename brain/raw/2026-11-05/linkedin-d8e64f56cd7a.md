8x8 delivered another strong performance in Q2 FY2027, with a solid "beat and raise," while raising full-year outlook across the board.

Over the past 12 months, $EGHT shares are up +27%, reflecting strong operational discipline and accelerating momentum.

The catalyst behind this growth? This shouldn't be a surprise but it's native AI is driving platform adoption and utilization revenue. 

As CEO Samuel C Wilson noted during the call: "Our promise is to make every conversation count. AI gives that promise new power. It can automate the work a conversation sets in motion, help people resolve problems faster and turn conversation data into knowledge that makes the next interaction more useful."

Key operational and AI highlights from the quarter:
58% YoY Growth in AI Recurring Revenue: AI solutions are scaling rapidly across enterprise workflows.
Increasing AI Attach: Customers using at least one AI solution now represent ~13% of total recurring revenue (nearly doubling from 7% a year ago).
Platform Usage Surged 51% YoY: Driven by voice/digital AI interactions and communications APIs, reaching all-time record levels.
AI Studio Adoption Booming: Interactions powered by AI Studio surged ~450% since April as healthcare, IT, and utility enterprises deploy real-world AI agents to production.
Multi-Product Expansion: Customers deploying 4+ revenue-producing products grew over 50% YoY.

AI isn't just an add-on feature—it's creating an operational flywheel. As Wilson put it:
"A customer solves one problem, sees the value, identifies another use case and builds another agent. Partners can multiply this effect... It’s a classic flywheel."

With 6 consecutive quarters of YoY revenue growth, 23 consecutive quarters of positive operating cash flow, and more than $250M in principal debt paid down from peak levels, 8x8 is proving that AI innovation paired with balance sheet discipline delivers sustainable value.
