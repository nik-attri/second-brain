🚀 WE’RE HIRING | Accounting Manager / Chief Accountant

We’re looking for an experienced Accounting Manager to lead our accounting operations and join our Finance team!

📍 Location: Nasr City, Cairo
⏰ Full-time | Reports to the Finance Director / CFO

🔹 Key Responsibilities:

Supervise day-to-day accounting operations, journal entries, and the document cycle.
Manage month-end, quarter-end, and year-end closing within agreed deadlines.
Prepare draft financial statements and analytical reports for the Finance Director.
Lead and develop the accounting team (AR, AP, Fixed Assets, Inventory, Banks).
Oversee inventory counts and fixed assets accounting.
Ensure full tax compliance (VAT, withholding, payroll taxes, e-invoicing).
Coordinate with external auditors and tax authorities.
Strengthen internal controls and accounting policies.

🔹 Requirements:

Bachelor’s degree in Commerce (Accounting), grade Good or above.
Professional certifications (CMA, CPA, DipIFR) or an MBA in Finance are preferred.
7 to 10 years of accounting experience, including at least 3 years as Accounting Manager or Senior Chief Accountant.
Experience in the automotive, large retail, or distribution sector is a plus.
Deep knowledge of IFRS, local accounting standards, taxation, and social insurance laws.
Advanced experience with ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, Odoo, Microsoft Dynamics).
Expert-level Excel skills (Power Query, Pivot Tables, financial models).
Strong leadership, analytical, and communication skills, with good business English.
Ability to work under tight closing deadlines.
