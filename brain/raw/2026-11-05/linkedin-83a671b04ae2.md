A whole year can pass while you are still thinking about starting.

As we approach the end of 2026, I have been reflecting on something interesting.

I have come across enquiries from people who expressed interest in our digital skills and AI training programmes as far back as January. Some asked for details, some requested training information, and others said they would enrol. Yet, here we are in November, and they still haven’t taken that first step.

It got me thinking about one of the biggest obstacles to personal and professional growth: PROCRASTINATION.

Sometimes, it is not that we lack opportunities. It is not that we lack information. It is not even that we lack resources. We simply keep postponing the decisions that could move us forward.

We say:

* I will enrol next month.
* I will learn AI when I have more time.
* I will start that business next year.
* I will upgrade my skills when things settle down.

Before we realise it, 12 months have passed, and we are still having the same conversations with ourselves.

2027 must be different!

I am considering hosting a special Year-End Strategy and AI Planning Session to help individuals, entrepreneurs and professionals deliberately prepare for 2027.

This will not just be another motivational session. It will be a practical opportunity to rethink our goals, identify the gaps holding us back and develop an actionable plan for the new year.

We will explore:

1. Personal and Professional Strategy: How to reflect on 2026, identify lessons and set meaningful goals for 2027.
2. Breaking the Procrastination Cycle: Moving from intention to action and building accountability.
3. AI for Personal Productivity: How to use AI tools to plan, organise, research and manage our time better.
4. AI for Business Growth: How entrepreneurs can leverage AI to innovate, automate workflows, improve marketing and increase productivity.
5. Skills for 2027: Identifying the digital and AI skills we need to remain relevant and competitive.
6. Your 90-Day Action Plan: Turning your 2027 resolutions into practical actions that you can start implementing immediately.

My biggest takeaway from this year is that opportunities are valuable, but opportunities acted upon are transformative.

Don’t carry the same unfulfilled intentions from 2026 into 2027.

You don’t need another year of saying “I will start.” You need a strategy, a decision and the discipline to execute.

Let’s stop postponing our growth. Let’s plan, position ourselves and make 2027 count!
