90% of work is the boring stuff, but it is necessary and worth it for the remaining 10%, when you get to enjoy what you do and sometimes celebrate big wins.

No one tells you that much of the work is simply about doing the necessary, repetitive, and sometimes the hardest things. The public sees only the results and judges whether they like them or not.

So, we always have a choice: do everything manually and spend most of our time repeating the same tasks, or delegate some of them to automation. It does things much faster, giving you more time for meaningful work where you can add something of your own.

This is what I like about Generative AI. I am against handing over everything to a robot. I think it is a big mistake. After all, how much of you is there in the product you are creating?

AI can be a good assistant that helps you do things better and faster. So, perhaps you can make 50% of your work fun instead of just 10%.

And this is exactly what I was thinking about during my recent AI Bootcamp at Yellow Park in Barcelona.

I had a unique chance to learn things I had missed or simply never thought about before. I realized that even though I use AI every day, there are still important tips and tricks that can make our work much easier.

Now, I am an AI Champion. It sounds a bit too grand, but I came away inspired to do things I couldn't do before.

Of course, I still decide how much power I want to give AI and how much I want to keep for myself.

But first of all, I want me in my work.
