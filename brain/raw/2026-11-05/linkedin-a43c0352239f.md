We are taking detailed notes while pre-screening candidates against job description.

We are understanding Job Description(& requirements beyond job description) from Hiring Manager OR HR of client before sourcing candidates.

We are doing two-level screening of resumes before sharing it to clients.

Yes It is time consuming & AI can do it fast but we do it with the best quality we can do.

AI can reduce time of sourcing, we can reduce time of hiring & increase quality of hiring.
