🌍 The computational expertise behind Earth system science

How can we make Earth system models run more efficiently, overcome computational challenges, and turn complex scientific data into useful information?

At the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, the Computational Earth Sciences Group – BSC (CES) brings together researchers and engineers in computing who work closely with the other research groups in the Earth Sciences Department.

💻 Our work focuses on:
🔹 High-performance computing (HPC): optimising the use of supercomputing resources to tackle demanding scientific problems.

🔹 Models and workflows: developing and improving software, tools and workflows that help researchers run complex simulations more efficiently.

🔹 Data and diagnostics: supporting the processing, analysis and visualisation of scientific data.

🔹 Artificial intelligence (AI): developing machine learning methods and AI agents to accelerate simulations, improve predictive skill, automate scientific workflows and open up new possibilities for Earth system research.

🤝 By combining computational expertise with close collaboration across scientific teams, we help researchers overcome technical challenges, improve their workflows, and advance Earth system research.

Our mission is to make scientific computing more efficient, accessible and effective, enabling new possibilities for Earth sciences.
