📢 WE ARE HIRING!

Position: Data Entry Site Officer

📍 Location: Project Site / Camp / Office
🏗️ Industry: Construction / Marine Construction / Dredging / Contracting

Job Responsibilities:

- Enter and maintain daily site operational data, project records, and progress reports.
- Maintain manpower attendance, timesheets, overtime, leave, and employee movement records.
- Monitor equipment, machinery, vessel, and vehicle utilization, including operating hours and breakdowns.
- Maintain records of materials received, issued, and consumed at the project site.
- Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly reports and ensure timely submission to management.
- Coordinate with site supervisors, engineers, HR, procurement, and head office for data collection and documentation.
- Maintain accurate hard-copy and electronic filing systems for site records, delivery notes, invoices, and supporting documents.
- Ensure data accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance with company procedures.

Requirements:

- Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Computer Applications, Accounting, or a related field.
- Minimum 2–3 years of relevant experience in data entry or site administration within the construction, marine construction, dredging, or contracting industry.
- Previous experience working at construction project sites or camps is preferred.
- Good knowledge of MS Excel, Word, and Outlook.
- Experience with ERP systems, preferably Microsoft Dynamics 365, will be an advantage.
- Strong data entry skills, attention to detail, and good organizational abilities.
- Ability to coordinate with multiple departments and meet strict deadlines.
- Willingness to work at project sites and extend working hours as required.
