Chaos. Order. Repeat. The BI pendulum is swinging again.

Remember Excel hell? Everyone had their own spreadsheet, their own calculations, and their own version of the truth. Meetings became debates over whose numbers were right instead of what to do with them.

Business intelligence brought order through centralized dashboards, certified data, and consistent metrics.

Then came self-service BI. Business users gained the freedom to explore data and answer questions independently, but that flexibility eventually recreated the same challenges: conflicting calculations, inconsistent definitions, and multiple versions of the truth.

Now, AI is accelerating the cycle. Anyone can upload data, ask a question in plain English, and get a convincing answer in seconds. But just because an answer sounds right doesn't mean it's grounded in the right data or definitions.

The answer isn't to restrict innovation or eliminate self-service. It's to build governance into the foundation, giving users the freedom to explore while ensuring every answer is grounded in trusted, consistent business context.

The next era of BI isn't about choosing between freedom and control. It's about making both possible.
