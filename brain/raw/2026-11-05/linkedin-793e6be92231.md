🕸️ Is Google showing different results to rank-tracking tools?
Nozzle analysed 29.9 million SERPs and found some surprising anomalies.
The numbers:
• 22.6% of SERPs were anomalous.
• Peak anomaly rate: 43.5%.
• 60.8% of affected SERPs saw the tracked site disappear from the top 10.
• 67.9% returned to normal within 3 days.
• Page two was 1.5x more affected than page one.
Why does it matter?
We often blame sudden ranking drops on Google updates, technical issues or competitors.
But what if the tracking data itself is wrong?
Researchers suggest Google may be altering results to disrupt automated scraping. This remains unconfirmed.
What should SEOs do?
→ Cross-check rankings with Google Search Console.
→ Compare multiple tracking tools.
→ Verify unexpected drops manually.
→ Check impressions and clicks before making changes.
Not every ranking drop is an SEO problem. Sometimes, the data is the problem.
