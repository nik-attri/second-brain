We were bleeding 20 full staff-days every single reconciliation cycle. 

Just gone. 

20 delivery technicians spending nearly a full day each tracking down missing stock, hunting through a chaotic warehouse, and fixing manual spreadsheets. 

Not because they were slow. 

Because our process was broken.

So we finally fixed it:

→ Wrote a dead-simple standard inventory SOP
→ Reorganized the entire warehouse with structured stacking and clear barcodes
→ Swapped manual guesswork for a barcode reconciliation flow

Of course, the team pushed back at first. 

Nobody loves a new system when they are already drowning in work. 

Instead of forcing it down from the top, I showed them the math: this cuts your discrepancy headaches so you can actually finish your deliveries and get home on time. 

Once they felt the time back in their days, the resistance vanished.

Now? That wasted time is going back into actual delivery operations where it belongs. 

Stop blaming your people when the process is the bottleneck.
