One CFD run answers one question. A surrogate model answers thousands. ⚡
At MR CFD, we now combine ANSYS Fluent with PyFluent (Ansys' Python API) to turn high-fidelity simulation into fast, data-driven design tools.
How it works:
🔹 Automate – PyFluent scripts the full workflow: geometry parameters, mesh, solver setup, runs and post-processing, with no manual clicking
🔹 Explore – we run a Design of Experiments (DOE) across your design space on our 512-core HPC
🔹 Learn – the CFD results train a surrogate model (response surface, Gaussian process or neural network)
🔹 Predict – the surrogate estimates pressure drop, drag, temperature, efficiency and more in seconds instead of hours
🔹 Optimize – run multi-objective optimization and sensitivity studies, then validate the best designs back in Fluent
Where this pays off:
✅ HVAC & data centers – rack layouts, airflow and cooling
✅ Turbomachinery – pump, fan and turbine blade optimization
✅ Heat exchangers & electronics cooling – fin, baffle and channel design
✅ Aerodynamics & marine – drag reduction, hull and wing shape studies
✅ Energy, process & biomedical – mixing, combustion, multiphase and blood flow
✅ Academic research – parametric studies, thesis and journal papers, with an academic rate
What you get: validated CFD, a trained surrogate model you can reuse, the automation scripts, and a clear report your engineers can defend.
