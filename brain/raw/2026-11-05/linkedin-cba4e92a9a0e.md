AI is no longer just a tool for answering questions.
We’re moving into the era of AI Agents systems that can actually take action.
An AI agent can:
→ Find and qualify leads  
→ Follow up with prospects automatically  
→ Update CRM records  
→ Analyze business data  
→ Research markets and competitors  
→ Handle customer conversations  
→ Automate repetitive workflows  
→ Support teams 24/7  
The biggest opportunity isn’t simply using AI
It’s asking:
What work in your business should no longer require a human to do manually?
That’s where AI agents can create real business value  saving time, reducing operational costs, and helping teams focus on higher-value decisions.
I’m currently working on AI agents and workflow automation, and I believe the next competitive advantage will come from businesses that build AI into their everyday operations.
AI won’t replace every business.  
But businesses using AI effectively will move faster than those that don’t.
