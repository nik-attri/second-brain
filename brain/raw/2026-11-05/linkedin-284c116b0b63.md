🌱 The most important part of a plant might be the one you never see.

🔎 When evaluating crop performance, we often focus on what happens above ground.
But roots determine how effectively plants access water and nutrients, withstand drought, and adapt to challenging environments.

🟤 For decades, root systems have remained difficult to study because they are hidden from view and labor-intensive to measure manually.
📷 Today, imaging technologies and automated image analysis allow researchers to quantify root traits with unprecedented speed and consistency.

➡️ Root angle. Root length. Branching patterns. Root density.

These characteristics may provide critical insights into the development of future climate-resilient crop varieties.

As agricultural challenges continue to grow, understanding what happens below ground may become more important than ever.
