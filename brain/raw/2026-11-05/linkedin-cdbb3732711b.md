The real cost of managing employee leave in spreadsheets isn't what you think. 

When you’re a 10-person team, Excel or Google Sheets is totally fine. Everyone knows who’s off, requests take two seconds, and HR doesn’t have to pull their hair out.

But what happens when you grow to 30, 50, or 100 people?

Suddenly, someone is stuck manually calculating leave balances, juggling different time-off policies, rolling over unused days from last year, and panic-checking if two key teammates just booked their vacations for the exact same week.

Managers start asking, 'Wait, who’s actually around next week?' Employees ask, 'How many days do I have left?' And HR spends half their week cross-checking formulas and answering questions that should take five seconds to answer.

The real cost isn't the spreadsheet itself. It’s the hours wasted maintaining it, the human errors that inevitably slip through, and the total lack of clarity for everyone involved.

We wanted to make leave management something employees and managers could handle without constantly bothering HR. Employees check their own balance and request time off in a couple of clicks. Managers get a clear view of team availability and approve requests instantly. And HR sets the rules once instead of babying a massive Frankenstein spreadsheet.
