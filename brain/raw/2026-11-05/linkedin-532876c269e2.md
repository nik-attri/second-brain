WE ARE HIRING: Regional Service Engineer (European Headquarters, Belgium).

Are you passionate about heavy machinery and thrive in an international environment? Do you want to have a real impact on the development of a rapidly growing dealer network across Europe?

Join the Shantui team at our European headquarters in Belgium and help us drive the highest customer satisfaction! 

As a 1st class Regional Service Engineer, you will become the cornerstone of our regional service operations, reporting directly to the European Service Manager. This is a role for a candidate who combines technical expertise with a talent for building trusted partnerships (from dealers and customers to our global headquarters).

What will you be doing?
🛠️ 2nd-line technical support – solving complex problems on new and used equipment.
🎓 Training – sharing knowledge and delivering technical training to trainers, engineers, and mechanics at our dealerships.
📝 Warranty and audits – pre-validating warranty claims and defining conditions for new machines.
⚙️ Service campaigns – overseeing the implementation of quality and safety improvements.
🔍 Inspections (PDI) and commissioning – controlling and handing over new machines for use.
💬 Information bridge – coordinating the flow of technical knowledge among dealers, customers, and Shantui Headquarters.

Who are we looking for?

- A proactive professional with excellent analytical and organizational skills, and a strong customer focus.
- An individual with the legal right to work in the EU (EU citizenship or appropriate work permit).
- A candidate with fluent English (comfortable use of technical terminology is a must!).
- Knowledge of an additional EU language will be a huge asset! 

What do we offer? The opportunity to tackle fascinating engineering challenges and have a real impact on the growth of a global organization.
