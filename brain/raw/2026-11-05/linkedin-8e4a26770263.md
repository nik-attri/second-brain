We're Hiring | Factory Manager
Industry: Manufacturing (Packaging Industry Preferred)
A well-established manufacturing company is seeking an experienced and result-oriented Factory Manager to oversee its factory operations and ensure efficient plant performance.
📍 Location: Mehran Town, Korangi Industrial Area, Karachi
💰 Salary Package: Rs. 250,000–350,000 per month
🕒 Experience: 10–15 years of relevant manufacturing experience.
Key Responsibilities
Oversee and manage overall factory operations to ensure smooth and efficient production.
Develop and execute production plans to achieve operational targets.
Monitor production efficiency, machine utilization, and plant performance.
Coordinate with Production, Quality Assurance, Maintenance, Stores, and Dispatch departments.
Control production costs, minimize material wastage, and optimize resources.
Ensure compliance with quality standards, workplace safety, and applicable regulations.
Implement process improvements to enhance productivity and reduce operational downtime.
Lead and manage factory staff and departmental teams.
Monitor operational KPIs and report performance to senior management.
Ensure effective preventive maintenance of machinery and equipment.
Required Qualifications & Experience
Bachelor's degree in Engineering, preferably Mechanical or Industrial Engineering. Master's degree will be an advantage.
10–15 years of proven experience in manufacturing operations is mandatory.
Strong background in factory management, production planning, process improvement, and workforce management.
Exposure to corrugated carton manufacturing and packaging operations will be preferred, but is not mandatory.
Excellent leadership, decision-making, and problem-solving capabilities.
