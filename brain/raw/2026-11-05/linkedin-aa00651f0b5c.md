Six months ago, Hollyhock Café was only an empty building, and one hell of a lot of ambition.
We are currently entering into our sixth month since we opened our doors for the first time, and there's so much to reflect on.
New Town Community Roots CIC started as three friends sat in a living room, discussing how to set up a community space for New Town. We had several different places we were looking at, and none of them really fit the bill. Then the café came along, at exactly the right time. The first day that we walked through the doors in April we found aggressively bright paint work, a very tired kitchen, and a place that felt completely uninviting. A have had the remarkable privilege to watch this place become what it is today. 

Since opening our doors, we've:
☕ Established Hollyhock Café as a seven-day-a-week community hub first, café second, supported by a fantastic team of staff and volunteers.
🎉 Brought people together through events including our grand opening, BBQ & Banter evenings, live entertainment, quizzes, and regular community gatherings.
🤝 Developed meaningful partnerships with organisations including Colchester Institute and Nova Training, creating opportunities for education, work experience, and personal development for young people in Colchester.
🍰 About to launch Bloom Kitchen, our exciting next step towards expanding what we do and creating new opportunities through food.
🏡 Progressed plans for the redevelopment of Hollyhock Café, with brand new architect drawings, and progressing to planning application shortly.
 💵 Secured over £500,00 in capital funding.
But, most importantly, we've started to see something that can't be measured by spreadsheets or funding applications. People finding Purpose. We have built somewhere, where people feel able to ask for help. Now, don't get me wrong, we've gotten things wrong along the way, there's been some mistakes, and there's been more than one time where I've questioned what the hell we're doing, but we've pushed through and I wouldn't change it for the world. I'm so proud of what we've accomplished in our own little team, and so completely grateful of every single volunteer, customer, group and supporter that has stepped through our doors. We couldn't do this without you. And there's something so important to remember here:

We're only entering month five.

There's so much more to come, and I genuinely believe we're only scratching the surface of what The Hollyhock Café and New Town Community Roots CIC can become. We've got our new trading wing, Boom Kitchen opening this Friday, meaning that we'll be able to create more of the fantastic food that we're well known for. In the next few months, you can expect us to give you more information about Petal Productions, and Little Roots.
Here's to building something that lasts.
Not just a café. A community. 🌱
