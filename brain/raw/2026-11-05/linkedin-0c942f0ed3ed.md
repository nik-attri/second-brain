Apparently, humans are becoming an expensive subscription.

Another week. Another round of layoffs. Another restructuring. Another corporate genius discovering that AI can replace half the workforce.

Brilliant. Let’s celebrate with a PowerPoint.

Meanwhile, LinkedIn is crawling with AI prophets explaining how to build a million-dollar business in a weekend with three prompts and a iced coffee.

And the annoying part? They’re not entirely wrong.

I’ve built software with AI to replace SaaS subscriptions I didn’t want to pay for. Sometimes because they were overpriced. Sometimes because they were useless.

AI is changing the game. No argument there.

But here’s what pisses me off.

AI has never spent years dealing with incompetent bosses, impossible customers, office politics, collapsing projects, and decisions that looked brilliant in a boardroom but turned into absolute disasters.

You have.

You’ve fixed shit nobody knew how to fix.

You’ve made decisions when nobody had a clue what to do.

You’ve dealt with egos, conflicts, failures, and situations no textbook could prepare you for.

Not another bloody certificate to decorate your LinkedIn profile.

People with decades of experience are questioning their own worth because some algorithm rejected their résumé.

Those are business assets. Not corporate leftovers.
