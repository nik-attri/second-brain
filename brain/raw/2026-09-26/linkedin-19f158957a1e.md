Following up on yesterday's post about Salesforce governance...

The biggest fear with setting up a formal intake process is that simple requests end up trapped in committee hell for six weeks.
The teams that avoid this usually break incoming requests into three distinct buckets:

Tier 1: Low impact (24–48 hours) 
• Small stuff: picklist values, list views, basic reports, minor layout tweaks. 
• Approval: Just the system admin or product owner. No meetings needed.

Tier 2: Medium impact (1–2 weeks)
 • Process tweaks: new custom fields, flow changes, email alerts. 
• Approval: A quick impact check to make sure another team didn't build the exact same field three months ago.

Tier 3: High impact (Monthly or quarterly planning)
• Major shifts: custom objects, third-party integrations, changing core data models. 
• Approval: Joint business and IT leads.

If you treat a Tier 1 request like Tier 3, people build shadow spreadsheets. If you treat a Tier 3 request like Tier 1, the org turns into a mess fast.
How do you guys handle simple admin tasks versus actual architecture changes?
