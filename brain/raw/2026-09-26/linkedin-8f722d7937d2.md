I put together this case study to break down the journey from initiation → requirements → technical integration → testing → risk management → stakeholder coordination → go-live.
I used Wise as the fintech reference point because its business sits at the intersection of payments, financial infrastructure, technology, operations, and cross-border transactions.
The goal was simple: show how I think about fintech delivery, not just project management theory.
As a Project / Programme Manager, I’m particularly interested in the messy middle where business teams, technical teams, partners, vendors, and stakeholders have to move together to get something live.
I’d genuinely love feedback from people working in fintech, payments, product, and programme delivery.
