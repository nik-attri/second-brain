digital.sfpe software has been updated to version v2.5.0

The v2.5.0 update is critical and must be installed by all users. You can update automatically using the button in the software, or manually by running the updater in the software folder.

What we did
1.On September 24, 2026, in the middle of the day, EMERCOM (Russia's Ministry of Emergency Situations) made significant updates to the work start/completion notification forms on the Gosuslugi portal. Some fields were removed, others were added. We responded quickly and released an update that allows automated notification submission through digital.sfpe to continue.

❗️ Import from the portal in the new format is not yet implemented in version 2.5.0. This was a deliberate choice, made to restore users' ability to submit notifications as quickly as possible. We plan to implement this functionality in the next version, expected to be released Monday morning, September 28, 2026 (Moscow time).

2.A column with the Client's Tax ID (INN) has been added to the notification export file, right after the Client column.
3.Other work was carried out according to the development roadmap and user requests.

What to check and fill in before continuing to submit notifications through digital.sfpe

In the Settings panel:
1.Date of birth and gender of the responsible person (under the "EMERCOM Licenses and Responsible Persons" tab)
2.Dates of birth and gender for all employees (under the "Employees" tab)
3.Indicate whether the legal and actual addresses match; if not, add the actual address by selecting it from the FIAS address directory (under the "EMERCOM Licenses and Responsible Persons" tab)
4.Remove (hide) from the list any tools and equipment that are not measuring instruments (i.e., that don't have calibration certificate numbers) (under the "Tools" tab)

❗️ Check whether all your measuring instruments pass verification on the Rosstandart showcase portal. If not, you'll need to decide whether to submit on time with a reduced list of measuring instruments, or hold off submitting until the portal resolves the issue (both options carry a risk of non-compliance).

In the notification records:
1.Select the types of work for each address.
2.If the facility has been put into operation, look it up in the ERVK register and enter its details into the notification record; if it's not listed there, leave the fields blank.

Please note that these changes are quite substantial, and it's possible we won't be able to resolve every issue with this first update. We welcome your feedback and suggestions, and we'll do our best to address them promptly in upcoming versions of digital.sfpe.
