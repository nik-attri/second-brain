This one is for my SaaS, MarTech, and vendor friends. 👋

If you're only using Claude to rewrite emails, you're leaving SO much on the table.
I've been using it daily for a long time (as I'm sure we all have!). Most days it's a great colleague. Every so often I build something that ACTUALLY changes how I work. This week was one of those times.

So this is a post about MCPs, and how connecting your systems truly makes a difference. (Go yell at your tech and infosec teams for permissions! 😬)

I built myself a report. Boring sentence, I know. But this one pulls from a bunch of connected systems (Salesforce, Tableau, Snowflake, Slack, and a couple more) and gives me my entire week in one view:

📈 The biggest growth opportunities across every account team
🚨 The renewals most at risk
🤝 Whether a strategist (my team) is already involved, bringing a non-sales POV on the real business impact our tech can have for the brand

And now? ONE click. It just runs!

Was the setup work? A 💩 ton of it. Connecting the pipes, getting infosec approval, nailing the logic, and arguing with myself (and Claude) about what key terms actually mean. Claude wasn't the only one making judgment errors. I kinda hallucinated too. But we caught flaws in each other's thinking along the way.

Worth every minute. 🙌

The old version was exports and spreadsheet gymnastics. Now I get my Sunday nights back (Survivor and The Amazing Race are calling my name). Business-wise, that time goes into the conversations and strategy clients actually feel.

So here's my push to everyone in tech: stop treating it like fancy autocomplete! Connect your tools. Build the thing you've been doing by hand every week. Push it until it breaks, fix it, and push again! 🔧

The result is more time on the part of the job you actually care about. And less spreadsheet hell.

Now if it could just get sales reps to update their Salesforce fields, we'd REALLY be onto something. 😅 A really simplified mock in the image you see!
