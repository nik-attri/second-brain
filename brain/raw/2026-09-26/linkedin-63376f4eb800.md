Sell the Problem and the outcome ! 

I built ProtosHealth almost alone from a lived use case. Verified the product market fit with few doctors and hospitals. 

Built the entire tech and the science using AI and few super specialist surgeons. 

❌ - No customer has ever been interested it that story. 

As we get closer to commercialisation, we've started speaking to a few micro markets. Two oncology speciality hospitals among them. Every conversation sharpens the same instincts. 

🟢 - Sales is solving a real problem and presenting the outcome.

❌ Not positioning. 
❌ Not awareness - A real problem needs no awareness. 

Name the problem in one sentence, in their words.

The best conversations start with them describing the pain and me listening.

 🎯 - Outcome is the only lever that moves a sale.

✅ - Complications avoided 
✅ - Lives saved 
✅ - Beds freed 

If you cannot draw a straight line to a number they already track, you don't have a sale. 

The customer does not care how it gets solved.

Tech or manual. 
Algorithm or a nurse !

Plenty of what ProtosHealth does now started as manual work. The problem shrank. That was the entire transaction.

Do it manually until the manual work breaks you. Then automate.

💡 ~ We use AI. We still don't sell it.

Internally we call our models doctor's time travel machines. They let a clinician see forward instead of backward. I believe in them completely.

They matter at scale, when there's enough data flowing. That is not today.

Today the hospital has one question: does the problem go away.

Be unshakeable on the science. Be quiet about the category. Don't overcomplicate the sale.

💰 - Lean is a weapon. It is not a pitch.

Building on a shoestring is the biggest advantage a technical founder has. 

📌 Time. 
📌 Control. 
📌 The ability to say no.

No hospital buys because you're lean. The investor cares about your burn. The customer cares whether you'll exist in eighteen months to support them and lean operations ensures that we don't die. 

Carry the leanness. Don't talk about it.

💻 - To every technical founder

Your engineering, AI and data science skill is your survival advantage. It buys you more shots at the problem than a funded team gets.

Build cheap. Ship fast. Sit in front of the customer. Let the product be the quiet part.

Nobody buys the stack. They buy the fact that something they were afraid of stopped happening.

Above all be in it to solve a hard problem only. Not to raise funding , not to be seen and not for money anyway. 

ProtosHealth.ai - Predict the crises, prevent the collapse.
