AI made everyone faster. It didn't make the company faster…

Your engineers ship quicker. Your marketers work quicker. Your reps research accounts in minutes. And yet the launch still slips, the handoff still stalls, and the pipeline review still runs on a spreadsheet. The speed is real – it's just trapped inside each person's tabs.

Closing that gap takes three things most companies don't have yet: 

1️⃣ AI needs the right data to act on, not a rep's copy-pasted research. 
2️⃣ It needs a shared place where work actually lives, so an agent can pick up a task right where a teammate left it. 
3️⃣ And it needs to know who it's acting for and what it's allowed to touch. 

Data, work, and permissions: that's the difference between AI productivity and AI connectivity. The question on every operator's lips used to be "which AI tools should we buy?" Today it's "how do we make the whole company faster, not just the people in it?"
