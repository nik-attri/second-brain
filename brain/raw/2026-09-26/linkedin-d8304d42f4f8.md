📢 Hiring at Innovative Biscuits (Pvt.) Ltd Biscuits!

We are looking for a Procurement Officer to join our team.

📌 Job Title: Procurement Officer
🎓 Education: Bachelors in Supply Chain  - BSSC - BBA supply chain
💼 Experience: 1–2 Years Relevant Experience - Entry level position 
⚡ Hiring: Immediate

Key Responsibilities:

- Manage procurement of packaging materials and ensure timely availability.
- Handle Master Carton execution and coordinate with vendors.
- Manage Ink Coding/Printer execution and printing requirements.
- Prepare Purchase Orders (POs) and coordinate with suppliers.
- Maintain procurement reports, records, documentation, and data entry.
- Ensure proper ISO documentation for procurement activities.
- Support New Product Development (NPD) sourcing and packaging requirements.
- Coordinate vendor payments and maintain payment records.
- Ensure timely delivery, quality, and compliance through vendor coordination.
- Perform other procurement-related tasks assigned by the line manager.

Candidate Profile:

- Bachelors in Supply Chain / BBA - Supply Chain qualification.
- 1–2 years of relevant FMCG procurement experience preferred.
- Strong communication, coordination, documentation, and follow-up skills.
- Proficiency in MS Office and basic reporting/data entry.
- Immediate joiners will be preferred.
