🧑‍💼 WHAT IF AI DOESN’T JUST CHANGE OUR JOBS — BUT CHANGES THE ORGANISATION ITSELF?

In this third post of mine, in the series of tracking developments in Microsoft, I elucidate the work orchestration in AI native organisations.

For decades, organisations have been designed around a simple principle:

Humans perform tasks.
Managers coordinate humans.
Technology supports humans.

Agentic AI challenges that structure.

Microsoft’s emerging “Frontier Firm” concept looks at organisations where humans and AI agents work together as a coordinated system.

This is more than adding Copilot to an employee’s laptop.

It means redesigning the workflow itself.

Imagine:

👨‍💼 A manager defines an objective.

🤖 One agent researches.

📊 Another analyses the data.

💻 Another develops the solution.

🔎 Another checks quality and security.

📧 Another communicates routine updates.

👨‍💼 The human makes the critical decisions.

That is not simply automation.

It is a new model of work orchestration.

Microsoft has been experimenting with this internally as “Customer Zero” — using its own organisation as a testbed for AI transformation.

The important question therefore changes from:

“How many people can AI replace?”

to:

“How much more can a human organisation accomplish when AI agents become part of its operating system?”

But there is a second side to this transformation.

More autonomous agents mean greater requirements for:

🔐 Identity
🛡️ Security
📜 Governance
⚖️ Accountability
👁️ Observability
🧠 Human judgement

The future organisation may not be human OR machine.

It may be:

Human + AI + Governance

That is the conversation universities, industry and policymakers need to start having now.

Because the next transformation may not merely automate work.

It may redesign work itself.
