📢 WE’RE HIRING!
We are seeking a knowledgeable and detail-oriented Case Processing Officer to handle end-to-end processing of immigration applications for both Canada and Australia.

Key Responsibilities: Case Management & Processing:

Canada Immigration:
* Process applications under Express Entry (FSW, CEC, FST)
* Handle Provincial Nominee Programs (PNPs)
* Manage PR Visa, Visitor Visa applications
* Stay updated with IRCC rules, CRS score updates, and policy changes

Australia Immigration:
* Handle Skilled Migration Visas (Subclass 189, 190, 491)
* Manage Employer-Sponsored Visas and Student Visas
* Coordinate with assessing authorities (ACS, VETASSESS, etc.)
* Ensure compliance with DHA (Department of Home Affairs) regulations

Client Coordination:
* Communicate with clients to collect required documents and information
* Provide clear guidance on documentation, timelines, and process steps
* Respond to client queries professionally and promptly
* Maintain high levels of client satisfaction and trust

Compliance & Documentation:
* Ensure all applications comply with immigration laws and regulations
* Identify risks or discrepancies in documentation and resolve proactively
* Maintain confidentiality of client data and records
* Conduct regular file audits for quality assurance

Tracking & Reporting:
* Monitor application status and follow up with authorities where required
* Provide regular updates to clients and management
* Maintain CRM updates and internal reporting systems

Process Improvement:
* Stay updated with the latest immigration policies for Canada and Australia
* Suggest improvements in internal processes for better efficiency

Requirements:
* Minimum 6–10 years of experience in immigration processing (Canada & Australia preferred)
* Strong Experience of Canada PR programs (Express Entry, PNPs) and Australia Skilled Migration programs
* Familiarity with IRCC and OMARA systems and procedures
* Excellent documentation and case management skills
* Strong attention to detail and accuracy
* Ability to manage multiple cases simultaneously under deadlines
* Proficiency in MS Office and CRM tools
* Excellent communication (English mandatory; additional languages are a plus)
* Preferred Qualifications:
* Experience in a reputed immigration consultancy
* Knowledge of ECA, IELTS/PTE requirements, and skill assessment bodies
* Strong client-handling and counseling skills

Salary & Benefits:
* Salary AED 4,500 - AED 5,000 per month
* Attractive incentives
* Employment visa & medical insurance
* Career growth and professional development
* Supportive and performance-driven work environment.
