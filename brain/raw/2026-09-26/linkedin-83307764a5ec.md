Attended an insightful masterclass hosted by upGradRise and mentored by Rajat Paliwal sir on how to break into a career in AI/ML skills, roadmap, projects, and mainly guidance.

 📌 The session offered a clear, engineering-first perspective on navigating today’s AI space.

1. The Market & Hiring Reality
AI/ML hiring is up 25% YoY in India, with GenAI/LLM demand up 58%.
 🔹 Even as AI tools write code, companies still need engineers to build, integrate, and maintain real systems.
 🔹 The barrier to entry has shifted: 18% of AI roles target 0–3 years of experience, but companies demand concrete proof of work, strong fundamentals, and DSA fluency.

2. The Model is Just the Tip of the Iceberg
 🔹 Fluent text generation is only a small part of a working system.
 🔹 Production-grade AI depends on company data integration, permissions, answer quality evaluation, cost and speed optimization, monitoring, and escalation paths.

3. Three Shifts Driving Everyday AI Engineering
 🔹 Accessible: Pre-built models bring language and vision into everyday software.
 🔹 Affordable: Lower inference costs make more use cases business-viable.
 🔹 Deployed: AI runs inside real production workflows, creating high demand for evaluation, infrastructure, and monitoring.

4. Specialized Roles Across the AI Stack
 🔹 One core product often spans six key roles: AI Engineer, ML Engineer, Data Scientist, Data Engineer, MLOps Engineer, and Applied Scientist.
 🔹 Success starts with a shared foundation—Python, SQL, Git, Data, Statistics, Evaluation, and Communication—before specializing into a specific pillar.

5. From Idea to Production (The Metamorphosis)
 🔹 Most portfolios stall at the Demo stage (a simple API wrapper).
🔹High-value engineering lies in moving from Demo -> Prototype -> Pilot ->Production, where systems handle real users, edge cases, permissions, and failures.

6. Execution Strategy
🔹Build more than you watch: Allocate time systematically across learning (2h), building (4h), testing/documenting (2h), and practicing/applying (1–2h).
🔹Focus on a 6-month structured roadmap: Master core tools, build baselines, focus on one role, deploy end-to-end, conduct failure analysis, and defend a complete case study.

Grateful to Rajat Paliwal sir and upGrad Rise for delivering such a clear, grounded framework for building a career in AI. It was a highly motivating session.
