What if, before your next leadership meeting, an AI agent had already analysed your business performance, identified what changed, investigated why, tested different scenarios, and highlighted what may need attention?

That is where Agentic AI becomes interesting.

AI has already transformed automation, content creation and information retrieval. Agentic AI can take this further by reasoning through business problems, connecting information across sources, identifying patterns and working toward a defined objective.

The real question isn't:

“How much can we automate?”

It is:

“How much better can we make our decisions?”

A simple business example:

Imagine a wealth management business sees slower-than-expected client growth.

Traditionally, an analyst may spend hours pulling reports, analysing segments and channels, investigating conversion rates, reviewing market trends and preparing scenarios for leadership.

An Agentic AI workflow could bring much of this analysis together — identifying where growth is falling short, investigating the underlying drivers, connecting relevant market signals and modelling potential scenarios.

Instead of leadership spending the meeting asking:

“What happened?”

the conversation can move toward:

“What should we do about it?”

That is the potential shift from analytics to decision intelligence.

And the application goes far beyond wealth management:

🔹 Retail: Identify drivers behind regional sales changes and evaluate pricing or customer scenarios.

🔹 Banking: Analyse acquisition, product penetration and cross-sell opportunities.

🔹 Manufacturing: Connect demand, inventory and supply-chain signals to evaluate operational scenarios.

🔹 Finance: Continuously identify material variances and emerging business drivers.

🔹 Strategy: Monitor KPIs and external signals to challenge assumptions behind the current strategy.

But Agentic AI shouldn't replace human judgment.

The stronger model is:

AI + Human judgment

AI brings scale, speed, synthesis and continuous analysis.

Humans bring context, judgment and accountability.

The real opportunity may therefore be to redesign the journey from:

Data → Analysis → Insight → Scenario → Decision → Action

That is why I see Agentic AI as more than an automation story.

It could become a decision-intelligence story.
