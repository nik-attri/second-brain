Hiring: HR Manager
Company: SISYA CLASS
Location: Visakhapatnam
Work mode: Work from office
Experience: Minimum 3 years in HR, including experience in the education sector

SISYA CLASS is looking for an HR Manager to lead talent acquisition and support day-to-day HR operations. We’re seeking someone who can manage hiring from initial screening through offer acceptance, communicate clearly with candidates and teams, and handle salary discussions with confidence and fairness.

Key responsibilities
* Manage recruitment for academic and non-academic roles.
* Source and screen candidates, coordinate interviews, and follow up through joining.
* Lead salary negotiations and prepare offers in line with approved budgets.
* Oversee onboarding, employee records, attendance coordination, and other HR operations.
* Work with department heads to understand hiring needs and resolve people-related issues.

Requirements
* At least 3 years of relevant HR experience.
* Prior experience working in the education or EdTech sector.
* Strong experience in talent acquisition, HR operations, and salary negotiations.
* Excellent communication skills in Hindi and English, both compulsory.
* Ability to work full-time from our Visakhapatnam office.

To apply: Send your resume to contactus@sisyaclass.com with the subject line “Application for HR Manager.”
