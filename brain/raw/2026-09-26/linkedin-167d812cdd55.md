A growing business can lose hours every week without noticing it.

Not because the team is slow.
Because the workflow is fragmented.

One person updates Excel.
Another checks email.
Approvals happen in chat.
Reports are rebuilt manually.
The same information gets entered more than once.

That is usually a systems problem — not a people problem.

Before hiring more people, I would map:

→ where data is entered twice
→ where work waits for approval
→ where someone manually moves information between tools
→ where managers still need someone to build a report

Then fix the expensive handoffs first.

Sometimes that means an integration.
Sometimes workflow automation.
Sometimes a focused custom application.

The objective is not "more software."
It is fewer manual steps between a customer request and the outcome.
