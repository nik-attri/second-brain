I Built an AI Automation to Clean Up My 15K+ Gmail Inbox
Let’s be honest email management is a problem almost every student and professional faces.
We receive emails every single day:
1)Job opportunities
2)Internship updates
3)College notifications
4)Promotional emails
5)Event invitations
6)Newsletters
7)Random subscriptions
And what do we usually do?

Ignore them. 
Over time, that turns into thousands of emails sitting in our inbox. In my case, my Gmail inbox had 15K+ emails, making it extremely difficult to identify important messages — especially those valuable job/internship emails from platforms like Unstop that can easily get buried among hundreds of other emails.
So I decided to build an automation to solve the problem. 

What I Built
I created an AI-powered Gmail automation architecture using n8n that can:
✅ Automatically scan unread emails
✅ Process emails in controlled batches
✅ Use AI to understand and analyze email content
✅ Identify the purpose and relevance of emails
✅ Process emails that have remained unread for more than a week
✅ Automatically clean up old unread emails
✅ Reduce the manual effort required to maintain a clutter-free inbox

Architecture
Schedule Trigger → Gmail → Email Processing → Loop Over Items → AI Agent → Gmail Automation
The workflow runs automatically at scheduled intervals instead of requiring me to manually go through thousands of emails.
The interesting part for me wasn't just building an email cleaner — it was designing an agentic automation workflow where AI can understand unstructured email content and work as part of a larger automation pipeline.
The bigger idea
This project made me think about how many repetitive tasks we perform every day simply because we haven't automated them yet.
Instead of spending hours going through:
15,000+ emails → manually identifying → manually deleting → repeating
the goal is to build systems that can continuously handle these repetitive tasks in the background.
This is just one small example of how AI + automation + APIs can turn a daily productivity problem into an automated workflow.
Less inbox clutter.
Less manual work.
More focus on the emails that actually matter.
