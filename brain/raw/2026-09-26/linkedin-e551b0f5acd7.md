Is your team drowning in lengthy, complicated written procedures?
 
I’ve been supporting organisations for nearly 20 years to think about better ways of working, using Lean principles. But I still encounter businesses that, whilst they want to eliminate paperwork, continue to drown in lengthy, complicated written documentation that tie up valuable resources.
 
I’d love to have a silver bullet that eliminates complicated paperwork altogether. If it’s not possible, then what about reducing the size of the piles and making the documents more streamlined to save my team members from drowning?
 
While it’s not a Silver bullet, this is the closest I’ve come to a complete documentation solution.
 
PPMai from Desertfire Online Talsico, is a continuous improvement tool designed to cut out waste and frustration by making it fast and easy to ensure your procedures, training and job aids capture ‘best practice’ and all in synch.
 
Scan that bulky document into a PDF, then open PPMai and it will create a draft PPM in less than 4 minutes! You can choose the whole document or just sections. It’s your choice. Including photos, diagrams etc.
 
Do you have an existing Supplier Manual in PDF form? PPMai can quickly convert that complex, confusing document into an easy-to-read format, like something complied by a professional technical writer.
 
First introduced in 1999, PPM software from Desertfire Online Talsico has just been getting better and better. I came across PPMs years ago and it continues to impress to all I’ve shared it with. Now supercharged with Ai, it’s most definitely a game changer.
