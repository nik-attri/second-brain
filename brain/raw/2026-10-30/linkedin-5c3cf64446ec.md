I promised my AI course students I would showcase their automations publicly. Because when you build something real you deserve the spotlight 🔥

Meet Mother Lilly's automation from Cohort 1. She built an AI Lead Intake system that turns every Google Form submission into a clean, classified, editable record and alerts your team on Slack. Automatically.

Here's how it works. A lead comes in through Google Forms. Gemini AI classifies the lead type and priority. Writes a summary. Drafts a reply for human approval. Then stores everything in Airtable as a clean record. And sends a Slack notification to the team. All automated. Zero manual work.

If a repeat lead comes in? It updates the existing record instead of creating a duplicate. Smart. Clean. Professional.

What she needed to build this? A Google Form. A Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. An Airtable base with the right fields. And a Slack workspace. All of which we cover in the course step by step.

This was built by a student in 10 days. Not a developer. Not a software engineer. A student who walked into the Automate and Elevate course, learned the tools and built something a business can use TODAY.

If you have a business drowning in leads and you want automatic classification, storage, team notifications and draft replies without lifting a finger this is what our students build. And this is just ONE example.

The course is NOT only for virtual assistants. I had VAs in mind because that's the hat I wear most visibly. But this course is for EVERYONE who wants to learn AI and automations. Business owners. Freelancers. Remote workers. Anyone.
