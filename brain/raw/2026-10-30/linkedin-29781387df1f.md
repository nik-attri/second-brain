I spent the week in a workshop with NYT bestseller Brianna Wiest (The Mountain is You, Pivot Year, +more). In parting, we were asked to share one piece of wisdom only we could offer.

This is what I read to our group, and what I now share with you:

- -

If you’re experiencing burnout right now, one thing that I wish for you to know is that it's not a state of being in burnout or not being in burnout. 

Instead, it’s a compression.

The longer you stay in burnout, the more and more you pile on, it compresses. And the longer and longer it's going to take for you to shovel your way out of it.

When I left Corporate America and took a year off, I thought it was going to take me a month away on vacation for me to start feeling like myself again.

In fact, it took 3 to 4 months before I felt any ounce of creative energy again.
It took 6 months for me to feel like myself, and a year for me to actually be excited to chase my career again.

So for you, if you are in that burned out state right now and it’s work related, let's get you out. Let's make that plan so that you can leave that shitty job in that environment that doesn't see the value that you bring to the table.

And if you’re not ready to quit today because the money is overwhelming or it’s not the right time, or you want to leave on your own terms, that’s okay. If you need a spreadsheet that calculates how much money that you save away, every hour that you don't (yet) quit your job- that was me, I can send you that spreadsheet.

So if you're looking for permission to leave, that life can be better than you ever dreamed on the other side, that there is another way. This is that moment.

And please let me know when you do, so I can tell you “hell yeah!” and cheer you on.
