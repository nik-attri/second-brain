*We're Hiring | AM - Warehouse | Night Shift | Karachi!*
*Bachaa Party* is looking to hire a skilled and experienced candidate for the position of *Assistant Manager – Warehouse* for its Central Warehouse *(Night Shift).*
*Duties and Responsibilities:*
•	Manage overall warehouse operations including receiving, barcoding, distribution, and replenishment operations
•	Ensure proper handing over between operational shifts
•	Ensure all operational KPIs are met
•	Ensure timely execution of putaway and picking operations
•	Ensure timely implementation of WMS new modules and provide process improvement areas ahead
•	Manage timely execution of transport operations
•	Ensure inventory accuracy at all storage areas
•	Communicate with commercial teams regarding operational delays, and all relevant cross functional collaboration
•	Identify training needs and align training for staff in collaboration reporting manager
•	Ensure reporting requirement and exception reporting to reporting manager
•	Keep effective communication with retail stores and regional warehouses
•	Team development as per capacity requirement and projected growth
•	Ensure staff administrative matters are managed
•	Ensure hiring needs are met timely
•	Identification of waste activities in overall warehouse operations - followed by corrective actions/addition of controls
•	Ensure safety and security of men, material, and equipment
•	Recommend HSE and safety related training 
*Requirements:*
•	Education: Bachelors, or masters (Preferrable)
•	Experience: 4-5 years warehouse operations experience
•	Strong team management skills, cross functional collaboration, communication skills
•	Proficient in Microsoft Office, Inventory management, and strong knowledge of warehouse management systems
•	Can do attitude, can handle pressure, self-starter, problem-solving skills, go-getter
•	Detail-oriented, and multitasking capabilities
•	Experience in working with cross-functional stakeholders in a fast-paced environment
*Interested candidates can email their CVs to careers@bachaaparty.com by mentioning the position in the subject line.*
Apply latest by 6th November 2026.Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
