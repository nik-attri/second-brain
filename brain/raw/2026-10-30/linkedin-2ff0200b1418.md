Job Description – Workforce Management (WFM) Manager
Role: Management – WFM Manager
Department: Operations / Contact Center
Industry: BPO / ITES / Customer Support
Experience: 7–12 Years (2–4 Years in a Leadership Role)
Reporting To: Head of Operations / Senior Operations Manager
Job Summary
Responsible for workforce planning, forecasting, scheduling, real-time management, and resource optimization to ensure operational efficiency and achievement of business SLAs.
Key Responsibilities
1. Workforce Planning & Forecasting
•	Develop short-term and long-term workforce forecasts.
•	Analyze historical volumes and predict staffing requirements.
•	Calculate FTE requirements considering shrinkage, attrition and absenteeism.
•	Identify staffing gaps and prepare hiring plans.
2. Scheduling & Capacity Planning
•	Manage employee schedules, shifts, weekly offs and breaks.
•	Ensure optimal staffing coverage.
•	Monitor schedule efficiency and adherence.
•	Coordinate staffing adjustments with Operations.
3. Real-Time Management (RTA)
•	Monitor real-time performance and SLA achievement.
•	Track staffing, absenteeism and schedule adherence.
•	Manage intraday staffing requirements.
•	Handle volume spikes and staffing shortages.
•	Escalate critical operational risks.
4. Team & Stakeholder Management
•	Lead and manage WFM teams.
•	Coordinate with Operations, HR, Recruitment and Training.
•	Drive workforce productivity and process improvements.
•	Prepare management reports and performance dashboards.
Required Skills
•	Strong leadership and team management.
•	Strategic workforce planning and forecasting.
•	Advanced Excel and analytical skills.
•	Knowledge of WFM tools (NICE, Verint, Genesys, Calabrio).
•	Stakeholder management and communication.
•	Decision-making and problem-solving.
•	Contact center performance analytics.
Educational Qualifications & Experience
•	Bachelor's degree in any relevant discipline.
•	7–12 years of WFM experience.
•	2–4 years of team management experience.
•	Experience in large-scale contact center operations.
•	Knowledge of voice, email, chat and back-office processes.
