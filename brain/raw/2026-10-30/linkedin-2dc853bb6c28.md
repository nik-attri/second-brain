🚀 AI should solve real business problems, not just answer questions.
While building our Real Estate AI Agent at H2HOSTY, one thing became clear to me:
A chatbot becomes truly useful when it can take action.
That's exactly what we've been working on.
Our Real Estate AI Agent can:
🏡 Find properties using available real-time data
💬 Handle property-related enquiries
📅 Check availability and book appointments
📊 Automatically record bookings in Google Sheets
Instead of simply responding to visitors, the AI helps turn conversations into organized business workflows.
💡 My biggest learning?
Don't start with the AI technology. Start with the business problem you want to solve.
As the Founder of H2HOSTY, I'm excited to keep exploring practical AI solutions that make everyday business operations easier.
