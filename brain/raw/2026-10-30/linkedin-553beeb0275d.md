"The advice I get every year on renewal from my broker is so important."

That was a man who runs a large construction company, chatting to me recently about his insurance. He kept coming back to the relationship, the person who knows his business and sits across from him every renewal.

I spend my working life building software that takes admin off brokers' desks. And I agreed with every word he said.

That line has stuck with me since. It comes back every time AI comes up, which is most weeks now. I'm very passionate and probably a little bit blunt on this one. I genuinely cannot see AI ever replacing insurance brokers or agents.

I tell brokers to use AI to take away the burden of the mundane processes, cut the turnaround times and make the servicing better. Brokers who do that will accelerate and probably gain more market share. But I can't see anyone building trust with an AI model, at least not yet anyway.

So now when someone asks me whether the broker has a future, I give them his line:

"The advice I get every year on renewal from my broker is so important."

He'll sit down with his broker again at the next renewal. What I want is for that broker to walk in with the spreadsheets already done, so the whole conversation can be the advice he values.
