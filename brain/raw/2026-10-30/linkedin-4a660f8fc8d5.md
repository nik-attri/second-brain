I spend a lot of time thinking about how we can run our organisation more efficiently.

Expense tracking, invoice follow-ups, reimbursements, resource utilisation and pending approvals — these are small tasks, but they add up.

Imagine an AI agent that tracks an incoming invoice, checks the details, flags discrepancies and prepares it for approval — without someone manually chasing every step.

I believe AI agents can help us manage these operational workflows by monitoring activities, identifying exceptions, preparing actions and keeping things moving.

The real opportunity isn't just building AI-powered products.
It's using AI to build a smarter organisation.
