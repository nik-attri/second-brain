HOT TAKE: Your business doesn't have an AI problem. It has a people, processes and systems problem. And AI isn't going to magically fix your shit.
There. I said it.

𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆𝗼𝗻𝗲'𝘀 𝗿𝘂𝗻𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗿𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱 𝗮𝘀𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴, "𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝗰𝗮𝗻 𝘄𝗲 𝘂𝘀𝗲 𝗔𝗜 𝗶𝗻 𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗯𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀?"
W𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.

The question you SHOULD be asking is: "Why the hell are we still doing things this way?"
Because if your business is running on messy processes, bottlenecked people and systems held together by spreadsheets, sticky notes and the contents of your brain…
Congratulations. You've just found a way to automate your chaos.
And now you've got a high-speed hot mess.

As a process engineer, here's what I want you to do BEFORE you start throwing AI at everything.

THE 5-POINT BUSINESS REALITY CHECK 👇

1. FOLLOW THE WORK.
Pick one process, from start to finish. How many steps does it actually take? Who touches it? Where does it get stuck?

2. FIND THE HUMAN BOTTLENECKS.
What can't move forward without someone answering a question, approving something or finding information? Is that person YOU?

3. SPOT THE REPEAT OFFENDERS.
What is your team copying, pasting, rewriting, chasing or manually updating every bloody week?

4. QUESTION THE SYSTEM.
Are you using five different tools to do something that could happen in one? Are your people working around your software instead of WITH it?

5. ASK THE HOLIDAY QUESTION.
If you disappeared for two weeks tomorrow, what would grind to a halt?

𝙏𝙃𝘼𝙏'𝙎 𝙮𝙤𝙪𝙧 𝙨𝙩𝙖𝙧𝙩𝙞𝙣𝙜 𝙥𝙤𝙞𝙣𝙩. 𝙉𝙤𝙩 𝙖𝙣𝙤𝙩𝙝𝙚𝙧 𝙨𝙝𝙞𝙣𝙮 𝘼𝙄 𝙨𝙪𝙗𝙨𝙘𝙧𝙞𝙥𝙩𝙞𝙤𝙣.

Once you understand what's actually happening inside your business, THEN we can work out where AI belongs.

What should be simplified.
What should be automated.
What absolutely needs a human.
And what shouldn't be happening at all.

Because the goal isn't to build the most technologically impressive business in town.
It's to build a business that works brilliantly, grows sustainably and doesn't require you to be the human glue holding the whole bloody thing together.
