Intelligent E-Commerce CRM & End-to-End Automation
Industry: E-Commerce | Retail | Digital Commerce
Technology: CRM • Workflow Automation • Order Management • Customer Communication Integration

The Challenge
Online retail businesses frequently depend on disconnected spreadsheets, messaging applications, and manual order-processing activities, resulting in administrative inefficiencies, delayed responses, and missed sales opportunities.

Our Innovation
slintel.ai is developing an integrated digital CRM ecosystem that connects customer information, spreadsheet-based records, order management, and customer communications within a single automated workflow.
The platform automates routine processes, supports order placement, manages customer interactions, and streamlines the entire customer journey from initial inquiry to order processing.

Expected Business Impact
Significant reduction in repetitive administrative work
Faster customer response and order processing
Centralized customer and transaction records
Fewer missed inquiries and sales opportunities
Improved customer experience and business scalability

Project Status: In Development
