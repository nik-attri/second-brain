WE ARE HIRING – FRONT OFFICE MANAGER (FOM)
AKMANI HOTELS GROUP

We are looking for a passionate, experienced, and service-oriented Front Office Manager (FOM) to join our team!

If you are a hospitality professional with strong leadership skills, a passion for guest satisfaction, and the ability to drive operational excellence, we would love to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities:
*Oversee daily Front Office operations to ensure smooth and efficient guest services.
*Lead, motivate, and develop the Front Office team to deliver exceptional hospitality experiences.
*Ensure high standards of guest satisfaction, service quality, and complaint resolution.
*Maximize room revenue through effective upselling, room inventory management, and collaboration with the Sales & Revenue teams.
*Monitor guest feedback, online reviews, and service performance.
*Ensure compliance with hotel SOPs, brand standards, and operational policies.
*Manage departmental budgets, manpower planning, and operational efficiency.

Requirements:

*Minimum 3–5 years of experience in Front Office operations, preferably in a supervisory or managerial position.
*Previous experience as Front Office Manager or Assistant Front Office Manager in a 4- or 5-star hotel is preferred.
*Strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills.
*Excellent knowledge of Front Office operations, reservations, and Property Management Systems (PMS).
*Strong understanding of room revenue optimization, upselling, and guest experience management.
*Hands-on, proactive, detail-oriented, and guest-focused.
*Excellent command of English and Bahasa Indonesia.
