Still manually typing invoice numbers into a spreadsheet? 📄

Here's the thing most teams miss about document data extraction:

It's not about OCR anymore. Old OCR tools needed a fixed template — same layout, same fields, every time. One new vendor format and the whole thing broke.

Modern AI reads documents the way a person does. It understands context, so it finds the invoice total, PO number, and date regardless of layout, scan quality, or vendor.

The part that actually matters for production use: citations. Every extracted field should link back to its exact spot in the source document. That's what makes the output auditable instead of a black box you have to trust blindly.

Pair that with human review on low-confidence fields, and you get a pipeline that's accurate enough to run unactually unattended for 90% of documents.
