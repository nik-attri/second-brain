AI Workflow or AI Agent ? When to Use Each One?
The decision is simple. Ask yourself:
Do I know the steps to complete the task?
1️⃣ YES → Use an AI Workflow
Example: Employee leave request
Receive request → Check leave balance → Get approval → Update HR system → Notify employee.
2️⃣ NO → Use an AI Agent
Example: Investigating a slow application.
You ask: "Find out why our application is slow."
The agent investigates logs, database performance, and recent changes to identify the problem.
💡 Remember:
Use workflows to automate what you know.
Use agents to solve what you don't know.

The goal: Less time, lower costs and better business results.
