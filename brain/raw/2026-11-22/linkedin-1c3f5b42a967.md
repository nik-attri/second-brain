Everyone in your business has one tech thing they've never admitted they don't know how to do. 🤫

Weekend thought, from the comfort of wherever you're reading this: the mail merge nobody has ever actually done. The second monitor that's always the wrong way round. The difference between Reply and Reply All, learned the hard way or never. Where downloads actually go. What MFA actually is, beyond "that thing that pops up".

None of it is a knowledge gap. It's a confidence gap. Everyone assumes everyone else was told, and nobody was.

Here's the quietly expensive bit: people don't leave the question unanswered, they build a workaround instead. Doing the thing manually, every time. Avoiding the feature entirely. Copying the file rather than risking the link. Saving it somewhere "safe". Each one sensible. Together, a small tax the business pays without noticing.

So a gentle weekend challenge: think of your one thing (you know the one), and just ask it this week. To IT, to a colleague, to whoever sits nearest. Nobody gets judged, and the "silly" questions are usually the exact ones three other people were also wondering about.

There are no silly questions in IT. Only the ones nobody asked, silently, for years.
