I had a good chuckle when I saw this on r/ProgrammerHumor. It's a classic example of how recursive processes can feel like they lead us in circles.

In the world of AI and automation, we sometimes find ourselves in a similar loop. Think about it: we set up systems to automate tasks, only to end up building elaborate workflows that mimic human behaviors—often duplicating what we already do. It’s like we’re looking for efficiency, but inadvertently creating more steps in the process.

To avoid this recursion in our workflows, we need clear documentation, consistent handoffs, and regular assessments. Are we genuinely enhancing our processes, or are we just automating our old habits?
