It's 9:47 PM. A potential client calls your firm.
No one picks up. By morning, they've hired the firm that answered.
This happens at good firms every week. Not because the lawyers are bad, but because the office still runs the way it did 15 years ago.

Look at where your team's hours actually go:
 → Typing the same client details into 3 different systems
 → Chasing clients for documents and signatures
 → Answering "any update on my case?" calls all day
 → Doing intake and follow-ups by hand
None of that is legal work. It's a workflow problem, and workflows can be automated.

What changes when a law firm automates the busy work:
 ✅ Every new inquiry gets a reply within minutes, even at night
 ✅ Intake forms go straight into your case management system
 ✅ Clients upload documents and get reminders without anyone chasing
 ✅ Status updates go out on their own when a case moves forward

Your attorneys spend their time on the law. Your staff stops drowning in admin. And fewer clients slip away to the firm that called back first.

We recently automated the operations of a US law firm at Codexyo, and the biggest surprise was how many hours were going into tasks nobody had questioned in years.
