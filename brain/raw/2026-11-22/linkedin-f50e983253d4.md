You see, proving ISO/IEC 27001 compliance doesn't have to mean drowning in spreadsheets or scrambling to justify unexpected database drift. We use a lightweight utility designed to automate the painful task of database schema and master data tracking.
Rather than relying on manual logs or ad-hoc scripts, ISOmDB PRO acts as a tamper-resistant black box. It takes automated, timestamped snapshots to capture precise database states, mapping directly to key ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls, such as:

Change Management (A 8.32): Captures pre- and post-deployment snapshots to give auditors clear, undeniable evidence of what changed.

Configuration Management (A 8.9): Runs scheduled hourly checks to catch unauthorized drift outside approved deployment windows.

Data Leakage & Deletion (A 8.12 / 8.10): Validates that security metadata—like masking rules and retention tags—hasn't been quietly modified.
