Over the last few weeks, I’ve jumped on calls with about 100 prospects for 3650HQ.

My biggest takeaway? The first call rarely moves the needle. In most cases, even the third call doesn't. Closing B2B deals requires meticulous follow-up, tracking call notes, remembering specific pain points, and knowing exactly where to pick up the conversation on the next dial.

As a solo founder juggling core software engineering, product direction, and demos, managing this pipeline manually is no longer scalable. Founder-led sales got us here, but to scale our conversational commerce platform, I need a dedicated closer.

I am opening up a remote B2B Inside Sales role to join my core team.

Our best clients are high-volume Instagram vendors and e-commerce brands drowning in manual DMs, payment confirmations, and logistics. Your job is to identify them, get them on the phone, manage the CRM tracking, and move them through the pipeline fast.

The Package: Competitive fixed base pay, plus aggressive milestone bonuses and uncapped commission. (Remote role).

I care about execution, not your CV.

If you know how to work a pipeline and close deals, show me. Study our website and social media, then DM me a brief, actionable strategy: How would you identify, pitch, and close 10 high-volume online merchants for us this week?
