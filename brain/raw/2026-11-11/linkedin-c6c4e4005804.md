Automate Your Nurturing

So, how do we solve that 'silent treatment' problem without having to manually email every single new lead? Automation is your secret weapon here! I am a huge advocate for setting up a simple, automated 3-part email sequence. This isn't about spamming people; it's about systematically building a relationship. Your first email can be a warm welcome, the second can offer more value related to their problem, and the third can introduce your solution. It works wonders for building trust and guiding them gently down the funnel. My unbreakable rule is to always automate the welcome. What's your favorite automation tool or trick? Let's share!
