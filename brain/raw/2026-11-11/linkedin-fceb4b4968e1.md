#hiring Centre Manager

Apply here: https://lnkd.in/g5nsxfgB

Routes to Roots is looking for an inspiring, compassionate and proactive Centre Manager to lead the day-to-day operations and future development of the Genesis Centre in Poole. This is not a role where you will spend every day behind a desk. You will be at the centre of a busy, welcoming homelessness service, making sure that people experiencing homelessness or housing insecurity receive the very best possible support. Your role will also involve leading the team, building partnerships, solving problems, and developing opportunities. Some days will require strategic thinking. Others will require you to roll up your sleeves and help make things happen. We are looking for someone who can do both! About the role As Centre Manager, you will take the lead for the day-to-day running and continued development of the Genesis Centre, providing hands-on support when required. Working closely with our Trustees, staff, volunteers and local partners, you will have real scope to shape how the service develops and to strengthen the Genesis Centres role within Pooles response to homelessness. You will be a visible and approachable presence within the Centre and the wider community, helping to raise the profile and reputation of Routes to Roots and strengthen our network of partners and supporters by:  Centre operations - running the day-to-day service of the Centre, managing budgets, resources, premises, donations, food supplies, health and safety, safeguarding and compliance. People & volunteers - leading and supporting employees and volunteers, including recruitment, induction, development, performance and wellbeing. Partnerships & community engagement - building strong relationships with BCP Council, homelessness services, health services, community organisations and other local partners, while representing Routes to Roots at networking events and community activities. Leadership & strategy - working with the Trustees to deliver the charitys strategic and operational plans, monitor outcomes and identify opportunities to improve and develop services. Fundraising & sustainability - supporting the Trustees with fundraising, identifying grant opportunities and contributing to the financial sustainability and future development of the charity. Governance & reporting - providing Trustees with clear information on performance, finances, risks, incidents and strategic progress, and ensuring policies and procedures are effective and up to date.  About you We are looking for someone with experience of managing people and/or volunteers and overseeing a service, programme or project, including budgets and resources.
