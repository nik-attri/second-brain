📢 We’re Hiring
  
Bilingual HR & Admin Executive (Malayalam/Telegu/Kannada/Tamil with Arabic)
  
📍 Location: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
💼 Department: HR & Admin / Government Relations
💰 Monthly Gross Salary: SAR 6,000–8,000 (All-inclusive) - depending on interview and experience.
 
My client is looking for an experienced HR & Admin Executive to manage their KSA government relations, employee documentation, and regulatory compliance.
The ideal candidate will be fluent in Arabic and English, with strong practical experience navigating Saudi government portals and managing time-sensitive approvals.
 
Key Responsibilities
• Manage work visas, Iqama issuance, renewals, sponsorship transfers, exit/re-entry, and final exits. Track passport, Iqama, and company document expiry dates to ensure timely renewals.
• Handle Muqeem, Absher Business, Qiwa, Mudad, and GOSI (including daily employee data updates), employment contracts, and sponsorship transfer requests.
• Manage transactions through Balady, Etimad, MISA, Chamber of Commerce and Ministry of Commerce portals, alongside SFDA approvals for samples.
• Monitor Nitaqat/Saudization compliance, work permits, and professional accreditations; follow up with MHRSD, Jawazat, the Labour Office, and Municipality on approvals, violations, fines, and absconding cases.
• Renew commercial registrations, Chamber memberships, and municipal licenses; maintain accurate government and employee records and coordinate legal documentation with the Dubai Head Office.
• Support employees with medical insurance linking, GOSI registration, and guidance on KSA rules and procedures.
 
Candidate Requirements
• Should be currently in KSA with a valid, transferable Iqama
• Should have 3–5 years of similar experience
• Should have a Bachelor’s degree in HR or other related fields
• Experience in the Food Ingredients or Food Manufacturing industry is highly preferred
• Malayalam/Tamil/Kannada/Telegu: Native (MUST) 
• Arabic: Fluent reading, writing, and speaking (MUST)
• English. Hands-on experience with Muqeem, Absher, Qiwa, Mudad, and GOSI is mandatory
• A valid Saudi driving licence and established professional contacts in Jawazat/Labour Office are advantages.
 
Additional Benefits:
• Medical insurance, car fuel, company mobile with SIM, and laptop.
• An annual bonus may be awarded based on individual performance and/or company results, at management’s discretion (not guaranteed).
