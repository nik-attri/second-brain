Do you share my frustration with AI?

AI can help us create almost anything. So why can't it find what we've already created?

Conversational AI is changing how we work, and voice-activated, agentic AI is rapidly moving towards becoming part of everyday life.

Increasingly, we're developing ideas through dialogue rather than creating and filing traditional documents. We're talking to AI, exploring possibilities and building projects through conversations, often on our smartphones rather than sitting at a desktop organising folders.

The conversation itself is becoming our workspace.

But six months later, how do we retrieve something specific?

We shouldn't have to remember which conversation contained an idea, or manually search hundreds of chats to reconstruct work we've already done.

And as we move towards increasingly sophisticated voice-activated and agentic AI, this problem becomes more pressing.

The more we entrust to AI, the more essential reliable retrieval becomes.

I'd be interested to hear your thoughts, particularly from those developing AI systems. Are there practical solutions already emerging?
