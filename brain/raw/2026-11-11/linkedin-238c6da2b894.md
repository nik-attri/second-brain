We are hiring 

Position: Process Coordinator
Location: Vidya Vihar, Mumbai
Experience: Freshers can apply

Job Overview

We are looking for a detail-oriented and organised Process Coordinator / Back Office Associate to support day-to-day administrative and operational activities. The ideal candidate should have good communication skills, basic computer knowledge, and the ability to handle documentation, data management, and coordination tasks efficiently.

Freshers with a willingness to learn and a proactive approach to work are welcome to apply.

Key Responsibilities

- Handle day-to-day back-office and administrative operations.
- Maintain, update, and organise records, documents, and databases.
- Perform data entry and ensure accuracy of information.
- Coordinate with internal departments and team members to facilitate smooth workflow.
- Monitor and follow up on ongoing processes and pending tasks.
- Prepare and maintain reports, spreadsheets, and other operational documents.
- Assist in processing, verifying, and managing relevant paperwork.
- Ensure timely completion of assigned tasks and adherence to internal procedures.
- Provide general operational and administrative support as required.

Candidate Requirements

- Freshers are welcome to apply.
- Minimum qualification: Graduate in any discipline.
- Basic computer knowledge and familiarity with MS Office, particularly Excel and Word.
- Good verbal and written communication skills.
- Strong organisational and time-management abilities.
- Attention to detail and willingness to learn.
- Ability to work independently as well as in a team.

Compensation

Salary will be offered based on the candidate's suitability, qualifications, and relevant work experience.
