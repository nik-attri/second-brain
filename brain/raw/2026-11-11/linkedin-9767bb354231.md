Celebrating Tom Repetti: A Well-Deserved Retirement! 🎉
After years of dedication, expertise, and countless contributions to Comport's success, we are excited to celebrate the retirement of Tom Repetti, one of the original members of our technical team!

Tom has been an integral part of Comport's growth and evolution over the years. With an incredible ability to turn complex data into meaningful information, Tom became our go-to expert for reporting, spreadsheets, and pulling seemingly impossible information from our systems. If there was data to be found, Tom could find it—often making it look like magic!

Beyond his legendary spreadsheet skills, Tom played a critical role in helping Comport navigate important technology and CRM transitions, ensuring our teams had the information, tools, and insights needed to support the business as it grew. His behind-the-scenes contributions have had a lasting impact on our company, helping shape the way we operate today.

But anyone who knows Tom knows that his greatest pride and joy has always been his family. As he begins this exciting new chapter, we're thrilled that he'll have more time to spend with the people who mean the most to him. And when he's not enjoying family time, you can bet he'll be out on his motorcycle, enjoying the open road and the freedom that retirement brings!
Tom, thank you for your years of loyalty, hard work, patience, and the countless times you made the impossible seem easy. Your knowledge, dedication, and contributions have been invaluable, and you leave behind a legacy that will continue to benefit Comport for years to come.
You will certainly be missed, and we're not quite sure who will work their magic on our spreadsheets now!
Please join us in congratulating Tom on an incredible career and wishing him a happy, healthy, and well-deserved retirement! Here's to family, new adventures, and plenty of miles on the open road! 🏍️
