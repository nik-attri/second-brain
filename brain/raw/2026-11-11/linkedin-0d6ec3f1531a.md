About a year ago, I decided to build software you could run your company in. Software that does things, and that you can talk to.

2,000+ hours later (not a typo), about 200 Markdown files of manuals, dev logs and guides, 1,100+ automated tests and a very small beta group, it's time to share it. I kept quiet because it wasn't done.

By September, the product was mature enough to open the company. Our CTO Davor Majc joined and things accelerated. I come from logistics, events and trades, not IT. His strengths complement what I haven't learned yet. Most of our time went into completing Craft11 V5 together.

We're letting the first clients in this week. We'll provide onboarding for anyone who needs it.

What does it do?

Keeps clients, offers, jobs, notes and reports connected, shaped around how your company works.

Answers questions from your business records, drafts offers and follow-ups, and suggests improvements.

Three ways to work: by hand, with the assistant, or let it run (in testing).

V5 isn't just a chat window attached to business software. Its own harness defines what the AI can do, which records it can use and when it needs approval.

Anything touching money, stock, sending or deleting waits for your yes on a card naming the exact record. Emails and notes are information, not orders.

When something is unclear, it asks. Inferred details stay marked unconfirmed. Nothing enters the company's knowledge Vault without the owner's approval.

People kept asking, “Could it do this too?” So I built standalone satellites that can also connect to V5.

FFC and LCC work alongside freight forwarders and logistics teams: tracking operations, communicating with vendors, collecting documents, preparing three comparable offers and marking tasks completed in their own system and V5 if connected. Less manual work, more decisions.

EventGenerator turns an event inquiry into a fresh programme, not last spring's with a new date.

Craft33 handles data-driven paid advertising and positioning in LLM answers, not more social posts. Honestly, I built it because I'm too lazy to do that manually. It runs in loops, using results to inform the next round. Craft11 itself will be its pilot.

No endless sales pitches. Spam kept me away from LinkedIn and X for long stretches. I don't want to become the reason I almost left. I'll share videos showing how these things work.
