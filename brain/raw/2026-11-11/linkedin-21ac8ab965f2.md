The most boring process in my company is now the most automated one.

Invoicing. 

Every payment for a Product Circle event needs an invoice, and it's never as simple as it sounds. The rules change depending on the buyer's country, the currency, whether they added company and VAT details, and which event they bought. Easy to get wrong, painful to fix.

Sergiu Lazar Angelescu and I wanted to automate it for years (since we started the company basically), but with our no-code automation tools, the exceptions and edge cases were too many, and it was more painful to fix than to just do it. So we postponed and kept doing it manually.

Then a couple of months ago I handed it to AI.

We had 3 years of historic data of payments, invoices and event applications, so it did an amazing job of surfacing the patterns and writing the SOP. We just had to review it, tweak it a bit, and give the go ahead.

Now, every weekday at 6am, an agent:
→ reads the new payments
→ matches each one to the right person in our database (also checking email for exceptions)
→ drafts the invoice following the SOP
→ posts a summary in Slack, flagging anything it isn't sure about

A human still approves every invoice. And if the agent isn't ~95% sure who a payment belongs to, it doesn't guess. It stops and asks.

So yes, as the photo shows, my job now is to chill in the pool 😎
