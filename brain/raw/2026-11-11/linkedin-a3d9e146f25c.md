Designation - Product Manager - Digital Journey
Product - Personal Loan
Industry - NBFC
Locations - Mumbai
Qualifications: Any graduation with minimum 6 years of relevant experience

Key responsibilities include:

1. Digital Journey & Funnel Optimization
End-to-End Journey Ownership: Manage and continuously enhance the Straight-Through Processing (STP) and assisted digital loan application journeys across web, mobile app (PLANET), and partner API ecosystems .
Waterfall & Conversion Analytics: Track daily/monthly digital application waterfalls, identify drop-off bottlenecks across journey stages (e.g., Bureau/CIBIL pulls, CKYC/OKYC, Account Aggregator/Perfios, e-Mandate/NACH, e-Sign, and V-KYC), and implement product interventions to maximize lead-to-disbursal conversion .
Turnaround Time (TAT) & Cost Optimization: Regularly review digital processes and third-party fintech/vendor API integrations to minimize processing TAT and optimize Cost of Acquisition (COA) per disbursed loan .
Customer Experience (CX): Deliver a frictionless, intuitive UI/UX loan application experience and post-disbursal self-service capabilities to boost Net Promoter Score (NPS) and customer delight .

2. Tech Execution & Cross-Functional Collaboration
Product Documentation & Agile Delivery: Translate business requirements into comprehensive Business Requirement Documents (BRDs), Functional Requirement Documents (FRDs), and user stories (JIRA) .
UAT & Go-Live Management: Lead User Acceptance Testing (UAT), bug resolution, and production sign-offs for LOS/LMS enhancements and new digital builds .
Stakeholder Alignment: Work closely with Credit Policy, Risk, Analytics, BIU/EDW, IT/Engineering, Operations, Marketing (CLM), and Sales/Contact Center teams to ensure seamless product delivery and GTM execution .
MIS & Executive Dashboards: Create and automate monthly/ad-hoc dashboards and funnel trackers (Excel/Power BI) for senior management reviews .

3. Product Strategy, Lifecycle & Portfolio Management
Product Strategy & New Launches: Develop and execute the end-to-end product roadmap for Personal Loans (Fresh sourcing, Pre-approved/Loyalty base, Top-up loans, and Balance Transfers) .
Market Benchmarking: Conduct ongoing competitive benchmarking on pricing (IRR/processing fees), product features, and emerging fintech trends to capture market opportunities .
Portfolio & Risk Health Monitoring: Track book movement, portfolio mix, attrition/foreclosures, and key risk metrics—specifically early warning signals (EWS), bounce rates, pending mandate registration (target <0.50%), and early/coincidental delinquency (e.g., 30+ DPD in 12M) .
Profitability & Pricing: Collaborate with Finance, Policy, and Business teams on yield forecasting and risk-adjusted pricing grids.

4. Regulatory Compliance & Governance
RBI Digital Lending Compliance: Stay updated on RBI regulations (including Digital Lending Guidelines, Key Fact Statement [KFS] norms, cooling-off periods, and data privacy) and ensure 100% compliance across all digital journeys and partner flows .
Audit & Submissions: Manage internal audits, statutory audits, Operational Risk Management (ORMG) assessments, and timely regulatory/RBI submissions.
