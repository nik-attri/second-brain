Everyone in FinTech wants real time payments. Almost nobody is ready for real time exceptions.

I spoke to an operations lead recently who was at her breaking point.

Her company had just launched instant payouts. The marketing campaign was a huge success. Customers loved the speed.

But behind the scenes, it was a disaster.

When a payment failed because a partner bank had a brief timeout, the money left the sender but never arrived at the receiver.

The customer panicked. The support team had no visibility. And the operations team had to manually trace the transaction across three different portals just to figure out where the money was stuck.

This is the hidden operational tax of real time infrastructure.

We spend months building the front end to move money in milliseconds. But we forget to build the back end to handle the exceptions.

In the old world of batch processing, if a payment failed, you had 24 hours to fix it before anyone noticed.

In a real time world, if a payment fails, the customer knows instantly. And if your reconciliation process still relies on spreadsheets and manual checks, you are going to drown.

Real time money movement requires real time risk management and automated reconciliation.

It requires an orchestration layer that can automatically retry failed transactions, route around partner outages, and update the ledger instantly.

If you are moving money fast but fixing exceptions slowly, you do not have a real time product. You have a real time liability.

When a complex payment fails in your system today, how long does it take your team to actually find out why?
