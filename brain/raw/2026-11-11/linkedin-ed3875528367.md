⚡ One firmware update. Several PLCs down. And a utility operator looking for the last working configuration. 😬
#AMDT #Octoplant #Octovision #OTDataManagement #OTCyberSecurity #OTSSM #SecureYourCrownJewels #SPS2026

For years, the OT conversation started with one question:
“How do we prevent an incident?”

Still important. But when essential services are at stake, another question matters just as much:

“How quickly can we recover?” ⏱️

🔧 Use Case: A misconfigured firmware update corrupts several PLCs at a utility site.

Without a reliable backup, engineers may need to travel on site, find the right project files and rebuild configurations manually. Meanwhile, the clock keeps ticking.

With #Octoplant, automated backups and version history give the team a known-good version to compare and restore. They can see what changed, who changed it and when, focus on the affected assets, and recover the correct configurations within minutes. 🔄

That is the shift from security-first to resilience-first.

Prevention remains essential. But recovery cannot depend on finding the one engineer who remembers where the backup lives. 😅

🚀 What does this mean for utility operators?

✅ Better visibility into changes across OT assets.
✅ Faster, more targeted recovery when a change goes wrong.
✅ Less time guessing whether the rest of the environment is affected.

A misconfiguration or cyber incident does not have to mean rebuilding everything from scratch.

Security helps protect your operations. Resilience helps get them moving again. ⚡

If your PLCs failed tomorrow, would you know which version to restore? ☕
