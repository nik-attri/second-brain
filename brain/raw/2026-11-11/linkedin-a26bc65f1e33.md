#Secure_Networks is Hiring
 Senior System Administrator  (Versa Secure SD-WAN) 

 Resident Engineer to join our team on-site in Riyadh, KSA.
If you are a dedicated professional looking to drive excellence in enterprise network infrastructure and SD-WAN operations, we invite you to apply!
Key Responsibilities:
Platform Administration: Administer and maintain Versa Director, Controller, Analytics, VOS devices, and Versa Concerto across hub, data center, and cloud environments.
Network Operations: Configure SD-WAN topologies, BGP, OSPF, VRFs, IPsec overlays, QoS, and handle zero-touch/staged site deployments.
Security & Compliance: Administer integrated security services (NGFW, UTM, access policies) and ensure system hardening and certificate management.
Support & Automation: Provide Tier-3 operational support, perform root-cause analysis, and automate workflows using Python, Ansible, or REST APIs.
Required Qualifications:
Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, IT, or a related field.
Experience:
7+ years in network or systems administration (enterprise or service provider).
3+ years of hands-on experience administering Versa Secure SD-WAN in production.
Technical Skills: Strong proficiency in TCP/IP, BGP, OSPF, VRFs, IPsec, NAT, DNS, DHCP, Linux administration, and virtualization (VMware/KVM).
Preferred Qualifications:
Versa certifications (VNX100 / VNX400) or industry credentials (CCNP, NSE, CISSP).
Experience with multi-tenant environments, Versa SASE, or cloud integrations (AWS, Azure, GCP).
Knowledge of automation tools (Terraform, Ansible, REST APIs) and ITIL/ISO 27001 frameworks.

Interested candidates are invited to send their updated CV to careers@secure-networks.com or reach out directly via messages for details.
