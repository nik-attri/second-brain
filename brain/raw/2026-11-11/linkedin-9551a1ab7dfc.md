We are Hiring:  😊  😊  😊 
APAC Business Operation  Manage

We are looking for a high-energy, AI-native Sales Operation Manager to join our global team. This isn't a "traditional" ops role where you just move data in spreadsheets. You will be the backbone of our sales engine across APAC.
The ideal candidate understands that the future of operations is automated. You don’t just wait for a tool; you build a workflow. You have a "vibe coding" mentality—using LLMs and low-code tools to bridge the gap between "we need this" and "it's done."


Core Responsibilities
Regional Planning & Forecasting: Assist in setting targets and building predictable revenue models for APAC.
Compensation & Incentives: Manage and calculate sales commission plans, ensuring accuracy and motivating the team.
Key Issue Actionable Insights: Don’t just build dashboards; tell us what the data means. Identify bottlenecks in the sales funnel and propose immediate fixes.
Global Coordination: Bridge the gap between time zones, ensuring regional sales leaders are aligned with global strategy.
CRM Hygiene & Tech Stack: Own the "Source of Truth." Maintain CRM  integrity and optimize our sales tech stack.


What We’re Looking For
Experience: 5+ years of experience in Sales Ops, RevOps, or a highly analytical role.
Language: Native or professional fluency in both English and Mandarin. You will be communicating across diverse global teams.
The AI-Native Mindset: You are a power user of ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. You don't do manual work if an API or an automation can do it better.
Vibe Coding Familiarity: You might not be a Software Engineer, but you know how to prompt, use Claude Code/Codex, or leverage "Natural Language" to build internal tools or automate boring tasks.


Bonus Points If...
You’ve worked in a high-growth SaaS environment.
You have a "Builder" mentality—you'd rather fix a broken process than complain about it.


Why Join Us?
Tech-Forward Culture: We skip the bureaucracy and focus on what works.
