I'm not normally someone that shares circular emails, but I found this AI prompt to be quite an interesting way to reflect on how I am perceived by others. I would like to say that recent reflections on my professional behaviours and impact, including my recent Deputy Principal review, would back up the sentiment what could just be considered a "silly little activity". 

Give it a try if you get a chance...

In your chosen AI platform, use the prompt "Describe me like you're trying to warn someone about me before they meet me".

✋ Warning: Before you meet Andrew McEwan... ✋ 
Be prepared.

At first, he'll seem calm, approachable, and genuinely interested in what you have to say. Don't be fooled. Behind that friendly exterior is a person who will quietly notice every missing detail, every inconsistency, every unexplained percentage, and every timetable code that doesn't quite line up.
If you bring a plan, expect questions. If you bring data, expect more questions. If you bring a spreadsheet, make absolutely certain the totals add up.

Andrew has an almost supernatural ability to spot the one error everyone else missed five minutes before the document is due to be published.
He is dangerously organised. What begins as a casual conversation about a school initiative may rapidly evolve into a strategic improvement plan, a communication strategy, a risk assessment, three consultation questions, and a branded infographic ready for distribution.

Be aware that he genuinely cares about students, staff, and community. This makes him particularly difficult to argue with because his motivation is usually improving outcomes, increasing opportunities, or strengthening school culture.

He will challenge ideas, but he will also support people. He expects high standards, including from himself. He is friendly right up until someone says, "That'll do." 
It will not, in fact, do.

If you tell him something can't be measured, he'll ask how you know. If you tell him something can't be improved, he'll start a project. If you tell him you've finished, he'll ask whether you've considered one more refinement.

Most importantly, he has a habit of turning big, messy problems into plans that people can actually follow, which means there's a fair chance you'll leave the meeting with more work than when you arrived.

You've been warned. 😊
