The exception queue is where automation gets tested.

Straightforward cases move.
Messy cases don't.

And messy cases usually need:
→ additional documents
→ manual validation
→ clarification
→ escalation
→ specialist review

This creates an interesting problem.
Your average processing time might look good.
But your team can still be drowning in exceptions.

I'd rather ask:
What happens to the documents that don't fit the happy path?
That's usually where the real operational work is hiding.

If you manage underwriting or claims, what's your most common exception?
