For most teams, affiliate recruitment still eats up 20 hours a week. Because you’ve gotta...  ⬇️

1. Find your leads (whether what’s manual scrolling or dealing with janky scrapers)

2. Work out whether they’re a fit (watching/reading a ton of their content)

3. Hunt down their email (hello captchas my old friend)

4. Write a personalised message (ChatGPT ain’t cutting it)

4. Update the spreadsheet (one of many, many spreadsheets)

5. Remember to follow up (my own personal hell. I can’t remember what I had for breakfast let alone who I spoke to 9 days ago)

All of these steps need doing consistently, to a high standard, at enough volume to move your program forward AND leave time to support the affiliates you have.

Ain’t easy to handle alone, I know - I’ve been there 😅
