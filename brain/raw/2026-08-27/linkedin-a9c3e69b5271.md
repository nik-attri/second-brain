I had no idea what MCPs were three months ago, but now I'm using them all day  in Claude to work. Those "you'll save so much time with AI!" promises are starting to feel true...but I'm feeling a little conflicted about it.

An MCP is a bit of back end tech that functions as a connector between your LLM and all of the other software you use. The most important part for most of us: MCPs enable us to perform work in other apps just by prompting Claude, or ChatGPT. Not only can we avoid all of that time-wastey task/app switching, but we can complete the same job a lot faster. 

Over time, all of those seconds and minutes saved really add up.

Setting aside for a moment the implications of becoming so reliant on a single interface (I've still mulling that over!) here are some of the ways I've been using MCPs to speed up my workday: 

→ I stopped manually checking my calendar for open windows and typing in events by hand. I tell Claude who needs to be in a meeting and it finds the time, books it, and sends the invite.

→ I used to build every deck from scratch inside Gamma. Now Claude drops the content straight into my account and I just open the draft and polish it.

→ I've just started using Trello, and instead of me learning the interface and building boards by hand, Claude sets up my to-do lists and builds templates for work I know I'll repeat.

→ And once a week, a Claude skill checks my email, my calendar, and my HubSpot deals board together, then flags where I need to add a new deal or shift one to a different stage. Three tools that used to live in three separate browser tabs, now just talking to each other.

→  Every meeting I take gets recorded through Otter, and Claude can pull straight from those transcripts, no re-listening or scrolling for the one thing someone said 40 minutes in.

→  I can pull docs from Google Drive and write straight back into it too, so a long working session with Claude ends with the actual file sitting where I need it

I'm sure there are tons of other ways to use MCPs, and I'd love to hear your ideas. Also, do you think it's a benefit (or should we be worried) that everything from browsing websites to interacting with our SaaS might be done in one place, with one LLM? (I'm extremely reliant on Claude now, so...all the thoughts and feelings about that).
