Most AI companies can build a voice agent that talks.
ABA was designed to do something more valuable:
Capture and grow revenue.
Booking appointments?
Rescheduling or cancelling them?
Checking availability in real time?
Answering several calls simultaneously?
That is old news.
Those capabilities matter—but they should already be standard in any serious voice AI product.
The real question is:
What happens before, during and after the conversation?
ABA turns isolated voice automation into a configurable revenue-capture system.
From an intuitive dashboard, businesses can adapt how the agent operates without depending on a developer every time something changes.
They can configure:
→ Inbound and outbound call flows
→ Lead-qualification questions
→ Appointment and callback processes
→ Different behaviours for different services or campaigns
→ Call outcomes and commercial handoffs
→ The information collected before a lead reaches the sales team
In our higher-tier subscriptions, ABA can go even further by providing hundreds of relevant business prospects every month and calling them automatically.
For one restaurant chain promoting its catering packages, ABA called 8,000 businesses across Bucharest.
The result?
34 leads with genuine buying intent.
Not 8,000 numbers sitting untouched in a spreadsheet.
Not 34 people who merely answered the phone.
Thirty-four businesses that expressed commercial interest in the client’s offer—identified without a human sales team manually dialling thousands of prospects.
That is the difference.

And businesses should not have to spend thousands commissioning a one-off voice workflow—or call a developer whenever their commercial process changes—to access that capability.
