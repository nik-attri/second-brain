Today marks my final day at Businesswise Solutions.

After just over 8 years, it’s difficult to sum up what the business, the people and the journey have meant to me.

I’ve had the opportunity to grow with the company through an enormous amount of change — building teams, developing new services, working with some brilliant clients, tackling some genuinely complex energy challenges and, most importantly, working alongside a group of people I’ve learned a huge amount from.

I leave incredibly proud of what we’ve built together and confident in the people taking it forward.

To everyone at Businesswise Solutions, past and present, and to the clients, partners and colleagues I’ve worked with along the way: thank you.

There have been plenty of challenges, plenty of laughs, a lot of miles travelled, and more spreadsheets than any reasonable person should ever have to look at — but I wouldn’t change the experience.

Now, onto the next chapter.

I’m delighted to announce that I’ll shortly be joining Best.Energy!

It’s a fantastic opportunity to take everything I’ve learned and apply it to a new challenge, working with a business passionate and focused entirely on turning energy data, technology and optimisation into measurable value for customers.

I’m particularly looking forward to getting into the business next week, getting to know the people, customers and opportunities, and helping shape the next stage of growth.

For today though, it’s simply a final goodbye to Businesswise Solutions.

Eight years. A hell of a journey.

Thank you to everyone who has been part of it.

Onwards. 👊
