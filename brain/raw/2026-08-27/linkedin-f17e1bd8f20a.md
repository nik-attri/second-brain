One month you're drowning in leads.
The next, your phone is dead silent.

The "Feast or Famine" cycle is the silent killer of real estate growth.

Most agents react to the silence by working harder.
More cold calls. More manual follow-ups. More "hustle."

But you can't hustle your way out of a broken system.

The cycle happens because your lead generation is "event-based" rather than "system-based."

You turn on ads when you're hungry.
You turn them off when you're busy.

This creates a rollercoaster of cash flow that makes scaling impossible.

To break the cycle, you need an "Always-On" engine:

1. Meta campaigns running 24/7 to keep the top of the funnel full.
2. AI automation to qualify leads instantly, so you only spend time with serious buyers.

Stop chasing leads. Start building a machine that delivers them.

Do you find it harder to generate new leads, or to qualify the ones you already have?
