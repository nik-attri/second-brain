The word ‘we’ quietly wipes the value out of some of the best interview answers I hear from my new clients

After years of leading teams and sharing credit they feel uncomfortable saying ‘I’ because they think it makes them sound arrogant or like they’re claiming every win for themselves

So the interviewer asks what they did and they say…

‘We changed the process’

‘We brought the customer back’

‘We got the project under control’

It all sounds positive but the person in front of you still doesn’t know what you actually really did

Did you spot the problem or did somebody else tell you what was wrong?

Did you make the unpleasant callout or did you sit in the meeting while somebody else made it?

Did you lead the work or did you update the spreadsheet and nod at the right moments?

A strong interview answer lets ‘we’ explain the team effort and uses ‘I’ to show your own judgment and actions

Something like this…

‘We had a major customer renewal at risk because three teams disagreed about what had gone wrong
I pulled the account data together and brought the three team leads into one conversation where I got them to agree on the two issues the customer needed fixed first
We kept the customer and used the same recovery plan for the next two accounts showing the same warning signs’

Now the interviewer can hear the team context and your contribution and the result without you pretending you did every single thing yourself

Before your next interview write down one of your examples and circle every ‘we’

Keep the ones explaining the shared problem or result then change the parts that hide your thinking and your actions into ‘I’

This matters even more when you’ve spent twenty or thirty years becoming the person who makes difficult work look easy because the interview panel can’t give you credit for experience you’re too polite to call out

Your dog already understands the difference between the pack and her own contribution

She knows you threw the ball and she knows everybody watched but she also knows exactly who chased it across the park and carried it all the way back

She doesn’t steal your part and she sure as hell doesn’t give away hers

That’s The Dog Code in an interview

Share the credit but own the evidence because the interviewer needs to understand the value you personally bring

Your life is not a dress rehearsal so live like someone left the gate open
