You don't need another AI bot. You need a jig.

A customer had 7,000 locations to geofence.
For each one: find it on a map, draw the boundary elsewhere, feed the GeoJSON to Claude, paste the coordinates into a spreadsheet, upload.
Repeat 6,999 times.
Ouch.
A woodworking jig doesn't replace skill. It makes one job easier to repeat.
So to help with this, we're building one screen:
View the location. Outline it. Annotate it. Save.
Human judgment draws the geofence. The jig removes the copy-paste insanity around it.
It doesn't make the list smaller, but it makes it a hell of a lot faster.
AI isn't only a bot inside your systems. It can help you build the tools you're missing.
Build jigs.
