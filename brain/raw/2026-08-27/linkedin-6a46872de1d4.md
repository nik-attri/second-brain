i've been on hundreds of calls with founders where the product was already built and there wasn’t even a real problem being solved

the conversations were always backwards

we built x, the tech does y  
ok... but who needs it?

i saw it constantly over five years at NEAR. a narrative would get hot, money would start moving toward it and suddenly a pile of almost identical products appeared with slightly different features and a customer reverse engineered into the deck

ai is making this so much worse because it can make a made up problem look researched in about 20 minutes

the founders i had the most confidence in (and that were usually the outliers) usually knew the problem in boring detail before they knew what the product should be

they'd lived with the workaround. they knew what people were already paying for, where the process broke and what happened when it did

if i was thinking about building something now, i'd want somebody to screen share exactly what they do when the problem hits

show me the spreadsheet they made, the other product they open, who they pay to do it manually and where they finally say fk it and give up

then use ai to find more people doing the same thing and work out whether there's enough there to build around

if the strongest evidence you have is a list of people saying "yeah i'd use that", i'd be terrified of spending the next year building it
