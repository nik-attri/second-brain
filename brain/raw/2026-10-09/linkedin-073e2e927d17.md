I find it interesting how easily we get used to doing things the hard way.



Not because there isn't a better way.
Sometimes, we just stop questioning the process.

Take content creation, for example.
Imagine spending two hours recording a podcast.

You had a great conversation. There were moments where someone shared something insightful, said something funny, or made a point that could spark an entirely new conversation online.

Somewhere in that recording are probably several pieces of content worth sharing.
But finding them?

You have to sit through the recording again.
Pause. Rewind. Write down timestamps.
Find the interesting parts, cut them out, edit them, and get everything ready to post.
And this is after you've already spent hours creating the content.
The next day?

Another recording. The same process.
At some point, I started wondering...
Why should creators have to spend so much time searching through content they've already created?

That question is part of what led me to start building Cliprol.

The idea is simple.
You already did the hard part. You created the content.

Cliprol helps you get more out of it by analysing your recordings, identifying interesting moments, and turning those moments into shorter clips you can review and share.

Think about a two-hour podcast.
Instead of manually searching through the entire recording for those few interesting conversations, you could let Cliprol find potential highlights and explain what makes each moment worth considering.

You still decide what makes the final cut.
You create once. Cliprol helps you find more content inside it. And while building this, something else has been on my mind.

How many things do we do every day simply because that's how they've always been done?

How many processes have we accepted as normal, even when they're unnecessarily difficult or time-consuming?

I think there's a difference between a problem that doesn't have a solution and a problem we've simply become comfortable living with. And sometimes, the second one is much harder to notice.

I'm building Cliprol around one of those problems, and I'm curious:

What's one frustrating process you've gotten so used to that you barely question it anymore?
