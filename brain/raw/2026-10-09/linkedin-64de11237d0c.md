5 years waiting for Autodesk to introduce a Minimum Surface feature in Civil 3D… So I built my own! ☕
For years, creating an excavation surface using the lowest elevations between two surfaces has been a challenge in our earthworks projects. We often need to combine:
- Stripping Surface: Excavation required to remove unsuitable material.
- Underside of Build-up Surface: Minimum excavation levels required for the design build-up.

My previous workflow involved finding surface intersections, identifying the lower areas, creating multiple boundaries, and pasting dozens of small surfaces together.

When the surfaces intersected in many locations, this could take hours!
My latest CP Toolkit plugin automates this entire process.
Just select two surfaces, run the tool, and grab a coffee while it does the work! ☕
Has anyone else faced this challenge in Civil 3D?
