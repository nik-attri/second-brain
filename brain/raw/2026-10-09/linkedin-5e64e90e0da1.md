I arrived in the U.S. from Mumbai with a student visa, student debt, and no network.
What I did have was a belief that if the work was measurable, it would eventually speak for itself.

So I measured everything.

At Amazon, program managers were each spending hundreds of hours a week manually hand-ingesting vendor CSV data into Excel. I built a QuickBase-Python pipeline that validates data at the point of entry and feeds a real-time live dashboard.

→ Thousands of annual labor hours eliminated
→ Over hundreds of thousands in annual savings
→ Adopted as a replicable template for other programs

Then I built four more platforms that:

→Helped in providing faster site evaluation
→Cut business decision time
→Automated order processing
→Automated Flash Reporting

But the part I’m proudest of isn’t a dashboard.

While building these, I started FutureTechnologyFemale because the women who could do this work weren’t in the room. They didn’t lack ability. They lacked access.

Today FTF has 25K+ followers, 80+ enrolled students, and hiring pathways with JPMorgan Chase and Goldman Sachs. The next chapter is a fund for women-led startups.

I didn’t ask for a seat at the table. I built part of the infrastructure it runs on, then started teaching others to do the same.

What’s one system you built that you wish more people knew about? 👇
