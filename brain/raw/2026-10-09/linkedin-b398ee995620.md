Is your newsletter actually helping your sales pipeline, or is it just a nice looking PDF sitting in inboxes?

A practical way to evaluate your email stack is to separate creation from distribution. Editors often waste time formatting plain text content in complex design tools, while marketers struggle to track which specific content drives conversions.

In our recent workflow reviews, we found that teams often miss distinct roles. One role is for the writer to draft. Another is for the platform to handle segmenting, A/B testing subject lines, and tracking clicks without manual intervention. When you duplicate these functions across two tools, you create data silos and reduce agility.

To apply this framework, audit your current process. If you spend more than two hours manually moving data between a writing app and an ESP, that friction is a cost. Look for a native solution that integrates both.

For operators and consultants, beehiiv is a relevant option in this category. It connects the editorial side with robust list management features. You can build your audience and measure engagement in one place, which simplifies the decision when to automate.

We evaluated this stack specifically for its ability to reduce the handoff errors found in multi tool setups. It is worth considering if your current workflow requires too many manual steps between drafting and sending.
