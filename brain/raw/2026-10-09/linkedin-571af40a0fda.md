You know that shiny new AI ops dashboard your team just got? The one with all the graphs and alerts and "predictive insights"? Chances are, it’s just making your existing problems look fancier.

Here’s the truth: AI ops isn’t about dashboards. It’s about fixing the broken parts of your workflow first. The manual handoffs. The unclear ownership. The "someone else will deal with it" tickets that pile up. If your team is still drowning in alerts because no one’s actually fixing the root cause, no amount of AI will save you.

We’ve seen this play out too many times. A client comes in with a half-dozen monitoring tools, all screaming at different times, and no one knows who’s supposed to do what. Their "AI ops" setup was just more noise. The real fix? Start with one broken process, maybe it’s ticket escalations or deployment bottlenecks, and automate that first. Then layer in the AI tools to handle the repetitive parts.

And here’s the kicker: most "AI ops" tools won’t work unless your workflows are already clean. You can’t automate garbage in, garbage out. So before you spend another naira on dashboards, ask yourself: What’s one process in your team that’s still a mess? That’s where you should start.
