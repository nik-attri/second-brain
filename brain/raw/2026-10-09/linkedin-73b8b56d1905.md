Ask a mum how her daughter’s month has been and she’ll probably remember the row.

The slammed door.

The tears.

The afternoon where everyone ended up in their own rooms wondering what the hell had just happened.

Because that’s what our brains do.

The hard days shout.

The ordinary days whisper.

And the lovely days?

They can disappear completely from the story.


So I have a ridiculously simple suggestion:

For a month, write down one word a day.

Not a diary.

Not a behaviour chart.

Not a spreadsheet tracking every bodily function known to woman.

😂

Just one word.


Bouncy.

Chatty.

Grumpy.

Quiet.

Exhausted.

Happy.

Social.


Then look back.

Because sometimes what feels like:

“She’s been moody for weeks.”

actually looks more like:

Monday - chatty
Tuesday - bouncy
Wednesday - social
Thursday - exhausted
Friday - prickly
Saturday - laughing
Sunday - quiet

That’s a very different picture.

And that is the whole point of Connect the Dots.

We don’t need to label the girl.

We need to see the picture.

The cycle might be one dot.

Sleep might be another.

Food.

School.

Friendships.

Stress.

What happened yesterday.

What happened six months ago.


And sometimes what Mum is carrying too.

Because behaviour is never just about one thing.

If you work with girls, what patterns have you noticed become visible when you stop relying on memory and actually write things down?

I’d genuinely love to hear what you see.
