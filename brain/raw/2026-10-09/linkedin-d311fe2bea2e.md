The one leadership skill AI will never have is the one we've been told to ignore.

Everyone's talking about AI taking over our jobs. But AI can't walk into a room and feel that something's off before anyone says a word.

So many of us women have that gut feeling. We just know things. We sense them. And for years we've been told to ignore it and stick to the data. 📊

I've done it myself more times than I can count. Pushed the feeling down, went with the spreadsheet, and then a few weeks later thought, bloody hell, I knew it.
I actually think it's one of the most important leadership skills we've got. 

Especially now.

But here's the catch. You can't hear your gut when you're stressed out of your mind. All that noise just drowns it out.

So if you want to lead in a world full of AI, start with the basics. Learn to calm yourself down enough to actually hear what you're thinking. 🌱
