There’s a sentence I hear that usually means we’re about to find something interesting.

“We just do that manually.”

Okay...

How often?
Who does it?
How long does it take?
What happens if they forget?

Quite often, something everyone assumed was a small job turns out to happen hundreds of times across the year.

That’s why I like digging into how businesses actually work day to day.

The irritating little jobs are often where the biggest improvements are hiding.
