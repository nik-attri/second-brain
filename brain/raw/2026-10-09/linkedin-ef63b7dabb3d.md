130 cold emails. Zero replies. The problem wasn't the copy. It was the audience.

We were pitching "AI automation" to every fintech founder we could find. 130 sends. 0 replies.

Then we read their job postings.

The ones hiring "Financial Crime Ops Manager" and "KYB Analyst" — those are the teams drowning in manual ops. The ones hiring "Growth Lead" don't have the pain yet.

So we stopped casting wide. Started building settlement-break triage demos for the 5 companies whose job posts screamed ops pain.

That's when the work changed from "please reply" to "here's your workflow, automated."

Day 30. Still zero replies. But the outreach finally sounds like someone who read their job description.

If you're hiring for an ops role right now, what's the one task you'd hand to AI first?
