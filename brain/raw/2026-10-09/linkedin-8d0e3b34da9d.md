🏆 LADIES AND GENTLEMEN… WE HAVE A WINNER! 🏆

You'll recall that around six weeks ago, we announced that our wonderful Finance Director, Stephen Horrocks, had been nominated for the Midlands Finance Director Award.

Well, after weeks of banter, a few jokes at his expense and no doubt the odd prediction or two, the big night finally arrived!

The suit had been taken in, the injured knee brace was firmly in place, and off Steve went to the awards alongside his fellow directors.

The various honours were announced, his category came around, and then… STEVE HORROCKS!

The cheers went up, the fans went wild, and our very own Finance Director hobbled up to collect his award. The applause was deafening, the smiles were huge, and for once, the finance department had something to celebrate that didn't involve a spreadsheet! 👏😂

And if that wasn't enough, yesterday was also Steve's birthday, making this quite possibly the best birthday present he could have wished for. Although we're sure he'll find a way to put a monetary value on it!

Jokes aside, this is a fantastic achievement and one to be genuinely proud of.

These awards recognise the talent, dedication and professionalism of people who often work behind the scenes. Here in the Midlands, where hard work, integrity and teamwork remain at the heart of so many successful businesses, that recognition means a great deal.

As we said in our original nomination post, being nominated by fellow professionals is a real testament to the respect you've earned. Winning makes it even more special.

So, from everyone at J C Payne (UK) Ltd a massive congratulations to Steve. Thoroughly deserved, and we're all incredibly proud of you! 🏆👏

And finally… before this gets any more emotional, Steve, we haven't forgotten about the samosas, bhajis and paneer rolls to celebrate your "day of birth" 🥘

A day late perhaps, but surely an award-winning performance deserves an award-winning birthday spread?

Congratulations, Steve! Now, about that knee… we need you back on your feet for the next celebration as does the paddle court! 😂
