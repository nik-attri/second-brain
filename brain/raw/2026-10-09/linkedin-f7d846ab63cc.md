We built Molton Brown, a bilingual WooCommerce system for the UAE and Saudi Arabia. But it wasn't the website that mattered here. It was what happened when an order came in.

Molton Brown needed to test new markets. Fast. Without building the whole operation from scratch. But the tricky bit was that orders were coming in from multiple storefronts. All needing to go to different warehouses. Different courier services in each country. Stock moving around.

They could have managed that manually, or we could build something proper.

We built them a Hub. Basically, when an order arrived from any store, the system routed it to the correct warehouse. Warehouse staff picked, packed and marked it ready. The Hub automatically called the right courier. Stock updated in real time across both territories.

And we didn't do it from behind a screen. We attended meetings with their team in Dubai to make sure the warehouses were happy with the solution. We knew a system would only work if the people using it every day were on board.

The websites ran in English and Arabic. Proper right-to-left design, not a hack. They handled White Friday and peak season without breaking. That said something about how they were built.

But the real win was this: Molton Brown was able to trial a market on WooCommerce and see if the demand was there, without betting the farm on day one.

That's what we do. Not templates. Not another store that looks like every competitor's store. The judgment to see what your business actually needs. Then build it.
