I don’t like fanatics! I don’t like fanatics of any type. Religious fanatics, vegan/vegetarian/keto fanatics, sports fanatics, apple vs android fanatics, windows vs linux fanatics, car fanatics, right wing fanatics, left wing fanatics, MAGA fanatics, Antifa fanatics, anti abortion fanatics, flat earthers, round earthers (just kidding the Earth is round), the list goes on and on. And the only fanatics that bothers me in my day to day life are Agile fanatics. I don’t believe the only way to get to heaven is using Scrum, and no, using Kanban boards does not make you a saint. Waterfall does not automatically mean you go to hell. 

So, these fanatics comes in many shapes and forms but most common in my opinion is the dogmatic Scrum Master. Some examples: My first standup in a new team. The scrum master yells at a team member. “Do not lean against the table, this is a standup.” . 

On different team I was told. “No, you cannot come to the sprint retrospectives, you are a project manager, project managers do not get to go to sprint retrospectives.”. 

Another one. “All our requirements are kept in index cards because that is how you do it in agile”. I say “How many cards do you have”. Response “1200 or so in those three boxes over there”. I say “And it is not a good idea to at least index them in spreadsheet or something?”. Response “No, we are doing agile and index cards it is”. 

This is from when I was working for a software vendor. In sprint planning I asked the following. “The customer has asked for a change request. Can you please estimate it so we can get the formal paperwork done.”. Response was; “Sure, it is 4 story points.” My response to that was; “We do not charge the customer in story points, we charge them in man-days, can I please have a work estimate.”. Response – “No this is Scrum we estimate in Story Points”.  

Agile is about efficiency and adaptability and I am sorry to say in many places where I worked that is not the case. I have seen great example were it worked wonders too. So, this issue is not everywhere but in many places it is. I call it Inflexible Agile for the times when there isn’t a lot of flexibility in the agile but rather a lot of dogma.  What do you think? What is your experience?
