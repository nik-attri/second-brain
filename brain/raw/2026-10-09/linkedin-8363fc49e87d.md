What’s the value of urban design? 

Working from home yesterday and I walked by my wife who's on a call with her team going through upcoming workshops. Christy’s team is a collection of contractors organized around a AEC small biz that does big things, all women, many moms, mostly of MS + HS kids- kids or not all veterans of AEC and they’ve all chosen to work together. 

It’d be easy to make lazy assumptions about feminine energy or support network or shared experiences but you'd be wrong. What they all share in common is they dngaf what you think of them. Not a political statement or social stand, they just don’t care what you think. Dues paid long ago, not even curious. 

There’s a semi-famous RTKL alumni story of the time Christy losing patience with a kind of uniquely 90s shoulder pad jr architect methodically & without a trace of anger verbally dismantling him in the middle of the studio to the point of tears (the 90’s were hella Stranger Things different). Maybe when I knew I was in love & that I was going to have to raise my game a x% multiplier. 

Christy and I both worked in the same planning + urban design team in RTKL London for a great boss + mentor who is still a friend. Mahmood wouldn’t appreciate me highlighting him here because he’s built a highly successful P+UD practice off a intentional + accidental anti-marketing strategy of taking projects off the big firms without them ever realizing they’ve lost the job. At peak our RTKL P+UD team was 15 people working across the UK, Europe, Middle East, Russia + India, later China. 

For those of you unfamiliar, RTKL was a peer of Gensler, HOK etc back in the day. Sold to engineering group Arcadis, laterally bought Callison, rebranded under Arcadis after grafting like a half-dozen PM firms & now all being packaged up for sale again. Getting into some serious show me you face before your father was born shit here. 

In all those corporate moves and some years after Christy & I left the team, some inherited Managing Director entirely focused on cost + disconnected from value looked at a spreadsheet and saw P+UD as the place to find savings. Pretty much overnight Mahmood & a dozen urban designers + planners were let go. 

What did all those master planning and urban design clients do? They called Mahmood…and he called the team….and they did the work. 15-years later and this bespoke team is competing for massive projects based on trust and relationships & what’s left of RTKL is being sold as junk metal on a weight-basis. 

I apologize if this story seems a bit disparate. It’s actually only intended for an audience of one person. And I hope she reads it and it offers some understanding + maybe a few breadcrumbs, but mostly I very much hope she dngaf what I think. 

So..what’s the value of urban design? A hell of a lot more than the cost.
