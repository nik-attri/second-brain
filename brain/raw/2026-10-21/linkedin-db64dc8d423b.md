NIGERIANS ARE USING AI TO CREATE CONTENT, BUT HOW MANY ARE USING IT TO CREATE SOLUTIONS?

Let me rant small. 😂

Nigerians have discovered AI, and honestly, the creativity is impressive. Everybody is generating cinematic videos, talking babies, AI versions of themselves, impossible luxury lifestyles, talking animals, and images that look like they cost millions to produce.

Our people are talented. No argument.

But I have one question: After creating the beautiful video, what problem have you solved?

We are busy asking AI to make us look rich, but are we asking it to help us build wealth?

We are creating images of futuristic cities, but are we using AI to improve the businesses operating in the cities we already have?

We are generating videos of people speaking perfect English, but are we using AI to preserve our indigenous languages before some of them disappear?

We are making AI influencers, but are we using AI to help real businesses find customers, manage their operations, reduce waste, and make better decisions?

We are creating content about AI, but are we learning how to build systems that use AI to do actual work?

Because AI is much bigger than image generation and video creation.

AI can help a school identify patterns in unpaid fees. It can help a small business analyse customer complaints. It can help a logistics company organise operations. It can help a researcher process information that would take weeks to review manually. It can help an entrepreneur turn scattered business records into useful intelligence.

And yes, it can help you create stunning videos, too. There is absolutely nothing wrong with that.

The problem begins when we confuse what AI can show us with what AI can help us accomplish.

A viral video is not automatically a business model. A beautiful image is not operational efficiency. A million views do not necessarily mean a million naira. And knowing which prompt generates a talking goat does not mean you understand how to integrate AI into a business.

We need more Nigerians moving beyond the spectacle.

Learn automation. Learn data analysis. Learn workflow design. Learn how to connect AI to real business processes. Learn how to evaluate its output, protect sensitive information, and measure whether it actually improves anything.

And if you are a creative, go deeper. Build a service around your skills. Help businesses communicate better. Develop a repeatable production system. Turn creativity into something people are willing to pay for.

The real opportunity is not merely to use AI to create something impressive. It is to use AI to make something work better.

At S.E. Global, this is the distinction that matters to me: AI should not just make us look futuristic. It should help us solve problems in the present.

Diagnose the problem. Redesign the process. Implement the solution. Verify the result.
