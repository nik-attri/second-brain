You cannot program loyalty. In hospitality, it has to be earned by something, and the something was never the points.

Watch what this industry does when the returning guest stops returning: it builds a program. Tiers, member rates, early check-in, the birthday upgrade. Hell it even creates the most expensive hospitality esque lifestyle private club. Structure where meaning should be. Ten plus years of watching these launches has taught me how they age: strong first year, softening second, and by the third the team is adding perks to slow the churn. Hospitality is the only industry that calls a discount a relationship. A membership without a position is a discount schedule with dues. Even if you shovel a bunch of cash behind it.

Here is the distinction that decides whether a program compounds or decays. The guest who returns for the points will leave for points, loyal to the economics, and the economics are for sale from any property with a spreadsheet. The devoted patron returns for what the house means: they plan the anniversary around it, bring the people they love to it, never open the comparison tab. Your system recognizes their member number. Devotion is when they feel the house recognizes them, and that feeling was never built by a database. It was built by a decided brand.

Run the one-question audit: if the perks disappeared tomorrow, who still books? That number is your actual membership. Everyone else is a rate shopper with a card. The memberships people wait years to join are never the richest, they're the clearest. Built on a position so decided that belonging says something about the guest. The program didn't create that. The position did; the program just gave it a door.

Decide what the house means and the membership writes itself. Skip the deciding and no benefit schedule is generous enough, you're renting a feeling the position was supposed to own.

Programs buy repetition. Positions earn devotion.

If your members would leave with the perks, comment REEL because you don't have members. Side note, check what your renewals are actually "renewing".
