[AI Agents amp; Security: What we excel at, and what we are still learning]

Following up on yesterday’s post about our Email Quotation AI Agent, I want to touch on the biggest hurdle in moving AI automation from a simple PoC to a production-ready B2B tool: Security.

When building backend pipelines with Python and FastAPI, and connecting data via PostgreSQL, we inevitably face challenges like RBAC, data leakage prevention, and defending against prompt injection attacks.

To be completely transparent: while we have strong expertise in designing business logic and integrating AI to automate workflows, deep enterprise-grade security is an area where we consider ourselves continuous learners. Rather than claiming perfection, we are humbly studying and evolving our architecture every day to meet strict security standards.

So, where do we deliver the most undeniable value? In our core development execution.

From robust React and Next.js frontends to solid backend architectures and AI automation integration, we are a hands-on technical team. If your company is facing a shortage of development resources or needs a reliable outsourcing partner to execute swiftly, we are ready to step in as your extended engineering team.

We will keep refining our security practices. For everything else that requires fast, reliable software development—let’s talk.
