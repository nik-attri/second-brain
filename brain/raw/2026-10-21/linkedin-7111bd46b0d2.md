AI IS NOT THE WHOLE STRATEGY. 

If AI is the only thing making you work smarter are you really working smarter?

AI is here. 
Its ability to help us work more efficiently is undeniable.

However, I've been thinking: beyond AI, what skills, habits and systems are we bringing to the table?
I know, I know😂 We have beef with this “what are you bringing to the table?” question. Ladies, relax 🤣!!! I'm talking about productivity here.

Yesterday I had a conversation with a prospect and something they said stuck with me:
“We are the first intelligence. Artificial intelligence cannot exist without learning from us.”

That hit the nail on the head.

AI learns from human intelligence. 
It can help us process information, generate ideas and automate repetitive tasks.

But we still need to know what questions to ask, how to evaluate its output and when to trust our own judgment.

So what else can we do to work smarter? 

Here are six things I'm learning to combine with AI:

1. Build systems not just to-do lists.
-A good system helps you know what needs to be done, when it needs to happen and how to do it consistently. 
-Think SOPs, checklists and repeatable workflows.

2. Prioritise before you start working.
-Not every task deserves your attention right now. 
-Identify what's urgent, what's important and what can wait. 
-AI can help you organise your priorities but you need to understand your goals.

3. Master your tools.
-You don't need twenty productivity apps. 
-Learn to use the tools you already have: keyboard shortcuts, calendar features, email filters, templates and task-management systems.

4. Protect your focus.
-Batch similar tasks together. 
-Set aside time for deep work. 
-Turn off unnecessary notifications.
-Sometimes the smartest thing you can do is stop switching between ten different tasks.

5. Delegate and automate strategically.
-You don't have to do everything yourself.
- Automate repetitive, predictable tasks. 
-Delegate where appropriate. 
-Keep human judgment involved in decisions that require context, empathy or accountability.

6. Keep learning and sharpening your skills.
-AI can give you an answer but your expertise helps you recognise whether that answer is useful, accurate and appropriate.

The real advantage isn't simply knowing how to use AI.

It's knowing how to combine AI with human intelligence, practical skills and effective systems to get better results.

Because the goal isn't to use AI for everything.

The goal is to make our work easier, our decisions better and our time more valuable.

Now I'm curious: 
What's ONE thing you do to work smarter that has nothing to do with AI?
