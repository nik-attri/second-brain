Most AI agency founders are doing it wrong. They spend weeks trying to build a fancy interface or a complex custom model. That is a trap. Clients do not pay for AI. They pay for results. If you want to start an agency with zero dollars, stop looking at the tech and start looking at the paperwork. Find a niche industry with high document volume. Law firms and logistics companies are drowning in manual data entry. Use no code tools to automate their intake process. You do not need a developer salary to start. You just need to understand how to connect an API to a spreadsheet. The barrier to entry is not money. It is the ability to map a business process accurately. If you can fix one broken workflow, you have a business. Are you building tools or are you solving problems? #AI #Automation #TechTips #AgencyLife
