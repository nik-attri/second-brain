For Multi-Family Offices, co-investments are a key value proposition.

They allow families to access larger deals and reduce fee layers by investing alongside the office's primary fund or other clients.

But operationally, co-investments are a nightmare for standard PMS platforms.
A single private equity deal might be held by five different family trusts, each with a different entry date, capital commitment, and carried interest structure.

Most off-the-shelf systems force you to treat this as five separate manual positions or, worse, keep it entirely outside the system in a shadow ledger.

This creates a disconnect:
 • Performance reporting for the co-investment doesn't match the family's main dashboard
 • Fee calculations for carried interest become a manual quarterly scramble
 • Capital calls and distributions must be allocated manually across multiple entities

When co-investments remain siloed, they stop being a strategic advantage and start becoming an operational liability.
The solution is to build a flexible co-investment module that sits on top of your core data layer.
This module tracks the master deal and automatically allocates P&L, fees, and cash flows back to each participating family entity based on their specific terms.

This ensures that every family sees their true net exposure without your ops team drowning in allocation spreadsheets.
