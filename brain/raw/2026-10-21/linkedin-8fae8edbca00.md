Data entry should never be a full-time job.

Recently, a client came to us drowning in repetitive manual work. Their team was spending countless hours hand-copying every single new record from Microsoft Dynamics 365 and duplicating it onto their WordPress website.

Not only was this incredibly tedious, but it also led to inevitable copy-paste errors and a website that was always lagging a few days behind their actual CRM data.

We stepped in and eliminated the manual work entirely by building a custom API bridge between Dynamics 365 and WordPress.

Here is what we implemented:

- Complete Automation: We mapped their Dynamics 365 data fields directly to custom post types in WordPress, allowing the systems to talk to each other seamlessly.

- Customizable Sync Schedules: Instead of a rigid sync, we built a settings dashboard where the client can define exactly how often the data updates—whether that is every 15 minutes, hourly, or daily.

- Error Handling & Logging: If a record is missing a field in the CRM, the system flags it in a log rather than breaking the website layout, ensuring 100% uptime and clean data.

The result?
What used to take hours of mind-numbing administrative work each week now happens completely in the background. The client’s website is always up-to-date, and their team is finally free to focus on actual business growth instead of data entry.

Your website and your CRM should work as one unified system.
