What kind of companies does Vendep invest in?

For the past few weeks I've been fascinated by the problems our portfolio companies are solving, and I've come to think "B2B SaaS" needs a rebrand. Many people picture it as yet another semi-useful tool that semi-optimises a desktop workflow. That couldn’t be more wrong.

With AI, the problems you can solve today are mind-blowingly complex, and yet somehow so very obvious.

Take Complir, a Danish company building the AI infrastructure for product compliance, and one of our latest investments.

In the early days, the Complir team spent time inside Flying Tiger Copenhagen's HQ, learning how the retailer onboarded hundreds of new products every month across 44 different countries. The thing is, before a single toy, candle or lip gloss can go on a shelf, someone has to check it against each country's rules. Is the label in the right language? Has the right safety test been done? Is the right warning printed on it? Thousands of checks every month, mostly done by hand.

The result? Complir built a product that automates those checks, got into Y Combinator and, in under a year from our pre-seed, raised an $11M seed round from General Catalyst🔥

Impressive work, Gustav Bang, Tine Kühnel and Marc Kaa Brejner!! Proud to get to follow your journey💫

So, what kind of companies do we invest in? Ones that take the dull, hidden, painfully complex work inside a business and make it disappear.
