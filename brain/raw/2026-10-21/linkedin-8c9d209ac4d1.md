"You know, with all the things you can do for a living... and all the people you can do it with... to get to do something that you love, with people that you love... yeah, you won the lottery."

We just finished watching The Bear. In one of the last episodes, Tina is trying to describe to her husband what it's like to work in that kitchen with her team. She's exhausted but elated, and she's struggling to explain it to someone who exists outside that intense world, knowing he won't understand how deeply she feels it. The show's writing is always beautiful, but that conversation stopped me in my tracks, because it also sums up my industry perfectly.

Two and a half years. More than 15 programs ranging from 500 - 16,000 people. Thousands of miles traveled and countless hours spent planning. So. Many. Spreadsheets. So. Many. Decks. But, in the end, the numbers don’t matter.

All I will remember are the people. My brilliant, strategic, hardworking colleagues. It has been a pleasure to work alongside these high-functioning, passionate, Type A planners and I will miss them every day. 

I will share my next adventure shortly, but, today I am celebrating my time at EEG. 

It was a hell of a good time.
