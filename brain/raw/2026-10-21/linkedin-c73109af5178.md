Tesla just published its 2026 annual report. Then I had my finance AI agent read through it (a 204-page PDF) and explain what I need to know about it. A few minutes later, he had written himself a new skill.

Actually, I did not ask for that. I only wanted to know what it was about and what I should do with it.

The agent is "Shiro" (named after my cat). He lives in my homelab along with the other Hermes agents, and I talk to him through Telegram. I didn't set up a specific system at first. I just sent him screenshots from my investment apps and talked to him, and he updated my portfolio, set up a process behind the scenes, and checked the numbers against actual asset prices in real time. Every day at 09:30, he sends me my portfolio position automatically. So I don't need to manually check my spreadsheets or investment apps anymore.

He also gave me a public page that shows my portfolio allocation. You can access it here: portfolio.arnan.to

Here’s the honest part:
This is not zero setup. I run the agent myself at home, with my internet and electricity running 24/7, plus my monthly credit for the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model (~$20/month, covering all my use cases, not just this finance agent).

But you don't need to do the same thing. In September, Meta launched Muse and OpenAI launched dots. Both are personal agents that run in the background, and you can use them as a service without worrying about my kind of setup (unless you’re moving towards sovereignty)✌

Given this new way of interacting with a system, I think the future of interaction between humans and agents will be as simple as chat or voice, where we don't need to know how the system works. When in doubt, you can just ask "them" to explain it to you, either in the simplest terms or with visualizations. 

You can start with one agent, one task, and a few screenshots.

PS: this is not financial advice 😶‍🌫️
