OpenAI acquiring Orchestra Bio and folding it into Rosalind is a signal for anyone in peptide sourcing. When AI starts managing CDMO coordination, batch tracking and clinical supply timelines, the bar for suppliers shifts. Buyers won't just ask 'can you supply?' — their systems will ask 'can your data plug into our workflow?' That means structured COAs, batch-specific impurity profiles, and lead times that can be verified, not just promised. For peptide ingredient suppliers, the moat isn't AI. It's real wet-lab data and consistent manufacturing. The more AI automates discovery, the more valuable physical reliability becomes. 😎
