YOUR PROJECT MANAGER SHOULDN'T HAVE TO BE A HUMAN JIRA API. 🤖

Updating tickets. Refining backlogs. Searching through repositories. Checking documentation. Preparing releases.

How much time does your team spend connecting the dots between tasks, code and project decisions?

At Mobile Reality, we built Compass, our own Jira AI agent designed to take the busywork out of backlog refinement.

Here's what it can do:
🔍 Search Jira tasks, comments, documentation and code repositories.
🧠 Remember project conventions, technical decisions and ownership rules.
⚡ Draft tasks, update descriptions, change labels and statuses, and prepare releases.
🤝 Turn bulk requests into ready to review proposals, so teams can approve changes instead of editing dozens of tickets manually.

And here's the important part: Compass never changes Jira without human approval. Every proposed update must be reviewed and explicitly applied by a team member.

Building a useful AI agent isn't just about connecting an LLM to an API. It's about giving it the right context, tools, memory and guardrails.

We break down the architecture behind Compass, the technologies we chose, what didn't work in our first approach and the engineering lessons we learned along the way.
