Position Summary
The Senior Functional Tester will be responsible for end-to-end testing for ADP WorkForce
Suite within the Global Time Entry program. This role validates time capture, evaluation,
approvals, labor allocation, payroll outputs, security, reporting, and integrations with SAP
S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, payroll platforms, and other enterprise applications while
partnering with business, product, development, integration, and global QA teams.
Key Responsibilities
 Translate business, functional, integration, architecture, and data requirements into
risk-based test strategies, plans, scenarios, test cases, data, and traceability.
 Responsible for QA estimation, scheduling, execution, defect management, status
reporting, risk escalation, and quality sign-off readiness.
 Validate ADP WFS processes for time entry and evaluation, approvals, absences,
quotas, wage and premium calculations, labor distribution, cost allocation, and
payroll outputs.
 Plan and execute component, integration, end-to-end, regression, smoke, security,
role-based, and user acceptance testing.
 Validate interfaces and reconcile data across ADP WFS, SAP S/4HANA or ECC,
SuccessFactors, payroll platforms, Databricks, and connected time-entry
applications.
 Verify employee and workforce data, worked time, absences, approvals, wage types,
payroll results, cost allocations, and related SAP objects.
 Manage Azure DevOps defects, facilitate triage and root-cause analysis, verify fixes,
and communicate risks and dependencies.
 Maintain reusable regression assets and coordinate UAT readiness, test data,
execution support, evidence, defect closure, and business sign-off.
 Follow QA COE standards and collaborate with automation and performance teams
to expand continuous-testing coverage.
Required Qualifications
 Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a
related STEM field.
 8+ years of software Testing experience across component, integration, end-to-end,
regression, API, and data testing.
