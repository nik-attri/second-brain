So... I'm one month in to my time at SetMenu and I wanted to give you all a little update of what we've been getting up to! 

Mildreds kicked off their first week with a bang: 32 group bookings, 245 covers, and £12,000 in revenue processed, all without a single manual spreadsheet in sight. 

Welcomed Artezzan Restaurants Ltd: Integrating our tech directly with OpenTable to help their teams streamline pre-orders and cut down on back of house admin. 

Gusto Italian came to the table: Helping their teams bring pre-orders and allergens into one unified workspace before guests even sit down.

Having been in the sales and events chaos of pre-order back and forth, SetMenu has taught me this: group bookings don't need to take over your admin time! When your tech works together, your teams spend less time drowning in admin and more time doing what they do best, delivering brilliant hospitality!

Massive thank you to our incredible partners and the amazing SetMenu team for such a warm welcome. We're just getting started!
