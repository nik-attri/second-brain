Your business may be unique. But is every step in it unique?
Even highly specialized companies repeat certain actions: collecting details, clarifying needs, checking availability, and notifying the right person.

You don’t have to automate the entire workflow. A bot can handle standardized steps while a specialist keeps the expert work.
For example, the bot collects initial information according to your rules, and a professional evaluates the situation and recommends a solution.
The boundary between standard and non-standard work shows where automation is genuinely useful.
Instead of asking, “Can we automate the whole business?”, ask: “Which part of our workflow repeats often enough to be worth automating?”
