WE'RE NOT TOO OLD. WE'RE JUST GETTING INTERESTING.

Something pretty wonderful is happening with STORMEE. And surprisingly, it's not all about ginger.
At 51, after 20+ years building FMCG brands, I found myself back at the kitchen bench creating something I couldn't find in the market.
I'd stopped drinking alcohol. Not because I'd stopped enjoying a good time, but because I didn't want to sacrifice tomorrow for tonight, and love feeling so much better.
I still wanted the beautiful glass, the 5pm ritual, the complexity of wine.
Instead, I was being offered lemonade or soda.
Seriously?
It made me wonder whether an entire generation of women had been overlooked.
Women in their 40s, 50s and beyond. Active, adventurous, with spending power, strong opinions and changing priorities.
We're not trying to be 25 again. We're not giving up on having fun. And we're certainly not ready to disappear quietly into the background.
So I created STORMEE.
Fierce ginger. Tart, not sweet. A proper grown-up drink.
Our first batch sold out in 14 days.
But the biggest surprise hasn't been the sales. It's been the people.
And especially the women. Bloody hell, the women!
Buying bottles for friends. Sharing posts. Making introductions. Opening doors. Offering advice. Cheering on another woman having a crack.
Some I've known for years. Others I've met for five minutes while pouring drinks at a market.
And a special mention to the men and husbands who've discovered STORMEE makes a rather excellent addition to tequila and whisky. Turns out fierce ginger has a few fans on both sides of the bar!
After two decades of business plans, budgets and negotiations, I've been reminded of something wonderfully simple.
People build brands. Not spreadsheets.
Getting out there, having conversations, listening to what customers actually want. Discovering the unexpected ways they're enjoying STORMEE.
And realising that sometimes the people you set out to serve become the very people helping you succeed.
Of course, selling out is exciting. But building something people genuinely connect with, share and come back for? That's where the real value lies.
I started STORMEE because I was tired of being overlooked as a consumer.
What I didn't expect was to feel so bloody supported as a founder.
To every woman who's bought a bottle, shared a post, made an introduction or simply encouraged me along the way, thank you.
And to the men who've embraced a fierce ginger mixer, you're very welcome at the party too!
We're not slowing down. We're just getting started.
And this next chapter is looking rather interesting.
