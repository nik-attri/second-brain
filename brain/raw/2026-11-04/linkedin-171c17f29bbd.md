I've worked with Wayne Wu at nutpods, so being on the Shane White Show following an episode titled “The GOAT of CPG Investing” is a hell of a setup. Appreciate you keeping the bar reasonable, Shane  😂 

Episode 280 of The Shane White Show is out: The Software Amazon Operators Need. We recorded it a couple weeks after Rainsight launched to the public, so you get the origin story while it was still raw.

The short version of that story: I’ve been selling on Amazon since 2011 and I spent years assuming everyone else had a tool for the morning I kept living. Pull the reports, compare two periods, notice one had an extra Saturday, start over. Jump into a meeting, lose your place, start over again. I asked every operator I knew what they used. The answer was always some version of a spreadsheet.

(For a while my pitch was “a Bloomberg terminal for your Amazon business.” That lands great with people who know what a Bloomberg terminal is. That is not most people. Shane got it, which is probably why we’re friends.)

Shane and Kyle run NoBul Partners LLC, a kickass operator-focused Amazon agency for CPG brands, so the conversation kept crossing between the brand side and the agency side, and I think it’s better for it.

One thing from the episode that matters more in November than it did in August. We recorded when Q4 was still a plan. The planning is done now, for better or worse, so the job this month is noticing what moved while it’s still cheap to do something about. Nobody autopsies the winners. Conversion creeping up on a product that hasn’t come up in a meeting all quarter. Conversion sliding on one that was fine in October. A buy box you’ve been in and out of that’s quietly trending the wrong way. None of those show up in the topline until it’s too late to matter, and in Q4 “too late” is about four days.

So this week: pull the last two weeks against the trailing twelve, by child ASIN, sessions and conversion. Anything that moved more than you’d expect goes on a list, and the list gets a question: what does this need right now?

That pull is the whole reason Rainsight exists. It reads your catalog overnight, every ASIN against its own baseline, and hands you that list in the morning with the why attached. It won’t fix it for you but it will tell you where to look, in a few minutes, every day. If you’d rather see it on your own catalog than listen to me describe it for an hour, that’s the faster route.

Thanks to Shane for having me on, and to Kyle and the NoBul Partners crew for making the show happen. This one was fun. Can’t wait to come on again and get even more in the weeds on finding alpha in your Amazon business.
