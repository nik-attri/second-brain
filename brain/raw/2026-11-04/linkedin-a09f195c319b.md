How much billable time does your firm leave on the table? 💸
Every lawyer knows the month-end feeling: a call between two meetings, a quick email, a document reviewed on the train. If it isn’t captured in the moment, part of it is gone. Nobody notices, but the firm pays for it. And with manual timers and paper notes, that gap is the norm.
Two firms, two continents, one lesson:
🔹 Oziel Law (Toronto): After switching to WiseTime® by Anaqua, the firm reports up to 10% more billable hours and less admin work. Managing Partner Allan Oziel: “If each timekeeper captures even one extra billable hour per week, it more than pays for itself.”
🔹 Spruson & Ferguson (Asia-Pacific): In a two-week pilot with a small group, attorneys ran WiseTime alongside their usual timekeeping. WiseTime captured more than double the work time they had accounted for manually. It also cut timekeeping admin by about one hour per week on average. The firm also captured fixed-fee work that had not been recorded before.
A fair note on the numbers: the Spruson & Ferguson figures show hours of time and attention captured against matters, not necessarily billed hours. But the message is clear. What isn’t recorded can’t be invoiced or analysed.
That is also why reporting matters. Oziel Law asked for consolidated reports from several systems on one platform. Anaqua responded with WiseTime Enterprise Data Connect, which makes posted time data available in the reporting tool of your choice via Google BigQuery.
