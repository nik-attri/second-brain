🚀 A Journey of Growth, Leadership and Teamwork – Kolkata | YesMadam

The Durga Puja season of 2026 has been a significant milestone for Kolkata Operations at YesMadam. 🎉

Comparing this season with the previous year, we have achieved 218% growth in completed bookings and expanded our expert network by 282%. 📈

🌟 Our growth journey throughout 2026 has also been encouraging:
- 📊 43.2% quarterly growth in Q2 compared to Q1.
- 📊 21.5% quarterly growth in Q3 compared to Q2.
- 📈 15.2% average month-on-month growth in completed bookings from February to October.
- 🔥 54% growth in October 2026 compared to September 2026.

Alongside business expansion, we have continued strengthening operational efficiency, service delivery and expert availability.

As the City Manager – Kolkata, it has been a rewarding experience to lead this journey, work closely with different teams and contribute towards building a stronger and more scalable operation. 🤝

This achievement would not have been possible without the consistent efforts and collaboration of our Operations, Hiring, Training and Product logistics teams. I sincerely appreciate everyone's dedication and contribution. 👏

I would also like to extend my special thanks to Dan Singh Jasaawat and Deepak Jha for trusting me and giving me the opportunity to be a part of YesMadam. Their guidance, support and confidence have played an important role in my professional journey.

Proud of what the Kolkata team has achieved together. 💪 Looking forward to taking on bigger challenges, creating more milestones and achieving greater heights. 🏆

Growth is a continuous journey, and we are just getting started! 🚀
