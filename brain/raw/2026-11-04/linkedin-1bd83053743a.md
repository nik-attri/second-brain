The future of procurement isn’t just about what we do differently. It’s about how we think differently.

A privilege to be part of Asia Pacific Procurement Week, contributing to both the CPO Mentoring session and the CPO Vision Panel – Designing the Future.

Two very different conversations, but a common theme emerged: Procurement is at an inflection point, and we have a real opportunity to redefine our value.

A few reflections I walked away with:

🔹 From efficiency to impact. Procurement has spent decades perfecting processes, driving compliance and delivering savings. These remain important, but they cannot be the ceiling of our ambition. The real question is how we create greater enterprise value.

🔹 From incremental change to reinvention. With AI and agentic workflows, we have an opportunity to rethink our operating models from a clean slate. Not simply automate what we do today, but challenge why we do it that way in the first place.

🔹 From managing teams to developing future leaders. One of my biggest takeaways from the mentoring discussions was the importance of curiosity, courage and adaptability. As leaders, our responsibility isn’t just to navigate change, but to create the confidence and space for others to lead it.

My biggest reflection? The future of procurement will not be defined by technology alone. It will be defined by the boldness of our leadership and our willingness to let go of what has made us successful in the past.

Really enjoyed the candid exchanges, fresh perspectives and reconnecting with so many passionate procurement professionals. Thank you to the organisers, fellow panellists and mentoring participants for the energising conversations!

The future isn’t something we wait for. It’s something we have the privilege and responsibility to shape.
