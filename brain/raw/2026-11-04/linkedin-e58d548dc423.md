The ARC auditor doesn't start with your workshop.

He starts with a pen and a list. And in the first 10 minutes he'll ask for the same five records, every time:

1. Refrigerant handling licences for everyone who's touched gas
2. The charge specs for each unit — factory and current
3. Your gas ledger: in, out, recovered, disposed
4. Service history for every chiller and AC unit on the fleet
5. Records of any leak repairs and re-gassing

I've watched maintenance managers with spotless workshops scramble for a week because the gas ledger was three spreadsheets and a notebook.

The work was always done. The records just couldn't prove it.

If the auditor walked in tomorrow, which of the five would have you digging through drawers?
