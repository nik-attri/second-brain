🚀 WE ARE HIRING – INFRA DELIVERY LEAD
We are looking for an experienced Infrastructure Delivery Lead to manage end-to-end 24x7 infrastructure operations across multiple technology towers for global enterprise clients.
Position: Infra Delivery Lead
Experience: 12+ Years
Relevant Experience: 5+ Years in Infrastructure Delivery / Managed Services
Team Management: 20+ Engineers (Onshore & Offshore)
Work Model: 24x7 Infrastructure Operations
Locations: Gurgaon, Pune, Bangalore, Chennai & Hyderabad / NCR

Key Responsibilities
Own end-to-end delivery of L1.5 and L2 infrastructure operations.
Manage multiple technology towers including Hosting, Network, Telephony, DBA and Data Platforms.
Lead the Infrastructure Command Center and coordinate with tower leads.
Manage SLAs, KPIs, backlog, operational performance and governance reviews.
Lead P1/P2 Major Incident Management (MIM), client escalations, RCA and problem management.
Oversee infrastructure changes, patching, backup, disaster recovery and business continuity.
Manage client stakeholders, SOW deliverables, invoicing and contractual governance.
Lead resource planning, shift rosters, hiring, performance management and team development.
Drive automation, service improvements, operational efficiency and service transitions.

Required Technical Skills
Cloud: AWS (EC2, S3, ELB, CloudWatch, CloudTrail, IAM), Microsoft Azure.
Infrastructure: Windows Server, Linux/Unix, VMware.
Networking: Cisco Routers, Switches, WLAN Controllers, Firewalls, Load Balancers.
Telephony: Cisco Call Manager / PBX.
Database: Oracle, MS SQL Server, Cloud Databases.
Monitoring: Datadog, PagerDuty.
ITSM: ServiceNow (ITSM, CMDB, Reporting & Dashboards).
Data Platforms: Airflow, Talend, Snowflake, Tableau, Power BI.

Mandatory Experience
12+ years in IT Infrastructure Operations.
5+ years in Infrastructure Delivery / Managed Services leadership.
Strong experience managing 24x7 multi-tower infrastructure operations.
Experience leading 20+ engineers across onshore and offshore teams.
Strong exposure to SLA management, MIM, client escalations, invoicing and contract governance.
Experience overseeing Cloud, Windows/Linux, VMware, Networking, Database and Data Platform operations.
Excellent communication and senior stakeholder management skills.
Good to Have
ITIL 4 Foundation.
AWS Solutions Architect Associate / Microsoft AZ-104.
CCNA / CCNP / Oracle OCP.
Terraform, PowerShell, Bash or Python scripting.
Experience in service transition from an incumbent provider.
Tableau, Power BI, Excel, Jira and Confluence.
