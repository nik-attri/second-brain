At AAI Labs we built AI Scan. An AI agent that interviews employees at a company over a few weeks, one topic a day. Answers get pooled, de-identified, and synthesized into a report showing where AI can help and where it can't.

The interviewer is an agent called Jonas.

Jonas collects a defined set of evidence for each person: what they do, how often, what steps, where the friction is, what gets handed off. It asks one question at a time. When an answer is vague, it follows up. When someone mentions something unexpected, it logs that as a lead and comes back to it.

It asks for the next missing piece of evidence. Optional topics only surface when the participant opens them. Evidence survives across sessions and topic changes.

If someone says "I handle exceptions manually," Jonas does not move on. It asks what kind, how often, how long. If they say it's a one-off, it drops the lead. If they say every Monday, it keeps going.

We ran AI Scan at a manufacturing company. 9 of 10 people contacted took part. The report surfaced 41 recommendations across 6 business areas. The biggest time sink it found was workplace improvement and automation at 20 hours a week
