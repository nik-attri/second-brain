There was a moment when I understood that there is an API between all of Stativa systems. It’s expensive as hell and its name is Alex 😄

Ads spread between Meta campaigns. 
Bookings in Lodgify. 
Leads in ManyChat. 
Partners payouts - Spreadsheet
Inventory - other spreadsheet…
And of course something is just founders knowledge or somewhere in WhatsApp chats. 

Between all of these processes there’s something (someone) who connects it together. Me, myself and mine 🙃

Check inboxes, check the dates, calculate price, send to the lead. Answer questions, help with the reservation. 
Once a week - check which channel is performing better, update marketing report, calculate partners payouts…

These operations are not complicated by themselves, they take maximum 5 minutes, however it became chaotic on the scale of 8 houses. And, btw, I think it’s normal for the young business… 

However there was a moment when I understood, that we should dive into automations of Stativa operations. 

But where to start? Chaos is so big already! I started from writing down the structure:

- where do information come from 
- who has access to change it 
- who (or which system) is the source of truth
- what’s possible to automate with AI
- where do we still need people

Because if you are taking Zapier, Make and some AI slop… it won’t manage chaos, it will start to produce shit quicker 

And it was a moment when I start to automate everything. In the middle of the hot hotel season! 

In the next posts I will tell you more of what’s under the hood of Stativa, what was our path, mistakes and what’s ahead. And the most important… where AI was useful and where usual spreadsheet still beats all of the modern posh/glitchy SaaS startups 😄

Stay Stativa 🌿
