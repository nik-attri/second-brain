On 16 October, Willie Peters walks into Port Moresby as full-time Head Coach of the PNG Chiefs.  He joins a full year before our players walk into camp. 

On 1 November, the NRL player market opens.
Most clubs will treat those as two separate events. We don't.

Here's the uncomfortable truth about recruitment in this game: too many clubs let the market lead and the leaders follow. Player managers set the tempo. Coaches react. Boards sign off on what's left.

We're doing it the other way round.

Willie arrives 16 days early. He'll spend two weeks in PNG watching the Kumuls, learning the country, and seeing for himself what rugby league means to 12 million people before he sits across the table from a single player.
That's not a scheduling detail. That's a leadership decision.

Chammas and I don't want a coach who recruits from a spreadsheet. We want one who can look a player in the eye and tell them, from lived experience, what they're being asked to join.

Because here's the part some clubs won't like: on 1 November, they'll be defending. Protecting rosters, managing caps, keeping people happy. 

We'll be building something the game has never seen. A new club. A new market. A nation behind it. And an offer no other NRL team can put on the table.

The NRL has talked about PNG for years.
Leadership isn't talking. Leadership is turning up first.

From 16 October, watch what PNG does next.
