AI is actually good. How can you make it better?

Using standalone tools to write an email or summarize notes is fine, but we do not want to stop on fine.

The actual use of AI will only happen when it talks to your main systems and is part of an impactful workflow.

Let's understand the difference with an example :

Option A: Your sales rep opens a tool, types a prompt, and manually drafts a proposal.

Option B: A lead fills out a form on your site, the system cleans up the info, drafts the proposal automatically, and drops it in the appropriate rep’s inbox just needing a quick thumbs-up and the possibilities from here are endless.

Option A will save a few minutes, which isn't bad.

Option B changes how your company actually grows, uses AI and sets up the right direction for innovation.

If you are implementing AI, automation and agents, do it this way.
