The Feature Nobody Turned On

Sometimes the problem isn't that Odoo is missing a feature.
It's that the feature is already there, sitting unused.

A company was spending hours every week manually following up on routine tasks, updating records, and checking whether certain actions had been completed. The team assumed they needed more customization.

Before building anything new, we reviewed what Odoo was already capable of doing.

Several automated actions and workflow rules could handle most of the repetitive work. We configured them around the company's actual process instead of adding another custom module.

Real-life example

The sales team was manually updating follow-up activities for every new quotation.
With the right automated rules, those activities could be created automatically when specific conditions were met.

Less clicking. Fewer forgotten follow-ups. No unnecessary custom development.
Before asking, "What should we build?"
It's worth asking, "What can Odoo already do that we're not using?"
