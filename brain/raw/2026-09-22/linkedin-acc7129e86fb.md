Attended 𝗠𝗶𝗰𝗿𝗼𝘀𝗼𝗳𝘁'𝘀 𝗔𝗴𝗲𝗻𝘁-𝗮-𝘁𝗵𝗼𝗻 and honestly it was such a great experience!!! 🚀

Went through the whole Agent Explorer track and learnt so much about how AI agents actually work behind the scenes. Chat is great for one-off questions, but agents are built for the stuff that keeps repeating again and again. Think policy queries, onboarding questions, process guidance basically anything your team gets asked on loop.🔁 

Also learnt how important it is to give an agent a clear purpose and feed it trusted knowledge sources instead of just letting it guess. And testing/refining the agent's behaviour isn't a one time thing, it's an ongoing process to keep it reliable.

The final challenge was to actually spot a real repetitive problem in a work environment and design an agent around solving it. Just mapping that out on paper changed how I look at everyday work problems now. 📚 

In 2026, with businesses drowning in repetitive queries and manual processes, this is exactly the skill that's going to matter. Agents aren't just a buzzword, they're becoming the backbone of how teams scale support without scaling headcount. 💡

Thanks to Microsoft and Founderz for putting this together. Grateful I got to learn this early. 🙌
