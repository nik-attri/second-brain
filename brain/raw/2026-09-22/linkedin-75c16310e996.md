People spend 40 minutes building a single Google Form.
Question by question. Field by field. Manually setting "required" on every single one.
Then one of our mentors showed us something that changed how we work:
Describe the form to AI instead of building it.
Title, sections, questions, options, required fields — all in plain English.
AI turns that into a ready-to-run Google Apps Script.
Paste it into script.google.com. Hit Run. Authorize.
Your form exists. Fully built, fully structured, zero clicking through menus.
40 minutes → under 5.
No coding. Just describing clearly enough for AI to build it for you.
I've used this for feedback forms, surveys, quizzes, event registrations — anything I used to build by hand.
The real skill isn't JavaScript. It's learning to describe what you want.
Next repetitive task — don't ask "how do I do this manually?"
Ask: "Can AI do this for me?"
