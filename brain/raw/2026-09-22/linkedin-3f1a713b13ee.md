I automated my compliance checks with Akai. No coding. No config files.

Every time a regulation changes, I'd spend two days cross-referencing: read the requirement in Jira, identify what changed, test calculations, validate the system config. Four or five steps, all manual.

Here's what I did instead: I recorded my screen while explaining the steps I take. That's it.

Akai captured my voice, my screen, the logic. It built a gap analysis in seconds—flagged exactly which components need updates, caught the edge cases I would've missed manually.

Now my team runs this workflow for any compliance change. It learns. It gets faster. It gives us audit trails and visibility without anyone needing to write code or understand our system deeply.

18 hours of manual work gone. And I didn't rebuild how we operate to do it.
