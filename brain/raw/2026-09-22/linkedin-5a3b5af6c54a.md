5,000 faculty members. Collaborating via email attachments and shared drives. At a large research university where curriculum development, grant collaboration, and cross-departmental research are supposed to be core strengths.

That was the digital workplace reality for a US university when they came to us. Every department operating in its own silo. Research documents buried in shared drives that only half the team knew how to find. Curriculum updates sent as email chains with version-12 attachments. Accreditation reporting requiring weeks of manual data gathering from disconnected systems.

And for faculty, the friction isn't just inefficiency. It's time stolen from research, teaching, and student outcomes. Every hour spent hunting for the right document version or chasing email threads is an hour not spent on the work that actually matters.

The compliance layer made it more complex. FERPA student data privacy, accreditation reporting requirements, and Title IV financial aid compliance meant any collaboration platform needed to be fully governed and auditable from day one not bolted on later as an afterthought.

Previous vendors had tried and stalled. Not because SharePoint is hard — but because they arrived without higher education context and spent weeks learning terminology our team brought from the first workshop.

We deployed a fully configured SharePoint + Microsoft Teams collaboration platform department-specific sites, curriculum management workflows, resource sharing libraries, Power Automate automation for approvals and notifications, and Power BI dashboards for operational visibility designed specifically around how academic departments actually work.

The verified, measured results:
🎓 5,000+ faculty on one unified platform — verified
🏛️ Department collaboration fully unified — no more silos
📚 Curriculum management — end-to-end on SharePoint
⚡ Power Automate workflows — approvals & notifications automated
📊 Power BI dashboards for academic leadership visibility
✅ Full FERPA, accreditation & Title IV compliance maintained
🗓️ Delivered in 8–14 weeks — no 18-month intranet programme
🎯 80%+ faculty adoption within the first month

The biggest lesson? Technology that fits into how faculty already work gets adopted. Technology that forces new workflows gets abandoned. We designed every site, every workflow, and every library around existing academic processes not the other way around. That's what drove 80%+ adoption in a faculty population that is notoriously resistant to technology mandates.

For higher education CIOs, provosts, and technology leaders: if your faculty are still collaborating via email chains and shared drives, that's not a culture problem. It's a SharePoint and Teams opportunity deliverable this quarter.
