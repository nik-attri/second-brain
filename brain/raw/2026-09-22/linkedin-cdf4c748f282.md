Dogfooding Crismo internally and talking to our customers is the best way for me right now to wrap my head around AI adoption. 

So far, I’m seeing these six capabilities as key. And the best part: Process Management has a role in all six. In some, it’s core.

Strategic Focus → Connect strategic priorities to the processes and value streams where that value is created.

Operational Clarity → Create a shared view of processes, decisions, data, systems, roles and dependencies. Textbook BPM.

Workflow Redesign → Use the process model as the design artefact to reimagine it, decide what to eliminate, automate, augment, and define the human-AI collaboration model. BPM at its best.

AI Capability → Use Level 3 and Level 4 process models as the bridge between operational design and technology implementation. The classic BPM role of connecting business and IT, just in a new context.

Adoption → Make the new work mode explicit in processes, roles, responsibilities and decision rights. Give people a clear model of how their work changes with AI.

Impact Scaling → If you baseline the as-is process, redesign it around value, then re-measure it, the process change and the business case become one and the same. 
Now you have a system to scale: clarify → redesign → build → adopt → measure → repeat.

That last bit is probably the one I’m most excited about.
