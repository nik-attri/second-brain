One of the key sectors that Strategic Year closely looks into is Education, as we believe it remains both resilient and foundational to long-term economic development.

As AI reshapes how people learn and work, the skills people need and how they acquire them are changing as well. This is creating opportunities for education models that are more flexible, technology-enabled, and better aligned with the skills needed in an AI-driven world.

At Strategic Year, we are particularly interested in innovative education businesses that are adapting to these shifts and creating more value for learners and the workforce.

Our portfolio companies, Teky and Cakap, are examples of how this can take shape:

TEKY ACADEMY is integrating AI into STEM education, including a dedicated AI course for students aged 11–15. The programme goes beyond introducing AI as a concept, helping students understand how it works and develop practical skills for an increasingly digital world.

Cakap is using AI to make learning more personalized, with AI-powered learning paths and an intelligent student companion providing real-time support. Beyond education, Cakap is also developing AI-driven solutions that help businesses automate workflows and improve operational efficiency.

Different models, but a common theme: rethinking how education can evolve to prepare people for a rapidly changing world.
