Excel Hell is solvable. You do not need a data warehouse first.

Excel Hell is what happens when a single spreadsheet becomes the source of truth for a whole department, is copied, modified, and emailed in 11 different versions, and nobody is entirely sure which one is correct.

It is painful. It is also extremely common.

The mistake most organisations make when they try to escape it is assuming the solution has to be large. A data platform migration. A new ERP. A full data strategy project.

Often the first step is much smaller.

Map where the spreadsheet data comes from. Find the 1 or 2 fields that matter most for decisions. Build a single, agreed version of those fields, maintained in one place.

That is not a data warehouse. That is a defined source of truth for a specific question.

Once you have that, you can ask whether automation or AI adds value. Until you have it, you are building on sand.
