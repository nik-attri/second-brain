I automated my own job search and it taught me more about client automation than any pitch deck could.

Most job alert tools give you a firehose of postings that don't match what you actually need. 

So I built my own with Make.com:

→ 5 parallel searches across my specializations — AI automation/workflow engineering, automation consulting, data analysis, Web3 gigs, and geology/GIS

→ A hard filter: if the posting doesn't list a salary or rate, it gets dropped. No guessing games.

→ GPT does the heavy lifting — reading every result, grouping by category, and formatting it into a clean, ready-to-read brief

→ One email lands in my inbox every morning. That's it.
It wasn't a clean build. I hit auth errors, model access issues, blank outputs, JSON that broke because of unescaped characters, the usual real-world mess that never shows up in tutorials.

But that's exactly the value: this is the same debugging and architecture work I bring to client automations, lead gen pipelines, CRM integrations, voice agents.

The tools change, the process doesn't.

If you're an SME or team drowning in manual, repetitive research work, this is the kind of thing I build.
