Rilo is now part of Adobe 🤝

I was on the founding team, and what a run it was! Hackathons that pulled in 2000+ builders, a discord (& beyond) community grown from zero into the thousands, 100k+ workflows built on the platform. We even handwrote letters, (actual pen and paper!), inviting our first 150 users in SF to our first event.

The biggest thing I took away was watching users teach us what to build. The moment people wanted something their automation tool didn't natively support, they'd drop out of it and start stitching APIs together by hand. That's what Rilo became: describe what you want in plain english, and it builds and runs the workflow for you.

A few things I picked up along the way in SF:

- Events are a terrible discovery channel. The people you actually want as users know better than to attend one. Signal-to-noise ratio is BAD. If you must host one, curate the list yourself and make people want in. 

- If you know exactly what you want and exactly how to get there, AI makes automating it easy. If you don't, you'll spend longer iterating with AI than just doing the thing yourself. So do it by hand first, and automate once you know exactly what you want.

- Talk to 100 users before you touch growth. Your copy, your angles, your cuts - all of it sharpens to a level you can't imagine from the inside.

- Never book Air India for a 20 hour direct flight. A layover halfway is fine. 20 straight hours is not. 

- You will land in SF planning to try every cuisine known to mankind. You will be eating Mexican daily within two weeks.

- Don't walk into a dive bar from the 1800s and ask for a menu card.
