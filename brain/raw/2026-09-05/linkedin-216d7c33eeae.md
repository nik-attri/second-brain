🎁 Free Resource Friday!

🩺 Before you write another fundraising strategy, launch another campaign, or decide you just need to "raise more money," I have one question: Do you actually know how healthy your fundraising program is? 

This week's FREE resource is Queer For Hire's Development Health Analysis, designed to help nonprofits look beyond the last campaign or revenue total and understand the fundraising infrastructure underneath it.

👀 Because hitting your goal doesn't necessarily mean your fundraising program is healthy. And missing a goal doesn't necessarily mean it's broken. 

The Development Health Analysis gives you the tools to examine your fundraising from multiple angles, including:
📊 31 suggested metrics across financial performance, donors, board and staff engagement, programs, Culture of Philanthropy, external relations, training, and resources
💰 A four-year Financial Fundraising Summary to understand where your revenue has actually been coming from and how performance is changing
💬 Culture of Philanthropy interview questions for staff and board members to uncover how people understand and experience fundraising across the organization
📝 A 37-question Culture of Philanthropy Survey to add quantitative data to what you're hearing

The goal isn't another spreadsheet full of numbers. It's understanding what's working, what's vulnerable, and what needs to change before you decide what comes next.

🌱 This also pairs perfectly with my current Sincerely, Queers series on Sustainable vs. Transactional Fundraising. Sustainable fundraising starts by understanding the whole system, not judging your fundraising by the last check you received.
