Do I use AI? Yes.

Do I blindly believe everything it tells me? Hell no.

Does it always do what it's told? Also no. 😂

Today I had a spreadsheet with all the dates I'm teaching and doing support sessions for a new course.  I gave it to ChatGPT in the new work move, and told Gary (that's his name) which calendar I wanted them adding to, how long the sessions were and what needed to go in.
Gary came back with a very detailed plan of exactly what he was going to do.
Excellent. Go for it.
Then I checked my calendar, and he had added everything to the calendar I'd asked for... but also to my main calendar as well. 
"Oh," he said, "I invited you to all the events."
🤦‍♀️

At no point in his very detailed explanation of what he was about to do did he mention that little creative decision. 🙄

He fixed it. No harm done. But this is exactly why I still check its work.  A lot.

AI is a realy useful tool. I use it every day. But without a human who knows WTF is going on behind the scenes and checks what it's actually done, I'm not handing over the keys just yet.
So I'm firmly in:
Useful. But I'm checking your work. 👀

Where are you on my AI trust/fear axis? (Made by Gary)
