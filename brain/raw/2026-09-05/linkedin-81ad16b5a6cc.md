JUST SOLD | Buyer Represented 🥂

Sometimes the best retirement plan isn’t a spreadsheet. It’s 5+ acres and one hell of a view.

After years in the home she’d known for a very long time, my buyer was ready for a change—and while retirement isn’t here quite yet, she knew exactly what she wanted the next chapter to look like.

So we found it.

Five+ private acres perched above Hood Canal, sweeping water and mountain views, room to breathe, and Roosevelt elk casually wandering through like they’re part of the HOA. Honestly, they kind of are.

It’s peaceful. It’s beautiful. It’s slightly off-grid without requiring her to actually become a pioneer. And most importantly, it’s a place she can enjoy now while building toward the life she wants later.

There’s something especially cool about watching a woman make a big move entirely on her own terms—leaving a longtime home behind and choosing something completely different because this is what she wants next.

Acreage. Views. Quiet mornings. A huge covered deck. The Olympic Peninsula outside her door.

She’s not retired yet.

But the retirement real estate? Handled. 🥂🏡
