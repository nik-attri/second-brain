Before you leave Big Tech for a startup...3 Big Tech PMs, SWEs, and leaders I know quit or got fired from their new AI startup jobs after a few months or even days recently.
These mistakes were avoidable. Check this before signing.

1/ Don't drink the founder Kool-Aid

Founders are not all magical gifted beings. They are humans with flaws and make mistakes. 
One of the biggets failures is not doing due diligence on them, the business, and especially how you will work together. 
If you're a leader, not trialing how you debate and work through a disagreement with them on a strategic decision is a big miss. You need to know how much they're willing to give up control and if you're comfortable in that position. 

2/ Don't trust that your equity will 2x or 3x. 

Don't trust any projection spreadsheets. 

Use dumb recruiter math: 
Take this:
- What did investors pay per share in the last funding round? 
- What is the price you're going to have to pay per share to exercise your option (n/a if RSUs)
- How many of those do you get? With what vesting schedule? 
That's how you calculate it: (Preferred - Strike) x # Shares = $ value. 

That's how you compare across companies. 

Yeah - lots of caveats, but I literally saw an AI unicorn put a spreadsheet showing what the person's total compensation would look like if they had OpenAI's valuation. Seriously???!

My last company even had lawyers come after them about "reasonable" projections they showed candidates. 

3/ Protect your options on equity (pun intended)

Startup equity rules are stacked against employees like you! 

You can have your own risk tolerance, but 2 out of 3 in-the-money option grants don't get exercised because employees get screwed. 

If you leave the company or get fired/laid off, you get 90 days to exercise all your hard earned options over the years or get effed. 

Push for a 3-10 year post-termination exercise window. Do it. Don't not do this. 

A FAANG SWE texted me after I told him he could have negotiated this for his first startup role saying: 
"WTF? I could have got that? This is my biggest regret - what the hell will I do if I decide to leave? Will I lose all that equity?"
