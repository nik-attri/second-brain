🚀 Your Invoice Process Shouldn’t Be This Complicated
Invoices arrive by email, PDF, different formats and from different countries.
Then someone has to download them, read them, extract the relevant information, validate the data, route them for approval and prepare them for accounting.
For many businesses, this still means too many manual steps.
CypherIntern is built to change that.
Our platform helps businesses automate invoice processing and move toward a smarter, more structured e-invoicing workflow.
With CypherIntern, companies can:
✅ Capture and process incoming invoices digitally
✅ Extract invoice data using AI and OCR
✅ Reduce repetitive manual data entry
✅ Convert invoice information into structured formats such as XRechnung
✅ Create more transparent invoice and approval workflows
✅ Prepare for an increasingly digital and interconnected e-invoicing environment
✅ Keep invoice processes centralized and easier to manage
But invoice automation is not only about saving a few minutes.
It is about creating a process that can scale.
As e-invoicing requirements continue to expand across Europe and other markets, companies need systems that can adapt to different formats, regulations and integration requirements.
That is exactly the direction we are taking with CypherIntern.
We are building a platform where invoice processing, automation, structured e-invoicing and future integrations such as Peppol connectivity can come together in one workflow.
💡 Our goal is simple:
Less manual invoice work.
Better data.
Faster processes.
More automation.
