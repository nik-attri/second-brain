🚨 WE’RE HIRING | Parts Purchasing & Inventory Audit Specialist

Are you someone who enjoys working with inventory, purchasing, reconciliations, and data accuracy? This could be the right opportunity for you! 🚀

We are looking for a Parts Purchasing & Inventory Audit Specialist to support purchasing controls, inventory accuracy, and work-order reconciliation.

📌 Key Responsibilities:
• Audit credit card purchases and ensure compliance with purchasing policies
• Match purchases with Work Orders, receipts, and invoices
• Verify parts, quantities, and costs against WOs
• Audit inventory transactions and scan-out records
• Identify recurring unplanned purchases and recommend stocking additions
• Maintain and update the Parts On Order report
• Coordinate with technicians to resolve missing or incorrect information
• Track discrepancies related to price, quantity, missing WO#, and unapproved purchases
• Support additional parts and inventory audits as required

🎯 What We’re Looking For:
✔️ Experience in parts, inventory, fleet, or purchasing operations preferred
✔️ Strong spreadsheet and reconciliation skills
✔️ Excellent attention to detail and numerical accuracy
✔️ Clear written communication skills
✔️ Ability to work independently and follow up with stakeholders

💼 Role: Parts Purchasing & Inventory Audit Specialist
👤 Reports to: Parts Strategy & Analytics Manager
