The biggest shift in AI is not chat. It is delegation.

When teams can build custom agents without code, AI stops being a pilot project and starts becoming part of daily operations. That matters because most business value is not in one big transformation, it is in dozens of small workflow improvements across sales, service, finance, and operations. 🤖

Gemini Enterprise is pushing that model forward. People can create agents that handle repeatable tasks, connect steps, and reduce manual effort without waiting on a long development cycle. For companies in Thailand and Singapore, this is especially relevant where speed, talent efficiency, and multilingual workflows all affect competitiveness.

Our view at Digigen is simple, leaders should not ask only, “What can AI answer?” They should ask, “Which workflows should AI own end to end?”

Start with one process that is high-volume, rule-based, and slows your team down. Then measure time saved, error reduction, and how quickly the team adopts it. That is where the business case becomes real.

Over the next 12 months, the winners will be the companies that treat agents as operational infrastructure, not experiments. 📈
