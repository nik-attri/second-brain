Job Title: Purchase Manager – BOP & Outsourcing
Location: Sonipat, Haryana
CTC: ₹6–9 LPA
Reporting To: Head – Procurement

Key Responsibilities
Manage procurement of BOP materials, including components, fittings, packaging materials, hardware, and other indirect items.
Develop and manage vendors for outsourced manufacturing.
Source ready-made finished products and negotiate commercial terms.
Drive cost reduction initiatives through effective supplier negotiations.
Identify and develop alternate vendors for critical materials.
Ensure quality compliance and minimize in-house rework through supplier quality management.
Handle supplier rejections, quality issues, and vendor claims.
Coordinate with Production and Stores to maintain optimum inventory levels.
Strengthen procurement documentation, purchase contracts, and vendor agreements.
Monitor procurement activities through ERP systems and prepare periodic MIS reports.

Key Result Areas (KRAs)
Optimize procurement costs for BOP materials and outsourced products.
Develop a strong and reliable vendor base for critical items.
Ensure timely material availability to support uninterrupted production.
Maintain quality compliance for outsourced and purchased products.
Build long-term supplier relationships across multiple locations.

Qualification
Graduate in any discipline.
MBA (Operations/Supply Chain/Materials Management) preferred.
Experience
5–10 years of experience in BOP procurement, outsourcing, and vendor development.
Preferred Industry

Candidates should have experience in one or more of the following industries:

Stainless Steel Utensils
Cookware & Kitchenware Manufacturing
Houseware Products
Sink Manufacturing
Metal Fabrication
HoReCa Supply Manufacturing
Allied Manufacturing Industries

Required Skills
Vendor Development & Supplier Management
Strategic Sourcing
Commercial Negotiation
Cost Reduction & Cost Analysis
Inventory Planning
Procurement Documentation
ERP Systems (SAP/Oracle/ERPNext or similar)
Advanced MS Excel
Strong Communication & Negotiation Skills
Analytical and Problem-Solving Ability
