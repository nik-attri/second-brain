We automated 80% of a manual process in 3 weeks. Here’s what actually happened.

A mid-sized logistics company was drowning in manual invoice reconciliation — 4 people, 6 hours a day, matching POs to invoices to shipping records. Error rate: ~12%.

Here’s what we did:

1️⃣ Mapped the workflow before touching any tech
Turns out 70% of “exceptions” followed the same 5 patterns. Most automation projects skip this step and pay for it later.

2️⃣ Built a rules + AI hybrid, not a full black box
Simple mismatches → deterministic rules.
Ambiguous cases → AI model flags and suggests a resolution, human approves.
Nobody trusts a system they can’t audit.

3️⃣ Kept humans in the loop, not out of it
The team didn’t lose 4 headcount. They moved from data-entry to exception-handling — higher leverage, less burnout.

The results after 60 days:

→ 80% of invoices processed with zero human touch
→ Error rate dropped from 12% to under 2%
→ Reconciliation time: 6 hours/day → 45 minutes/day
→ Team redeployed to vendor negotiations and forecasting

The lesson that surprised me most: the hardest part wasn’t the AI. It was getting the team to trust a system that could be wrong sometimes — because it made its reasoning visible.

AI automation isn’t about replacing people. 
It’s about removing the parts of the job nobody wanted to do in the first place.

Where is your team still doing this kind of manual matching?
