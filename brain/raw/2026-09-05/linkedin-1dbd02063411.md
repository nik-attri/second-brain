I just discovered Lady Whistleblown on Substack. 

Isn't it exciting when you find a writer who articulates brilliantly what you've been thinking about? I've linked the article in the comments.

Animal liberation will happen. Just like slavery's abolishment and a woman's right to vote finally happened. Did it seem impossible? Did it take a long time? Hell yes. Entrenched systems are difficult to break. But break them we must. And we will. 

I love this quote from the article:

"The moment someone tells you the fight is unwinnable and the sensible thing is to make peace with a violent system, suggest to the men proposing we breed away an animal’s mind that we instead breed away their doubt. And to the women in this movement who have felt too nervous or too polite to say any of this aloud in a room full of confident men waving their spreadsheets at you – I see you, and I urge you to speak up. Because what you have to say matters, and could very much influence the direction this movement is headed next."

To any big money who might stumble upon this, consider the sanctuaries. Consider the investigations and legislative work. Sanctuaries have saved more animal lives and made more vegans than any welfare pledge that only promised less torture and was not kept anyway. 

As always, I am cheering you on nonprofits doing the hard work in this movement to liberate animals once and for all. (Actually liberate as opposed to genetically modifying them to feel less pain.) 

In solidarity.  💚
