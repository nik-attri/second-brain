I got tired of forgetting tasks, so I built a WhatsApp bot to remind me...

Sounds simple, right? But here's how it started..

We all have tasks that seem easy to remember at the moment, but somehow slip our minds when it’s time to get them done. I wanted a simple way to stay on top of my tasks without having to open another app, navigate through menus, or manually set reminders.

Then I thought, what if I could just message a chatbot on WhatsApp in plain English, and it would take care of the rest?

So, instead of looking for one, I decided to build one...

Introducing my WhatsApp Reminder Bot!

It understands natural language, figures out when you want to be reminded, and sends you a reminder at the scheduled time. No complicated commands, no unnecessary steps — just tell it what you need to do, the way you’d tell a friend.

And that’s not all! You can also:
- Create reminders through a simple conversation
- List all your active reminders
- Find your next upcoming reminder
-  Cancel reminders whenever your plans change

The idea was simple: make staying organised as easy as sending a WhatsApp message.

This project has been a fun learning experience, from building the backend logic to integrating AI-powered conversations with WhatsApp and handling scheduled reminders.
