If you’re still collecting headshots, bios, links, and release forms manually for every podcast guest, automate the intake.

You booked the guest.

Great.

Now somebody needs:

Bio.

Headshot.

Company name.

Website.

Social links.

Book link.

Offer link.

Preferred title.

Release agreement.

Maybe pronunciation notes.

And somehow half of it arrives in four separate emails.

Then the episode is scheduled to publish and somebody realizes:

“We never got the headshot.”

HERE’S HOW I’D FIX IT

I’d separate guest approval from guest onboarding.

Approval asks:

Should this person be on the show?

Onboarding asks:

What does production need now that they ARE?

Once the guest is approved, send one onboarding form.

Everything goes there.

Upload the headshot.

Paste the bio.

Add the links.

Agree to whatever permissions are required.

Give the producer the information in a standardized format.

Then store it with the guest record.

Now when production starts, the team isn’t searching:

Inbox.

Slack.

Google Drive.

Calendly notes.

Instagram DMs.

Everything needed for the episode is attached to ONE workflow.

HERE ARE SOME PROMPTS TO FIX IT:

PROMPT 1

Create a post-approval podcast guest onboarding form. Include bio, preferred name/title, headshot, website, social links, resources mentioned, pronunciation notes, and any production information the team needs.

PROMPT 2

Here is our current podcast guest onboarding process: [PASTE]. Identify every place information is collected manually or stored inconsistently, then propose one standardized workflow.

PROMPT 3

Create an internal guest-preparation checklist that confirms all required assets and approvals are complete before the episode moves into production.

Your producer shouldn’t have to become a detective every time you book somebody.
