Before I touch your AR, I ask 5 questions. 

Most AR problems don’t start where they show up. They show up in collections, but they started upstream. 

So before I fix anything, I ask: 

1. Do your contract terms match what’s actually being invoiced? 
2. Where does a dispute go once it’s raised, and who owns it? 
3. How long does cash sit unapplied? 
4. Which customers keep paying late, and is it them or your process? 
5. Who looks at the aging report, and what do they do with it? 

The answers tell me where the leak is. After 15+ years across mortgage, cards, receivables, and fraud investigations, I read an AR ledger the way I’d work a fraud case. The pattern points to the exact problem. 

What that’s meant for clients:

✅ ~40% less manual reconciliation time (NetSuite + Salesforce + AI tools)

✅ 98%+ client satisfaction on cross-border collections and disputes 

✅ 30%+ reduction in aged debt

✅ AR and DSO dashboards leadership actually uses
