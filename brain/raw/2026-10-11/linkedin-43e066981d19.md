𝗪𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗜𝗚𝗔 𝗯𝗲 𝗱𝗲𝗮𝗱 𝗶𝗻 𝗮 𝗳𝗲𝘄 𝘆𝗲𝗮𝗿𝘀? 😊You’ve probably heard that prediction from thought leaders on your LinkedIn feed. I’m not making that prediction. But I do believe something equally important: 𝗡𝗼𝘁 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝗜𝗔𝗠 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱𝘀 𝗮𝗻 𝗜𝗚𝗔 𝘀𝗼𝗹𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.
At CyberSolve (a Hexaware Technologies company), we’ve helped hundreds of customers implement leading IGA platforms using industry best practices. We’re proud of our partnerships across the IGA ecosystem - SailPoint, Saviynt et al. But our responsibility as IAM practitioners isn't to sell or implement an IGA vendor solution everywhere. It's to solve the customer's problem with the right solution, at the right time, and at the lowest practical cost.
Here’s a recent example from a US-based healthcare supplies manufacturer and distributor.The 𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺: The customer’s SAP team spent hours every week manually fulfilling access provisioning requests. Each request originated in a ServiceNow ticket, required a check in Pathlock for potential Segregation of Duties (SoD) conflicts, and depended on a human correctly interpreting the result before proceeding. Much of the work was essentially repetitive data entry - with manual effort and room for error.
𝗢𝘂𝗿 𝗮𝗽𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗮𝗰𝗵: Instead of starting with “Which IGA platform should we implement?”, we started with “What business outcome does the customer actually need?” 
#𝗖𝘆𝗯𝗲𝗿𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘃𝗲𝗜𝗻𝗻𝗼𝘃𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗟𝗮𝗯𝘀 built an Agentic AI-powered solution using 𝗡𝗲𝘂𝗿𝗼𝗜𝗗:
1️⃣ ServiceNow → NeuroID: Access provisioning requests flow directly from ServiceNow tickets to NeuroID.
2️⃣ NeuroID → SAP: An AI agent connects to SAP Cloud and executes the fulfilment steps that an SAP administrator would otherwise perform manually.
3️⃣ NeuroID → Pathlock: Another agent checks for potential SoD conflicts and orchestrates the appropriate actions - recording rejections, updating tickets, and notifying stakeholders.
The result? An automated provisioning workflow that connects ticket-driven requests, GRC checks, and SAP fulfilment - without making a new IGA implementation the starting point.
𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗶𝗴𝗴𝗲𝗿 𝗹𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗼𝗻: IGA isn't dead. But neither is it the answer to every IAM problem. Sometimes the right answer is deploying IGA vendor solution. Sometimes it’s an operational accelerator. Sometimes it’s Agentic AI working alongside the systems a customer already owns.
