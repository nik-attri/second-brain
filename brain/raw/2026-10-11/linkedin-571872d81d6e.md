Most solopreneurs are drowning in manual tasks.

You write a long-form post, then manually copy-paste it to LinkedIn. Then you rewrite it for X. Then you pull a quote for Instagram. Then you draft an email newsletter.

It’s not just busy work. It’s a burnout trap.

If you want to scale without adding 20 hours to your work week, you need to stop creating content and start creating a system.

Here is the 3-Step Data-Mapping Protocol to automate your distribution:

1. The Source of Truth (The Input)
Pick one high-value medium (video, audio, or long-form writing). This is your 'Primary Asset.' Don’t create anything else until this is done.

2. The Mapping Logic (The Routing)
Define the rules for your assets. For example: 'A LinkedIn post always becomes a 3-part thread on X, a quote graphic for IG, and a summary bullet point for my newsletter.'

3. The Automation Pipeline (The Execution)
Use tools like Make, Zapier, or Notion automations to bridge the gap. Once the 'Primary Asset' is uploaded to your database, the system triggers the creation of the derivative versions automatically.

Stop being the engine and start being the architect.
