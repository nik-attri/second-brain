One of the most underrated benefits of pop-up product sampling? The surprise and delight factor. 

Think about it. You're walking down a sidewalk or boardwalk, minding your own business, and out of nowhere, a brand hands you something cool, free, and totally unexpected.

Pretty damn nice, right?

That's a whole different experience than seeing another ad or grabbing a sample at Costco.

You get genuine reactions, social content, all that good stuff.

But there's something more. 

People remember things differently when emotions are involved.

And when a brand catches you off guard in a good way, that feeling sticks. It becomes part of how you remember the brand.

That's a pretty big deal when you think about it.

And something that doesn't exactly show up in your brand activation recap spreadsheet.

But it sure as hell should.
