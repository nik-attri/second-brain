Will robots take our jobs?

I don’t think that’s the right question.

We’re already using robots for tasks that used to require several people. The FieldPrinter, for example, can handle layout printing much faster than doing it manually — with one person operating the machine.

But the robot still needs to be maintained, programmed and operated.

And that means new skills, new roles and even new businesses will emerge around this technology.

Robotics won’t simply replace people. 

It will change what people do — and how we work.

The real question is: are we ready for that change?

What do you think the construction job site will look like in 5–10 years? 👇
