My first Data Analytics project. My first pitch. A moment I will never forget. 📊

Looking back at my internship at NHUB, Jos, I remember how it all started.

Every Friday, interns from different tech stacks would gather to pitch their work and share their progress for the week.

Then came my turn.

I was to present my first Excel data analytics project, analysing Nigerian Internal Revenue data.

Honestly, I was nervous as hell. 😂

Standing in front of everyone and explaining my analysis felt like a big deal at the time. But I knew I had to give it my best.

What made that presentation special was that I didn't just present the revenue generated. I went further to highlight some of the challenges affecting revenue collection and suggested ways the process could be improved.

When I finished, I received warm applause and encouraging feedback for a job well done.

That moment meant a lot to me. It gave me the confidence to keep learning, keep asking questions, and look beyond the numbers to understand the problems they tell us about.

That was when I began to understand that data analytics is more than working with spreadsheets. It's about finding insights, identifying problems, and helping people make better decisions.

Today, I look back at that first project with gratitude. It wasn't perfect, but it was the beginning of something amazing.

To everyone starting their journey in data analytics: don't wait until you feel completely ready. Start with what you know, learn as you go, and have the courage to share your work.

You never know which small step will become the beginning of something great.
