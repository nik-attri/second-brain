Thunder Bear.

"TORBS! WE NEED TO TALK!"

"WHAT THE BLOODY HELL IS HAPPENING ON LINKEDIN?!"

Ehhh...what do you mean?

"YOU spend hours writing thoughtful posts. Hours engaging. Building relationships. Sharing experiences. And then what happens?"

Ehhh...

"SEVEN PEOPLE SEE IT! THREE OF THEM ARE SELLING SOMETHING! AND ONE IS A BOT!" 🤣

BIG mistake.

Fair point.

Effective immediately:
🧸 Thunder Bear takes full control of consumer experience.
🙋🏼‍♂️ I become his assistant. On probation.

🎶 Strategic expansion to YouTube accelerated.

"At least on Life Set to Notes, people can choose what they want to listen to. Not what an algorithm has decided they're allowed to discover!"

"Obviously, honey doesn't count." 🍯😂
