Should a growing Australian SMB buy a new ERP? Not automatically.

Too many mid-market businesses pull the trigger on a massive, costly system replacement when they don't actually need one yet. They chase a new software brand when they should be targeting a specific internal bottleneck.
Before you commit to a major overhaul, look at where the breakdown is actually happening:

📱 Scenario A: Fix the Friction with Power Platform
If your underlying core finance system is sound, but your team is drowning in slow approvals, blindspot reporting, or manual paper trails out in the field, a full ERP migration is overkill. You can bypass the disruption entirely by wrapping your current setup in Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI. This targeted layer strips away daily friction, automates workflows, and builds clean dashboards without moving your database.
⚙️ Scenario B: Upgrade the Core with Business Central
However, if your operational foundation is structurally disconnected—meaning inventory counts don't match purchasing, fulfilment can't see sales orders, and financial controls are managed on disconnected spreadsheets—patchwork fixes won't save you. In this case, moving to a unified ecosystem like Dynamics 365 Business Central is the right strategic move to establish a single source of truth.

The takeaway? Start with the operating problem, not the product logo.
Focusing on the operational reality first saves growing businesses hundreds of thousands of dollars in unnecessary software licensing and implementation headaches.
