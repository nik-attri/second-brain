📢 We’re Hiring | HR Generalist
We are looking for an HR Generalist to join our team at our Free Zone facility in Alexandria.
🔹 Responsibilities:
Handle day-to-day HR operations and employee requests.
Manage personnel files, contracts and employee documentation.
Handle attendance, leaves, permissions and overtime records.
Support payroll preparation and review.
Manage recruitment activities from sourcing to onboarding.
Coordinate interviews and follow up with candidates and hiring managers.
Handle employee relations and resolve HR-related issues.
Ensure compliance with Egyptian Labor Law and Social Insurance requirements.
Support performance management and employee evaluation processes.
Prepare HR reports and maintain accurate employee data.
Support onboarding, offboarding and employee lifecycle activities.
Assist in developing and implementing HR policies and procedures.
Coordinate with government entities and external HR service providers when required.
🔹 Requirements:
Bachelor’s degree in HR, Business Administration, Commerce or a related field.
2–4 years of experience as an HR Generalist or in a similar HR role.
Good knowledge of Personnel, Payroll, Recruitment and Employee Relations.
Good knowledge of Egyptian Labor Law and Social Insurance.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
Good command of MS Office, especially Excel.
Very good English is preferred.
Strong organizational and problem-solving skills.
Ability to work independently in a fast-paced industrial environment.
💰 Salary: EGP 15,000 – 20,000 Net Monthly
📍 Location: Free Zone, Amreya, Alexandria
