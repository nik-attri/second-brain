How difficult is it to retain talent when the structure is a mess?
 ⬇️ 

I’ve experienced this in one of my projects.

There was a lot of staff turnover. People didn’t stay for long, and new employees quickly realized that it wasn’t the right place for them.

I spoke with the directors.

I explained what was happening, what wasn’t working, and what the consequences would be if we didn’t change certain things.

For a while, we managed to give the team more structure, organize part of the operations, and make the situation more stable.

But there was a much bigger problem:
What happens when you work with managers who don’t want to change anything, even when they can see the ship is sinking?

You can find a temporary solution.

You can support the team.

You can put out the fires.

But if the structure causing the problem stays the same, when that temporary solution disappears...

the problem comes back.

And it did.

Sometimes companies think they have a talent problem.

But actually, they have a structure problem that is making good people leave.

Before hiring another person, maybe we should ask:
Why didn’t the others want to stay?

Have you ever worked with managers who could see the problem but still didn’t want to change?
