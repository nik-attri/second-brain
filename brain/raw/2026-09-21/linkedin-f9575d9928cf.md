Meta Ads just became part of Reacher.

And honestly, this changes the workflow quite a bit.

Before: 

Creator posts a video → upload it to Reacher → leave Reacher → open Meta Ads Manager → set up the ad → manage it somewhere else.

Now?

Creator posts the video in Reacher → you turn it into a Meta ad without leaving Reacher.

And once it’s live, you can:

→ Launch the ad
→ Pause/manage it
→ Track spend
→ Track revenue
→ See exactly what each creator is earning in commission

All tied back to the campaign.

That last part is what I’m most excited about.

Because creator marketing and paid media usually live in two completely different worlds.

Now your team can see: Creator → Content → Ad → Spend → Revenue → Commission - in one place.

Less jumping between tools.
Less spreadsheet hell.

Way easier to understand what’s actually driving revenue.

The team has been shipping like crazy lately.

And this is just the beginning. 👀
