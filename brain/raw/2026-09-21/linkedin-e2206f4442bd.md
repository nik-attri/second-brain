From admin burnout to their best quarter ever. 📈
 
I spent this morning digging into 90-day retention and performance data for a mid-market SaaS team we onboarded onto Outplay earlier this year — and the numbers made my day.
 
Before Outplay:
 Reps were losing ~3 hours a day to manual work — copy-pasting call notes into the CRM, tracking sequences on spreadsheets, and manually setting follow-up reminders.
 
What we rebuilt:
 → Automated multi-channel sequences (email + phone + LinkedIn, working together)
 → Bi-directional CRM sync, so notes and task updates flow automatically
 → Live engagement alerts, so reps call back the moment a prospect opens an email
 
The 90-day results:
 🔻 Rep admin time down 65%
 🔺 High-value prospect touches per rep — doubled, with no extra hours
 🔺 Demo booking conversion up 28% team-wide
 
Software should never slow sales reps down. It should clear the runway so they can do what they do best — build relationships and close deals.
