📣 Final funded places available!

Our member Cosmic UK has just two funded cohorts remaining for its Power Platform Skills Bootcamp—and there is currently no confirmed continuation funding.

The next cohort begins on 21 September, followed by the final funded cohort in October 2026. Eligible employers can still enrol themselves or members of their team at no cost.

This practical programme helps participants use Microsoft’s Power Platform to solve real business challenges:

📊 Power BI – create live dashboards and turn data into useful business insights
⚙️ Power Automate – streamline repetitive tasks and workflows
📱 Power Apps – build bespoke business applications without complex coding
🤖 Copilot Studio – develop AI-powered assistants and automation tools

Organisations are already using these skills to reduce manual reporting, improve data accuracy, automate administrative processes and give decision-makers faster access to the information they need.

As one participant explained:
💬 “Instead of spending time building reports, I can spend time understanding them… It has fundamentally changed how quickly we can identify opportunities, spot issues and make informed decisions.”

If you or someone in your network could benefit, now is the time to explore the opportunity before the final funded places are allocated.
