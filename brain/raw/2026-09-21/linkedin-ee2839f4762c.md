Your business isn't slow because of your team.

It's slow because every decision still runs through you.

Here's the pattern behind most "slow" small businesses:

→ Your team waits for your approval on small things
→ Customers wait for your reply on big things
→ Nothing moves when you're offline

It feels like a hiring problem. Most of the time, it's a systems problem.

Three changes fix a lot of it:

1. Write down the 5 decisions you make most often, and the rule behind each one
2. Let your team make those calls without asking you
3. Automate the repeat work (follow-ups, reports, data entry) so people spend their time on judgment

The goal isn't to work less. It's to build a business that keeps moving when you step away.

What's one decision you're still making that someone else could handle?
