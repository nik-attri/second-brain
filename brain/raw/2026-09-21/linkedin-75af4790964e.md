During one of my recent workshops, a manager updated a task from the same window in ChatGPT.

Then the face changed.

“Wow, I really could have done it. And it is so simple.”

That is my favourite moment in an AI workshop.

A moment before, ChatGPT was another tool to learn. Now the manager could see how it connected to work that already had to happen that day. We created a ticket and checked the follow-up with the team from the same place.

Of course, this requires the right systems to be connected and the right access to be configured. But once that foundation is there, a leader does not have to carry information manually from a conversation into every task and ticket.

We take the work people already recognise: managing a team, following up after meetings, creating tickets, updating tasks. Then we practise how AI can help with that work and where the person still needs to check, decide or approve.

You can see the “wow” moment. People realise that it is simpler than they expected and that they can do it themselves.

Creating these practical AI workshops for leadership teams has become one of my passions. I am learning with every company, and I love seeing the moment when AI becomes useful on Monday morning, not someday in the future.
