You will not transform in your comfort zone.”

Dr. Stephanie Wigner @drstephaniewigner of @thewealthypractitioner said that to @macymcneely in July, in the @thesalesgirlsofficial studio. The room stopped moving.

I have only ever leaped forward when I was uncomfortable.

I dropped out of college at 19 to backpack alone. Do you think that was comfy?

I read one book about real estate investing and bought my first rental at 27. Single. No experience. Was that in my comfort zone? Hell no.

I did it anyway.

The doctor clearing $400,000 who has never bought a rental. The attorney who has read 12 investing books and analyzed zero deals. The engineer with a beautiful spreadsheet and no offer submitted.

That is not lazy. That is comfortable. And comfortable is costing them more than a bad deal ever would.

Dr. Stephanie said something that stuck harder: stop lying to yourself that that is what you want when your behavior is the complete opposite of that.

I own 20 units across four states. Every single one started with a decision I did not feel ready to make.

Where are you comfortable right now? Tell me in the comments.
