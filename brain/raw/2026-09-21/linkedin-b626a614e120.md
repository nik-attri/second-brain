With our API, you can issue unlimited stablecoin wallet addresses, empowering your users to transact effortlessly.

In 2023, I was building an app for a customer for a crypto platform that wanted to issue every user their own dedicated crypto wallet, the same way you get one on Binance or Bybit.

I looked for infrastructure that could do this automatically. The one I found wanted a one time fee of roughly $23,000, plus a percentage of every crypto inflow after that. the Client couldn't afford it.

So we shipped it manually. Every single wallet confirmation, done by hand, because the automated version was priced for companies far bigger than a small team building something new.

I never forgot that wall. It's part of why TesaPay's infrastructure exists the way it does today.

Any fintech or crypto platform, centralized or decentralized, can now plug into our API and offer their users a dedicated stablecoin wallet address, fully custodied and controlled by them, with no upfront cost to integrate.
