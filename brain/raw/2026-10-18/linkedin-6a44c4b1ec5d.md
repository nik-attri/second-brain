Join Haier Egypt — Were Hiring!
Role: Service Center Manager - Alexandria

Responsibilities:
-Oversee the daily operations of the service center, ensuring smooth workflow and efficient handling of service requests.
-Manage the allocation of service requests to technicians, optimizing resource utilization and minimizing response times.
-Ensure that all service activities, including installations, repairs, and maintenance, are performed according to company standards and quality procedures.
-Implement and maintain service management systems and software to track service requests, manage customer information, and generate reports.
-Coordinate with other departments, such as sales and logistics, to ensure seamless service delivery and customer satisfaction.
-Lead, mentor, and motivate a team of technicians and administrative staff.
-Conduct regular team meetings to communicate updates, address issues, and foster a collaborative and positive work environment.
-Set performance goals and conduct regular performance reviews for team members, providing feedback and identifying areas for improvement.
-Ensure that all team members adhere to company policies, procedures, and safety regulations.
-Ensure the delivery of exceptional customer service, addressing customer inquiries, resolving complaints, and exceeding customer expectations.
-Develop and implement customer service policies and procedures to ensure consistent and high-quality service delivery.
-Maintain strong relationships with key customers and stakeholders.
-Develop and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) to track service center performance.
-Analyze service data and generate regular reports for management, identifying trends, areas for improvement, and opportunities for optimization.
-Prepare and manage the service center budget, controlling expenses and maximizing cost-effectiveness.
-Implement and maintain quality control processes to ensure service quality and consistency.
-Ensure compliance with all relevant regulations and industry standards.

Qualifications:
•Bachelor’s degree in engineering (Electrical, Mechanical, or equivalent).
•A Minimum of 6-10 years of experience in service center operations, ideally in home appliances or consumer electronics.
•Proficiency in English communication.
•Excellent experience managing service center operations within the budget.
•Requires knowledge of process and policy implementation.
•Excellent communication skills.
•Strong leadership and team management experience.
•Proficiency in ERP and service management systems.
•Proficient in MS Office.
