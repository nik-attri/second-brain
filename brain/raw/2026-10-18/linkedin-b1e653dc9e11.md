We Are Hiring "General Accountant"

Role Description
 The General Accountant at Vernbro Global investment is a full-time, on-site role based in Cairo . This role is responsible for recording daily financial transactions, maintaining the general ledger, and ensuring accurate posting of revenues, expenses, and journal entries. The General Accountant prepares and reviews financial statements, reconciles bank and account balances, and monitors cash flow to support operational needs. The role also involves managing payables and receivables, assisting with budgeting and forecasting, and supporting monthly and annual closing activities. In addition, the General Accountant helps ensure compliance with relevant accounting standards, internal policies, and local regulatory requirements, and collaborates with management to provide clear financial reporting and analysis.

Qualifications
Strong skills in financial accounting, including general ledger management, journal entry posting, and preparation of financial statements.
Experience with accounts payable and receivable processes, bank reconciliations, and cash flow monitoring.
Proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Excel, with the ability to work with spreadsheets, reports, and financial data.
Solid understanding of budgeting, cost control, and basic financial analysis to support operational and strategic decisions.
Knowledge of relevant accounting standards, internal controls, and local tax and regulatory requirements.
Strong attention to detail, accuracy in data entry and documentation, and ability to meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
Effective written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams and management.
Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field; professional certifications or ongoing accounting studies are an advantage.
Prior experience in an accounting role, preferably within a sports, leisure, or service-oriented organization, is beneficial.

Experience
5 Years of Experience
