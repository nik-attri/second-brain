Ever heard of the "Law of Requisite Variety"?

Cybernetics gave us a lot of brilliant mental models, but the Law of Requisite Variety might just be the ultimate cheat code for leadership, business strategy in navigating the South African landscape 2027 more than ever before. 

In plain English? Only variety can absorb variety.

If your internal complexity doesn't match the complexity of the environment you're operating in, you're going to get steamrolled. 

We see this everywhere right now:
 • In many South African business & particularly retail, sticking to rigid, legacy distribution models while consumer expectations shift in real-time is a fast track to irrelevance. (Trust me, trying to unify consumer and commercial portfolios across a massive channel ecosystem will test your variety limits real quick), my particular favorite example for SA is how international companies try copy tried and tested international models to deploy here in Africa, and keep failing!

 • In the same way with AI & Tech.... If you throw a static, single-prompt model at a chaotic, multi-variable enterprise workflow, it fails. That’s why robust RAG architectures and multi-agent systems matter—they build the structural complexity needed to handle messy reality. (I had to learn a lot about RAGs in 2025/6)

Think about this, how many organizations try to solve complexity by holding more meetings? Spoiler alert: ten meetings about how complex things are doesn't add a single unit of systemic variety. It just burns calories. I hate meetings and have worked to eliminate playing spin the corporate bottle on who's spreadsheets are the best or who's ppt deck is the prettiest!

So, here is the real question on this topic: Is your system actually adapting to the chaos around you, or are you just hoping the chaos will calm down and play by your rules?

Spoiler: IT WON'T! 

Building more adaptive capacity and agility into a business isn't chaos, its strategic, and when done correctly it's grounded in intent! The ability to see disruption as inevitable and prepare a resilient team, makes for a solid foundation, and a hell of a fun environment!
