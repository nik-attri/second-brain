🚀 NeuraX Hackathon 3.0 | Team Nexova

Happy to share my participation in NeuraX Hackathon 3.0 (2026), where our team, Nexova, worked on a solution under the AI in Industries Automation theme. 🤖

💡 Our Idea – AuditFlow: Autonomous AI-Powered Freight Invoice Auditing and Dispute Recovery

Freight invoice auditing can be a time-consuming and error-prone process, with issues such as incorrect rates, fuel surcharge errors, duplicate charges, and unauthorized fees leading to financial losses for businesses.

To address this challenge, our team proposed AuditFlow, an AI-powered platform designed to automate the freight invoice auditing workflow. The proposed solution combines multimodal AI and OCR for invoice data extraction, AI agents for automated verification, and a web-based dashboard to identify discrepancies, calculate overcharges, and generate evidence-backed dispute packages.

Working on this idea gave us an opportunity to explore how AI and automation can be applied to practical industrial problems. It also helped me understand the importance of identifying real-world challenges, collaborating as a team, and translating an idea into a technology-driven solution.

Grateful for the opportunity to participate, learn, and collaborate with my teammates throughout this experience. 🙌
