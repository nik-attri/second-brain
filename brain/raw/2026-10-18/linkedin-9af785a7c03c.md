The best AI automation doesn’t start with “What can we automate?”

It starts with a better question:

“What repetitive work is keeping our people from doing their best work?”

That might be summarizing support tickets, routing incoming requests, drafting first-pass reports, or finding answers across internal documents.

A practical way to get started:

1. Pick one frequent, time-consuming task.
2. Map the workflow before adding AI.
3. Keep a human in the loop for decisions that need judgment.
4. Measure time saved, accuracy, and user experience.
5. Improve the process before scaling it.

AI can make a messy process faster—but it can also make a bad process faster. Start small, set clear guardrails, and let the results guide what comes next.

The goal isn’t to automate everything. It’s to make work more focused, useful, and human.
