Tesco Ireland is hiring 1,725 temporary colleagues for Christmas — 600 more than last year. Start dates: 2, 9 and 16 November.
I've onboarded Christmas teams from the floor, and here's what people outside retail don't quite get: you don't get six months to train someone. You get two shifts. Then it's the Christmas rush.
And every year the same thing surprises me. The temps who thrive aren't the ones with the tidiest CVs. They're the ones who show up on time, ask the senior colleague where they went wrong, and don't disappear when the shop gets loud.
Some of the strongest full-time people I've ever worked with started exactly this way — a November temp role they took for Christmas money.
So if you know a student, a parent returning to work, anyone looking for a few months of graft over the holidays: tell them to apply. It's one of the best doors into retail in Ireland.
Operators and line managers: how do you get a new temp shift-ready in a week? Genuinely stealing ideas every year.
