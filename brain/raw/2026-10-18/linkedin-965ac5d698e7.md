Solutions managers understand the gap between a generic answer and one that actually maps to a customer's environment.

Your customers see it too.

Marcela Quintela of Spectro Cloud flags the risk in our latest Radically Transparent episode. When AI writes everything, output drifts toward average. Her take: "then you're just like everyone else."

The fix is structural. Let AI handle research, sourcing, and drafts. Keep strategy, judgment, and final decisions human. That's where expertise shows.

For B2B marketers, the same logic applies. AI can raise your quality floor and your output velocity. But the teams that win aren't producing the most content. They're the ones embedding strategic judgment into the workflow so AI amplifies expertise instead of replacing it.

We built our platform around that principle. AI should make B2B marketers more effective at connecting social activity to leads, pipeline, and revenue. Not automate away the thinking that drives real business outcomes.
