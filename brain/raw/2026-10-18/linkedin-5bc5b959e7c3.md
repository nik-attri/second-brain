Anthropic's AI agents logged six hours per staff hour. Junior hires fell to 9%.

@Anthropic said in a report on 16 October that junior roles fell from 27% to 9% of hires in its technical teams since early 2025. It said that by August its AI agents, software that carries out tasks on its own, worked six hours for every staff hour. In April, about a third of its machine learning managers and a fifth of its software managers said they were hiring less because of AI.

Anthropic's calendar data show the median employee spends 20% more time in meetings than a year ago. It said about 80% of an interactive session with Claude, its AI assistant, is spent waiting for a person, and that reading all of Claude's output would take over half a working week, even outside engineering.

Anthropic said it is building an early warning system for jobs at risk and moves staff internally. Neither step addresses the slowdown in junior hiring, it said.

For a company running SAP, an agent can post an entry the system accepts that is still wrong, such as the wrong account or period. People usually learn to spot those errors in junior roles. Anthropic's figures show junior employment slowing most where Claude does the hardest work.
