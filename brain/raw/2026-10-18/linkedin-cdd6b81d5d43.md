AI isn’t a new concept.
We’ve just given it a new name.

𝗧𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸 𝗮𝗯𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝗮 𝗖𝗘𝗢.

They don’t clean the office themselves.
They pay someone to do it.

They don’t build every Excel sheet themselves.
They pay someone to do it.

And now…

They can pay for a 𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗰 𝗺𝗮𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗲 to perform physical tasks, 𝗔𝗜 𝘀𝘂𝗯𝘀𝗰𝗿𝗶𝗽𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 to create the first version of a presentation, analyse data or automate a repetitive task.

So maybe the real question isn't:

“𝗪𝗶𝗹𝗹 𝗔𝗜 𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗹𝗮𝗰𝗲 𝗵𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻𝘀?”

The better question is:

“𝗪𝗵𝗶𝗰𝗵 𝘁𝗮𝘀𝗸𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼 𝗹𝗼𝗻𝗴𝗲𝗿 𝘄𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗵 𝗱𝗼𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗺𝗮𝗻𝘂𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆?”

Businesses have always paid for people, software and machines to reduce the time spent on work.

AI and robotics are simply taking this one step further.

The interesting part isn't AI itself.

It's how quickly we adapt our definition of “𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸.”

And for professionals, perhaps the biggest advantage won't be knowing AI.

It will be knowing what NOT to do manually anymore.

𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗱𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸?
