Stop getting ready for me.

Seriously.

Don't spend half your Tuesday cleaning up the spreadsheet before you send it.
Don't rename all the images.
Don't hunt down every missing dimension.
Don't spend an hour trying to figure out what the hell “Quantity of Next Lower Level Trade Item” means.

And for the love of all that is holy, do not make a new spreadsheet to organize the other spreadsheets.

That's my mess now.

You make food. You know your products. You know how they're packed. You know who your customer is. And you've probably got most of the information I need scattered across six different places and four different people.

Perfect.

Give me access. Forward me the email. Send me the files. Point me toward the people who know the answers.

Then go do your actual job.

I'll figure out what we've got, what we're missing, what they're asking for, and where it needs to go.

You do not need to make your item setup work presentable before handing it to the Item Setup Guy.

I assure you. I've seen worse.
