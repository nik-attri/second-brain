We Are Hiring!
Operation management specialist 

We are looking to hire an Operation Management Specialist to join our team at EPx, with openings available at site located in El Obour City .

Qualifications:

Bachelor's degree in Business Management, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, or an equivalent discipline. 
Minimum 3–5 years of experience in order operations, order management, customer service, or supply chain, preferably within a Pharmaceutical, Diagnostics, FMCG, or similarly regulated environment. 
Good understanding of end-to-end order management and supply chain processes. 
Working knowledge of ERP systems such as SAP and order processing workflows, including order intake, validation, IDocs, billing, and related transactions. 
Good understanding of inventory management principles and order fulfillment processes. 
Strong customer service, communication, and relationship management skills. 
High level of accuracy and attention to detail, particularly in data entry and order verification. 
Strong analytical, problem-solving, planning, organizing, and prioritization skills. 
Ability to work effectively in a cross-functional environment and manage multiple priorities. 

Requirements:

Manage the end-to-end order management process from order receipt through validation, processing, fulfillment, and closure. 
Receive and validate customer orders to ensure accuracy, completeness, and compliance with agreed requirements. 
Process customer orders through the relevant ERP and order management systems, ensuring accurate and timely system transactions. 
Monitor order status and proactively follow up on pending, delayed, or exceptional orders. 
Coordinate with Warehouse, Inventory, Transport, Customer Service, and other relevant functions to ensure timely order fulfillment. 
Monitor inventory availability and coordinate with relevant teams to address shortages, allocation issues, and other inventory-related constraints. 
Ensure accurate order data entry and maintain high data quality across relevant systems. 
Monitor order processing workflows, including order intake, validation, system processing, IDocs, billing, and related transactions. 
Identify order discrepancies, exceptions, and process issues and coordinate with the relevant stakeholders to ensure timely resolution. 
Provide customers with accurate and timely updates regarding order status, availability, and delivery requirements. 
Maintain effective relationships with customers and internal stakeholders to ensure a high level of service and responsiveness. 
Prepare and maintain order management reports, trackers, and performance data to support operational monitoring and decision-making. 
Analyze order trends, recurring issues, and operational gaps and recommend appropriate corrective actions. 
Ensure compliance with established order management processes, SOPs, and customer-specific requirements.
