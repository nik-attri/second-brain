Meet Ecosir in Riyadh: Smarter Logistics for Better Hospital Care

Less manual handling. Better hygiene. More time for patient care. Ecosir’s Drop & Go™ solutions automate hospital waste and soiled laundry collection, helping healthcare facilities streamline daily operations and reduce staff workload.

Ecosir will be joining our partner Telelift at Global Health Exhibition in Riyadh this October on 26ht till 29th. Come and meet Sami Ojamo and Juhani Ojala at the Telelift Intelligent Technology stand, H2A.H15.

➡️ Let automation take care of waste and laundry ✅ 

Moving waste and soiled laundry through hospital corridors should not get in the way of care. Ecosir’s automated vacuum transfer solutions transport these materials through a sealed pipe network to central collection, reducing manual handling and trolley traffic while supporting hygiene and infection control. Drop & Go is linked throughout the system lifecycle with accurate data to monitor your flows and instant information if something is not working as planned - Stay Connected and keep the flow on! 

Designed around each hospital’s layout and workflows, Drop & Go™ helps create cleaner, more efficient hospital logistics, allowing healthcare professionals to focus on what matters most: patients. 

Whether you’re planning a new hospital or improving an existing one, come and discover what could be next in automated waste and laundry logistics.
