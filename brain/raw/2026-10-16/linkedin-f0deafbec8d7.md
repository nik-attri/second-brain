As AI automates more of our daily decisions, where does human accountability actually end and machine responsibility begin? 

At Digital Marketing Association of the Philippines' DigiCon 2026, over 2,000 industry leaders gathered in Manila under the theme "Interoperability: Welcome to the Age of Collaboration" to tackle how organizations can connect emerging platforms without compromising ethics, privacy, and digital trust. 

Discussions emphasized that true digital transformation goes far beyond speed and tech adoption; it requires seamlessly coordinating data, security, and responsible AI practices across organizations.

Whether applying algorithms to business workflows or navigating consent-based open finance, companies must preserve human judgment, establish clear lines of institutional accountability, and maintain transparency.
