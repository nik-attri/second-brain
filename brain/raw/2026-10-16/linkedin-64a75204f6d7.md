Reading Kyle Poyar and Maja Voje’s 2026 State of B2B GTM report, I couldn't help thinking: much of what's working today is what we've been doing in complex B2B tech for the past 15 years.

Founder-led sales remains dominant, often for too long. Not because founders shouldn't sell, but because too many companies fail to build the GTM fundamentals that make growth repeatable beyond the founder.

Signal-based outbound is gaining traction. We used to do it manually: tracking accounts, organizational changes, buying triggers and the right moment to engage. AI now makes it faster and more scalable. The thinking hasn't changed.

Trusted relationships and intimate events are among the most effective channels. No surprise. A well-curated dinner with the right people can create more value than hundreds of automated messages.

And perhaps most importantly, positioning and messaging are coming back into focus. 
Something I spend a lot of time working on with B2B tech CEOs. 

Because if you haven't figured out who you're selling to, why they should care and why you deserve to win, and build the right GTM choreography around that, more technology will only help you scale confusion.

AI is changing how we execute GTM. Not the fundamentals of why customers buy.

Excellent work by Kyle and Maja. It's refreshing to see data bringing attention back to commercial fundamentals that never stopped mattering.
