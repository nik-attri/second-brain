Over the past few months, I’ve been working on solving a problem that almost every tech professional has faced:

The job search "black hole." 🛑

You spend hours tailoring your resume, filling out the same repetitive forms across 50 different job boards, and waiting weeks - only to get automated rejections or complete silence.

At the same time, recruiters and hiring managers are drowning in 400+ irrelevant resumes for every single open role.

Coming from a Mechatronics Engineering background, I look at problems through systems and automation: when a pipeline is this manual and broken, you build a smarter system to fix it.

Today, I’m excited to officially introduce CVDB (@CVDB) 🚀

CVDB is an AI-powered talent discovery platform built to flip the hiring loop:

✨ Upload Once, Discovered Forever: You upload your CV once. Our AI automatically builds a clean, structured skills profile in seconds.
✨ 98% Smart Match Engine: Instead of dumb keyword matching, our 5-factor engine matches verified technical skills, salary expectations, and work mode.
✨ Direct Recruiter Invites: Verified companies find pre-ranked candidates and send interview invites directly to you.
✨ 100% Privacy Control: Keep your profile completely anonymous to your current employer.
✨ Free AI Job Match Scanner: Paste any Job Description and get an instant 0–100% ATS match score and keyword gap analysis in 10 seconds.

No ghosting. Real-time hiring transparency. 100% free for job seekers.
