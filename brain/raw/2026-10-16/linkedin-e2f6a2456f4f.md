When I joined Dotfile as its first finance hire, most of my time went into getting the data, not using it. Exports, reconciliations, spreadsheets stitched together before any real analysis could start.

Today, most of my work starts with a question. Billing, accounting, CRM, payroll: all connected to my AI assistant. Building our ARR model, running budget vs actuals, preparing investor reporting: the data comes to me, and I spend my time on what it means.

The biggest change isn’t speed. It’s that I can now build things I simply wouldn’t have had the bandwidth for alone.

This week, Dotfile joined that list. With our new MCP connector, compliance teams can work the same way, from the AI tools they already use: automate workflows or update their configuration, all through conversation.

Like finance, compliance often runs with small teams and a lot on their plate. Giving them that same leverage is the part of this release I’m proudest of.
