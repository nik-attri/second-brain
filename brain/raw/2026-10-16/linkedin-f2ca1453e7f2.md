If your team says "check the sheet" ten times a day, this is for you.

Spreadsheets are amazing.
Until they become your operating system.

Signs you're in spreadsheet hell:

→ Three versions of the same file, and nobody knows which is right
→ One person who "knows how the sheet works"
→ Data copied from WhatsApp into Excel by hand
→ Reports that take a full day to prepare

None of this means you did something wrong.
It means your business grew.

The fix isn't always custom software.
Sometimes it's a better tool. Sometimes it's a simple internal system.

But the first step is admitting the sheet is now a bottleneck.

What's the one spreadsheet your business can't live without?
