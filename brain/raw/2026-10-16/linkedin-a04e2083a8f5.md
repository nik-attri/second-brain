Feeling grateful to have been part of an amazing Innovation Forum last week in Chicago. Bringing together over 1,000 customers, prospects, partners, and "Descartians" in one room was truly special.

A key highlight was the announcement of our Descartes Systems Group Agent Control Plane (ACP), our answer to agentic AI and a major step forward in helping organizations automate complex logistics workflows while maintaining human oversight. Lots of great feedback from customers!

Equally valuable were the conversations throughout the event. Hearing directly from customers and partners about how their ecommerce operations are evolving, what's keeping them up at night, and where they're investing for growth provided insights that simply can't be gained anywhere else. These discussions not only help everyone learn, they help build stronger relationships.

Thank you to everyone who I met, shared their perspectives and made our event such a success!

Looking forward to the next opportunity to connect, learn, and innovate together.
