I’m a relationship guy. Give me a room full of people, a good conversation and my Stetson, and I’m pretty much in my natural habitat.

Put me in a room full of spreadsheets, dashboards and people asking, “What does the data tell us?” and apparently we’re at work. 😂

One of the things I appreciate most about Property Masters Inc  is how well we know our business. Twenty two years of experience, and we’re still asking questions, sharpening processes and finding ways to get better.

We’ve been digging into our sales workflow, operations and Dynamics functionality, and we’ve learned that “We had a great conversation” is usually followed by “Awesome. Who owns the next step, when is it happening, and did you put it in the system?”

Fair questions. All three of them.

Meanwhile, I’m over here channeling my inner Samuel L. Jackson, wallet and all, feeling like a badass. Knowing that we are doing what we should be doing to help grow the business. Then my boss and killer CEO reminds me that even a badass team needs a solid workflow and transparent process. 

That’s how we turn big promises into the details that matter on every project.

Relationships open doors. Having an organized sales strategy behind us helps turn those conversations into commitments we can actually deliver on.

So here’s to the data wranglers who keep us relationship people honest and give us the tools we need.  We bring the “hell yes!” They bring the “here’s how!” 😂

And our customers get the benefit of both. 💚
