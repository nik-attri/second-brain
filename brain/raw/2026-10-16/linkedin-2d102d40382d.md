DAY 3/30: Stop Using AI Only to Write. Start Using It to Create.

Imagine selling clothes online.

You have your product. You have the pictures. But your product display doesn't look as professional as the brands you admire.

A professional photoshoot might be expensive. Hiring a designer for every product might not be practical.

Meanwhile, you're using AI to write captions and asking it questions.
But what if you could do more?

With the right AI tools and instructions, you can transform an existing clothing photograph into a polished product display featuring a faceless mannequin, professional studio lighting, and a premium background.

No need to start by buying a camera or learning complex design software.
Here's how to try it yourself:

1. Upload a clear picture of your actual product to an AI image-generation or image-editing tool.
2. Tell the AI to preserve the product's original colour, design, texture, and details.
3. Describe the display you want, such as a luxury studio setting, a faceless mannequin, and professional lighting.
4. Specify the format you need, such as a 3:4 vertical image for social media.
5. Review the result carefully. AI can alter product details, so ensure the final image accurately represents what you're selling.

Then experiment with different backgrounds and display styles.
