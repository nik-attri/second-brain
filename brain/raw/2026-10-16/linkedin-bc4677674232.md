One thing I’ve always wished CRMs did: look across everything I’ve already recorded and suggest what needs my attention next.

Keeping a task list up to date takes work in itself.

You have to remember what to add, change the date when plans move and notice when something in a record makes an old task more urgent.

I’d like to explore what happens if a CRM can review that information and pull useful next steps into view.

If your notes say a seller is giving their estate agent another month, it could bring that conversation back into view when the month is up, without you having to set another reminder manually.

You’d still decide what to do and when. The suggestion could help you spot opportunities to follow up that you might otherwise miss.

We’re exploring this as an idea for a purpose-built CRM for UK property sourcers, and I’d like to understand where it would be useful in a real working day.

What would you want a CRM to surface that your current one misses?
