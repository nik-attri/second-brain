When support starts to feel like it's drowning, the first instinct is to hire. 

Sometimes that's the right call. A lot of the time, though, new people walk into the same chaos and just drown a little slower.

So here are 5 signs your support team needs structure before it needs more headcount 
(swipe through for what each one looks like and how to fix it):

- The same questions keep landing in the queue
- Every escalation ends with one person
- Nobody can tell you why customers are reaching out
- Your team repeats the same manual steps all day
- Customers find you in five places and nobody owns any of them

Each of these has a structural fix: a knowledge base, clear tiers and escalation paths, consistent ticket tagging, a few smart automations, and an owner for every channel. 

The biggest thing my experience has taught me is that structure gives people room to own their work. 

That's still how we build teams at Virtua. 

Great remote teams come from empowering people, and the right foundation is what makes that possible.
