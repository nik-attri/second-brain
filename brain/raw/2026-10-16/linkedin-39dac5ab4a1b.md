AI personalization has fragmented our digital lives, but nobody talks about it.

We try to be efficient using many apps, each with its own AI, but they don't communicate.

The real advance isn't just better algorithms. It's a mobile, multi-agent AI system that lives with us, learning our habits and anticipating needs across platforms.

Imagine an AI stack that understands a morning routine from a calendar, suggests articles based on a current project from notes, and pre-fills meeting agendas based on recent communications. We wouldn't have to switch apps or manually enter data.

This isn't about one super-app. It's an integrated system of specialized agents, each good at a task, but managed by a central intelligence that prioritizes a workflow.

It’s the ultimate personal assistant, not stuck on one device or service, but truly part of how we work and live.
