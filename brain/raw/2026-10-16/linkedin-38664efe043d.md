Ten years. One Sunshine Coast office. A whole lot of families in homes — and a bloke who's probably checking golf scores between appointments. I want to introduce you to Adam Williams of MoneyQuest Australia's Maroochydore Franchise — one of the OG's of our franchise group, and this year's Qld Regional Franchise of the Year. 🏆 

And here's the bit that makes it mean something: this isn't his first time holding that trophy. He's won it before. Winning once can be a good year. Winning it again is a pattern — ten years in, still setting the benchmark, still the one everyone else in the region is measured against. That's consistency, and it's much harder to earn than a single big year. Adam came to broking the long way round. Twenty-plus years in finance, including time at eChoice and helpmechoose.com.au, before he backed himself and opened MoneyQuest Maroochydore. That was over a decade ago. In an industry that's changed about six times since then, Adam just kept doing the same thing: picking up the phone, learning something new, and treating every client like the only one he's got. If you've ever tried to get a loan as a self-employed business owner, you already know why his clients love him. That's his patch. Complex structures, unusual income, SMSF lending, commercial and equipment finance — the deals other brokers hand back, Adam takes on. He's built a business that people send their friends to, and there's no marketing budget on earth that buys that. 

Off the clock, Adam is exactly the same bloke. He'll take a trip anywhere the map allows, he's got a genuine golf habit, he'll talk fast cars with anyone who'll listen, and there is no situation on earth that calls for boring Nikes. Sneaker rotation is serious business. Some people decompress with a spreadsheet — Adam decompresses travelling somewhere sunny or cold, chasing a little white ball or a fresh pair of kicks. 🏌️👟🏎️✈️ What I love most is that he'd rather tell you about his clients wins than his own. His words, not mine: he controls the things he can control, and doesn't waste a minute on the rest. That's not a slogan — that's how he runs his business. Adam, ten years in and still raising the bar. Congratulations mate — thoroughly deserved.
