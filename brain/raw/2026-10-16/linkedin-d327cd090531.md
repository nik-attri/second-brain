We cut CPC by 48% in 2 months using a new creative‑scaling framework. The second week, our ROAS jumped from 3.2x to 5.8x.

A native ad team was drowning in creative fatigue — over 30 variants sprouting from the same headline, no clear hierarchy, and each costing $12 CPM. The dashboard showed nothing useful, and the account was heading for burn‑out.

I built a rank‑based creative engine that assigns each asset a “score” using weighted keyword importance and title‑case consistency. First, we parse every headline, tag it with a priority score (SEO keyword weight × audience intent). Next, we generate three headline variants per rank: a direct call, a benefit‑driven, and a curiosity hook. Finally, we feed the ranked list into a meta‑asset pipeline that automatically tags, stores, and re‑uses the top 5 per campaign. The process runs in under 5 minutes and scales across 12 countries.

After implementing, we cut CPC from $0.92 to $0.48, bringing CPM down to $6.3. The average frequency dropped by 35%, and the total spend fell by $18,000 while conversions rose 27%.
