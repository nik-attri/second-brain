If onboarding a new client depends on someone remembering what to do next, you don't have a process. You have a memory test. 👀

Recently, I created a Client Onboarding SOP designed to make the onboarding process more structured, consistent, and easier to follow.

Because getting a new client is exciting.

But what happens immediately after they say YES matters.

Who sends the welcome email?

Where are their details recorded?

Who sends the contract and invoice?

When is payment confirmed?

What information needs to be collected?

Who sets up their workspace or project?

When does the actual work begin?

Without a documented process, its easy for important steps to get missed, especially as a business grows.

So I created an SOP that turns client onboarding into a clear, repeatable process instead of something the team has to figure out every time.

And that's one thing I enjoy about operations:

I don't just look at what needs to get done.

I think about how we can make sure it gets done consistently, even as the business grows.

If your team keeps repeating the same process manually, relying on memory, or constantly asking, “What do we do next?”

You may not need more people yet.

You may need a documented process.

Im currently open to helping businesses organize workflows, document processes, and create systems that make execution easier.
