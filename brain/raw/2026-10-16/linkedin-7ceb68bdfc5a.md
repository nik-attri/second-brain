🚨 We’re Hiring | Store & Stock Manager - Garment Export Industry 👕📦

Samshek Fashion Pvt. Ltd. is looking for an experienced Store & Stock Manager to manage our Stores & Inventory operations at our Noida Sector 65 facility.
📍 Location: Noida Sector 65 | Onsite
💼 Employment Type: Full-Time
🏢 Department: Stores & Inventory
👗 Industry: Garments Export

🔹 Key Responsibilities
-Maintain accurate stock records for fabrics, trims, threads, buttons, labels, packaging materials & accessories.
-Manage material inward/outward entries and GRNs.
-Issue materials to production against requisitions and maintain proper records.
-Conduct regular physical stock checks and reconcile with system records.Update inventory transactions in Zoho Inventory/Zoho Books in real time.
-Monitor shortages, slow-moving materials and reordering requirements.
-Ensure FIFO is followed for material issuance.Maintain a clean, organized and traceable store.
-Coordinate with Production, Procurement & Quality teams for smooth material flow.
-Prepare inventory reports, stock registers and Excel-based trackers.
-Take ownership of overall store discipline, stock accuracy and material traceability.

🔹 What We’re Looking For:
✅ 3+ years of experience in stores/inventory, preferably in a garment export house
✅ Strong knowledge of garment raw materials, fabrics, trims amp; accessories
✅ Good command of MS Excel – VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, data entry amp; stock formats
✅ Working knowledge of Zoho Inventory/Zoho Books – Must
✅ Strong stock management and reconciliation skills
✅ Good communication and team coordination skills
✅ Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
✅ Ability to manage work independently and under pressure

🎓 Preferred QualificationDiploma/Graduate in Textile Technology, Garment Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Inventory Management or a relevant field.
