If your data isn’t clear to you, it sure as hell isn’t clear to the AI.

Every broker I talk to wants to know what AI can do for them. AI is all the hype right now. But the quality of AI depends almost entirely on two things: 

1. The quality of your data
2. The quality of the workflow it sits on top of

Here’s an example we see more often than you'd think:
A broker shows us a spreadsheet. In one row, the name, company, and mobile number are jammed into a single cell. In the next row, they’re split across three columns. This continues for 1,000+ rows.

Point AI at that and it will scale the mess right along with everything else. This is what leads to inaccurate or hallucinating AI. 

Data and context are the engine of AI. 

A clean spreadsheet with 600 records beats a messy spreadsheet or CRM with 10,000 inaccurate or inconsistent rows.
