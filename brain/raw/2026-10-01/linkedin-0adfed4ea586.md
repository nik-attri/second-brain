A lot of finance teams managing large-scale cashflow are still doing it in Excel.

That becomes a real problem fast. No one can say with confidence when a loan needs to be drawn, when preshipment financing should go in, or what cash will look like two weeks out. 

Every number gets pulled manually before a management meeting, and by the time it's presented, it's already outdated.

What finance teams actually need is one place to see cash, AR and project-linked financing together, with enough forecasting to get ahead of a loan before it's due instead of reacting to it.

That's what we build with ebizframe.ai. Cash flow, receivables and inventory health sit on one dashboard, with #predictiveintelligence that flags when financing will be needed, tied to the projects driving it. 

EVA, our AI Co-Pilot assistant, lets you simply ask when the next loan is due or what cash looks like next month, instead of waiting on a report.

The real shift isn't the dashboard. It's that the finance team spends its time deciding what to do about a number, instead of producing the number.

If you're managing cashflow at scale, is forecasting still manual for you, or have you moved past it?
