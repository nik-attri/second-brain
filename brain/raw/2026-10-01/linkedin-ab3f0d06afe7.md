Many of my conversations in September followed the same pattern.

A company learns it has S$10,000 in SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit expiring on 30 November. The first instinct is to find a course, any course, before the deadline.

But the companies getting the most out of it are asking a different question: what work does my team repeat every week that AI could take off their hands?

A finance team spends Friday afternoons turning system exports into a summary for their manager. An operations lead chases reports from every outlet before the weekly meeting. An HR executive retypes the same onboarding notes for every new hire.

When training is built around tasks like these, people leave with something they can use the next morning, not just a certificate.

Two things that often surprise employers:

1. SFEC is a reimbursement. You pay the course fee first, and the credit is paid back to your company's PayNow account after the course.
2. Training can be built around your own team. With 8 or more participants, a private class can use your team's actual tasks, documents and tools instead of generic examples.

The deadline matters. But "how do we use it up?" is the wrong question. "What should our team stop doing manually?" is a better one.

If your company still has SFEC to use before 30 November, I'm happy to help you work out how to make the most of it.
