My working day is less 'consultant in a blazer' and more 'professional problem-solver with snacks.' 😂

A completely realistic day in the life of a self-employed social care consultant, not just any consultant, me...:
7:03am
Answering a client message while simultaneously making toddler breakfast.
Multitasking level: questionable.
Commitment level: excellent.

8:21am
Coffee.
This is not technically a work task, but it is absolutely part of the operational plan.

9:14am
CQC application. Forms. Supporting documents. Business plan.
The usual. 

10:27am
Arguing with a policy. Snacks.
Not literally arguing. Although sometimes it feels like it.
“Why does this say you do this?” “Because that's what the policy says.” “Yes… but do you actually do it?”
👀

11:46am
Business planning.
Looking at the service, the numbers, staffing, systems, how it's actually going to work and whether the person launching the business has accidentally created themselves a 17-hour working day.
Because sometimes the business plan needs to protect the owner too.

1:02pm
“Have you actually got evidence of that?”
My most-used sentence.
Probably...

2:30pm
Client call. Registration questions. Operational questions.
“What happens if CQC asks this?” “Does this policy cover that?” “Can we change this?”
“Yes.” “No.” “Let's have a look.”
😂

4:17pm
Completely different problem.
Because apparently being a consultant means nobody has ever heard of staying in your lane.
Registration → governance → staffing → policies → management → “oh, and while I've got you…”
And somehow we're now discussing something completely unrelated to what was on my calendar.

6:02pm
Close laptop.
Probably.

8:47pm
Think about a client problem while doing absolutely nothing related to work.
Suddenly… THAT'S IT. The solution. The thing I couldn't work out all afternoon.
Obviously.

9:43pm
Opening the laptop again because apparently my brain has chosen this moment to become extremely productive.
Write down the idea before I forget it. Then another idea appears. Then another.
This is why my Notes app is essentially a graveyard of half-written thoughts, random business ideas and sentences beginning with: “Could make this into a…”
😂
And somewhere amongst all of this, the actual work gets done.
Applications get submitted. Businesses get built. Problems get untangled. Documents get rewritten. Clients get a little less stressed.

And occasionally someone goes from:
“I have absolutely no idea what I'm doing.”
to:
“Okay. I think I've got this.”

And honestly?

That's probably my favourite part of the job.
Never the blazer. Not the laptop. Not even the spreadsheets.

It's being the person someone can call when everything feels a bit overwhelming and helping them turn the “where the hell do I start?” into an actual plan.

So yes…

I'm a consultant.

But I'm also a mum, a problem-solver, a policy interrogator, a professional overthinker and someone who apparently does their best thinking at 9:43pm.
Very glamorous. 😂
