Most finance and pricing teams share the exact same nightmare: it is Friday afternoon, margins are tightening, and a CFO asks why packaging or freight costs went up 😬

Suddenly, an analyst is trapped in Excel hell 🔥, opening 14 different browser tabs to pull energy indexes, metals, polymers, freight rates, and central bank exchange rates. The result is usually a Frankenstein spreadsheet held together by broken formulas 🧟‍♂️ that nobody fully trusts.

We built ScrapeMonk Commodity because we got tired of watching smart people do manual data janitor work.

It puts public cost indexes into one clean workspace: energy, metals, polymers, steel, packaging, freight, agriculture, currencies, and macro indicators, with decades of clean history. Instead of paying tens of thousands for Bloomberg terminal seats you do not actually need, you get the public statistical series that actually drive your costs.

A few things that make it genuinely different:

✨ You decide what is a direct input in your bill of materials and what is just contextual noise around it. No black-box guessing, you set the logic.

✨ You can look at major world events since 1973 across conflicts, trade shocks, and pandemics, and see exactly how your specific indexes moved in the 30 days after.

✨ Because we love building tools that feel like magic, you can build and manage your dashboards through our MCP server just by asking Claude or Cursor in plain English.

✨ When you need an executive overview, AI Insights summarizes the trends. From there, clean data flows straight into PriceMonk to calculate contract clauses, or into your own BI tools.

It replaces fragile spreadsheets, manual analyst pulls, and static PDFs nobody reads. If you already have a trading terminal, keep it, you can import your licensed data right alongside everything here.
