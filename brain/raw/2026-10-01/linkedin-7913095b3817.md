You have a “what the hell is happening to our leads?” problem.

I know because I built a system for this exact problem while working in corporate.

We were generating leads. Lots of them.

From campaigns. Events. Field activities. Digital channels. Referrals.

The leads were coming in, and the sales team was talking to prospects.

People were following up.

But every time I wanted to understand what was actually happening inside the pipeline, I had to piece the story together from different places.

1. How many leads came in?
2. How many had been contacted?
3. How many were qualified?
4. Who was following up with which prospect?
5. Which leads were still active?
6. Which ones had gone cold?
7. How many were likely to convert?
8. And where exactly were we losing people?

The frustrating part was that the information wasn't organised into something you could look at and immediately understand.

Because you can have 500 leads sitting somewhere and still have absolutely no idea what your sales pipeline looks like.

So I did what I tend to do when a process is messy.

I built a system.

I created a simple way to capture the lead, assign ownership, track the stage, record follow-ups and see the numbers without having to reconstruct the entire sales process every time someone asked for an update.

Since I started my business consultation service, speaking to business owners who were spending money on ads, getting enquiries through WhatsApp, collecting contacts at events or receiving referrals, but couldn't confidently tell me what happened after the first interaction.

Like they had leads, but they just didn't have visibility.

- Some were keeping everything in WhatsApp.

- Some had an Excel sheet that hadn't been updated in three weeks.

- Some had multiple spreadsheets.

- Some were relying entirely on the salesperson's memory.

And some were simply saying, “We get a lot of leads, but sales are not really reflecting it.”

If you don't know your lead numbers, follow-up numbers, pipeline and conversion rate, you are not really managing sales; you are managing assumptions.

That is when I decided to turn the system I had built for myself into something other businesses could actually use.

That is how the Sales Lead System came about.

Because I had already experienced the problem, built a solution for it, and realised there were a lot more businesses trying to solve the same problem with scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats and crossed fingers.

And if you can't read that data, you can't properly manage the process.

Know your leads. Know your pipeline. Know your numbers.
