You might not know this but the secret to creativity is walking the dog and checking the bus timetable.
At least, according to Bradley Charbonneau.

Bradley is a writer, creator, YouTuber, former advertising guy and general maker-of-things who has published 40 books and uploaded thousands of videos.

His current obsession?
Becoming a “mathemagician.”

Yeah, a Bradley-ism! Meaning: finding the balance between the math — habits, routines, structure, deadlines — and the magic — instinct, play, accidents and those weird moments when an idea drops out of nowhere.

But I should probably let Bradley say it in his own words:
“I’m open to the magic… but could you give me the bus schedule anyway so I could check it?”

What a way to run a creative business!
His point is that you can wait for inspiration OR you can build the conditions that make inspiration useful when it turns up.

So map it:
* Create regularly.
* Know when your brain works best.
* Build habits that make starting easier.
* Get the bloody thing finished.
And then, leave enough room for something unexpected to happen.
