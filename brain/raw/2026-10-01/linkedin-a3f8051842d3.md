We used to spend hours writing automation rules across different tools. Half the time they'd break or not quite fit how we actually worked.

Then we realized the problem wasn't the automation itself—it was that we were trying to force generic workflows into specific team needs. Our approval process wasn't like anyone else's. Our handoff between sales and operations had quirks. Our decision logic had context.

So we started building custom agents instead of configuring templates. Define the exact rules your team follows. Assign ownership so decisions land with the right person. Automate the judgment calls, not just the busywork.

It sounds complex, but the no-code agent builder in monday.com made it feel straightforward. Thirty minutes in, we had our first agent running—handling status updates and routing tasks based on actual conditions we cared about, not what some software company guessed we'd want.

The difference? Our workflows stopped fighting us.
