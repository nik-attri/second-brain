The brief for us was simple: throw our hat in the ring, submit the tender and bring our city BACK TO LIFE.

And from the minute we decided to go for it, there was no way I was giving up on this one.

Our vision was clear from the beginning. This wasn’t about creating an event for us, it was about creating something for our city.

Something that supported multiple small businesses at a time when our local economy needs it. Something built around simpler times family fun, community, live entertainment and thousands of people gathering together in the heart of a city we LOVE.

For us, this was so much more than a “job”.

Our GetBranded team might be small, but I tell you what, we make up for it in creativity, banter, muscle, big ideas and a pretty relentless never-say-never attitude.

We also happen to thrive on short notice 😉

From a tender submission and an idea on paper, to 15,000+ people filling Flinders Street, I’m incredibly proud of our team and what we delivered from start to finish.

The long days, hundreds of moving parts, phone calls, spreadsheets, problem solving, lifting, shifting, laughing and occasional “how the hell are we going to pull this off?” moments were all worth it when we stood there and watched Townsville come alive.

This is why we do what we do.

Proud of our team. Proud of our city. And very bloody proud of this one. ❤️

If you need an event done in Townsville, you know who to call.
