Mass outreach is getting more annoying and less effective. Here is WHY and HOW TO FIX IT


Prospects get the same spray and pray messages every day, and because of that they became reluctant to cold outreach in general.

Here is the real problem.

Around 3% of the market is buying now. The other 97% is not.
And everybody is competing for the same 3%.

Salespeople are still trained to find clients through volume. Send more, hope someone replies.

So how do you compete for the 3% if spray and pray does not work?

We call it trigger based outreach, some call it signal based.
We look for signals that tell us, this prospect may be buying now.

Triggers we look for:
-New regulation
-Public expression of the problem
-A negative review or negative statement
-Recruitment
-Funding
-Many more

When we see a trigger, we approach the client with a specific message, about a specific problem and a specific solution.

The catch is research. Finding these triggers manually costs a lot of time.

That is why we built our own platform. It helps us target the triggers, and it helps us run the outreach.
