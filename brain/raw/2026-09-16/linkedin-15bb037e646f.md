Reviewing a transfer transcript used to take weeks for a university's admissions team.

Now it takes seconds.

Most colleges take two to eight weeks to complete a transfer credit evaluation.

This university was reviewing transcripts across 8,000+ courses, manually, every cycle.

Every transfer student meant cross-checking credits by hand.
Different formats. Different grading scales. Different course names for the same content.

One misread course, and a student gets the wrong credit decision.

We built a private AI system using Claude Vision to handle this instead.

The model reads the transcript directly from the document, no manual data entry.
It extracts course names, credit hours, and grades, and maps each one to the university's own credit equivalency system.

Flags mismatches instead of letting them slip through.

The error rate dropped by 99%.
Reviews that took weeks now take seconds.

And no student record ever left the university's own environment. The entire system runs inside their private infrastructure.

This is the kind of build we do at Abstrabit.
Private AI, engineered around sensitive data, built to actually hold up in production.
