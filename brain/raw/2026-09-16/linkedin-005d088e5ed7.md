🌍 ✨  CUSTOMER SPOTLIGHT | BSH Hausgeräte GmbH✨🌍

How do you integrate laser marking into an existing labeling workflow, without recreating every design in proprietary laser software?

That was the challenge Prolaso helped solve for BSH Home Appliances Group!
BSH uses TEKLYNX CODESOFT to create artwork for both label printing and laser marking. PDF files are generated using the standard Microsoft PDF printer driver, while TEKLYNX SENTINEL manages the workflow.

From there, the Prolaso PDF to Coherent Converter takes over.
⚙️ The converter continuously monitors the designated input folder
⚙️ Newly generated PDF files are detected automatically
⚙️ Files are converted within seconds into the native Coherent VLM format
⚙️ Laser parameters and hatchings are generated automatically
⚙️ The converted files are ready for use with the Coherent laser

BSH can therefore continue working with its established CODESOFT and SENTINEL environment, without manually rebuilding artwork in separate laser software.
The result is a fully automated workflow with no manual intervention, greater consistency and a reduced risk of errors.
This is where Prolaso creates value: not by replacing a manufacturer’s existing production environment, but by connecting the systems that already work.

We sincerely thank the entire BSH team and bystronic rofin for their trust and for allowing us to share this successful collaboration. We look forward to continuing our partnership.
