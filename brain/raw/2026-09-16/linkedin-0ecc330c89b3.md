Let me gist you: for a hot minute there, I almost threw in the towel on 'AI automation' for my own business.

I’d been sold on the promise – 'Automate everything! Be 10x more productive!' So, I dived headfirst, bought into a shiny new AI tool, and tried to automate a multi-step, deeply messy client onboarding process.

Big mistake. Huge. 

The tool was powerful, yes. But my process? It was a spaghetti junction of manual hand-offs, inconsistent data, and 'we'll figure it out' moments. The AI didn't 'fix' it; it just amplified the chaos, creating more errors faster than I could blink. I felt like I was drowning, convinced AI automation was just another pipe dream for big tech, not for solo operators like me.

I was frustrated. I was ready to quit. But then, a moment of clarity hit me: The problem wasn't the AI. The problem was my *process*.

I took a deep breath, stepped back, and decided to tackle it differently. I spent days mapping out that messy onboarding, simplifying each step, eliminating redundancies, and establishing clear rules *before* even thinking about AI again. 

Only then, with a clean, streamlined process in hand, did I re-introduce AI. And guess what? It worked. Flawlessly. The AI now had a clear, reliable pathway to follow, amplifying efficiency instead of multiplying mess.

This is the real gist of AI automation: it’s not about finding the fanciest tool, it’s about having a solid, calm system first. AI is an amplifier. If your system is messy, AI amplifies the mess. If your system is reliable, AI amplifies the reliability.

I'm still here. Still building. Still learning. And still advocating for process first.
