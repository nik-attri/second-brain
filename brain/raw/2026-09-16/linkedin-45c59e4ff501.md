Ever found yourself in PowerPoint hell?
 
Hours searching images, formatting financials, "improving" the deck while you're sitting outside the board meeting minutes before it's presented? If you're a recovering perfectionist like me, you know PowerPoint's infinite options can eat a day. And procrastination always came along for the ride. But... not lately.
 
Watched a video on procrastination this morning. Made me think about why I've been running into it less and less.
 
With AI, I'm more in the role of a Producer now. 

Yesterday I helped a friend work through a real business problem — membership data, financials, a pile of CSVs that normally means hours of dread before I even open the first spreadsheet. Instead we fed it into AI and asked questions in plain English. Charts, breakdowns, analysis — in real time. My job was to assign it, review it, push it further.

Different than the job of grinding through cells one at a time. Usually creating new spreadsheets as went. 
 
Procrastination probably doesn't disappear. But the switch to reviewing and managing output, instead of constantly generating it, has truly made a difference. It's better, faster, cleaner, and keeps me in the realm of ideas and insight, instead of grinding execution. 

I haven't created a PPT presentation in more than a year. 

I don't miss it.
