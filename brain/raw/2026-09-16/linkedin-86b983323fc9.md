DAY 25 

Imagine opening the internet today…

and there was no Google.

No “Google it.”

No search bar answering your random 2AM questions. 😂

You want information?

Good luck finding it.

But that's basically the problem people faced as the Web started growing.

At first, there weren't that many websites.

You could actually keep track of them more easily.

But as more people started creating websites…

the Web became crowded.

More pages.

More information.

More links.

More websites.

And suddenly:

“Where do I find what I'm looking for?”

became a serious problem.

So people started building ways to organize and search the growing Web.

Some early services worked more like directories.

You could browse categories and find websites that had been manually organized.

Think:

Internet Yellow Pages. 😂

But manually organizing the entire Web wasn't going to scale forever.

There were simply too many pages being created.

And that's where search engines became increasingly important.

Eventually, one search engine would change the game completely.

You probably know its name.

👀

But before we get to Google…

let's appreciate how strange the early Web was.

Imagine telling someone today:

“Don't worry, I'll send you a list of websites you might like.”

Instead of:

“Just search it.” 😂

Technology doesn't just change what we can do.

Sometimes it changes what we consider normal.

Tomorrow:

**How did search engines actually find things on the Web?**
