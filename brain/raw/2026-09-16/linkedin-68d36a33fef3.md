The Manual Workaround Files
Case File #3: "We'll just hire someone to handle the data entry."

It sounds like a reasonable fix. The systems don't talk, so you add a person to bridge the gap. Problem solved, right?

Except that person isn't solving the disconnect. They're absorbing it. Every re-key is a chance for a typo, a dropped row, a number that doesn't match by the time it lands in the second system. And the job doesn't stay the same size. As the company grows, so does the pile of manual entry, until you're not hiring one person to cover the gap, you're hiring a small team to do a computer's job.

The real fix was never "add headcount." It's asking why two systems that both matter to the business still can't share information on their own.

Has your team ever hired to patch a problem that was really a systems problem in disguise?
