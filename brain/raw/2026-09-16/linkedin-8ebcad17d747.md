I want to make the investment.

I am also terrified it will not work.

There. That is the honest version.

I am considering a year of one-on-one coaching to help me build a kick-ass keynote and step onto a global stage.

The opportunity makes my heart pound. So does the cost.

At first, I kept asking myself: Should I do it?

That question left me spinning because it demanded a perfect answer about an unpredictable future.

A better question finally moved me forward: What would have to be true for this to become a responsible yes?

Now I can work with something real: the budget, the time, the goal, and what I will need to do for the investment to have a fair chance.

This week's Hell Yes Edit is for every woman who has researched the opportunity to death when the deeper question is whether she trusts herself to use it.
