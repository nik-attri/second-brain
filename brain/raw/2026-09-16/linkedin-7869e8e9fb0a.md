The Indian media agency recruitment ecosystem has a Job Description problem: 

Expecting a five-person department rolled into one junior professional—at entry-level compensation.
Take a hard look at what a standard 2–4 years experience requirement now looks like:
 Account Director Duties: Own full client relationships, manage senior stakeholders, and coordinate cross-functional teams.
 Senior Strategist Duties: Drive integrated campaigns across OTT, e-commerce, gaming, and emerging tech/AI.
 Creative Director Duties: Ideate award-winning launches and write festival-worthy case studies.
 Ad-Ops/Analyst Duties: Flawless tactical media execution, UTM tracking, and endless reporting decks.
All of this packaged under a junior title, demanding 3 years of tenure, and budgeted at a standard 2–4 year CTC band—often just ₹5 to ₹8 LPA in an expensive city like Mumbai.
The math simply doesn’t work.
To genuinely master cross-vertical strategy across six disparate ecosystems (gaming, OTT, search, social, programmatic, and AI), a marketer needs exposure, time, and structured leadership. Instead, the ground reality for someone with 3 years in an Indian agency looks completely different:
 Pulling raw data at 9:00 PM because the client needs a pacing update "right now."
 Getting zero mentorship from managers who are themselves drowning in unmanaged deadlines.
 Being treated as a manual data pipeline whose sole mandate is to "run the tool and vomit the numbers into PowerPoint."
You cannot starve employees of strategic mentorship, chain them to day-to-day dashboard grunt work, and then put out job posts expecting a full-funnel digital visionary on a junior paycheck.
If an agency wants someone who can autonomously strategize across every emerging platform and win industry awards, they need to hire for senior capability and pay market rates for it. And if they want a 3-year specialist, they need to build an environment that actually trains them rather than burning them out under impossible scope creep.
