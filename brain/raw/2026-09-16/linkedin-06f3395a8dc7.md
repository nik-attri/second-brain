If you sell across marketplaces and run Odoo for accounting and inventory, here's how the two actually connect. 

Base sits between your marketplaces and Odoo. Orders come in from wherever you sell, get turned into one clean format, and pass through to Odoo as sales orders, invoices, or both. Stock and pricing flow back the other way, so Odoo stays your source of truth and every channel reflects it.

Base isn't a replacement for Odoo. It's the piece that lets Odoo do its job without someone manually reconciling five marketplaces by hand.  

We put together a short video and a full breakdown of what syncs, what doesn't, and how to set it up.
