The only SaaS businesses left to build:

1. AI-native vertical SaaS
Not a chatbot bolted onto a dashboard. The product is the agent. The CRM is just the database.

2. Workflow collapse tools
One painful SaaS job (onboarding, renewals, reporting, support triage) end to end. Outcome pricing, not per seat.

3. Proprietary data loops
Your moat is the dataset the model keeps learning from. Clone the UI and you still lose.

4. Distribution-first SaaS
Same model quality as everyone else. You win because you own the audience and the channel.

5. Vertical agents for boring industries
Insurance ops. Property management. Specialty logistics. Spreadsheet hell with budget.

Most founders still ship another horizontal AI wrapper.

The money is in niche SaaS that owns a workflow, a dataset, or a distribution wedge.
