With AI being very topical at the moment, it doesn't mean your business automation and systems have to use it.

With AI seemingly being added to everything at the moment, it's easy to assume that digitising or automating a business process means introducing artificial intelligence.

More often than not, it doesn't.  Of course, I have integrated AI into numerous systems but some of the most useful improvements I've developed for businesses have been much simpler:

• automatically sending information at the right time
• moving data between different systems
• creating tasks when something happens
• sending reminders when something hasn't happened
• connecting to a third-party service without someone having to log in and re-enter information
• taking a repetitive process that happens 50 times a week and making it happen automatically

None of that necessarily needs AI. It just needs someone to look at the process and ask: "Why are we still doing this manually?"

AI can be incredibly useful when there's genuinely a job for it to do.

But sometimes the smartest solution isn't artificial intelligence.... it's simply good software.
