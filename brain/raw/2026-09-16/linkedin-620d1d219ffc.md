Every month, hundreds of employee expense claims came in as photos on WhatsApp, Excel sheets, and paper bills. Finance took 12–15 days to reimburse anyone. Duplicate claims slipped through. Staff were frustrated, and the finance team was exhausted.

They came to us with one ask: "Make this painless."

We deployed Cling Invoice, our employee reimbursement automation platform, customised for their approval hierarchy across regions.

What changed:

Employees now upload a bill from their phone in under a minute. OCR reads the amount, date, and vendor automatically.

Managers approve with one tap. Policy limits are enforced before a claim even reaches finance.

Every claim has a full audit trail. Duplicates are flagged instantly.

The results after 90 days:

Reimbursement cycle dropped from 15 days to 3.
Invoice processing time reduced by 70%.
Duplicate and out-of-policy claims cut by over 90%.
Finance freed up nearly 120 hours a month.

The best feedback came from a store manager: "I finally stopped chasing finance for my own money."
