📑 JOB OPENING | ADMINISTRATIVE INTERN

SAASA B2E  is hiring an Administrative Intern to support its operations in Douala, Cameroon.

The role is suited to an organised, proactive and detail-oriented candidate interested in gaining hands-on experience in administration, operations and coordination.

Key Responsibilities
• Support day-to-day office administration and operational activities
• Manage emails, correspondence, filing and administrative documentation
• Prepare reports, presentations and meeting materials
• Carry out data entry and maintain company records
• Handle administrative errands across Douala, including visits to banks, government offices and other institutions
• Liaise with vendors, suppliers, service providers and other stakeholders
• Support meetings, record minutes and follow up on action points
• Assist with cross-functional projects and operational coordination

Profile
• Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Management, Project Management, Operations Management or a related field
• 1–2 years of experience in administration, operations, project coordination or a similar role
• Strong organisational, planning and problem-solving skills
• Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
• Proficiency in Microsoft Office
• Fluency in both English and French is mandatory
• Professional, adaptable and able to work independently and collaboratively

What the Internship Offers.
• Hands-on experience in administrative operations
• Professional mentorship
• Exposure to a dynamic business-services environment
• Possibility of full-time employment based on performance

📍 Location: Douala, Cameroon
💼 Type: Internship
💰 Compensation: 70,000–100,000 FCFA
