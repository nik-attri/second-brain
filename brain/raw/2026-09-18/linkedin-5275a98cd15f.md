McKinsey’s latest State of AI report (https://lnkd.in/eADN79Cd) highlights the gap:

• Only around 6% qualify as AI “high performers.”
• High performers are more likely to redesign workflows and transform how their businesses operate, rather than simply adding AI tools to existing processes.
