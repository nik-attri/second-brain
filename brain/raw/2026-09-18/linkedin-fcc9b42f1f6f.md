Where do you take your managers to blow off some steam after another week in this bitch of a multifamily market?

Apparently, you hand them axes and point them toward a wall.

Rents are down, expenses are up, concessions are everywhere, occupancy is a street fight, owners want answers and lenders want miracles.

So tonight, no spreadsheets, no variance reports, no delinquency calls and no “what the hell happened to occupancy?”

Just axes.

Cheaper than therapy and a hell of a lot more satisfying than another Teams meeting. And I love seeing who the killers are. You learn a lot about your team when you put an axe in their hands.

Great night with the LYND team. Sometimes leadership means getting everybody out of the office and letting them throw sharp objects at something that isn’t the budget.
