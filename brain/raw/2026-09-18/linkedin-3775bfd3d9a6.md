When an RFQ drops for a multi-million rand show, every supplier in the industry loses their mind.

Everyone fights for it. You slash your margins, undercut the competition, and push to be the most cost-effective option on the table.

And you win! Hell yeah.

You just won a project with a mountain of upfront expenses. By the time the show is over, your bottom line has barely moved. You exhausted your crew, stressed yourself out, and didn't do a single thing to move your business forward.

Big, flashy shows are almost always one-off internationals or some executive's random passion project. They do not repeat.

Worse, you have to fund all the upfront costs. The organiser promises 30-day payment terms, but they inevitably miss the date, and suddenly, you are floating a massive invoice on your accounts receivable for two quarters, just hoping you don't go under.

Everyone in this industry is addicted to chasing the shiny, expensive mega-projects.

I love boring, cash-strong businesses.

Quality revenue isn't a flashy one-off. It is the repeatable, boring income that actually pays the bills and builds a company.

Keep your massive, stressful payday that sits unpaid on a spreadsheet for six months. I will take the boring, profitable work every single time.
