You’re the one holding it all together.

The bills, the passwords, the names of the guys who fix the stuff that breaks, and you know, deep down, that if you’re not there tomorrow, the whole thing comes crashing down.

That’s not pride talking. That’s a fact.

A Life Manual isn’t about trust. It’s about not forcing the people you love to play detective when they’re already drowning. It’s the difference between them cursing your name and thanking you for the one time you actually made things easy
.
You think you’re leaving behind memories and money. What you’re really leaving behind is a to-do list, and if you haven’t written it down, that list is just a pile of stress for someone else to sort through.

I’ve been in the room when the spouse doesn’t know the password to the joint account. I’ve been on the phone when the kid can’t find the deed. I’ve seen the look on people’s faces when they realize they have no idea what to do next.

It’s not pretty, and it’s 100% avoidable.
