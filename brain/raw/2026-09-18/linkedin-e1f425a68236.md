When I first mentioned that AI could enhance decision-making, the CEO of a packaging manufacturing company chuckled and said, “That sounds nice, Anu, but AI can’t replace our human strategy.” Little did he know, that mindset would soon be put to the test.

A few months later, I received a call from him. Their manual processing team was on the brink of burnout, deadlines were slipping, and inconsistencies in results were glaring. They were stuck in a cycle of analysis paralysis, grappling with choices instead of making them.

The real issue? They lacked the speed to make informed decisions. While the market moved rapidly, their team was drowning in data, hesitant to take bold steps. The fear of missteps loomed large—and the clock was ticking.

Then we had an “aha” moment. Instead of clinging to the idea that AI would replace their strategy, they realized it could turbocharge their decision-making process. AI doesn’t dictate; it illuminates paths, providing insights that humans can act upon confidently.

We rolled out our AI-driven decision support system, which sifted through mountains of information, highlighting actionable insights. Suddenly, instead of wasting hours deliberating, the CEO’s team could focus on strategy and creativity, armed with better data.

In just 30 days, their decision-making time was cut in half, and productivity shot up by 70%. They could now pivot quickly and effectively without the dread of information overload.

The lesson here? In the world of packaging manufacturing and export, embracing AI isn’t about replacing the human touch; it’s about enhancing your ability to make swift, informed decisions. True growth lies in the partnership between human ingenuity and AI efficiency.

So I ask you: what decision-making bottleneck are you still facing that AI could solve?
