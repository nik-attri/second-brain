🚨 WE’RE HIRING | ASSISTANT MANAGER – SITE OPERATIONS

📍 Location: Talegaon Dabhade, Pune
💼 Experience: 5–8 Years
🎓 Qualification: BE/B.Tech – Civil / Mechanical

We are looking for an Assistant Manager – Site Operations to manage and coordinate site operations, production planning, reporting, and cross-functional activities.

 🔹Drive regular follow-ups with site teams and resolve operational issues.
 🔹Coordinate with Planning, Purchase, Maintenance, Sales, Accounts, and other departments.

 🔹Track production costs, site expenses, and operational variances.
 🔹Support preventive maintenance planning and equipment servicing.
 🔹Ensure adherence to quality, safety, and operational procedures.
 🔹Handle site-level coordination, problem-solving, and administrative activities.

Candidate Profile
 🔹5–8 years of experience in Construction / Site Operations.
 🔹BE/B.Tech in Civil or Mechanical Engineering.
 🔹Strong hands-on exposure to planning, coordination, MIS reporting, and site follow-ups.
 🔹Candidates with stronger planning & coordination experience rather than pure site execution are preferred.
 🔹Good communication and interpersonal skills.
 🔹Proficiency in MS Excel, Word, PowerPoint & Outlook.
 🔹Good command of written and spoken English.
 🔹Strong problem-solving and stakeholder coordination abilities.

📌 Preferred Location: Candidates based in Pune are preferred due to the work location at Talegaon Dabhade.
