🚀 Sophiie AI Raises A$5M Seed Funding

Gold Coast startup Sophiie AI has raised A$5 million (US$3.54M) in seed funding at a A$30 million valuation to build an AI operating system for trades and service businesses. The round was backed by Archangel Ventures, Admiralty Capital Group, Antler, Gandel Invest, and Aussie Angels.

Key Takeaways:

🔹 Founded in 2024 by Jacob Banks, Luke Kelleher, and Juan Castro, Sophiie automates up to 16 hours of weekly admin for tradies including quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and customer communication
🔹 Platform evolved from an AI virtual receptionist into a full AI operating system that manages the journey from first enquiry through to job completion and payment
🔹 Already serving thousands of businesses across Australia, New Zealand, and the UK with 10-15% month-on-month growth
🔹 Capital earmarked for product development, hiring, and US market entry planned for later this year
🔹 CEO Jacob Banks previously built a marketplace featuring over 35,000 trades businesses, giving the team deep domain expertise in an underserved sector

This matters because AI is moving beyond consumer novelty into the operational backbone of blue-collar industries that have historically lagged in digital adoption. As AI agents take over scheduling, invoicing, and customer communication for small service businesses, data, AI, and analytics governance becomes essential to ensure autonomous decisions around pricing, customer data, and financial transactions are accurate, auditable, and compliant. Without governance, the efficiency gains risk eroding trust and introducing liability.

How long before an AI operating system runs your trades business while you focus on the tools?
