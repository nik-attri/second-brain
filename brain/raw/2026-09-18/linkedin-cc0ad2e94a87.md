Anyone responsible for generating leads needs to read this. And yes, even if you don’t use Google Ads.

Because the bigger point here isn’t Google. It’s that we’ve spent way too long celebrating lead volume when the real question should be: did any of these people actually have a snowball’s chance in hell of becoming a customer?

More form fills ≠ better marketing.

If sales is spending half its time sorting through junk leads, that’s not just a sales problem. That’s a marketing problem too.

The good stuff in this piece is the shift toward using first-party data, CRM outcomes and actual customer behavior to tell our platforms what a good lead looks like. Not just “someone filled out the form, everybody clap.” 👏

The crawl, walk, run framework at the end is especially worth stealing, regardless of what platforms you use.

Clicks are nice. Leads are nice.
Revenue is nicer.

Marketing and sales have to close that loop. Otherwise we’re just getting really efficient at generating names in a spreadsheet.
