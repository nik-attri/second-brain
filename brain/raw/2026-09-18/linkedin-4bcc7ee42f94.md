When everyone's baseline work is elevated by using AI, how do you stand out?

This came up after a Data to Insights training I ran recently, where participants work through turning a business problem into insights and recommendations. 

In the first half, I had everyone work through the case study manually. In the second half, I explicitly asked them to analyse and create the deck using AI.

I did it on purpose. It's like learning to drive a manual car even though an automatic car is available. I don't mean to be millennial (or old school) about it. But when something goes wrong with automation, we ought to understand the first principles. 

Anyway once everyone was using AI, the baseline quality of work went up quite obviously. In a short amount of time, the slides looked polished and the recommendations sounded reasonable. In fact, everyone's recommendation started to look the same. So we spent a good amount of time discussing the process of prompting AI, its limitation, and how we can use our learned concepts to prompt AI better. 

Whoever you are presenting to does not care that you used AI. They care about what you want them to do, and what value it brings them. They care about your experience and context behind the data. The time you save by using AI can be spent by thinking about a story to tell. Hopefully that's how you can stand out.
