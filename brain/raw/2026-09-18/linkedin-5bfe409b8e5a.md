Introducing post.buzz

We didn't start building post.buzz because we wanted another social media scheduler.

We built it because we got tired of managing social media ourselves.

We'd be travelling, taking photos and videos we actually wanted to share, and then they'd just sit in our camera roll.

Not because we didn't have anything to post.

Because posting meant:
→ figuring out what to say
→ editing the content
→ writing captions
→ adapting it for different platforms
→ scheduling everything
→ remembering to keep doing it consistently
→ checking what worked

And we kept thinking:

If AI can understand our brand, our content and the context behind it, why are we still doing all of this manually?

That question became post.buzz.

We're building post.buzz as an AI social growth team that can understand your brand voice, images and videos, then help take content from an idea all the way to publishing and learning from the results.

Create. Adapt. Generate. Schedule. Publish. Engage. Learn.

The goal isn't to generate another generic AI caption.
It's to make maintaining a strong social presence something you don't have to spend your entire day managing.

Today, we're launching post.buzz on Product Hunt.

If you're building a company, creating content, or managing social media, I'd love to hear:

What part of social media do you wish you could just hand over to an AI agent?
