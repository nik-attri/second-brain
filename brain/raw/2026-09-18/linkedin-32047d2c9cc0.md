Master’s level finance is brutal for career changers.

Not just because the math is hard, but because the learning resources are scattered everywhere. 

Between University Canvas modules, 1,000-page textbooks, and external video lectures, I was spending 40% of my time just trying to figure out what to study, what extra information and needed, and ensuring external resouces are aligned with what I need to know.

So, I built a solution!
Using Antigravity, I engineered a unified, offline "Knowledge Architecture" dashboard for my USYD FINC6001 course. It acts as a semantic search engine that instantly maps any finance concept across all my disparate study materials.

Here is what it does under the hood: 
🔍 Semantic Mapping: If I search for "asset pricing", it instantly queries a custom JSON knowledge graph to pull up the exact external video lectures (AllThingsMathematics) and University Canvas modules  I need. 
📖 Deep PDF Routing: I parsed the indec of the Bodie, Kane & Marcus 13th Ed. textbook so that clicking a search result forces the local 70MB PDF to open to the exact page number (e.g., #page=344). 
🎓 Automated Instructor Kit Linking: It dynamically maps chapter results to the official publisher's local DOCX Solutions Manual and Instructor Notes, entirely offline.

Instead of drowning in 5 different browser tabs and massive PDFs, I have a sleek, unified curriculum blueprint. I now spend 0 minutes searching, and 100% of my time actually mastering the math.
