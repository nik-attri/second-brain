Monday’s Airtable class with Koka Academy Ltd humbled me a little. 😂

Not in a bad way.

Just enough to remind me that knowing a tool and really understanding what you can build with it are two different things.

We started by completing the field types with Formula, and I actually enjoyed working with it.

One feature I liked was being able to preview the formula before using it.

Small thing? Maybe.

But when you're building a system, being able to catch errors before they become part of your workflow matters.

Then we moved into Forms.

We created one from scratch, explored the features, and I immediately started thinking beyond the classroom.

Because a form isn't just a form when it's connected to a system.

It's a structured way of collecting information.

Then came Automation.

This was where I got excited.

I've worked with automation in my own business workflows, but seeing how it works inside Airtable was different.

Information comes in.
Something happens.
The system responds.

And suddenly, you're not manually chasing every step.

Then...

SCRIPTING.

Sighs. 😂

That one fought me.

There were definitely moments of:

"Okay... what exactly am I doing wrong here?" 😭

But eventually, it clicked.

And honestly, that was one of my favourite moments of the class.

Not because scripting suddenly became easy, but because I experienced the difference between being shown something and actually understanding it.

By the end of the class, I wasn't just thinking about Airtable features anymore.

I was thinking about systems.

When you bring together structured data, formulas, forms, linked records, lookups, rollups, automations and scripting, you start creating something that can do more than store information.

It can help information move, trigger actions and support decisions.

And that's where I see the connection to the work I want to do in Executive Support and Operations.

When a founder says,

"I need help keeping track of this."

The answer doesn't always have to be:

"Sure, I'll keep track of it for you."

Sometimes the better question is:

"Why are you having to keep track of this manually in the first place?"

That's the kind of thinking I'm learning to bring to my work.

Not just managing a process, but understanding it well enough to see where it can be structured, simplified, connected or automated.

By the time we got through everything, Formula had sharpened my attention to detail, Forms had me thinking beyond the classroom, Automation had me genuinely excited, and scripting...

Well, scripting just humbled me. 😂

But somewhere in all of that, the bigger picture started becoming clearer.

Airtable is only the tool. The real skill I'm building is learning to think in systems.

Grateful to Adaeze Nnamdi-Udekwe and the Koka Academy Ltd team for making the learning challenging, practical, and worth the stretch.

One class at a time. 🚀
