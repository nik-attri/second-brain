Today, I stood inside our new building, surrounded by wood, steel and concrete, and had one of those moments where you just have to pause and take it in.

This is happening.

For years, this project lived in conversations, spreadsheets, drawings and dreams.

Now, it's coming to life.  

And if I’m being honest, there is another feeling standing right beside the excitement:

How are we going to finish this?

This project started as a $500,000 vision.

Today, it has grown to more than twice that as we’ve uncovered hurdle after hurdle with the infrastructure of a 40-year-old campus.

There have been plenty of moments when I’ve thought, "How in the hell are we going to do this?"

But we’ve made a decision along the way:

We don’t want another Band-Aid.

We don’t want to build something beautiful only to find ourselves facing the same problems a few years down the road.

We want to build something sustainable.

Something that can serve animals, people and this community for decades.

Something that gives Town & Country a foundation for the future, not another temporary fix.

That’s the part of leadership nobody really talks about.

Sometimes you have to make decisions today for a future you can’t fully see.

You have to keep moving when the path isn’t completely clear.

You have to carry the uncertainty and the hope at the exact same time.

Today, surrounded by wood, steel and concrete, I felt both.

The weight of what it will take to finish.

And the incredible hope of what we’re building.

I don’t have every answer yet.

I don’t know exactly where every remaining dollar will come from.

But I know why we’re doing it.

And I know we’re building something worth fighting for.

So for a minute, I just stood there.

Surrounded by wood, steel and concrete.

And let myself believe it.

This is happening. ❤️
