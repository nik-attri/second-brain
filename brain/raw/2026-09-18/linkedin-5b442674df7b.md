🕒 Most people spend half their day on tasks that could be done in seconds.

We get so used to the friction in our daily workflows that we stop seeing it. We treat manual data entry or sorting through the same five spreadsheets as part of the job.

🎯 Identifying these pain points is the first step toward building a system that actually works for you. You cannot automate or optimize what you have not named. If you want to scale your output, you have to look for the patterns that repeat every single day. The goal is to spend less time on the process and more time on the result.

What is the one repetitive task that drains your energy every single week? Let's talk about it in the comments.
