I’ve reached the point where AI books my flights. It even sorts my visas.

And believe it or not, travel is one of the smaller jobs I hand over to it.

In fact, there is very little in my working life that AI doesn’t touch.

It sources company data when I need to know what’s happening across my businesses.

It builds dashboards that show me that data the way I need to see it.

And when the software I need doesn’t exist, I build it instead of waiting for someone else.

Together, this represents a very different way to run a company compared to just 5 years ago.

And according to BCG, the CEOs seeing value from AI are moving in the same direction.

In June, they reported that 72% of CEOs are directly responsible for AI decisions inside their organisations. Yet only 15% are generating meaningful value from it.

One trait among that 15% was spending at least 8 hours every week developing their own AI capabilities.

And I can understand why.

Every hour I spend working directly with AI changes the way I see my own role.

A report is just questions AI can investigate for me.

An idea is something AI can turn into working software.

A knowledge base becomes a system that ensures hundreds of people on my team make better decisions.

That’s what being an AI-native leader means to me.

I own strategy, quality and judgment calls. And I hand over the work surrounding those decisions to AI.

Then I teach my people to do the same.

So here’s a quick exercise I’d give any leader reading this:

Look at your last 5 working days.

At the information you requested, the decisions you prepared for, the tools you wished you had and the admin that followed you around.

How much of that still depends on you manually moving information between people, meetings and systems?

And what would your role look like if AI started carrying more of it?

That question becomes even more interesting when you explore it alongside other leaders.

A CEO, CFO or COO may see a completely different opportunity in the same piece of work. One person’s working method can save another person months of experimentation.
