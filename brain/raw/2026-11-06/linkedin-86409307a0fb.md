Exploratory Data Analysis Can’t Be Automated Away
I think it bears reiteration in the modern age – when doing an analysis, it is still required to personally, manually, look through the data with your own eye balls. Exploratory Data Analysis is simply not something that LLMs can make completely go away, despite how much AI frontier companies, startups, and overly enthusiastic stakeholders want you to believe. For all sorts of reasons. Even in my attempt to actively force the bots to do my job, the end results were just so frustratingly bad that I’m confident in saying that, as of 2026, we are very much not there yet, if we ever are even going to get “there”…
