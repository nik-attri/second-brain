A customer, right after his first LPR report:

"Yeah, I knew we didn't have our App Registrations under control. But I didn't expect this much of a mess."

That's the reaction we hear most often. Not surprise that there's a problem — surprise at the scale of it.

Because nobody manually reviews every single App Registration every day. Because "it's running fine" isn't a security strategy. Because you can't really picture the scale until you see it in black and white.

That's exactly what the LPR Report is for — full transparency, no manual analysis.

Would you have guessed how much is actually off at your place — or do you know exactly?
