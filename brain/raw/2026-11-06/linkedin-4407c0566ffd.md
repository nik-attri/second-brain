Five skills you can learn as a hobby are exactly
what businesses are paying for right now.
 
Hi my colleagues!
 
Your colleague is a healthcare data analyst learning
out loud as she grows her expertise, personal brand,
and financial freedom.
 
So for the ambitious data analysts like me, here's
where I would start.
 
A hobby feels lighter than a course. You learn at
your own pace, and the pressure stays low.
 
Choose the right one, and it can become income.
 
1. Copywriting
Every business needs words that sell, from product
descriptions to WhatsApp broadcasts. Practise by
rewriting adverts you see daily.
 
2. Video editing
Short form video drives sales on every platform.
CapCut on your phone is enough to begin.
 
3. Graphic design with Canva
Small businesses need flyers and social posts every
week, and Canva lowers the barrier to entry.
 
4. Spreadsheet automation
Owners drowning in manual records will pay for a
sheet that calculates itself. Start with formulas,
then explore Power Query.
 
5. No-code automation
Tools like Zapier and Make connect apps without
code. Automating invoices or follow ups saves a
business hours every week.
 
Turning the hobby into money is simpler than it looks.
Practise for 30 days on personal projects first.
Offer two businesses a discounted trial.
Collect real before and after examples.
Raise your price with each new client.
 
Every one of these complements data analytics,
since they all train you to solve business problems.
