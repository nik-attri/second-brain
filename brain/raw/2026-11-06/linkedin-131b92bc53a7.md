Chatting with a dozen engineering teams about their quote-to-cash process wasn’t on my 2026 bingo card. But it's been a hell of a learning opportunity.

The same story keeps coming up: a billing system built in a hurry, rev rec is an afterthought, everything breaks when you scale beyond PLG, and the timeline to IPO-readiness is faster than ever.

Our CTO Andrew Bihl shared a write-up (link in comments); here are a few takeaways. 
→ Billing isn't one system: Metering, invoicing, and contracting require deep cross-funk alignment. 
→ Don’t build your own rev rec engine: Engineers shouldn’t be interpreting ASC 606, and spreadsheets become a nightmare fast.
→ Run everything off one shared model of your contracts: That way product and finance aren’t reconciling two versions of the truth.

Our core belief at Numeric is that accounting is a data problem. The data work needed to scale a consumption revenue stack is massive.
