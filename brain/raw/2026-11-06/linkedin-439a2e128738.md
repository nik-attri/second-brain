Friday afternoon. I’ve made it to the end of the week without resolving the central mystery of what the hell I’m doing.

I understand this qualifies me for senior management.

Yesterday’s one per cent somehow got me here. Today, I’ve been examining what keeps draining it.

A surprising amount goes into having arguments with people who aren’t in the room.

Then there’s preparing for disasters that haven’t happened, revisiting conversations everyone else has forgotten, and checking my inbox as though the next email might explain my purpose on Earth.

“Hi Dominic, just circling back on your existence.”

This morning, I caught myself doing something ridiculous.

I was postponing having a good afternoon until I’d sorted my life out.

The business. The money. The direction. That nagging suspicion that everyone else received an instruction manual while I was looking for my glasses.

Quite an ambitious entry requirement for enjoying a Friday.

Apparently, I need financial certainty and a coherent five-year strategy before I’m allowed to appreciate some sunshine.

By then, the sun will have become a red giant. Which should finally warm Melbourne up.

So I’ve decided to leave a few things unfinished.

This has caused considerable unrest upstairs. My brain has requested an emergency meeting and produced a spreadsheet of everything that could go wrong.

I admire its commitment. I question its qualifications.

There’s something faintly terrifying about realising there will always be another problem available. You could spend your entire life being responsible and still leave a magnificent to-do list behind.

Someone would probably ask whether you’d updated it before leaving.

I’d like a bit more afternoon in my life before that happens.

A laugh that goes on too long. A conversation that achieves absolutely nothing. Getting home to three cats whose faith in my usefulness remains entirely dependent on a cupboard opening.

I still want the work. The opportunities. The chance to build something I’m proud of.

I’m also going to enjoy this Friday while it’s here.

Monday can have a reasonably competent man with several promising ideas.

The weekend gets this idiot.

He’s earned an outing.
