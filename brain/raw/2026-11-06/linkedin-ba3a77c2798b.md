I can buy another house.

I can’t afford to lose the trust of someone who helped me buy it.

That’s how I look at private investment.

It’s easy to get caught up in the numbers.

£30,000.
£50,000.
£100,000.

Put them into a spreadsheet and they become part of a project budget.

But that money came from somewhere.

Years of working.
Building a business.
Saving when it would’ve been easier to spend.

Someone has chosen to let me put it to work in my business.

That’s a big responsibility.

I love finding a deal. I love seeing a wreck of a house brought back to life.

But having someone choose to work with us again means a hell of a lot.

They’ve experienced the communication, watched the project progress and decided they’re comfortable doing it again.

You can write whatever you like about yourself on LinkedIn.

Someone coming back makes me prouder than anything I could put in my bio.

I’m building a property portfolio, and I want the relationships behind it to last just as long.

If you’ve been following along and wondered how private lending works, drop me a message.

I’m always happy to start with a conversation.
