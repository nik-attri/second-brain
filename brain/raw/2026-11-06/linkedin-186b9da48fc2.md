In 15 minutes with a founder, I can often see $20,000, $40,000, sometimes up around $100,000 a year in value we could redirect.

Why would you leave that money on the table?

Take the Monday report. 3 people spending a day each every week pulling numbers together. At $50 an hour all in, that could be about $60,000 a year in people's time.

That frustrates the hell out of me. You've got people who could be following up sales opportunities or working out why a product isn't selling, and they're stuck feeding a spreadsheet.

You're paying for their judgement too. Let them USE it.

There are 2 jobs in that report. Pulling the numbers together, and deciding what to do about them. If we can hand over the pulling, those people can put their time and judgement into work that earns more for the business. THAT'S where the force multiplier can come from.

We need to check what can actually be handed over, what it costs to build and run, and who handles the exceptions. Some of it might suit AI. Some might just need a trigger and a few rules.

But you have a part in this too. Give people something worth doing with that time, and the authority to get on with it. Don't free them up and then make them ask permission for every little thing.

If you don't like leaving money on the table, let's talk and do the work.
