Someone here posted that they went live on Campfire in 17 days. Suddenly my careful 75-day migration plan seemed unnecessary and timid. We went live in 10.
16 hours. That's how long our first month-end close took. 3 days. Me, Ember and Claude. Could've probably done it in under a day if I'd actually followed the post-migration checklist.
We picked Campfire over 4 other incumbent and native AI ERPs after a full RFP. It won on depth: native integrations with our payroll and spend platforms, and AI that actually does work in the close, moving what used to live in spreadsheets into a system with a real audit trail.
It's not everything, and that's what makes it work. Inventory and WIP still live outside the GL for us. If anyone's building the Campfire of that, I'd like to hear from you.
