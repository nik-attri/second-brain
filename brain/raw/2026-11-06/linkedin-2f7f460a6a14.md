A referral ask sent within 7 days gets 42% more responses than one sent a month later.

In commercial real estate and property management, client satisfaction peaks right when the deal closes. But that is also when your team is drowning in operational handoffs.

The referral ask gets pushed to next week. Then next month.

Eventually someone sends a manual email three weeks later, long after the momentum has died.

An automated post-close sequence fixes the timing without adding work to your team.

When a lease or management contract updates to closed in your system, a timed, personalized referral request triggers automatically within that 7-day window.

The client gets asked while they are still thrilled with the outcome. Your team does zero manual follow-up. And referral volume turns into a reliable pipeline metric instead of an afterthought.

imo, relying on human memory for growth is just leaving revenue on the table.
