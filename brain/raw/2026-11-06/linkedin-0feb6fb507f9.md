“Are we going to use it because it’s the best or because it’s free?”

One of our technicians asked me that last week. I’ve been thinking about it ever since.

After PestWorld, we heard from manufacturers, software companies, even uniform companies who saw what we built and wanted to do something with Nice. Honestly, that’s pretty cool. Some of the offers are wild. We’ve been offered free product and even free software for life if we’d switch.

Would I love to save $60,000 on product this year? Hell yeah, I would.

But this guy has worked for several pest control companies. He knows what it feels like to stand on somebody’s porch and explain a service he believes in. He also knows what it feels like to do that with a product he wouldn’t have picked himself.

What stuck with me was how he explained it. He trusts what’s on his truck. He believes in every step of our service. And he thinks customers can feel that when he talks to them.

I think he’s right.

We’ve had over 900 referrals this year. When somebody gives their neighbor our number, they’re putting their name behind ours. How much of that starts with the confidence of the person who was just at their house?

I can put $60,000 in savings on a spreadsheet today. What’s harder to see is whether that decision could cost us $200,000 in growth next year. By the time I could measure that, our technicians would’ve felt it for months.

We’ve tested products for manufacturers before, and I enjoy that part of what we do. If there’s something better out there for our customers, I want to know about it.

But the person who could really sell me on switching is the same guy who asked me that question. Let him try it on his route. If he comes back and tells me, “Trav, I want to keep this on my truck,” that’ll mean something to me.

And I’m glad as hell he felt comfortable asking me the question in the first place.
