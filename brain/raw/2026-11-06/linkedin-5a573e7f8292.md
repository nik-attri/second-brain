Temp or perm? Choosing the right route 

One of the most common questions we’re asked by hiring managers is whether a role should be filled on a temporary or permanent basis. 
The right answer depends on what the business needs, both now and in the months ahead.

Temporary is often the right fit when:
 • Demand is seasonal or project-based
 • You need to cover absence, such as maternity leave or long-term sickness
 • Speed matters and you need someone in place within days
 • The role’s long-term scope is still being defined

Permanent is usually the better choice when:
 • The role is core to your operations and long-term growth
 • Specialist knowledge or continuity is essential
 • You’re investing in training and development

Where temp-to-perm fits
Temp-to-perm offers a middle ground. You can assess a candidate’s skills, reliability and cultural fit in a live working environment before you make a permanent offer. For candidates, it’s a chance to experience the role and the team first-hand. The result is often a more confident decision on both sides, with better long-term retention.
