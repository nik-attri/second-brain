We are Hiring!!!

Compliance Coordinator (Durban Based)

Role purpose
We are looking for a highly organised and detail-oriented Compliance Coordinator to help ensure that our marketing materials, customer communications, records and internal processes meet all relevant regulatory and company requirements.
The ideal candidate will have strong administrative and data-capturing skills, an excellent eye for detail, and the confidence to identify and follow up on potential compliance issues. Experience or knowledge of marketing and basic accounting or financial administration would be an advantage.
Review marketing campaigns, advertisements, customer communications and supporting documents for compliance before publication.
Check that approved wording, disclaimers, disclosures, terms and conditions, logos and product information are used correctly.
Maintain accurate compliance registers, approval records, supporting documents and audit trails.
Coordinate compliance reviews and approvals between Marketing, Legal, Compliance and other stakeholders.
Track outstanding approvals and follow up to ensure deadlines are met.
Identify and escalate missing information, inconsistencies, errors and potential compliance risks.
Capture, update and verify information accurately across spreadsheets, trackers and internal systems.
Conduct regular quality checks on marketing assets, customer data and campaign records.
Assist with the preparation of compliance reports, audits and management updates.
Support basic reconciliations, invoice tracking and financial administration where required.
Help improve compliance checklists, templates and internal processes.
Keep up to date with relevant policies, procedures and regulatory requirements.
Support the team with general coordination and administrative duties.

Minimum requirements
A relevant diploma or degree in Compliance, Business Administration, Marketing, Finance, Accounting, Law or a related field.
At least 2–3 years’ experience in a compliance, quality assurance, administrative, data-capturing or coordination role.
Strong attention to detail and a high level of accuracy.
Experience working with spreadsheets, reports, trackers and document-management systems.
Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Outlook.
Ability to manage multiple tasks, deadlines and stakeholder follow-ups.
Ability to handle confidential and sensitive information responsibly.
Experience reviewing marketing or customer-facing communications.
Knowledge of South African regulatory requirements such as POPIA, the CPA, NCC requirements and FSCA-related marketing standards.
Experience in financial services, insurance, healthcare or another regulated industry.
Data-entry, bookkeeping, accounting or reconciliation experience.
Familiarity with marketing channels such as SMS, email, WhatsApp, websites, social media and paid advertising.
Experience maintaining evidence and records for audits.
