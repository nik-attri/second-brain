I have this very boring spreadsheet that I use before spending money on marketing.

Trust me, it's SUPER exciting.

Saved me from a few very expensive stupid decisions, too.

It's basically built off one question:

What the hell do we ACTUALLY want to get out of this?

Because if the answer is attention, I might look at a celebrity.

If it's trust, I might look at 20 micro-creators.

If I want to test purchase intent, I'm probably looking at paid social.

And if I already have good content, proof or a story worth repeating, I might invest in distribution.

Same marketing budget.

Different jobs.

And I think this is where founders sometimes make things harder than they need to be.

They ask:

"Should we do creators?"

"Ads?"

"Big name?"

But I think the BETTER question is:

What do I need this money to do?

That's why I made this very boring spreadsheet.

For each option, it tells you:

→ what it's actually good for
→ what you should measure
→ when you probably shouldn't use it

It's basically the sheet I wish I had before making a few of my own marketing decisions 🐼
