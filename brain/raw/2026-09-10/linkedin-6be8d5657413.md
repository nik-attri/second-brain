Salesforce and Anthropic are bringing Claude much deeper into enterprise workflows. That raises a question I find more interesting than: what can Claude automate?

How do we harness Claude so it can adapt to the complexity of the enterprise including legacy systems, business rules and years of accumulated context?

Let’s face the truth m, companies are messy .

Their knowledge isn’t sitting in one database.

It lives across CRM, emails, meetings, documents, ERP systems, old applications, mainframes and, very importantly, in the heads of people who know how the business actually works.

Sales is exactly the same.

Knowing that an opportunity is at Stage 4 isn’t enough.

The AI needs to understand: Why is it really at Stage 4?

What does our sales methodology require?

What has the customer actually said?

Who influences the decision?

What has worked in similar opportunities?

And what should happen next?

This is where I think the next big challenge for enterprise AI lies.

Not building a smarter generic model.

Building the layer that allows a powerful model like Claude to understand and operate within the unique complexity of each company.

We’re working on exactly this problem in sales.

Take the intelligence of the foundation model.

Add the company’s methodology, processes and context.

Then continuously adapt the guidance to what’s actually happening with the customer.

The companies that solve this won’t just have “AI connected to their systems.”

They’ll have AI that understands how their business works.

So the question I’m increasingly asking is:

How do we make Claude adapt to the enterprise instead of making the enterprise adapt to Claude?
