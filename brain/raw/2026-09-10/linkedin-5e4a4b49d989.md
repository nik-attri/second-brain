We’re Hiring | Administrative & Procurement Specialist 

Full-time: Office-based
Location: Amman, Jordan

About the Role
Roya Media Group is looking for a highly organized and proactive Administrative & Procurement Specialist to support administrative operations, procurement activities, logistics coordination, and warehouse management. 

Key Responsibilities:
Procurement:
Prepare purchase orders for requested materials and services.
Obtain and compare quotations from multiple suppliers.
Coordinate supplier selection and purchase approvals with the Operations & Logistics Manager.
Ensure all purchases are properly documented with official invoices and approved according to company procedures.
Coordinate with the Finance Department to process supplier payments.

Administration & Government Relations:
Prepare authorization letters and documentation for renewing licenses, permits, and contracts.
Coordinate with government entities and external authorities to complete administrative procedures.

Logistics & Fleet Coordination:
Assist in coordinating vehicle registration and insurance.
Support the monitoring and coordination of company vehicle operations.
Coordinate schedules and logistics with drivers to ensure efficient operations.

Warehouse & Inventory:
Assist in monitoring warehouse operations and storage facilities.
Support periodic and annual inventory counts and maintain accurate inventory records.

Shipping & Customs:
Coordinate with customs clearance agents regarding international shipments and deliveries.

Office Administration:
Ensure office facilities are clean, organized, and well-maintained.
Manage office stationery and facility supplies, ensuring adequate stock at all times.

Qualifications:
Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or a related field.
2–4 years of experience in administration, procurement, logistics, or a similar role.
Excellent customer service and interpersonal skills.
Ability to work effectively in a team environment.

What We're Looking For
Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.
Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities.
Problem-solving mindset and proactive approach.
Professional communication and coordination skills.
Ability to work in a fast paced environment while maintaining accuracy.

If you are organized, detail oriented, and passionate about supporting operational excellence, please send your resume and a cover letter to Careers@royamediagroup.com , and mention "Administrative & Procurement Specialist" in the subject line.
