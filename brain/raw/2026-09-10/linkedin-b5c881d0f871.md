Meet Faisal — Sales Operations Manager at Tierra Properties. 
On paper: sales strategy, CRM, lead distribution, team building. In reality: owner coordination, marketing input, recruiting agents, doing viewings, hunting listings, building trackers, backing telesales with market data. Monday mornings: team meetings to fire everyone up.
Before real estate, he was a Compliance Officer at an American company in Pakistan — stable, decent. But he asked: what field could change his life if he worked hard enough? Real estate. He landed in Dubai Feb 2022, 23 years old, AED 500 in his pocket. Everything since came from that one decision.
Ask him for a proud moment at Tierra, and he's honest: "That moment hasn't come yet. Everything I've done so far is just me doing my job, nothing extra." He'll feel proud when he builds a big team, brings big numbers, and the CEO takes everyone abroad to celebrate. He's saving that feeling for that day.
The part most people would never guess: how unglamorous it actually is. People picture viewings and handshakes. His week is spreadsheets, policies, review cadences, coordinating five people per listing. That invisible structure is why the visible deals happen.
Outside of work, you'll catch him swimming or deep into a physical or mental game — anything that keeps him sharp. Some weekends, the opposite: the entire day in bed. No in-between.
He couldn't tell us the one word his teammates would use to describe him — he genuinely wants to know too. If you work with Faisal, drop it in the comments.
Here's one he'll give you himself: people who meet him usually don't love how blunt and straightforward he is — it's just who he is. But he understands people are different, so he adjusts how he engages. Those who know him say they've never had a coworker or friend quite like him. He's not bragging — he wants people to know the real him. Everyone's a mix of good and bad — we just have to figure each other out. At work, serious and professional. Outside, a completely different person. 360 degrees.
His motto: real estate isn't about selling properties. It's about building trust. Everything else — referrals, repeat clients, long-term relationships — comes from that.
What Tierra means to him is personal. Since joining, he's earned real respect from the people around him. His role comes with responsibility and difficult conversations at times, but things are settling into place. What he loves most is the CEO: genuinely passionate, always positive, someone he's learning from as much as doing his own job—humility, business mindset, hunger, dedication. His team is chill, easy to work with, and they've finally made him feel like part of the Tierra family — that alone keeps his morale high. He wants to put in the work to take Tierra where it deserves to go, with his team beside him—support staff to sales staff.
That's Faisal. That's Tierra.
