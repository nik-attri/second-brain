This is our first real attempt at solving a genuine problem with AI on Creso. Not AI as a checkbox feature but AI doing work our distributors used to do manually.

Client meetings generate real signal: goals, concerns, things a client wants followed up on. Most of it either stays in your head or gets written down badly. So we built something that takes your conversation and turns it directly into tasks and action items in the CRM while the context is still fresh. Not a summary to read later but actionable follow-ups that show up where you already work.

This is the direction we want to keep building in: AI that removes real friction from a distributor's day, not AI for the sake of having AI in the product.
