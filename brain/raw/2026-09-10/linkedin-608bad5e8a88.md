Career Opportunity: Procurement Manager! 

Our strategic partner is a global agricultural supply-chain company seeking a Procurement Manager to lead sourcing strategies, supplier performance, contract negotiations, and cost-effective purchasing operations. 

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

✔️ Develop and execute procurement strategies aligned with business objectives, budgets, cost targets, and supply requirements. 
✔️ Source, assess, qualify, and develop suppliers while managing performance, corrective actions, and long-term partnerships. 
✔️ Lead tendering and quotation processes and negotiate contracts, pricing, payment terms, delivery schedules, and service levels. 
✔️ Manage purchase orders from requisition through delivery and coordinate inventory needs with relevant business functions. 
✔️ Monitor commodity markets, procurement spend, supply risks, compliance, and team performance while driving continuous improvement. 

If you have seven to ten years of sourcing or procurement experience, including managerial experience, and strong supplier negotiation and contract management skills, we welcome your application at giang.nguyen@peoplewise.vn or application@peoplewise.vn.
