Last week I watched a pharmacy manager spend Thursday and Friday rebuilding three months of controlled-item logs by hand. Audit was Monday.

The spreadsheets didn't talk to the billing system. Dispensing records lived in two places. Every compliance entry had to be manually cross-checked and reformatted. What should have been a one-day task became a weekend scramble.

This isn't unique to that pharmacy. Most Malaysian pharmacies still run dispensing, billing and audit logs as separate islands. When the regulator calls, you're back in spreadsheet hell.

The fix isn't better spreadsheets. It's an ERP that does the work once. Dispensing data flows directly into billing. Controlled-item logs build themselves in real-time, audit-ready from day one. No manual rebuild. No Friday night panic.

One pharmacy client went from 3 days of audit prep to 4 hours. Their compliance officer actually had a weekend.

If your audit prep still means rebuilding records by hand.. what's actually stopping you from consolidating into a single system?

ST Comp's ERP handles exactly this for pharmacies across Malaysia.
