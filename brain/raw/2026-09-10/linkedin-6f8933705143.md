🚀 Running an SME or corporate service firm in HK usually means drowning in paperwork and fragmented records. 🧵👇

As part of my Build in Public AI Challenge, I’m demonstrating production AI engineering, moving from AI-assisted workspaces to AI-native multi-agent systems.

First up: Product #1: HK Founder Desk, an AI-assisted workspace for HK founders, operators, and corporate service firms to run onboarding and compliance on autopilot.

🔗 Live Preview: https://lnkd.in/gjCXqJqK
(Uploads are encrypted at rest using AES-256 in AWS S3 with tenant-isolated access control.)

MVP & Operational Impact ⚡

• 📄 Instant Document Intelligence: Extracts BR, CI, and NAR1 filings in ~10s (vs ~20m manual) with source-linked evidence. 
• 📅 Automated Compliance: Calculates statutory deadlines and builds calendars in ~5s to eliminate late-filing fines. 
• ⏱️ 99% Time Reduction: Cuts admin onboarding audit time from ~30m to 15s per entity. 
• 🏢 Agency Scale: Manages multi-entity portfolios in one view. Reclaims ~580h of manual labor per cycle for agencies managing 1,000 client entities.

Architectural Highlights 🛠️

• 🔒 Decoupled Server Boundaries: Processing, tenant isolation, compliance rules, and keys stay behind server microservice boundaries. 
• 🌐 Content Classification: Identifies documents by structural content, preserving bilingual (EN/TC) facts without flattening entities. 
• 🛡️ Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails: AI outputs are candidate data. Strict JSON schema validation and confidence checks prevent silent overwrites. 
• ⚙️ Cost-Aware Model Cascading: Automated escalation (Tier 1 Vision ➔ Tier 2 Reasoning) triggered on quality failures.
