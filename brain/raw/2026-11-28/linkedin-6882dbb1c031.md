Looking at paid media agencies from the outside, it’s kinda mad how much genius and strategy is held together with tape and spreadsheets. 

Agencies have access to more data, tools, automation, then ever before. 

But managing paid media across many different clients, often different platforms, introduces its own challenges. 

I think there is an interesting opportunity for AI to make many of these ops a hell of a lot easier and more effective - without replacing existing platforms or reinventing another tool. 

Connecting the dots, increasing output per per unit of time, helping make better use of the info you already have. 

I’m particularly interested in exploring these opportunities exist in this field.
