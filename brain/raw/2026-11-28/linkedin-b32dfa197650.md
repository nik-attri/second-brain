State of hands-on AI -- time for the tinkerer roundup! 

Holiday tinkerers, if you were playing with AI recently, answer this quick question to share your wisdom with the AI newcomers (and maybe inspire them to become tinkerers too): 

1) SUCCESS: What's one AI thing you tried this week succeeded for you?
2) FAILURE: What's one AI thing you tried this week that failed for you?

Here are some of mine from the last 72h:

SUCCESS: Hacking my wearable ring to be a controller for multiple Android apps.
SUCCESS: Driving Canva via Claude plugin to create a logo wall of companies that have booked me as a speaker (https://makecassietalk.com), below. (The 3rd image is what vanilla ChatGPT makes without Canva -- subtle errors, uglier layout.)
SUCCESS: Generating a personalized 84 page handbook for a new hire by having ChatGPT remix directly from guides in Google Drive.*
SUCCESS: Delegating the hunt for rather niche Black Friday deals to Claude -- it's fun to watch it control the browser and dig through the internet so I don't have to.

FAILURE: Using ChatGPT to get trustworthy answers from a big multitab spreadsheet. (For data science tasks, I'm still finding that using AI to write the code for analyzing a spreadsheet (e.g. Apps Script) gives better results than asking ChatGPT/Claude about the spreadsheet directly.)
FAILURE: Patching whisper.cpp to extract the logs it discards while converting speech to text. (Trying again today.)
FAILURE: Self-evaluating keynote-transcript-to-blog pipeline that preserves intentional quirky word choices while removing filler, stutters, etc. (Part of a long pet project, not just a one-day thing, still working on it. The dream is to be able to speak the ideas and have AI present them to you without garbaging them along the way.)
FAILURE: Getting ChatGPT's Dot to create a daily briefing that's better than the one I get by running Google searches via the OpenAI API each morning. If anyone has found Dot impressive, lmk, I genuinely don't get the hype.

What have you been succeeding or failing at, tinkerers? Is there anything on my lists that surprises you? If you found anything here useful, a repost makes my heart happy.  💗 

*If you're tempted to follow my example on the AI-generated handbook, do it safely! In this case: (1) The connected Google Drive does not have access to sensitive info (if our culture guide leaks, for example, whoever stole it needs it more than we do) AND (2) I did edit out obvious mistakes AND (3) all team members are trained to know AI is a bullshitter, which means little errors in the guide won't cause much harm - he'll just ask his manager about anything odd and correct the handbook himself along the way. This application would be horrible with AI-gullible new hires. What it's actually for is giving him fodder for dropping into AI voice mode to have a conversation with our company info so he can ask questions that he might feel shy asking his manager on day 1 (and yes, she and I both playtested it).
