Post 6 — Workflow breakdowns
End-to-end event management—one smooth flow from signup to post-event feedback.

Event teams spend days on manual admin: registrations, seat allocations, reminders, and surveys.

Here’s a fast breakdown I use:
- Registration feeds Airtable CRM
- Make.com handles seat assignments, calendar invites, and emails
- Softr powers a public attendee portal
- ManyChat/email confirms and updates throughout
- Post-event survey data flows back into Airtable for reporting

The result: huge time savings, cleaner data, and a better attendee experience.
