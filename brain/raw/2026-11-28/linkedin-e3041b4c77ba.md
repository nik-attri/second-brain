WE ARE HIRING | Admin cum HR Assistant
Company: DXO Global
Location: Dubai, UAE
Work Arrangement: On-site and Remote opportunities available
Employment Type: Full-time
DXO Global is seeking a proactive, organised and technology-driven Admin cum HR Assistant to support our growing business operations. The ideal candidate should have a strong understanding of HR and administrative functions, with the ability to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools to improve productivity, automate routine tasks and enhance operational efficiency.
Key Responsibilities
Human Resources:
Manage end-to-end recruitment, candidate screening and interview coordination.
Coordinate employee onboarding and offboarding.
Maintain employee records, attendance and leave management.
Prepare employment contracts, HR letters, policies and procedures.
Support employee engagement and performance management.
Assist with HR compliance and documentation.
Administration:
Manage day-to-day administrative and operational activities.
Prepare reports, presentations and business correspondence.
Coordinate meetings, appointments and schedules.
Maintain company records, documentation and internal trackers.
Coordinate with clients, vendors and external stakeholders.
Provide administrative support to the management team.
Artificial Intelligence & Process Automation:
Utilise AI tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and other productivity platforms.
Leverage AI for recruitment, research, documentation and reporting.
Identify opportunities to automate repetitive HR and administrative tasks.
Develop efficient digital workflows and improve existing business processes.
Ensure responsible use of AI while maintaining data confidentiality.
Requirements:
Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field.
1–2 years of experience in HR and administration.
Practical knowledge of AI tools and prompt engineering.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office and digital productivity platforms.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
Strong analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills.
Ability to work independently and adapt to emerging technologies.
Knowledge of UAE labour regulations is preferred.
Immediate joiners are preferred.
How to Apply
Interested candidates should submit their updated CV to info@dxo.global with the subject line Admin cum HR Assistant – Job Application.
Please indicate your preferred work arrangement (On-site or Remote) when applying.
