WHEN YOU TURN UP TO AN EMERGENCY CALLOUT AND THINK… WHAT ON EARTH HAS HAPPENED HERE?!

This was certainly not your average emergency tree job!

A large poplar had failed at the base, landing almost completely flat across the roof of a building, with the root plate pointing skywards. Somehow, the tree was just sitting there, almost floating on the roof!

The challenge? Pretty much every conventional option was off the table.

🚫 Crane access wasn’t possible due to multiple BT telephone lines.
🚫 A MEWP couldn’t be safely positioned or operated in the available space.
🚫 Manually cutting from underneath was far too dangerous due to the instability of the structure.
🚫 The roof itself couldn’t be accessed safely.

We spoke to several companies, and most understandably wanted nothing to do with it.

But we don’t believe in walking away from a difficult job without thoroughly assessing every possible option.

After carefully assessing the situation, planning the safest approach and working methodically, we developed a solution and successfully dismantled the tree, cleared the debris and removed the timber.

The result? A very challenging emergency operation completed, with safety remaining our number one priority throughout.

I always say there’s no such thing as an impossible job, but there is always a safe way to approach a difficult one. Sometimes it just takes a bit more thinking outside the box!

How would you have tackled this one?

We’d genuinely love to hear how other arborists would have approached this situation.
