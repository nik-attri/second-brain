My mind wants to do.
My body wants me to pause.

I’m sitting here trying to force my mind to respond to an email, to populate an Excel spreadsheet, when I can distinctly sense that my body is asking something else of me.

What the hell is wrong with you? Why can’t you concentrate? Why can’t you just get on with your work?

But there is nothing wrong with me.

I’m a human being learning how to inhabit this body and listen to it in ways that society hasn’t taught me. Ways that I wish our education system would make more space for.

My body is loud today. Not necessarily through pain or physical discomfort, but through the clarity with which I can sense what it needs.

It’s not asking me to do 50 burpees or feed it 5,000 supplements.

It’s asking me to sit and allow.

Allow what?

Whatever wants to emerge. Whatever I sense I’ve been holding within my body that is now ready to surface, to be seen, to be felt.

I know what happens when I give my body this kind of attention. The tears come. The body shakes. I feel waves of energy moving through me.

I don’t always need to know what I am processing to give myself permission to process it.

There is something deeply liberating about allowing this movement to happen. I feel myself letting go, making space for change, flowing with life rather than resisting it.

And when I override what I sense, when I refuse to listen, I feel stuck.

Perhaps part of learning how to heal is also learning how to be human. How to inhabit a body, how to listen to it and how to respond to what we sense.

Today, my body is asking for my presence, not my productivity.

Can I give it that?
