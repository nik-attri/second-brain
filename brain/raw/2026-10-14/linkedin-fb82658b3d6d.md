Our founders presented their companies to investors from Mangrove Capital Partners and Founders Future, with one objective: communicate the essentials quickly, answer difficult questions and get an external perspective on their story.

10 companies took the stage:

Clovis: Helping construction teams manage projects and collaborate more efficiently.
Dockflow: Simplifies and automates maritime logistics operations.
Eagr: Using AI to help sales teams analyse conversations and improve performance.
Junga: Building AI-powered tools to simplify the work of wealth advisors.
Mankinds: Helping companies evaluate, monitor and manage the risks of AI systems.
Peliqan: simplifies access to enterprise data to build custom AI systems and workflows.
TwoWay: Turning data chaos into competitive advantage for traders.
PRESAGE: Building world models for cloud infrastructure to anticipate failures before they happen.
Kompa: Automating administrative work for small construction businesses with AI.
MinersAI: Combining AI and geoscience data to improve mineral exploration.

No long presentations. No unnecessary slides.

Just founders, investors and straightforward conversations about product, market, traction and ambition.

Beyond capital, part of our role at welovefounders is to create opportunities for our founders to meet relevant investors, sharpen their fundraising narrative and build relationships before they actually need them.

A big thank you to Yannick Oswald from Mangrove Capital Partners and Louis Sautet from Founders Future for their time, thoughtful questions and honest feedback.
