A couple of weeks ago we had our annual National Conference in Melbourne and besides the collaboration, the learning, the relationship building and the crazy and fun events, we also held out annual awards night.  Qld repped strong in numerous categories with some regular faces and some new ones.  Thanks, Team Qld, for another record year in every metric and congrats to the award winners.

Let me to introduce you to just one of them — Martin Ferreira of MSF Credit 🤝 Martins been helping Queenslanders into homes and businesses into finance since 2017, if you know him, you already know the thing that doesn't show up on a spreadsheet: he genuinely cares whether the deal works for you, not just whether it settles.

His clients say it far better than I can — a long run of five-star reviews that he's never once had to chase. At the Money Quest Group national conference this year, that work got the recognition it deserves. Martin was named Qld Metro Broker of the Year — and at the same event, he was appointed to our Diamond Club 💎 Two honor's in one night. Both thoroughly earned. The Diamond Club isn't handed out for one good quarter — it goes to the brokers who show up consistently, month after month, for their clients and for the people around them. And Metro Broker of the Year? That's the room voting with its feet. When your peers look at your numbers and your standards and say that's the benchmark — that means something. Shout out here to Dr. Tony Hayek of Blue Wealth Property for co presenting this amazing award to Martin.

What I love most is that Martin takes it all in his stride. Hell thank you, crack a joke, and be back on the phone to a first home buyer before the coffees cold. That humility is exactly why hes so good at this. Martin, congratulations — and welcome to the Diamond Club and congratulations on your much deserved award.
