Why design everything manually when your vessel design software can do much of the work for you? 

We are currently developing an automated workflow that transforms geometry inputs and structural design results directly into: 
• 3D renderings and technical sketches 
• A clearer visual understanding of the final design 
• A STEP file that can serve as a starting point for the construction department 

This means that customers can see what their vessel or component will look like within minutes — before the detailed design process is even completed. 
The benefits are significant: 
✅ Fewer interface errors between design and structural analysis 
✅ Major time savings 
✅ A continuous workflow from calculation to construction
✅ Automated and reproducible engineering processes 

The goal is simple: reduce manual work while improving consistency and communication between engineering disciplines.
