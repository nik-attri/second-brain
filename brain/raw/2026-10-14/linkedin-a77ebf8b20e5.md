From the outside everything can look perfect and here's something that isn't talked about enough... 

It can look like it's running smoothly. Clients are coming in. The team are getting on with things. Everything looks like it’s going really well. And sometimes it is. 

But what people don’t see is everything happening behind the scenes. The difficult conversations. The decisions you’re lying awake thinking about. The team issues you’re trying to handle fairly. The client problem you’re sorting before anyone else even knows there’s a problem. The days where you’re questioning whether you’re actually getting it right. The pressure of knowing that ultimately, the decisions land with you. 

Nobody sees the spreadsheets at midnight. Nobody sees the conversations you have with yourself. Nobody sees the moments where you think: “How the hell am I going to sort this?” 

Because when you’re the business owner, you still have to show up. You still have clients to look after. You still have a team relying on you. You still have to make decisions. You still have to be the one saying, “It’s fine, I’ve got it”… Even when you're not entirely sure you have. 

So yes, it might look smooth from the outside. But smooth on the surface doesn’t mean easy underneath. And I think that’s something we probably don’t talk about enough. 

Business isn't always the wins you see on LinkedIn. Sometimes the biggest wins are the problems nobody ever knew you had to solve.
