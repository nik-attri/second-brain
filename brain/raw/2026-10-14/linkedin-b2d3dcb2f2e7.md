The next crucial role in Pharma and other Emerging Markets might not exist yet.

Over the next 3 - 10 years, I believe that innovative companies will need a new kind of leader: Someone who connects AI, business processes, and human capabilities to fundamentally rethink how work gets done.

Not another digital transformation manager focused on implementing technology, and adding some AI-features on top, but a leader redesigning end-to-end workflows, orchestrating human & AI collaboration, and turning innovation into measurable business value.

The real opportunity isn’t simply to automate what we do today. It is to challenge why we still do it that way.

The question is no longer how we can use AI, but how we should redesign our organizations around it to move towards autonomous operating models with human oversight.
