Creating a form shouldn't feel like a whole project.

If you've ever created a Google Form for an event, survey, application, or registration, you probably know the process.

You already know what you want to ask. You know the information you need to collect.
But then comes the actual work.

Adding questions one by one. Choosing the right question types. Arranging everything properly. Going back to make changes.

It's not necessarily difficult. It just takes time, especially when you're doing it repeatedly.
And I started wondering: what if you could simply describe the form you need and let AI help you create it?

That's the idea behind Intake.

Instead of manually putting everything together, you describe what you need in plain English, and Intake helps turn that description into a form.

We're starting with AI-assisted form creation and editing, but the bigger vision is to make collecting, organizing, and working with information much less tedious.

I'm building Intake because I believe we can do better than spending so much time setting up the tools we use to get work done.

We're still early, and I'd love to have people who regularly work with forms help shape what Intake becomes.

If you're an event organizer, researcher, founder, community manager, or anyone who works with forms, I'd love for you to join the waitlist.
