1.8 million applications for just 5,000 places. 

Sound familiar, recruiters?

Well, not quite applications! Singer RAYE has just revealed that 1.8 million people registered for tickets to her upcoming Royal Albert Hall concert, with hundreds of thousands of fake registrations generated by AI bots.

Her teams solution? Go back to manually reviewing registrations to identify genuine fans.

Sound familiar?

The latest Institute of Student Employers (ISE) Student Recruitment Report 2026 reveals that 92% of employers now suspect or have confirmed misconduct during recruitment assessments, with unauthorised AI use a major concern.
 
AI is undoubtedly transforming recruitment, bringing huge opportunities but also significant challenges. For early careers recruiters, the question is increasingly not just how to manage application volumes, but how to identify genuine talent, motivation and potential in an AI-driven world.

And for candidates, how do you stand out when everyone has access to the same technology?

Are we heading towards a recruitment process where human interaction becomes more valuable than ever?
