In September the two largest beverage ERP systems collided. 

Here’s my read on the acquisition.

For years, beverage operators lived in a split universe: 

VIP owned supplier reporting and depletion intelligence. 

Encompass powered distributor operations, warehouse execution, and mobile workflows.

Everyone lived with the gap between them.

Now they’re one company and this isn’t just consolidation. It’s a long‑overdue fix to the industry’s biggest friction points:

 * The Data Silo Tax: Supplier depletions finally meet real‑time warehouse execution.

 * The Compliance Maze: Automated reporting replaces spreadsheet gymnastics.

 * End‑to‑End Visibility: A single backbone for forecasting → execution → retail fulfillment.

I believe that within 24 months, “ERP integration” becomes a relic. We’re entering the Agentic Beverage Supply Chain with predictive inventory, automated retail ordering, and native supplier reporting without reconciliation hell.

The tech stack is finally catching up to the speed of the street.
