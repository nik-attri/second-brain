For the second post today, I’d use a more direct client focused angle. It speaks to business owners who are considering outsourcing their store management.

🚨 Before hiring an e-commerce manager, ask these 5 questions.

You are trusting someone with your store, customers, and daily operations.

So don’t hire based only on a profile or a list of skills.

Ask:

1️⃣ How do you handle product research?

You want a process, not random product uploads.

2️⃣ How do you manage customer issues?

Look for someone who understands communication, returns, refunds, and buyer satisfaction.

3️⃣ How do you monitor store performance?

A good manager should know which numbers matter and what action to take from them.

4️⃣ How do you handle problems?

The right person brings solutions instead of waiting for you to identify every issue.

5️⃣ How will you communicate with me?

Clear updates create trust, especially when you’re managing stores remotely.

The right e-commerce manager should give you more than completed tasks.

They should give you visibility, consistency, and time to focus on your business.
