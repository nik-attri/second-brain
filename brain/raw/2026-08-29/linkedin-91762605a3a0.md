We're teaching designers how to use AI. I'm more interested in what happens when they start questioning it.

For the last few years, I've watched the relationship between design and technology change faster than the way we are taught to design. AI is no longer something happening somewhere outside our discipline. It's already changing how we generate ideas, visualise spaces, automate repetitive work, communicate concepts and experiment with entirely new design workflows.
And yet, much of the conversation around AI in design still sits at two extremes.

Either we fear what it might replace. Or we simply learn how to generate a better image. I believe there is a much more interesting space in between.
A space for designers to experiment.

That is what Labs exists to build. And it's why I'm excited to open registrations for our next cohort:

Artificial Intelligence & Design Innovation
A 10-week certification program.

This isn't about learning one AI tool before the next one replaces it. It's a hands-on exploration of how AI can become part of your design practice:
→ Prompt-Driven Ideation
→ Workflow Automation
→ Generative Design Tools

We'll explore how emerging AI tools can help us generate, test, automate and iterate, while also asking a larger question: What does design practice look like when experimentation becomes part of the workflow?

We built this program around the same principle @LABS is built on:
LEARN. → APPLY. → BUILD. → SCALE.

You  experiment with new technologies You apply it to your own work.
You build new workflows. And hopefully, you leave with a different understanding of what your practice could become.

Starts 10th October, 2026.

If you're an architect, interior designer or creative professional interested in moving beyond simply using AI and towards experimenting with what it can enable, I'd love to have you in this cohort.
