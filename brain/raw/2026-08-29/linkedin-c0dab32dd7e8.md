LinkedIn banned two of my accounts before I finally gave up on automation.

This profile is my third attempt. 

When I created it, I made a vow: no more third-party bots. 

Losing an account wipes out your network, conversations, and months of pipeline instantly.

So, I went manual. And let me tell you, it felt like moving at a snail's pace.

Every day, I'd spend hours opening posts, reading updates, crafting thoughtful comments from scratch. It took two to three hours daily just to maintain visibility and keep client chats alive.

I didn't set out to create a product for the public. I just wanted my evenings back.

That's when I developed a simple Chrome extension for personal use. The idea was straightforward: it analyzes posts, drafts comments in my voice based on my writing style, but leaves the final review and send decision to me. This keeps the account safe from platform detection.

A few founder friends saw my setup and wanted in. Two months later, 67 users were onboarded without spending a dime on marketing.

It hit me then, I wasn't alone in this struggle. B2B founders, recruiters, and sales teams faced the same dilemma: avoiding bans from aggressive bots while drowning in manual work needed to build authentic pipeline through comments.

I still check my LinkedIn settings every morning out of habit to see if everything's intact. Thankfully, this third profile is holding steady.
