5 Automations Every Founder Needs In Week 1.

I wish someone gave me this list when I started.

Most founders waste Week 1 on:
- Manual lead tracking
- Writing the same follow-ups
- Taking meeting notes twice
- Drowning in emails
- Creating content from scratch

I automated all 5 in one day. Saved 10+ hours every week since.

Here are the 5:

1. LEAD CAPTURE → CRM
Typeform / Tally → Google Sheets / Notion
Every new lead auto-added. No CSV. No copy-paste.
Tool: Zapier or Make. Cost: $0

2. AUTO FOLLOW-UP
New lead → Gmail template sent automatically after 10 mins
My open rate went from 40% to 68% because I stopped forgetting.
Tool: Gmail + Zapier

3. CONTENT REPURPOSE MACHINE
1 Loom video → Transcript → Perplexity gives me 5 LinkedIn posts + 3 tweets
I record once on Monday, I'm done for the week.
Tool: Loom + OpusClip + ChatGPT

4. MEETING NOTES → TASKS
Otter.ai / Fireflies → Auto-summarize → Push to Notion tasks
I stopped spending 30 mins after each call writing notes.
Tool: Otter + Notion

5. INBOX CLEANUP
Gmail filter: If from client or subject: invoice → Auto-label "PRIORITY"
My inbox cleans itself every night.
Tool: Gmail filters (free)

You don't need a $500/mo stack.
You need Gmail + Sheets + Loom + Otter + one automation tool.

I made this carousel so you can save it and build one per day.
