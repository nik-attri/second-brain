Your comp spreadsheet is not a system. It's a liability. 🫠 

3–5% overpayment error rate. Weeks to close each comp cycle. Reps DMing you on a Friday asking why their check is off.
And the worst part? The more complex your plans get, the worse it gets.

That's the reality for most RevOps operators managing incentive comp manually. And it doesn't have to be.

We sat down with Allie Wenberg, Senior Solutions Engineer at Performio, for a full walkthrough of their incentive compensation management platform, and it's genuinely impressive.

Here's what stood out:

🧱 Component-based plan building: no nested IF statements, no formula hell. You tell the system what to look at, and it handles the logic. SPIFFs spin up in minutes, not weeks.

📊 Analytics Studio: live dashboards with drill-down capability so you can answer leadership's follow-up questions without rebuilding a pivot table every time.

🤖 Admin Assistant (AI): this one's different. It's not a generic chatbot. It knows your instance, your plans, your data. It can trace any deal from source to payment, detect anomalies automatically (like a split configured at 60x instead of 60%), and even configure the platform for you, with human-in-the-loop approval on every change.

The AI-assisted implementation piece is wild too. A configuration phase scoped at 6 weeks? Done in 2 days. 👀 

If you're running comp on spreadsheets, or on an ICM tool that requires IT involvement every time the business wants to make a plan change, this is worth your time.
