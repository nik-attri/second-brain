Small business owners often feel like they’re drowning in paperwork because they are acting as the manual bridge between digital systems. You get a PDF invoice, then you manually type that data into your software.

This is a waste of your most valuable resource: time.

The solution isn't to let AI take over your bank account. The solution is AI document extraction that prepares the work for you. By using an invoice workbench, the system pulls the vendor name, line items, and totals, then presents them for a human to approve with one click.

Takeaways for your back office:
- AI handles the data extraction, not the payment.
- A "human-in-the-loop" ensures 100% accuracy.
- Most teams recover 4-10 hours per week, per person.

Stop being a data entry clerk and start being an operator.
