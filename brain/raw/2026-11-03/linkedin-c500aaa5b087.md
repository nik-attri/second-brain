What does it take to automate sterility testing?
More than automation technology.

Developing SteriNow® required bringing together expertise in radiopharmacy, quality control, microbiology and sterility testing to design a solution that supports compliance, reduces contamination risks and improves operational efficiency.

SteriNow® is an automated sterility testing workflow developed specifically for radiopharmaceutical production, that helped turn an industry challenge into a practical solution.

But how did the project begin, and what made it possible?

In this video, Charles Vriamont and Thomas Vergote, PhD, share the story behind SteriNow® and the collaboration with CUP Contract Labs that supported its development.

Their experience highlights how complementary expertise, close collaboration and a shared commitment to innovation can help address some of radiopharmacy's most complex operational challenges.
