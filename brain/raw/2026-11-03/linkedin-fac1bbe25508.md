In real estate, investors obsess over cap rates, IRR, and financial ROI.
Yet, as luxury real estate advisor Rishad D’Cruz points out on India’s Most Influential, the most critical metric often gets ignored: ROE (Return on Emotion).

"You can buy property, you can stretch for it. But if you get stressed with the budget, stressed with the bankers, and stressed in your personal life—it becomes hell. At the end of the day, you don't want an axe around your neck. You want to chill."
Key takeaways for prospective homebuyers and founders deploying capital:
• The Happiness Index: A home should enhance your quality of life, not add administrative and financial anxiety. 
• The Cost of Overleveraging: Stretching too far for status often turns an asset into a psychological liability. 
• Symmetric Evaluation: When acquiring lifestyle real estate, calculate your peace of mind and family well-being with the same rigor you apply to your financial spreadsheet.
