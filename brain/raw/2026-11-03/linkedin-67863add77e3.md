📢 WE’RE HIRING | Assistant Manager – Project Management Office (PMO)
📍 Location: Lahore, Punjab
🏢 Organization: Pakistan Solar Association (PSA)
📂 Department: Secretariat
Pakistan Solar Association (PSA) is looking for a motivated and organized professional to join our Secretariat as an Assistant Manager – Project Management Office (PMO).
The position will support the planning, coordination, monitoring, and execution of PSA’s organisational projects, strategic initiatives, and departmental workplans. The role will contribute to strengthening internal coordination, improving project delivery, and ensuring that organisational priorities are translated into structured activities with clearly defined responsibilities, timelines, and deliverables.
Key Responsibilities:
▪️ Support project planning, implementation schedules, budgets, and resource requirements.
▪️ Monitor project progress against approved timelines, targets, and deliverables.
▪️ Maintain project trackers, progress dashboards, and implementation monitoring systems.
▪️ Coordinate with departmental leads and facilitate cross-functional activities.
▪️ Identify implementation challenges, operational bottlenecks, and potential project risks.
▪️ Prepare project status reports, meeting minutes, presentations, and management briefs.
▪️ Support the development and implementation of organisational policies, procedures, and SOPs.
▪️ Assist in monitoring PSA’s organisational strategy and annual operational plans.
Qualifications & Experience:
▪️ Bachelor’s degree in Project Management, Business Administration, Engineering, Management Sciences, Economics, or a related discipline.
▪️ 2–4 years of relevant experience in project management, programme coordination, operations, or organisational development.
▪️ Experience in project tracking, reporting, documentation, and coordination.
▪️ Experience working with associations, non-profit organisations, development projects, or multi-departmental teams will be preferred.
Professional Skills:
▪️ Project planning, scheduling, and implementation monitoring
▪️ Performance indicators and progress reporting
▪️ Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, dashboards, and management reporting
▪️ Project management tools such as Microsoft Project, Trello, Asana, or Monday.com
▪️ Documentation, SOP development, and cross-functional coordination
▪️ Proficiency in project management tools such as ClickUp, Primavera P6, Microsoft Project, Trello, or equivalent platforms.
