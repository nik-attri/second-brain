Case study #1 is live: Linfy Beacon 🚀

Most small businesses are harder to find online than they need to be. No website, no clear presence - and finding those gaps manually is slow, inconsistent work.

So I built Linfy Beacon: a locally developed prototype that discovers public business data, assesses website presence, and drafts AI-assisted outreach - all designed to be reviewed by a human before anything goes out.

The stack: Python, Supabase, OpenStreetMap, Tavily, Anthropic and Resend.

Still a prototype - orchestration and scheduling are next. But this is the kind of practical AI work we do at Linfy Tech Solutions for small businesses.
