I think most founders are seriously underestimating what they can do with AI.

And I'm not talking about writing emails or generating social media captions.

I'm talking about things that would have taken weeks, cost thousands of pounds, or required hiring someone to implement.

And now? Well, here's some of the things we've been up to:

✨ Built our own customer service platform with an AI agent that identifies issues, drafts replies and actually takes actions, from updating orders and changing addresses to creating replacements.
✨ Built our own Shopify apps to manage offers, upselling, and free gifts, without needing another monthly subscription.
✨ Using AI to update recipes, bills of materials and manage operational tasks directly in Tallyvo, our ERP system.
✨ Making changes directly in Shopify, updating inventory, product information, building landing pages and improving SEO.
✨ Having AI organise our ideas, social media content, leads and tasks directly in ClickUp, without having to manually keep track of everything.

And the list keeps growing.

It's honestly mind-blowing how much you can do with AI, especially as a founder running a product business where there are about 300 things that need your attention every single day.
But you know what's funny?

Despite all of this, I still finish some days feeling like I haven't done enough. 😂

I think we've become so used to moving fast that we've completely forgotten how things used to take days, weeks and months of work in some cases. 

Now we get them done in hours, immediately raise the bar for ourselves and somehow find another 20 things to do.

Apparently, becoming more productive just means finding more work. 😂

I'm genuinely curious, how are you using AI in your business?
Would love to hear some interesting use cases I haven't thought of yet.
