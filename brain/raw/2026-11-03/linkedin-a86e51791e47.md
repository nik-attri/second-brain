When business operations rely on staff acting as the human integration layer between multiple software tools, manual tasks quickly eat up valuable capacity. The practical question is identifying which processes justify a bespoke build and how to approach implementation sensibly.

Key considerations from our implementation guide:

- Fix the process before automating: Automating a broken workflow simply automates the mess. Identifying high-leverage intervention points first delivers reliable compounding value.

- Bespoke workflows over SaaS silos: Off-the-shelf tools struggle with legacy databases and custom CRM fields. Custom automations built on APIs and standards like Model Context Protocol (MCP) securely link models to internal tools with defined read/write permissions.

- Focus on high-volume, rule-driven areas: Reliable returns consistently come from inbound enquiry triage, customer operations routing, and structured document processing pipelines.

- Scope upfront: Fixed-fee audits confirm where AI will actually pay off before committing to engineering, modeling the full cost of ownership including compute, hosting, and maintenance.
