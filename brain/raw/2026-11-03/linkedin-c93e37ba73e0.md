Who We Are at BST Consulting 👇
Behind every efficient financial institution are systems, processes and people that make things work.

At BST Consulting, we help financial institutions strengthen all three.

So, what exactly do we do?
🔹  Risk & Regulatory Solutions: We develop IFRS 9 ECL engines, credit risk rating systems, Basel compliance solutions and RegSuite for regulatory reporting.
🔹 Process Automation: We automate critical business processes, from tax computations and payments to regulatory reporting, credit approvals and operational workflows.
🔹 Technology Solutions: We design bespoke systems, intelligent dashboards and reporting tools built around our clients' data, models and operational needs.
🔹 Training & Talent Development: We equip professionals and organisations with practical skills in financial risk management, regulatory compliance, business analysis and technology.

Our work spans financial institutions across Africa, Asia and beyond, with 50+ projects delivered and over 2,000 professionals trained.
