Storytelling - without sounding like a CringeLord.

🔎 Insight #1 from 55 meetings with top GTM operators and enterprise hunter sellers in the last 30 days.

Never ceases to amaze me how often I land on a company website / Linkedin 'About' section and read something like:
“An AI-powered unified integrated orchestration platform that automates intelligent workflows, optimises enterprise operations and unlocks scalable insights.”

Cool. What?

From 2 decades as an operator I've sat with some incredible storytellers in GTM. The common thread? They all story tell from a human perspective to engage customers.

Example: We’re running a Founding GTM search right now for a company that’s built an agentic DBA. On it's own that really doesn't mean much. But, explain it from the buyer  perspective: You're a VP of Platform or DevOps. Because of AI, you are shipping 5x more code 5x faster. Everyone is cockahoop. More code creates more pressure downstream.  DBA's get buried. Imagine this problem at scale. 100k databases, and 300 queries per database every week. Our client has built the first agentic DBA to get work off their desk so they can focus on the most complex issues. 
✅ Pain uncovered
✅ Compelling case for investment realised
⚡️Add in the $60Bn addressable market 
➡ Killer opp to engage top gtm operators, unlocked ✅
