The fleet operators who buy fastest aren't the ones with the biggest pain.

They're the ones who already tried to solve it themselves.

The operator who built a spreadsheet system for 200 drivers and watched it break — he doesn't need convincing. He already knows Excel doesn't scale. He just needs to see that an alternative exists.

The operator who's "managing fine" with WhatsApp and Google Drive? He'll take 6 months to buy. Because he hasn't hit the wall yet.

I stopped trying to convince people who haven't felt the pain. I wait for the ones who've already tried to build the solution themselves and failed.

They convert in one call. Because they've already done the research. They just need someone who's done it before.

How do you find prospects who are ready to buy vs ones who need convincing?
