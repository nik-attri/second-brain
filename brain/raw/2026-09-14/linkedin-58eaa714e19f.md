Do you actually know who has signing authority across your 200 global bank accounts? Let’s talk about Bank Account Management (BAM). 🏦

Global expansion is great for business, but it usually leaves a chaotic trail of fragmented banking relationships. When subsidiaries open local accounts over decades, central treasury often loses visibility into what accounts exist, who can authorize payments, and how much idle cash is trapped inside them.

Here is how finance teams attempt to manage this sprawl and why a centralized tech stack is the only way to regain control.

1️⃣ SPREADSHEET TRACKING (The Governance Blind Spot)
Think of this as the "hope and pray" method of corporate governance.

👉 HOW IT WORKS: The treasury team maintains a massive, manual Excel master file that lists every known bank account, its purpose, and the authorized signers.
👉 THE BENEFIT: It costs nothing to set up and provides a basic, static inventory of your banking landscape.
👉 THE CATCH: It is almost immediately out of date. When a regional finance director leaves the company, central treasury rarely gets notified in time to remove their signing rights, leaving the company exposed to massive internal fraud risks and audit failures.


2️⃣ MANUAL KYC & MANDATES (The Administrative Bottleneck)
Think of this as drowning your most expensive talent in administrative red tape.

👉 HOW IT WORKS: When updating a banking mandate or opening a new account, treasury manually coordinates with legal to gather wet signatures, board resolutions, and KYC documents to physically mail to the bank.
👉 THE BENEFIT: It gets the job done eventually and satisfies local banking regulations.
👉 THE CATCH: It takes months to execute simple changes. Treasurers spend hours chasing executives for signatures instead of analyzing liquidity and managing financial risk.


3️⃣ CENTRALIZED VISIBILITY (The FinanceKey Advantage)
You cannot govern your cash if you don't even know where the accounts are.

👉 THE CHALLENGE: Maintaining strict governance requires replacing static spreadsheets with dynamic, real-time banking data.
👉 HOW WE HELP: FinanceKey’s universal API connectivity gives you a single, real-time dashboard of every active bank account across your entire corporate group.
👉 THE BOTTOM LINE: You can instantly audit account structures, identify unauthorized or redundant accounts, and track your global liquidity footprint. You move from reactive spreadsheet chasing to proactive, bulletproof governance.


A forgotten bank account is a major security vulnerability.
