I bought my mum our childhood home🏡❤️

Grateful is an understatment. At 19 i had a dream, and at 27 i made it come true… it’s moments like these that remind me to keep going even when the road gets bumpy. 

Everything thats happened until now has made sense and has been a stepping stone towards giving back to the people that mean the most to me. 

If you ever feel like giving up, let this be a reminder to keep on pushing. Never once have i felt comfortable - but nothing grows in comfortable environments. 

I still have hell of a journey ahead of me to keep on blessing and giving back to everyone that has supported my vision. 

Remember why you set out on your journey. 

I’ve been so obsessed with becoming a “winner” that I forget to look back and realise I’ve already won.

Every property course, every rejected deal, every 9pm call to an investor, all of it was for a moment I couldn't have explained to anyone at the start, because I wasn't doing it for a spreadsheet. I was doing it for her.

Whatever you're building right now, however slow it feels, however far off the "why" seems - it's closer than you think.

Just keep going...
