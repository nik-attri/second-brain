Friday thought: Sometimes the problem isn’t the workload. It’s the way the work gets done.

You can have a good team, good people, and plenty of experience…

…and still have a process that makes everyone’s job harder than it needs to be.

A report takes half a day because the information is in three different places.

A simple update requires checking emails, spreadsheets, and the CRM.

Someone spends Friday afternoon making sure nothing was missed during the week.

After a while, people stop questioning it.

“That’s just how we do it.”

That sentence can be expensive.

Before hiring another person or adding another software subscription, it’s worth looking at the process itself.

Where are the bottlenecks?

Where are people doing repetitive work?

Where are things getting copied, checked, or chased manually?

Sometimes the biggest improvement isn’t adding more people.

It’s removing the unnecessary work from the people you already have.

What’s one process in your business that everyone has accepted as “normal,” even though you know it could be much easier?
