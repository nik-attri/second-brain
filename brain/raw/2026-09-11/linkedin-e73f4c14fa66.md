Strategy and Operations is one of the fastest moving job categories we are seeing in 2026.

Every week we get briefed on roles that sit somewhere between "figure out why this is broken" and "now fix it." Not pure strategy. Not pure ops. Both at once, under pressure.

The problem is most candidates apply the wrong way. They lead with execution experience and bury the commercial thinking. Or they lead with strategy credentials and cannot show a single thing they actually shipped.

Hiring managers for these roles want one thing: evidence that you have diagnosed a business problem and then stayed in the room long enough to see it through.

Consultancy background with an operator mindset. That is the profile that wins.

If you are positioning yourself for a Strategy and Ops move and it is not landing, the issue is almost certainly your narrative. Not your CV.
