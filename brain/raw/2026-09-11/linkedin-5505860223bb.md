Your spreadsheet does not need a 14-slide cinematic universe.

Yet somehow that is exactly what happens when you ask Copilot to "make a presentation."

Slide 1: Q3 Performance
Slide 2: Revenue increased
Slide 3: Churn decreased
Slide 4: Pipeline remains strong

Thank you Microsoft. Very cool.

The problem was never making the slides. Copilot can make slides. The problem is getting it to figure out what the hell the slides should actually say.

So I built a prompt around one idea: turn the spreadsheet into a story before you turn it into a deck.

It starts by asking who is in the room and what decision is actually being made. Then it reads the numbers and tells you what moved.

Then, instead of hallucinating some consultant sounding explanation, it stops and asks you why.

If nobody knows why? Perfect. That becomes part of the story too.

Because this:

"September missed plan because one renewal slipped to October, and it closed on the 3rd."

is something a human can actually say out loud. Q3 Performance is not.

Spreadsheet in. Actual point out. Simple.
