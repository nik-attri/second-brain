The road to hell is paved with good intentions. So is your last data leak.
 
Nobody in your organisation woke up planning to send customer data to a third party. They had a deadline. A support ticket that needed answering in four minutes. A contract that needed summarising before a call. A quarterly report that needed drafting by end of day.
 
So the record gets pasted in. The contract gets uploaded. Not as an act of carelessness, but because the tool was open and the work was due. Your teams are moving at the speed you asked them to move.
 
Block the tools and the work does not stop. It moves to personal phones and personal accounts, where the data still leaves and you no longer see any of it. That is not less risk. That is the same risk, out of sight.
 
AI Usage Guardrails protects data before it leaves your boundary.
 
Prompts and file uploads are inspected before they reach the AI tool, against the classification policy you already run. Uploads matter as much as typing. That spreadsheet nobody scrolled to the bottom of is usually where this goes wrong.
 
Sensitive values are masked, not blocked. The AI receives realistic, correctly formatted stand-ins, so the answer comes back just as useful. Real values are restored on your side of the line, never on theirs.
 
Inspection happens inside your own environment, your VPC or your data centre. Only the protected version of a prompt or file ever reaches the public service.
 
Every event is logged and exports to the SIEM you already run. Personalised reports go back to employees, so most policy slips get corrected by the person who made them, once somebody tells them.
 
It works with #ChatGPT, #Gemini, #Claude, or whatever your teams open next.
 
The work is not compromised. The data is not compromised.
 
Masked, not blocked. Your teams keep the speed. The data stays home.
