We are Hiring!

Maamba Energy Limited is recruiting for the position of Shift Boss – Mining Production, based in Maamba, Sinazongwe District, Southern Province.

Key Responsibilities:
- Allocate shift tasks and supervise execution of approved production plans
- Monitor production, equipment utilisation, haul routes and stockpile activities
- Enforce safety standards, mine rules and operating procedures
- Identify and address hazards, delays, equipment constraints and non-compliance
- Supervise operators, crews and contractors and provide performance feedback
- Prepare accurate shift reports and ensure effective handovers
- Recommend improvements in safety, productivity and operational performance

Required Qualifications:
- Grade 12 Certificate or equivalent
- Diploma/Degree in Mining Engineering, Mine Operations, or a related discipline is preferred
- Supervisory or frontline leadership training is preferred
- Professional registration or eligibility with EIZ/ERB is advantageous

Experience:
- Minimum of 3 years' relevant experience in mining operations or production supervision.
- At least 1–2 years' experience supervising crews, operators or contractors is preferred.
- Experience in safety enforcement, equipment coordination, shift reporting and incident escalation is required.
- Open-cast coal mining and contractor coordination experience are advantageous.

If you meet the above qualifications and experience, submit your application enclosing a detailed CV, certified copies of qualifications certified by the Zambia Qualifications Authority (ZAQA), and three (3) traceable references, to applications@maambaenergy.com, with the subject line "Application for Shift Boss – [Applicant Name]", not later than 18th September 2026.
