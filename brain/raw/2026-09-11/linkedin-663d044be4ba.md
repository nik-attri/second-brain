A client's sales team was drowning.

They were burning $12K/month on ad spend, but every single lead had to be manually reviewed before sales could touch it. Sales reps spent 4-6 hours a day just sorting through garbage—unqualified prospects, tire kickers, wrong fit. The conversion rate was abysmal because by the time a rep got a qualified lead, half the day was gone.

The problem wasn't the ads. The problem was they had no filtering layer.

We built a custom AI qualification system that scored every inbound lead on 5 revenue-relevant signals: budget confirmation, buying intent, industry fit, timeline, and authority. The AI auto-routed qualified leads to sales within minutes. Unqualified leads got a different workflow.

Results:
- Sales conversion jumped 34%
- Cost per acquired customer dropped 41%
- Sales reps reclaimed 3-4 hours daily

The lesson: Manual processes don't scale. Neither does bad data. But if you filter at the source with AI doing the heavy lifting, your team's time becomes productive instead of wasteful.

This is what actually moves revenue.
