Operational resilience is not a quarterly risk report. It means knowing what is at risk, how disruption affects mission delivery, and whether remediation reduces exposure while conditions change.

For public-sector agencies, IRM and SecOps must operate as one risk-to-remediation loop. SecOps supplies evidence: active vulnerabilities and incidents, failing controls, affected assets and dependencies, and verified remediation.
