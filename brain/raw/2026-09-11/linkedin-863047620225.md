🎬 Corporate Diaries, Episode 1: "Welcome to the Set"
 
"We know something, but we don't know everything." Iss gap mein hi asli seekhna hota hai — theory mein nahi, jab actual kaam saamne aata hai tab.
 
Last week Interkhojo mein Market Research Associate Intern ke role mein join kiya. Socha tha data analytics ka kaam hai, mujhe aata hai. Pehle hi hafte mein pata chal gaya — aata hai, par utna nahi jitna laga tha. Kabhi kabhi lagta hai main khud ek rom-com ke set par hoon, jahan main roz khud ko ek naya "interesting" trap mein fasa leti hoon —  😅
 
Plot twist #1 — data ready-made nahi mila, collect karna tha. Ek pre-built bot use kiya uske liye, aur wahi database wali knowledge jo maine pichli interview ke time seekhi thi (tab laga tha bas interview ke liye seekh rahi hoon), wo achanak first real step ban gayi. Funny hai kaise chhoti si cheez kaam aa jaati hai.
 
Phir Python mein data cleaning, standard format, DAX queries, Power BI report — ye part familiar tha toh smooth gaya, Gen AI workflows ne bhi kaafi time bacha diya.
 
Plot twist #2 — bot se collect hui data ko review karte time kuch inconsistencies mili, AI bhi galti karta hai turns out. Wahan se manually check karke sahi kiya, filters laga ke relevant data hi rakha.
 
Ek aur galti bhi hui beech mein — backup folder submit kar diya jisme duplicates the, feedback aaya, wapas se pura data review kiya, cleaning dobara ki. Ab submit karne se pehle do baar check karti hoon, seekh gayi hai ye — attention to detail yahan se aayi.
 
Aur ye sirf dashboard banane tak nahi rukta — data study karna, present karna, research report likhni hai recommendations ke saath. Honestly, beech mein itna kuch chal raha hota hai ki check-in karna bhi bhool jaati hoon kabhi kabhi. Par kaam time pe ho raha hai, that's what matters abhi.
 
Still figuring a lot of this out. But ek hafta hi hua hai, aur already itna clear hai ki "knowing something" aur "knowing everything" mein bahut fasla hai. Iss show ka next episode dekhna baaki hai. 🎬
