Picture a sales floor five years from now.

Half the reps are drowning in admin work, guessing at what worked on last week's calls, hoping the next quarter looks like the last.

The other half? They're already 10 steps ahead — call grading itself, insights surfacing before the rep even asks, and every rep improving at a pace that used to take years of coaching.

That's not a prediction. That's the sales org Chris McDonough (VP of Sales at Campfire) is building right now — and he just laid out the entire playbook in one conversation.

If you lead a sales team — or want to — this is required listening.
Here's what stood out:

 𝐓𝐡𝐞 10𝐱 𝐑𝐞𝐩 𝐈𝐬 𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐢𝐧𝐠. Forget the "AI will replace reps" narrative. Chris's take is sharper: AI won't flatten the playing field, it'll blow it wide open. The reps who use it to self-learn and execute faster will pull away from the pack — hard.

𝐙𝐞𝐫𝐨 𝐓𝐞𝐜𝐡 𝐃𝐞𝐛𝐭, 𝐁𝐲 𝐃𝐞𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐧. Instead of duct-taping AI onto old dashboards, Chris built an org where every call is automatically graded against real win/loss data. No more manual admin. No more guesswork. Just reps getting sharper, faster.

𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐒𝐃𝐑 (𝐒𝐚𝐥𝐞𝐬 𝐃𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐥𝐨𝐩𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐑𝐞𝐩𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞) 𝐁𝐞𝐧𝐜𝐡 𝐈𝐬 𝐘𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐅𝐮𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐞𝐫𝐬𝐡𝐢𝐩 𝐁𝐞𝐧𝐜𝐡. In a world obsessed with AI outbound, Chris makes the case that your SDR org is still your most valuable long-term asset — the talent pipeline that scales your culture from the inside out.

𝐒𝐞𝐥𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐁𝐞𝐲𝐨𝐧𝐝 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐒𝐢𝐥𝐢𝐜𝐨𝐧 𝐕𝐚𝐥𝐥𝐞𝐲 𝐁𝐮𝐛𝐛𝐥𝐞. Not every deal closes on brand recognition and VC pedigree. Chris gets tactical about winning in the "real economy" — where local references and in-person trust still beat a flashy logo.

𝐓𝐡𝐞 "𝐖𝐨𝐫𝐤𝐝𝐚𝐲 𝐑𝐮𝐥𝐞." A simple, funny, oddly brilliant heuristic for knowing exactly when it's time to leave a scaling company and jump back into early-stage chaos.

An AI-native org isn't about bolting AI onto what you already have. It's about building the stack so data flows cleanly from end to end — so the output is only as good as the input is clean.
