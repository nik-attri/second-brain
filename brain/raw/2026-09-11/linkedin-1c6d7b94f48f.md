📂 Improving Our Performance as Document Controllers Through Digital Tools

In today's fast-paced project environment, Document Controllers play a vital role in ensuring information is accurate, accessible, and available when needed. By combining strong document control practices with digital tools, we can improve efficiency, compliance, and team collaboration.

✅ Maintain accurate document naming conventions and revision control
 ✅ Keep document registers updated and error-free
 ✅ Monitor review cycles and turnaround times
 ✅ Share lessons learned and best practices with the team
 ✅ Perform regular audits of controlled documents.

🔹 SharePoint Tips for Document Controllers

✅ Create structured document libraries based on project disciplines and document types.

✅ Use metadata columns instead of relying only on folders. This makes searching and filtering documents much faster.

✅ Enable version history to track changes and quickly restore previous document versions when necessary.

✅ Configure document approval workflows to automate reviews and reduce manual follow-ups.

✅ Create custom views for different stakeholders such as Engineering, QA/QC, Procurement, and Construction teams.

✅ Use document alerts and notifications to stay informed about revisions, approvals, and updates.

✅ Apply permissions strategically to protect confidential documents while maintaining team collaboration.

✅ Utilizes co-authoring features to allow multiple users to work on documents simultaneously without creating duplicate files.

✅ Develop dashboard pages to monitor document status, overdue actions, and key performance indicators (KPIs).

✅ Implement retention policies to ensure compliance with project and organizational requirements.

✅ Use SharePoint search effectively by tagging documents with keywords, project numbers, discipline codes, and document categories.

✅ Integrate SharePoint with Microsoft Teams for seamless document access, collaboration, and communication.

✅ Connect Power Automate with SharePoint to automate document transmittals, review reminders, and approval notifications.
