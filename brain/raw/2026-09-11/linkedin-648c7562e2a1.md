🤖 Meet the Multi-Purpose Lab Robot, the result of a Master's thesis completed at INVITE GmbH.

Designed and built from scratch, from the initial concept to a fully functional prototype, the system automates a buffer preparation process and demonstrates how automation can support laboratory workflows. ⚙️ 🔬 

Key features of the Multi-Purpose Lab Robot:
✅ Modular platform architecture for flexible adaptation to new laboratory workflows
✅ Scalable automation concept that enables continuous expansion with low integration effort
✅ Advanced control software based on a dual state-machine architecture
✅ Comprehensive system integration, spanning robotics, machine vision, analytical equipment, industrial computing, and custom-developed hardware
✅ Experimentally validated performance, achieving dosing accuracies within ~0.05-0.23 g of target weight

The project was developed by our Master's student Rugved Jabade from Technische Universität Hamburg under the supervision and guidance of Marius Kling and Aditya Tandon, with valuable support from Prof. Dr. Kay Smarsly, Dr. Carl-Helmut Coulon, and many colleagues along the way.
We are proud to have supported this work and to see it evolve into a fully functional prototype through interdisciplinary collaboration, technical expertise, and dedication.
At the same time, this achievement marks the successful completion of an important milestone: a Master's degree in Mechatronics at TU Hamburg. 🎓 🚀 
Congratulations on this accomplishment and thank you to everyone who contributed along the way.
