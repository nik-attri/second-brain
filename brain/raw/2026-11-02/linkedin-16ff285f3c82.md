Not everything in Amazon Ads should be automated. And not everything should be managed manually.

The real challenge is knowing where to draw the line.
Automation can save hours of work. But if you automate the wrong decisions, you can end up making mistakes faster.

Here’s how I think about it.

⚙️ What I would automate:

Repetitive bid adjustments based on clear performance rules and defined limits.

Budget monitoring to flag campaigns spending too quickly or approaching their limits.

Performance alerts for sudden changes in CPC, spend, sales, or conversions.

Routine reporting so the team spends less time collecting data and more time interpreting it.

These tasks follow patterns. Once the rules are clear, automation can help teams act faster and more consistently.

What I would keep under human judgment:

Major budget shifts when the decision affects overall profitability or growth priorities.

Inventory-sensitive decisions when scaling ads could create stockout risks.

Strategic keyword decisions where low sales today might not tell the whole story.

Unusual performance changes that need context before any action is taken.

A campaign with zero sales isn't always a bad campaign. A high ACOS isn't always a reason to cut spend. And a winning campaign isn't always ready to scale.

The numbers tell you what is happening. Context helps you decide what to do next.
That’s why I believe the best Amazon Ads workflow isn't full automation. It's automating the repeatable work while keeping humans in control of decisions that need business context.
