Why Do We Still Use Manual Processing Sometimes?​
Mechanized production is essential for improving efficiency and ensuring product consistency. However, not every process is suitable for automation.​
In actual production, manual processing may still be the better choice in two special situations:​
① Oversized Magnets​
Some magnets are too large to fit into existing equipment due to machine capacity limitations. In these cases, certain processes must be completed manually.​
② Small Order Quantities​
For small batches, adjusting machines and switching production setups may take more time than manual processing. It can be more efficient to handle these orders manually while keeping the machines running for products better suited to automated production.​
True manufacturing efficiency is not about automating every process. It's about choosing the most suitable production method based on product specifications and order quantities.
