Congratulations to Model ML and Five on being named to the 2026 CB Insights Fintech 100! 🎉

This year's list highlights how some of the most exciting companies in fintech are tackling big problems behind the scenes, and we're proud to see two of our portfolio companies leading the way:

🤖 Model ML, by co-founders Chaz Englander and Arnie Englander, is building an AI-powered operating system that automates complex workflows across investment banking, asset management and advisory, from research and due diligence to financial analysis.

✋ Five, by founder Maria Marti, is transforming the checkout experience with biometric palm payments, enabling consumers to pay in as little as 0.3 seconds without a card, phone or physical contact. Its technology makes payments faster and more seamless for consumers and merchants alike.

Both companies demonstrate the breadth of innovation reshaping financial services, and we're excited to see their progress recognised on a global stage. Well-deserved!
