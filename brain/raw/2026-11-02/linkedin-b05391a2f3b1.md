Why Your AI Strategy Is Burning Out Your Best People. 

We need to stop pretending that handing employees an AI tool is "saving them time."
Over the past year, talking to founders, HR leaders, and managers across Singapore and Indonesia, I keep hearing the same pattern:
Companies rolled out enterprise AI licenses expecting an instant productivity miracle. Instead, their people are more exhausted than they were before.
Here is the unfiltered reality:
When you give an overwhelmed employee a tool that generates text, slides, or code in seconds, you don't reduce their workload.

You raise their quota.
Managers start expecting 3x the output. Inboxes fill with auto-generated noise that nobody has the capacity to read, let alone audit. Deadlines get compressed. And instead of finishing work earlier, people spend their evenings checking AI outputs and putting out fires.
Then HR comes in with a "Mental Health Awareness Week" and a subscription to a meditation app.

Let’s be honest:
A team working 14-hour days drowning in manual reconciliation doesn’t need breathwork. They need someone to fix their broken workflow.

A team running on pure cognitive overload doesn't need another tech tool dumped on their dashboard. They need leaders who know how to protect their cognitive bandwidth.

AI doesn't burn people out. Lazy implementation does.
If you don't teach your managers how to strip away redundant work before automating it, AI just turns into a machine for manufacturing burnout at scale.
At Intelligent Teaching, we don’t teach AI adoption in a silo, and we don't treat employee wellbeing like a side perk. We teach them together: automating the operational grind so teams can actually breathe, think, and perform sustainably.
Stop buying more tools until you have fixed the human workflow behind them.
