🚀 We’re excited to announce the finalists for the Digital Change Maker Award! 🚀

The response to this year's award has been incredible, with a huge number of outstanding nominations showcasing the talent, innovation and leadership driving digital transformation across our industry. Selecting the finalists was no easy task, so congratulations to our top five shortlisted nominees:

🏆 Carolina Jarreta Aranaz
Driving digital transformation through innovation, governance and capability building, with industry-leading work in BIM, digital delivery, asset digitalisation and ISO 19650 implementation.

🏆 Nifras Khan,RIBA, Intl.Assoc.AIA, LEED GA
Transforming infrastructure delivery through practical digital solutions that automate complex processes, improve information management and drive measurable efficiencies across multidisciplinary teams.

🏆 Pouya Rezaei
Creator of Brooks Connect, a company-wide digital ecosystem that has revolutionised business operations, digitised hundreds of workflows and delivered significant efficiency gains across the organisation.

🏆 Rick Titulaer 
A leader in computational design and AI adoption, helping deploy innovative digital tools globally while building digital capability through training, mentoring and knowledge sharing.

🏆 Dr. Salah Omar Omran
A respected digital delivery leader, educator and industry advocate whose work in BIM, information management and professional development is helping shape the future of digital delivery across the built environment.

The Digital Change Maker Award recognises individuals who aren't just keeping pace with change, but actively driving it through innovation, leadership and measurable impact. Whether by developing transformative technologies, implementing new digital processes or creating opportunities for others, these finalists are helping move our industry forward.

👏 Congratulations to all five finalists and thank you to everyone who submitted a nomination.
