🚨 WE ARE HIRING – CARE COORDINATOR | IMMEDIATE START
📍 Location: Croydon, London
🏢 Company: MHILD Care Ltd

💼 Job Type: Full-Time | Permanent
We are looking for an experienced and proactive Care Coordinator to join our Supported Living Services team.


Key Responsibilities:
Develop, review and update care plans, support plans and risk assessments.
Manage staff rotas, shift allocations and emergency cover.
Coordinate daily supported living operations.
Ensure person-centred support and service quality.
Monitor safeguarding, compliance and CQC standards.
Liaise with support workers, team leaders, families and healthcare professionals.
Maintain accurate records and support quality audits.

Requirements:
Previous experience in Supported Living services.
Experience in care planning, support planning and risk assessments.
Strong rota management and organisational skills.
Good understanding of safeguarding and CQC requirements.
NVQ/RQF Level 3 in Health & Social Care is desirable.
✅ Immediate joiners preferred!
