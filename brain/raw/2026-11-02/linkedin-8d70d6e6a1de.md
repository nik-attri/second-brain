So I had a funny discussion with a friend from school a few weeks back. He'd been following my content for a while, but couldn't really figure out what exactly I do.

I mean, I get it. Over the years, I've done everything from copywriting and graphic design to video editing and even complex automated spreadsheets (exhibit a: the spreadsheet from hell), funnily enough. But at FC, I have an amazing team that takes care of all of this stuff. I mostly just give them a direction and they work their magic. So, that got me thinking what do I actually do now?

Took me a while to figure that out, but what I really do now is... *drum roll* I see patterns where most people see noise, and then I use those patterns to solve problems.

Specifically, I work with established B2B businesses in traditional industries like construction/contracting, industrial manufacturing, heavy equipment, etc.

These established companies have real operating capabilities and high value projects, but their buying processes are super complex that can involve several decision-makers, qualification stages and lengthy evaluations. Not to mention the certification and regulatory requirements that they frequently contend with.

I help them identify and fix the gaps in their branding, marketing and sales collateral that make it harder for buyers to understand what they do, assess their capabilities and choose them. I help them present themselves better so their buyers find it easier to choose them.

Instead of burying buyers in confusing technical jargon, we fix the friction points across their positioning, brand identity, and entire sales suite from capability statements to pitch decks, and even social media content.

We diagnose exactly where their buyers are getting stuck, fix the trigger, and smooth out any wrinkles we come across.

The end goal is to make the sales process easier for the people selling the business and the people evaluating it.
