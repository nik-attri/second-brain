What the hell the job market has become?

Well, since it has became abnormally slow on projects/service for what I do the most, I started interviewing for some roles I found attractive hoping to land something and putting my business in the freezer. However, I've noticed something awful; Critical thinking and having a problem solving mindset seems to be a red flag for recruiters and companies now. Added to the fact that many recruiters don't see with good eyes the fact that I've had multiple entrepreneurships.

They're looking for "yes men/women everywhere", rather than people capable of solving real problems, generate value and do the work in a timely manner, within budget and creating/maintaing good relationship with internal and external customers. And the few that are not following that pattern are either straigh AI interviews or pay below the market rate.

Gril/Boy! I've been fixing/rescuing projects, putting out fires, wearing multiple hats, leading people, keeping the morale of teams high, and saving money while keeping customers happy and even rescuing accounts about to fell of the revenue. I've changed the reds to greens in many spreadsheets, CRM's, ERP's, and financial balances, in both my native language and english, in Mexico and US.
Beisdes that, give me tools and I'll make things happen, give me leads and I'll sale, give me an idea and probably I'll make it reality, I don’t freeze up or fall into inaction at the slightest shift in the wind. I'm an action guy, that's what having entrepreneurships had taught me. And yet just because I'm multidisciplinary with three fingers of common sense, and able to solve problems I am the problem?

FYI, no problem gets solved by just agreeing to everything the chain of command says. Assertiveness, confrontantion, demanding, and even sometimes raising the voice is needed. It's part of being an adult and a professional to face the problems and have the determination to fix things rather than just pretend things are going well, when they aren't while people (many times very talented people) is just burning out because of lack of action.

Hell!... I'm about to quit this search and just wait for next year hoping for better horizon.

Sorry, I needed to vent it, I can't be the only one that is experiencing this crap!
