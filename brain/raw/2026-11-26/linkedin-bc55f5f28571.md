Every ERP vendor now says "AI-powered."
 
Imagine you buy one.
 
The demo looks brilliant.
 
Smart insights. Forecasts. Alerts on your phone.
 
Then you go live.
 
Stock gets entered once a week. Scrap goes into a register. Half the orders still move on WhatsApp.
 
And the AI starts giving you very confident answers built on very wrong numbers.
 
So before you say yes or no to AI, ask a few honest questions:
 
Is your data entered daily, or in a weekend batch?
 Does the AI explain why it's flagging something, or just show a red dot?
 Will your supervisor act on it, or ignore it like every other alert?
 Is the AI doing real work, or is it a chatbot bolted onto old software?
 
These questions matter because AI doesn't fix a factory. It amplifies whatever is already there.
 
At Onexis, we built AI insights to do one simple job: tell the owner what needs attention today. Late orders, stock running low, scrap creeping up. In plain words, with the reason behind it.
 
Of course, AI is not magic.
 
If the floor doesn't feed the system, no AI will save you. Fix the data habit first.
 
But once your numbers are live, refusing AI just means doing manually what software could flag for you every morning.
