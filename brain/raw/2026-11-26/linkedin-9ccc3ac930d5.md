In a world run on messy Excel sheets...

One system. Built to end it.
Meet IronLedger, a multi tenant B2B SaaS platform that runs an entire gym. Members. Billing. Front desk check ins. All in one place.
This is not a side project. This is production. This is business.

1) Every gym gets its own sealed workspace.
Owners. Trainers. Front Desk. Each role locked to exactly what it needs. Nothing more.

2) At the top, a Super Admin Control Plane.
Passcode plus TOTP 2FA. One time recovery codes. Full audit logs. And when a grace period ends, an automated kill switch pulls the plug.

3) Migration? One click.
Drop in your Excel or Google Sheet. Columns mapped. Duplicate phone numbers wiped. Every member linked to their plan, active or expired.

4) The internet dies. The front desk does not.
An installable PWA with HMAC signed QR member cards. Check ins queue offline and sync the second you reconnect. Updates roll out on their own.

5) Every rupee, accounted for.
Integer paise math. Idempotent payments. End of day cash reconciliation. Private S3 presigned uploads. Instant PDF receipts.

6) Built to be attacked. Built to hold.
PostgreSQL with strict rollback guarantees. 33 automated unit and security tests, all aimed at tenant isolation and billing rules.
