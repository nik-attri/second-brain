Traditional CRMs and BI tools have a fatal flaw: they only show you what happened yesterday. They don’t tell you what to do tomorrow—or help you execute it.
That’s why we built TedariX Business Twin—and why we are currently in our final pre-launch stretch.
Instead of forcing business owners to dig through complex tables, we unified four critical engines into one proactive decision-support system:
• Company Analytics: Real-time financial & operational health check.
• Market & Opportunity Discovery: Finding new buyers, suppliers, and untapped demand.
• Integrated CRM: Seamless pipeline tracking without manual clutter.
• ESYAR (AI Voice Assistant): Your executive co-pilot—delivering insights and next-step recommendations by voice.
Our roadmap is clear: Launch first to empower Turkey’s ~4 million SMEs, then scale globally.
We are currently in discussions for early-stage investment and strategic acquisition opportunities ahead of our rollout.
