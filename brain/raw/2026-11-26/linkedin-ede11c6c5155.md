💡 E-invoicing can do much more than simplify compliance. But businesses need to rethink how they use it.

As Eugen Bursuc, Accounting Director at Accace Romania, explains, mandatory reporting creates structured data that opens the door to automation, better control and more useful business insights.

Companies that treat e-invoicing purely as a reporting obligation risk adding another manual process to their existing workflows. Those that integrate it into their operations can automate the entire invoice lifecycle: from creation and validation to submission, receipt, matching and posting.

The real value comes from making structured data work for the business.
