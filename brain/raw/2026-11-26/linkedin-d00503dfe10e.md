Any other Graphic/UI designers getting a bit nervous? Genuinely considered retraining as a joiner or a floor tiler.

People I meet poke me ("So, aren’t you worried that your job will be taken by blah blah?"). Omair hides nervousness, acts non-defensive, confident, uses rehearsed bit… ‘average’, blah ‘taste’, blah ‘judgement’. 

I should probably just close my eyes and nod slowly instead. Over the last three months, watching AI reshape design has been scary. Felt like my job is slipping away.

Back to herringbone oak parquet flooring? I love the world of interior design - it’s aesthetic, you see real physical progress, and you get exercise doing the fitting. Odd thing is while I’m browsing Screwfix for knee pads and laser levels, I kept getting a steady stream of freelance clients.

Some clients have new AI toys, and they’re realising they need an experienced designer to help them turn experiments into something good and viable. 

"We love how fast this is, we just don’t want it to look like AI."

I’m pretty excited now that I've been using Claude a lot. I use everything, and I'm software agnostic, but manually laying out 130 pages or screens of componentised, grid-based work is dead.

I'll use my skills to help teams turn fast, messy ideas and rough AI prototypes into cohesive, launch-ready print/digital products and brands. Yes AI spins up a really good draft in an afternoon. Getting something to 70% done has never been quicker (but that final 30% je ne sais quoi is what separates it from a design people actually trust and can use).

Be a strategic design guy. I’m the one to bridges that gap between fast layout and UX/UI experimentation and genuine, tasteful, human craft. The age-old rule still stands for any design: The right words + good visuals = real flavour. (I’ve been watching a lot of Marco Pierre White clips)

Take the early concepts and elevate them with:
• Typographic excellence & readability: we still read words on 2D surfaces. It has to be right, balanced, effortless to digest and beautiful.
• Distinct brand personality: who wants to create forgettable shit? 
• Intuitively easy and visually interesting user flows.
• Authentic visual direction: No mannequin-like, plastic AI aesthetic with imagery that gets human trust (your audience isn’t stupid).

How’s the change feeling for you? Let’s do some group therapy.
