🤖 VSUJ / ASJE / SICA Legal Technology and Operations Practice Group: Event Recap!

💡 How are AI agents reshaping the way in-house legal teams work?

On 18 November 2026, we brought together legal professionals at Restaurant Bauernschänke in Zurich for an engaging evening of insights, discussions and networking, exploring the opportunities, challenges and practical implications of agentic AI in legal practice.

🔎 Here are our four key takeaways from the panel discussions:

🔷 1. Agentic workflows create greatest value where you can let them run.

The panel agreed: automate frequent, low-value tasks as far as possible, and keep legal judgement with lawyers.

🔷 2. Buying and customizing is faster than building from scratch.

A platform you can adapt to your own workflows gets you to market fastest, with manageable maintenance. Building completely in-house only makes sense where no tool fits your specific requirements.

🔷 3. Accountability is designed in, not added later.

Keep a human at the decision point and log it. That gives you an audit trail you can show when questions come. For global teams, privilege differs by jurisdiction, so the safe approach is to set what may go into an AI tool at a level that works for lawyers everywhere.

🔷 4. Legal teams will get smaller, more specialised and more technical.

Routine work moves to agents; lawyers increasingly verify AI output in their area of expertise. New skills are needed: understanding the IT landscape, system integration and AI architecture, while still being able to judge whether the AI is legally right.
