Clients expect a consultant to have every answer. Dean Schaffer, a Principal Technical Consultant at Adaptavist, builds trust by saying "I don't know yet, but here's how we'll find out," and then finding out.

Dean is our next Maestro, one of the champions inside Tempo Software's partner network. He also spots the workarounds teams have stopped seeing: a manual export every Friday, a spreadsheet that shadows the tool. After five minutes of watching someone work, he'll ask, "You know that can be automated, right?"

What excites him about Tempo: Tempo Capacity Insights captures effort from Jira worklogs, calendars, and git repositories without manual entry. Workforce Intelligence ties AI usage, human effort, and cost to the Jira work behind them. Finance, delivery, and leadership all get a seat at the table, as Dean puts it.
