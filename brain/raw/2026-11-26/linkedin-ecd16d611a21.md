Dubai might be tax-free.

But bloody hell, it's not cheap.

I think there's a perception amongst people back in the UK that you move to Dubai, stop paying income tax and suddenly everybody is swimming in cash.

It's not the reality for 'ordinary' folk.

When you start adding up:
- School fees
- Renting a family villa
- Health insurance
- Eating out
- Keeping the kids in sports
(And trying to leave a Spinneys without spending AED 500.)

Some recent surveys from Dubai residents threw up some interesting numbers.

A third have made significant lifestyle adjustments to manage rising costs.

20% have seriously considered relocating.

But here's the bit I found particularly interesting.

Even amongst those who have seriously considered leaving, over 50% still intend to spend another decade here.

And I can understand why.

Most of the people I knock about with aren't here just for the tax benefits anymore.

Their kids are settled in school. They've built businesses and friendships. They enjoy the lifestyle.

Uprooting all of that isn't quite as simple as doing a comparison of living costs on a spreadsheet.

Dubai isn't necessarily somewhere you move to live cheaply.

For many people, it's somewhere they're prepared to pay more to live.

The question is how much more.

I'd be interested to hear from people who've lived here for five years or more.

Do you still think Dubai represents good value for the quality of life you get?

P.S. For me, the opportunity, the lifestyle, and safety- I don't think many places can match it. I can't do another summer here, though.
