*I used to think being a Virtual Assistant meant knowing everything.

This week in class, I learned it’s actually about 3 things:

*1. Self-motivation — Can you work without someone watching you?

*2. Time Management — Can you handle multiple tasks without dropping one?

*3. Email Etiquette— Can you reply clearly, politely, and quickly?

Hard skills like data entry can be taught.
Tools like Trello can be learned.

But showing up, managing your time, and communicating well,That’s what makes a client trust you.

I’m still a beginner, but I’m building those skills every single day.
