I'm still going to manually edit my videos. 

There’s a hard push right now to get Claude to do everything for you. Run your business, make your graphics, edit your videos...

But there’s an art to editing. And even if you hand it over to AI, you still need to understand what makes a good edit to know what to ask for. 

Editing gives you a second chance to tell the story. 

Example: 
You could have a shot of a cat sitting on the street. 

With the right cuts and music it could suddenly become a shot of a cat on the hunt for it's next meal.  

And if you’re building a personal brand or a business, the way you edit is part of how people recognise you. Why spend all that time building a brand that feels like you, then un-do it by creating a video that looks like everyone else's?

We got tired of AI writing pretty quickly (and that horrendous dash). I think we’ll get just as tired of seeing the same cuts, captions and pacing in every video.

Sorry AI, you lose this one.
