What happens when a global brand outgrows the setup behind its products?

Björn Borg has grown from an underwear label into a global sports apparel brand, selling to women, men and kids across international markets. Behind the success, the team were managing product listings to every single market and channel manually and ran their 39 different product feeds on a setup an agency had built ten years earlier.

For every change they wanted to do they went through an agency and an external developer before reaching Google or Meta. Weeks of waiting with costs at every step and the frustration behind disapproved products.
Scaling on manual workflows and legacy feeds wasn't an option.
That's where Emfas came in.

Today, Björn Borg runs its product data and feeds in-house, in one place, owned by their own team without costly lead times and pending developer tickets.

The results:
 → 39 live feeds, run in-house
 → 13 country feeds launched in 3 days
 → Changes that took weeks now done in minutes
 → Lowest disapproval rates ever
 → Early ChatGPT feed outperformed standard campaigns

"It has changed our feed quality, but it has also changed how we work. It feels like we have only scratched the surface, and it is already so much better."
- Julia Törnqvist, Product Data Specialist, Björn Borg

Product data is now a full-time strategic role at Björn Borg. 

Julia's only regret? Not starting sooner.

Thank you Julia Törnqvist, Lina Liljebladh, Robin Salazar and the whole Björn Borg team 🙌
