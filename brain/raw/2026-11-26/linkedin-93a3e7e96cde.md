Most founders don't need another "developer who can code." They need someone who can take an idea and turn it into a deployed, working system in the shortest time possible.
Last week I was working on an AI extraction workflow. The client's pain point wasn't "I don't have AI," it was "my team spends 3 hours every day manually copying data from emails."
What I did: Used an OpenAI agent + webhook to automatically turn unstructured client conversations into structured JSON and push it to their CRM.
Result: That manual operation went from 3 hours to 12 minutes of review.
The tech stack isn't the point. The point is: your team's time should be spent on high-value decisions, not copy-paste.
If your business has a workflow that's "drowning in manual labor," let's talk about whether it can be automated.
