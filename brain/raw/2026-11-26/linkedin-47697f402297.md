Finding good people is a hell of a hustle. 
You look and look, and then one day you’re sitting across from someone who says something so smart or so funny that you think, I want this person in the room with me.

And then, because this is agency life, an account goes away and you have to open a spreadsheet and there’s the name of somebody it took you blood to find. And you think, How the hell do I keep this person?

Being surrounded by talented people is one of my favorite things in the world. It makes me happy. I love walking into a meeting thinking I’ve figured something out and having somebody say, “Wait, why are we doing it that way?” And then they say something that makes me see the whole thing differently. I want to work with people who wow me with their wit, their smarts, what they know. People who teach me something every day.

Finding those people is one of the most important things I do as a director. And some days, figuring out how to keep them is the hardest part of running an agency.
La Colectiva
