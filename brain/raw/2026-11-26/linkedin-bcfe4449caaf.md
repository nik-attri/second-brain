Stop automating your mess!

I keep seeing businesses approach AI as though it's going to magically fix years of operational dysfunction.

𝗜𝘁 𝘄𝗼𝗻'𝘁.

If your information is scattered across emails, spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages and six different documents, your team is constantly chasing answers and every significant decision still requires your approval, introducing AI isn't necessarily going to solve those problems.

It could amplify them.
Congratulations. You've potentially just created a faster, more efficient mess.

This is why I approach AI as a process engineer.

I ask:
• Why does everything still run through you?
• Where is your team spending unnecessary time?
• Where is information getting stuck?
• What keeps slipping through the cracks?
• Which decisions genuinely require your involvement?
• Which processes shouldn't even exist anymore?

Then we identify which process, if we fixed it, would make the biggest difference to your business.

We simplify it. Restructure it. Build intelligent systems around it. Get your team comfortable using it.

And then we move on to the next one.

❌ I'm not interested in introducing more technology for the sake of it ❌

I'm interested in creating a business where your team knows what to do, information is where it needs to be and you don't have to manually check everything six times.

AI is incredibly powerful.
But throwing powerful technology at a fundamentally chaotic operation isn't a strategy.
It's an expensive experiment.

Because before fore we introduce AI, let's untangle the mess that's keeping you tied to your business. The scattered information, the endless approvals, the unnecessary processes and all the knowledge that exists exclusively inside your head.

Marie Kondo your business first. GET IT ORGANISED AND MINIMISE THE MESS. Then introduce the magic.
