Position: Site Manager Industry: Water / Wastewater / Utilities / Civil Engineering Job Purpose To manage and oversee the day-to-day activities on site, ensuring projects are delivered safely, efficiently and in accordance with programme, quality and contractual requirements. Key Responsibilities   Manage and supervise all site activities, ensuring works are completed safely and on schedule.   Coordinate site operatives, subcontractors and specialist contractors.   Ensure works are carried out in accordance with drawings, specifications and method statements.   Monitor daily progress against project programmes and report any delays or issues.   Ensure compliance with Health & Safety legislation, site procedures and environmental requirements.   Conduct site inductions, toolbox talks and regular safety inspections.   Manage site resources, materials, plant and equipment.   Liaise with Project Managers, clients, engineers and subcontractors.   Identify and resolve site issues, escalating any significant concerns.   Maintain accurate site records, daily reports and quality documentation.   Ensure completed works meet the required quality standards and are ready for inspection and handover.   Essential Requirements   Previous experience as a Site Manager within construction, engineering or infrastructure.   Experience managing site teams and subcontractors.   Background in M&E, MEICA and/or Civil Engineering projects.   Good understanding of construction methods, drawings and specifications.   Strong knowledge of Health & Safety requirements.   Excellent organisational, communication and leadership skills.   Ability to manage site activities and work to tight project deadlines.   SMSTS qualification.   CSCS card.   Desirable   Previous experience within the Water and Wastewater industry.   Experience working on Scottish Water, United Utilities, Severn Trent or similar utility projects.   First Aid qualification.   EUSR / SHEA Water or relevant industry-specific safety training.   The ideal candidate will be an experienced Site Manager with a strong engineering or construction background, capable of taking responsibility for site operations, leading teams and ensuring projects are delivered safely, efficiently and to the required standards.
