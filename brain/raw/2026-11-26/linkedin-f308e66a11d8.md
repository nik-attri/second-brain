Weston Homes Gets a New Owner

Nearly 40 years of founder-led ownership, gone. Private equity firm Endless has bought Weston Homes.

Bob Weston started the business in 1987. He's stepping back as chairman, but he'll stay involved. Peter Gore carries on as CEO.

Here's the bit that jumps out. The builder's loss-making. It completed 595 homes last year, up from 537. So volumes are growing and it's still losing money. That's a tough place to be.
It's been shifting away from private sales too, towards affordable housing and build-to-rent through Weston Partnerships. And the deal promises fresh funding, which tells you something about why now.

In my opinion, when prices slide and mortgages cost more, a builder that leans on selling to individual buyers gets squeezed first. Deep-pocketed new owners tend to turn up shortly after.
So is this one family firm, or the first of many? Will other founder-led builders hand over the keys before the market turns?

New owners always open the spreadsheets first. If the appraisals and DCF assumptions don't stack up, it gets awkward fast. Stress-testing those numbers is what we do at 6% Training.
