A 70-hour close week isn't a badge of honor for a CFO.

It's a symptom of broken technology.

Deloitte's Finance Trends 2027 report — surveying 1,434 finance leaders across 26 countries — just dropped a number that should stop every finance executive cold:

EQB Inc. used agentic AI to replicate a key quarterly report with 91% accuracy.

A process that took several people 3 full days?

Done in 10 minutes.

And Wolters Kluwer's Future Ready CFO Report (1,672 senior finance leaders globally) confirms the urgency:
→ 87% of finance leaders say AI is already expanding what's expected of the CFO
→ 85% believe AI will reshape their role within the next 12 months
→ Yet only 18% of finance teams are truly digitally advanced

The gap between those 18% and everyone else isn't just a technology gap.

It's a life gap.

The CFOs leading digitally advanced teams are attending their kids' recitals.
They're taking real vacations.
They're thinking strategically — not drowning in reconciliations.

Here's the Executive Playbook:

🔹 Audit your close process TODAY. If any step takes days and involves multiple people doing manual work, that's your first automation target.

🔹 Build a controlled "experimentation studio" for agentic AI — test it on one quarterly report before you scale. EQB did. The results speak for themselves.

🔹 Stop measuring your team's dedication in hours worked. Start measuring it in decisions made and value created. Burnout is not a KPI.

The Deloitte report calls it "touchless accounting" — AI handling tax, treasury, FP&A, and controllership autonomously.

That's not the future.

That's what the top 18% are building RIGHT NOW.

So here's the question that should keep every CFO and CEO up at night:

If your competitor's finance team closes the books in 10 minutes while yours takes 3 days — what does that mean for every other strategic decision they're making faster than you?
