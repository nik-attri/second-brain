Last night, we had the pleasure of welcoming Andreas Hell back to the Entrepreneurship Centre. This time, however, not as an Operational CFO supporting startups, but as a founder himself 🥳 

🤔 Andreas founded RaiseRoom with an interesting idea: how could he replace himself?

In an era where AI can build spreadsheets, financial models, and projections with a single prompt, Andreas saw an opportunity to turn years of hands-on experience supporting startups into a ready-to-use solution. RaiseRoom helps founders become investor-ready in hours, not weeks.

Yesterday, Andreas shared his journey into entrepreneurship and what it took to turn the idea into a product. From validating the initial concept to continuously fine-tuning the product, he gave our community an honest look at what building a company really involves. 

It was a great opportunity for our community to learn from his experience and hear about the realities of building a company and preparing for growth.

🙏 Thank you, Andreas, for sharing your journey with us, and thank you to everyone who joined us for another FSEC Community Get-Together.
