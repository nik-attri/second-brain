𝐀𝐩𝐩𝐥𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐧𝐭: No, but I've survived Windows 95, dial-up internet, Excel spreadsheets from hell, and managers who thought everything was urgent.

Here's the thing about hiring experienced professionals.

We've made mistakes. Expensive ones.

We've built processes that failed spectacularly, fixed them, and eventually built systems that actually worked.

We've managed difficult people, impossible deadlines, questionable leadership decisions, and projects held together by coffee and blind optimism.

We've seen enough chaos to know that most problems aren't really new. They're just wearing different clothes.

And sometimes, that's worth a whole lot more.
