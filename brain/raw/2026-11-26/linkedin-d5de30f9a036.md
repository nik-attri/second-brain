WE'RE HIRING! 🚨
 
📈 Sales Coordinator – Enterprise Sales (IaaS & Cloud Services)
 
Join RapidCompute's fast-growing Enterprise Sales team and help grow our IaaS portfolio and partner services 🚀
 
💼 Key Responsibilities
 
🔍 Identify new business prospects through market research, cold outreach, and database building to generate qualified leads for our IaaS-based portfolio and partner services.
 📅 Schedule and coordinate introductory meetings, product presentations, and discovery sessions between prospective clients and the Enterprise Sales team.
 🤝 Accompany Assistant Managers and Account Managers on client visits, presentations, and external engagements whenever necessary to capture key discussion points, track action items, and assist with client follow-ups.
 📊 Compile, reconcile, and analyze pipeline movement, lead conversion metrics, and team activity logs to prepare accurate fortnightly sales review data and performance reports for management.
 🖥️ Use relevant applications, portals, and CRM systems to log leads, maintain up-to-date opportunity records, and facilitate the Enterprise sales team in internal processes.
 📝 Coordinate with Account Managers, Pre-Sales, Legal, and Finance teams to prepare commercial proposals, pricing quotations, NDAs, and tender/RFP documentation within set deadlines.
 📣 Collaborate with internal stakeholders to support targeted lead-generation campaigns and manage post-closure commercial documentation (POs, CAFs, and onboarding forms) for smooth handoff to Technical Account Management and Customer Success teams.
 
🎯 Requirements
 
🎓 Bachelor's degree in BBA, Commerce, Marketing, IT or a related field
 🧑‍💼 1–3 years in sales coordination, inside sales or B2B sales operations (Cloud, Enterprise IT, Telecom or Managed Services preferred)
 🗂️ Hands-on experience with CRM records, management reports and commercial documentation
 ☁️ Basic understanding of IaaS, data center hosting, backup/DR and enterprise IT services
 📑 Familiarity with B2B procurement, NDAs, RFPs and client onboarding
 📈 Strong MS Excel / Google Sheets skills (formulas, lookups, pivot tables)
 🗣️ Professional presence and strong English and Urdu communication
 ⏱️ Organized, with strong follow-through across teams and deadlines
