I am encouraged, watching the BBC report on the US Washington Conference on AI regulation.

President Trump praised the AI companies for taking the self-initiative to regulate AI.  I am especially encouraged ay Open AI halting its new release due to serious risks.

Trump said they are going to do a good job at regulation and where they don’t we are going to step in.

As an aside some remaining fears were discussed including AI taking over peoples jobs.  In my opinion this is inevitable. Elon Musk pointed out this will happen but people will grow into new jobs.  This happened with the introduction of the digitall computer.  People used to manually calculate bank interest rates and were replaced by computers.  Musk points out he doesn’t think anyone is longing to go back and perform.calculations.

I am enthused to see the proactive work AI CEOs are doing.  Moreover I applaud President Trump making this government priority so soon after recent concerning events.

I remain more than enthusiastic for AI evolution.  AI can do so much good for humanity.  For example it enables non-vocal people to talk again.  No one should be confined to silence.

We need to follow such amazing feats to counter-act the danger that has been identified.

We are not going to stop developing or applying AI to enhance humanity.  Such developments will fuel regulation so that AI will be a positive force in the world.
