The feature I did not expect to use as much as I do: asking The Vault to prep me before a conversation.
 
I clicked "prep me for my next interaction with [contact]" before a call last week. It pulled everything on file, where we met, and what I already knew, and was honest that there was not much logged yet. Instead of guessing, it told me exactly what was missing and offered to help me fill the gap.
 
Most people either over-prepare by scrolling through old messages trying to remember context or under-prepare and walk in cold. 

The Vault removes both failure modes. It tells you exactly what it knows, and just as usefully, exactly what it does not.
 
The integrations are what make this actually work day to day. 

Google Contacts and Calendar sync natively. Apple Contacts imports in under a minute. Apple Calendar runs a live two-way sync. 

None of this requires me to manually re-enter anything I already have somewhere else.
 
The result is one place where a client, a mentor, an old classmate, and a business partner all live with the same structure: what I know about them, when I last spoke to them, and what I should ask before I do again.
 
If you are managing relationships across too many surfaces, notes apps, memory, and old email threads, this is worth a look.
