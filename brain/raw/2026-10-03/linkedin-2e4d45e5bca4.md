There once was a spreadsheet with more tabs than anyone could count. I called it Excel Hell. I never want to go back there.

Territories, license windows, partner deals — 400 tabs all living in one file, held together by sheer will and intellect of me and my team. I remember checking every contract twice because I couldnt trust the spreadsheet. I still cringe at the thought of chasing a partner for a report thats three months late. And once, a licensor handed us rights to a territory theyd already sold to someone else. We got a strike on our own channel for it!

Everyone in this business knows this problem. Why do we choose to keep living with it? I couldn’t. 

Just like I did with Netflix, just like I did with YouTube — I made the decision that BB Media Group is going to adapt. And with AI entering the chat… let’s just say we figured it out. 

No more Excel Hell!
