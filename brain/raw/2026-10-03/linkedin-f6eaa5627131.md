As a parent, I get this. 

You watch your son or daughter spend years studying, working hard, qualifying and finally being offered a role, only for them to be left in limbo waiting for a contract and wondering what the hell is going on. For newly qualified nurses, that uncertainty is now very real.

When you listen to people saying, “I’ve got the job, but I haven’t got a contract yet,” you have to ask what is happening inside the system.

These are qualified nurses who are ready and willing to work in a health system that needs staff, yet somehow they are still being left waiting without clarity. 

Behind every delay there is a real person trying to start a career, a patient waiting for care and a family depending on the health service.

At some point, we have to ask: where is the accountability? 

Because ultimately the health service isn’t about systems, spreadsheets or departments. It’s about people.
