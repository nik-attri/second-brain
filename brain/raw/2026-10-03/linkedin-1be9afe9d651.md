Keeping clients updated during ongoing cases is often quite difficult. I have seen advisers spend countless hours sending manual updates to clients, time which could be better spent on strategic planning or advancing the work itself.

When I began managing administrative tasks for advisers, I quickly found myself in the same situation. Writing update emails was taking up far too much of my day, and I realised there had to be a more efficient method.

That is why I developed an automated update system. Updates are triggered automatically at crucial milestones, and if something is not yet set up, I intervene to send them manually. Whether it involves receipt of documents, the start of analysis, or its completion, these updates keep clients informed without constant manual input from me or the advisers I support.

This system has notably decreased the number of “where are we with this?” enquiries. Clients feel well informed, and advisers can concentrate on progressing the work rather than providing status updates.

How do you currently ensure clients stay informed during your administrative work? I would be interested to hear about your experiences.
