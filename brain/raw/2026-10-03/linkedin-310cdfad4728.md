Manual purchase order matching is a drain on your office staff and a common source of hidden costs.

Automating this process doesn't mean letting an AI pay your bills. It means using document extraction to compare invoices against POs line-by-line, flagging every price discrepancy and quantity mismatch before a human ever sees it.

The goal isn't just speed; it's accuracy. By using an "invoice workbench," your team only spends time reviewing the exceptions and giving the final "OK" on the matches. We consistently see teams recover 4 to 10 hours per week, per person, by eliminating the data entry grind.

If you are a service business owner running high volumes of work orders or parts orders, this is how you protect your margins without hiring more admin staff.
