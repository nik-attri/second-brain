We’re Hiring! 🚀

Are you an automation wizard who loves turning complex, repetitive manual workflows into seamless, lightning-fast automated processes? We are looking for an experienced Automation Engineer (3–5 Years) to join our team at Orange Tree Systems! 

📍 Location: Onsite, Lahore  
🌙 Timing: Night Shift  
💼 Position: Automation Engineer  

In this role, you will architect and implement robust automation solutions across web portals, desktop environments. If you thrive on solving technical bottlenecks and building scalable, secure enterprise pipelines, we want to hear from you!

What You’ll Do:
• Web Automation: Build and maintain scalable bots and scripts to automate complex web-based applications, dashboards, and portals.
• Desktop Automation: Streamline legacy and modern desktop applications using advanced RPA tools.
• Cloud Integration: Deploy, scale, and manage cloud-based automation workloads and infrastructure.
• Enterprise & Healthcare Automation: Design secure pipelines that handle sensitive data while adhering to HIPAA compliance standards including prior hands-on experience automating prominent healthcare enterprise platforms.

What We’re Looking For:
• Experience: 3 to 5 years of professional experience in automation, software engineering, or RPA roles.
• Core Languages & RPA: Strong proficiency in Python and UiPath (along with familiarity with tools like Microsoft Power Automate, Automation Anywhere, or Blue Prism).
• Web Testing & Scraping Frameworks: Experience with Playwright, Selenium, or BeautifulSoup/Requests.
• Cloud Platforms: Hands-on experience with cloud environments (AWS / Azure).
• APIs & Databases: Expertise in integrating REST APIs and handling secure data storage via PostgreSQL or MySQL.
• Security & Compliance: Knowledge of data protection frameworks and HIPAA guidelines for handling sensitive enterprise data.

How to Apply:
Ready to make an impact? Send your resume and portfolio directly to hr@otsys.co or drop a message in my DMs! 📩
