IMDA invited Voltade to speak on stage to a few hundred mid-cap businesses, and we had the chance to showcase an early preview of our product - Volty - to a live audience that tried it out!  It was risky... 

For a live demo where few hundred people testing = hundreds of ways it can go wrong… and…  It kinda went okay. Didn’t go down. Volty kept going. Audience had fun. 

The topic was on vibe coding, and we showed how in minutes you can build WhatsApp automations, visualise dashboards of your messages, extract invoices from emails, organise spreadsheets, connect to all your other systems and more, by simply chatting with Volty. 

Ever since I started my career I’ve always wanted to equip non-technical builders with no code tools. In 2017 I started FormSG, and from 2017 - 2023 as Head of Product in Open Government Products built a suite of no code tools - Postman, Go.gov.sg, Isomer etc.  

In 2023 I left to start Voltade with the same vision of no code tools, but direct it at growing small and medium businesses. Given AI was early then, we spent 3 long years building custom projects to learn what were the use cases businesses were willing to pay for. 

And finally - since June 2026, we pivoted to a product called Volty, which empowers everyone to build their own AI employee that automates workflows in Email and WhatsApp.

Some of the tech founders I spoke to at the event cautioned me that “Hey isn’t this a very crowded space?”  

Yes it’s crowded. Every space is. If your space isn’t crowded yet it certainly will in the next 12 months. Even hardware is going to be crowded.  

But crowd = demand. And it’s the same game as before, to win we have to be the best solution that actually works at scale, not just the one that looks good in a demo. 

We just opened sign ups with a 7 day free trial, if you’d love to vibe code automations on top of your Email and WhatsApp, check it out and give us feedback: https://volty.voltade.com/
