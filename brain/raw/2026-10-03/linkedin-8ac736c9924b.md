DAY 14

DONT FORGET TO HAVE FUN DOING IT!

30 degrees, sand in my eyes, headscarf on like I know what the hell I'm doing, no idea where I was going.

Me and the lads got stuck on these quads in the Dubai desert and within about four minutes I'd convinced myself I was a professional rally driver. Nearly flipped the bloody thing over a dune taking it a bit too seriously, laughed so hard I had sand in places sand should never be.

This is the bit nobody shows you about this whole journey. It's not all deal rooms and spreadsheets and sounding dead serious on LinkedIn. Sometimes it's six grown men absolutely bombing it across the desert dressed like extras off a film nobody cast us in, laughing like idiots.

Sat there after watching the sun go down, proper took a minute, thought about how far we'd all come together as mates, how mad different this all is to where we started.

Don't forget to actually enjoy building something while you're doing it. If you're too stressed to nearly flip a quad and piss yourself laughing about it, you're doing it wrong, simple as that.
