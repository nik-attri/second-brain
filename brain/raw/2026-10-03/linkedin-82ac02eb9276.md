Today’s tip is going to save your keyboard and your sanity before next week even starts.

✨ The "Never Type It Twice" Rule

The moment you find yourself writing a response, explaining a client onboarding process, or typing out instructions for the second time, stop and save it!

Turn it into a reusable template, canned response, or a quick screen recording.

Why? Because retyping the exact same answers silently drains your productive energy throughout the week.

Building your business without a template vault means you're acting as your own backend bottleneck!

Automation starts right here!

Turning manual repetition into simple templates that cuts backend friction so smart systems handle the routine lifting while you scale.

When you embrace the "Never Type It Twice" habit:

✨ You build an automated operational system on the fly without extra setup time
✨ You can delegate tasks to a VA or team member in seconds
✨ You free up hours of mental bandwidth for big, needle-moving work

If it happens twice, turn it into a system!
