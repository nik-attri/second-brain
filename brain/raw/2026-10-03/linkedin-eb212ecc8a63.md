That’s exactly what my latest AI Agent, Retail Analytics Dashboard is designed to do.

Happy to share another milestone in my AI Professional Journey, the successful completion of a practical Retail Analytics AI Agent.

Instead of manually going through reports, spreadsheets, and database records, a user can simply ask:

“Which customer generated the highest net sales?”
“Which product performed best?”

The AI Agent understands the question, connects with the relevant business data, performs the required analysis, and provides a clear, business-friendly response in seconds.
