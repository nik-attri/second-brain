Here's a question I've been asking business leaders lately:

"If a new regulation or a sudden cost shock hit tomorrow, how long would it take you to know which of your contracts are affected?"

Most pause. Then the honest answer comes: "Weeks. Maybe longer."

And that's the problem.

Business is moving faster than ever. Tariffs shift overnight. Supply chains get disrupted. India's data protection rules now require companies to know exactly what their vendors can and can't do with customer data. Every one of these changes leads back to the same place: your contracts.

Which suppliers can raise prices? Who carries the risk if a shipment is delayed? Which vendors handle customer data, and are their terms still compliant?

The answers exist. They're just buried in thousands of documents across legal, procurement, and email inboxes. So teams spend weeks reviewing manually while decisions wait and risks grow.

When the next change comes, your team won't be searching. They'll be ready.
