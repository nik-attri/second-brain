🔹 Position: Talent Acquisition Manager
🔹 Openings: 01
📍 Location: Mandideep (Bhopal), M.P
👨‍💼 Experience: 6–10 Years in Recruitment
🎓 Qualification: MBA – HR / PGDM – HR / Equivalent
💰 Salary: Negotiable for the Right Candidate

🔍 Key Responsibilities – Talent Acquisition

✅ Lead end-to-end recruitment for multiple positions across locations.
✅ Understand manpower requirements and develop effective hiring strategies in coordination with HODs and Hiring Managers.
✅ Manage recruitment for technical, managerial, operational, and support functions.
✅ Develop and maintain strong candidate pipelines for current and future requirements.
✅ Source candidates through LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, job portals, employee referrals, consultants, social media, and other channels.
✅ Screen profiles and conduct initial interviews to assess candidate suitability.
✅ Coordinate technical, managerial, and final interview rounds with respective stakeholders.
✅ Handle salary negotiations, offer discussions, offer rollout, and joining coordination.
✅ Ensure continuous candidate engagement to minimize offer dropouts and joining backouts.
✅ Drive recruitment TAT and ensure timely closure of critical positions.
✅ Build and maintain a strong talent pool for recurring and critical positions.
✅ Track recruitment metrics including sourcing, interviews, selection, offers, joining, and conversion ratios.
✅ Prepare and present recruitment MIS and hiring reports to management.
✅ Manage recruitment agencies and external hiring partners whenever required.
✅ Identify new and effective sourcing channels to improve hiring efficiency.
