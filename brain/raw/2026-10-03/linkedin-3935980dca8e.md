Most companies do not have an AI problem. They have a coordination problem.

Information is scattered across inboxes, spreadsheets, CRMs, WhatsApp conversations and internal systems. Sales teams spend too much time following up manually. Managers are often working with delayed or incomplete information. And a surprising amount of operating capacity is still absorbed by repetitive work that software should already be handling.

This is the problem we are building Cadr to solve.

Cadr is an AI business suite designed around the way your company actually operates. It connects to your workflows, understands what is happening across the business and turns fragmented operational data into tailored, actionable insights.

The first layer is intelligence.

Instead of relying on static dashboards, Cadr can continuously analyse what is happening across sales, customers and operations. Which leads are going cold? Which customers require attention? Where are orders getting delayed? What changed this week? What should management be looking at today?
The goal is not more data. It is better visibility into the things that require action.
The second layer is execution.

Businesses can hire AI coworkers through Cadr to take responsibility for specific workflows.

An AI sales coworker can respond to inbound enquiries, qualify leads, prepare offers, follow up consistently and keep the CRM updated.

An AI coordination coworker can track orders, communicate with customers and internal teams, identify delays and make sure tasks do not disappear between people or systems.

Over time, businesses should be able to deploy multiple specialised AI coworkers across different functions, each operating within clearly defined responsibilities and escalating decisions where human judgment is required.
We think this is where enterprise AI becomes considerably more interesting.

The opportunity is not simply to give every employee a chatbot. It is to build an operating layer where AI understands the business, surfaces the right information and actively participates in getting work done.

For smaller and mid sized businesses in particular, this can change the economics of building operational capacity. Functions that previously required additional headcount can increasingly be supported by software, while existing teams spend more time on relationships, judgment and higher value work.

Cadr is being built around a simple idea: your software should not only tell you what happened in your business. It should understand what is happening, tell you what matters and help you act on it.

Your business intelligence and your AI workforce, in one place.
