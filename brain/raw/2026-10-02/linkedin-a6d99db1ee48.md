Some of the tools that save us time took quite a bit of work to build. 
Others are surprisingly simple.

We have worked on a Revit plugin that automates repetitive steps in our workflow.

And then there are things like a simple Excel calculator that helps us determine the right lattice girder height without going through the same manual check every time.

Very different tools, but they usually start with the same question:
If we keep doing this step over and over again, does it really need to be done manually every time?

Some parts of engineering change with every project. Geometry, loads, openings, and structural conditions still need someone to look at them, think, and make a decision.

Other steps simply repeat.

Shaving a little time off one step may not seem like much. But when that same step repeats across one floor, several storeys, and then the next project, the difference starts to add up.

The less attention repetitive steps require, the more we can give to the decisions that are different every time.
