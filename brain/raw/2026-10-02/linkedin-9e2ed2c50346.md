9 to 5…ish for Divij Goyal PE analyst at EMK Capital.  

Working in the value creation team of a European mid-market private equity fund, his mornings begin with a quick check of emails and messages, followed by planning the day ahead and a trip to the gym (read: being forced to go by his flatmates).

His office day usually begins between 9:30 and 10 AM, with work ranging from researching businesses and developing growth strategies to evaluating acquisitions and collaborating with colleagues across different time zones. All of this involves an unhealthy amount of caffeine and an even unhealthier number of Excel sheets and memos.

His favourite thing about the job is the sheer variety. One day, he's working on the marketing strategy of a retail chain in the Netherlands; the next, he's trying to understand the unit economics of the recycling and compliance industry in the US.

Between all the spreadsheets and meetings, he and his colleagues make sure to find time to unwind. Besides their monthly karaoke ritual, they've developed a collective obsession with solving every NYT game and discovering new ones. (P.S. TimeGuessr and Worldle come highly recommended.)

With work never really ending in the virtual world, his day typically wraps up with EOD updates and a to-do list for the next day. Of course, with the eternal optimism that he'll catch up on all his lost sleep and plans over the weekend.
