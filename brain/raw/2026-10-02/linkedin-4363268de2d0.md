Wedding season is officially upon us in the Southern Hemisphere, and I have a question for my fellow hospitality leaders: Why are we still pretending it’s 2016? 

No one plays Pokémon Go anymore. No one is rocking the 'Heal the World' wristbands. So why are so many catering businesses still running on the 'Sheets-plus-inbox' stack? 

I see it every day. Brilliant Chefs and Catering Owners drowning in what I call 'admin exhaust.' You’re spending hours—precious hours—manually updating spreadsheets, fishing for wedding lead details in your Outlook subfolders, and trying to patch together a workflow that was never meant to handle the volume of a modern wedding season. 

It’s the equivalent of driving a beat-up Honda to a premium venue. It might get you there, eventually, but at what cost to your brand? At what cost to your sanity? 

The reality is that your real competitor isn't the caterer down the street. Your competitor is the habit of sticking to a broken system because 'that’s how we’ve always done it.' 

We built CaterFlowAi to change that narrative. We are the catering platform for 2026, not 2016. 

Specifically, we introduced Quotebot—which is currently the ONLY catering-specific quoting software in Australia. While others are trying to adapt generic CRM tools to fit a food-service mold, we built something specifically for the chaos, the allergens, the floor plans, and the rapid-fire nature of wedding bookings. 

When you move away from the manual stack, three things happen:
1. Your conversion rate on wedding leads skyrockets because your response time drops from hours to seconds.
2. Your brand is immediately perceived as premium and exclusive.
3. You actually get to focus on the food and the hospitality, rather than the data entry.

September is here. The peak season is moving fast. If your current system feels like it’s held together by duct tape and prayers, it’s time for an upgrade. Don't let your backend operations be the reason you miss out on your biggest season yet. 

Let’s step into 2026 together. It’s much more efficient over here.
