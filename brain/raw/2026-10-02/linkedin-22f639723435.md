AI. How did we get here?
Not an entirely comprehensive slide but it has some of the key milestones.

The idea of mathematical neurons and non-learning neural nets (weights would be manually applied, simple logical ops) was around since the 1940s. As early as the 1980s, scientists were saying that then-supercomputers were not powerful enough to do what they really wanted to compute. It wasn't until GPUs, and the formulation of the problem so it could use GPUs, made that possible.

There were systems like the FPS 164/MAX that did matrix processing (https://lnkd.in/gniT_XDT) that could have helped, but there was also a disconnect between AI researchers being aware of such systems and the supercomputing world being aware of such workloads.
