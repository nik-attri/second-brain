Every fashion brand wants the same thing: one consistent brand experience, whether a customer walks into a store, opens an email or shops online.

Few manage it without the team drowning in manual work for every campaign.
That's exactly what Samsøe Samsøe is working on, and I'm really looking forward to hearing how they're tackling it at NXT Commerce Summit.

Javier Artal (Director of Marketing & Digital at Samsøe Samsøe) and Pieter Verhoyen (Marketing Manager Benelux at Voyado) will share how they're connecting product discovery and customer engagement across email, SMS, onsite, digital wallet and stores.

What I like about this session: It's about how the right setup gives creative and marketing teams their time back to do what they're actually good at.
