"You just blow my mind. We knew you were clever, because obviously we wouldn't be working with you otherwise, but bloody hell, that's..."

That's what a client said to me last week, after the conversation somehow turned to what I used to do before this.

I went back to university in my early forties. Studied molecular and cellular biology, then started a PhD in neurobiology. Worked as a research scientist, then a manufacturing scientist, right up until 2024.

It doesn't come up much in client meetings, so the reaction caught me off guard a little too.

What I didn't expect was how much of it still shows up in how I work now. Following a procedure properly. Keeping to logical steps. Not skipping the boring bit because it's boring. Data analysis without flinching at a spreadsheet.

Turns out a science background is decent training for running someone else's operations.

I'll take the compliment. Even if it did come with a bit of swearing. 🤣
