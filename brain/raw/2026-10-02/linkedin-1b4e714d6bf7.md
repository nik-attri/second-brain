As the Assistant Managing Director of Chess Corner, my job revolves around one simple word: Efficiency. 

This is me upgrading from starred messages on WhatsApp and writing the next steps on to-do lists; I made Asana solve my problem of managing our tasks and projects to setting up reminders and reducing the stress of replying and updating my team members on what assignment to do next. We recently had our monthly tournament games, and here's how I handled it: 

1️⃣ Naming the operational stages: Instead of dumping tasks into a generic 'To Do' column, we mapped our tournament lifecycle into clear, time-bound phases: 

Pre-Tournament and Executive Board Alignment: Board meeting scheduling, budget locking, and tournament rules approval. 

Link and Website Setup: Tournament link generation and real-time website updates.

Publicity and WhatsApp Updates: Scheduled community broadcasts, registration drives, and 24-hour reminders. 

Post-Tournament and Awards: Standings auditing, congratulatory email dispatch, winner verification, and prize disbursals.

2️⃣ Accountability and Timing: Every assignment was assigned to a team member, an exact due date, and a specific time tag. Instead of me manually checking in, Asana’s push notifications handled reminders automatically.

3️⃣ Task Dependencies: Most times we need a task done first before proceeding to the next. Example: We need to generate tournament link before we upload tournament details on the website; so by linking this tasks, the next task will be unlocked; this actually helps for zero manual chasing and smooth tournament experience for both our team and our players. 

Moving from reactive messaging to proactive systems changed everything for us at Chess Corner. Even to setting up automations for tasks!
