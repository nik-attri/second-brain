What does O&G have in common with FIFA?

For those of us who attended the OEUK Data & Digital Conference 2026 on 29th September, we’ve discovered that it’s rather a lot! 

Think about it: 200+ teams, all with different taxonomies. Sound familiar? 
FIFA faced a huge master data challenge — different taxonomies across teams and nations, much like the different tags and classifications we see across assets and systems in offshore energy. 

The answer was for FIFA to clean and standardize the data, much in the way we would using ISO and CFIHOS, creating one immutable ID tracking across 211 nations. 

And, as we at MODS understand well, master data management was only the beginning. FIFA then combined AI agents with human oversight to automate and reduce heavy ticketing workflows while capturing and passing on veteran knowledge as an organizational asset. 

There were plenty more lessons from across the day, including: 
AI is moving beyond productivity assistance towards a discovery engine, showing what’s possible when data, algorithms, and AI come together. 
The workforce is already moving quickly with GenAI, but training, guidance, and leadership need to catch up. 

And a consistent theme throughout: AI can only move as fast as the information and data behind it. 

For us at MODS, this reinforces the importance of building the right foundations: clear definitions, strong governance, connected data, and a roadmap that links data strategy directly to business and AI use cases. 

A fascinating and enlightening day of ideas around data and digital from well beyond our own sector. Thank you to Offshore Energies UK, the speakers, contributors and everyone who shared their experiences and perspectives. Lots for the O&G industry to take away — and plenty to think about.
