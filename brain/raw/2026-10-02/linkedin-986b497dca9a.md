I've been working on something fun and I'm finally ready to share it!

We're building a house right now, and I got pretty tired of trying to make spreadsheets answer questions they really weren't built to answer.

How much house can we actually afford? How much cash do we need? What happens to our payment if the build costs another $30k? Where the hell is all this money going? 😂

So... I built Groundbase.

It's a home build planning tool that brings your budget, financing, costs, and projected mortgage together in one place.

I built the whole thing from scratch, and it's been really fun getting back into the weeds of designing AND building a real product again.

If you're building a house, thinking about building one, or know somebody who is — check it out! It's free to try.

👉 getgroundbase.com

And seriously, if you try it, send me feedback. I want to keep making this thing better.
