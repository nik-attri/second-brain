FRIDAY FUNNY. 

Too much agentic automation? We’re entering the moment in the sci fi movie where AI doesn’t just answer questions but sends messages, updates systems, triggers workflows, schedules things and sends that birthday card all on its own, without our intervention.

Which is useful. Until someone asks: “Wait… did I send that?”

Yes, this has happened to me. Except it wasn't just a birthday greeting, but full client outreach that went wrong. 

As we automate more processes, we need to be more alert than before, not less. We need to know what our agents are doing, what triggered them, what they changed and what's happening without our direct approval. Otherwise nothing we do will mean anything.
