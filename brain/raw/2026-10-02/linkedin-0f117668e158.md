$3.55 Per Invoice: What If You Didn’t Need the Invoice at All?

When we talk about automation, we often ask: how can we process invoices faster? But, Do we need to process every supplier invoice in the first place?

APQC benchmarking reports a median personnel cost of $3.55 per invoice processed in Accounts Payable. At 100,000 invoices per year, that is $355,000 in personnel processing cost alone.

This is where Evaluated Receipt Settlement (ERS) in SAP S/4HANA becomes interesting.

=> The normal process: Purchase Order → Goods Receipt → Supplier Invoice → Invoice Check → Payment

=> With ERS: Purchase Order → Goods Receipt → Automatic Settlement → Payment

For suitable suppliers, there is no need to wait for a supplier invoice. SAP uses the agreed Purchase Order and the actual Goods Receipt to create the settlement automatically.

Sounds simple—but good automation needs good processes. For ERS to work properly, you need: Correct PO prices, Accurate Goods Receipts, Reliable supplier master data, Clear payment terms and tax information, Good purchasing discipline. 

Good automation is not about making every manual step faster. It is about removing steps you no longer need. For CEOs, CFOs, CPOs and COOs, the opportunity is clear: Lower Cost | Less Manual Work | Faster Processing | Better Data | Easier Scaling. 

So here is the question for your organisation: How many processes are you still doing manually simply because “this is how we have always done it”?
