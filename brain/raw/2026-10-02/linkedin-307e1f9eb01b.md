📢 We’re Hiring – HR Manager
We are looking for an experienced and highly motivated HR Manager to join our team and lead the Human Resources function.

Job Responsibilities:
* Develop and implement HR policies, procedures, and strategies in line with company objectives.
* Manage the full employee lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, performance management, and offboarding.
* Experience in preparing presentations, HR analytics, and annual departmental budgets, with the ability to align departmental plans, budgets, dashboards, and organizational hierarchy to support business objectives.
* Lead recruitment activities and ensure the hiring of qualified candidates.
* Handle employee relations and resolve workplace issues professionally.
* Ensure compliance with Egyptian Labor Law and social insurance regulations.
* Develop and monitor performance appraisal and KPI systems.
* Manage training and development programs.
* Prepare HR reports, analyze HR data, and provide recommendations to management.
* Maintain accurate and up-to-date employee files and HR documentation.
* Lead and support the HR team and ensure effective daily operations.
* Support management in organizational development and workforce planning.

Job Requirements:
* 10+ years of relevant HR experience, with at least 3 years in a managerial or supervisory role.
* Previous experience in a factory or industrial environment is highly preferred.
* Strong knowledge of Egyptian Labor Law and Social Insurance.
* Excellent leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills.
* Strong organizational and decision-making abilities.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Office and HR systems and Odoo.
* Very good command of English.

📍 Location: 10th of Ramadan City
💼 Employment Type: Full-time
