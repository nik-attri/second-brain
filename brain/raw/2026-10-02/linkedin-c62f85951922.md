​To the incredible sales team, for regularly filling out the sales forecasting sheet, and ensuring that the targets we plugged in actually converted to real revenue, consistently month on month.

​To the ops team, wrestling with the production planning sheet, keeping the supply chain moving, and making sure the factory floor ran smoother than my pivot tables.

​To the support team, for filling out all the trackers, dealing with the chaos on the ground, and making sure everything didn't catch fire while we were busy scaling.

​To the corporate team, for keeping the wheels turning, and holding the culture intact. 

​Building this up alongside all of you has been unforgettable, and I will always cherish it.
