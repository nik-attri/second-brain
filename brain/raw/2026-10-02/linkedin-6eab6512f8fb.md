Is AI an overhyped buzzword or a true game-changer in engineering? 

Every Friday, I reflect on how quickly our field is evolving—and AI consistently stands out for its transformative impact. From predictive maintenance in manufacturing to generative design in civil projects, machine learning is reengineering the way we solve problems and innovate solutions.

For engineers and fresh graduates, the shift isn’t just about mastering new tools. It's about changing our mindset: thinking algorithmically, collaborating with data scientists, and embracing lifelong learning.

One practical takeaway: Begin experimenting with open-source AI frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch. Even basic hands-on experience can help you see how machine learning models can optimize complex workflows or automate repetitive design tasks.

As the lines blur between traditional engineering and computer science, the question isn’t if AI will redefine our profession—but how you’ll stay ahead.
