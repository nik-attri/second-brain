Having only a few users at the beginning gives you a luxury you won’t always have: you can talk to every single one of them. You can ask what they were trying to do, what helped, what confused them and what they still do manually. One honest conversation can teach you more about what people need than months of building around your own assumptions. Of course I want us to have more customers at Jarvis. But while we’re still small, I want to use that access properly. It’s much easier to figure out what needs to change when you can just ask the person using it :)
