🔥 BEFORE YOU BLAME YOUR SALES TEAM FOR NOT HITTING THEIR NUMBERS…

Ask yourself one question:

WHAT DID YOU DO TO HELP THEM?

Did you train them?

Did you role-play with them?

Did you get in the field with them?

Did you listen to their calls?

Did you teach them how to overcome objections?

Did you actually COACH them?

Or did you sit behind a computer, look at a spreadsheet and ask…

“Why aren’t you hitting your numbers?”

A quota isn’t training.

A Teams call isn’t coaching.

And telling someone to “sell more” sure as hell isn’t leadership. 🤣

Sometimes the problem isn’t the sales team.

Sometimes it’s the person leading them. 🎤
