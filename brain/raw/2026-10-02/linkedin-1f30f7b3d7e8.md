Well… after 6 and a half years, my time at MJH Life Sciences has officially come to an end. 

When I started at MJH, I was a newly single mom with a 6 month old baby, and I definitely did not expect to still be here six years later. Let alone to have worked my way through Market Research, Circulation, Print Production, and eventually Project Management.

And SO much life happened while I was here. I had my son while I was employed here. I got married while I was employed here. I met my best friends here, who are now basically my sisters. And when my dad got sick, and eventually passed away, the support I received from the people around me meant more than I can probably put into words. They gave me the space and understanding I needed during one of the hardest times in my life, and I will always be grateful for that.

Somewhere in between all of that, I also learned more than I probably ever wanted to know about magazines, print production, spreadsheets, project management, and how to somehow make a project happen when approximately 47 things are actively trying to prevent it from happening. 

There have been some incredibly challenging days, some really fun ones, more laughs than I can count, and plenty of moments where I wondered, “How the hell are we going to pull this off?” …and somehow, we did.

Six years is a long time. This company was there for some of the biggest moments of my life, both the really good ones and the really hard ones. So while I’m ready to close this chapter, I’m leaving with a lot of gratitude for the people, the friendships, the lessons, and even the occasional Workfront-induced breakdown. 

I’m proud of how much I’ve grown since I walked through those doors six years ago, both professionally and personally.

So, MJH… thanks for the memories, the lessons, the friendships, the character development, and for being a part of so many chapters of my life.

On to the next chapter 💛
