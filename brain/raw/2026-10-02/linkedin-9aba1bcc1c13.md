India's hiring market did not slow down last month. It changed shape. Naukri's September JobSpeak reads 2% growth in white-collar hiring. Underneath that one number, a single experience band shrank.
 
0 to 3 years: up 1%.
4 to 7 years: down 2%.
13 to 15 years: up 7%.
16 years and over: up 5%.
 
The middle is the only band that fell. That is the story, not the headline.
 
Now lay the skills on top. AI and ML roles grew 20%. IT and software services fell 4%. One month earlier those same two lines read plus 31% and plus 11%. IT and software swung fifteen points in four weeks.
 
Info Edge MD and CEO Hitesh Oberoi called the market "steady, but more selective".
 
Selective has an address. Three growth lines cross in the same two cities.
 
GCC hiring grew 23% in Hyderabad and 13% in Chennai.
Inside GCCs, BPO and IT-enabled services hiring grew 59%.
Auto hiring grew 11%, led by Hyderabad at 26%, Chennai at 19%, Bengaluru at 18%.
 
So the upside is not "learn AI". It is "take what you already know into a room that is still buying".
 
Five steps if you are four to seven years in.
 
1. Search by pocket, not by title. Filter GCC roles in Hyderabad and Chennai. Put auto and manufacturing on your list, not only IT services.
2. Pick one business process you already understand. Claims, returns, dealer inventory, month-end close. The domain is the scarce part, not the framework.
3. Build one thing end to end on that process. The business problem, the data you chose and why you chose it, the model boundary, the evaluation, the handoffs you removed.
4. Write down the number it moved. Hours saved a week, or exceptions caught. A selective market buys evidence, not enthusiasm.
5. Apply where your old sector is the buyer. An engineer who knows IT services and can show that chain is worth more to an auto GCC than to another IT services firm.
 
Freshers, one note. Entry hiring grew 1% overall, but Coimbatore grew 20% and Vadodara 13%, and banking and oil and gas posted double-digit fresher growth. The open door is not in the usual city or the usual sector.
 
I would watch the four-to-seven band, not the AI headline.
 
Mentoring a 2-year engineer? Which one would you put first this week: data modelling, cloud deployment, evaluation, or business process mapping?
