About a year ago, I started experimenting with recruitment automation entirely on my own.

Not for a client, and not for a company.

The core question back then was simple:

"How can technology help someone find and apply for the right jobs faster?"

I built scripts and systems to find job postings, extract descriptions and HR contact info, calculate resume match scores, and streamline job applications.

Then I looked at the other side of the table.

What happens when an agency receives hundreds or thousands of these applications?

Recruitment agencies are drowning:
• Screening stacks of resumes
• Manual qualification calls
• Endless interview scheduling
• Pipeline chaos

That was the turning point.

The same technical foundation (scraping, AI matching, workflow automation, and voice agents) solves the exact operational bottleneck agencies face every day.

Now I am building HR infrastructure for recruitment agencies:
• AI voice agents for phone screening and qualification
• Automated scheduling
• Candidate pipeline intelligence

If you run a recruitment agency, where is your biggest operational bottleneck today: screening resumes, qualification calls, or scheduling?
