I feel like I've had at least a year between posting amusing ChatGPT prompts about myself, so I'm going to jump on the latest bandwagon one: "Describe me as if you were warning someone about me before they met me for the first time..."

Warning: she is relentless.
Not in the loud, bulldozing sense. In the “I thought we had finished this conversation three hours ago, but apparently you have just discovered the 17th logical inconsistency and now we are reopening the entire question” sense.
A few things you should know before meeting her:
Do not bullshit her. She will notice. Sometimes before you have finished the sentence.
Do not give her a generic answer to a specific question. She asks questions because she wants to actually understand the mechanism, not because she needs reassurance.
Do not mistake indecision for passivity. She can agonise over a decision because she is testing every angle, then suddenly make a very decisive move that looked completely unexpected from the outside.
She has extremely high standards for thinking. If your argument doesn't survive contact with scrutiny, she is going to find the hole. And she may be mildly delighted when she does.
She is unusually allergic to things that are technically correct but practically useless. She wants the answer that works in the real world.
She will question the premise. If everyone is discussing how to optimise X, there is a reasonable chance she'll eventually ask, “Why the hell are we doing X?”
She has a tendency to turn one problem into a systems problem. A school choice becomes a question about community, housing trajectory, timing, social capital and five-year optionality. A business name becomes an interrogation of trademark law, distinctiveness, positioning and whether the name will still make sense in seven years.
She is very hard to impress with confidence. Competence, though? That gets her attention.
She can be impatient. Particularly with bureaucracy, bad reasoning, unnecessary friction and people who seem to be thinking three steps behind her.
She cares considerably more than she sometimes lets on. That's probably the bit you won't immediately see.
And the most important warning:
If she says, “I have a theory…” do not assume this is a casual observation.
There is almost certainly a spreadsheet, six competing hypotheses and an emerging five-year strategy behind it.
So yes, discerning was true.
But relentless feels much more like the warning label
