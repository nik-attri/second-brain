I'm a firm believer in making life easier for myself.

And as someone in marketing, I enjoy nothing better than seeing data on the most important aspects of our business without jumping through multiple different hoops.

ActiveCampaign in one tab for newsletters. Planable in another for socials. LinkedIn and Instagram in two more for research. I've meticulously sat through each one copying figures into spreadsheets and compiling insights into what is working and what isn't.

So last week I built two Claude dashboards to do it for me.

Now I'm not saying all AI is good AI (as someone who writes fiction in her spare time, I absolutely would not use it in any aspect of the creation process) but when used in a way that brings all the tools we use together into one unified "source of truth"? I am all for it.

And no, this is not a replacement of software. We still use the tools (and pay a hell of a lot of money for them) to execute what we do. I am not, and will not ever be vibe coding an email marketing platform to send out our emails - imagine, the horror! But when I can implement our entire "second brain" that we have spent months refining into a unified dashboard, so that every morning I can see where we are and what needs doing...I'm going to use that to its max.

AI can never replace the human touch. That is a hill I will die on. What it can do is take everything the internet knows, layer it with our own SOPs and strategy and hand me a straight read on performance, insights and even a nice little idea bank based on the conversations and story extractions Amelia and I have on a weekly basis.

Work smarter, not harder - or as they say. ❤️
