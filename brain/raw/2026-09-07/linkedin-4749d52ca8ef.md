Seven days. That's how long it took the account manager to notice a customer had gone quiet.

Not because she was bad at her job. Because the job was: open dashboard, scroll the list, squint at dates, flag the ones that looked wrong. Every Friday. One hour minimum. The gap between "something changed" and "someone found out" was structural.

The agent runs the diff overnight now. Drops it in the morning: who stopped logging in, who came back, whose license expires in ten days. The call task creates itself.

Nobody built a product. Nobody gave a talk about it. The manager just stopped being a courier.

That's most of what I've seen AI actually do in operations - not replace judgment, just eliminate the part where a person manually carries information from one place to another. The boring part. The part that takes an hour and still loses you a week.

If you're looking at a similar gap in your customer success or sales motion - where the signal exists but nobody sees it in time - I'm happy to walk through how we structured this. DM works.
