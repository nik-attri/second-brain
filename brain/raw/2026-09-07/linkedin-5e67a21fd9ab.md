I recently built something that came from a problem I was personally experiencing. 

As someone applying for jobs, I realized I was constantly checking emails and manually updating my job application tracker.

Applied for a job? Open the spreadsheet and add it.

Got an interview? Go back and update the status.

Received a rejection? Open the spreadsheet again. 😅

After doing this repeatedly, I stopped and thought:

“Why am I doing all of this manually when I can automate it?”

So I built an AI-powered Job Application Tracker. 

Now, when a job-related email comes in, AI helps identify important information like the company, role, and status, while my automation updates my Google Sheet.

For example:

An interview email comes in → AI understands what the email is about → My tracker automatically updates from Applied to Interview.

Building this project reminded me that automation doesn’t always have to be something complicated or meant only for big companies.

Sometimes, it simply starts with asking:

“What am I doing repeatedly that a system could help me do?”

And honestly, that question has changed the way I look at everyday tasks.

I’m still learning and exploring, but I’m excited about this journey into AI and automation.

Zapier was a great starting point, and n8n is next.

I’m looking forward to building more solutions, solving more real-life problems, and seeing just how much we can simplify with AI and automation.
