Here is a real automation system I built using n8n 👇
 
The Problem:
 An HR team was struggling with high applicant volume — manually filtering candidates and scheduling interviews was taking hours every single day.
 
The Solution:
 I built a fully automated HR candidate screening pipeline connecting:
 
🟢 Google Sheets — as the applicant database trigger
 📧 Gmail — for instant automated email responses
 📅 Google Calendar — for automatic interview scheduling
 
Here is how it works automatically:
 
When a new applicant row is added to Google Sheets the system instantly:
 
⚡ Sends an automated confirmation email to the applicant
 ⚡ Routes them based on years of experience:
 
1-5 years experience →
 Automatically sends an applicant update email keeping them informed
 
5+ years experience →
 Automatically sends an interview invitation AND creates a Google Calendar event — fully scheduled without any human involvement
 
The Results:
 
✅ Saved 90% of manual screening time
 ✅ Eliminated all scheduling delays for top candidates
 ✅ Zero manual effort after setup
 ✅ Runs 24/7 automatically
