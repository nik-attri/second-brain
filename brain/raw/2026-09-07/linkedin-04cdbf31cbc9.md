Most ATS tools were built to store candidates.

We built Rebee.ai to understand them.

Greenhouse, Lever and the rest are excellent filing systems. They track who applied, which column they're in, and who owes a scorecard. But the actual thinking — is this person a fit? — still happens in a recruiter's head, one resume at a time. Or worse, in a keyword filter that rejects a payments engineer because their resume says "digital wallet" instead of "payments."
Rebee flips the model. Every stage in the pipeline reads the candidate:
→ Resume Parser + JD-Resume Matching are on by default. Not keyword matching — semantic. "Mentored a squad" counts as leadership even if the word never appears.
 → Quick Assessment: an AI-picked MCQ screen that runs itself.
 → Pre-Screen: AI or manual call, before a human spends an hour.
 → Custom Assessment, Interview rounds, Hire — all on one drag-and-drop canvas. Save it as a template. Done.
No stitching together four vendors. No losing good people to a filter that can't read.
Who this is for: lean TA teams drowning in volume, staffing firms, growth-stage companies hiring for skills, not buzzwords.
Who it's not for (yet): if you have a 10-year Greenhouse setup and a compliance team that loves it, keep it — and plug Rebee in as the intelligence layer.
