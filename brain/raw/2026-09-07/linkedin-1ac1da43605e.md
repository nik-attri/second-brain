📍 Jounieh, Lebanon

We are looking for a Customer Service Specialist to deliver an exceptional customer experience across all communication channels and at our facility’s front desk.

-Welcome and assist clients professionally.

-Provide accurate information about services, procedures, and requirements.
-Register customer requests and verify documentation when required.
-Coordinate with internal departments to ensure timely resolution of requests.
-Track cases and follow up until resolution.
-Maintain accurate customer records and service documentation.

-Resolve customer concerns and escalate complex cases when necessary.
-Follow up to ensure customer satisfaction and service completion.
-Prepare reports and ensure accurate data entry in CRM/internal systems.
-Identify recurring customer concerns and contribute to service improvements.

-Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Communication, Hospitality, or a related field.
-2–3 years of experience in customer service or front-desk roles.
-Strong communication skills in Arabic and English; French is a plus.
-Proficiency in MS Office and CRM/customer management tools.
-Professional appearance and strong interpersonal skills.
-Ability to multitask and work effectively in a high-volume environment.
-Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
-Customer-focused, professional, adaptable, and solution-oriented.
 
🎯 Key Competencies
 
-Customer orientation
