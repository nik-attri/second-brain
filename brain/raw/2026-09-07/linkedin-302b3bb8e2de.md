🧠 I HAVE A BETTER USE FOR ALL THIS EXECUTIVE BRAINPOWER:
TELL ME WHAT IS WRONG WITH THE National Builders League. Not my spreadsheet. Not one calculation. Not whether I classified your company correctly.

📍THE CONCEPT📍
  
Bring your smartest skeptic.
Let's try to break the idea before anyone shares a single piece of data.
I am literally getting notes saying: “I FOUND MORE ERRORS IN YOUR MATH.”
Sometimes they are right. If my math is wrong, I will fix it.
But increasingly, the math isn't the disagreement.

THE DEFINITION IS.

Builder A tells me to calculate something one way. Builder B tells me something different.

WHO DO I LISTEN TO?

 ❌ And please stop saying, “It’s in the 10-K.” ❌ 

I READ THE 10-Ks.

As I showed yesterday with K. Hovnanian, KHOV itself presented two different versions of ASP in its disclosures. The filing can be accurate. The math can be accurate. And we can still end up with different answers.

THAT IS THE PROBLEM.
I DO NOT HAVE GREAT INFORMATION.

I am one person, on the outside, painfully building examples on the fly from information the industry itself does not define or report consistently.
If you are judging the viability of the NBL based on the precision of those examples:

YOU ARE MISSING THE POINT.

The NBL isn't supposed to make my numbers better.

IT IS SUPPOSED TO MAKE THIS EXERCISE UNNECESSARY.

So let's talk about the harder questions. Why won't it work? Governance? Legal? Competitive concerns? Data security? Wrong statistics? Bad structure? What haven't I thought of?

BETTER YET, LET'S TALK BEFORE ANYONE SHARES A SINGLE PIECE OF DATA.

The first meeting needs NO DATA.
Put the concept on a whiteboard and try to break it.
Every builder could benefit from establishing better standards independently.
But if all 12 eventually participated, I believe the benefit could be 10X FOR ALL.
Same definitions. Same calculations. Same standards.
Then compete like hell on performance.

🎯 HERE IS MY CHALLENGE:

If ONE BUILDER wants to have this conversation, I will travel anywhere in the country to meet with you. No data. No obligation. No commitment.

If TWO OR MORE BUILDERS want to talk together, come to Dallas.
I will personally pay for two nights at the Swexan Hotel and all your meals.
Let's try to break the idea before we try to build it. Proving one of my numbers wrong is easy.

PROVING THE CONCEPT WRONG WOULD BE FAR MORE VALUABLE.

And if we can't? Maybe we have something worth building.

MEASURE IT. PROVE IT. COMPETE.
