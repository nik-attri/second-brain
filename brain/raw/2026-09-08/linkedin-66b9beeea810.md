📢 We're Hiring | *Clinical Research Associate (CRA)*

📍Location: *Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan*

ClinSite is an emerging Contract Research Organization (CRO), providing a unified platform for investigators, sponsors, and CROs to manage clinical trials across multiple therapeutic areas. The organization focuses on delivering reliable, high-quality clinical trial data through streamlined processes and robust operational support. A skilled and professional team ensures projects are executed according to timelines while maintaining strong client satisfaction. With a network of 40+ clinical trial sites globally, ClinSite offers access to a diverse target population. This reach enables inclusive, efficient, and impactful clinical research opportunities.

Role Description: 
This is a full-time, hybrid role based in Karachi. This role supports the planning, coordination, and monitoring of clinical trial activities in accordance with study protocols, regulatory requirements, and internal standard operating procedures. Day-to-day tasks include assisting with site identification, qualification and initiation and routine monitoring visits, verifying source data and ensuring accurate entry into clinical trial systems, and documenting findings in monitoring reports. The role involves working closely with site staff to support patient recruitment, informed consent processes, and adherence to protocol and Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines. The Clinical Research Associate also helps maintain essential documents, tracks study milestones, and collaborates with the clinical operations team to address site issues and maintain high-quality data.

Qualifications:
* Strong understanding of clinical trial protocols and the ability to interpret and apply protocol requirements accurately.
* Knowledge of Clinical Research Associate function within clinical trials.
* Experience of clinical trials, including study setup, monitoring activities, and data review.
* Familiarity with clinical operations, including site coordination, documentation, and compliance.
* Bachelor’s degree in Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Nursing or Medicine.
* Knowledge of ICH-GCP guidelines, and relevant regulatory standards (extensive training will be provided).
* Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines.
* Effective written and verbal communication skills and the ability to collaborate with multidisciplinary teams.
* Proficiency in MS Office and comfort working with EDC and CTMS.
* Willingness to learn, adapt, and build a long-term career in clinical research; prior internship or exposure in clinical research is required.

If you're looking to be a part of a professional and dynamic team, we'd love to hear from you!
