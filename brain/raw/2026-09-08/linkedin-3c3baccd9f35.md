📝 Project Manager — Pipeline Installation
Our client is a leading civil engineering and pipeline construction specialist with 75+ years of experience in transport pipelines, underground infrastructure, and industrial installations. We are seeking an experienced Project Manager with a proven track record on complex pipeline projects, with strong knowledge of phased construction, welding standards, pressure testing, and working in port environments with environmental constraints.

🏗️ Project Overview
20" and 6" insulated pipeline system over 5.5km in South West England, comprising aboveground sections, concrete culverts, underground sections, 3 HDD crossings, and one micro tunnel.
Phased installation: aboveground pipes welded at ground level and lifted onto existing pipe rack; underground pipes welded and wrapped at ground level, trenched per temporary works design, lowered and final welds in situ; PE coating and insulation; pressure testing.

✅ Responsibilities
✅ Manage phased pipeline installation across aboveground and underground scopes
✅ Oversee welding operations and ensure adherence to welding standards
✅ Coordinate HDD crossings and micro tunnel works
✅ Manage pressure testing protocols and PE coating/insulation works
✅ Ensure compliance with UK CDM regulations and environmental constraints
✅ Supervise safe working practices in a port/marine environment
