NO, you don't need 20 complex automations to fix your productivity.🙂

We sometimes get so excited about what AI and automation can do that we forget to ask a more important question:

What exactly needs fixing?

You should start with auditing your week and writing a list for your biggest time drains....those tasks that are repetitive and constantly slows you down.

It can look like manually typing the same onboarding email to every new client, manually following up with leads or manually copy and pasting an invoice details into a spreadsheet.

Then start by asking yourself:
🟣 What keeps taking up my time?
🟣 How often am I doing it?
🟣 Can part of it be simplified or automated?

Pick the top three of your list and start there.

Fixing just those three repetitive bottlenecks will buy you back hoursss of mental clarity. 

Remember that true efficiency is not about automating everything. It's about finding the things that repeatedly waste your time and simplifying them.
