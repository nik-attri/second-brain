“Shipping doesn’t have a data-capture problem, it has a problem with integration, automation, and validation,” said Spinergie Senior Sales Manager Satu Lipponen after this year’s SMM Hamburg conference. “The message from the market is that they have the data, they just need the right tool to refine and check that data before sending it to the reports and teams that need it.”

Our team heard a similar story from many people at the conference—that while they are capturing a lot of data, it’s never used. Performance, sensor, and onboard data is collected in vast volumes but doesn't reach the shore team in a usable form to drive performance improvements. Market players are seeking tools they can rely on to ensure the accuracy of their data. Spinergie Head of Product Bertrand Fatus said: “Verification loops matter. We’re seeing a lot of interest in how our Spinergie Operations solution captures errors and flags data-quality issues before submission, not after.”

A further topic kept cropping up: workflow gaps. Bertrand continued, “Many companies have automated parts of their emissions and performance reporting, but many still have a gap in one of three places: integrating data sources, automating the workflow, or validating data before submission.” 

Satu added: “Short-sea shipping is a good example. Fixed routes run hundreds of times a year, and several reports per vessel, per day, create enormous reporting volume that has not been fully automated.” Spinergie Operations automates and processes that data, taking the administrative burden off crews and providing onshore teams with the clean analytics they need to make effective improvements.
