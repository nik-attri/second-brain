Dear Manu,
You're dead-gorgeous in the article's photo…
And you're DEAD WRONG in the interview. 

I think you know it.
Both.
And I think you play your cards right.

Here are my educated arguments:

Primo,
The digital advertising ecosystem in which you operate.

A report this week from internet hosting company Cloudfare asserted that 57% of online activity is coming from bots and only 43% is human.

The Score: Bots 57 - Humans 43

Also, according to NBC News, a human may visit five websites before making a purchase. 
An AI-programmed bot will visit five thousand — leaving a long and winding trail of dodgy metrics.
Bots are running wild all over the web, jacking up phony 'KPI' numbers that look fabulous on a spreadsheet. 

Which means the reports you're getting about the amazing success of your online ad campaigns are frequently complete and utter HØRSESHĪT.


Secundo.
Dr. Karen Nelson-Field.
There is no attention-data scientist more appreciated and revered than she is in the educated world of marketing (please, Google her)

She revealed research showing that an ad needs about 1.5 seconds of active human attention before it has any chance of leaving a memory. 

Below that line, nothing happens. 
No encoding. 
No effect. 
The money evaporates in expenses without any impact.

And on scrollable social feeds, most impressions live and then die below that line. Nelson-Field’s data suggests the audience you thought you bought on scrollable media can shrink by as much as 70% once attention enters the equation. 

70%!!!
That’s one hell of a motherphucker waste, don’t you think?!

Through your agency, your customers buy impressions that no human being ever cognitively processes.
Served, counted, billed, invisible.


Tertio.
Organisations are obsessed with performance. 

When a CEO wants results by Friday, a cheap, clicky, trackable online impression beats an expensive, attentive, well-crafted TV brand-building campaign that pays out in 2028.
I get it.
Your metrics are measurable, they're defensible in a boardroom, and they feel like control in a world that IS out of control. 

Online promotion is not winning because it works harder. 
It is winning because it is easier to buy and easier to defend.

We’ve all allowed measurement to be manipulated by the digital channels, measuring something that suits their business model. 
Exclusively.

But scientific research after scientific research in the last two decades, the data kept pointing to the same uncomfortable truth:

You’re gambling CLIENT MONEY like in a Casino, at tables where the house dictates the real winners.
The cards we’re dealt are dodgy.
And more than half the other players aren’t even human.

Your clients think you’re optimizing chances for their performance. 
In reality, you’re simply playing longer at tables designed to drain them.
You're just slowly phucking them.

That’s the ugly truth
I think you know it
But as an agency owner, you also know how to play your cards right
