Hi connections.

Honestly, I didn't think I'd be writing this when I first stepped into PETRONAS. 

What started as a 6-month internship somehow turned into another chapter through GEES, and before I knew it, a year and a half had passed.

A lot had happened in between. I learned things I never expected to learn, work on things I never thought I'd be involved in, survived countless deadlines, meetings, spreadsheet, and "can you help me with this?" moments🤣.

But if there is one thing I will remember most, it won't be the work. It will be the people.

The colleagues who made the office feel less like an office. The bosses who gave me the opportunity to learn and trusted me along the way. The friends who made the random days the best one. And everyone who, knowingly or not, left a little something in this chapter of my life.

From being the intern who was still figuring out things out to wrapping up my GEES journey. Its been one hell of a ride.

Thank you for everything, untill we cross paths again 💚
