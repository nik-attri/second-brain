Experience managing multiple customers simultaneously
Exposure to SAP AMS / SAP S/4HANA services preferred
Strong understanding of ITSM, KPIs, governance, and service lifecycle management
Excellent customer communication and stakeholder management skills
Experience handling challenging/demanding customers
Strong intercultural and global team coordination skills
Experience with ServiceNow, SAP tools, or other ITSM/ticketing platforms

🇸🇦 Fluent Arabic – Mandatory
