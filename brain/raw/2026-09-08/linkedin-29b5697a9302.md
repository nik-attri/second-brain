We’re Hiring: Assistant Brand Manager – Pharmacy & Wellness

Naheed is looking for a marketing professional to support the growth of its *Pharmacy & Wellness vertical* across Naheed stores and Naheed.pk.

Key Responsibilities:

* Develop and execute marketing plans for pharmacy, OTC, vitamins, supplements, personal care and wellness categories
* Create monthly content and promotional calendars across digital and in-store channels
* Identify category growth opportunities through customer, competitor and market analysis
* Plan integrated campaigns with pharmaceutical, healthcare and wellness brands
* Manage digital campaigns across Meta, Google and Naheed’s owned platforms
* Coordinate health-awareness initiatives, seasonal campaigns and in-store activations
* Work closely with category, e-commerce, pharmacy operations and creative teams
* Monitor campaign performance, sales impact, engagement and return on marketing investment
* Ensure all pharmacy-related communication is accurate, responsible and compliant with applicable guidelines
* Maintain consistent brand communication across stores, social media and Naheed.pk

*Candidate Profile:*

* Pharm-D or a bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business Administration or a related field
* 2–4 years of experience in pharmaceutical, healthcare, retail, FMCG or e-commerce marketing
* Experience in brand management, category marketing or digital campaign execution
* Understanding of OTC, vitamins, supplements, personal care and wellness categories
* Familiarity with Meta Ads, Google Analytics and performance reporting
* Strong communication, coordination and project-management skills
* Commercially aware, creative and comfortable working in a fast-paced retail environment

📍 *Location:* Karachi
🏢 *Company:* Naheed

Interested candidates may apply through this post or share their CV at jobs@naheed.pk, mentioning “Assistant Brand Manager – Pharmacy & Wellness” in the subject line.
