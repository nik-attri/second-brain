Nothing drains a privacy team's week quite like a DSAR buried in five years of nested, unstructured email threads.

You are staring at thousands of messages, wondering how to extract personal data without over-disclosing confidential business information.

Here is the 4-step triage framework to cut through the noise.

1. Scope the search strictly. Define the exact date ranges, keywords, and sender parameters before you even touch your eDiscovery tools.

2. Filter the noise. Run deduplication immediately and automatically strip out standard corporate newsletters, system alerts, and calendar invites.

3. Redact by pattern first. Mask out third-party personal data, like unrelated phone numbers and email addresses, using automated rules before manual review begins.

4. Apply proportionality. If an email thread contains overwhelming amounts of non-relevant business data, extract the specific personal data into a clean summary document rather than handing over raw emails.

Unstructured data is a massive liability.

A rigorous triage process is your best defence against drowning in it.
