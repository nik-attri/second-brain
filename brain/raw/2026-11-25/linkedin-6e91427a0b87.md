A successful GRC journey goes beyond technology, workflows and automation. 𝐈𝐭 𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐫𝐭𝐬 𝐰𝐢𝐭𝐡 𝐠𝐞𝐭𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐟𝐮𝐧𝐝𝐚𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐚𝐥𝐬 𝐫𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭.

Before embarking on your GRC implementation, consider whether your organisation has:
1) Clear risk and control ownership across departments
2) Defined management expectations and accountability
3) An established risk appetite
4) Effective escalation mechanisms
5) Leadership commitment and oversight

Technology can enable and automate governance processes, but it cannot replace the ownership, direction and commitment that must come from management.

With the right governance foundation and the right technology working together, organisations can unlock greater value from their GRC initiatives.
