A SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) platform is the backbone of most Security Operations Centers. It collects logs and events from across the environment (endpoints, network, cloud, identity, applications), normalizes them, and applies correlation rules, analytics and increasingly UEBA and AI-assisted triage to surface the incidents that matter.
 
Done well, it lets analysts spend their time on the critical alerts instead of drowning in noise. Done poorly, it becomes an expensive log bucket. Knowing the platforms, and the logs that feed them, is a core skill for anyone on the blue team.
