Today, we're introducing Creative Intelligence at Dolnai

A NEW WAY to understand what's happening inside your PMax ads

For years, we've helped advertisers understand which products sell, where budgets go, which assets perform and where campaigns lose money.

But there was one question we couldn't answer properly.

WHY do certain ads perform better than others?
And this new capability?

It helps us investigate exactly that.

Here's why we built it:

1. Google Ads already tells us which images and headlines get clicks and conversions. But knowing that an image achieves a 7% CTR doesn't explain what makes it more appealing. Is it the colour, composition, product placement or promotional message? We wanted to investigate those characteristics.

2. Campaigns can contain hundreds or thousands of images, headlines and descriptions. Reviewing every asset manually and comparing its characteristics with performance takes enormous time. Finding consistent patterns across that volume is difficult, even for experienced PPC specialists.

3. Agencies spend hours reviewing creative assets and preparing recommendations for clients. Yet many decisions still depend on personal judgement. We wanted to connect creative characteristics with actual campaign data, so teams can identify patterns and build better-informed creative briefs.

There's a lot more to explore, but we'd be here all day.
We've just launched Creative Intelligence Reports for Performance Max.

Analyse your images and ad copy, discover patterns in your own campaign data, and find out what your best-performing creatives have in common.
