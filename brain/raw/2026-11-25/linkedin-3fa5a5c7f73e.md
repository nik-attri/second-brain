We are Hiring at Nahar Organics !

We are looking for an experienced ICS Manager (Internal
Control System) to join our team in Guwahati Assam.

Company: Nahar Organics
Department: Quality Assurance / Organic Certification
Job Location: Guwahati- Assam
Qualification - Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture, Agronomy, Horticulture, Organic  Agriculture or a related field. (Male Candidate)
Experience - 3-5 years of experience preferred.

-Maintain updated ICS Manuals, Procedures, formats and records.
-Monitor implementation of organic compliance systems across all relevant operations.

-Ensure proper farmer registration, agreements, farm records, and organic declarations.
-Maintain farmer master data, farm maps, land details, crop details, and cultivation          history.
-Monitor farmer compliance and identify non-compliance or prohibited practices etc.
