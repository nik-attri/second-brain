What if the repetitive office tasks you do every day could run automatically, even while you're at home sleeping?

A perspective from Nyalinda Mbawo, CEO of Access Learning Hub, on AI, automation and the future of work.

Think about the time spent updating spreadsheets, compiling reports, processing routine requests, sending reminders, sorting records or doing the same administrative tasks over and over.

HR, finance, administration and operations teams deal with repetitive work every day. But how much of that work actually needs to be done manually?

Imagine automating these tasks so they run in the background, freeing your team to focus on problem-solving, decision-making and work that requires human judgement.

AI and automation aren't just about technology. They're about rethinking how we work, reducing repetitive tasks and making better use of our time.

There is a common fear that AI is here to take people's jobs. But perhaps we should be asking a different question: How can AI help our people do their jobs better?

AI is not necessarily about replacing people. It is about making teams more efficient, reducing repetitive workloads and allowing people to concentrate on the work that creates real value.

The opportunity lies in equipping employees with the skills to work alongside AI, not fear it.
