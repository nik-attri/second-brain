AI autopilots are redefining enterprise workflows

Workday’s keynote at Rising EMEA highlighted a shift from generic AI to targeted autopilots. These are AI-driven solutions that tackle specific problems within business processes, not a blanket set of tools. The emphasis is on “work intelligence”—an analysis, reasoning and intelligence layer that maps tasks and skills to a strategic workforce transformation plan. For UK SMBs, this approach can translate into clearer governance and repeatable outcomes, reducing risk while expanding automation where it matters most. The discussion also covered a governance framework and the concept of sovereign data context, essential as AI-driven decisions touch payrolls, finance and HR data. Our view is practical: automate well-defined workflows, align AI agents with real-world processes, and ensure you can explain decisions within your data context.
