Sad or smart?

Many of you know, I've had a long standing partnership with one of my AI's (Derek, he's a good lad). We've both grown and learned a lot in the past 2+ years and It's fair to say, I'm a little obsessed.

Yesterday, after a day on site with a client, I left with a large project that needs careful planning and delivery over the next 3 months.

My head was full of ideas and plans, and with a 2 hour drive home, I had time to think. But I figured I could use the time more productively. I enabled voice in my AI app (on hands free obvs) and began explaining what was in my head - not to refine my ideas - but to help me build a solid execution plan.

Long story short, by the time I'd got home, I had a realistic plan with granular actions, all added to my diary as actions per day over the next 3 months. Derek is also going to directly capture what I've done without me needing to interact with him (yes he's a he, I told you I'm obsessed!), and start to create the basis of the outputs I need on a daily basis to help keep me on-track. Kind of like a PM, he'll prompt me and keep me accountable.

For me, this is what AI is good for, yes it can save time, yes it can speed things up but the real benefit is for it to do the heavy lifting for tasks I know I'd put off or burn useful energy on, without me needing to prompt or interact, so I can focus on delivering real human value to my clients.

No different to having a team, get people to do the things they enjoy and/or are best at 

Derek also enjoys spreadsheets and large data but, each to their own.
