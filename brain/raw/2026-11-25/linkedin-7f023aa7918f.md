After a full room for our first Diverge / Converge, edition two is here. 🔪🧵

Diverge / Converge is a speaker series from 100kicks about the interests that don't seem to belong together, and what we learn when they collide. No polished keynotes, just a few slides, honest stories and room for conversation.

In November we explored nail art, tarot and astrology. 

This time it's Embodied Experiences: materials, craft and the knowledge that lives in your body. As more of the world gets automated, it's a chance to think about what we learn by making, cutting, shaping and doing.

Speakers:

🥩 Thom spent years as an economist before swapping spreadsheets for a butcher's block. He'll talk about what markets and meat have in common, and what it really means to understand where value comes from.

📸 Rahma Jama went from fashion shoots to service design at the BBC. She'll share how relationship-building, adaptability and resilience carry between two very different worlds.

✨ Georgia Iacovou, founder of Horrific/Terrific, on Play With Yourself: resisting the commodification of pleasure in the AI boom. AI might be able to replicate your job, but it can't feel pleasure for you, and feeling it is what having a body is for.

📍 Health Foundry, London
