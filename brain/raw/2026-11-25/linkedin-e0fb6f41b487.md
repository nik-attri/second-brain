Change Is Hard. Staying the Same Can Be Even Harder.

As a COO with over 40 years of experience, I've witnessed tremendous changes in technology, business operations and laboratory workflows. One thing has remained consistent — change is rarely easy.

I've seen anatomical pathology and histology laboratories evolve from manually sketching specimens to using consumer-grade cameras for gross and slide imaging, from handwritten documentation to barcoding and automated specimen tracking.

I understand the reluctance to change. When you've spent years perfecting a process, stepping outside your comfort zone can be intimidating. Add staffing shortages, increasing caseloads, budget constraints and daily operational pressures, and it's understandable why change gets postponed.

Yet, looking back over my career, some of the most rewarding experiences have come from embracing the very changes we initially resisted.

There is tremendous satisfaction in eliminating repetitive tasks, reducing unnecessary stress, improving productivity and giving talented professionals the freedom to focus on the work they were trained to do.

At SPOT Imaging, our approach to automation and LEAN workflows isn't about changing everything overnight. It's about identifying opportunities, taking manageable steps and building upon each success.
