Cinema upgrade ho gaya. Aap kab hoge?

I came across this billboard last night, right below the poster of Nitesh Tiwari’s Ramayana.

And honestly, the marketing caught my attention.

Not just because of the ₹9 offer, but because of the question.

“Cinema upgrade ho gaya. Aap kab hoge?”

Think about it.

We’ve gone from black-and-white cinema to colour.

From single screens to multiplexes.
From physical sets to incredible VFX.
And now, even stories we’ve heard for generations are being reimagined for IMAX.

The stories haven’t necessarily changed. The way we experience them has.

But when it comes to our professional lives, something interesting is happening.

Many of us are still doing things the way we’ve always done them.

→ Spending hours preparing reports that could be automated.

→ Building presentations slide by slide when AI can help create the first draft.

→ Manually analysing data that AI tools can help us interpret.

→ Repeating the same administrative tasks every single week.

And no, I’m not saying everything needs to be handed over to AI.

But shouldn’t we at least know what’s possible?

The real advantage of AI might not be knowing how to build it. It might be knowing how to use it to do your existing job better.

That’s what made this Be10x billboard interesting to me.

They are introducing professionals to practical AI applications for everyday work, including presentations, reports, emails and data analysis, without requiring a coding background.

And the starting offer is just ₹9.

What I liked most was the way the message was communicated.

No complicated explanation of AI.
No technical jargon.
Just one simple question that makes you reflect.

As someone pursuing an MBA and increasingly interested in how technology is changing the way businesses operate, this is a shift worth paying attention to.

Because industries will keep upgrading.

The question is whether our skills will keep up.

Cinema upgrade ho gaya. Aap kab hoge?
