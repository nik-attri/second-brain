🚨 From QA to Scrum Master | ₹25 LPA Interview Preparation – Part 3 (Final Round)
We've covered Scrum fundamentals, real-time scenarios, Sprint execution and delivery challenges.

Now let's look at the final round, where interviewers assess leadership, stakeholder management and practical decision-making.

🔹𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗥𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱: 𝗟𝗲𝗮𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗵𝗶𝗽 & 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗿𝗶𝗮𝗹 𝗤𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀
1️⃣ A senior stakeholder directly assigns tasks to Developers without involving the Product Owner. How would you handle this?
2️⃣ Your team members don't trust each other, and collaboration is poor. What would you do?
3️⃣ The same problems keep coming up in every Sprint Retrospective, but no improvement actions are implemented. How would you address this?
4️⃣ Management asks you to increase the team's velocity by 20%. How would you respond?
5️⃣ Multiple Scrum Teams are working on the same product, and dependencies are delaying delivery. How would you manage them?
6️⃣ The Product Owner and Developers disagree on priorities. How would you facilitate a resolution?
7️⃣ Your team is meeting Sprint deadlines, but production defects are increasing. How would you identify the problem and help the team improve quality?
8️⃣ If you join a new team as a Scrum Master, what would your first 30 days look like?
9️⃣ Coming from a QA background, how would you use your experience in quality, defect management and team collaboration to succeed as a Scrum Master?

🎯 What interviewers want to assess
✅ Leadership without authority.
✅ Stakeholder communication and conflict resolution.
✅ Team coaching and facilitation.
✅ Understanding of quality and continuous improvement.
✅ Ability to apply previous experience in a new role.

💡 Remember: Moving from QA to Scrum Master is not just about learning Scrum terminology. It's about demonstrating that you can facilitate collaboration, handle challenges and help teams deliver value.
