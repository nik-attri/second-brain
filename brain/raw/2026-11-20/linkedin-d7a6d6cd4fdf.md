After 20 years in the United States Marine Corps, I'm searching for my next mission, and I'll be honest: it's been harder than I expected.

I started my career in 2006 and worked my way to becoming a senior intelligence professional, eventually serving as an All-Source Intelligence Analyst, Intel-Operations Chief, Intelligence Chief, and being hand-selected to fill an Officer-level billet on a U.S. Navy ship as their Intelligence Officer.  I have briefed general officers in Afghanistan, I've led teams, made decisions under pressure, and worked in some tough environments. None of that fully prepared me for the civilian job search.

I've rewritten my resume more times than I can count, translating military experience into language that makes sense to hiring managers. I've submitted applications that disappeared without a reply. I've learned that being qualified isn't always enough to get noticed, and that silence can be the hardest part.

I'm not sharing this for sympathy. I'm sharing it because I know many transitioning service members, and plenty of civilians too, are going through the same thing right now. If that's you, you're not alone.

Here's what I'm looking for:

• Roles in intelligence analysis, security, or operations
• Organizations that value leadership, integrity, and mission focus
• I hold a TS//SCI clearance and I'm based in San Antonio, Texas

Most of all, I'm looking to connect. If you work in these fields, are hiring, or have made this transition yourself, I'd welcome a conversation. A referral, advice, or even a quick introduction can make a real difference.

Thank you for reading, and thank you in advance to anyone willing to share this post. It may reach the right person.
