Grant-seeking shouldn’t feel like a second full-time job. So we built Proxifund.
 
If you’ve worked at an NGO, nonprofit, or social enterprise, you know the drill: hours spent digging through grant databases, rewriting applications from scratch every time, tracking deadlines in scattered spreadsheets, and getting rejected over gaps you didn’t know you had.
 
Proxifund answers the hard questions of grant-seeking: what fits your mission, how to write it, and whether you’re ready to win.
 
Semantic grant discovery. One search covers six live sources, including Grants.gov, neural web search, and international grant feeds. It ranks every open call against your mission, sectors, and geography, so it finds funders that a keyword search would miss.
 
AI proposal writer. Turn any match into a draft that follows the funder’s template and sounds like your organisation. Edit it like a normal document, and export to Word, PDF, or Excel.
 
Pipeline tracking. One board covers every stage: identified, in progress, submitted, won. You get a compliance checklist for each grant and a deadline reminder 14 days out.
 
Readiness audit. An honest review of your governance, finance, and safeguarding, with a score out of 100 and the exact items to fix before a funder sees your application.
 
Everything happens in one workspace, from your first search to the submitted proposal.
 
It’s free to start, and no credit card is needed: https://proxifund.ai
 
If you work in fundraising or development, or know a team stuck in grant hell, we’d love your feedback. Please share this with them.
