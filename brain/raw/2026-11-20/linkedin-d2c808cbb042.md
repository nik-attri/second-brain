Next up in our Team Spotlight series: Liam Hybl, Associate Consultant.

Liam brings a thoughtful, steady presence to WRS, along with a sharp eye for detail and a willingness to take on just about anything. With a background in business and finance, he enjoys solving complex problems and collaborating with teammates who bring different perspectives.

1. He's a bit of a Swiss Army knife.
Research? He's on it. Project coordination? Happy to help. Something unexpected comes up and you need help in a pinch? Count him in. Liam enjoys wearing different hats and being involved in all kinds of work across WRS. He's equally comfortable digging into an analysis or helping a teammate get a deliverable across the finish line, and he likes that no two days look quite the same.

2. He likes a good research rabbit hole.
With an MBA and a master's in finance, Liam has a natural interest in and aptitude for research, numbers, and figuring out how things fit together. He enjoys digging into information, finding patterns, and uncovering details that might otherwise get overlooked. Give him a complicated question and some room to investigate, and he's happy.

3. He's learning that the people are often the most interesting part.
One thing that surprised Liam about consulting is how often the biggest challenges come back to people. Even projects that start with research or strategy eventually involve different perspectives, relationships, and figuring out how to move forward together. Working across organizations has given him a new appreciation for the human side of business, something that doesn't always show up in a spreadsheet.

4. He's the person you want around when things hit the fan.
Liam isn't one to shy away when things get complicated. He's calm, willing to help, and ready to figure out what needs to happen next. His teammates can count on him to follow through and pay attention to the little things. In fact, his friends and family know that when Liam says he's doing "one last check," he'll probably check it three more times just to be sure.

5. He's happiest somewhere between the mountains and the ocean.
Snowboarding in the mountains, surfing in the ocean, or spending time with family and friends, Liam loves being outside. Surfing has become his latest obsession, and he's always looking for another opportunity to get in the water. Whether it's a day on the slopes or a trip to the coast, he's happiest when there's a little adventure involved.
