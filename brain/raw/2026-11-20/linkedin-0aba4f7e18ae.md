This will not sit well with many of my GTM connections.
Building workflows, waterfalls, harnesses, and GTM context is a waste of time.
Anthropic went public today. Two launches ahead of its IPO show why.

Anthropic added spreadsheets and whiteboards to Claude, then gave it a seat in Google Meet. Workflows, carefully crafted by dozens of startups over many years, suddenly became irrelevant.

There is next to zero value left in naked software or business automations.
It is all in the model’s intelligence needed to recreate them.

Skills and harnesses helped you get weaker models through a GTM playbook a year ago.
The illusion is thinking they still give you a GTM edge. The same thing just happened to AI notetakers.

A frontier model already knows hundreds of GTM playbooks, and feeding it your team’s assumptions just makes the results worse.

The biggest asset you have left is creativity.
This is where 90% of your GTM energy should go.

Research the hell out of your client’s space and product. Create totally unique messaging for their project, spell out their unmatched value, or maybe simply walk away if there is nothing there.

Go crazy about understanding your target segments and finding any signals you could use that correlate with fit and conversions.

We isolated the intelligence behind target account and contact list building in our specialized GTM search model. You will not outperform it.

DiscoLike’s model covers 98% of all business websites.
It has the same contacts database as others and knows how to find many more on the fly.

You can scrape maps, parse and match trade directories, and waterfall multiple providers.
You will still fall short.

Your automations will get you a worse list and rob you of time better spent on creating a campaign turns heads.

Hold on to the past and watch others pass you and move on.
Or let machines take care of the repetitive part and learn to create.
