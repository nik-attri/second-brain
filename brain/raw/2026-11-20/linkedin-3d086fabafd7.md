Ramayana movie made ₹1,547 crore at the box office in just 15 days


spotted this billboard right below the Ramayana poster 

"cinema upgrade ho gaya, aap kab hoge?"

now, whoever came up with that line understood the assignment

because cinema really has come a long way
black and white to colour, single screens to IMAX, painted backdrops to VFX

meanwhile, some of us are still spending monday mornings copying last week's excel report, changing a few numbers and calling it a new report😭

we have AI tools that can summarise documents, analyse data, build presentations & help automate repetitive work

yet most people are still using their computers like it's 2015
and that's what made this billboard interesting to me

it wasn't really asking people to learn some complicated new technology

it was asking a much simpler question: why are we still doing manually what technology can help us do faster?

that's the idea behind Be10x's AI courses, which focus on practical office tasks like Excel, presentations, reports and emails, without requiring a coding background
anyway, i'm still thinking about that billboard
