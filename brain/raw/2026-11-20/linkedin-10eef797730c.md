My most expensive lesson business was AUD400k. 

60 containers. A 10-day holiday. One payment that hadn't cleared.

It cost us $400,000 in port storage. 💸

Here's what happened, and the 3 lessons I'd pass on 👇

We buy all our mesh from China.

When the war rerouted vessels, sailings out of Tianjin dropped from 4 a week to 1. We were fighting for space on every ship.

So our containers came in lots: 1 here, 3 there, 10 on another vessel.

Within 2 weeks, 60 of them were sitting at the port in Melbourne.

We'd paid the supplier. The payment hadn't cleared.

The next day, China shut for a 10-day holiday.

No master Bill of Lading. No clearance. Just the storage meter running at around $900 per container, per day.

In the end we paid $400,000 to Patrick, DP World and VICT. (True story)

💡 What we learnt

1⃣ Communication is king 🗣️

After the free days ended, storage was around $900 per container, per day at VICT.

Then a gap in communication with our customs broker meant containers that could have cleared on Friday sat over the weekend.

That weekend alone cost us an extra $70,000.

If we'd communicated clearly and lodged those clearances a day earlier, we'd have saved it.

Never look down on communication. Sometimes it's worth millions.

2⃣ Have everything in writing 📊

We built one master spreadsheet: all 60 container numbers, every payment, and how many days of storage each one had racked up.

That's how we decided where our cash went first: the containers that had been sitting longest.

The moment a BL landed, we lodged clearance, paid the invoices and got it moving.

Everyone needed to know exactly what was happening. it was all written down, we eventually got through it.

3⃣ Perspective 🔥

Remembering that life is just a game, and that money lost can always be made back, makes life hell of a lot easier as an entrepreneur.

Don't let yourself spiral. If you've built a business to the point where it works, you can make it back.

What doesn't kill you really does make you stronger.

Life is just a game. 

Money lost can always be made back.
