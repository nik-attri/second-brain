We keep upgrading everything around us. But are we upgrading ourselves?

I came across an interesting billboard recently, placed right below the Ramayana poster. One line caught my attention:

“Cinema upgrade ho gaya. Aap kab hoge?”

And honestly, it made me think.

Look at the evolution of cinema:

Black and white turned into colour.
Single screens evolved into multiplexes.
Practical sets transformed into incredible VFX.
And now, stories we’ve grown up listening to are getting an IMAX experience.

The stories haven’t changed. The technology behind them has.

But when I look at our everyday work culture, I see something different.

We’re still spending hours preparing reports manually, creating presentations from scratch, managing repetitive Excel tasks and writing the same emails again and again.

Meanwhile, AI is changing how work gets done.

My take? The real advantage of AI isn’t replacing people. It’s empowering people to do more with the same time.

Imagine completing a task in 15 minutes that previously took two hours. That’s not just productivity; that’s a competitive advantage.

That’s what caught my attention about Be10x .

They are helping working professionals learn practical AI tools for everyday office tasks, including reports, presentations, emails and data analysis, without requiring coding skills or a technical background.

And the entry point? Just ₹9.

Sometimes, the smallest investment can introduce you to the biggest shift in how you work.

We’ve upgraded our phones, our cars, our entertainment and almost everything around us.

Maybe it’s time to upgrade the way we work, too.
