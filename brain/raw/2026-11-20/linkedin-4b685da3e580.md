What a week! Just landed back in Madrid but my head's still at Berkeley. Wasn't sure I was going to make it at all after my flight got cancelled this weekend!

So glad I made it after all. After months of planning, it was incredible to be UC Berkeley for the launch of the IE Silicon Valley Hub, developed by IE University in collaboration with CITRIS and the Banatao Institute.

Working in Comms can sometimes feel overwhelming before a big launch... From the early days of developing the narrative and key messages, to cross-Atlantic collaboration on designs, guest lists, crazy spreadsheets, minute-by-minute schedules and filming plans - there's been a lot going on behind the scenes!

But then when you finally seeing it all come together... hell yeah it's worth it.

And then on the day making last minutes changes to the presentations, doing photocalls,  interviewing spokespeople and digging into their perspectives on what this collaboration could mean for education, research, AI and entrepreneurship.

I also got to put my whistle to good use, corralling some pretty important people, including IE's President, Santiago Iniguez. Apologies for any burst eardrums! 😅

All of it thanks to an incredible team from both sides of the pond. Huge thanks to our inner IE School of Science and Technology circle, our visionaries Ikhlaq Sidhu Geoffroy Gérard and Leticia Cabral-Calvillo; our new CITRIS family, Alexandre Bayen, Shiv Harris, MSc, Emily Hartman, Julie Shapiro, Camille Crittenden, Tamara Bock Daisy and the wider team; the hardest-working IE US crew, Elizabeth Stallard, Timothy Howe and Dani Escalante-Regalado; and JJ and Techboogie Media. So lovely to touch base again Michael E Marks, Trond Petersen and chew the ear off Ines Drieselmann (sorry!), debate with Alexandr Chevtaev what's REALLY going on with those outtages, and get some great student perspectives including Paul Ruiz Alliata and many other alumni and partners.

They say it takes a village. This really did!

Lots of conversations started this week, and when I get over this high (and the jetlag) I can't wait to get my teeth into the next steps in building this connection.
