I was pooing my pants over a board game.

Not playing it. Getting it made and shipped.

When we took on the project, I’d thought a hell of a lot about the thing we were making. What I hadn’t thought through nearly as well was the gap between when the money had to go out and when it would come in...

Funny how quickly you become interested in shipping dates when it’s your own cash!! 🤣😆

This is the bit I mean when I say Do Hard Things in business. Not “take a massive punt and hope for the best”. I’d actually recommend a bit less of that, thanks.

I mean opening the spreadsheet when you’d rather shut the laptop. Working out what’s due, what’s moved and who you need to ring. Finding out which bit of the problem you can actually do something about. Eat the frog!! 🐸

I’m glad we took on the project. I’d take on something that stretches us again.

I’d also ask a lot more bloody questions about the shipping.

#DoHardThings
