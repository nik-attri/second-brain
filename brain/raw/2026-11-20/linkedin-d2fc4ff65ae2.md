We went partying yesterday.
Somewhere between all the chaos, conversations and celebrations, we also decided to build a SaaS product. 😂
Because apparently, having a social life and building something aren't mutually exclusive.
A few of us got together and built a Market Intelligence Tracker using Emergent.
The idea was simple:
What if we could build a tool that scrapes data from across the market, brings it all together, and analyses it to make sense of what's actually happening?
Instead of manually jumping between websites, collecting information and connecting the dots, we wanted one place that could do the heavy lifting.
And so, we built it!
The product was fun, but the part I keep thinking about is how quickly an idea went from a conversation to something tangible with the right tools.
BCom graduate by degree, now spending my time building products and exploring business. Nights like this one make the journey even more fun.
Big shoutout to Emergent for making the building process so much easier and Scaler School of Business for creating an environment where ideas turn into experiments.
Now, back to partying. Or maybe there's another product to build. 👀
