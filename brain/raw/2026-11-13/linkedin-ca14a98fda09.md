🚀 Introducing CivicCare – Smart Public Complaint Management System!
As part of our mini-project for the Natural Language Processing (NLP) course under our Double Minor in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML), my friend Nucolas Fernandes and I developed CivicCare, an application that uses NLP and Machine Learning to automate public complaint classification.

How it works:
1. Citizens submit complaints through the application.
2. TF-IDF and Logistic Regression automatically predict the complaint category.
3. Administrators review predictions and manually correct incorrect classifications.
4. These corrections are added to the labelled training data and used to retrain the model, creating a feedback-driven learning workflow.

Technology Stack: Python, Tkinter, Scikit-learn and Pandas.

One of the key learning outcomes was exploring a human-in-the-loop machine learning approach, where human feedback contributes to improving model performance over successive training cycles.

As Mechanical Engineering students, this project helped us strengthen our understanding of AI/ML, data processing and practical software development beyond conventional engineering applications.

A great hands-on learning experience!
