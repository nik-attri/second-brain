Our Founder and CEO, Dino Dragun, will speak at the Offshore Wind Asia (OWA) Summit (OWA2026), part of the Offshore Energy & Engineering Global Conference (OEEG), in Shanghai from 17 to 19 November.

As offshore wind projects move into deeper water and more demanding seabed conditions, the quality of marine geophysical data, and how quickly it can be processed and interpreted, has a direct effect on project uncertainty and execution. Dino will share how data processing and digital solutions can support the next generation of offshore wind projects, drawing on more than 100 offshore projects our team has worked on since 2020.

If you’ll be at OWA2026 in Shanghai, Dino would be glad to meet and show you Marelia by Hidrocibalae, the desktop software platform we launched this month to automate and streamline marine geophysical data processing and interpretation workflows.

Send us a message here on LinkedIn to arrange a time.
