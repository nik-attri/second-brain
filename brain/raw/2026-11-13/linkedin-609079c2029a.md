🚨 BREAKING: Microsoft outage triggers unexpected business continuity exercise! 😂

With Microsoft currently having a wobble and Copilot taking an unexpected day off, it looks like many of us are having to activate our disaster recovery plans.

Unfortunately, mine involves something I haven’t used properly in a while…

MY BRAIN. 🧠😂

Yes, that’s right. I’m having to revert to manually thinking, remembering things, solving problems and, heaven forbid, coming up with my own ideas!

No Copilot. No AI assistance. Just me, a keyboard and a rapidly fading memory of how I used to do things before technology took over.

On a serious note, it’s a good reminder of how reliant we’ve become on technology and the importance of having a Plan B.

Although, judging by how long it’s taking me to write this post, I might need to add a few more hours to my disaster recovery SLA! 🤣
