We use Claude Code to test and scale hundreds of campaigns automatically, it's helped improve performance by 65% in the last 3 months.

Most campaigns are still assessed manually, someone checks the results, decides what to scale and what to stop, and gets to it when they have time. 

Ours are assessed every day, and we didn't hire anyone to do it.

Here is how it works:
1. Claude Code pulls every live campaign, every step and every variant

2. Each variant is scored on the sales opportunities it produces, against its own campaign's baseline

3. Winners are scaled, and any overall play below our threshold is stopped and flagged for a rewrite

4. We approve the changes with one click in Claude, and they go straight into the campaigns

5. Sending shifts towards what is winning

Then it runs again tomorrow 🤑 

That daily loop is a big part of how we have helped clients generate 400,000+ sales opportunities this year.
