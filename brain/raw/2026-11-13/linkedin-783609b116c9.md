Why one subscription matters ?

Since Tuesday's new site went live, the question we've had most often from manufacturers is:

"How does putting compliance, data, environmental reporting, and advisory under one subscription actually change things on our side?"

Three concrete answers:

1️⃣ The data stops forking
The most common failure pattern we see: compliance data lives in one system, environmental data lives in another, distributor data lives in a third. When regulations update, they update independently. Within six months, the three "sources of truth" tell three different stories.
One subscription means one data model. One update propagates everywhere.

2️⃣ The expertise is embedded, not transactional
Traditional model: compliance consultants quote per hour, LCA practitioners quote per project, regulatory lawyers quote per question. Each one costs money even to ask the question.
One subscription means the expertise is on tap. The right answer to "do we need to notify under this delegated act?" is a Teams message, not a billable call.

3️⃣ The deadlines stop being surprises
A single compliance-intelligence feed covering UKCA, CE, CPR, ESPR, and the sector-specific delegated acts means your team knows what's landing six to twelve months out. Not when the trade press catches on. Not when a customer flags it. Six to twelve months out.

For manufacturers selling into the UK and EU with product lines in categories that are already named under ESPR delegated acts (electronics, textiles, construction products, batteries, detergents) — this is where the cost of fragmentation gets measurable.
