Beyond Seller Central: Is Your Amazon Data Showing You the Whole Market?

Your Amazon account can tell you a lot.

You can analyse sales, advertising performance, inventory, search-term reports, listing data and other available account insights.

But there's another side of the market worth investigating:

What happens before customers reach your listing?

What questions are they asking?
What alternatives are they comparing?
Which trends are gaining attention?
How visible are competitors outside Amazon?
Is demand growing, declining or shifting?

This is where external market-research tools and AI-connected workflows become valuable.

Rather than collecting tools randomly, consider six business decisions:

1. Understand customer questions

AnswerThePublic • AlsoAsked • Perplexity

Discover questions, related topics and information customers seek.

2. Measure search demand

DataForSEO • Semrush • Ahrefs

Investigate keyword demand, search results, competition and SEO opportunities.

3. Identify trends and timing

Google Trends • Exploding Topics • Glimpse

Explore changing interest and emerging product-related topics.

4. Research competitor websites

Similarweb • Firecrawl • Browserbase

Investigate public website content, competitive positioning and estimated traffic patterns.

5. Understand search visibility

SerpApi • Brave Search • Moz

Explore search results, visibility and relevant SEO signals.

6. Collect public web intelligence at scale

Bright Data • Apify • Exa

Search, discover and extract publicly accessible information for structured research.

Where does MCP fit in?

Model Context Protocol (MCP) allows compatible AI applications to connect with external tools and data sources.

Instead of manually moving information between platforms, an appropriately configured AI workflow can retrieve relevant data, compare findings and help produce research.

But an important distinction: these are 18 research tools, not 18 universally available, official, plug-and-play MCP servers. Some offer vendor-supported MCP connections, others provide APIs or require third-party integrations. Access, pricing and capabilities vary.
