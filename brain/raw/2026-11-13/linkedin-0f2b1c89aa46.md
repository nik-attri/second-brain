Anyone Can Do Standard

Standard equipment, anyone can supply.
When a client asks us for something built around their process, that is where our team gives its best: engineering, creativity and craftsmanship in the same project.
This low pressure manifold is a good example. The brief: distribute and isolate liquids across ten independent lines from a single header, in a stainless steel package that is safe to operate and easy to move.
What we delivered:
·        10 DN100 branches, each with its own manually operated butterfly valve
·        Header and reducers in AISI 316 stainless steel, support frame in AISI 304
·        Designed for 10 bar maximum allowable pressure, from -10°C to +60°C
·        Equipotential bonding on every branch, pressure gauge and lifting lugs
·        Materials fully traceable with EN 10204 type 3.1 certificates
·        Built to sound engineering practice under the PED, delivered with its Declaration of Conformity to Design and operating manual
Two units, built by our team in Ortona and ready for service.
