Why do 90% of people get robotic, hallucinated answers from Claude 3.5 and GPT-4o?
Because they are still "vibe prompting" like it's 2023.
They write one casual sentence:
"Act as a copywriter and write an email for my new SaaS..."
Frontier LLMs get confused, produce 500 words of generic fluff, and waste your time.
Anthropic’s official prompt engineering documentation reveals that frontier models perform with 40% higher accuracy when instructions are structured inside XML tags.
Why?
XML tags (<system_role>, <task_directive>, <operating_rules>) build mathematical boundaries. They stop context-bleed and force the model to follow instructions strictly.
The problem? Manually typing XML tags every single time takes 3-5 minutes of friction.
