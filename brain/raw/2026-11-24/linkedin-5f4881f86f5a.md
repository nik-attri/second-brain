Some moments are worth looking back on. On May 6, 2025, at its Think event, IBM announced new hybrid technologies to help enterprises build and deploy AI agents using their own data. The announcement covered new agent-building tools in watsonx Orchestrate, the webMethods Hybrid Integration solution, an updated watsonx.data platform for unstructured data, and the IBM LinuxONE 5 platform.

In that press release, our CEO, Malcolm Adams, appeared as a client voice for watsonx Orchestrate. He explained how Avid Solutions uses Orchestrate on AWS to automate repetitive tasks across customer service, project management and accounting.

For a company our size, that matters. IBM's enterprise announcements are usually about very large organizations. Being included showed that agents are not only for companies with huge budgets and big IT departments. A smaller, research-driven firm can put them to work on everyday business problems and see the difference.

It also reflects how we think about AI. We didn't start with a grand vision of replacing anyone. We started with the work that nobody on our team wanted to do by hand, and we automated it one workflow at a time. Those early wins taught us what to build next.

Since then, that same practical approach has shaped how we design agentic systems, with people still approving the decisions that matter.

Thank you to IBM for including our story. IBM Bob recaps the announcement in the video, and the full press release is linked below.
