🚀 You built an accounting workflow with AI. Great! Now what?

Yesterday, I introduced PythonMuse Lab and our first article about making AI-built workflows usable by the entire accounting team. Today, let's look at five key takeaways.

1️⃣ Not every accountant needs to become a developer. As AI changes how we work, we'll need people who design, build, operate, review, and maintain workflows. These are responsibilities, not necessarily new job titles.

2️⃣ Give your team a better front door. Your AR specialist shouldn't need to open VS Code or understand Python to run a monthly AR aging review. A simple interface with clear instructions can do the job.

3️⃣ A green check is NOT a sign-off! Automated checks can validate files, calculations, and reconciliations. But someone still needs to review exceptions, question unusual results, and apply professional judgment.

4️⃣ Test the handoff, not just the code. Can a colleague run your workflow, recognize a problem, and validate the output without asking you for help?

5️⃣ And here's a bonus: potentially greener AI! 🌱 When we use AI to design and build a workflow, then let tested scripts handle repetitive execution, we can avoid unnecessary LLM calls. That can mean lower computing costs and potentially less energy consumption, especially for frequently repeated processes.

AI doesn't always need to RUN the process. Sometimes its greatest value is helping us BUILD a better one!
