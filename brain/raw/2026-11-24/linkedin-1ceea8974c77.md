AI is not killing tech jobs. It is killing the FIRST job.

The 2026 data for India:
• Tech openings for 0 to 2 years of experience fell 44% vs last year (Xpheno)
• 55% of Indian IT firms cut entry-level hiring, vs 14% for senior roles (Nomura)
• Indian firms say 37% of entry-level tasks are already done by AI
• But India's AI/ML job postings grew 25% while overall IT hiring fell 3%

My take: the jobs are not disappearing, the entry door is moving. Freshers who show real AI-assisted projects, not just certificates, will stand out.
