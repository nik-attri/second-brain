Getting warehouse teams to actually follow SOPs is nearly impossible when you rely on reminders and meeting. (Quoting an experience from my initial days of career as a part of Warehouse Excellence team.)

I learned this the hard way.

We spent weeks writing the perfect Standard Operating Procedures for our warehouse. Detailed steps. Clear timelines. Beautiful docs.

Compliance rate? Almost zero.

People were too busy. The old habits settled hard. And frankly, manual data entry feels like a chore when you are trying to move physical goods out the door.

So, I stopped asking people to care about the SOP.

Instead, I changed the system.

We updated our workflow so that manually generated invoices and Delivery Challans could not be used for a Gate Pass anymore. They had to come directly from the order fulfilled, completed SAP entry from the system.

No entry. No invoice. No dispatch & delivery.

Suddenly, compliance jumped to 1000% overnight.

Not because the team changed their mindset. But because the correct path became the only path to get the job done.

Stop trying to train people out of bad behaviors when you can just design a system that makes the right behavior automatic.

How do you usually drive adoption for unglamorous internal processes?
