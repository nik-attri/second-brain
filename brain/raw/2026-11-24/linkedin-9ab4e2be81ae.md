𝗪𝗲’𝗿𝗲 #𝗛𝗶𝗿𝗶𝗻𝗴: 𝗛𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻 𝗥𝗲𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗿𝗰𝗲𝘀 (𝗛𝗥) 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗿 (𝗟𝗮𝗵𝗼𝗿𝗲)
Sanyou Machinery & Technology International is seeking an experienced, strategic Human Resources (HR) Manager to lead our people operations in Lahore. If you have a strong background in technical recruitment, HR compliance, and team leadership within an engineering or industrial environment, we would love to connect with you.

Key Responsibilities:
• Talent Acquisition: Lead end-to-end hiring for engineers, field service technicians, sales engineers, and support staff, including interviews and onboarding.
• HR Operations: Manage personnel records, attendance, leave, and payroll coordination.
• Performance Management: Run KPI frameworks, performance reviews, and annual appraisals.
• Employee Relations: Handle grievances, resolve conflicts, and build a positive team culture.
• Training & Development: Identify skill gaps and arrange technical and soft-skills training.
• Policy & Compliance: Draft and enforce HR policies, codes of conduct, and labor-law compliance.
• Compensation & Benefits: Manage salary structures, incentives, medical benefits, EOBI and social security contributions, and annual increments.
• Employee Engagement: Plan team events, recognition programs, and retention initiatives.
• Health & Safety: Enforce safety practices for office staff and for installation and service teams working on client sites.
• Employer Branding: Work with marketing on the careers page and LinkedIn to attract talent.

Qualifications & Experience:
• Experience: At least 5 years in HR, preferably in engineering, trading, technical services, or manufacturing.
• Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in HR or Business Administration.
• Recruitment: Proven track record hiring technical, sales, and managerial roles.
• Labor Laws: Strong knowledge of Pakistan labor laws and statutory requirements.

How to Apply:
Send your updated CV to job@sanyou.com.pk and mention "Human Resources (HR) Manager" in the email subject line.
