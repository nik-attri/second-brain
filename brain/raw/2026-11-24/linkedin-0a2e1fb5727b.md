I’d been at my desk since 7. 

Non-stop calls, 20 million emails and about 500 spreadsheets. Clients needed answers, suppliers needed chasing, commissions needed working out and and and....

By lunchtime, I needed five minutes away from my desk, so I went to Woolies.

I’d just paid for my lunch and was walking out when I overheard an elderly lady asking the security guard if he knew where Nando’s was. She’d heard they had ice cream on special and wanted to go find it.

The penny dropped for me. In that moment, her priority was finding that ice cream special. Mine was figuring out who to deal with first when I got back to my desk and how the hell to keep myself sane while running a business.

I just thought it was interesting. Two people in the same place, going about their day with completely different things on their minds. It gave me a bit of perspective in the middle of a manic day.

I’m sure plenty of business owners can relate. At this time of year, everyone has something they want wrapped up before the break. Some days, deciding what needs your attention first is half the job.

And yeah, the December break is looking very, very attractive right now. I won’t lie.  😅
