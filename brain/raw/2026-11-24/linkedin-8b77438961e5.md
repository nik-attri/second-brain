Just over two months ago I started a new position as Operations Associate at Maze, and it’s been a busy one!

If you’ve been following cyber news, the last few months have been pretty crazy. October was dubbed “Black October” after a wave of serious cyber attacks around the world, and it’s starting to look like the new norm.

With new and more powerful AI models coming out every few weeks, finding and exploiting vulnerabilities is more accessible than ever. More have been published this year than ever before, drowning security teams in open tickets from their scanners, many of which aren’t even relevant to them.

That’s the problem Maze is solving. We deploy a fleet of AI agents that investigate each vulnerability like an expert security professional would, work out which are reachable and exploitable in your cloud and code environments, and help teams fix the ones that matter before attackers get to them.

I joined to support Phil O'Hagan, our VP of Operations, across all areas of the business (people, finance, legal, compliance, and go-to-market), helping us keep up with our rapid growth and finding ways to do things more efficiently. Highlights so far have been organising a full company trip to Paris for our October offsite and developing bespoke internal tooling and processes to stay ahead of our compliance and operational requirements.

From motorsport to web3 to cyber, I’ve always been drawn to fast-moving industries where things change quickly and you have to keep innovating. A lot of my project and product management thinking carries over, from spotting where a process is slow or manual to working out how to fix it and getting it done. The difference here is that with AI, I can often build the fix myself.

Working in an AI-first company has been liberating, and the pace and quality of what this team ships still amazes me. I feel very lucky to be learning from such talented people every day. Looking forward to what's next!

Big thank you to Jumpstart for making the connection.

Keep an eye on Maze at https://mazehq.com/
