Distributed clean power portfolios are drowning in fragmented data with telemetry scattered across inverters, smart meters, SCADA systems, and contradictory spreadsheets. ⚡📊
London-based Metris Energy raised a $5M (€4.35M) seed round led by PT1 Ventures, Octopus Ventures, AENU, and Blackfinch Ventures (bringing total funding to $7.5M) alongside launching Metria AI to unify renewable asset operations.
🔹 Turning Scattered Telemetry Into Actionable Workflows 🤖 Led by CEO Natasha Jones and William Whatley, Metris reconciles hardware, grid, and financial data into one continuously updated system of record. Its new agentic layer, Metria AI, diagnoses underperformance, dispatches maintenance, and manages PPA deadlines , already powering 10,000+ solar sites (500 MW) with 8x YoY revenue growth.
🔹 Expanding Beyond Solar Into Europe 🌍 Deploying its fresh capital into Germany and broader European markets, Metris is extending coverage into wind turbines and combined heat and power (CHP) assets, strengthening distributed clean energy unit economics where manual admin previously eroded margins.
The key test ahead: proving deterministic reconciliation accuracy holds as the platform expands from standardized solar arrays into complex multi-technology fleets.
What’s the biggest margin killer in distributed clean energy today: equipment downtime or fragmented back-office operations? 👇
