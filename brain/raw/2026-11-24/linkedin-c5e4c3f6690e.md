A plumber finishes a job at 6pm.

Then the real work starts.

Quote in a spreadsheet. Client details in WhatsApp. Materials in a notebook. Invoice in Word. Signature on paper he'll lose by Friday.

Five tools. Zero connection between them.

That's the daily reality for freelance technicians and small service companies. Electricians, plumbers, IT support. Same mess.

Generic tools like Sheets or Notion weren't built for dispatching a technician or tracking stock in a van.

So I built TecniGest. Clients, work orders, quotes, inventory, invoices. One platform. Built only for technicians.

If you know a field service owner still running on spreadsheets, send this to them.
