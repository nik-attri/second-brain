Most of the contractors I talk to in the UK have been told they need to do something with AI. Very few know where to start, and that's a fair place to be. Planning lives in spreadsheets and in people's heads, and nobody wants an AI agent let loose on that.  

Contractors in Norway and Sweden have been at this for a while. Labour there is expensive, working-time rules are strict and certificates get checked on site, so many of them moved their planning into one live system years ago. Now they're adding AI on top of it, one workflow at a time.  

On Friday 4 December at 11:00, I'm hosting a 30-minute session with Kristian Mella, our CIO and co-founder, on what they've learned. Kristian will cover what AI agents can and can't do on a project today and how to pick your first workflow. He'll also show resource planning live, from a warning about expiring CSCS cards to a forecast that tells you whether you have the people for the bid you're about to win.
