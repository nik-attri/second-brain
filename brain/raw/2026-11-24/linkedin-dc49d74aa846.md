I have a hard time accepting “That's just how we've always done it.”

When I look at a business, I don't just see how it operates. I immediately start thinking about how it could operate better.

Why is this taking so long?
Why are we doing this manually?
Why does something so simple require five different steps?

My mind is constantly looking for a better way.

And sometimes, the smallest change in a process can make the biggest difference in a company's day-to-day operations.

I think that's what I love most about what I do. Not just managing businesses, but discovering their potential and finding ways to bring it out.

I'm rarely satisfied with something simply because it works.

I'm more interested in how much better it could work.
