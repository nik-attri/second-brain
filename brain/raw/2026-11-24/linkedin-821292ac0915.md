Connecting People with Opportunity – Building Human-Centered Hiring Journeys 🌟

Meet Caroline Maher Acquisition Specialist at Union Group in Cairo, an HR professional focused on sourcing, head hunting, and building candidate experiences that put people first.

Caroline joined Union Group in November 2025, after about two years as Office Manager at Not Guilty Inc, where she coordinated meetings and events, managed schedules, and handled confidential employee and client information. Earlier, she worked as a freelance Data Entry Specialist, as a Program Facilitator at Wellspring planning training camps and corporate events, and in virtual meeting facilitation at 360 Solutions. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Accounting and Business Management from Future Academy, with a grade of 3.56, and completed the Human Resources Foundation Program at AUC.

Her profile shows a recruiter who is building depth quickly. At Union Group, she attracts, sources, interviews, and hires talent, builds candidate pipelines, and partners with hiring managers. Her recent hiring work spans Finance Business Partner, IT Infrastructure and Operations Team Leader, Junior Sales Representative, and Outdoor Sales Representative roles, and she supports the company's Summer Internship Program 2026, which offers paid internships across eight functions for recent graduates. She also attended the Marsh IMEA People Forum, where a colleague called her humble and dedicated.

Caroline describes herself as passionate about understanding people, supporting their growth, and contributing to positive workplace cultures. She is committed to learning and to creating meaningful, human-centered interactions throughout the hiring journey. Her own words after a recent recognition were gratitude for the experiences, challenges, and opportunities that shaped her career, and the privilege of helping people take the next step in their professional journeys.

We honor Caroline Maher for her dedication to talent acquisition and people-focused recruitment, and for her contribution to Egypt's HR and recruitment community.
