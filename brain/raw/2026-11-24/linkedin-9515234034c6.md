Certified Pick up (CPu) and Secure Container Release are now live at the Port of Bruges (Zeebrugge).

This marks another major step toward a safer, pincode-free, and digitally secured port environment.

For MSC and Hapag-Lloyd containers in Zeebrugge, the entire chain takes place in SCR, from release to pickup preparation. SCR communicates with Certified Pick Up in the background, allowing the terminal to verify the collection at the gate based on the trucker’s Alfapass.

How does it work?  
SCR integrates directly with CPu behind the scenes. This means you manage your digital release rights, automate your workflows, and hand over pick-up rights to subcontractors with a single click in SCR.
