Enterprise Service Management - Part 3: The Handoff. 

The original process: Where was the ball dropped? Handoff buried in an email, buried in an inbox? Lack of clarity? Handoff reassigned in another email? Accountability shifted in a spreadsheet that the new party doesn't have access to? I thought I told you, I needed this done yesterday. 

The new process: The request; opened through portal, triage automated to agent. Actioned and task completed. More to it? Different department. That's okay. Assign it, dont email it. Communicate through the ticket to keep back and forth within the request. CC or forward as you please. Assign to groups, to people. Is there a next part? No? Close the ticket. Done. No loose ends. Loop closed. 

Were there attachments somewhere along the way? Where are those damn things? Oh, right... In the ticket.

Need reporting and analytics? It's now a hell of a lot easier than counting emails and finding the latest copy of a spreadsheet...if you have access.
