The AI startup Just Raised $250 MILLION to do what consultants charge thousands for.
~Financial models.
 ~Pitch decks.
 ~Investment research.
 ~Due diligence.
  
YEH. That stuff.
Model ML just hit a $1.5 BILLION valuation.
And WHAT I think some consultants should get Nervous!
  
Because here is the Uncomfortable Question.
If AI can build your financial model, Then why the HELL should someone pay YOU?
-For dragging Excel formulas?
-Making 30 beautiful slides?
-Copying market research into a fancy Word? 
  
Come on!
AI is getting Damn good at that.
But, HERE things get interesting.
A founder says:
~” We'll grow 50% every year."
~AI can Calculate it.

But who's going to Stop and Ask:
"Based on WHAT?"
Who is going to Question the Customer Acquisition Costs? 
Who will test “What If Sales collapse?
And WHO will tell a founder:
"Your expansion plan makes no financial sense."
  
Just Listen!
I DO NOT believe that AI will kill good consultants.
But it will Expose consultants who sell documents instead of Critical Analysis and judgment.
Because founders do not need another beautiful spreadsheet.
  
They need someone who can tell them whether the numbers are complete BULLSHIT. Or Not.
The real question isn't whether AI can do your job.
It is whether you are adding any value beyond what AI can already do.
Because AI is getting better every damn day.
And what it can't do today, it might do tomorrow.
So consultants, What Are We Actually Bringing to the PLATE?
Are we ready for this conversation?
