One of my fav things about working with Sara McNamara is how quickly a seemingly simple project can turn into 47 additional questions.

Take a product launch.

My PMM brain is thinking:
What's changing?
Why does it matter?
Who needs to know?
How do we explain it?
What do customers need to do?

Great. Let's communicate.

Aaaaaaannnnnndddddddd then we start pulling on the ops threads.

Which customers are actually impacted?
Are the workflows in place to support the change?
What if they're already in another campaign?
What happens if they don't take action?
Are we even reaching the right audience?
Are there signals we're missing that should change how we approach them?
Are the right people at Vector getting looped in?
How does Sales know when to follow up?
And how the hell are we measuring all of this?

Suddenly that "quick communication" is an entire GTM motion 😂

This is where I've found the PMM + RevOps partnership to be SO valuable (fr no bs).

Sara and I spend a LOT of time challenging assumptions, connecting the dots, figuring out what we're actually trying to accomplish + working backward into what needs to happen.

Bringing these functions together BEFORE the plan is already in motion is key.
And keeping them together = a major key. 

We meet every week.
We Slack constantly.
Memes, actual work, more memes.
Occasionally a spreadsheet or data-heavy deck that ruins everyone's day.
Because if we aren't aligned, we're not just creating confusion for ourselves.

We're creating it for our internal teams, prospects, and customers.
And automating it at scale💀
