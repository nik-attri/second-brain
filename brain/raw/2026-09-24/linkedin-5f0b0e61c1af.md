🤔 Why do you need a middle-pack machine?
You already have a Hanging Tissue Packing Machine.
So why add another machine for the middle pack?
In real production, 10 packs of Hanging Tissue need to be grouped into one middle pack. If this is done manually, operators have to open the bag, arrange the products, and push them into the middle-pack bag one by one. 📦
It works — but as production volume grows, labor, consistency, and packing efficiency become real challenges.
That’s where a middle-pack machine makes a difference. ⚙️
From the factory floor, we design solutions around what happens after the individual pack is finished — helping customers reduce manual handling, improve packing efficiency, and keep the process more consistent.
