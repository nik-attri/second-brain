How can we move from AI experimentation to large-scale transformation in R&D?

The Roland Berger Paris AI Transformation Club brought together Christophe Le Ligné CTO at Valeo, Raphaël Leclercq, Director Data & AI at SOCOTEC, Denis Goudstikker, Ambassador, Siemens, and Jules Giraud, Head of Data & AI at Alta Ares around this central question.

The discussion moderated by our Partner Eric Esperance highlighted six key priorities:

🔹 From POCs to process transformation: moving beyond isolated use cases to rethink processes end-to-end.

🔹 Breaking down silos: connecting business functions, data and systems to unlock systemic gains.

🔹 Towards agentic AI: embedding intelligence directly into processes by connecting data, tools and workflows.

🔹 Make, buy or rent? Leveraging external models and infrastructure while retaining control over data, context and orchestration.

🔹 Measuring value at process level: looking beyond individual productivity gains to assess impact on costs, duration and overall performance.

🔹 Evolving roles and skills: as AI automates execution, judgment, design, validation and business expertise become increasingly critical.
 
👉 The challenge is no longer simply to use AI in R&D, but to rethink how R&D operates through AI.

Thank you to all our speakers and participants for the exciting discussions!

Ready to move beyond AI experimentation and make AI a true transformation engine for R&D? Let’s continue the conversation.
