I sit in boardrooms all the time, and I see the exact same scam everywhere.

An executive walks in, turns on the projector, and puts up a massive dashboard for a multi-million dollar digital transformation. Every single traffic light on the screen is green. On time. Under budget. Tremendous success, they say. Everyone claps.

It is a total fake. A complete illusion!

If you leave that beautiful glass boardroom and actually walk the operational floor, you find out the truth. The new system is a disaster. It’s clunky, it’s slow, and it doesn't work in the real world.

So what do the frontline workers do? They secretly run the entire business on manual, duct-taped Excel spreadsheets just to survive the day!

The project managers get their bonuses for a "green" launch, while the actual workers are drowning in administrative garbage. The executives sit upstairs patting themselves on the back, completely disconnected from reality.

Dashboards don’t tell the truth. If you manage your business by staring at a screen instead of watching how the work is actually getting done with your own two eyes, you are flying blind. You are going to crash.
Stop looking at the green lights. Go walk the floor!
