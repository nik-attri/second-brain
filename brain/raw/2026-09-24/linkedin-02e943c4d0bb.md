“ये तो ChatGPT का काम है.”

Today I spoke to a founder who runs a digital marketing institute and agency. He shared an interesting classroom problem.

His older students want to learn digital marketing to grow their businesses or get better salaries.

Some of his Gen Z students have a different brief.

“Why should I learn this? ChatGPT can do it. Teach me something ChatGPT can’t.”

Fair enough. Fees भरेंगे. सीखेंगे नहीं. Principles हैं.

The poor fellow now has to check ChatGPT’s latest capabilities before deciding what he is allowed to teach 😂

I understand the question. Why spend hours doing something a tool can finish in seconds?

But “Why should I do this manually?” and “Why should I understand this?” are two very different questions.

Let ChatGPT write the ad. How will you judge whether it is worth running? Let it analyse a campaign. How will you spot nonsense delivered with excellent grammar?

Apparently we all want to supervise AI. Learning the work we’re supervising feels a little beneath the designation.

You can outsource the work. You still need enough knowledge to question the output.

Because when the client’s budget disappears with nothing to show for it…

“ये तो ChatGPT का काम था” might be a difficult invoice to defend.
