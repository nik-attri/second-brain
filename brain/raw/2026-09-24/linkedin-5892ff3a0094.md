LinkedIn has become terrible.

I've been on here for a while. Over the past year, the signal-to-noise ratio has collapsed.

My feed is increasingly full of people selling something, people pretending to be experts, generic motivational bollocks and an extraordinary amount of crypto content.

And frankly, 99.9% of the crypto content I see on here is absolute junk.

I've reached the point where most of the Bitcoin accounts I repeatedly encounter have been blocked. The same recycled narratives. The same charts. The same predictions. The same f'ing arguments.

Then there are the connection requests.

“Please connect with me.”

Ten minutes later:

“Thanks for connecting. I help businesses…”

Spam with a handshake attached.

I'm increasingly convinced LinkedIn has become the amateur's network rather than the professional's network.

It's become middle-management hell.

And anyone who's worked as a consultant knows exactly what that means. You see the difference between people actually doing the work and people generating meetings, PowerPoints, processes and corporate fucking noise around them.

The irony is that the people actually running serious businesses aren't necessarily spending their days posting about how to run serious businesses.

They're busy running them.

That's actually why I've been posting less.

I've been working on Hostile Solutions.

I'll be posting considerably more about that in the coming weeks because the AI question needs proper due diligence, not another thousand posts telling us that AI produces slop, can't be trusted and hallucinates.

Most of that analysis is about as sophisticated as the people writing it saying they can't use a spreadsheet properly.

AI deserves better analysis than that.

Frankly, most of it deserves a can in the face.
