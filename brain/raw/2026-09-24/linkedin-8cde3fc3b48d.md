We built a sales dashboard.

It immediately exposed 18 deals nobody knew were dying.

Not the CRM.
Not the spreadsheets.
Not the Monday sales report.

An Australian property investment advisory had a problem:

HubSpot had the deals.
JotForm had the meeting outcomes.
Google Calendar had the bookings.
Drive had the documents.

But nobody had the truth.

So we built ONE live sales intelligence dashboard.
And the first time they opened the 90-day view?

It exposed 18 finance scenarios still sitting open.
Some had been waiting 60+ DAYS.

Now, instead of rebuilding spreadsheets, the team can instantly see:

→ Every deal across 4 sales stages
→ Who's moving deals (and who's not)
→ Conversion rates by agent
→ No-shows killing the funnel
→ Deals untouched for 14+ days
→ Missing forms + Drive folders
→ Finance scenarios going stale
→ This week's upcoming meetings

All from the systems they already use.

HubSpot.
JotForm.
Google Calendar.
Google Drive.

No new CRM.
No manual Monday reporting.
No spreadsheet archaeology.

The biggest lesson?

Most businesses don't need more data.
They need their existing data to finally tell them what the hell is going on.
