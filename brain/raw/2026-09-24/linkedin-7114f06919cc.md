Dreamforce 2026: our week in review 🌉

What a week in San Francisco. Our CEO Laith Al-Hashimi was on the ground, and the Sirocco Group team followed every keynote closely from Stockholm and beyond.

The main keynote brought Marc Benioff, Jensen Huang, and Dario Amodei to the same stage. Koa, Claudeforce and AIforce were not presented as separate launches. They were one argument: the interface is changing, but the trust underneath it is not up for negotiation.

Three things that stood out to us:
🔹 The UI is no longer the moat. With AIforce and Claudeforce, Salesforce data and workflows now show up inside Claude, Slack and other AI tools, running on the same permissions and business rules. Permission design and data quality are becoming the product.

🔹 Agents are becoming packaged products. Named, job-ready agents and multi-model choice (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI on the way) mean the question is no longer "which AI wins." It's who has the governed, contextual data to make any model useful.

🔹 Process comes before automation. Apromore, now part of Salesforce, gives companies a real view of how their processes actually run before agents are pointed at them. Automate a broken process and you just scale the problem faster.

That last point is exactly where Sirocco is doubling down.

Our Data Foundations package helps companies get their data, permissions and governance ready for AI. And we're building deep expertise in Apromore, using process mining and simulation to find where agents will genuinely pay off. We deliver it all with SAFe, so process insight turns into prioritized, measurable change across teams, not just a nice report.

Our takeaway for Swedish companies: technology is not the blocker anymore. The real work is human. It's pointing AI at the right problem, with the right context, inside the right guardrails.

If you're wondering where to start, start with your data and your processes. We'd love to help.
