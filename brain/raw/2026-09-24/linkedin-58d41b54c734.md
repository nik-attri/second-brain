I built an escape room inside a Google Sheet.

It's called Escape Spreadsheet Hell. Three levels. Each level can only be beaten with a keyboard shortcut.

If you know the keystroke, you're out in seconds. If you don't, you're clicking around a spreadsheet forever.

Which is the whole point, because that's the actual difference between people who are fast in Sheets and people who aren't. Not formulas. Fingers.

The fast people jump to the edge of data with a keystroke. Find anything in two keys. Move between tabs without touching the mouse.

The slow people scroll. And scroll. And scroll.

I made learning it a game because nobody practices shortcuts from a list. But everybody wants to escape a room.

Three shortcuts. That's the ransom. Most people can't name them.
