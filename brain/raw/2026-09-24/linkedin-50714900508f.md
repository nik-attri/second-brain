Xiaomi might quietly be becoming one of the most interesting AI companies in the world.
I was looking at Artificial Analysis and their latest MiMo model looks to be around the same level as GPT-6 Sol on their Intelligence Index, but at a much lower cost.

My team and I spend quite a bit of time discussing the latest AI developments, testing them ourselves, and assessing their safety and relevance to our sector and our clients.
Is it actually useful? Is it safe? Is it worth our time? More importantly, is it worth our participants' time to learn? We still do this manually. We don't use AI agents to feed us the latest AI news. I want us to read, test, discuss and challenge each other. It forces us to exercise our own critical thinking and professional judgement.

The latest thing everyone seems to be talking about is Jev from TypeSafe AI.

But the one that really shocked me was Xiaomi because no one is talking about this.

Xiaomi Technology! I still think of them as the company making phones, robot vacuums and EVs. Now they are building AI models that appear to be competing with some of the best models out there. China is catching up incredibly fast.

Sometimes the most interesting developments aren't the ones everyone is talking about.
