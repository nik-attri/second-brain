Hulas Infra is looking for a motivated and detail-oriented professional to join our team as:
SAP Executive
Location: Birgunj, Nepal

-Handle day-to-day SAP transactions and ensure accurate data entry.
-Manage and maintain SAP master data and records.
-Coordinate with Finance, Procurement, Inventory, Production, and other relevant departments for SAP-related activities.
-Monitor and resolve SAP transaction and process-related issues.
-Generate SAP reports and provide required information to management.

-Assist in improving and streamlining SAP processes.
-Perform other SAP-related duties as assigned by the management.

-Bachelor’s degree in IT, Computer Applications, Management, Accounting, or a related field.
-Prior experience working with SAP ERP will be preferred.
-Good understanding of SAP modules and business processes.
-Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
-Good knowledge of MS Office, particularly Excel.
-Strong communication and coordination skills.
-Ability to work independently as well as in a team.
-Freshers with relevant SAP knowledge may also be considered.

How to Apply:
Interested candidates are requested to send their updated CV to recruitment@hulasgroup.com with the subject line “Application for SAP Executive – Birgunj.”
