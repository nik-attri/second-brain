VHirex Consulting Pvt Ltd is hiring Admin Executive (Contract-to-Hire) 
 
We are looking for a detail-oriented, well-organized Admin Executive to join our team and keep our office running smoothly. If you enjoy vendor coordination, facility management, and being the go-to person for day-to-day operations, this role is for you.

Role: Admin Executive
Employment Type: Contract-to-Hire
Experience: 3-5 years in an administration role
Education: Any Bachelor's degree
Location: Bengaluru 

Roles and responsibilities:

Printing & Stationery:
* Handle requests for visiting cards, ID cards, and other printed material
* Manage stock of letterheads, envelopes, and stationery
* Prepare monthly stationery requirements

Vendor Management:
* Coordinate with vendors on service issues and invoicing
* Obtain and compare quotations, negotiate rates, and prepare rate contract letters
* Check and process invoices with accurate system entries
* Oversee vendors for flowers, beverages, lunch and snacks, newspapers and magazines, and tea/coffee vending
* Run sample testing and annual contracts, and monitor payments to keep operations cost-effective

General Administration:
* Book hotels and arrange transport for guests, and communicate with guests and senior management
* Process admin invoices in the internal tool
* Allocate workstations, cupboards, and drawers for new hires, and maintain the workstation database
* Prepare documents for mobile and data card connections based on approvals
* Prepare monthly Airtel and Vodafone mobile statements
* Manage inter-office vehicle requests, the shuttle between Silk Board Metro and the office, and parking
* Manage company vehicles and handle vehicle and employee insurance
* Build and manage corporate tie-ups with all-star category hotels
* Coordinate with local police and authorities for routine facility checks
* Front Office & Visitor Management
* Manage visitors and front-office operations

Required skills
* 3-5 years of hands-on admin experience
* Strong vendor negotiation and follow-up skills
* Good communication skills for working with senior management and guests
* Accuracy with invoices, records, and data entry
* Ability to multitask and stay organized in a fast-paced environment

Interview: Shortlisted candidates must be available for a face-to-face interview on 29th September.

Interested candidate can drop your resume a pavithra.r@vhirex.com
