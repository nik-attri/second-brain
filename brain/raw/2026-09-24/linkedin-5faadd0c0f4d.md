We’re Hiring | HR Assistant – Dubai
ANAROCK Middle East is growing, and we are looking for an energetic and organized HR Assistant to join our team in Dubai, UAE.
The HR Assistant will support the day-to-day HR operations and work closely with the HR Manager across recruitment, onboarding, employee records, HR administration, and employee engagement.
Key Responsibilities:
• Support recruitment activities, including CV screening, interview coordination, and candidate follow-ups
• Prepare HR documents, and employee correspondence
• Coordinate new joiner onboarding and employee documentation
• Maintain accurate employee records and HR databases
• Support visa, insurance, leave, attendance, and other HR administration
• Assist with employee engagement activities and internal HR initiatives
• Ensure confidentiality and proper handling of employee information
What We’re Looking For:
• 1–2 years of HR or HR Administration experience, preferably in the UAE
• Good understanding of general HR processes
• Strong organizational and communication skills
• Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Excel and Word
• Detail-oriented, proactive, and able to manage multiple priorities
• Professional English communication skills
 Location: Dubai, UAE
Company: ANAROCK Middle East
If you are passionate about HR and looking for an opportunity to develop your career within a growing organization, we would love to hear from you.
Apply by sending your CV to: hr@anarock.me
