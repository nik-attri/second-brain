HRSG - Innovative Business Solutions - Innovative Business Solutions- Innovative Business Solutions is currently hiring for the following position for one of its clients.

Position: Terminal Supervisor
Salary: 60,000 to 65,000/- 
Location: Port Qasim Karachi
No of Positions: 01
Industry: Industry: Multinational Shipping and Logistics Sector 
Contract – Full time regular

Job Responsibilities:

A. Yard & Empty Container Operations
Supervise daily empty container receiving, inspection, handling, stacking, shifting, and delivery operations.
Ensure containers are positioned in the correct yard blocks according to size, type, condition, status, shipping line, and operational requirements.
Maintain proper yard discipline and optimize available grounding and stacking capacity.
Ensure FIFO and other applicable yard-planning principles are followed.
Coordinate with the Data Entry / Documentation team to ensure container status is updated accurately and on time.
Monitor container movements and ensure no unauthorized shifting or release takes place.
Ensure damaged, dirty, OOS, repair, PTI, and sound containers are properly segregated.
Monitor yard utilization and highlight capacity constraints to the Depot Site Manager.

E. HSE & Safety Compliance
Ensure all depot activities are carried out in accordance with company HSE requirements and approved SOPs.
Conduct regular Toolbox Talks (TBT) and safety briefings for operational staff.
Ensure mandatory PPE requirements are followed by employees, visitors, drivers, contractors, and other stakeholders.
Ensure safe distance is maintained between personnel and operating MHE.
Monitor safe container handling, lifting, stacking, and unstacking practices.
Immediately stop any unsafe activity and report hazards, near misses, incidents, and unsafe conditions.
Participate in emergency response, drills, incident investigations, and corrective actions.
Maintain good housekeeping and ensure fire access, emergency routes, and safety areas remain unobstructed.


Required Experience: Minimum 5 years of experience in container depot, terminal, or yard operations.
Experience in empty container handling, yard operations, container inspection, repair, or MHE operations preferred.
Previous supervisory experience is required.

Education: Minimum Intermediate / Diploma or qualification in Logistics, Transport, Supply Chain, or Operations will be an advantage.
