🚨 WE ARE HIRING | FACTORY MANAGER – ODISHA 🚨

S V Ispat Pvt. Ltd. is looking for an experienced, hands-on Factory Manager to take complete ownership of plant operations at our facility in Jharsuguda, Odisha.

📍 Location: Jharsuguda, Odisha
👤 Position: Factory Manager – Odisha
📌 Reporting To: Management
🏭 Industry: Manufacturing | Mineral Processing

🔹 About the Role
This is a senior operational leadership role with end-to-end accountability for the plant, covering Man, Machine & Material.

The ideal candidate should be capable of independently managing plant operations and driving performance across production, manpower, machinery, materials, quality, cost, administration, and P&L.

🔹 Key Responsibilities
✅ Manage manpower planning, recruitment, discipline, safety & statutory compliance
✅ Own production planning, execution, machinery uptime & preventive maintenance
✅ Support plant expansion and implementation of new projects
✅ Manage raw material planning, procurement, stores & inventory
✅ Oversee quality control, wastage and inbound/outbound logistics
✅ Take ownership of plant P&L and cost optimization
✅ Manage overall factory administration
✅ Monitor and report production, cost and operational performance to Management

🔹 What We're Looking For
🔸 Strong experience in manufacturing/production leadership
🔸 Experience in mineral processing would be preferred
🔸 Exposure to refractory, coal, ferro alloy or related industries is an advantage
🔸 Strong knowledge of machinery, maintenance, civil work & fabrication
🔸 Proven experience managing plant-level teams and operations end-to-end
🔸 Working knowledge of ERP systems and standard computer applications
🔸 Strong numerical, analytical and P&L management skills
🔸 Odisha/Odia language proficiency would be an advantage
🔸 Willingness to be based full-time in Jharsuguda, Odisha
