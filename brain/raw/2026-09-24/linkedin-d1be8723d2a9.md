I genuinely don't understand how people live without a password manager.

I've been using one for over 12 years, and it is the one piece of technology I find myself talking about outside of work.

Not even because of the security. I mean, that's great, but mostly because I'm lazy and the thought of remembering 50 different passwords sounds miserable and was exhausting!

Left to my own devices, I know exactly what I do. I use the same password everywhere, change a character or two when forced to, and hope for the best.

My dad, who spent most of his career in IT, still has his own elaborate password algorithm that only he understands. I love the man, but I'm fairly certain we'll need a decoder ring if we ever have to access any of his accounts.

A password manager takes all of that off my plate. Unique passwords, no memorizing, no resetting my Netflix password for the 14th time.

And from a business perspective, the benefits go well beyond convenience. Securely managing and sharing credentials, controlling access, and not having important logins living in someone's head or a mystery spreadsheet.

Better security AND less hassle? That feels like a rare win in technology.

To those of you still managing passwords in your head, I have one question.

How the hell are you doing it?
