Amazon just brought Claude directly into the Amazon seller ecosystem.

Amazon has launched its Selling Partner Plugin for Anthropic’s Claude, currently in beta for sellers in the U.S.

And this is much bigger than simply connecting another AI tool to Seller Central.

With the plugin, sellers can connect their Amazon data directly to Claude, including:

• Listing information
• Real-time performance metrics
• Inventory levels
• Sales analytics

But the interesting part is what Claude can do with that data.

The plugin brings Seller Assistant’s intelligence into Claude, including its ability to reason across a seller’s business, provide recommendations, and take actions with the seller’s permission.

Imagine asking Claude:

“Analyze my Amazon business and identify the biggest opportunities in pricing, inventory, and advertising.”

Instead of manually exporting reports, uploading spreadsheets, and connecting different data sources, Claude can work with the Amazon data directly.

Amazon says the connection can be completed in roughly 60 seconds with no coding required.

And this is only one part of Amazon's broader AI direction.

Seller Assistant is also gaining persistent business memory and workflows that can continuously monitor things like inventory, pricing, account health, and product performance.

Sellers can define guardrails and choose whether AI simply provides recommendations or takes action with approval. Actions are recorded through audit trails.

Why this matters for Amazon sellers

The role of AI in Amazon is moving beyond:

“Ask AI a question.”

toward:

“Give AI access to the right business context and let it help operate the business.”

For Amazon brand managers and agencies, this could significantly change how we approach:

PPC → Inventory → Pricing → Listings → Analytics → Reporting

The real advantage may no longer be simply knowing how to use AI.

It may be knowing how to build the right systems, prompts, workflows, and guardrails around it.

Amazon is making that transition much more real with Claude.
The AI assistant is becoming an AI operator.
