What's the business advice you followed for years before you realized it was wrong?

I asked Paul R. DesRoches II, CEO of MOSS Building & Design. And he didn't hesitate. "Manage by numbers."

Of course, he's not against numbers. He's against running a company from a beach chair.

Paul walks the contradictions that come with leadership. He builds AI tools, and he'll tell you that you can't run a business from a spreadsheet. He wants to stop being the bottleneck, but he never wants to lose touch with the work. Most owners think you have to pick one. Paul doesn't.

His episode of The Last Person Who Knows is live. Link in the comments.

So what's yours? The advice you finally stopped following.
