AI won’t fix a broken process. It will help you break it faster. 🤖

Everyone is asking: “What can we automate with AI?”

I think we should start with a better question:

“Which part of our process is creating the most rework?”

Before introducing AI, ask four simple questions:

✅ Is the process clearly defined?
✅ Is the source data reliable?
✅ Who reviews exceptions and errors?
✅ How will we measure success?

In project delivery, saving 30 minutes means very little if the workflow creates more defects, confusion or rework.

A better approach is to start small:

* Automate meeting actions and follow-ups.
* Use AI to draft SOPs and test cases.
* Generate delivery summaries from verified project data.
* Keep human review where accuracy and accountability matter.

AI maturity isn’t about how many tools a company uses. It’s about how many reliable outcomes those tools deliver.

What’s the first workflow you would automate in your organization?
