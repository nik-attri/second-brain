Freight Tiger
Engineering movement to power India’s commerce forward.

Recognised by the World Economic Forum as a Technology Pioneer, Freight Tiger is building India’s digital freight infrastructure, powered by software, AI, and network intelligence.

We connect enterprise shippers, logistics service providers, and fleet owners on one intelligent platform that helps businesses plan, procure, move, track, and settle freight seamlessly.

Our platform brings together three core products:
Transportation Management System (TMS): End-to-end software connecting freight planning, procurement, execution, visibility, and settlement in one system.
TigerSight AI: Intelligence built on a deep understanding of how Indian freight actually moves. Combining real-world freight data, network intelligence, and operational context, TigerSight works alongside existing TMS and ERP systems to automate workflows, improve decisions, and help logistics teams anticipate and solve problems before they arise.
Freight Marketplace: Connecting freight demand with reliable trucking capacity across India, making freight procurement more transparent, efficient, and competitive.

The impact: Our customers have achieved 25–30% reduction in working capital, 8–10% lower logistics costs, 15–20% improvement in service levels, and end-to-end digital traceability.

Our scale today:
Nearly 5% of India’s road freight is digitally enabled by Freight Tiger
400+ enterprise manufacturers
1,800+ logistics service providers
500K+ fleet partners (with Tata FleetEdge integration)

We’re bringing software, AI, and network intelligence together to make Indian freight more efficient, reliable, and connected.
