Day 7 | Kalaratri: The fierce strength you forget you have 


I was a finance associate at a semiconductor company when our payroll software broke down, two days before Form 16 was due. 

We had already told employees the date. Pushing it was not an option. 

I wasn’t the most senior person in the room. I couldn’t fix the system or change the deadline. So I did what felt impossible: I rebuilt the entire thing manually in Excel, over two days and nights. The Form 16s reached every employee exactly when we had promised. 

I had no automation and no backup. What I had was a decision: this will be done. 

I remember that incident every time a coachee tells me, “What if I’m not ready?” 

Technology has made so much easier today. But the obstacles haven’t disappeared. They’ve moved inside. The mid-career professionals I coach rarely lack skill. What holds them back is: 
🔸 The “what if” loop: rehearsing every way it could go wrong 
🔸 Imposter syndrome: discounting their own track record 
🔸 Self-doubt: which quietly stops them from giving their best 

Kalaratri is the fiercest form of the Goddess. She doesn’t fight fear with more planning. She removes it by standing in her strength. 

Here is a simple 3-step practice I share with leaders aspiring for the next level: 
1. Recall your proof. Write down one moment you delivered when it seemed impossible. Be specific: what was at stake, and what did you do? 
2. Name the strength. Not “I worked hard,” but the actual quality: persistence, ownership, calm under pressure. 
3. Borrow it for today. Pick the one thing you’re doubting right now and ask: “What would that same strength do here?” 

That strength is already yours. It has been there through every deadline you’ve met. Which moment from your career proves your inner strength? Share it in the comments. 👇
