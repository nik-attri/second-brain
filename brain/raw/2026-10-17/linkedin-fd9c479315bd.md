KAMPUS TALENT – HIRING FOR OUR CLIENT

Position: Senior Manager – Training

Industry: BPO / BPM – International Travel Process

Location: Gurgaon

Work Mode: Work From Office

Job Responsibilities:

• Lead the end-to-end training function for international voice-based travel processes.

• Design and implement training programs for new hires, process updates, and refresher sessions.

• Ensure training coverage of travel reservations, ticketing, cancellations, reissues, refunds, and customer service procedures.

• Drive training effectiveness, certification, nesting, and trainee performance.

• Collaborate with Operations and Quality teams to identify skill gaps and improve process performance.

• Monitor training KPIs, including certification pass rates, nesting performance, quality scores, and training attrition.

• Develop training content, SOPs, assessments, and knowledge management resources.

• Ensure adherence to client requirements, process guidelines, and customer experience standards.

• Lead, mentor, and develop a team of trainers.

• Conduct root cause analysis and implement continuous improvement initiatives.

• Present training performance reports and improvement plans to senior management and clients.

Candidate Requirements:

• 8–12 years of experience in the BPO / BPM industry, with significant experience in Training.

• Strong experience in international voice-based travel processes.

• Knowledge of GDS platforms such as Amadeus, Sabre, or Galileo is preferred.

• Proven experience managing training teams and large-scale training operations.

• Strong understanding of training methodologies, quality metrics, and customer experience.

• Excellent communication, presentation, stakeholder management, and leadership skills.

• Ability to manage multiple training batches and deliver measurable business outcomes.

• Flexibility to work in rotational shifts, as per business requirements.
