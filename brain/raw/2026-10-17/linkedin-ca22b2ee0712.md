There's a HUGE GAP between what AI can do and how a lot of businesses still work.

This week, I spoke with the owner of a construction business whose main scaling problem is managing spreadsheets.

He's manually pulling expense data from bank statements and messages from his managers. 

As the business grows, he won't be able to keep doing this himself. He'll need to hire people just to keep this mess under control. He wants an easier way to record expenses and see where the money goes.

It made me think about HOW MUCH work like this is still out there.

While everyone's hunting for the next shiny SaaS idea to make millions, plenty of small and medium-sized businesses need simple tools to track expenses, manage their teams, and see what's going on.

AI makes it easier to build tools for exactly these problems.

Help them save time or money, and you've got something worth paying for.

I'm betting on traditional businesses with everyday operational problems.

Someone will close this gap and get rich in the process  😉 

I'm definitely going to play my part
