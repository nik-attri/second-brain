QUESTION: ❓ What Happens If My Salary Is Not Paid on Time in the UAE?
ANSWER: Getting paid on time is one of the most important responsibilities in the employer-employee relationship.

Under UAE Labour Law, employers must pay wages on their due dates according to the approved systems and applicable requirements.
📖 Law: Article 22(2), Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.
Let's understand with simple scenarios 👇

📌 Scenario 1: Your salary is due on a specific date
Example:
Your employment contract specifies when your salary is due.
➡️ Your employer is required to pay your wage on the due date, subject to the applicable rules.
📖 Law: Article 22(2).


📌 Scenario 2: Your company pays through WPS
Example:
Your company is registered with MoHRE and your salary is processed through the Wages Protection System (WPS).
➡️ WPS is designed to help ensure employees receive their wages in full and on time.
📖 MOHRE: WPS monitors wage-payment compliance.


📌 Scenario 3: Salary is repeatedly delayed
Example:
Your company regularly pays salaries after the agreed due date.
➡️ This can create WPS compliance issues for the employer and may lead to action under the applicable MoHRE procedures.
MOHRE's WPS guidance provides monitoring and consequences for establishments that fail to comply with wage-payment requirements.


📌 Scenario 4: HR wants to avoid payroll delays
Example:
Payroll is prepared manually using several Excel files.
Employee data → Excel
Attendance → Excel
Leave → Excel
Overtime → Excel
Payroll → another Excel file
WPS/SIF → separate process

➡️ The more manual steps involved, the more opportunities there are for errors or delays.
