Helping founders become exceptional operators who build businesses that run lighter, earn more, and scale beyond the founder.

YOUR REVENUE GOAL IS NOT YOUR REVENUE PLAN.

I’ve had the same conversation with two different founders in one day. And I’ll be having it again in a couple of weeks.

These are smart people running real businesses, with ambitious goals and plenty of opportunity. But when I ask exactly how they’re going to produce that revenue, things get fuzzy.

It blows my mind how much time founders spend debating revenue goals without figuring out how the hell they’re actually going to hit them.

LAST YEAR PLUS 25% IS NOT A GROWTH STRATEGY.

I love big goals. There aren’t many things I enjoy more than making a company bigger AND more profitable.

But adding 25% to last year’s revenue doesn’t make it a plan. It makes it a number you’re hoping to hit.

Here’s what I want to know first.

Where is the revenue actually coming from? Start with what your existing business will realistically produce. Then show me the new revenue. Which customers, markets, or channels? How much, and when?

And please don’t put $2M in a spreadsheet cell labeled “New Business” and expect me to get excited.

What will it take to produce that revenue? More sales might require people, marketing, inventory, equipment, or capacity. Those things cost money, and they need to happen in the right order.

Will the growth actually make us more profitable? Who’s responsible for delivering the number, what indicators are we watching, and how much of that revenue hits the bottom line?

Because growing revenue while destroying profitability is a pretty shitty accomplishment.
That’s part of the work we do through the Uprisors Founder’s Operating System™. We take the revenue ambition, work backward, and build the discipline to manage against it

You won’t know exactly where every dollar will come from. Some revenue is predictable, some depends on assumptions, and some is opportunity you’re pursuing. What matters is knowing the difference.

YOUR NEXT CONTROLLABLE STEP

Block 60 minutes with your leadership team. Put your 2027 revenue goal at the top of a spreadsheet and break it into four categories:

EXISTING REVENUE: What will your current customers, contracts, products, and services realistically produce?
NEW REVENUE: Which specific customers, markets, products, or channels will generate it?
ASSUMPTIONS: What needs to happen for those numbers to become real?
OWNERSHIP: Who is responsible for delivering each part of the plan?
Add it up. If you’re $1M short, don’t plug it into a line called “Other Growth.” Figure out where that million is coming from, or admit you don’t have a credible plan yet.

A revenue goal tells you where you want to go. A revenue plan tells you how you’re going to get there.
