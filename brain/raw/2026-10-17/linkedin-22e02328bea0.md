🚀 It’s official — Ecom Trust Shield is LIVE! 🛡️

After working on the idea, building the platform, and refining the initial features, I’m excited to share that Ecom Trust Shield – Phase 1 (Beta) is now live!

The idea behind this product is simple: e-commerce sellers shouldn’t have to manually inspect every shipping label to identify suspicious orders and potential fraud patterns.

So, we built a starting point to help make that process smarter. 💡

What can you do with Phase 1?

📦 Upload your shipping labels directly
🔍 Identify potential duplicate orders
⚠️ Uncover suspicious order and customer patterns
📍 Spot repeated addresses across orders
📊 Explore sales analytics and useful business insights

This is our Beta version, so we’re starting small, learning from real-world usage, and improving the product based on feedback.

🎉 And yes — Phase 1 is completely FREE to use!

No paid plan needed to explore the beta. We want e-commerce sellers to try it, share their experiences, and help us build something genuinely useful.

If you’re an e-commerce seller, D2C founder, or someone working in the e-commerce ecosystem, I’d love for you to give it a try.

Your feedback, suggestions, and honest criticism would mean a lot at this stage.

This is just the beginning. More to come! 🚀
