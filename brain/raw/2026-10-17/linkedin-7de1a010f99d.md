Well, For Fun on a Friday Afternoon, I Asked AI to Write My Obituary.

Now, I realize some folks might consider that a little morbid. Personally, I was sitting here giggling like a six-year-old who just heard his first fart joke. I figured after so many years in telecom, my time in the Marine Corps, and a lifetime of questioning why people insist on making simple things complicated, surely AI could come up with something entertaining. Lord, did it ever. It described me as a man who could identify a bad plan three meetings before everybody else figured out it was a bad plan. Said my favorite question was, "why in the hell are we doing it that way?" and I thought "Fair enough". 

Then it suggested my headstone should read; "He wasn't always right. But he damn sure wasn't afraid to find out". I laughed. Then I made the mistake of mentioning that my ex-wife might prefer something about me being a "Transformational Thought Leader". AI suggested she'd have it engraved in Comic Sans just to make sure I couldn't rest in peace. Now I'm laughing hard enough to scare the dog. I pointed out that my current wife would probably just thank God for the quiet. AI figured she'd finally get to enjoy an uninterrupted cup of coffee. 

Then we got to heaven. Apparently, within eleven minutes of arriving at the Pearly Gates, I'd be questioning Saint Peter about his preventive maintenance program, inspection records, and why nobody had addressed the single point of failure at the main entrance. 
Saint Peter explained that the gates had stood for eternity. My response? 

"THAT AINT A MAINTENANCE PLAN, PETE. THATS JUST LUCK WITH A LONG TRACK RECORD!"

At which point God reportedly asked Gabriel "who let the Marine in". Gabriel replied, "Sir, he's started a spreadsheet". I absolutely lost it. 

And somewhere in all that foolishness, I realized something. We've gotten so wrapped up in using AI to write professional emails, analyze spreadsheets, develop strategies, and make ourselves sound like important executives that we sometimes forget its perfectly acceptable to use technology just to have a little fun. Not everything needs a business case, measurable ROI, or a strategic implementation plan. Sometimes a good laugh is enough. 

So here's my Friday afternoon question: What's the funniest, most ridiculous thing you've ever asked AI to do? 

And if anybody needs me, Ill be reviewing the Pearly Gates maintenance contract. Apparently, they've been operating without a proper inspection schedule for several thousand years.
