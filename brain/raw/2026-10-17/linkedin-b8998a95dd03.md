🚀 From Ideas to Innovation: My Second AI Hackathon Experience! 🤖💻

I’m delighted to share my achievement in my second AI Hackathon! 🏆 This experience gave me an opportunity to turn an idea into a practical solution by designing and developing my own website — SmartSeat AI: Exam Seating Arrangement Generator.

💡 About My Project: SmartSeat AI Exam Seating Arrangement Generator 

Managing exam seating arrangements manually can be time-consuming and challenging. To address this, I developed a website that makes the process smarter, faster, and more organized.

✨ Key Features of My Website: 
✅ 📂 CSV Student Data Import – Upload student details, including roll numbers, names, and branches. 
✅ 🏫 Multiple Exam Room Management – Configure room capacities and seats per row. 
✅ 🤖 Smart Seating Arrangement – Generate seating plans while keeping students from the same branch apart in neighbouring seats. 
✅ 📊 Room-wise Seating Visualization – View student allocations clearly for each examination hall. ✅ 🔍 Roll Number Search – Quickly find a student's room, row, and seat. 
✅ 📄 Export & Print Options – Export seating details to CSV and print organized seating plans. 
✅ ✔️ Validation & Verification – Check room capacity and seating constraints before finalizing the plan.
