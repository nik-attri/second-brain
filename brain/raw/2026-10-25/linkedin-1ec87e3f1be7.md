Most sales teams turn their CRM into a spreadsheet with extra steps.

They create forty manual tags nobody remembers—or skip tagging entirely and scroll through hundreds of leads hoping the right one surfaces.

The fix is simple: Status tells you the stage. Tags tell you the story.

Status answers where a lead sits in a straight line. Tags answer what makes them worth acting on right now—like VIP, High Value, or Follow Up.

But manual tagging fails the moment your pipeline gets busy. Nobody tags a lead by hand at 4 PM on a Friday.

If a system sees the event, it should apply the tag automatically. That is why Ledo handles the context work for you, so your pipeline updates instantly without endless dropdown menus.

Stop drowning in manual CRM admin and let your pipeline organize itself.
