Exploring Salesforce Agentforce Grid: Turning CRM Data into AI-Powered Insights!
As part of my Salesforce Agentforce learning journey, I explored Agentforce Grid and built a practical example using AI to summarize Salesforce records.
●       What is Agentforce Grid?
Agentforce Grid is a spreadsheet-like workspace for working with Salesforce data and applying AI-powered capabilities to records at scale.
●       Why do we use Agentforce Grid?
●       To work with multiple Salesforce records in a grid-based interface.
●        To apply AI-powered Prompt Templates to record data.
●        To reduce repetitive manual work through AI-assisted workflows.
●       To generate useful insights and update Salesforce records more efficiently.
My Hands-on Example
I created a practical use case to generate a summary of a Salesforce record and add the generated summary to its Description field.
Here’s what I explored:
1.      Selected Salesforce records in Agentforce Grid.
2.      Used a Prompt Template to generate a meaningful summary.
3.      Applied the generated summary to the Description field.
4.      Explored how AI can help make CRM information easier to understand and maintain.
 My Key Takeaway
Agentforce Grid helps bring AI into everyday Salesforce data workflows. Instead of manually reviewing and summarizing records one by one, we can explore AI-powered approaches to make this process more efficient.
This hands-on experience helped me understand how Agentforce Grid, Prompt Templates, and Salesforce records can work together to solve practical business problems.
