My creative strategy Mentor was in a rare humble mood the other day, so he said:

“I’m not the best creative strategist in the world, but no one can beat me in research.”

I’ll fight him on the first part. 

But the second part? Dead accurate.

The research process this man drilled into me is so thorough that most marketers would assume it was invented by someone who enjoys reading spreadsheets on a Saturday night.

I’m not gonna spill his entire playbook. But the two pieces that matter most:
1- Customers
2- Almost Customers

These two groups will hand you more money-making insight than every Reddit rabbit hole and AI deep research tool combined.

People on Reddit are there to vent. 

That’s useful, but it’s like learning about marriage by only talking to divorce lawyers.

Customers and almost customers either already pulled out their credit card, or came within inches of doing it. 

They’re telling you, in their own words, what pushed them toward the buy button or yanked them away at the last second.

When you mirror their exact language back in your ads? 

Their brain short-circuits: “How the hell does this company know not just WHAT I’m thinking, but HOW I’m thinking it?”

That reaction is the holy grail of advertising. 

It comes from listening so carefully your prospect feels like you’ve been hiding under their bed taking notes.

So here’s what you do.

Go to the founder and say: “I need to survey and interview past buyers. Pull me a CSV from Stripe or Shopify with name, email, product purchased, and approximate date.”

Surveys give you volume. 

Interviews give you depth. 

One good 20-minute call with a real buyer gives you lines of copy no copywriter in a coffee shop could dream up alone. 

Doing both is ideal.

Most people send out requests, get crickets, and throw their hands up. 

Of course nobody responded. 

You have to bribe them. 

The ethical bribe!

For surveys: “Hey, last month you bought X from us. Down for quick survey? We’ll send a $25 Amazon gift card your way.”

For interviews, same approach, fatter bribe. $100 gift card. 

More time, more money.

The specific questions to ask? 

Those belong to my Mentor. 

Use your brain. 

Think about which questions would get a buyer to reveal the raw, unfiltered version of why they purchased, the one they’d only admit at 2am.

That covers Customers. 

Now, Almost Customers.

These are people who got to the edge of buying and walked away. 

Pull up the last 50 to 100 support calls, emails, and DMs. 

Read every one like your rent depends on it. 

Buried inside are all the objections your ads need to handle and the exact moments where prospects went cold.

Find where in the thread they disappeared. 

That’s where the real objection lives. 

Mirror their exact language back in your next round of ads.

Class dismissed.
