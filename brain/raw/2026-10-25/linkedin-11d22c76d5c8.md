One customer conversation changed everything. Let's have it.

Most owners I talk to have tried ChatGPT. Almost none have found the ONE manual step quietly eating their week alive. That's the whole problem. Not strategy. Not tooling. Diagnosis.

This is what happens if you skip that step: you buy another $99/month AI tool, nobody uses it, and the same person is still copy-pasting invoices at 7pm on a Friday.

Here's the 20-minute test I run with every owner:

1. Ask one person: "What do you copy-paste every single day?"
2. Time it. If it eats 20+ minutes a day, that's about 80 hours a year of pure grunt work.

3. That task — not the shiny new tool — is the first thing you automate.

We ran this on our own content workflow. One video used to turn into 14 manual jobs. Now it's 1 click. About 80 hours a year, back.
