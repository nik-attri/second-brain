Four months into Tranche 2, there’s a good chance your AML process looks a little different to the one you planned pre-July 😉

And that’s not necessarily a bad thing.

When new processes are introduced, you find ways to make them work. You create spreadsheets. Add extra steps. Copy information from one system into another. You introduce a temporary workaround because you just need to get the job done while you fully understand the nuances of the new requirements.

The problem is when “temporary” quietly becomes “this is just how we do AML now” 😵‍💫

So, now is a good time to look at your process with fresh eyes.

👉 Where are you entering the same information twice?
👉 What are you still tracking manually?
👉 Which steps were introduced as a workaround?
👉 Are you collecting information you don’t actually need?
👉 Is there anything your team does simply because “we just do”?

Your AML obligations aren’t going anywhere. But the clunky processes you created while finding your feet don’t have to stick around forever.

Four months in, you know a whole lot more about how AML actually fits into your business.  Use that knowledge to make the process better 🙌
