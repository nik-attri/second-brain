One year at Superior University 🎉

When I joined, I knew I’d be hiring people. I didn’t expect how much I’d learn from them. This year our Talent Acquisition team has welcomed faculty and staff across different faculties and departments, and more. Each hire is someone who will shape our students’ futures.

Here’s to year two and the people we’ll bring through the door next. 🚀
