For years I've had a visual content problem.

I work with a wide range of technical organisations, but many understandably prohibit on-site photography or recording because of confidentiality requirements. I get it. 

That meant I often had two choices:
A) Really CHEESY super stereotyped stock photos
or
B) No visuals at all

The problem is that most leadership and business stock photography makes me cringe.

You know the ones:
• executives shaking hands
• teams laughing at spreadsheets (like who the hell has ever done that?)
• perfectly posed board meetings (all male, white 50+)

They bear little resemblance to real work life.
So I've been experimenting more with AI-generated images and footage.

Not because it's ‘in’, but because I’m not big on compromise and for me it solves two genuine challenges:

✅ Full compliance with confidentiality rules
✅ Creative freedom to create visuals that actually match the topic

The biggest lesson?
I stopped prompting for subjects and started prompting for a really specific situation.
Instead of:
"Executive meeting"
Try:
"Moments before a difficult board discussion"
or
"Non-executive director preparing for an audit committee meeting"

The results are dramatically more authentic despite being ‘artificial’ if you get my drift!

Help me. 

Let’s get beyond the endless library of cringe-worthy stock photos by putting the time and yes … thought into prompting much more specifically. You can be insanely bossy and demanding which you can’t and wouldn’t want to with people.

Have you started using AI visuals yet, or are you still relying mostly on stock photography?
