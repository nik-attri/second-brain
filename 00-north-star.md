# North Star

> **The gate.** Nothing in `engine/` gets written until this passes the verifier's test.

## Statement

> **Currently a full-time AI engineer at Attri with zero independent consulting clients, by November 2026 I am running an AI automation consulting practice with 3 paying SMB clients on $5,000/month retainers — verifiable by three signed contracts and three months of invoices — because I want $15,000/month of income that is not tied to one job.**

## The four parts

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| **Current state** | Full-time AI engineer at Attri, zero independent consulting clients, no signed contracts |
| **Time-bound goal** | 3 paying clients at $5,000/month retainer, by **30 November 2026** |
| **Provable activity** | 3 signed contracts + 3 months of issued invoices, each ≥ $5,000/mo |
| **Reason** | I want $15,000/month of income that is not tied to one job |

## Verifier's test

*Could a stranger verify this after the deadline?*

A stranger is handed this sentence on 1 December 2026. They ask for three things:
1. Three signed client contracts, dated on or before 30 November 2026
2. Invoices showing ≥ $5,000/month per client
3. Payment confirmation for at least one billing cycle each

If all three exist → success. If two contracts exist → failure, unambiguously. **No interpretation required. Test passed.**

## Qualified-client definition (used by the scoring engine)

"SMB" at a $5K/mo price point is not a 3-person shop. A qualified prospect:

- **Size:** 10–200 employees
- **Revenue:** $1M+ annual
- **Budget signal:** already pays for SaaS (HubSpot, Zapier, Notion, Shopify Plus, etc.)
- **Pain signal:** publicly describes manual/repetitive work, hiring for ops/admin roles, or scaling strain
- **Reachable:** a named decision-maker (founder, COO, Head of Ops) with a public contact path

Anything failing **size** or **budget signal** is disqualified regardless of how interesting the post is.

## Pick your reason (part four)

The reason is the one part I can't write for you. It has to be true, and it has to be the thing that keeps you working on this at 11pm. Candidates:

Replace `[REASON]` above with your pick, or write your own.
